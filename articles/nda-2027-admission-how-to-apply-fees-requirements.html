NDA 2027 admission: How to apply, fees, requirements

By Punch Punch 9h ago 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Nigerian Defence Academy has opened applications for admission into its 79th Regular Course for the 2027/2028 academic session, with eligible male and femal

The academy announced on Monday that the application portal would remain open from November 23, 2026, to April 30, 2027.

Prospective applicants are expected to meet the admission requirements, participate in the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board examination and pass the academy’s screening exercises.

Here is what candidates need to know about the admission process.

When does the NDA 2027 admission application close?

The application portal opened on Monday, November 23, 2026, and will close on April 30, 2027.

According to the admission announcement, “Online application for admission into the 79 Regular Course (RC) of the Nigerian Defence Academy (NDA), Kaduna will be available from Monday 23 November 2026 to 30 April 2027.”

It added that admission was open to “both male and female Nigerians.”

How much is the NDA application form?

The application fee is ₦7,100.

Candidates are required to visit the academy’s admission portal, select the purchase access code option and complete their details before making payment through Remita.

The announcement stated, “Candidates should log on to the application portal rc.nda.edu.ng, select ‘Purchase Access Code’ and fill in their details to pay for their application via REMITA in the sum of ₦7,100.00 only.”

It warned that “only payment against RRR Codes generated via the application portal would be accepted.”

How can candidates apply?

Interested candidates are required to complete their applications online through the NDA admission portal.

The academy advised applicants to download and carefully read the instructions provided on the website before proceeding with their applications.

“Interested candidates are advised to go online and complete the application on the website indicated above. Please download and read carefully, the instructions provided on the website,” the announcement stated.

Candidates are also required to submit their applications alongside scanned copies of their Senior Secondary Certificate Examination, National Examinations Council or equivalent results and their National Identification Number details.

The academy said, “All NIN details must be in tandem with details on other credentials.”

After submitting their applications, candidates are expected to download an acknowledgement form.

Is JAMB required for NDA admission?

Yes. Applicants must register for the 2027 Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination organised by JAMB and select the Nigerian Defence Academy as their first-choice institution.

The academy made this requirement clear in its announcement, stating, “Applicants seeking admission into the NDA must also apply to the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) and must select NDA as first choice institution.”

It added that such applicants were “expected to sit for the JAMB Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME).”

What is the required UTME score?

Candidates seeking admission into Engineering programmes must score at least 210 in the UTME, while those applying for other faculties must obtain a minimum of 180.

The academy stated that only candidates who satisfied the admission requirements and obtained the required UTME scores would be able to print their screening test admission cards and participate in the screening exercise.

What are the other admission requirements?

The specific requirements for the various undergraduate degree programmes are available on the NDA website and application portal.

However, the academy stated that candidates must have SSCE, NECO or equivalent results not more than five years old at the time of admission.

It also identified knowledge of French or Arabic as an added advantage, stating, “Knowledge of French or Arabic will be an added advantage to applicants.”

The announcement further emphasised that results from SSCE, NECO or equivalent examinations “must also not be more than 5 years as of the time of admission.”

When will the NDA screening test hold?

The NDA announced Saturday, June 8, 2027, as the date for the first phase of its screening test, which will serve as the post-UTME exercise.

The announcement stated, “The NDA Screening Test will be conducted as Phase One of the Post-UTME screening and is scheduled to hold on Saturday 8 June 2027.”

Candidates who qualify are required to present their acknowledgement forms, screening test admission cards, JAMB result slips and two postcard-sized photographs measuring 3.5 by five inches.

According to the academy, the photographs must show candidates from the chest upwards and contain their names, examination numbers, states, examination centres, courses of study and signatures at the back.

What happens after the first screening test?

Candidates who successfully pass the first phase will be invited for the Armed Forces Selection Board exercise, which constitutes the second phase of the screening process.

The academy stated that “only successful candidates at the NDA Screening Test will subsequently be invited for the Armed Forces Selection Board (Phase 2 of the NDA Screening Test).”

It added that the list of admitted candidates would be published after the exercise.

How long is the NDA training?

Candidates admitted into the 79th Regular Course will undergo five years of military and academic training as officer cadets.

The academy stated that “candidates admitted into the NDA for Regular Course will undergo military and academic training as officer cadets for a duration of 5 years.”

Upon successful completion of the programme, graduates will receive honours degrees in their respective disciplines, including Bachelor of Science, Bachelor of Engineering and Bachelor of Arts degrees.

The academy added that graduates would “equally earn a Presidential Commission into the Armed Forces of Nigeria.”

Who pays for the training?

The Federal Government will bear the cost of administration and logistics for all Regular Course cadets throughout their training.

The academy stated, “All Regular Course cadets will train in NDA and the cost of their administration and logistics will be borne by the Federal Government of Nigeria.”

Where can applicants get more information?

Prospective applicants can obtain further information about the admission process, available courses, eligibility requirements and screening centres through the following channels:

• NDA website: www.nda.edu.ng
• Application portal: rc.nda.edu.ng
• Enquiries/support: info@ndaapplications.net

The academy also announced that a compendium of past questions and answers was available for interested candidates.

According to the notice, “The Academy offers a Compendium of Past Questions & Answers at a taken fee to interested candidates. This can be purchased from the Registry, NDA.”
