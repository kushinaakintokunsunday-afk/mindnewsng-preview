Ukraine Claims Strikes On Two Russian Boats Off Libya

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 2 min read

Photo: Channels TV

President Volodymyr Zelensky on Monday said Ukraine was behind strikes on two Russian vessels off Libya, adding that one of them sank in the latest spillover from Moscow’s war with Kyiv.

The attacks on Sunday near the Libyan port of Benghazi come at a time when Ukraine has stepped up its strikes against Russia far from the frontlines.

But the United Nations condemned the strikes, warning against the prospect of a country still divided after the Libyan civil war becoming “a battleground for foreign conflicts”.

“It all took place off the coast of Libya. Our warriors deployed seven Ukrainian-made naval drones. Despite all the enemy’s efforts to counter the attack, the Russian corvette Merkury was sunk,” Zelensky wrote on X.

The Ukrainian leader claimed the Merkury was “one of their newest ships, commissioned in 2023, during the full-scale war”.

“Our second target was the Russian dry cargo vessel MV ANGARA, which the enemy used to transport weapons. Result: target hit,” he added.

A Ka-27 combat helicopter was also destroyed in the strike on the Merkury, Zelensky said.

Sunday’s strikes came after a drone hit a Russian cargo ship in the eastern Baltic Sea on Saturday, close to the waters of Finland, whose border guard said the strike was Ukrainian.

The UN mission in Libya expressed “serious concern” over the attacks, calling for “an immediate, independent and transparent investigation”.

“Libya cannot afford, and must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts,” the UNSMIL wrote on X.
