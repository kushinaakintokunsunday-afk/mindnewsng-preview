Data intelligence crucial to effective governance – CREDICORP boss

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Nigerian Consumer Credit Corporation, Uzoma Nwagba, has commended the Data Analytics Group for Asiwaju for it

Nwagba gave the commendation on Thursday during a courtesy visit to the headquarters of DAGFA, where he met with the organisation’s leadership and members to discuss its work and growing relevance in using data analytics to understand emerging trends and public narratives.

He said data analytics and intelligence were now central to strengthening strategic communication, information credibility and evidence-based decision-making.

Nwagba said, “Data intelligence is becoming increasingly significant in modern governance. Timely access to credible information, accurate data interpretation and strategic analysis are now essential to institutional communication and decision-making.”

He noted that DAGFA had continued to advance the application of data analytics and intelligence in examining public narratives, interrogating information and providing a more structured understanding of developments around the Presidency.

“Your work reflects the growing need for data-backed perspectives in an environment where information travels rapidly, and public perception can easily be influenced by unverified claims and competing narratives.

“We need data-backed perspectives now more than ever. In an environment where information travels rapidly, public perception can easily be influenced by unverified claims and competing narratives,” he said.

During the visit, Nwagba also discussed CREDICORP’s journey, achievements and progress since its establishment, as well as the importance of innovation and institutional capacity in delivering on national development objectives.

“CREDICORP was established under the administration of President Bola Tinubu to expand access to consumer credit and promote financial inclusion. Under my leadership, the corporation has been advancing initiatives aimed at making consumer credit more accessible to Nigerians. We are committed to making credit more accessible to Nigerians and supporting national development objectives,” Nwagba said.

He added that before he was appointed CREDICORP’s MD/CEO in April 2024, Nwagba served as Chief Operating Officer of the Bank of Industry, where he was involved in driving microcredit and financial intervention programmes.

He further disclosed that he had worked at Microsoft and African Capital Alliance and holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and a First-Class degree in Engineering from Howard University.

The visit culminated in the presentation of a special appreciation award to Nwagba by DAGFA in recognition of his service to the government and contributions under the current administration.

The recognition, according to DAGFA, further reinforces its commitment to advancing data intelligence as an important resource for understanding public discourse, strengthening information credibility and supporting strategic engagement around the Presidency.
