Brands can share product value in a concise manner using short visuals, movement, and memorable messaging in short promotional clips. These videos include key features with a focus on social media and digital advertising. But to create interesting promotional material, you need clear creative direction and appropriate visuals. Dreamina offers an AI-driven platform to convert written ideas into promotional videos. It has tools that include text prompts, product references, and video production. Describe the desired air, product presentation, visual sequence, and storyline direction. This method can be used to make marketing concepts applicable to short-form video content without the need for traditional production setups.

Understand How Text Prompts Shape Short Promotional Videos

Text prompts convey the desired look, motion, and flow of the story in promotional videos. They give information regarding products, backgrounds, lighting, camera moves, and environments. For instance, a skincare bottle on a marble surface sets the scene. Incorporating directions for camera movement makes for a more dynamic presentation. Certain adjectives also have a specific effect on the visual atmosphere, such as warmth, brightness, contrast, and color relations. Use clear language, such as good advertising, as vague words don’t offer much in the way of visual guidance. Rather, describe what you see, how the products interact, and what is going on in the environment. Dreamina’s Marketing Studio delivers written instructions and AI video generation. This enables ideas to be expanded into visual advertising material via descriptive prompts.

Identify the Essential Elements of an Effective Promo Clip

There are a number of creative elements that are combined to create an effective promotional video that sends one message that is easily understood by all. All elements align with the video’s structure, visual consistency, and promotional intent. Keep these elements in mind when developing your creative direction:

  • Opening Visual: Start with a good composition to introduce the product. It has a unique opening frame that sets the context and draws in attention.
  • Product Focus: Make sure that the main product is easily recognizable throughout the main sequence. Positioning is important, as it tells viewers what exactly the ad is about.
  • Visual Movement: Add intentional movement by moving the camera or interacting with products. The controlled movement is visually interesting but not distracting.
  • Brand Atmosphere: Create appropriate colors, lighting, and environmental elements. Visual treatments that are always applied contribute to the desired brand.
  • Promotional Message: Deliver a short message that talks about a single product benefit. Don’t clutter the composition with conflicting promotional material.
  • Closing Moment: End with a product-related visual/promotional statement to remember. A clear ending provides a familiar conclusion to the ad.

Write Detailed Prompts That Translate Ideas into Visual Sequences

Write instructions for a specific product and state the promotional goal. Determine if the clip should be an introduction to a product, a feature demonstration, or an offer announcement. Then, write the initial shot, product interaction, and intended final shot. Add elements of the environment: indoor settings, store displays, or outdoor scenes. Lighting directions can set a mood with soft lighting, shadow play, or daylight. Camera moves should be described, such as a gradual zoom, tracking, or a rotation in camera direction. Reference pictures can convey product appearance, colours, and composition more accurately. Storytelling structure, movement, and overall creative style can be inspired by reference videos. Include instructions in terms of actions seen, not random descriptive words. By providing clear and concise instructions, AI can better understand the desired sequence and generate more cohesive promotional visuals.

Draft Short Promo Clips from Text Prompts via AI Marketing Studio

Step 1: Open the studio and write your text prompt

  1. Sign up or log in to Dreamina first, so you can start drafting your clips.
  2. Next, click “Marketing Studio” on the left bar to open your promo drafting workspace.
  3. Here, pick “AI video” for clips, “AI image” for stills, or “AI agent” for both.
  4. Inside “AI video”, tap “Change style” to choose the look your promo clip should follow.
  5. Then click “Product” to upload your photo, and click “Avatar” to add a presenter too. You can also choose existing products and avatars from the built-in library whenever you prefer.
  6. Write a text prompt describing the look and feel of your clip, and add custom direction freely.

Step 2: Pick your model and generate the draft

  1. Click the “Reference” tab to add a video that guides the AI’s look and feel.
  2. Choose a model for your draft, such as HappyHorse 1.1 or Dreamina Seedance 2.5. Other choices include Dreamina Seedance 2.0, Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Fast, Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Mini, Wan 3.0, etc.
  3. Select a custom size and quality from the “Aspect ratio” and “Resolution” tabs for your clip.
  4. Click the “Duration” tab to select a custom length that keeps your promo short.
  5. Use the “@” option to mention or highlight the specific product within your video.
  6. Finally, click “Generate” to begin creating your first draft.

Step 3: Review the draft and save your clip

  1. Watch the finished draft on full screen and check the product framing, the avatar’s delivery, and the pacing.
  2. If the draft misses your idea, use “Edit prompt” or “Regenerate” to refine the result.
  3. You can also open “Edit in canvas” to replace the background, add effects, crop the video, and more.
  4. Once the clip looks right, click “Download” to save it and share it across your marketing channels.

Create Different Promotional Concepts from One Product Description

There are several different written instructions that can be used with a single product reference to support multiple advertising concepts. A beverage description can generate product introductions and demonstrations that focus on product features, for instance. Seasonal prompts can add festive backgrounds, appropriate decorations, and even lighting. Packaging, product information, and announcement graphics can be highlighted in limited-time promotional concepts. There are lifestyle shots, studio demos, and product reveals as well. Using product references will keep similar looks if there are variations of the creatives. At the same time, there is visual diversity as camera directions, environments, and storytelling approaches are changed. This is a way to develop a campaign without having to swap out the product repeatedly.

Refine Short Clips Through Visual Editing and Creative Adjustments

Promotional videos that are generated should be carefully reviewed before publishing. Analyze whether the movement is effective in conveying the message and maintains the visibility of the product information. Background replacement can add appropriate retail environments, studio settings, or lifestyle compositions. You can use Edit in Canvas to crop video clips and add visual effects. Be sure to check framing, as too much cropping may result in the loss of important product features. When clips have several visual transitions, scene continuity is important as well. If the original result does not go in the desired direction, tweak the prompt of the original result. You can re-generate the video with better instructions to get a better composition.

Prepare Prompt-Based Promotional Clips for Different Marketing Channels

Video dimensions vary depending on the marketing channel where they will be displayed. Vertical formats tend to be used in mobile-first placements such as short-form social media videos. Square compositions are also a choice for flexible social media advertising. Landscape is used for larger formats, websites, and some digital advertising spaces. Choose the aspect ratio based on where the video will be published. The length of the video also affects the story, the speed of the video, and the amount of information conveyed. When changing dimensions, keep product names, packaging, and important information visible. Dreamina supports various output configurations to develop multiple versions of the same creative concepts. Check each version individually for proper framing and clarity.

Conclusion

Text prompts give a versatile starting point for creating short promotional videos. Product presentation, atmosphere, movement, and storytelling direction are defined by clear descriptions. Visual generation is also informed by product mentions and available AI models. The editing tools can be used to make the background, framing, and other creative elements perfect. Dreamina combines all these features into one content creation platform. Promotional concepts are turned into reusable video assets with purposeful instructions and thoughtful refinement. A consistent visual direction allows for a recognizable product presentation in each of the marketing channels.
