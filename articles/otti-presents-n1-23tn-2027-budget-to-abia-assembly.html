Otti presents N1.23tn 2027 budget to Abia Assembly

By Punch Punch 5d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Abia State Governor, Alex Otti, has presented a proposed budget of N1.23tn for the 2027 fiscal year to the state House of Assembly for consideration and approva

Presenting the appropriation bill before the Speaker and members of the 8th Assembly on Thursday, Otti described the proposal as an ambitious but realistic plan to sustain the state’s development drive.

“Today, when I come to this hallowed assembly to lay the draft of the 2027 Appropriation Bill for the next fiscal year, a total budget estimate of N1.23 trillion shall be tabled before this Honourable House for consideration,” Otti said.

He said N647.23bn was expected from internal and external revenue sources, including internally generated revenue, statutory allocations from the Federation Account Allocation Committee, Value Added Tax and other federal revenue sources, as well as capital receipts.

The governor said the remaining N552.67bn would be raised through concessional loans and dedicated to infrastructure projects with regenerative value.

“The balance of N552.67 billion shall be sourced through concessional loans and committed strictly to infrastructure projects with regenerative value,” he stated.

Otti put the state’s projected internally generated revenue for 2027 at N199.8bn, saying the amount, if realised, would cover 180 per cent of recurrent expenditure.

“The projected IGR output for the coming fiscal year is estimated at N199.8 billion, and if achieved as projected, will be enough to cover 180% of our recurrent expenditure, which puts us in a good fiscal position,” he said.

He added that the proposed budget represented an 80 per cent increase over the current year’s budget and a 648 per cent rise in IGR compared with 2023.

The governor said 80 per cent of the proposed expenditure would go to capital projects, while the remaining 20 per cent would cover recurrent spending.

“We are devoting 80% of the entire budget, that is N960 billion in absolute numbers, to capital expenditure, leaving the remaining 20% for recurrent expenditure,” Otti said.

He said the administration had prioritised human capital development, with 35 per cent of the proposed budget earmarked for education and health.

According to him, education would receive 20 per cent, while the health sector would get 15 per cent of the total budget.

“This necessitates the commitment of 35% of the 2027 fiscal outlay to the education and health sectors, with basic, secondary and tertiary education receiving 20%, while the health sector gets 15%,” he stated.

On sectoral allocations, Otti said 55 per cent of the proposed budget would go to the economic sector, covering agriculture, works, transportation, petroleum and solid mineral development, power and public utilities, among other areas.

He listed other areas under the sector to include the Greater Aba Development Authority, land administration, arts, culture and the creative economy, digital capacity development, infrastructure and support for small and medium-scale enterprises.

The governor said 7.2 per cent of the budget was allocated to the social sector, covering education, health, environmental protection, sports development, local government and chieftaincy affairs, poverty alleviation, social protection and women’s affairs.

He added that the administrative sector, including law and justice, would receive 5.1 per cent of the proposed expenditure, with N17.3bn earmarked for it.

Describing the proposal as both ambitious and achievable, Otti said the state had demonstrated the capacity to sustain its development plans.

“The proposed budget is as realistic as it is aspirational, reflecting the magnitude of our ambition,” Otti said.

“The ship has since left the harbour, and we have demonstrated sufficient capacity and resilience to weather the storms that lie ahead. The job now is to keep our focus, maintain the course and stay committed to the project,” he added.

He said the proposal was prepared with contributions from stakeholders, including business leaders, development partners, civil society organisations, community leaders and traditional rulers.

“We have painstakingly aggregated the views of our people to put together a realistic and responsive budget that promises to further expand the sea of opportunities for everyone while protecting vulnerable individuals and households,” he stated.

Otti thanked the lawmakers for their support and urged them to scrutinise the proposal to ensure it reflected the aspirations of residents.

“The expectation is that, as you have done in the last three years, you will meticulously study and critique the bill, which I shall shortly lay before you, to ensure that it reflects in its entirety the collective aspirations of our people,” he said.

He expressed optimism that the Assembly would pass the bill before the end of the year, saying its timely passage would provide clarity for investors and development institutions seeking opportunities in the state.

Responding, the Speaker of the House, Rt. Hon. Emmanuel Emeruwa, said the increased budget proposal indicated progress in the state.

He assured the governor that the lawmakers would examine the estimates and ensure their speedy passage before the end of 2026.

The proposed N1.23tn budget represents another increase in Abia State’s annual spending plan under Otti’s administration.

In December 2024, the governor signed the N750.28bn 2025 budget into law, with 82 per cent allocated to capital expenditure and 18 per cent to recurrent spending.

In November 2025, Otti presented a N1.016tn proposal for the 2026 fiscal year, representing a 13 per cent increase over the 2025 budget.

The proposal allocated N811.8bn, or 80 per cent, to capital projects and N204.4bn to recurrent expenditure. It was subsequently signed into law on December 29, 2025.
