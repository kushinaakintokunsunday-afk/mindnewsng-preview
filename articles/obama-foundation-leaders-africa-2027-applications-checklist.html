Obama Is Picking Just 35 Young Africans. Here Is the Exact Checklist You Must Hit to Make the Cut

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 10 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

Applications just opened for the Obama Foundation Leaders Africa programme - one of the most prestigious leadership programmes on the continent, with only 35 sp

Every year, a select group of just 35 changemakers from across Africa is chosen for one of the most prestigious development programmes on the continent - run personally by the Obama Foundation.

Applications for the next cohort have just opened (they went live on November 9), and with thousands competing for those slots, the difference between selection and rejection is knowing the official checklist - point by point - before you start typing.

The exact eligibility checklist (straight from obama.org)

  • Age 24-45 during the programme year.
  • Live in Africa now - you must currently reside in a country or territory on the continent. Africans living abroad are not eligible.
  • At least the last 5 years in Africa - regardless of your citizenship or residency status.
  • 3+ years of experience in your issue area - you must show real, demonstrable impact, not just intentions. You do NOT need to work on it full time.
  • Professionally fluent in English.
  • Work focused on Africa - your change-making must be aimed at your local community, your nation, or the wider region.
  • No political campaigns: from acceptance to programme end, you cannot stand for elective office.
  • US travel and restrictions: you must be free of any legal or regulatory bars that would stop you joining US-based Foundation programming or travelling to America if an in-person component applies.

The fine print most applicants miss

  • One programme only: the Foundation runs Leaders programmes for Africa, Asia-Pacific, Europe and the USA - eligible for two? You may still apply to just one.
  • Previous Obama Leaders (any region) cannot reapply - but past rejected applicants are welcome to try again, and last year's application is not held against you.
  • References are required - brief yours before you apply, and email globalleaders@obama.org early if you have eligibility questions; the volume of applications means they warn of response delays.
  • The programme runs about 8 months (starting around September), including 8-12 hours of virtual programming per month, coaching sessions and community group discussions, with planned breaks.

What winners actually get

The programme - free of charge - builds a permanent network of values-based leaders across the continent, deepens leadership skills through coaching and peer learning, and connects members to Obama Foundation work globally. Alumni go on to tackle climate justice, food security, human rights and more across Africa.

Nigerians are already in the room

The current 2026-2027 Africa cohort already includes two Nigerians - Phebe Ilesanmi and Yewande Benn - alongside leaders from Kenya, Ghana, Egypt, South Africa, Rwanda and beyond. For young Nigerians and Kenyans chasing their first big international platform, this is one of the few programmes where a track record of community impact matters more than credentials.

Applications are time-sensitive and programmes and dates can change - verify every requirement directly at obama.org/programs/leaders/africa and follow the official guidance.

Source: The Obama Foundation - Leaders Africa eligibility and FAQ pages.
