PHOTOS: APC supporters throng Kebbi stadium for Tinubu’s rally

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu’s presidential rally in Kebbi State on Tuesday attracted a massive crowd of All Progressives Congress supporters, party loyalists and poli

The rally, held at the Haliru Abdu Township Stadium in Birnin Kebbi, witnessed a large turnout of party members, women, youths, farmers and grassroots mobilisers ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Governor Nasir Idris, who hosted the President and other top APC leaders, described the massive attendance as a demonstration of the people’s support for the party and its leadership.

The stadium was filled with supporters carrying party flags, banners and campaign materials as they gathered to welcome Tinubu and reaffirm their loyalty to the ruling party.

The turnout followed weeks of mobilisation by the state government, APC leadership and various support groups across the 21 Local Government Areas of the state.

Speaking ahead of the presidential visit, Idris had assured the President that the state was fully prepared to give him a rousing reception.

He said the government, party structures and supporters had put necessary arrangements in place to ensure a successful rally.

The rally also provided an opportunity for APC leaders to mobilise support for Tinubu’s re-election bid and strengthen the party’s political structures ahead of the 2027 elections.

The presence of party stakeholders and supporters from different parts of the state underscored the APC’s efforts to consolidate its political influence in Kebbi.

The presidential visit further highlighted the growing political activities in the state as parties intensify preparations for the next general elections.
