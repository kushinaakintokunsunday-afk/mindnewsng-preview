Liverpool boss wary of Pochettino’s tactics ahead of Man Utd clash

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola fears Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino will have an advantage over him on Sunday because he does not know the Argentin

Pochettino will be taking charge of United for the first time since leaving his role as USA national team coach to replace the sacked Michael Carrick.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea boss has had limited time working with United’s players since arriving in Manchester this week.

That leaves Iraola guessing how Pochettino will deploy his team at Anfield.

“We’ve faced each other. Obviously he has a lot of Premier League games that we can look at, and we can look at games in the US, but they still have this small advantage,” Iraola told reporters on Friday.

“We don’t have references to prepare because they can go different ways that we don’t actually know.

“The staff have been working with them, so they are going to arrive well prepared. We don’t have the references that we have against other teams.”

Iraola will have to adapt on the fly and the Spaniard plans to switch up his game-plan if he is caught out by Pochettino’s tactics.

“Especially the beginning of the game will be important as we have to adapt to certain things and manage the situation as quick as possible,” he said.

“Every time there is a new manager you want to impress. It happens everywhere. But we have been focusing more on us, how we can improve.”

While Iraola would love to know Pochettino’s plans for his return to the Premier League, the Liverpool boss was quick to criticise the YouTube channel that used a drone to film part of a United training session this week.

“They were sending me the footage yesterday, but I think the important thing about this is that it shouldn’t be legal. It happened to Manchester United but it could happen to me in a training session in the future,” he said.

“It isn’t about the advantage, it is the fact that there is a reason we train behind closed doors because there are some things that you have to respect.”

Former Bournemouth boss Iraola will get his first taste of one of England’s fiercest rivalries when United arrive at Anfield.

Liverpool, who have not beaten United at home since 2023, sit in fourth place in the top-flight while their old foes are in the relegation zone after their worst start for 40 years.

“You don’t find bigger games than this honestly. I feel lucky to be here and privileged to be part of these games,” Iraola said.

“We have to acknowledge the importance of the game, and also a lot of ingredients, such as their new manager.

“It is important for us in lots of different ways because we want to improve our form at home and the first chance we have to do that is on Sunday.”
