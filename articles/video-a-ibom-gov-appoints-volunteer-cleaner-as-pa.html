VIDEO: A’Ibom gov appoints volunteer cleaner as PA

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Akwa Ibom State Governor Umo Eno has appointed a long-serving volunteer at the Government House in Uyo, Edwin James, as his Personal Assistant, after voluntaril

The governor also directed the Secretary to the State Government to make James’ appointment effective from June 1, 2023, as recognition of his years of dedicated and selfless service, saying, “We have been owing him.”

Eno disclosed this while addressing worshippers during the monthly Government House prayer service held at the chapel on Sunday.

Eno also stated that his administration has paid approximately ₦102 billion of the inherited ₦111 billion gratuity backlog, while pensions and educational bursaries continue to receive attention.

In a video shared on the Akwa Ibom State Government’s Facebook page, James was seen approaching the governor before he received the news of his appointment.

While the governor was giving his speech, lauding James for his dedication and selfless service to the chapel and the state, James could be seen feeling emotional as his eyes became teary.

“This man, unfortunately, to this day, he has not been given an appointment. He has been doing this work voluntarily, cleaning this place. How come we have not remembered him? How come he has not been given an appointment? For almost four years?

“These are things to remind somebody about. It would have been bad if this government could finish, and this man has been serving God here, serving the state, cleaning and keeping, and we forget him; even God won’t be happy, but it’s not deliberate; honestly, these things have to be brought.

“So, please, SSG, on Monday morning, confirm his appointment as my PA, but backdate it to 1st of June 2023; that’s when he started working. So the state government has been owing him; let us pay.”

Eno prayed for the progress of the state, saying, “We will never go backwards. God will keep bringing leaders that will keep taking us forward.”

After the announcement, some members of the congregation congratulated James on his new appointment amid cheers and joyful screams from the crowd.

In a post on his LinkedIn account, the governor revealed that, beginning in June 2027, his administration would pay ₦200,000 each as bursaries to students studying general courses, while those pursuing professional courses such as Medicine, Law, Architecture, ICT and Pharmacy will receive ₦250,000 each.

He wrote, “Building a better Akwa Ibom State requires consistent investment in people, education and the welfare of those who have served our State. Today, at our monthly Government House Prayer Service in Uyo, I reflected on our administration’s progress, our commitment to the wellbeing of our people and our political priorities ahead of the 2027 general elections.

“Our administration has so far paid ₦102 billion out of the ₦111 billion inherited gratuities backlog, demonstrating our commitment to honouring the contributions of our retired public servants. In education, we have progressively increased student bursaries from ₦10,000 to ₦20,000 and subsequently to ₦50,000.

“Beginning in June 2027, students studying general courses will receive ₦200,000 each, while those pursuing professional courses such as Medicine, Law, Architecture, ICT and Pharmacy will receive ₦250,000 each.

“These investments reflect our conviction that no Akwa Ibom child should be denied access to education and the skills needed to succeed because of the circumstances of their birth.”

He also announced James’s appointment, saying, “I also announced the appointment of Mr Edwin James, who has faithfully served as a cleaner at the Latter House Chapel for several years, as my Personal Assistant, with effect from June 2023.

“His story reminds us that dedication, humility and faithful service deserve recognition, regardless of one’s position in society.

“As we continue implementing the ARISE Agenda, our focus remains on building an inclusive State where opportunities are accessible, service is valued, and every citizen has a chance to succeed.”

The governor encouraged residents to obtain their permanent voter cards and campaign in their wards ahead of the forthcoming 2027 general elections.
