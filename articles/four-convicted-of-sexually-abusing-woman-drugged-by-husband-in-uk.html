Four convicted of sexually abusing woman drugged by husband in UK

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Four men have been found guilty of sexually abusing a woman whose husband secretly drugged her and advertised her to strangers on the internet over a 13-year pe

As reported by the BBC on Thursday, following a trial at Winchester Crown Court, a jury of eight men and four women convicted Norman Macksoni, 48, Richard Wilkins, 62, Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, and Mohammed Hassan, 38, of multiple sexual offences against Joanne Young.

The verdicts follow earlier guilty pleas from her ex-husband, Philip Young, 50, a former Conservative Swindon borough councillor.

He previously admitted to 56 charges—including 11 counts of rape, 11 of administering a substance with intent, and 14 of voyeurism—after using websites and apps such as Craigslist, Grindr, and Fab Swingers to invite men to his home in Swindon, Wiltshire, to abuse his wife without her knowledge.

The Convictions
Norman Macksoni, 48: Guilty of one count of rape.

Richard Wilkins, 62: Guilty of one count of rape and one count of assault by penetration.

Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32: Guilty of one count of sexual assault by penetration and one count of sexual assault by touching.

Mohammed Hassan, 38: Guilty of one count of sexual assault by touching.

A sixth defendant, Dean Hamilton, 47, of Street in Somerset, admitted one charge of rape, one of assault by penetration, and two of sexual assault at an earlier hearing in February.

Judge Mr Justice Saini commended Joanne Young for showing “amazing bravery through these proceedings” and warned all defendants to expect lengthy jail terms when they are sentenced on 21 and 22 December.

Survivor Speaks Out
Joanne Young, who divorced Philip Young last year and waived her right to anonymity, wiped her eyes as she spoke on the steps outside the court.

“The person I trusted most in the world betrayed me in the most horrific way,” she said, adding that the abuse had “cost me my life as I knew it” and had a devastating impact on her and her children.

Addressing the actions of the co-defendants, she stated: “What happened to me was not the act of one man alone, others were invited to take part and they chose to. That choice makes them complicit in what was done to me and today’s verdicts recognise that.”

She urged the government to review the law of consent “as a priority” to ensure women are properly protected, while telling other survivors: “You can be believed and understood.”

Investigation and French Parallels
Specialist Crown Prosecution Service prosecutor James Foster said the case marked the first time a UK jury had been asked to consider organised, drug-facilitated sexual abuse orchestrated by a husband.

Police launched their investigation in 2023 after being alerted that Philip Young, whose most recent address was in Enfield, north London, had uploaded extreme pornographic images online.

Detectives found he had recorded 480 intimate images and videos of his wife across 200 separate occasions—mostly at their Swindon home—and posted images online at least 500 times.

During the inquiry, UK police sought expertise from French investigators who handled the case of Gisèle Pelicot, whose husband, Dominique Pelicot, was jailed for 20 years in France in 2024 for drugging her and recruiting dozens of men to abuse her.

Wiltshire Police confirmed they are still searching for another male suspect wanted in connection with alleged sexual offences against Joanne Young.

The force noted that organised drug-facilitated sexual assault may be significantly under-reported, as victims are often unaware they have been drugged or experience fragmented memories.
