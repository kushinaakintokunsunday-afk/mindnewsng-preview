Ex-FUTA VC donates books, cash to Lagos pupils

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

A former Vice-Chancellor of the Federal University of Technology, Akure, Peter Adeniyi, has donated educational materials and N50,000 each to 10 indigent pupils

The beneficiaries, selected from three public secondary schools in the Ojokoro area of the state, received the support on Wednesday during the Education Support Programme Presentation Day organised by the Professor Peter Olufemi Adeniyi Foundation.

The schools are Ijaiye Ojokoro Senior High School, Ijaiye Ojokoro Senior College and Ijaiye Housing Estate Senior College.

Other dignitaries at the event included the founder’s wife, Modupe Adeniyi; a Board of Trustees member, Ademola Ayodele; the Principal of Ijaiye Ojokoro Senior High School, Ogunleye; the Principal of Ijaiye Ojokoro Senior College, Oyefunke; and the Vice Principal of Ijaiye Ojokoro Senior High School, Otiniyi.

Speaking at the event, Adeniyi said the intervention was aimed at preventing poverty and other socioeconomic challenges from disrupting the education of pupils from financially disadvantaged backgrounds.

He noted that some pupils struggled to afford basic educational materials, transportation costs and other necessities required for regular school attendance.

According to him, such challenges could undermine academic performance, weaken pupils’ motivation and, in some cases, force promising pupils to abandon their education.

He said these concerns informed the foundation’s decision to establish the Education Support Programme to assist indigent pupils in public secondary schools.

The beneficiaries received textbooks, school supplies and cash to ease the financial burden on them and their families.

Adeniyi acknowledged that the number of beneficiaries was small compared with the scale of educational needs in society but expressed optimism that the intervention would make a meaningful difference.

“Every great journey begins with a step. We regard today’s intervention as one such step, and we hope that, with sustained support and stronger partnerships, the programme will grow to benefit many more deserving students,” he said.

He urged the beneficiaries to remain disciplined, hardworking and committed to their studies, stressing that education should go beyond passing examinations and obtaining certificates.

“Knowledge without character can become a danger rather than a blessing. Our society needs not only academically accomplished individuals, but also people of integrity, compassion, courage, humility and a genuine commitment to the common good,” he said.

The former vice-chancellor said the foundation’s broader mission included improving educational opportunities, strengthening learning environments, supporting teachers, developing school libraries and promoting community participation in education.

He added that the government alone could not meet every educational need, noting that families, communities, businesses, philanthropic organisations and individuals also had important roles to play.

Adeniyi said the foundation would continue to complement government efforts through targeted interventions, advocacy and partnerships aimed at achieving sustainable educational development.

He appreciated the Lagos State education authorities, school administrators, teachers, donors and partners for supporting the programme, particularly acknowledging Jana Heemskerk for her contributions to the foundation’s educational initiatives.

Also speaking, the foundation’s Programmes Manager, Temitope Odetoye, said the beneficiaries were selected from 29 nominated pupils through a rigorous process.

He explained that the final 10 were selected based on their Junior Secondary School qualifying examination results and verification of their parents’ or guardians’ financial status.

Odetoye said the foundation, which focuses on innovation, research advocacy and scholarships, designed the exercise as a pilot phase for Senior Secondary School 1 pupils, with plans to extend the sponsorship to the tertiary level.

Reacting on behalf of the state government, the Tutor-General/Permanent Secretary, Olufunke Oyetola, represented by the District Officer, Esther Oni, thanked the foundation for supporting the pupils.

She urged the beneficiaries to make effective use of the assistance for their academic growth and development.
