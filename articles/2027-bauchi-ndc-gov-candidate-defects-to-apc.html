2027: Bauchi NDC gov candidate defects to APC

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Bauchi State governorship candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress, Ibrahim Kashim, has defected to the All Progressives Congress ahead of the 2027 gene

Kashim, a former Secretary to the Bauchi State Government, announced his defection on Monday at a ceremony held at the APC State Secretariat in Bauchi.

His defection came despite the publication of his name on the list of governorship candidates released by the Independent National Electoral Commission.

Explaining his decision, the former SSG said he joined the APC because it offered Bauchi a stronger opportunity to participate in national governance and attract development.

He described the APC as the governing party at the federal level, with an overwhelming presence across Northern Nigeria, as well as the political structure and institutional capacity to advance the state’s interests.

Kashim said, “The APC is currently the governing party at the Federal level and has an overwhelming presence across Northern Nigeria. It is also the party with the political structure, national reach and institutional capacity to provide a viable platform for the political and developmental aspirations we seek for Bauchi.

“I believe that Bauchi must be strategically positioned within this political reality.

“We cannot afford to gamble with the future of our state by placing our developmental aspirations on a platform that lacks comparable national reach and institutional influence.

“Our decision must be guided by the interests of Bauchi, not sentiment, political nostalgia or the convenience of individuals.”

He argued that remaining outside the ruling party at the centre could limit the state’s ability to attract federal projects, influence national policies, facilitate investments and secure adequate representation in the corridors of power.

The former SSG said, “The question is not simply which party can put forward a candidate. The question is which party has the organisational strength, national reach, political influence and institutional capacity to advance Bauchi’s interests.

“I am convinced that the APC offers the stronger platform.”

Kashim said his decision to leave the NDC followed his resignation from the party on October 16, 2026, adding that his commitment to the political struggle for a better Bauchi remained unchanged.

He also appealed to his supporters, political associates and other stakeholders to join him in the APC, insisting that his political ambition was driven by the desire to improve governance and development in the state.

According to him, his priorities include creating employment opportunities for youths, empowering women, improving agricultural productivity, providing quality education and strengthening healthcare delivery.

He added, “Our ultimate objective is not the triumph of one political party over another. It is the triumph of good governance over poor governance, development over stagnation, opportunity over deprivation and hope over despair.

“I have chosen strategic engagement over political isolation. I have chosen a broader national platform over limited political reach. Above all, I have chosen what I sincerely believe offers Bauchi State a better opportunity for development and prosperity.”

The former governorship candidate further called on the people of the state to support his decision, saying Bauchi should not be left behind in accessing opportunities available through the federation.

PUNCH Online recalls that Kashim announced his resignation from the NDC on October 16, 2026, in a statement, saying the decision followed careful reflection.

He then stressed that his resignation would not affect his commitment to the political struggle for a better Bauchi State.
