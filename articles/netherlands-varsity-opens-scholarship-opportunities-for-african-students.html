Netherlands varsity opens scholarship opportunities for African students

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read

Wageningen University & Research (WUR) in the Netherlands has opened applications for its fully funded Africa Scholarship Programme, offering talented African students the opportunity to pursue two-year master’s degrees beginning in September 2027.

In a post obtained from the varsity’s website on Wednesday, the scholarship covers full tuition fees, monthly living allowances, return air tickets between Africa and the Netherlands, visa expenses and health insurance throughout the 24-month programme.

“The initiative is designed to support outstanding African graduates seeking postgraduate education in areas related to life sciences, agriculture, environmental studies and other disciplines offered by the university,” it stated.

According to the university’s scholarship guidelines, applicants must be citizens of an African country and hold a bachelor’s degree with First Class Honours or an equivalent qualification.

“Candidates are also required to have a minimum grade point average of 80 per cent and satisfy the university’s English language proficiency requirements.

“Applicants must have completed their undergraduate studies and secured unconditional admission to an eligible master’s programme at Wageningen University before the scholarship application deadline,” the varsity added.

The university also gives preference to candidates who have not previously obtained a master’s degree.

How to Apply

Interested candidates are required to apply directly for an eligible master’s programme through Wageningen University’s online application portal.

There is no separate scholarship application form. Instead, the university will identify eligible candidates from those who have submitted applications for admission and invite qualified applicants to participate in the scholarship selection process.

The deadline for submitting applications is December 1, 2026, for the September 2027 academic session.

Shortlisted candidates are expected to be contacted between March and April 2027, while final scholarship offers are scheduled to be announced on April 7, 2027.

The Africa Scholarship Programme provides an opportunity for outstanding African graduates to access international postgraduate education without bearing the major financial costs associated with studying abroad.

Wageningen University & Research is internationally recognised for its research and academic programmes in agriculture, food systems, environmental sciences and life sciences.

Through the scholarship initiative, the institution aims to attract talented African students and provide them with advanced academic training and research opportunities that could contribute to addressing development challenges across the continent.
