Real Madrid will never be sold – Pérez

By Punch Punch 3d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Real Madrid president Florentino Pérez has ruled out the possibility of the Spanish football club being sold, insisting that its members will remain its sole ow

Pérez said the club’s ownership structure would not be handed over to external interests, dismissing suggestions that Real Madrid could eventually come under the control of investors or other individuals.

He spoke on Saturday during the opening address at the club’s 2026 Ordinary General Assembly of Delegate Members, according to a statement published on Real Madrid’s official website.

The president said the issue of ownership had generated misinformation during the club’s recent electoral campaign, prompting him to reaffirm its membership-based structure.

He said, “Many things were said: that we wanted to sell the club, that the members would cease to be the owners, that Real Madrid would fall into other hands. All false.

“So I want to take advantage of this Assembly to say it once again, here and in front of all of you, so that there is no doubt: Real Madrid is not for sale. It has never been considered for sale and it never will be.”

Pérez stressed that the club belonged to its members and would remain under their ownership.

“The club belongs to and will always belong to its members, and even more so than it does today,” he added.

He also explained why proposals to extend membership to family members of existing members could undermine the current ownership arrangement.

According to Pérez, Real Madrid has approximately 100,000 members, but opening membership to their family members could increase the figure to between 300,000 and 400,000, potentially reducing the influence of existing members.

“Those who accused us during the campaign of wanting to sell the club even suggested opening the door of Real Madrid to allow all family members of current members to become members themselves.

“Do the math with me: today we are about 100,000 members, and with such a measure, we could very quickly find ourselves with 300,000 or 400,000 new members, which would reduce the current members to a minimal part of the membership base. That would indeed be giving Real Madrid away to others, without any control,” the president stated.

The club president described members as custodians of its history and financial assets, saying they had a responsibility to preserve its heritage for future generations.

Perez noted, “Members are not only the owners of Real Madrid; they are above all its guardians. They are the ones who have defended its heritage and history for 124 years, and it is upon them that the responsibility falls to pass it on intact, and even greater, to future generations.”

He added that the club was working on reforms aimed at reinforcing members’ ownership status and protecting its long-term interests.

“And that is precisely what we aim for with the reform we are working on: to reinforce our ownership status, solidify the club for the long term, and safeguard the heritage we have built together since 1902.

“Because Real Madrid’s leadership must be eternal, safeguarded, and collective, and it must never depend solely on the strength of a Board of Directors or a president,” he added.

Pérez said the club’s members had reaffirmed their confidence in his leadership during the last presidential election, describing their support as a responsibility to protect Real Madrid’s institutional independence.

He also insisted that the club’s direction would continue to be determined by its members.

“The destiny of Real Madrid will only be decided by its members. It has always been this way, and it always will be,” Perez assured.

The president said the club was also working on a new Madridista loyalty programme designed to establish a direct relationship with supporters worldwide.

The proposed programme would recognise fans’ interactions with the club, including watching matches, buying jerseys, visiting the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium, following content on Real Madrid Play and participating in supporters’ club activities.

Pérez, however, clarified that the initiative would not affect the ownership rights of existing members.

“I want to make something very clear because I know it’s important for the members: this program does not change what it means to be a member of Real Madrid. The members are the sole owners of the club.

“We hold a unique and exclusive status that cannot be compared to any other form of connection with Real Madrid, and we will continue as owners, always occupying a role that is unparalleled,” he concluded.

He said the programme would also create new revenue opportunities for the club by allowing it to engage directly with millions of supporters without intermediaries.
