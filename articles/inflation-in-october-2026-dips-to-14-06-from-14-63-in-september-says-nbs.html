Inflation in October 2026 dips to 14.06% from 14.63% in September, says NBS

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Isaac Anumihe, Abuja The Statistician General of the Federation (SG) and Chief Executive Officer of the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), Prince Adeyemi

From Isaac Anumihe, Abuja

The Statistician General of the Federation (SG) and Chief Executive Officer of the National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), Prince Adeyemi Adeniran, has said that inflation for October 2026 has dipped further to 14.06 per cent, compared with the August and September 2026 rates of 15.39 per cent and 14.63 per cent respectively.

In a statement, Adeniran maintained that the month-on-month headline inflation rate in October 2026 was 0.43 per cent, which was 0.38 per cent higher than the rate recorded in September 2026 (0.06 per cent).

Adeniran explained that at the divisional level, the three major contributors to the headline inflation were food and non-alcoholic beverages (5.63 per cent), restaurants and accommodation services (1.82 per cent) and transport (1.50 per cent).

Meanwhile, the least contributors were recreation, sport and culture (0.04 per cent); alcoholic beverages, tobacco and narcotics (0.05 per cent); and insurance and financial services (0.07 per cent).

The food inflation rate in October 2026, the SG said, was 19.13 per cent on a year-on-year basis, lower than the 16.30 per cent recorded in October 2025. Also, on a month-on-month basis, the food inflation rate in October 2026 was -0.75 per cent, up by 0.80 percentage points from September 2026 (-1.55 per cent).

“This can be attributed to the rate of change in the average prices of the following products: Vegetable oil (75cl), titus sardines (125g), green peas (prepacked), mackerel frozen, croakers (apo), rice local, catfish fresh, beef tripes, palm oil (75cl), millet flour (sold loose), among others.

“Core Inflation, which excludes the prices of volatile agricultural produce and energy, stood at 10.78 per cent in October 2026 on a year-on-year basis. On a month-on-month basis, the core inflation rate was -0.53 per cent in October 2026, down by 1.64 per cent compared to September 2026 (1.11 per cent),” the NBS said.

Regarding the newly introduced indices, the inflation rate of the sub-indices for October 2026 shows that the monthly changes recorded were farm produce (-0.40 per cent), energy (1.74 per cent), services (-0.94 per cent), goods (-0.01 per cent) and imported food (-0.38 per cent).

Meanwhile, the inflation rate on a year-on-year basis recorded farm produce (18.38 per cent), energy (12.30 per cent), services (11.73 per cent), goods (13.58 per cent) and imported food (8.39 per cent).

“On a year-on-year basis, the urban inflation rate in October 2026 was 14.84 per cent. On a month-on-month basis, the urban inflation rate was 0.43 per cent in October 2026, down by 0.10 per cent compared to September 2026 (0.53 per cent). The rural inflation rate in October 2026 was 12.17 per cent on a year-on-year basis. On a month-on-month basis, the rural inflation rate in October 2026 was 0.18 per cent, up by 1.07 per cent compared to September 2026,” the SG noted.
