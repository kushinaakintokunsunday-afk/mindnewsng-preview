Peter Obi Lacks Competence To Lead Nigeria, Tinubu 100% Better — Abure

By Channels TV Channels TV 4h ago 3 min read

Photo: Channels TV

Former National Chairman of the Labour Party, Julius Abure, has said the party’s former presidential candidate, Peter Obi, lacks the character and competence to lead Nigeria, insisting that President Bola Tinubu is “100 per cent better”.

Abure said he would not support Obi’s presidential ambition in the 2027 general election, citing his experience working with the former Anambra State governor and the internal crisis that fractured the Labour Party.

He made the remarks during an interview on Channels Television’s Politics Today, where he explained why he considered Tinubu a better choice among the leading contenders for the presidency.

Abure was a key ally of Obi during the 2023 presidential campaign and campaigned extensively for him. However, Obi subsequently left the Labour Party following a protracted leadership crisis.

He blamed Obi’s caretaker committee decision for deepening the Labour Party’s crisis, driving elected officials away and raising questions about his leadership.

“If indeed Obi was a good leader, why did they not follow Peter Obi? Peter Obi doesn’t have the character and competence to lead the country; we are sorry to say. Given my experience, I now know better,” he said.

Asked whether his decision to campaign extensively for Obi in 2023 had been a mistake, Abure admitted that it might have been.

“Yes, it may have been an error because I now know better. Experience, they say, is the best teacher. Now I have experienced working with Peter Obi and that he is unstable, unreliable, a pretender who cannot be trusted because of the way he treated the leadership of the party,” he said.

Asked whether he still considered Obi “a saint,” Abure declared his support for Tinubu, describing the president as a more suitable candidate.

“President Bola Tinubu is 100 per cent better. Today, we have the right to support whatever candidate we want. Obviously, Peter Obi is out of the question for us to support, given our experiences with him. We made a critical analysis of all the candidates: Tinubu, Atiku and Obi.

“We feel Tinubu is the best candidate for us to support, given his trajectory so far in office and his ability to resolve conflicts compared to Peter Obi. The stage we are in now requires someone who can bring people together because the country is more divided than ever before. We need a bridge-builder who can unite people, and I do not believe Peter Obi has that capacity at this critical time in our history,” he said.

He, however, dismissed allegations that he was responsible for Obi’s departure from the Labour Party, describing such claims as a “clear misconception”.

Obi has since joined the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) and will fly the party’s flag for the 2027 presidential election.

Other prominent figures expected to feature in the election include Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress (APC), Atiku of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) and Omoyele Sowore of the African Action Congress (AAC). In the 2023 presidential election, Tinubu won with 8,794,726 votes, while Atiku secured 6,984,520 votes. Obi finished third with 6,101,533 votes.
