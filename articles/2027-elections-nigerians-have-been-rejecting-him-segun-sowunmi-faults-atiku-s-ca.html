2027 Elections: ‘Nigerians Have Been Rejecting Him,’ Segun Sowunmi Faults Atiku’s Candidacy

By Channels TV Channels TV 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

A Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) chieftain, Segun Sowunmi, has criticised African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar&rsquo;s repeat

A Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) chieftain, Segun Sowunmi, has criticised African Democratic Congress (ADC) presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar’s repeated bids for Nigeria’s presidency, claiming that Nigerians have consistently rejected his ambition at the polls.

Sowunmi, who is also a member of the All Progressives Congress (APC) campaign council, made the remarks on Monday during an appearance on Channels Television’s Politics Today.

Responding to a question about Atiku’s belief that he is better positioned than President Bola Tinubu to govern Nigeria, Sowunmi said the former vice president should reconsider his ambition.

“Well, he’s been saying that for a while, and Nigerians have been rejecting him,” he said.

“If you have done elections the number of times Waziri (Atiku) has done, there is something he himself must consider. At what point in time does he expect younger, sharper northern people to get tired or be fatigued at running around with him?” he asked.

North Presidency Calculations

The PDP chieftain, who was a spokesperson for the Atiku Abubakar Presidential Campaign Organisation in 2023, also questioned the former vice president’s political calculations, noting that the North had already produced a president who governed for eight years under former President Muhammadu Buhari.

He further questioned Atiku’s candidacy under the African Democratic Congress (ADC), following a Federal High Court judgment nullifying the leadership of the caretaker committee led by former Senate President David Mark.

The November 5 judgment, delivered by Justice Peter Lifu in Abuja, followed a suit filed by Nafiu Bala Gombe, a former deputy national chairman of the ADC, challenging the emergence of the Mark-led leadership.

The ruling affected decisions taken by the committee, including the process that produced Atiku as the party’s 2027 presidential candidate.

The ADC group has rejected the judgment and appealed.

Sowunmi, however, maintained that Atiku’s candidacy remained uncertain.

“As at today, he is not on the ballot. Because you appeal a case you lost in the lower court, does that mean you are going to win it? The bottom line is that, as at today, he is not their candidate,” he said.

He also accused Atiku of mishandling the dispute involving Gombe, describing his approach as an “emir complex”.

“Emirs don’t beg, emirs don’t visit people, call others. Emirs sit at home, and everybody comes to meet them. Yes, you can be an emir, but you can’t be an emir if you are looking for a ticket in another man’s party,” he added.
