Nigerians Firmly Behind APC Despite Criticism And Insults — Yari

By Channels TV Channels TV 10h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The Director-General of the All Progressives Congress (APC) Presidential Campaign Council, Senator Abdul’azeez Yari Abubakar, has said the massive turnout at the party’s governorship campaign flag-off in Zamfara State demonstrates the people’s acceptance of the party despite what he described as campaigns of calumny and insults against it.

Yari said the APC was consolidating its support base to secure victory at all levels in the 2027 general elections, attributing the party’s growing acceptance to its closeness to the masses.

He stated this on Monday during the APC campaign flag-off held in Talata-Mafara, which attracted party faithful, support groups and members of the public.

Crowds of party supporters welcomed Governor Dauda Lawal’s motorcade to Talata-Mafara town before proceeding to the town’s Eid ground, venue of the rally.

Speaking at the event, Senator Yari said the large turnout reflected the enthusiasm and commitment of party members and supporters towards the party’s preparations for the 2027 elections.

“Make sure this message gets to the public: we are determined to deliver victory for the APC, from President Tinubu down to members of the State House of Assembly.

“The overwhelming response to our call demonstrates that, despite the insults and campaigns against the APC, the people of Zamfara have understood our message and are firmly behind the party,” he said.

Also speaking, Governor Dauda Lawal expressed gratitude to party supporters for their show of solidarity, while commending the people for their resilience and commitment to peaceful coexistence.

He reaffirmed his administration’s resolve to promote inclusive governance, equitable development and sustained engagement with communities across the state.

“This crowd speaks for itself; there is very little to say other than to thank Almighty God. Whenever I turn around and see this mammoth crowd of supporters, I am filled with gratitude.

“At this point, I will simply say, let us wait for election day and, ultimately, the swearing-in day,” Lawal said.

The APC campaign train is expected to move to other local government areas of Zamfara State as the party intensifies its mobilisation and grassroots engagement ahead of the 2027 general elections.
