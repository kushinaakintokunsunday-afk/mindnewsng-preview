2027 Grammys: 10 female artistes leading nominations

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

It’s women o’clock at the 2027 Grammy Awards, as female musicians take centre stage in this year’s nominations, dominating several major categories and emerging

It’s women o’clock at the 2027 Grammy Awards, as female musicians take centre stage in this year’s nominations, dominating several major categories and emerging as the biggest contenders for some of the music industry’s most prestigious honours.

From British singer-songwriter RAYE, who leads the nominations with an impressive 11 nods, to Spanish sensation Rosalía, American pop star Olivia Rodrigo and Nigerian singer Tems, women are making their presence felt across pop, R&B, country, dance, rock and other musical genres.

The nominations, announced on Monday, have placed several female artistes ahead of their male counterparts, with seven women featuring among the nine leading nominees.

RAYE’s 11 nominations also equal the record for the most Grammy nominations received by a female artiste in a single year, previously set by Beyoncé in 2025.

The Grammy Awards, organised by the Recording Academy, recognise outstanding achievements in the music industry, honouring artistes, songwriters, producers, composers and other music professionals.

The 69th edition of the ceremony is scheduled for February 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, United States.

Key Top Nominations (The “Big Four”)

• Record of the Year: Features tracks from Olivia Dean, Ella Langley, Bruno Mars, RAYE, Olivia Rodrigo, Rosalía with Björk and Yves Tumor, and Taylor Swift.

• Album of the Year: Nominees include projects by Phoebe Bridgers, Olivia Dean, Ella Langley, Bruno Mars, RAYE, Olivia Rodrigo, Rosalía, and Taylor Swift.

• Song of the Year: Recognized songwriters include teams behind tracks by Rosalía, Ella Langley, Olivia Rodrigo, Ariana Grande, Taylor Swift, and Olivia Dean.

Here are 10 female artistes making significant impressions in the 2027 Grammy nominations:

1. RAYE – 11 nominations

British singer-songwriter RAYE leads the 2027 Grammy nominations with 11 nods, becoming the year’s most-nominated artiste.

She earned recognition in some of the awards’ biggest categories, including Album of the Year for This Music May Contain Hope, and Record and Song of the Year for her hit single, Where Is My Husband!

Her other nominations include Best Pop Solo Performance, Best Pop Vocal Album, Best Dance Pop Recording, Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance and Songwriter of the Year, Non-Classical.

Her 11 nominations equal Beyoncé’s record for the most Grammy nominations received by a female artiste in a single year.

2. Rosalía – Nine nominations

Spanish singer-songwriter Rosalía follows RAYE with nine nominations, making her the second-most-nominated artiste at this year’s awards.

Her album, Lux (Complete Works), earned her a nomination for Album of the Year, while her song Berghain, featuring Björk and Yves Tumor, received recognition in both Record and Song of the Year categories.

She also earned nominations for Best Pop Solo Performance, Best Alternative Performance and Best Alternative Music Album, among others.

3. Ella Langley – Seven nominations

American country singer Ella Langley is among the biggest revelations of this year’s nominations, securing seven nods.

Her song Choosin’ Texas earned her nominations for Record of the Year, Song of the Year and Best Country Solo Performance, while her album Dandelion is competing for Album of the Year and Best Contemporary Country Album.

She also earned nominations for Best New Artist and Best Country Song.

Langley is the first country artiste since Bobbie Gentry in 1968 to receive nominations in all four general field categories.

4. Miranda Lambert – Seven nominations

Country music star Miranda Lambert also secured seven nominations, with her contributions as a performer, songwriter and producer earning her recognition across multiple categories.

She received nominations for Producer of the Year, Non-Classical, and Best Country Song for Choosin’ Texas and Hardwired.

Her other songwriting and collaborative contributions also feature in this year’s country nominations.

5. Olivia Rodrigo – Seven nominations

American singer-songwriter Olivia Rodrigo earned seven nominations, continuing her impressive run at the Grammys.

Her song The Cure earned nominations for Record of the Year and Song of the Year, while her album, You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love, is competing for Album of the Year.

She also received nominations for Best Pop Solo Performance, Best Pop Vocal Album, Best Rock Performance and Best Rock Song.

6. Phoebe Bridgers – Seven nominations

American singer-songwriter Phoebe Bridgers received seven nominations, including recognition in some of the awards’ major categories.

Her album, Lost Weekend, is nominated for Album of the Year and Best Alternative Music Album.

She also earned nominations for Best Rock Performance, Best Rock Song and Best Alternative Performance.

7. Olivia Dean – Six nominations

British singer Olivia Dean earned six nominations, placing her among the leading contenders at the 2027 awards.

Her song Man I Need received nominations for Record of the Year and Song of the Year, while her album, The Art of Loving, is competing for Album of the Year and Best Pop Vocal Album.

She also received nominations for Best Pop Solo Performance and Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance.

Earlier in November, Dean was named Apple Music artiste of the year.

8. Taylor Swift – Five nominations

American pop superstar Taylor Swift earned five nominations, including recognition in the three major general field categories.

Her album, The Life of a Showgirl, is nominated for Album of the Year, while The Fate of Ophelia received a Record of the Year nomination.

Her songwriting on I Knew It, I Knew You earned her a Song of the Year nomination, while The Life of a Showgirl, featuring Sabrina Carpenter, is nominated for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance.

She also received recognition in the Best Remixed Recording category.

9. Kehlani – Five nominations

American R&B singer Kehlani secured five nominations, featuring prominently in the R&B categories.

Her self-titled album, Kehlani, received a nomination for Best R&B Album, while her song Out the Window is nominated for Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song.

She also received nominations for Best R&B Collaboration or Duo/Group Performance for Sweet Nuthins, featuring Leon Thomas, and Best Melodic Rap Performance for Safe, featuring Cardi B.

10. Lainey Wilson – Five nominations

American country singer Lainey Wilson completes the list with five nominations.

She earned recognition for Best Country Solo Performance for Can’t Sit Still and Best Country Duo/Group Performance for Phone, Keys, Wallet, alongside John Mayer.

She also features in the nominations for Best Country Song, Best Country Duo/Group Performance for Light of a Clear Blue Morning alongside Dolly Parton, Miley Cyrus, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire, and Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song for The Jesus I Know Now, alongside Brandon Lake.

OTHER NOTABLE FEMALE NOMINEES

Beyond the 10 leading artistes, several other women have also made significant impressions in the nominations.

Nigerian singer Tems earned four nominations, including Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song for What You Need, Best African Music Performance for Big Daddy and Best Global Music Album for Love Is a Kingdom.

American singer Jill Scott received five nominations, including recognition in the R&B categories, while Ariana Grande earned four nominations.

Other notable female nominees include Madonna, Cardi B, Brandi Carlile, Kacey Musgraves, Tinashe, Zara Larsson, Doechii, Beyoncé, Lisa, Angélique Kidjo and CeCe Winans.

Beyoncé, despite receiving only one nomination this year for Best R&B Solo Performance with Morning Dew (Donk), reached a major career milestone, bringing her total Grammy nominations to 100.
