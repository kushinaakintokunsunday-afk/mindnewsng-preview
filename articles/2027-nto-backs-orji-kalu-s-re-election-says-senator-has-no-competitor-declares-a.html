2027: Nto backs Orji Kalu’s re-election, says senator has no competitor, declares Abia APC united

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 3d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

A former All Progressives Congress (APC) senatorial aspirant for Abia North, Prof Philips Nto, has thrown his weight behind the re-election bid of Senator Orji

A former All Progressives Congress (APC) senatorial aspirant for Abia North, Prof Philips Nto, has thrown his weight behind the re-election bid of Senator Orji Uzor Kalu, declaring that the former Abia State governor has no serious competitor in the race for the 2027 senatorial election.

Nto, a former Commissioner for Finance and Economic Planning in Abia State, also declared that “the APC in the state remained united”, pledging to mobilise his political structures, supporters and resources towards Kalu’s re-election.

He made the declaration on Saturday when he hosted Kalu and members of his entourage at his residence in Ututu, Arochukwu Local Government Area, following the senator’s ward-to-ward tour of the clan.

The former senatorial aspirant, who accompanied Kalu throughout the tour, said his decision to support the senator was informed by Kalu’s performance and pedigree, and his commitment to party unity and the collective interests of Abia North.

He expressed confidence in Kalu’s political experience and grassroots connections, insisting that the senator remained the candidate to beat in Abia North.

The former commissioner further pledged to deploy his political network and resources towards mobilising support for Kalu across Ututu and other communities in Arochukwu Local Government Area.

Earlier, Kalu, who represents Abia North Senatorial District and chairs the Senate Committee on the South East Development Commission (SEDC), had toured Ututu Wards A, B, C, D and E, where he interacted with constituents on infrastructure, education, community development and other pressing needs.

The senator commenced his engagements with a courtesy visit to His Royal Majesty, Eze Solomon Nwaizu Okereke, JP, Ezeoma I of Ezema Ututu, who received him alongside other community leaders.

Welcoming the former governor, the traditional ruler recalled Kalu’s longstanding relationship with the people of Ututu, describing him as a “familiar figure whose association with the community extended beyond his years in the Senate”.

The monarch particularly recalled how Kalu appointed sons of Ututu to positions of responsibility during his administration as governor of Abia State between 1999 and 2007.

He commended the senator for maintaining his relationship with the community over the years and expressed support for his continued representation.

The traditional ruler, however, appealed to Kalu to facilitate additional developmental projects, particularly in areas requiring government intervention, to improve the living conditions of residents.

Also speaking during the tour, Princess Evelyn Nwankwo applauded Kalu’s representation of Abia North, describing him as a longstanding friend of the Ututu people.

She noted that the senator had maintained close ties with the community over the years and expressed appreciation for his developmental interventions.

Nwankwo pledged the community’s continued goodwill and support, expressing hope that the relationship would translate into further improvements in infrastructure and other essential services.

Similarly, the APC candidate for Arochukwu State Constituency, Prince Ike Okorafor, commended Kalu for attracting developmental projects to Arochukwu Local Government Area.

Okorafor said the senator had demonstrated his commitment to the area through interventions in road construction, educational infrastructure and other constituency projects.

He described Kalu as one of their own, noting that his longstanding relationship with the Aro and Ututu people had strengthened his connection with the communities.

In his remarks, the APC House of Representatives candidate for Arochukwu/Ohafia Federal Constituency, Chief Ikenna Nicholas Ukwa, pledged to collaborate with Kalu, if elected, to attract additional federal projects and development opportunities to the constituency.

Ukwa stressed that cooperation between elected representatives at the National Assembly would help address infrastructure deficits and other challenges confronting rural communities.

A community leader, Ohachi Victor, also expressed support for Kalu’s re-election, declaring that Ututu stood firmly behind the senator.

Addressing constituents during the tour, Kalu thanked the traditional ruler, community leaders, women, youths and other stakeholders for their reception and continued goodwill.

The former governor recalled his longstanding relationship with the people of Ututu, particularly the opportunities he provided for indigenes of the community to serve in his administration.

He also highlighted his constituency interventions across Arochukwu Local Government Area, including rural road construction and rehabilitation, classroom projects, solar-powered streetlights, educational materials and overseas scholarship opportunities.

Kalu explained that his ward-to-ward engagements were designed to provide an opportunity for direct interaction with constituents, assess their priorities and identify areas requiring further legislative and developmental attention.

He assured the people that their concerns would continue to receive attention, stressing that effective representation required regular consultation and sustained engagement with communities.

The tour concluded with a reception hosted by Nto at his residence, where Kalu and members of his entourage interacted with party stakeholders and community leaders.

Kalu expressed appreciation to his host for the hospitality and goodwill, while reiterating his commitment to strengthening his relationship with the people of Ututu and the wider Arochukwu Local Government Area.
