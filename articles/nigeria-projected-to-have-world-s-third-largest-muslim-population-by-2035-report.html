Nigeria projected to have world’s third-largest Muslim population by 2035 — Report

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigeria is projected to have the world’s third-largest Muslim population by 2035, behind Pakistan and India. This is according to a new report by the Pew Resear

This is according to a new report by the Pew Research Centre, which was posted on Monday and went viral on Friday.

The report, titled Future of World Religions, examines anticipated changes in the size and distribution of religious populations across 201 countries and territories.

The report read, “Pakistan is expected to record the highest number of Muslims by 2035, followed by India, Nigeria and Indonesia, which currently has the largest Muslim population.”

The projection comes amid expectations that Islam will continue to grow faster than Christianity, potentially becoming the world’s largest religion.

It said, “The global Muslim population is projected to increase by 80 per cent, exceeding 3.6 billion, while the number of Christians is expected to grow by 30 per cent to just below three billion.”

The report attributed much of the anticipated growth to differences in fertility rates among religious groups.

Between 2020 and 2025, Muslim women had an average of 3.1 children, compared with 2.5 among Christian women and 1.6 among women from other religious groups.

It explained that a population generally needs an average fertility rate of 2.1 children per woman to maintain its size across generations.
Pew said its projections cover 201 countries and territories, accounting for 99.98 per cent of the world’s population.

However, it cautioned that the estimates are based on the assumption that existing patterns of fertility, mortality and religious conversion will continue.

The Centre noted that unforeseen developments, including armed conflicts, natural disasters and economic changes, could affect the projected outcomes.

Looking further ahead, the report projected that Africa would account for four of the 10 countries with the largest Muslim populations by 2070.

Nigeria, Egypt, Ethiopia and Sudan were listed among the African countries expected to feature prominently in the ranking.
The report also forecast major changes in the global distribution of Christianity.

It projected that the United States would lose its Christian-majority status, although Christians would remain the country’s largest religious group.

The Democratic Republic of the Congo is also expected to overtake the United States as the country with the largest Christian population in the world.
