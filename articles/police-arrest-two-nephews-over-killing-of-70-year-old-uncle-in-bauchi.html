Police arrest two nephews over killing of 70-year-old uncle in Bauchi

By Punch Punch 2d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Bauchi State Police Command has arrested two men for allegedly killing their 70-year-old paternal uncle, Sa’idu Malam, over allegations of witchcraft in Nin

The suspects, identified as Dahiru Yakubu, aka Dangi, 30, and Musa Alhaji Yakubu, 22, were arrested on November 21, 2026, following an intelligence-led investigation into the murder.

The command’s Police Public Relations Officer, SP Nafiu Habib, disclosed this in a statement issued on Sunday.

According to Habib, the command received a distress call from a traditional ruler in Kyata village, Burra, on November 19, reporting that the victim’s lifeless body had been discovered beside a river.

He said the deceased, a resident of Jejin-Giwa, Kyata Ward, Burra, was found with marks of violence on his neck, hands and other parts of his body.

“On receipt of the report, police operatives attached to Burra Division swiftly mobilised to the scene. No exhibit was recovered at the scene at that time,” the statement read.

Habib said the victim’s remains were taken to the General Hospital, Burra, where a medical practitioner confirmed him dead before they were released to his relatives for burial in accordance with Islamic rites.

He said preliminary investigations revealed that the suspects were the deceased’s paternal nephews, being the children of his younger brother.

“During interrogation, the suspects confessed to the crime and claimed they attacked the deceased over an allegation of witchcraft. They alleged that the deceased had been bewitching their wives, and that whenever their wives conceived, the pregnancies were terminated as a result of the alleged bewitchment,” Habib stated.

The police spokesman added that a search conducted at the suspects’ shelter and premises led to the recovery of a blood-stained cutlass suspected to have been used in the crime.

He said the case remained under preliminary investigation and would soon be transferred to the State Criminal Investigation Department, Bauchi, for discreet investigation and further necessary action.

Meanwhile, the Commissioner of Police, Sani-Omolori Aliyu, commiserated with the deceased’s family and the Jejin-Giwa community over the incident, describing the killing as cruel and inhuman.

“The Commissioner of Police has assured that the Command will leave no stone unturned to ensure that justice is served,” the statement added.

The CP also warned members of the public against taking the law into their own hands over allegations or superstitious beliefs, urging them to report grievances to the police or other constituted authorities for proper investigation.

He stressed that “no allegation justifies murder” and reaffirmed the command’s commitment to protecting lives and property.
