PHOTOS: NAF nursing college matriculates 115, graduates 98

By Punch Punch 1d ago 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Nigerian Air Force College of Nursing Sciences, Kaduna, has matriculated 115 students and graduated 98 others at a ceremony held at the Air Force Institute

According to the photo news shared on its official Facebook account on Monday, the ceremony, held on November 20, 2026, took place at the Ibrahim Alfa Auditorium of the institute.

The Chief of Training, Nigerian Air Force Headquarters, AVM Eneobong Effiom, who represented the Chief of the Air Staff, Air Marshal Sunday Kelvin Aneke, “urged the graduating students to uphold professionalism, compassion and nursing ethics.”

He also encouraged the “newly matriculated students to embrace discipline and continuous learning.”

The Chief of the Air Staff “reaffirmed the Nigerian Air Force’s commitment to quality healthcare, personnel welfare, operational readiness and human capacity development.”

The Acting Commandant of the college, Wg Cdr Victoria Dung, appreciated stakeholders for their support for nursing education.

Highlights of the ceremony included the presentation of awards to outstanding graduates and the unveiling of the second edition of the NAFCONS magazine and the college crest.
