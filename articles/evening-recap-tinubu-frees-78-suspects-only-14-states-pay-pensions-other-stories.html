The recap in full:

1. Argentine footballer Abel Núñez dies after head injury during match

An Argentine footballer, Abel Núñez, has died days after suffering a head injury during a match while representing Ezeiza Football Club.

The club announced his death in a statement released on Sunday, expressing sadness over the loss of the young player, who had undergone surgery after the incident.

“After suffering a hard blow during a match, proudly wearing our Ezeiza shirt, we deeply regret his departure,” the club said.

Read more details here.

2. Only 14 states pay pensions regularly – PenCom

The National Pension Commission has disclosed that only 14 of Nigeria’s 36 states are paying pensions regularly, acknowledging that some state governments are performing poorly in meeting their obligations to retirees.

The Director-General of PenCom, Omolola Oloworaran, made this known during an interview with ARISE NEWS on Monday, describing pension payments as a continuing challenge in several states.

“We have 14 states that are paying up. But 14 out of 36 states is a lot. And some states are doing very badly,” she said.

Read more details here.

3. Tinubu grants clemency to 78 suspects arrested over convoy attack

The Federal Capital Territory Police Command has said 78 suspects arrested over the attack on President Bola Tinubu’s motorcade in Abuja are to be released following the President’s extension of clemency to them.

In a statement released on Monday by the command spokesperson, Josephine Adeh, the suspects were arrested after the attack on the President’s convoy on Friday, November 13, at a mosque in Garki District.

The incident happened shortly after Tinubu observed Jumu’ah prayer

Read more details here.

4. Nigeria cannot afford Atiku’s tertiary education privatisation plan — Oshiomhole

Former Edo State Governor, Adams Oshiomhole, has criticised former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar’s advocacy for the privatisation of tertiary education, saying Nigeria cannot afford such a policy when millions of children are still out of school.

Oshiomhole spoke on Monday in Abuja at the unveiling of the Renewed Hope Education A+Z Scorecard by the Minister of Education, Dr Tunji Alausa.

He recalled that Atiku pursued a privatisation agenda while serving as Vice-President, questioning the rationale behind his advocacy for private-sector control of tertiary education in a country where access to basic education remains a major challenge.

5. VIDEO: Explosion sparks fire at Lagos NNPC filling station

A gas explosion at an NNPC filling station near Savannah Bus Stop, Command, Ipaja, Lagos State, on Monday sparked a fire, raising concerns among residents.

The fire reportedly spread to nearby buildings, while emergency responders were said to have arrived at the scene.
