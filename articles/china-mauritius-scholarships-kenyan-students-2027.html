30 Fully Funded Scholarships Just Opened for Kenyan Students - But One Has a Deadline That Runs Out in Days

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 10 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

China and Mauritius have together offered Kenya 30 fully funded scholarships for the 2027 academic year - covering tuition, living costs and airfare for degrees

Kenya's Ministry of Education confirmed it this week: 30 fully funded scholarships have opened for Kenyan students for the 2027 academic year - 15 funded by the Government of China and 15 more by the Government of Mauritius.

The catch for the biggest prize? One of them wants your documents by November 18, 2026, at 5pm - less than a week away.

Scholarship 1: Chinese Government Scholarships (15 slots)

China is offering 15 government scholarships for undergraduate, master's and PhD studies at Chinese universities in the 2027/2028 academic year, awarded through competitive selection. Kenyan applicants must apply through the Ministry of Education, which acts as the forwarding agent; the Chinese Embassy in Kenya confirmed students may file applications through the Ministry.

  • Deadline: applications must reach the Ministry of Education by Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 5pm.
  • The screening trap: all applicants must first pass the Ministry's own preliminary selection - and shortlisted candidates face an online assessment around January 19, 2027 with compulsory Chinese Language and Mathematics sections plus selective Physics and Chemistry tests.
  • Details of the application procedure are published on education.go.ke.

Scholarship 2: Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme 2027 (15 slots)

Mauritius is offering another 15 fully funded slots - tuition fees, a living-expenses contribution and return airfare to and from Kenya included - for undergraduate (max 4 years), MPhil, MPhil/PhD and PhD studies at public higher education institutions in Mauritius.

But this one is a two-stage application, and missing stage one disqualifies you:

  1. First, apply directly for a full-time on-campus programme at a public HEI in Mauritius - by the end of January 2027. (There is an application fee - but it is refunded to successful scholarship applicants.)
  2. Then send two copies of the Ministry of Education's standard application form (from education.go.ke), the completed Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Application Form 2027, plus the conditional offer letter and supporting documents to the Director of Higher Education in Nairobi.

Full guidelines sit on the official portal: tertiaryeducation.govmu.org (Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme page).

Nigerians, take note

Nigeria is not among this batch's recipients - but the Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme is designed for citizens across the African Union, running similar offers via participating governments, and China's CSC scholarship programme operates the same way through national dispatching organs. Nigerian students chasing a 2027 full ride should track their own Ministry's notices and the CSC list - these slots move fast.

This article is general information only; application requirements and deadlines are controlled by the respective governments. Always verify on education.go.ke and tertiaryeducation.govmu.org before applying.

Sources: Kenya Ministry of Education notice; Chinese Embassy in Kenya; Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme 2027 official call; Capital FM, Nairobi.
