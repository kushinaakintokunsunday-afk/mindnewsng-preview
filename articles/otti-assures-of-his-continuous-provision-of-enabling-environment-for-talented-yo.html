Otti assures of his continuous provision of enabling environment for talented youths to thrive

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Governor Alex Otti has assured that his administration would continue to create an enabling environment for talented individuals to develop their abilities and contribute meaningfully to society.

Governor Otti gave the assurance while speaking during the 13th edition of the Vision Africa Icons Music Talent Show held in Umuahia, Abia State.

The Governor, represented by his Chief of Staff (CoS), Caleb Ajagba, observed that the event demonstrated what could be achieved when the right leadership and supportive institutions provided opportunities for people to realise their potential.

According to him, Abia possesses enormous human resources which, if properly identified, encouraged and supported, could produce individuals capable of achieving recognition beyond the state.

“Abia is blessed, and we shall continue to identify, encourage and support the talents that God has deposited in our people,” the governor stated.

Otti also expressed his administration’s readiness to partner with Vision Africa in advancing talent discovery and development, commending the organisation’s founder, Archbishop Dr Sunday Ndukwo Onuoha, whom he described as his personal friend, for sustaining the initiative.

The governor described the performances as evidence of the diverse gifts with which God had endowed the people of Abia, noting that the different voices, musical expressions and artistic styles displayed by the contestants reflected the richness of the state’s human resources.

“What you are seeing today shows that we are many parts. Looking at all the contestants, you will know that God has blessed them with different voices. They have all demonstrated their uniqueness,” he said.

Otti commended the contestants for demonstrating the depth of musical talent and creative abilities within the state.

The governor further assured that the eventual winner would receive the necessary support and exposure to pursue opportunities on the international music stage.

The founder and President of Vision Africa, Archbishop Dr Sunday Ndukwo Onuoha, called for greater tolerance, justice, equity and peaceful coexistence among Nigerians, irrespective of their ethnic, religious, linguistic and cultural differences.

He noted that Nigeria’s diversity made it unrealistic to expect citizens to speak the same language, exhibit identical cultural practices or approach life from the same background.

He however maintained that these differences should not constitute barriers to national unity, insisting that Nigerians could preserve their individual identities while working together towards a common national destiny.

Onuoha emphasised the importance of allowing individuals and communities to express themselves freely within their respective capacities, arguing that mutual respect and acceptance were essential ingredients for sustainable peace.

The Chairman of the occasion, Dr Noah Nuhu Dallaji, represented by Chief Theo Ubani, commended Onuoha for his contributions to human development and humanitarian causes, describing the Vision Africa initiative as an important platform for identifying and nurturing emerging talents.

He expressed appreciation for the organisation’s consistency and progressive development, noting that the competition was helping young Nigerians discover their abilities and prepare for opportunities that could contribute to the country’s future growth.

Dallaji also applauded Vision Africa for providing a platform through which Nigerian talents could gain wider exposure and potentially take their creative abilities to international audiences.

In her remarks, Nollywood actress and film producer, Sophia Williams, described the competition as a distinctive initiative deserving of commendation, particularly for its commitment to discovering and developing emerging musical talents.

She praised Onuoha for his confidence in African creativity and his determination to create opportunities for gifted young people to showcase their abilities.

Mr Antia Elvis Ifeanyichukwu, contestant number eight, has emerged winner of the 13th edition of the Vision Africa Icons Music Talent Show.
