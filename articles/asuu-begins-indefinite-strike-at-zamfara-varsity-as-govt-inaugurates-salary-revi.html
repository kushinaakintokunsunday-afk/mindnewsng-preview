ASUU Begins Indefinite Strike At Zamfara Varsity As Govt Inaugurates Salary Review Committee

The Academic Staff Union of Universities (ASUU), Zamfara State University branch, has commenced an indefinite strike over the alleged failure of the state government to meet its demands on salary agreements and outstanding staff entitlements.

The industrial action came as the Zamfara State Government inaugurated a committee to review salary structures and address welfare concerns in state-owned tertiary institutions.

ASUU announced the strike after its congress held on Tuesday at the university’s temporary campus in Talata Mafara.

Speaking after the meeting, the Acting Chairman of the branch, Anas Dogo, accused the state government of failing to address issues affecting lecturers despite several appeals and engagements through established channels.

He said the decision followed the expiration of an ultimatum and a subsequent warning strike declared by the union, which he said did not receive any positive response from the government.

Dogo explained that the union could no longer guarantee the provision of academic services under the prevailing conditions.

He listed the union’s demands to include the implementation of the December 2025 Federal Government-ASUU salary agreement, payment of promotion arrears and other outstanding financial obligations owed to lecturers.

The ASUU leader said the union had held several meetings with the university’s governing council, management and other relevant stakeholders before deciding to embark on the indefinite strike.

He added that attempts to secure meetings with the Commissioner for Education, the Head of Service and the Secretary to the State Government were unsuccessful.

Meanwhile, the Zamfara State Government has inaugurated a committee to review the non-implementation of revised salary structures and other staff welfare issues in the state’s tertiary institutions.

The Secretary to the State Government, Abubakar Nakwada, who chairs the committee, inaugurated it and presided over its maiden meeting on Thursday in Gusau.

According to a statement issued by the Senior Special Assistant on Media to the SSG, Suleman Tudu, the committee is expected to examine relevant government circulars, determine the financial implications of outstanding obligations and consult stakeholders towards finding an amicable resolution.

Speaking at the inauguration, Nakwada reaffirmed Governor Dauda Lawal’s commitment to workers’ welfare, industrial harmony and the repositioning of tertiary education in the state.

He acknowledged that demands presented by the Forum of Zamfara State Tertiary Institutions (FOZASTI) and Zamfara State University over salaries, allowances and promotion benefits remained a concern to the government.

The SSG said the committee would approach the assignment with objectivity and sincerity, taking into consideration the interests of workers and the financial realities of the state.

He cited the increase in the state minimum wage from ₦7,000 to ₦70,000, clearance of gratuity arrears, and implementation of a new salary scale for healthcare workers as evidence of the administration’s commitment to workers’ welfare.

Nakwada appealed to aggrieved unions to give dialogue a chance and allow the committee to complete its assignment, warning that industrial action at this critical moment would hurt rather than help the education system.

Members of the committee include the commissioners for finance, education, science and technology, local government and chieftaincy affairs, and budget and planning.

Others are the special adviser on economic and financial matters, representatives of FOZASTI, the Nigeria Labour Congress in Zamfara State and Zamfara State University, while the permanent secretary of the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology will serve as secretary.

The government’s intervention is expected to provide a platform for addressing the outstanding welfare issues, although ASUU has already commenced its indefinite strike over the unresolved demands.
