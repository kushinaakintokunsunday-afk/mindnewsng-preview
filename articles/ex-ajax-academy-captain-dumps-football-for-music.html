Ex-Ajax academy captain dumps football for music

By Punch Punch 18 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Former Ajax academy captain Elikia Mbinga has traded his football dreams for a career in music, emerging as one of the Netherlands’ rising Afrobeat artists with

The 25-year-old once dreamed of becoming a professional footballer and playing in front of packed stadiums after sharing the pitch with several players who have gone on to establish themselves in the Premier League.

During his four-year spell at Ajax’s renowned De Toekomst academy, Mbinga played alongside the likes of Jurrien and Quinten Timber, Ryan Gravenberch and Brian Brobbey.

The midfielder, who captained his academy side, remembers his former teammates as exceptionally talented players but admits their subsequent success was not something he could have predicted.

“We had a feeling that we had joined this journey together. We stepped in together. Already then, you could see that Quinten and Jurrien were like machines. They were built like machines from a young age,” he told BBC Sport on Wednesday.

Mbinga also recalled Gravenberch’s technical ability and Brobbey’s physical strength, qualities that helped them progress to professional football.

However, while his former teammates continued their footballing journeys, Mbinga struggled to secure a breakthrough at Ajax and returned to his boyhood club, PEC Zwolle, in 2021.

He made his professional debut against Ajax in September 2021 but managed just five further first-team appearances before Zwolle’s relegation.

He subsequently dropped down the Dutch football pyramid, playing semi-professionally in the fourth tier while developing his interest in music.

Although football remained a significant part of his life, his desire to create music and establish something of his own gradually became stronger.

“I think it was also part of the personal growth. I really liked the music. I also liked looking up to the other artists at the time who were popping,” he said.

“For me, it was also a dream to do something in music, create my own songs. But at that time, I was really talented with football, but I couldn’t make music yet.”

The decision to pursue music has since paid off, with Mbinga building an impressive following in the Dutch Afrobeat scene.

He now boasts almost 500,000 monthly Spotify listeners, while his 2024 single, Mentality, has surpassed 16 million streams.

Interestingly, the former midfielder says the creativity he developed on the football pitch continues to influence his approach to music.

“As a footballer, I played in midfield and was a very creative player. That was my style on the pitch, and I try to bring that same creativity to my music,” he explained.

“I want to experiment with songs, be as creative as possible and develop a sound of my own.”

For Mbinga, musical success provides the same excitement he once experienced while playing football.

He compares the success of a song to scoring a goal or providing an assist, while a major hit feels like scoring the winning goal in an important final.

“And of course, if one of my songs really takes off, then it feels like I’m scoring the winning goal in a very important game – like a final!” he said.

Although his journey has taken a different direction from that of his former Ajax teammates, Mbinga has found another platform to showcase the creativity that once made him stand out on the football pitch.
