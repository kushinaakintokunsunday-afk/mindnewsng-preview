NDC condemns 200-man vigilante in Ebonyi LG, urges investigation

From Uchenna Inya, Abakaliki

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in Ebonyi State has condemned the reported establishment and inauguration of a 200-member local security codenamed Neighbourhood Watch outfit by the Chairman, Ohaozara Local Government Area, Osborn Nweze Umahi.

Umahi inaugurated the 200-man vigilante at the weekend in Obiozara, headquarters of the local government.

NDC wondered why the action occurred at the critical period when the state was  approaching another electoral season.

In a statement signed by the state Chairman of the party, Alum Enyinnaya Udensi and the state Secretary, Okereke Nnanna, the party said that while it  recognises the legitimate responsibility of government to protect life and property, the party is deeply concerned about the timing, circumstances, composition, and possible implications of establishing such a large security outfit barely months before a general election.

“We find it particularly disturbing that a party that has had the opportunity to serve the people for the past 11 years, yet has failed to substantially improve the living conditions, economic fortunes and general welfare of the citizens, is suddenly demonstrating such urgency in establishing a 200-man security structure at a politically sensitive period.

“The question that begs for an answer is: What is the real intention behind this sudden mobilisation of 200 security personnel, and whose interests are they expected to protect?”, the party queried.

The party stated that if the initiative was genuinely intended to strengthen community security, the government must demonstrate transparency by making public the legal framework guiding its operations, the nature of equipment to be deployed, its chain of command, training arrangements, and the mechanisms for ensuring accountability.

It  noted that the party was  particularly concerned by any possibility that an ostensibly community-based security arrangement could be transformed into an instrument of political intimidation, harassment, voter suppression, or electoral violence.

“Let it be clearly understood that the security of our communities must never be converted into a political project or a weapon for intimidating political opponents and innocent citizens.

“Our warning is unequivocal: Whoever stokes violence, promotes intimidation or attempts to manipulate the electoral process through coercion will ultimately be consumed by the consequences of such actions.

“No individual, political office holder, or group should mistake the peaceful disposition of the people for weakness. We wish to remind those in positions of authority that the mandate given to them is an opportunity to improve the lives of ordinary Ebonyi people, provide employment, strengthen infrastructure, improve healthcare, support education, and create an environment where citizens can live with dignity and security.

“Unfortunately, the people are still confronted with enormous developmental challenges”.

The party maintained that it is unacceptable that the urgency now being demonstrated in establishing a massive security structure is not matched by the same level of commitment to addressing unemployment, poverty, insecurity, and the numerous hardships confronting our people.

“The NDC further wishes to remind the authorities of an important African proverb: “Whosoever harvests an ant-infested firewood has invited the lizards to a feast.”

“Those who create conditions for political tension, intimidation, and insecurity should not be surprised when the consequences become difficult to contain. Those who sow violence must understand that its consequences may extend far beyond their immediate expectations.

The party called on the Nigeria Police Force and other relevant security institutions to urgently investigate the development.

“Specifically, we demand that the security agencies establish the legality of the newly inaugurated 200-member outfit and ascertain the legal authority for possessing and deploying such arms.

“Investigate the recruitment process, training, operational guidelines, and actual mandate of the outfit. Ensure that no member of the outfit is deployed to intimidate political opponents, interfere with political activities or influence the choices of voters.

The party also urged the security agencies to put adequate safeguards in place to prevent the misuse of public resources and security structures during the forthcoming elections.

“We emphasise that the responsibility for maintaining law and order rests with constitutionally recognised security institutions. Community-based security arrangements must operate strictly within the law and under appropriate supervision”, the party said.

The party noted that it will not fold its arms while any development capable of undermining the peace, safety, and democratic rights of our people goes unquestioned. The party also called on the people of Ohaozara and, indeed, all Ebonyi citizens to remain calm, vigilant, and law-abiding, and also urge their supporters and members to resist provocation and conduct themselves peacefully while exercising their democratic rights.

“Finally, we remind the security agencies that their constitutional responsibility is to protect the lives and properties of all citizens, irrespective of political affiliation. They must live up to their expectations before, during, and after the elections.

“Ebonyi State belongs to all of us, and its security must never be sacrificed on the altar of political ambition. Our democracy must be protected, our people must be free, and the will of the electorate must prevail.
