2027: Tinubu must win all 18 Cross River LGs – Minister

By Punch Punch 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Minister of State for Industry, Trade and Investment, John Enoh, has urged Cross River residents to secure President Bola Tinubu’s re-election in 2027, warn

Enoh said this on Friday in Ikom during the inauguration of the Tinubu/Shettima Project Beyond 2027, a support group mobilising for the President’s re-election in the northern part of Cross River State.

He challenged the group to improve on Tinubu’s performance in the 2023 presidential election, when the President won seven of the state’s 18 local government areas.

“In 2023, out of 18 local government areas, the President won seven. In 2027, we must leave here with a determination that Mr President is going to win 18 over 18 in Cross River. And that is possible,” he said.

The minister argued that the state needed to remain politically aligned with the ruling administration to retain its relevance in national affairs.

Enoh also compared Tinubu’s leadership to that of Singapore’s former Prime Minister, Lee Kuan Yew, whom he credited with transforming the Asian country through pragmatic leadership and good governance.

“I likened President Bola Tinubu to a one-time Prime Minister of Singapore, Lee Kuan Yew. Singapore became a top-notch country from nowhere because of the pragmatic leadership and good governance of that Prime Minister.

“Lee Kuan Yew did not look at politics the way a politician looks at the next election, but at the next generation. That is what President Bola Tinubu is doing. President Bola Tinubu is looking at the next generation, not the next election,” he said.

Defending the President’s economic policies, Enoh recalled the economic conditions Tinubu inherited when he assumed office in 2023.

He claimed that Nigeria’s net foreign reserves were below $1bn at the time, international transactions using Nigerian ATM cards were failing, and letters of credit could not be honoured.

According to him, Tinubu took difficult decisions on his first day in office out of concern for Nigerians and future generations.

“There is no doubt that those decisions may have affected us adversely because of the hardship people have had to go through. But like a bitter tablet that cures sickness, after that bitterness, you will get well. Three and a half years later, recovery has started and stability has set in,” he said.

Enoh said the administration’s next priority was to improve prosperity, Nigerians’ purchasing power and the cost of goods and services.

He also claimed that inflation had fallen to about 14 per cent from the high levels recorded when Tinubu assumed office, adding that the President was determined to reduce it to single digits.

“I am aware that there are market women here, people who go to market every day and experience the pain. Mr President is determined to take inflation to single digits,” he said.

The minister urged the support group to take the administration’s reform message to wards and communities across Cross River, insisting that the state could not afford to remain in opposition after the 2027 election.
