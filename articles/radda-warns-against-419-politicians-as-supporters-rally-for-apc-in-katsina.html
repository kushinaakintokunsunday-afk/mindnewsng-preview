Radda Warns Against ‘419 Politicians’ As Supporters Rally For APC In Katsina

Katsina State Governor, Malam Dikko Umaru Radda, has urged voters to reject what he described as “419 politicians” in the 2027 general elections, declaring that only leaders with proven records of service deserve the people’s mandate.

The governor appealed on Tuesday during the Tinubu/Dikko 2027 Campaign Council rally at the Mai’adua International Border Market in Mai’adua Local Government Area, where thousands of APC supporters gathered as the campaign tour continued across the Daura Senatorial Zone.

Addressing the enthusiastic crowd, Governor Radda said the massive turnout reflected public confidence in his administration’s performance, particularly in healthcare delivery, education, teacher recruitment, youth empowerment and other development programmes.

He expressed appreciation to the people of Mai’adua for their overwhelming support and pledged to consolidate on the achievements of his administration if re-elected.

“We have nothing to say but to thank God for the people of Mai’adua. We are grateful for the support we have received from women, youths and the elderly. This massive turnout shows that the people are satisfied with our achievements and leadership, especially in healthcare, education, teacher recruitment and empowerment.»

“Please don’t make the mistake of electing 419 politicians. I know you are not in support of such politicians, and by the grace of God, we will reciprocate this confidence if re-elected. You have also demonstrated that you are genuine supporters and true admirers of the late President Muhammadu Buhari.”

The governor also welcomed thousands of defectors from the African Democratic Congress (ADC) into the All Progressives Congress (APC), describing the development as further evidence of the ruling party’s growing popularity across the state.

The rally’s highlight was the presentation of an Award of Excellence to Governor Radda by health workers in Mai’adua Local Government Area in recognition of what they described as his transformative leadership and commitment to strengthening the state’s healthcare sector.

Earlier, Governor Radda commissioned the newly constructed Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) Dumurkul Division in Mai’adua Local Government Area.

The security facility was built by the Chairman of Mai’adua Local Government, Hon. Mamman Salisu Na-Allahu, as part of efforts to strengthen grassroots security and enhance the fight against insecurity in the border communities.

Commissioning the project, Governor Radda commended the council chairman for his foresight and commitment to safeguarding lives and property, stressing that security remains the cornerstone of his administration’s development agenda.

He charged officers of the NSCDC to uphold professionalism, diligence, and discipline in the discharge of their duties while fostering stronger collaboration with other security agencies and host communities.

The governor reaffirmed his administration’s commitment to supporting local governments and security agencies with the necessary resources to sustain peace and ensure the safety of residents across Katsina State.
