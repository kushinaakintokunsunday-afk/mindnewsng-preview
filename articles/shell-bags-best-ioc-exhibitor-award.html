Shell bags Best IOC Exhibitor Award

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Shell Nigeria has won the Best International Oil Company (IOC) Exhibitor award at the 44th Annual International Conference and Exhibition of the Nigerian Associ

Shell Nigeria has won the Best International Oil Company (IOC) Exhibitor award at the 44th Annual International Conference and Exhibition of the Nigerian Association of Petroleum Explorationists (NAPE), with two of its employees also receiving recognition for outstanding technical contributions.

The recognition highlights the company’s contributions to Nigeria’s oil and gas industry through technical expertise, deepwater operations and knowledge sharing.

At the conference’s closing dinner, Abidemi Belgore, Senior Production Geologist at Shell Nigeria Exploration and Production Company Limited (SNEPCo), and her team emerged first runner-up in the Dr Olagundoye Olatunbosun Best Petroleum Geology Paper category.

Similarly, SNEPCo’s Asset Opportunity Development Lead, Oludayo Fakunle, received a plaque for Lead Technical Presentation for his paper on Bonga, Nigeria’s first deepwater development, which commenced operations 21 years ago.

Presenting the plaque, incoming NAPE President, Anthony Ofoma, described the presentation as insightful and consistent with the conference theme, “Unlocking More Barrels: Driving Reserves Growth through Exploration, Local Capabilities and Adaptive Technology.”

Shell was also a major sponsor of the conference, where it showcased its Nigerian operations spanning deepwater production, renewables and integrated gas, alongside its social investment initiatives. Executive Vice President and Country Chair, Shell Nigeria, Elohor Aiboni, had earlier addressed the opening session, commending ongoing reforms in the oil and gas industry, which she described as a step in the right direction towards realising Nigeria’s ambition of increasing oil production and unlocking more reserves.

Meanwhile, NAPE has conferred its Fellow award on seven members in recognition of their contributions to the development of the association and the geosciences profession.

Outgoing NAPE President, Cecilia Olajumoke-Ajayi, expressed appreciation to members and sponsors for their support towards the successful hosting of the conference.

Shell was a major sponsor of the conference in keeping with its longstanding commitment to the development of the oil and gas industry in Nigeria and complemented its support with an exhibition on its businesses in Nigeria including deepwater production, renewables, and integrated gas as well as social investments.

Executive Vice President and Country Chair, Shell Nigeria, Elohor Aiboni had addressed the opening session of the conference during which she commended the reforms in the oil and gas industry as a right step in Nigeria’s aspiration to unlock more barrels of oil.
