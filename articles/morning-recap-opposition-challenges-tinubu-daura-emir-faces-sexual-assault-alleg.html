Morning recap: Opposition challenges Tinubu, Daura emir faces sexual assault allegation, other top stories

The Emir of Daura, Faruk Umar Faruk, has faced a sexual assault allegation by a female tourist, prompting Amnesty International to demand an independent investigation by the authorities.

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency has arrested a South African professional golfer and a Kenyan chef over an alleged attempt to smuggle 50.40kg of cannabis into Nigeria through the Lagos airport.

The naira has come under renewed pressure as political spending ahead of the 2027 elections and increased foreign exchange demand during the Christmas season have been identified as factors contributing to its depreciation.

Liverpool midfielder Dominik Szoboszlai has emerged as the world’s best free-kick taker in 2026, finishing ahead of Lionel Messi and Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes.

And the Nigerian Education Loan Fund has commenced an organisational review after its student loan scheme recorded approximately 870,000 beneficiaries and over N375bn in disbursements.

See all top stories here:

1. 2027: Tinubu can’t save Alli, Yayi, Hamzat – Opposition

Opposition parties have challenged President Bola Tinubu’s influence ahead of the 2027 governorship elections in Lagos, Ogun and Oyo states, dismissing his endorsement of APC candidates as insufficient to secure victory. The parties insist that voters, rather than presidential backing, will determine the outcome of the elections, setting the stage for a fierce political contest in the South-West.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/2027-tinubu-cant-save-alli-yayi-hamzat-opposition/

2. Daura Emir faces sexual assault allegation, Amnesty seeks probe

The Emir of Daura, Faruk Umar Faruk, has been accused of sexually assaulting a female tourist during her visit to his palace in Katsina State. Amnesty International has called for a transparent and independent investigation, while a source in the emir’s palace has denied the allegation.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/daura-emir-faces-sexual-assault-allegation-amnesty-seeks-probe/

3. NDLEA arrests South African golfer, Kenyan chef with 50kg cannabis

The NDLEA has arrested a South African professional golfer and a Kenyan chef over an alleged attempt to smuggle 50.40kg of a potent cannabis strain into Nigeria. Investigators reportedly uncovered a luggage-tag swapping arrangement allegedly designed to conceal ownership of the drugs, with tracking devices also discovered in the consignments.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/safrican-golfer-kenyan-chef-arrested-with-50kg-cannabis-in-lagos/

4. 2027 politics, Christmas spending squeeze naira

The naira has weakened against the dollar amid rising foreign exchange demand linked to political spending ahead of the 2027 elections and increased festive-season transactions. Economists have warned that capital flight, policy uncertainty and reduced dollar inflows could further compound pressure on the currency.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/2027-politics-christmas-spending-squeeze-naira/

5. Szoboszlai tops Messi, Fernandes as world’s best free-kick taker in 2026

Liverpool midfielder Dominik Szoboszlai has been ranked the world’s best free-kick taker in 2026, ahead of Lionel Messi and Bruno Fernandes. The Hungarian’s impressive performances from dead-ball situations have earned him the top spot, while Cristiano Ronaldo and Arsenal’s Declan Rice also feature among the top 10.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/full-list-top-10-best-free-kick-takers-in-2026/

6. NELFUND reviews structure as student loan beneficiaries hit 870,000

The Nigerian Education Loan Fund has commenced a strategic organisational review following the expansion of its student loan scheme to approximately 870,000 beneficiaries, with over N375bn disbursed.

The exercise is expected to strengthen the agency’s operations and institutional capacity, while the fund has assured students that loan applications and disbursements will continue without disruption.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/nelfund-reviews-structure-as-student-loan-beneficiaries-hit-870000/
