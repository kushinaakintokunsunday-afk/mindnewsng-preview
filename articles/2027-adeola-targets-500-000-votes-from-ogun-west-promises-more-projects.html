2027: Adeola targets 500,000 votes from Ogun West, promises more projects

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Laide Raheem, Abeokuta

The Ogun APC governorship candidate and senator representing Ogun West Senatorial District, Solomon Olamilekan Adeola, has appealed to the residents of Ogun West to deliver 500,000 votes for him in the 2027 governorship election, declaring that the district has a historic opportunity to produce the next governor of Ogun State.

The senator made the appeal in Ilaro while addressing leaders and stakeholders of Yewa South Local Government Area at the conclusion of his engagement with stakeholders across the 20 local government areas of the state.

The senator, who expressed confidence in his governorship ambition, said the 500,000-vote target was achievable, noting that previous governors of the state had emerged with fewer than 300,000 votes.

He urged the people of Ogun West to close ranks and support his ambition, stressing that the district must not allow the opportunity to produce a governor in 2027 to slip away.

“My people of Ogun West, I want the votes that will make me the next governor of Ogun State to come from Ogun West Senatorial District. I am asking for 500,000 votes from Ogun West. With 500,000 votes, I will become governor,” he said.

Adeola, who described the growing political support for his ambition as unprecedented, said the entire three senatorial districts of Ogun State had, for the first time, united behind a governorship candidate.

He also claimed that his ambition had received massive backing from Ijebuland, disclosing that before his death, the Awujale of Ijebuland, Oba Sikiru Adetona, had instructed the Ijebu Council of Obas to support his governorship aspiration.

The senator, however, urged aggrieved party members and political stakeholders to close ranks, insisting that unity was necessary to secure victory in 2027.

“The reason for this meeting is for the development of Yewa South. I have not done anything to Yewa South yet. I will continue working until May 29, 2027,” he said.

The APC governorship candidate, who disclosed that he had facilitated more than 200 projects in Yewa South Local Government Area alone, said his political opponents were unsettled by his achievements and warned against attempts to intimidate his supporters.

He declared that Ogun West was determined to break what he described as “a 50-year political barrier” preventing the district from producing a governor.

The senator also questioned the possibility of another indigene of Iperu succeeding Governor Dapo Abiodun, who is also from the town, insisting that Ogun West deserved a fair opportunity to occupy the state’s highest political office.

He, however, announced plans to commence work on several projects across Yewa South LG within the next two weeks.

The proposed projects include roads in the Modeolu, Akorede, Oke Okuta, Isaga, Ifoyintedo, Ago Eyo and Old Sokoto-Badagry communities.

He also listed the construction of Oke-Odan Grammar School, Ahmadiya Community High School, an Intensive Care Unit (ICU) centre at the State Hospital, Ilaro, and Sayedero Market among projects expected to commence.

Adeola maintained that his ambition was backed by what he described as “divine intervention and growing political support”, urging residents to seize the opportunity to elect a governor from Ogun West in 2027.

Speaking earlier at the inauguration of 357 APC Ward Coordinators in Yewa South LG, he cautioned against attacks on candidates of the APC, declaring that the party would no longer tolerate political violence, destruction of campaign billboards and other acts of intimidation.

“Nobody has a monopoly of violence. Stoning, destroying APC candidates and their billboards, we will not allow it anymore. Nobody is above the law,” he declared.

He stressed that attacks on President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, Vice-President Kashim Shettima and other APC candidates must stop, warning that the party would not condone further acts of political aggression.

He called on political actors and their supporters to abide by the rule of law and embrace peaceful political participation.
