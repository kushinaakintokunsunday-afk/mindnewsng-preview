Developers urged to prioritise SMEs in AI deployment

By Punch Punch 1d ago 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Developers have been urged to deploy artificial intelligence to boost small and medium-sized enterprises rather than focusing only on big businesses and governm

Special Assistant on Communication to the Governor of Oyo State, Mr Dayo Ogunbowale, made the call while delivering the keynote address on the theme ‘Building the Future: How AI and Digital Innovation Can Transform Governance and Society’ at the seventh annual Google Developer Groups event in Ibadan, the Oyo State capital.

Organised by the Ibadan Google Developer Groups, in collaboration with Kakanfo Inn and Conference Centre, the programme took place at the facility in Ibadan at the weekend, attracting about 3,000 young people from across Nigeria and beyond.

Addressing the theme, Ogunbowale identified small and medium-sized enterprises as the bedrock of Oyo State’s economy, stressing that digital transformation would remain incomplete if SMEs were excluded from the benefits of technological advancement.

“Small and Medium Enterprise are the backbone of Oyo State’s economy. If AI serves only big business and government while SMEs are left on the far side of the digital divide, then our “digital transformation” is a half-finised product,” he said.

Ogunbowale stated that this year’s DevFest theme, ‘Building Safe, Secure, and Scalable Solutions with AI and Cloud’ aptly captured the technical pulse of the moment with precision. He noted that Nigeria must move from being a consumer to a creator of technology, saying, “We must become creators of intelligence rooted in our reality and responsive to our aspirations.”

“If we rely only on foreign AI models, they will not understand our nuances, our culture, and our people. We need an AI that understands the meaning of ‘akwa,’ ‘ekaabo,’ ‘sannu’ and not just translates them,” he added.

In his remarks, the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Kakanfo Inn and Conference Centre, Mr Adedamola Are, expressed delight at being a co-host of DevFEST 2026: “We were very pleased to host the 2026 edition of DevFEST on 21 November 2026 here at Kakanfo Inn & Conference Centre.”

Their emphasis was on AI/ML and agentic AI, Google Cloud, Flutter, Web3, distributed systems, product design, data privacy, and tech community/mental health discussions.

According to Are, the primary goal of DevFest is to educate developers, builders, and tech enthusiasts on Google’s latest technologies while fostering community-driven learning and local networking.

Speaking with newsmen on the sidelines of the event, Developer Ecosystem Programme Manager, Google, Sodiq Akinjobi, disclosed that developers, engineers, programme managers and product managers are currently building for Nigeria’s markets.

“They are building their apps in Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, not just in English, so that they can cover everyone. So the information, the instruction and technology can reach everyone whether you are learned or not. And with user research, it is now getting easier to apply these technologies in your day to day activities,” he said.

Akinjobi commended the government for implementing initiatives right from secondary schools, aimed at utilising new technologies and taking advantage of AI opportunities.

“And if you walk into the conference hall, you will see many participants are within 22 and 25 age bracket. And you will also see that everybody here is young and upwardly mobile. And I believe they are taking the acquired skills back to their younger ones, so we are getting youths on the map.There are lots of emerging innovations. I have just spoken to a lady who is building in robotics, and she is already building prototypes in robotics for healthcare,” he added.

Responding to questions from journalists at the event, the Lead Organiser of GDG Ibadan, software engineer and founder of Abit Technologies Hub Limited, Abidemi Tiamiyu, said the team volunteers with Google Developer Group Ibadan, which organises DevFEST Ibadan, to equip young people with the skills needed to remain relevant in an increasingly technology-driven world.

“We are volunteering for Google Developer Groups Ibadan, the organiser of DevFEST Ibadan. We are driven by the inspiration to impact our community with the technical know-how, mentorship and to show them the pathway to success,” he said.

He added, “Many of the participants are already showing interest in how to use AI to drive products and services. So, I believe that taking that away from here is 100 per cent valuable for anyone.”

According to Abidemi, speakers at this year’s event came from Accra, Ghana; Lagos; Ado-Ekiti; and other parts of the world. “We have speakers that came from Accra Ghana, Lagos, Ado-Ekiti, and other parts of the world,” he said, adding that DevFEST has a presence in Accra, Nairobi, Lagos, Ibadan and Ogbomoso.

He asserted that training programmes provided by DevFEST for over a decade have immensely aided several youths with valuable skills to earn a living, saying that DevFEST recorded 2,500 participants in 2025 and about 3,000 participants in attendance at the 2026 DevFEST.

“In 2025, we recorded 2,500 participants. But as I speak with you, I believe we already have about 3,000 participants right in the hall because the crowd is far larger than what we had in 2025. That’s what we have been doing to groom young people in the past 10 years and many have benefited from it.”

The Co-Lead Organiser of GDG Ibadan and software engineer at Resilience17, Joshua Odejide, underscored DevFEST’s impact on young people, noting that the programme had equipped many participants with website development capabilities and other digital skills to become self-reliant.

He said, “One of the major takeaways from this programme is that everybody will actually become a builder. Before now, if you want to build a website, you will have to go to someone who would do it for charges. But if the charges are too much, one might drop the idea. But DevFEST equips you with skills to build it a website yourself.”

Highlighting the numerous benefits of artificial intelligence, Odejide noted that tasks such as designing graphic flyers could now be completed within seconds using AI tools. He stressed the need for professionals across various fields to adapt to emerging technologies and keep pace with rapid innovations.

The event featured panellists, including the Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer of Zeeh Africa, David Adeleke; Investor and Community Builder, Michael Toyinbo; Director of Expansion, Strategy and Operations at Chowdeck; Founder of Prine, Gbolahan Alli; and Developer Relations Lead at ALATPay, Haastrup Adegoke, among other dignitaries.
