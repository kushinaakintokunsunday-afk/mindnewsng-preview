Was Peter Obi Hospitalised? Fact Emerges as Viral 'Broken Neck' Photo Is Exposed as AI-Generated

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 11 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

A viral photo claimed Peter Obi broke his neck copying Tinubu's cycling exercise and was hospitalised. Fact-checkers have now exposed the image as AI-generated

A photograph showing the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) presidential candidate Peter Obi lying in a hospital bed with a neck brace has been circulating on social media - alongside a wild claim that he broke his neck while trying to copy President Bola Tinubu's famous cycling exercise.

The viral claim

The fake hospital photo was shared on X by a user with the caption mocking Obidients, paired with a real video of Tinubu riding a stationary bicycle to create the impression that Obi had a serious accident. It escalated fast: some accounts even posted a “medical update” claiming Obi was “recuperating well” with a six-month recovery timeline, and a screenshot of a fake Guardian report with a byline - which the named journalist later denied ever writing.

What the fact-checks found

  • The FactCheckHub (November 7) ran the image through three separate AI-detection tools - all three flagged it as synthetic, with no credible evidence of any neck injury or hospital admission.
  • Fact Check Africa went further: OpenAI's image-verification tool returned “Generated with OpenAI tools” - and detected a SynthID watermark originating from OpenAI, the digital fingerprint AI generators embed in their output.
  • Obi's spokesman, Idris Zekeri, told The PUNCH: “There is zero iota of truth in that rumour. No such thing happened.” He said he was with Obi around 7 a.m. on Sunday, November 1, before the former Anambra governor boarded a flight for an international engagement.
  • NDC Campaign Council deputy spokesperson Dr Sam Omale added on Channels TV that Obi is in good health - just physically exhausted from a punishing campaign schedule that included a 34-hour round trip to meet candidates on November 3.

Verdict: FALSE

The claim that Peter Obi broke his neck and was hospitalised is false, and the hospital photo is AI-generated. It is also not an isolated incident: fact-checkers have documented a wave of AI-manipulated images and fake newspaper front pages targeting 2027 election figures - including a fake APC rally photo and fabricated Punch and Tribune front pages. As the 2027 race heats up, treat viral screenshots of politicians with maximum suspicion - check for AI watermarks, verify the newspaper headline on the paper's official page, and wait for a fact-check before sharing.

Sources: The FactCheckHub (November 7, 2026); Fact Check Africa; The PUNCH interview with Idris Zekeri (November 2, 2026); Nigeria Info FM; Channels TV interview with Dr Sam Omale.
