2027: Any plot against Tinubu will fail – Akpabio

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Senate President Godswill Akpabio on Sunday described President Bola Tinubu as a “child of covenant,” warning that any conspiracy against him ahead of the 2027

Akpabio spoke on Sunday at the Akwa Ibom State inauguration of the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement in Uyo, where he called on his supporters to reject attempts to undermine the President’s re-election bid.

“We are saying today that President Bola Tinubu is a child of covenant. And therefore, any conspiracy against him, and all conspirators against him, shall be consumed by fire. Holy Ghost, Fire! Holy Ghost, Fire! Holy Ghost, Fire!” he said.

The Senate President also described Vice-President Kashim Shettima as a child of covenant, warning that any conspiracy against him would not stand.

Akpabio said the National Assembly was fully behind Tinubu.

He said the legislature was supporting the administration’s efforts to address the country’s economic challenges.

“The National Assembly is behind the President 100 per cent. And we are doing this with a lot of personal sacrifice and patience,” he said.

He listed some of the administration’s initiatives, including the Nigerian Education Loan Fund, the Consumer Credit Scheme, the Presidential Compressed Natural Gas Initiative and agricultural interventions, as programmes aimed at improving the lives of Nigerians.

Akpabio also defended the removal of the petrol subsidy, describing those advocating its reversal as confused.

“No government can remedy what it does not understand. If you don’t understand that subsidy was killing Nigeria, can you remedy it? That is why they said they want to go back to subsidy. We wish them luck, because it is not in Nigeria. We have left them behind,” he said.

The former Akwa Ibom governor further claimed that political parties and support groups in the state had united behind Tinubu’s re-election bid.

“When we say here, A, B and C, and we say Akwa Ibom United, it means that all political parties have collapsed into one to vote for the President in 2027. It means that all political parties and all support groups have joined door to door to canvass support for the President come 2027,” he said.

Akpabio also said opposition parties had little political presence in the state, claiming that those who attempted to campaign there had struggled to attract supporters.

He commended the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement for taking its campaign to Akwa Ibom, saying the initiative recognised the importance of engaging voters in their homes and communities.

The movement, convened by Niger Delta leader Government Ekpemupolo, popularly known as Tompolo, is mobilising grassroots support for Tinubu’s re-election in 2027 through state, local government, ward and polling unit structures.
