Ekiti deputy gov mobilises monarchs, PDP members for Tinubu’s re-election

By Punch Punch 6d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Ekiti State Deputy Governor, Mrs Monisade Afuye, has intensified mobilisation for President Bola Tinubu’s re-election, seeking the support of traditional ru

Afuye, an All Progressives Congress chieftain, appealed to monarchs and other traditional leaders to support Tinubu at the ballot box on January 16, 2027, in appreciation of what she described as his administration’s infrastructural commitment to Ekiti State.

According to a statement issued by the Director of Press in the Office of the Deputy Governor, Tope Ariyo, on Wednesday in Ado Ekiti, Afuye spoke during a visit to the palace of the Ogoga of Ikere, Oba Adejimi Adu-Alagbado, who is also the Chairman of the Ekiti State Council of Traditional Rulers.

The visit involved a coalition of Peoples Democratic Party and APC members mobilising support for Tinubu’s re-election.

Afuye said the President had contributed significantly to the development of Ekiti State, citing federal appointments given to indigenes of the state and what she described as unprecedented infrastructure projects under the Tinubu administration.

She listed the projects to include the Ado-Ekiti–Ikere–Akure Road, the modern Army Brigade barracks in Ikere, the Ado-Ekiti–Iyin–Aramoko–Itawure–Osun border Road, the Ado–Ijan–Ilumoba–Ikole Road, the Ado–Ilawe–Ilumoba–Ikole Road and the Ifaki–Kabba–Ado-Ekiti Road.

She said the projects were among several federal infrastructure interventions ongoing in the state.

Afuye said, “We have come to you today to seek your support for Mr President. As our traditional leaders, you hold a special place in the heart of this government. The Tinubu administration has given unprecedented support to Ekiti State, especially our Ikere community here. We can now boast of an Army Barrack that has positively changed the face of infrastructure in this axis.

“Several roads that were death traps for years are now being reconstructed across the state. We owe Mr President a massive vote from this axis of the country. We have come to seek your blessings and support because we know you are important in the scheme of things.”

The PDP candidate in the 2026 Ekiti governorship election, Dr Wole Oluyede, who recently joined the coalition for Tinubu’s re-election, said his decision was driven by the need to advance the state’s development beyond political and ideological differences.

Oluyede urged members and supporters of different political parties to prioritise collective prosperity over personal interests, saying the welfare of the people should take precedence over political benefits.

In his response, the Ogoga of Ikere, Oba Adu-Alagbado, expressed delight at the coming together of members of the APC and PDP, particularly the deputy governor and Oluyede.

The monarch said, “Your coming together is symbolic because Ikere Ekiti is one and indivisible. I am really elated that the coming together of the political class in Ikere is happening during my time. Let us sustain this development because there is much to gain when we work as one. Our strength is in our unity.”

Adu-Alagbado commended the Tinubu administration for what he described as improved infrastructure in the community, particularly the Army barracks, which he said had contributed to the expansion of the area and increased commercial activities.

He expressed hope that more development projects would come to the community and said the President deserved the support of residents of Ikere and Ekiti State because of his administration’s infrastructure interventions.

The mobilisation comes amid efforts by political groups in Ekiti State to build a broader alliance ahead of the 2027 presidential election.

In August 2026, Oluyede announced an alliance involving the PDP, Accord, Action Democratic Party, Young Progressive Party and Action Alliance, among other groups, under the Oluyede Progressive Alliance for Renewed Hope Agenda, to support Tinubu’s re-election bid.

Although Oluyede has declared support for Tinubu, he has maintained that the decision does not amount to leaving the PDP, saying he remains a member of the opposition party.

The Independent National Electoral Commission has fixed January 16, 2027, for the presidential and National Assembly elections, while the governorship and State Houses of Assembly elections are scheduled for February 6, 2027.
