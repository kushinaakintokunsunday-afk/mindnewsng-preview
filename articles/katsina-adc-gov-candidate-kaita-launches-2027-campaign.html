Katsina ADC Gov Candidate Kaita Launches 2027 Campaign

By Channels TV Channels TV 2d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The governorship candidate of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) in Katsina State, Senator Ahmed Kaita, has officially launched his 2027 campaign in the Funtua senatorial zone, drawing a crowd of supporters as residents of Funtua Local Government pledged to back ADC candidates in all elective positions during the forthcoming general elections.

The campaign rally, held on Sunday at Aya Primary School in Funtua, attracted scores of supporters from across Funtua and neighbouring communities, signalling the party’s determination to mount a strong challenge in the state.

Addressing the crowd, Senator Kaita expressed gratitude for what he described as the overwhelming voluntary turnout of supporters, insisting that those in attendance were driven by a genuine desire for change and good governance rather than political inducement.

He said the campaign would unveil its comprehensive policy agenda during the next rally scheduled for Faskari Local Government Area on Tuesday, explaining that the decision was deliberate to distinguish the ADC’s approach from that of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

“Today marks the beginning of the change that Katsina people have been yearning for. Everyone you see here came willingly because they desire good governance. I sincerely appreciate our supporters for the warm reception. By the grace of God, we will unveil our agenda in Faskari on Tuesday. We want to show the difference between our campaign and that of the APC-led government,” Kaita declared.

Before proceeding to the rally venue, the ADC governorship candidate paid a courtesy visit to the Sarkin Maskan Katsina and District Head of Funtua, where he sought royal blessings, prayers, guidance and support ahead of the elections.

The event also featured the ADC senatorial candidate for Funtua zone, Hon. Jamilu Muhammad Lion, whose appearance drew loud cheers from the large gathering of party faithful.

The Funtua rally marks the first major campaign outing by Senator Kaita in the 2027 election season and sets the stage for the ADC’s statewide mobilisation as political activities gather momentum across Katsina State.
