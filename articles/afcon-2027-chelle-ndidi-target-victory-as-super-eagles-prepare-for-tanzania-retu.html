AFCON 2027: Chelle, Ndidi Target Victory As Super Eagles Prepare For Tanzania Return Leg

By Channels TV Channels TV 17 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Super Eagles head coach Eric Chelle says Nigeria are motivated to secure victory against Tanzania as both sides prepare for their return-leg encounter in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers at the Amaan Stadium in Zanzibar on Tuesday.

The Super Eagles emerged 3-1 victors when the two teams met on Friday at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium, with goals courtesy of Alhassan Yusuf and a brace from Tolu Arokodare.

Chelle, in a pre-match press conference, acknowledged that Tanzania would be eager to win on home soil but insisted that his players were ready for the challenge despite a difficult journey ahead of the match.

The Nigerian coach said he was surprised by Tanzania’s tactical approach in their previous meeting, particularly their decision to deploy a five-man defence instead of the formations they had traditionally used.

Another victory would take the Super Eagles to nine points and increase the pressure on Group L leaders Guinea-Bissau, who also face Madagascar on Tuesday.

Meanwhile, Tanzania have already qualified automatically as a tournament co-host.

“I was a little disappointed because they went to Uyo and played five at the back. Normally, they used to play in 4-2-3-1 or 4-1-4-1. So I was a little disappointed,” Chelle said.

“But now I think they are at home and they want to win this game. Like us, we are ready to make a good game. The travel was not easy yesterday, but we are here, excited and motivated to play this game.”

The Super Eagles coach also defended his tactical decisions, insisting that his choice of formation would depend on the players available, the opposition and the team’s overall philosophy.

Chelle said preparations for the encounter had been ongoing for months, stressing that selecting the best-performing players remained central to his approach.

“We prepared for the game against Tanzania during two or three months before the game. We saw a lot of things, and after that, you have to put the best players,” he said.

He added that players who regularly feature in the starting lineup must continue working hard to maintain their places, explaining that every member of the squad must be at their best.

Chelle also praised the commitment of his players, insisting that they continued to give their all for the national team even when results did not go their way.

“I can tell you that these guys, they give everything for this jersey,” he said.

Meanwhile, Super Eagles captain Wilfred Ndidi has expressed confidence in the team’s blend of experienced players and emerging talents, describing the integration of younger players into the squad as encouraging.

Ndidi said the younger members of the team had settled in well and urged them to communicate freely with their more experienced teammates, both on and off the pitch.

“It’s very inspiring seeing the young lads coming into the team and actually gelling with the team. For us as a team, old players and young players, we are all the same,” the midfielder said.

He maintained that communication and unity would be crucial as Nigeria seek to maintain their momentum, adding that the players must remain committed to the coach’s philosophy and game plan regardless of the pressure they face.

Reflecting on the team’s previous performance, Ndidi noted that Nigeria improved in the second half, attributing the response to the players’ ability to stay together as a unit.

Ndidi said the team remained focused on securing victory in their next outing, insisting that their immediate priority was the encounter against Tanzania before turning attention to subsequent matches.

“We as a group, we have a plan, we have a philosophy, we have a game plan and we try as much as possible to win every game. Every game that comes our way now, that sits at the top of the table. So the most important game now is the game tomorrow. We get this one done, and then we look forward to the next one,” Ndidi said.

Match Details

  • Match: Tanzania vs Nigeria
  • Date: Tuesday, November 17, 2026
  • Time: 2:00 PM WAT
