Army Intensifies Offensive In Katsina, Kills Terrorists, Recovers Motorcycles

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Troops of the 17 Brigade, Nigerian Army, Joint Task Force (North West), Operation FANSAN YANMA, have intensified the offensive against terrorist groups in Katsi

Troops of the 17 Brigade, Nigerian Army, Joint Task Force (North West), Operation FANSAN YANMA, have intensified the offensive against terrorist groups in Katsina State, neutralising suspected terrorists, recovering motorcycles and dismantling criminal hideouts during coordinated clearance and fighting patrol operations across Sabuwa, Musawa and Malumfashi Local Government Areas.

The operations, conducted between November 18 and 19, 2026, formed part of the military’s ongoing efforts to deny terrorists freedom of movement and restore security in communities affected by banditry and other criminal activities.

According to the Nigerian Army, troops of Combat Team 3, supported by the 17 Brigade Strike Force, launched a clearance operation on November 19 against terrorist enclaves on the outskirts of Madachi Village in Sabuwa Local Government Area.

The troops reportedly came under fire from the terrorists shortly after making contact but responded with superior firepower, killing three suspected terrorists and recovering motorcycles abandoned during the encounter.

No casualties were recorded among the troops.

In a separate operation the same day, troops of the 83 Battalion, working alongside personnel from various Forward Operating Bases, the Administrative Company in Malumfashi and local hunters, carried out fighting patrols to Tsabe Village in Musawa Local Government Area and Tsamiyan Maigoro in Malumfashi Local Government Area following intelligence on the activities of a notorious terrorist kingpin, Rabe Rajaja.

During the operation, the troops engaged the terrorists at a deserted settlement, forcing them to flee with gunshot wounds while abandoning a motorcycle, which was recovered.

Troops also recovered farm produce allegedly exploited by the terrorists and have continued pursuit operations to track down the fleeing suspects.

The Army said the offensive had since been expanded, with troops of the 17 Brigade Strike Force and Combat Team 3 sustaining clearance operations around Madachi, Dungun Muazu and Dankolo forests in Sabuwa Local Government Area.

The operations featured coordinated dawn assaults, artillery bombardments on identified terrorist camps and the deployment of blocking forces along likely escape routes to intercept fleeing criminals.

Meanwhile, Commander of the 17 Brigade, Brigadier General Ishaya Ibrahim, undertook operational assessment visits to Forward Operating Bases in Mabai and Faskari on November 20 and 21 to evaluate troops’ combat readiness, operational effectiveness and welfare.

During the visits, the brigade commander received operational briefings, inspected military equipment and interacted with personnel, urging them to maintain discipline, professionalism and a high level of operational preparedness while addressing issues relating to promotion, uniforms and military kitting.

At FOB Faskari, Brigadier General Ibrahim also met with local hunters and vigilante groups, encouraging them to sustain the supply of credible intelligence and deepen collaboration with security agencies, while cautioning them against acts of hostility towards law-abiding citizens.

The commander also visited troops deployed at Machika to assess their operational posture as part of ongoing efforts to strengthen the Brigade’s capacity to confront emerging security threats across its area of responsibility.

In a statement issued on Sunday, the Acting Assistant Director, Army Public Relations, 17 Brigade, Nigerian Army, Captain Abayomi Adisa, reaffirmed the commitment of troops under Operation FANSAN YANMA to sustaining aggressive operations against terrorists and other criminal elements through enhanced collaboration with local security stakeholders.

He said the military remains resolute in its determination to restore lasting peace and security across Katsina State.
