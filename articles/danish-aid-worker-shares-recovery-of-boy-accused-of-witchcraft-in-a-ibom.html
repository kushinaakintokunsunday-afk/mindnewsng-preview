Danish aid worker shares recovery of boy accused of witchcraft in A’Ibom

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Danish aid worker Anja Ringgren Loven has shared pictures showing the recovery of a boy rescued four months ago after he was allegedly branded a witch, starved,

Loven shared a video showing the boy, identified as Godbless, severely malnourished and eating shortly after his rescue, with the caption, “Children are not witches.”

In a Facebook post on Friday, she also shared a recent photograph of the boy looking healthy and happy, four months after he was rescued alongside his brother and sister.

Reflecting on the children’s recovery, Loven wrote, “It has been four months since we rescued Godbless and his brother and sister.

“For nearly 14 years, I have rescued children accused of being witches. All of them would have been dead today.

“My eyes cannot stop looking at the first picture.

“How cute a face is a child allowed to have? I feel like squeezing those little munchkin cheeks.

“Support Land of Hope, and we can save more lives.”
Loven, who runs the child protection organisation Land of Hope, has shared several accounts of children rescued after being accused of witchcraft in parts of Akwa Ibom State.

In October 2025, she shared photographs of a young girl identified as Christiana, who was reportedly rescued from the streets of Akwa Ibom after being accused of witchcraft, beaten and abandoned.

Similarly, in August 2025, she shared the progress of Hope, a Nigerian boy rescued after being branded a witch and abandoned by his family.

Hope had graduated from primary school and was preparing to begin secondary education in September, according to the aid worker.
