EPL: Leeds thrash Chelsea 4-1, hand Alonso heavy defeat

By Punch Punch 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Leeds United stunned Chelsea with a 4-1 victory at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, handing manager Xabi Alonso another damaging Premier League setback. The visitor

The visitors recovered from conceding an early goal to score four times, with Noah Okafor netting twice, while Dominic Calvert-Lewin and substitute Brenden Aaronson also found the net.

Chelsea took the lead in the 25th minute through Emmanuel Emegha, who marked his first start for the club by finishing Morgan Rogers’ pass.

However, Leeds responded three minutes later when Okafor fired past Emiliano Martinez after being set up by Calvert-Lewin to restore parity.

The visitors took the lead in the 53rd minute after referee Michael Oliver awarded them a penalty for handball against Morgan Rogers. The decision followed a VAR review after Jayden Bogle’s effort struck the Chelsea midfielder’s arm.

Calvert-Lewin stepped up and converted the spot kick, sending Martinez the wrong way to put Leeds 2-1 ahead.

Okafor extended the visitors’ advantage six minutes later, completing his brace after Chelsea failed to clear an Ethan Ampadu throw-in and the resulting ball fell into his path.

Chelsea attempted to recover by introducing Danny Welbeck and Pedro Neto, but Leeds maintained their advantage and continued to threaten on the counter.

Aaronson completed the emphatic victory in stoppage time, scoring in the 90th minute to make it 4-1 and seal Leeds’ first victory at Stamford Bridge since 1999.

The defeat compounds Chelsea’s difficulties under Alonso, who had been seeking a response after his side’s disappointing run before the international break.

Chelsea were also dealt an injury setback during the first half when midfielder Moises Caicedo was forced off and replaced by Jordan Henderson.

The result gives Leeds a significant boost in their attempt to move away from the lower half of the Premier League table, while Chelsea will be left to reflect on a damaging home defeat.
