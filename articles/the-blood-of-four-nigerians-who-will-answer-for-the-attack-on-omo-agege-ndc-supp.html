The Blood of Four Nigerians: Who will answer for the attack on Omo-Agege, NDC supporters in Ughelli?

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 10h ago 8 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

At least four persons have been feared dead after gunmen opened fire on members of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign team accompanying former Deput

At least four persons have been feared dead after gunmen opened fire on members of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) campaign team accompanying former Deputy Senate President, Senator Ovie Omo-Agege, during a campaign visit to Uduere Community in Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State.

The incident, which occurred during a political campaign ahead of the 2027 general elections, also left several people injured, including a former member of the Delta State House of Assembly, Chief Evans Ivwurie, who was reportedly battling for his life alongside other victims receiving medical attention.

Among those reportedly killed was Moses Anaughe, a former Commissioner of the Federal Character Commission and aide to Omo-Agege.

Another victim, identified as Akima Umukoro, popularly known as Akhima, was also reported dead.

Omo-Agege, who is contesting the Delta Central Senatorial District seat under the NDC in the 2027 National Assembly elections, described the incident as a premeditated assassination attempt against him.

However, the Ughelli North Local Government Chairman, Olorogun Jaro Egbo, rejected the allegation, accusing members of Omo-Agege’s entourage of opening fire on his supporters.

The two camps have continued to trade accusations over the circumstances surrounding the violence, while the Delta State Police Command says investigations have commenced to establish what happened and identify those responsible.

Omo-Agege’s Camp Alleges Assassination Attempt

In a statement issued by his Media Adviser, Sunny Areh, Omo-Agege’s campaign organisation condemned the attack, alleging that armed men opened fire on the senator and members of his entourage at close range during what it described as a peaceful campaign visit.

The organisation claimed that Omo-Agege narrowly escaped death when bullets reportedly passed close to his head while he was addressing supporters.

It identified four people, including two of its aides, Moses Anaughe and Akima Umukoro, as among those killed in the incident.

The campaign organisation also listed Chief Evans Ivwurie, Chuks Erhire, Tobore Agba and Ayodo Wanogho among those who sustained gunshot injuries.

According to the statement, the victims were shot at close range and were receiving medical attention.

The organisation further alleged that eyewitnesses identified Egbo as the person leading the attackers.

It described the incident as a targeted attempt to assassinate Omo-Agege, insisting that it was neither a misunderstanding nor a clash between rival groups.

The campaign organisation called on Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori, to condemn the attack and ensure that those allegedly responsible were arrested and investigated.

It also appealed to the Inspector-General of Police to take over the investigation, expressing concerns about the handling of the incident by the Delta State Police Command.

The group, however, urged its supporters not to retaliate or take the law into their hands, insisting that the attack would not prevent it from participating in the 2027 elections.

Egbo Denies Allegations, Accuses Omo-Agege’s Supporters

But Egbo dismissed the allegations against him, offering a different account of how the confrontation started.

According to the council chairman, his supporters had visited the community ahead of an event he was scheduled to host when they learnt that Omo-Agege’s team intended to use the same community hall.

He claimed that his group had already booked the facility and that disagreements arose after members of the NDC entourage allegedly refused to use another location.

Egbo further alleged that his supporters had received intelligence that armed men, including individuals allegedly carrying AK-47 rifles, had been brought into the area from Sapele and Ughelli.

He said he contacted the Area Commander, the Divisional Police Officer and the military to ensure that security was maintained.

The council chairman claimed that despite efforts to prevent a confrontation, members of Omo-Agege’s entourage opened fire on his supporters as the senator was leaving the community.

He alleged that three of his supporters were killed, while others sustained critical injuries.

Egbo also accused Omo-Agege of being responsible for the violence, referencing the senator’s reported political slogan, “Rig & Die”.

However, these allegations have not been independently established, and the police say investigations are ongoing.

Omo-Agege Says Attack Was Premeditated

Speaking during an interview on Channels Television via Zoom, Omo-Agege described the incident as a premeditated attempt on his life, claiming that it was part of an ongoing plot to eliminate him.

The former Deputy Senate President said four members of his campaign organisation were killed, while about 15 others sustained serious injuries.

He explained that he was addressing supporters when gunmen suddenly opened fire, adding that he heard some of the attackers shouting that they wanted to kill him.

According to him, he survived because of the intervention of his Department of State Services (DSS) security personnel.

Omo-Agege said the shooting started less than five minutes after he began speaking at the campaign venue.

He said: “This is premeditated. It is not something that just happened today. I just gave you the buildup. This thing has been going on.

“As I was speaking, I just moved over to the side of a vehicle to be able to make eye contact with those I was speaking with. It was immediately I did that that the volley of gunshots started.”

He appealed to the Inspector-General of Police to take over the investigation, saying he had concerns about whether the state police command could deliver justice.

The senator also appealed to his supporters not to retaliate, insisting that the attack would not stop him from continuing his campaign.

“If the idea is that this is supposed to deter me from going forward with the campaigns, it’s a very, very big mistake and a big lie. It ain’t going to happen,” he said.

Delta Government Imposes Curfew

Following the incident, the Delta State Government imposed a curfew across Ughelli North Local Government Area to prevent further violence and restore calm.

The restriction, which took effect on the day of the incident, runs from 6pm to 6am daily until further notice.

The Secretary to the State Government, Dr Kingsley Emu, announced the directive in a statement, urging residents to comply with the restriction.

The government said the measure was necessary to bring the security situation under control following reports of gunshots and deaths in parts of the local government area.

Police Begin Investigation, Warn Political Actors

The Delta State Police Command condemned the violence and said it had commenced investigations to determine the circumstances surrounding the incident.

The Police Public Relations Officer, SP Bright Edafe, said Commissioner of Police Samuel Erale had directed intensified security operations in the affected area to prevent further violence and maintain law and order.

The police also urged residents to remain calm, cooperate with security personnel and comply with the curfew.

The Commissioner of Police reportedly ordered a full-scale investigation, assuring residents that anyone found culpable would face the law, irrespective of political affiliation or status.

He also warned politicians and their supporters against thuggery, intimidation and the unlawful use of firearms, stressing that political differences should not result in loss of lives.

However, in a separate account contained in the report, Edafe was also quoted as saying that the command was yet to receive an official report of the incident, despite claims that the Ughelli Area Commander was at the hospital where some of the victims were taken.

NDC Accuses APC of Attacking Its Members

The national leadership of the NDC also condemned the attack, accusing suspected agents of the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) of being responsible.

In a statement issued by its National Publicity Secretary, Osa Director, the party alleged that the attack was part of repeated attempts to intimidate its members and supporters in Delta State.

The party claimed that four people had been killed, including a close aide to Omo-Agege, while several others sustained injuries.

It described the incident as an attempt to silence opposition voices and discourage its members from participating in the democratic process.

The NDC called on the Nigeria Police Force and other security agencies to investigate the incident, arrest those responsible and identify anyone who may have sponsored the attack.

It also expressed condolences to the families of the deceased and wished the injured a speedy recovery.

The APC, however, rejected the accusations and accused the NDC of instigating the violence, claiming that three of its members were among those killed.

Third Major Political Violence Incident in Delta

The Uduere attack is the latest in a series of violent incidents linked to political activities in Delta State ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The first major incident occurred during a political procession in the Warri-Effurun axis on September 18, when supporters of rival political interests clashed. The incident reportedly involved allegations of armed violence, with an AK-47 rifle said to have been recovered afterwards.

Another violent confrontation occurred during a political procession in Asaba on November 7, involving supporters of the NDC and the Obedient Movement. The incident left one person dead and several others injured.

The police had said they received intelligence about the planned procession and advised against a road march. Political actors subsequently traded accusations over responsibility for the violence.

With the latest incident in Ughelli North, concerns have continued to grow over the safety of political campaigns and the possibility of further confrontations as the 2027 elections approach.

The conflicting accounts from the NDC and the APC, the disputed casualty figures and the allegations of an assassination attempt have placed pressure on security agencies to establish what actually happened.

For now, the police investigation, the identities of those responsible and the circumstances that led to the deadly shooting remain central to the search for justice.

As political activities intensify, the latest bloodshed serves as another reminder that electoral competition must not become a justification for violence, intimidation or the loss of innocent lives.
