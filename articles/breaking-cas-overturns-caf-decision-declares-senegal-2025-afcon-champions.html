BREAKING: CAS overturns CAF decision, declares Senegal 2025 AFCON champions

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 2h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) has ruled in favour of Senegal in the dispute over the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) final, confirming the Teranga

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) has ruled in favour of Senegal in the dispute over the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) final, confirming the Teranga Lions as champions.

In its ruling on Tuesday, the Switzerland-based tribunal overturned the earlier decision by the Confederation of African Football (CAF), which had awarded the trophy to Morocco.

CAS upheld Senegal’s appeal through “a unanimous decision”, restoring the team’s original 1-0 victory over Morocco after extra time.

“After having examined the arguments raised by the Parties, the Arbitral Panel considered that the Referee’s decision to resume the match or to stop it fell definitively within his sole authority. By blowing his whistle to restart play, the Referee considered that the conditions for resuming the match had been met – both teams were on the pitch again, and both expressed their willingness to resume,” the CAS ruling reads.

“In the Panel’s view, there is no doubt that the effects of the Referee’s decision were limited to the pitch. As such, the Panel considers that the Referee’s decision was covered by the ‘field of play’ doctrine. Since Articles 82 and 84 of the AFCON Regulations are intended solely to determine the outcome of a match when a team’s conduct leads the referee to call off the game permanently, they are therefore not applicable in this case. In conclusion, the Panel considers that in resuming the match, the Referee did not err in law.

“The Panel acknowledged that the Referee had to make a decision in an atmosphere of extreme tension. However, these circumstances do not justify describing the decision as “arbitrary”. On the contrary, the Referee’s judgment did not appear to have been affected by factors external to the game.”
