US Deportation List Rises To 157 Nigerians As Six More Returnees Land At Lagos Airport - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Legit.ng / NEMA 13 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Legit.ng / NEMA

NEMA received six Nigerian deportees from the US at Lagos airport as the American deportation list of Nigerians rose to 157 with names and photos published publ

The National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA) has received six Nigerian citizens deported from the United States at the Cargo Terminal of Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos.

The six returnees - five men and one woman - arrived aboard a Beechcraft 1900 aircraft at about 10:13 pm on November 4, 2026, in a reception coordinated with the Nigeria Immigration Service and other federal agencies.

Immigration officers documented and profiled the returnees after their late-night arrival as part of the Federal Government's effort to manage the return of Nigerian citizens from abroad in an orderly and dignified manner.

According to US authorities, the individuals were arrested in various states for offences including fraud, money laundering, identity theft, drug-related crimes, assault and child cruelty. The Department of Homeland Security says its enforcement operations focus on removing non-citizens with criminal records.

The development comes as the US government raised the number of Nigerians on its deportation list to 157 and published their names and photos publicly - a practice that has drawn attention within the Nigerian community in the United States.

The deportations are part of ongoing immigration enforcement operations across the United States, which have also seen legal residents with long-standing immigration cases detained in recent months.

Mindnewsng presents this report with respect for all those affected and their families. Details are based on official statements from NEMA and US authorities as reported by Legit.ng.
