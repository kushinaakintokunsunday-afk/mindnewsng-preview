2027 Elections: Tinubu Endorses Alli, Yayi, Hamzat

By Channels TV Channels TV 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

President Bola Tinubu has publicly endorsed the All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos states ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Tinubu endorsed the candidates on Saturday by raising their hands at the party’s presidential campaign rally held at Mapo Hall in Ibadan, Oyo State.

Introducing the candidates, the President told supporters, “The governor you will vote for, I will show you,” before raising the hand of Senator Sharafadeen Alli, the APC governorship candidate in Oyo State.

He subsequently raised the hands of Senator Solomon Olamilekan Adeola, popularly known as Yayi, the party’s candidate in Ogun State, and Obafemi Hamzat, its governorship candidate in Lagos State.

Tinubu also endorsed Remi Oseni, the APC senatorial candidate for Oyo South, by raising his hand at the rally.

The event marked the South-West leg of the President’s re-election campaign and the seventh state rally he had attended since the exercise began in Kaduna on October 24.

Vice-President Kashim Shettima, APC governors, members of the National Assembly and other party leaders attended the rally.

Tinubu, who arrived in Ibadan alongside his wife, Senator Oluremi Tinubu, said he was in the state to appreciate residents for their support during the 2023 presidential election.

He expressed confidence that they would vote for the APC again in 2027.

“I am just here to say thank you for standing with us. You gave us votes in 2023, and I am sure you will do the same in 2027,” he said.

The President, however, kept his speech brief and asked the audience to observe a minute’s silence in honour of victims of a boat accident in Ukubie, Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, on Friday.

Tinubu had earlier directed that the Ibadan rally be held without much fanfare in honour of those who died in the incident and in sympathy with their families.

He also disclosed that he had met with traditional rulers in Oyo State, including the Olubadan of Ibadanland and the Soun of Ogbomoso, expressing confidence in their support.

The President further promised improved electricity supply and industrial development, announcing that his administration had signed an agreement with Chinese automobile manufacturer Yutong to reopen the defunct Leyland factory in Ibadan for the assembly of compressed natural gas (CNG) buses.

“We will bring industries and provide jobs for our youths,” he said.

The APC is seeking to take control of Oyo State from the incumbent administration of Governor Seyi Makinde, while Alli, who represents Oyo South in the Senate, emerged as the party’s consensus governorship candidate in May.

In Ogun State, Adeola, who represents Ogun West Senatorial District, is the APC’s governorship candidate, while Hamzat, the incumbent deputy governor of Lagos State, is flying the party’s flag in the 2027 governorship election.

The endorsements placed the three governorship contests alongside Tinubu’s re-election campaign as the APC seeks to strengthen its political presence across the South-West.
