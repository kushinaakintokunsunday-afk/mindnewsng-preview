Kano targets $700m sesame, hibiscus exports by 2027

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Kano State exported sesame seeds and hibiscus flowers worth more than $350m in 2025, an official of the Nigerian Export Promotion Council has said. The State Co

The State Coordinator of the NEPC, Yusuf Ahmed, disclosed this on Monday during the National Single Window Phase Two Sensitisation and Joint Stakeholders Forum in Kano.

Ahmed expressed optimism that the value of the state’s exports of the two commodities could double to $700m by 2027 with the commencement of the National Single Window project, expected to begin operations in December.

“Kano exported over $350 million worth of Sesame and Hibiscus alone in 2025. But we can double this to $700 million by 2027 if documentation becomes easier.

“National Single Window phase two is the tool to double it,” he said.

Ahmed described Kano as Nigeria’s leading exporter of sesame seeds, hibiscus flowers and leather.

He commended Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf for supporting non-oil exports in the state, while acknowledging the contributions of other export regulatory agencies to the sector.

The NEPC coordinator urged exporters to participate actively in the engagement by asking questions and sharing their experiences to help achieve the project’s objectives.

Earlier, the NEPC North-West Regional Coordinator, Amina Abdulmalik, described the forum as timely, noting that Kano was a major commercial centre in northern Nigeria and accounted for more than 40 per cent of the region’s non-oil exports.

She listed sesame, hibiscus flowers, ginger, groundnuts, onions, cowpeas, chilli peppers and leather among the region’s major non-oil export products.

Abdulmalik, however, said exporters continued to face delays, multiple charges and repeated physical examinations at different government agency offices.

“This forum is timely and strategic. Kano State is the commercial nerve center of commerce in Northern Nigeria, contributing over 40% of non-oil exports from the North: Sesame, Hibiscus Flower (Zobo), Ginger, Groundnut, Onions, Cowpea, Chilli Pepper, and Leather.

“Yet our exporters face delays, multiple charges, and physical examination at every agency desk. National Single Window is the Federal Government’s answer to that problem,” she said.

The engagement is part of the Federal Government’s efforts to sensitise stakeholders, deepen understanding and encourage participation in the implementation of the National Single Window project.

The initiative is designed to establish a centralised electronic trade platform that will streamline import and export processes by integrating and harmonising documentation and procedures across government agencies and other stakeholders.

The National Single Window is a Federal Government initiative aimed at reducing the administrative burden associated with international trade by allowing traders to submit required information and documents through a single electronic platform rather than dealing separately with multiple agencies.

The system is expected to improve coordination among government agencies, reduce delays and make trade documentation more transparent and efficient.

For exporters in Kano and other parts of northern Nigeria, improved processing could help reduce the time and costs involved in moving agricultural commodities and other products to international markets.
