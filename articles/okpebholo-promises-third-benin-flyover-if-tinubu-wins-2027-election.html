Okpebholo Promises Third Benin Flyover If Tinubu Wins 2027 Election

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 3 min read

Edo State Governor, Monday Okpebholo, has pledged to commence the construction of a third flyover bridge in Benin City linking Agbor Road to Sakponba Road in Ikpoba-Okha Local Government Area, if President Bola Tinubu wins the 2027 presidential election.

Okpebholo made the promise during the All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally in Ikpoba-Okha, after leading the party’s campaign in Orhionmwon Local Government Area.

He said the proposed flyover would improve access to the area for residents and businesses but noted that its construction would depend on financial support from the Federal Government.

“I promise to start a flyover bridge from the back of Guinness to Sakpoba Road in Ikpoba-Okha. I need money to do this, and I can only do it with the support of President Tinubu,” the governor said, urging residents to vote for Tinubu and other APC candidates in the 2027 general election.

The governor also urged residents to support APC candidates, whom he described as intelligent, credible and competent, saying they would work with his administration to sustain development in the state.

Deputy Governor Dennis Idahosa thanked residents of Orhionmwon and Ikpoba-Okha for their support and urged them to vote for Tinubu and other APC candidates to sustain development.

Former Edo governor and Senator representing Edo North, Adams Oshiomhole, also urged voters to prioritise competence and credibility over ethnic considerations, saying the APC had performed well at both state and federal levels.

Former deputy governor Pius Odubu assured residents of continued development under Okpebholo, while APC leader in Edo South, Osagie Ize-Iyamu, commended the governor for the commencement of electricity projects in Orhionmwon, which he said had been without reliable electricity for about 20 years.

Director-General of the Presidential Campaign Council, Senator Matthew Urhoghide, praised the governor for what he described as improved security and called on residents to help deliver 2.5 million votes for Tinubu.

He also described the President’s reforms as a pathway to national growth.

APC State Chairman Jarrett Tenebe said the party had gained new members in Orhionmwon and remained united.

He added that cultism, kidnapping and other criminal activities had reduced across the state.

Tenebe attributed the party’s unity to Okpebholo’s leadership and urged members to work together to secure victory in the 2027 elections.

Deputy State Chairman Sylvester Aigboboh said Orhionmwon and Ikpoba-Okha had benefited from more than 50 development projects, urging residents to vote for Tinubu and other APC candidates to sustain the development.

The Ramat Park Flyover at Ikpoba Hill is the state’s first flyover.

It links the Benin-Auchi and Benin-Asaba highways.

The project also includes road expansion, improved drainage, and the redevelopment of a recreational park.

The second project, the Adesuwa Junction Flyover along Sapele Road, is designed to ease traffic congestion at the busy intersection connecting the Benin-Sapele-Warri corridor.

Approved in April 2025 and subsequently flagged off, construction is ongoing alongside the wider reconstruction of Sapele Road.
