A’Ibom gov seeks 80,000 LG votes for Tinubu, APC

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Akwa Ibom State Governor, Umo Eno, has tasked voters in Mkpat Enin Local Government Area with delivering 80,000 votes for President Bola Tinubu and other All Pr

Eno, who is seeking re-election on the APC platform, made the demand on Monday at the kick-off of the party’s campaign tour of the local government, where he urged residents to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards and mobilise support for the party.

The governor’s vote target came alongside promises of fresh development projects, including the reconstruction of the abandoned Ikot Akata Technical College and Ukam Market, for which he said he had directed the constitution of an emergency implementation committee.

According to a statement issued by the Akwa Ibom APC Campaign Council’s Media Directorate on Monday, Eno said his administration had undertaken several infrastructure and empowerment projects in Mkpat Enin and would consolidate the interventions if returned to office.

He listed about 94 kilometres of roads, bridges, a model primary school at Ikot Ebak, a model primary healthcare centre at Ikot Ekpe, renovated and newly constructed classroom blocks, and a solar-powered water facility at Ukpom Minya among projects executed in the area.

The governor also cited the ARISE Youth Friendly Centre, which is nearing completion, 13 ARISE Compassionate Homes and empowerment programmes for farmers, traders and small business owners.

He said beneficiaries of the administration’s empowerment schemes included recipients of ₦5m grants, while 210 residents had benefited from the mega empowerment programme. Two minibuses and a car had also been distributed.

Eno further announced plans to distribute 50 bags of rice to every village in the local government for Christmas, amounting to more than 5,000 bags.

He urged residents to support Tinubu and APC candidates in the National Assembly elections scheduled for January 16 and the governorship and state House of Assembly elections on February 6.

The Deputy Governor, Akon Eyakenyi, described the state as united under Eno and urged the people to sustain their engagement with the administration.

The Coordinator of the Office of the First Lady, Helen Obareki, said the governor had fulfilled commitments to his late wife’s community.

Also, the wife of the Senate President, Ekaette-Unoama Akpabio, who represented her husband, Godswill Akpabio, urged voters to identify with the APC during the elections.

The Director-General of the campaign council, Assam Assam, said Mkpat Enin had an estimated 80,000 registered voters and called on them to support APC candidates at all levels.

The rally marked the beginning of the APC campaign council’s tour of the state’s 31 local government areas.
