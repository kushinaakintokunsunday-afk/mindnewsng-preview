Youth minister weds in Ondo

By Punch Punch 3d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigeria’s Minister of Youth Development, Ayodele Olawande, has married his partner, Stella Elegbe, in Akure, the Ondo State capital. The white wedding ceremony

The white wedding ceremony took place on Saturday at the Christ Apostolic Church, Revival Centre, Akure, and was followed by a reception at the Dr Olusegun Mimiko Events Centre, popularly known as The Dome.

The wedding was the culmination of a series of ceremonies held by the couple over three days.

They had their marriage registry on Thursday, November 19, followed by their traditional engagement on Friday, November 20, before the church wedding on Saturday.

Family members, friends, political associates and government officials attended the ceremonies to celebrate the couple as they began their life together.

Among those who attended was Ondo State Governor, Lucky Aiyedatiwa, who was at the wedding reception.

The Lagos State Commissioner for Youth and Social Development, Mobolaji Ogunlende, also attended and said he represented Lagos State Governor, Babajide Sanwo-Olu, at the ceremony.

In a congratulatory message shared after the event, Ogunlende expressed the Lagos State Government’s goodwill towards the newly married couple, describing the occasion as an important milestone in the minister’s life.

He wrote, “Today, I had the honour of representing the Governor of Lagos State, Mr. Babajide Olusola Sanwo-Olu, at the Holy Matrimony ceremony of the Honourable Minister of Youth Development, Comrade Ayodele Olawande, and his beautiful bride in Akure, Ondo State.

“On behalf of the Governor and the people of Lagos State, I extend my heartfelt congratulations to the Honourable Minister and his wife on this beautiful milestone.

“May this union be blessed with enduring love, peace, understanding, joy and abundant blessings. May their home be filled with happiness, and may their journey together be one of lifelong companionship, grace and fulfilment.”

Also present at the wedding was the Minister of State for Petroleum Resources (Oil), Heineken Lokpobiri, who joined family members and friends to witness the exchange of marital vows.

Lokpobiri, in a message shared on X, celebrated Olawande and his wife, describing the occasion as the beginning of a new chapter in their lives. He also offered prayers for the couple, wishing them a marriage filled with love, companionship and God’s blessings.

He wrote, “I was delighted to join family and friends in witnessing the exchange of marital vows between the Honourable Minister of Youth Development, Hon. Ayodele Olawande, and his adorable wife, Mrs Stella Ifeoluwa Elegbe, as they embarked on a beautiful new chapter of their lives together.

“There is something truly beautiful about two people discovering themselves in love, choosing each other, and committing to a lifetime of shared joy, blessings, companionship and forever.

“Today, my friend and brother, the Honourable Minister, is that happy man, blessed with a wonderful woman to call his wife.

“I pray that God’s grace will continually abound in their home, that His blessings will never cease, and that every beautiful promise and blessing that comes with the gift of marriage will be theirs in abundance and beyond.”

Olawande currently serves as Nigeria’s Minister of Youth Development under President Bola Tinubu’s administration.

He is from Ondo State and has been involved in youth mobilisation and political activities before his appointment to the Federal Executive Council.

The wedding took place in his home state, with the couple’s civil, traditional and church ceremonies held in Akure.
