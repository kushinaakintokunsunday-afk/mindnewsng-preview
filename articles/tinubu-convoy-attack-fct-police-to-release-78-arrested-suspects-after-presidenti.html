Tinubu convoy attack: FCT police to release 78 arrested suspects after presidential clemency

From Molly Kilete, Abuja

The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Police Command said it is set to release all 78 suspects arrested last Friday over the attack on the motorcade of President Bola Tinubu at a mosque in Garki District of Abuja.

The Police Public Relations Officer, Josephine Adeh, who made this known, said their release followed the extension of presidential clemency to the individuals involved in the attack.

Adeh, in a statement, said the Presidency had directed that the suspects arrested in connection with the incident be warned and released.

The statement reads: “The FCT Police Command wishes to inform the public that seventy-eight (78) suspects were arrested following the attack on the motorcade of the President and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, His Excellency, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, GCFR, on Friday, 13th November 2026, at a mosque in Garki District, Abuja.

“The incident occurred shortly after the President observed Jumu’ah prayers at a mosque in Garki, when some individuals attacked the departing convoy with stones and other dangerous objects, damaging several vehicles and causing injuries to some security operatives.

“A joint security team, led by the Police, swiftly responded to the incident, while subsequent intelligence-led raids within the area led to further arrests, bringing the total number of suspects arrested in connection with the incident to seventy-eight (78).

“Following the extension of presidential clemency to the individuals involved in the attack, the Presidency directed that the suspects arrested in connection with the incident be warned and released. In compliance with the directive, the Command wishes to inform the public that the suspects are currently undergoing the necessary release processes.

“The Commissioner of Police, FCT Command, CP Ahmed Mohammed Sanusi, PhD, warns against acts of violence, public disorder and destruction of property. As preparations for the 2027 General Elections intensify, the Command urges all political parties, political actors and their supporters to embrace peaceful political engagement and shun violence, intimidation, inflammatory statements and incitement.

“The CP has consequently directed all Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and heads of tactical units to remain vigilant and intensify security operations to ensure the safety of residents and visitors. Residents are also urged to remain security conscious and promptly report suspicious persons and activities to the nearest Police station or through the Command’s emergency lines: 08032003913, 08061581938.”
