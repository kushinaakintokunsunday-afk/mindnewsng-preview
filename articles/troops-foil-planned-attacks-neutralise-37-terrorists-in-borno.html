Troops foil planned attacks, neutralise 37 terrorists in Borno

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 3 min read

Troops of the Joint Task Force (North East), Operation HADIN KAI (OPHK), have neutralised 37 terrorists and destroyed four terrorist vehicles in a series of intelligence-led precision air strikes across Borno State.

The operations, conducted by the Air Component of OPHK between November 16 and 23, 2026, also led to the destruction of a terrorist staging facility and disrupted suspected Improvised Explosive Device (IED) activities along troops’ operational routes.

In a statement issued on Monday in Maiduguri, the Acting Media Information Officer, Headquarters Joint Task Force (North East), Operation HADIN KAI, Captain Mohammed Goni, said six precision air strikes were conducted across five locations within the theatre of operations.

He said the strikes resulted in the neutralisation of 37 terrorists, as confirmed by Battle Damage Assessment (BDA), and the destruction of four terrorist vehicles.

According to him, the operations were carried out based on credible intelligence and sustained Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (ISR), targeting terrorist movements, hideouts and logistics assets across Monguno Local Government Area, Kerenoa and Fage in Marte LGA, and the Tumbuma Baba general area of Kukawa LGA.

Giving details of the operations, Goni said on November 16, troops launched a pre-emptive strike following verified intelligence that about 40 terrorists were moving north-east of Monguno to attack troops.

He said the subsequent BDA confirmed that 10 terrorists were neutralised, while several others sustained injuries.

On November 17, an ISR asset conducting surveillance over Arina Chiki identified a terrorist staging facility where insurgents were reportedly planning an attack on troops’ defensive positions.

The location was subsequently engaged with precision fire, destroying the targeted building, with no further movement observed within the structure.

Goni said that on November 20, an Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition and Reconnaissance (ISTAR) asset monitoring Kerenoa spotted two terrorist vehicles parked near a tree.

The vehicles were engaged in a precision strike and subsequently confirmed destroyed.

Similarly, an ISTAR asset operating over the Fage general area on the same day identified a terrorist vehicle at an IED assembly location, where preparations were reportedly underway to plant explosives along a troop movement route.

He said the vehicle was immediately engaged and destroyed, disrupting the terrorists’ suspected IED operations in the area.

The military spokesman further disclosed that on November 23, an ISTAR asset monitoring the Tumbuma Baba general area identified another terrorist vehicle following intelligence reports of terrorist activities in the location.

The vehicle was subsequently destroyed in a precision air strike, as confirmed by BDA.

He added that surveillance later tracked two suspected terrorists moving from a nearby structure to join six others taking cover under a tree.

After additional terrorists joined the gathering, the military launched another precision air strike, which, according to the BDA, neutralised all eight terrorists at the location.

Operation HADIN KAI commended the professionalism of its air and intelligence components, reaffirming its commitment to sustained offensive operations in collaboration with ground forces to defeat terrorism and restore lasting peace in the North-East.
