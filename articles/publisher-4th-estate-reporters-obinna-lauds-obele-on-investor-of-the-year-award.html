Publisher, 4th Estate Reporters, Obinna, lauds Obele on Investor of the Year award

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Media expert, public analyst, and the Publisher, 4th Estate Reporters, Njoku Macdonald Obinna, has joined other prominent Nigerians to congratulate High Chief Anthony Obele on his recognition by The Sun Publishing Limited as the Investor of the Year 2026.

Speaking on the significance of the recognition and the broader implications of the 24th edition of the award, Obinna, who attended the ceremony, described the recognition of Dr. Obele as a significant moment for indigenous entrepreneurship and investment in Nigeria.

He noted that the true value of investment should not be measured exclusively by financial returns but, also, by its impact on infrastructure, employment generation, economic empowerment, and community development.

He stated: “The recognition of High Chief Dr. Anthony Ikenna Obele as Investor of the Year particularly draws attention to the importance of indigenous investment in our national development.

“I strongly believe that Nigeria’s economic transformation cannot be left to the government alone. We need visionary entrepreneurs and investors who are willing to commit resources, build businesses, create employment opportunities, and participate in addressing the infrastructure challenges confronting our communities.

“One thing that stands out about this recognition is the opportunity it provides us to look beyond the award itself and examine the responsibilities that come with investment. Building a successful business is commendable, but building an enterprise that contributes to the economy and creates opportunities for other people is even more significant.

“High Chief Obele’s recognition should encourage more Nigerian entrepreneurs, particularly young people, to appreciate the importance of productive investment, innovation, local capacity development, and long-term economic planning.

“An award is an honour, but the greatest achievement is the legacy an individual leaves behind through the lives touched, opportunities created, and communities positively impacted,” he said.
