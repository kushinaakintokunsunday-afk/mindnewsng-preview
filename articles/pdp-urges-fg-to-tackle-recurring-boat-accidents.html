PDP urges FG to tackle recurring boat accidents

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Peoples Democratic Party has called on the Federal Government to take urgent steps to address the recurring boat accidents across the country, following the

The party condemned what it described as the government’s failure to address the persistent safety challenges on Nigeria’s waterways, including overloading of passenger boats, poor maintenance, lack of life jackets and weak enforcement of maritime safety regulations.

In a press statement issued on Sunday and signed by its National Publicity Secretary, Ini Ememobong, the PDP said Nigerians needed decisive action rather than routine condolence messages from the All Progressives Congress-led Federal Government.

It described the recurring boat accidents as evidence of government neglect of the maritime transportation sector.

The accident occurred on Friday, November 20, 2026, when a passenger boat conveying mourners from Ayama to a burial ceremony in Ukubie capsized near the river junction between Oweikorogha and Otuan communities in Bayelsa State.

According to the statement, the National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA) confirmed at least 24 fatalities, while search-and-rescue operations continued for those still missing.

Eyewitness accounts reportedly put the number of passengers on board at approximately 300.

Expressing sympathy with the bereaved families, the PDP said, “We extend our heartfelt condolences to the families who lost loved ones on a journey meant to honour the dead. We pray for the quick recovery of the survivors receiving treatment in Ayama and Yenagoa.”

The party also commended divers, marine police officers and community members involved in the rescue operations, noting that they had risked their lives in efforts to save victims.

However, the PDP argued that the tragedy was not an isolated incident but part of a disturbing pattern of boat accidents across Nigeria, insisting that the Federal Government must move beyond issuing condolence messages and address the underlying causes.

“For a country with 853 km of coastline and a vast network of inland waterways, maritime transport deserves far more attention than this government is giving it,” the statement said.

The opposition party maintained that Nigeria needed effective maritime safety laws and properly equipped enforcement agencies to prevent further loss of lives.

It specifically urged the Federal Government to accelerate the passage of a coast guard law, beginning with the reconsideration of the Nigeria Coast Guard (Establishment) Bill, 2026 (SB. 575), which the Senate stepped down on July 22, 2026, for wider consultations.

The PDP argued that the government should demonstrate the same urgency it showed in the passage of the state police bill in addressing maritime security and safety concerns.

It further called for adequate funding of the Marine Police and the Nigerian Navy to enable them to deploy personnel, boats and other necessary equipment for effective enforcement of safety regulations on the waterways.

The party also urged the government to direct the National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA) to collaborate with relevant agencies in enforcing passenger limits, conducting regular boat inspections and ensuring compulsory use of life jackets.

Additionally, it demanded a transparent investigation into the Bayelsa boat accident, insisting that the findings should be made public.

” Nigerians deserve a more responsive and responsible government than the one they have now,” the statement declared.

The PDP concluded by calling on Nigerians to make their voices count in the political process, noting that January 16, 2027, would provide an opportunity for citizens to make a choice about the kind of leadership they want.
