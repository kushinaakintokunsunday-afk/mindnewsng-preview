Don’t take sides, Bishop Okonkwo tells Fountain of Life Church members

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Okonkwo, who made a surprise appearance at the church, said he was prompted by the Holy Spirit to visit and bless the congregation amid the controversy that has rocked the church in the past two weeks.

“I have not slept since 1.30 am. The Holy Spirit said to me, son, go to the Fountain and bless them. That’s why I am here,” he said.

Recalling the death of the church’s founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, Okonkwo described the period as traumatic, saying the late cleric’s legacy must be preserved.

.

He said, “And the only thing is that his legacy must continue. And I told you that day that Fountain will never die.”

The bishop, who said the church had been battered by social media discussions in the past one or two weeks, noted that he could not separate himself from the church because of the covenant he believed God had established.

“You can’t separate me from Fountain. It’s not possible. Because it is a covenant that God himself created by himself. So what happens to Fountain affects me,” he said.

Preaching on the topic, “The Glory of the Church”, Okonkwo urged members to remember that the church belonged to God and was not a human institution or political party.

He referenced biblical passages, including Jonah 2:8, 2 Corinthians 2:14, 1 Peter 1:18, Ephesians 1:7 and Colossians 1:14, to emphasise the price paid for the redemption of the church.

Addressing the leadership dispute, he told members not to identify with either of the two sides but to remain committed to God.

“You are not for Tolu or for Jimmy. You are for God. None of them died for you,” he said.

He also warned members against becoming instruments of division in the church.

“We must be careful so that none of us will be an instrument of destroying the church of Christ,” he said.

Okonkwo expressed confidence in the future of the church, saying its growth would continue despite the current challenges.

“Whatever you have seen so far is nothing compared to what is coming,” he said, adding that everything associated with God was progressive.

The appearance came minutes after Jimmy Odukoya received a rousing welcome from members at the church’s Sunday service, with his arrival triggering loud cheers that almost disrupted the praise session.

The leadership dispute became public on November 17 when a faction of the church’s Board of Trustees announced that it no longer recognised Jimmy as Senior Pastor, citing a succession video reportedly recorded by the late founder in December 2021.

The trustees named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader, a decision Jimmy and the church leadership rejected.

The dispute has also led to court proceedings, with the Lagos State High Court issuing an interim order restraining Jimmy from presenting himself as the church’s Senior Pastor and Spiritual Head. The matter was adjourned until December 1 for mention.
