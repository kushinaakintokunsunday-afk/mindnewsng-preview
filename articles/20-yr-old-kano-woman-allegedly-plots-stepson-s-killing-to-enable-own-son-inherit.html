20-yr-old Kano woman allegedly plots stepson’s killing to enable own son inherit father’s properties

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 3 min read

The Kano State Police Command has arrested a 20-year-old woman, Hajiya Rukayya Auwal, and three others over the alleged conspiracy, abduction and culpable homicide of her 14-year-old stepson in an alleged plot to make her son the sole heir to her husband’s properties.

Police Public Relations Officer (PPRO) of the command, CSP Abdullahi Haruna Kiyawa disclosed this in a Tuesday statement.

According to the command, at about 4:39pm on April 29, 2026, a female resident of Danbare Quarters, Kumbotso LGA, reported that her biological son, Mahmoud Auwal, aged 14, of Sheka Quarters, Kano State, had been abducted.

“Upon receipt of the complaint, and on the direction of the Commissioner of Police, operatives of the Command’s Anti-Kidnapping Squad immediately swung into action,” the statement reads in part.

Following sustained investigative efforts and intelligence-led operations, at about 10:30am on November 3, 2026, one Muhammad Musa Isma’il, aged 20, of Sharada Quarters, Kano State, was arrested.

“During interrogation, the suspect confessed to deceiving and luring the victim on 20th April, 2026, at about 12:30pm. He further named the following persons as his accomplices: Abubakar Muhammad Isah, ‘M’, aged 27 years, of Semegu Quarters, Kano; and Auwal Muhammad, ‘M’, aged 26 years, of Dan-Agundi Quarters, Kano.

“He alleged that they conspired to kill the victim and abandoned his corpse at the scene. The corpse was later recovered by Police operatives attached to Kwalli Division while on patrol, and conveyed to Sir Muhammad Sunusi Specialist Hospital Mortuary for preservation,” the statement further reads.

All the suspects have since been arrested.

During further investigation, the suspects disclosed that they acted on the directives of the alleged mastermind and principal suspect, one Hajiya Rukayya Auwal, aged 20, of Mararaba Quarters, New Karu LGA, Nasarawa State, who was subsequently arrested.

“Discreet investigation further revealed that the principal suspect allegedly hired another set of thugs who attacked Muhammad Musa Isma’il at Mararaba, Karu LGA, Nasarawa State, in an attempt to silence him and prevent him from exposing the alleged crime,” the command said.

Further investigation revealed that the principal suspect is a co-wife of the deceased’s mother.

“The alleged motive, as disclosed during investigation, was to eliminate all four children of her husband, so that her only son would in future solely inherit his father’s properties, even though the husband is still alive,” the police said.

The command said the case would be charged to court upon completion of investigation.

The Commissioner of Police, Kano State Command, CP Ibrahim Adamu Bakori, condemned the act in the strongest terms and assured the family of the deceased and the general public that the command would leave no stone unturned in ensuring that justice is served.

The command appealed to members of the public to continue to support the police with timely and credible information that will aid in crime prevention and detection.
