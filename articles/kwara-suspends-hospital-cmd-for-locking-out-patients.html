Kwara suspends hospital CMD for locking out patients

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Kwara State Government has suspended the Chief Medical Director of the Children’s Specialist Hospital, Centre Igboro, Ilorin, Mosunmola Folorunsho, followin

The suspension followed an emergency visit to the hospital by the Executive Secretary of the Kwara State Hospitals Management Board, Dr Abdulraheem Malik, and members of the management team to investigate the circumstances surrounding the incident.

In a statement issued on Tuesday by the board’s Press Secretary, Adamu Sabi, the management said the CMD had also been issued a query to explain her role in the incident within 48 hours.

The board further directed the suspended medical director to hand over the administration of the hospital to the most senior doctor pending further directives.

Explaining the circumstances that led to the disruption, the management said its findings showed that the hospital experienced a temporary shortage of doctors after one of the medical personnel scheduled for morning duty was absent to attend a malaria-related training programme.

The absence, according to the statement, contributed to a backlog of patients awaiting medical attention at the facility.

“The Executive Secretary directed the immediate deployment of a doctor from the Board’s pool of medical personnel to fill the gap pending the return of the doctor from training,” the statement read.

Malik also warned the hospital management against preventing patients and their caregivers from accessing the facility, regardless of operational challenges.

“Under no circumstances should the hospital gate be locked in a manner that prevents patients and their caregivers from entering the facility,” the statement quoted the Executive Secretary as directing.

The board attributed the staffing challenge partly to the increasing number of patients seeking medical attention at the hospital, adding that steps were being taken to address the manpower shortage.

According to the statement, the state government had recently approved the recruitment of additional healthcare workers, including doctors and other medical professionals, to strengthen service delivery across public health facilities.

The management, while expressing regret over the inconvenience suffered by affected patients and their caregivers, assured residents that the hospital remained operational and that immediate steps had been taken to prevent a recurrence.

It added that the welfare and safety of patients, particularly children, remained a priority, promising to strengthen staff deployment, monitoring and supervision across its facilities.

The board also reaffirmed its commitment to accountability, professionalism and strict adherence to administrative procedures, urging residents to continue accessing medical services at the Children’s Specialist Hospital without fear of interruption.
