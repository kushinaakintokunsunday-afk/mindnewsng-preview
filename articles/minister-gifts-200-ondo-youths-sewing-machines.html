Minister gifts 200 Ondo youths sewing machines

By Punch Punch 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Minister of Youth Development, Ayodele Olawande, has distributed industrial sewing machines to 200 youths in Ondo State as part of a planned empowerment pro

Olawande said the initiative was designed to support young people with practical skills and help them create employment opportunities for themselves.

The minister distributed the machines in Akure, the state capital, on Friday, describing the gesture as part of his wedding gifts to youths in the state.

He got married on Saturday.

“We started with 200, and we are still going further. I’m getting married, and I believe I should do that with impact,” he said.

He explained that the initiative was informed by the needs of fashion designers who require industrial sewing machines to run their businesses.

“I realised that many fashion designers need industrial sewing machines. So, within Akure metropolis and extending to other parts of Ondo State, we decided to embark on this initiative,” he said.

Olawande said the programme would be extended to other local government areas, including Ifedore, Ese-Odo and Owo, as the ministry works towards reaching its target of 1,000 beneficiaries.

“We intend to meet the target of 1,000 beneficiaries. We will also extend the initiative to people in other occupations, including bricklayers. I really want to target people who work with their hands,” he added.

The minister said the programme reflected his belief in skills acquisition and the need to support people with practical abilities.

He also linked the initiative to the Federal Government’s Renewed Hope Agenda, saying employment should not be limited to white-collar jobs.

“The President and all of us are preaching the same message, not every job has to be a white-collar job,” he said.

Olawande cited his experience learning photography as an example of how vocational skills could create opportunities, recalling that President Bola Tinubu had given him the opportunity to acquire the skill.

“For instance, if President Bola Ahmed Tinubu had not given me the opportunity to learn photography, I might not have become a photographer today,” he said.

One of the beneficiaries, Ayoluwa, thanked the minister, saying the sewing machine came at a time when her previous equipment had developed a fault.

“This is a very big testimony for me. Just last week, my sewing machine developed a fault that appeared almost impossible to repair. I was already considering buying a fairly used machine, but someone advised me against it,” she said.

Ayoluwa said she was surprised when a friend asked her to check her phone and she discovered that her name and address were on the list of beneficiaries.

“This means a lot to me because it has saved me from a lot of stress and financial challenges. I also want to thank the honourable minister,” she added.
