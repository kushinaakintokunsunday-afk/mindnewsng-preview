Fountain of Life crisis: Trustees release Odukoya’s succession video, name interim leader

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Board of Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church, Lagos, has released a video in which its late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, named Pastor Kunle Areogun as

In the video, posted by Arise TV and sighted by our correspondent on Tuesday, Odukoya said Areogun should take over if he dies or becomes incapacitated, while his son, Jimmy, would serve as the successor’s administrative assistant and head of the youth church.

“In case of my demise, which I don’t expect to happen soon, or a situation where I cannot write or talk, please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun,” Odukoya said.

He added, “To be his immediate assistant, that is in terms of administration, is Jimmy Odukoya, who will be the head of the youth church.”

The late founder said, “Everything remains the same and that’s laid out in the constitution.”

Odukoya explained that he was making the pronouncement because of an aspect of the constitution they were trying to rework which had not been concluded at the time.

“We’re going to do that, but if for any reason I’m not able to see that through, this is what I do,” he said.

The trustees said the video was recorded in December 2021 at the founder’s request by Jimmy and witnessed by his elder brother, Pastor John Odukoya.

They said they did not see it until late December 2025, more than two years after the founder’s death.

In a statement on Tuesday, the board said it had appointed Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim leader pending consultations.

It also reaffirmed its resolution of November 16 that Jimmy was no longer recognised as Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church.

The trustees alleged that Jimmy told the board in 2023 that his father had handed the mantle of leadership to him.

They said the video was not presented when the succession was considered.

PUNCH ONLINE had earlier reported that the church rejected the removal.

In a statement earlier on Tuesday, it described the decision as constitutionally invalid and said Jimmy remains the duly appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees.

The church’s counterstatement also alleged that the trustees behind the decision had been asked to resign in March 2026 over alleged violations of the church’s constitution.

It said Jimmy’s appointment in September 2023 was unanimously approved.

The trustees alleged that the church’s official social media channels had been taken over without the board’s authorisation. They warned the public against treating posts on those platforms as the board’s position.

Jimmy, a Nollywood actor, was announced as senior pastor on September 17, 2023, weeks after his father’s death.

The announcement was made by Okpaise at a church service, and the board said it had unanimously elected him.

He was installed on September 30, 2023, with his sister, Pastor Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, as associate senior pastor.

The church was founded in 1992 by Pastors Taiwo and Bimbo Odukoya and is headquartered in Ilupeju, Lagos.
