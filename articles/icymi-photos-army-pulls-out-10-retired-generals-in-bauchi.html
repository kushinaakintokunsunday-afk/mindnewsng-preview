[ICYMI]: PHOTOS: Army pulls out 10 retired generals in Bauchi

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Nigerian Army Armoured Corps has formally pulled out 10 retired senior officers from service at a colourful ceremony held at the Brigadier General Y. Abubak

The ceremony, which took place on Saturday, was the climax of the week-long 2026 Nigerian Army Armoured Corps Biennial Conference and Reunion.

The event brought together serving and retired officers of the Corps from across the country to celebrate the distinguished careers and contributions of the retired generals.

According to a statement issued on Sunday by the Acting Deputy Director, Army Public Relations, Headquarters Nigerian Army Armoured Corps, Major Adekunle Akinleye, the officers honoured were Lieutenant General Abdulsalami Ibrahim, Major Generals Benjamin Sawyerr, Ibrahim Ali, Sani Mohammed and Ibrahim Jallo, as well as Brigadier Generals Victor Ita, Tuni Isa, Aminu Usman, Ezra Barkins and Suleiman Adamu.

Speaking at the ceremony as Special Guest of Honour and Reviewing Officer, retired Lieutenant General Abdulsalami Ibrahim described the Armoured Corps as more than just a military formation, saying it had become a way of life for him and other officers who served in the Corps for decades.

“Armour is not only a branch of the Corps, but it is a way of life,” he declared.

He urged serving officers, particularly the younger generation, to uphold the values of courage, discipline and professionalism in the discharge of their responsibilities, stressing that they represented the future of the Corps.

The retired general also expressed appreciation to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, and the Nigerian Army authorities for the confidence reposed in him and his colleagues throughout their military careers.

He further commended everyone who contributed to the successful organisation of the ceremony.

The event was attended by the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, who was represented by the Commander Infantry Corps, Major General Godwin Mutkut.

Other senior officers present included the Commander Armoured Corps, Major General Umar Abubakar; General Officer Commanding 3 Division and Commander Operation Enduring Peace, Major General Maxwell Dangana; Corps Commander Ordnance, Major General Abdullahi Ibrahim; Chief of Personnel Management, Major General Adewale Adekeye; Chief of Special Services and Projects, Major General Aminu Garba; and Commandant Nigerian Army Armour School, Major General Sunday Makolo.

Serving officers, soldiers, family members, friends, well-wishers and members of the barracks community also attended the ceremony to celebrate the retired generals.

Highlights of the event included a ceremonial march past, the traditional pulling-out parade, presentation of souvenirs and group photographs, marking the formal end of the officers’ active military service.
