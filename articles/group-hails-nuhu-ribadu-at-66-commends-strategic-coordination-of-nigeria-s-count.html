Group hails Nuhu Ribadu at 66, commends strategic coordination of Nigeria’s counter-terrorism effort

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 2d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Citizens’ Safety & Security Network (CSSN) has congratulated Mallam Nuhu Ribadu on his 66th birthday, describing his stewardship as National Security Advise

The Citizens’ Safety & Security Network (CSSN) has congratulated Mallam Nuhu Ribadu on his 66th birthday, describing his stewardship as National Security Adviser as a defining period in Nigeria’s renewed campaign against terrorism, insurgency, and banditry.

In a statement issued by its Convener, Arc. Timothy Danladi Usman, the group noted that President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s appointment of Ribadu in 2023 marked a deliberate departure from the longstanding tradition of reserving the Office of the National Security Adviser almost exclusively for retired senior military officers.

The decision, CSSN said, reflected a strategic vision to bring intelligence expertise, policing experience, and economic-crime-fighting acumen to the apex of Nigeria’s security architecture at a time when terrorists and bandits increasingly relied on illicit funding, logistics networks, and cross-border alliances.

According to the statement, the past three years have witnessed a profound shift characterised by intelligence-driven precision, improved inter-agency harmony, deliberate disruption of criminal financing, and the pragmatic restoration of international partnerships.

The results, CSSN observed, include the neutralisation of thousands of terrorists and bandit elements, the elimination or capture of high-value leaders across multiple states, the near-decimation of large-scale crude oil theft in the Niger Delta, and a reinvigorated Nigeria–United States security partnership that has delivered significant operational gains.

The group highlighted several high-impact successes under Ribadu’s coordination. In May 2026, a precision joint operation with the United States Africa Command in the Lake Chad Basin neutralised Abu-Bilal al-Minuki (also known as Abu-Mainok), described as the global second-in-command and director of operations for ISIS.

In the Northwest, security forces systematically degraded bandit networks that had terrorised Zamfara, Katsina, Sokoto, and Kaduna States.

Notable figures eliminated include Kachalla Halilu Sububu (also referred to as Halilu Buzu) in a targeted operation in September 2024; Alti, the so-called “TikTok bandit commander,” in early 2026; Dan Karami in a joint military operation in April 2025; and multiple other Kachalla warlords and lieutenants whose camps in Shinkafi, Isa, and surrounding areas of Zamfara were dismantled in coordinated raids.

In the Northeast, CSSN noted continued degradation of ISWAP and residual Boko Haram networks, with senior commanders and hundreds of fighters neutralised in Borno and Yobe States.

Overall, public reports indicate that well over 13,000 terrorists, insurgents, and associated criminal elements have been neutralised since the new security strategy took firm root.

Perhaps the most transformative contribution, the group stated, has been the deliberate fostering of synergy across Nigeria’s security and intelligence agencies.

For years, inter-agency rivalry and siloed operations had produced duplicated efforts, intelligence hoarding, and, in some cases, botched missions.

Under the Office of the National Security Adviser, the Armed Forces, Nigeria Police Force, Department of State Services, National Intelligence Agency, and other stakeholders now operate with greater seamlessness through joint planning cells, shared intelligence platforms, and unified command structures.

The result, CSSN said, has been faster, better-informed, and more decisive operations.

Drawing on his tenure as pioneer Chairman of the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission and his background as a police intelligence officer,

Ribadu has also applied forensic financial investigation techniques to disrupt the economic lifelines of terrorist and bandit groups. Extortion, kidnapping-for-ransom, illegal mining, and arms trafficking have been targeted through strengthened collaboration with financial regulators and international partners, complicating weapons procurement and eroding the incentive structures that once sustained prolonged violence.

On the diplomatic front, the group commended Ribadu’s low-key yet professional engagement with American officials, which helped rebuild trust after early concerns about the trajectory of Nigeria–United States relations.

This renewed partnership has already produced concrete dividends, most visibly in the May 2026 elimination of the senior ISIS figure in the Lake Chad region.

Beyond the traditional theatres of insurgency and banditry, CSSN pointed to decisive gains in the Niger Delta. Working with the military, specialised security contractors including Tantita Security Services, and relevant government agencies, the campaign against crude oil theft and illegal refining has reduced theft from levels that once exceeded 100,000 barrels per day to as low as 5,000–9,600 barrels per day in some periods.

This has contributed directly to the rebound in Nigeria’s crude oil production, which has climbed above 1.7 million barrels per day—the highest levels recorded in years—while restoring relative peace that enables communities and legitimate businesses to benefit from increased output.

The group commended President Tinubu for the courage and foresight to entrust security coordination to Mallam Nuhu Ribadu on the basis of results-oriented thinking rather than tradition.

It equally recognised Ribadu for his professional humility, tireless work ethic, and leadership style that combines intelligence expertise, financial acumen, diplomatic skill, and insistence on inter-agency unity.

While celebrating the substantial gains of the past three years, the group cautioned against complacency. Remnants of terrorist networks, surviving bandit groups, and emerging threats require sustained pressure so that every geopolitical zone—Northeast, Northwest, North-Central, Southwest, South-South, and Southeast—ultimately enjoys the same measure of security and normalcy that hard-won progress has begun to restore in key areas.

“Mallam Nuhu Ribadu must continue to build on these foundations by deepening intelligence capabilities, further strengthening inter-agency fusion, expanding regional and international cooperation, and ensuring that no part of Nigeria remains a sanctuary for terror or criminality,” the statement said.

On the occasion of his 66th birthday, the Citizens’ Safety & Security Network prays that God Almighty will grant Mallam Nuhu Ribadu many more years of sound health, wisdom, and strength in the service of God and country.

The group enjoins him to remain steadfast and upright in the discharge of his duties, and never to depart from the just path, integrity, competence, and love for the people that have defined his career and public service life.

Reaffirming its commitment, CSSN pledged continued support to the Office of the National Security Adviser in the collective effort to eradicate insurgency and restore lasting safety across the country.

Three years on, the group concluded, the trajectory is positive. With sustained focus, unity of purpose, and the support of all Nigerians, the vision of a Nigeria where every citizen can live, work, and travel in safety is increasingly within reach.
