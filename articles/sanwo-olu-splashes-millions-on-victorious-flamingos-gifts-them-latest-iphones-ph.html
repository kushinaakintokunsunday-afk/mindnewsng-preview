Sanwo-Olu Splashes Millions On Victorious Flamingos, Gifts Them Latest iPhones [PHOTOS]

Lagos State Governor, Babajide Sanwo-Olu, hosted Nigeria’s history-making Flamingos at a grand dinner reception at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday, where he announced a N300 million reward package for the team.

‎Each of the 21 players and nine members of the coaching and backroom staff will receive N10 million.

‎The U-17 players also received an iPhone 18 Pro Max and customised headphones, while members of the technical crew received bespoke outfits and other gifts.

‎The reception drew government officials, sports administrators, family members and supporters, all of whom turned out to celebrate the newly-minted world champions.

‎Sanwo-Olu congratulated the team for putting Nigeria on the world map, describing their victory as a product of hard work, discipline and belief. NigerianNewspaper Access.

‎He urged the young players not to rest on their laurels but to remain focused on their education and football careers, assuring them that Lagos would continue to support sports development and reward excellence.

‎The governor also praised head coach Akeem Ishola Busari and his technical crew for guiding the team to glory.

‎The Flamingos became the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup after defeating Japan 2-1 in the final in Rabat, Morocco, on November 7.

‎Awawu Bashiru and Oluebube Umejiaku scored the goals that secured Nigeria’s maiden title in the competition.

‎One of the evening’s emotional moments came when captain Harmony Chidi knelt before the governor to express gratitude on behalf of the team.

‎Five members of the squad are from Robo Queens of Mushin, Lagos, highlighting the state’s contribution to the team’s success.

‎The Lagos reward follows the Federal Government’s reception for the team in Abuja, where President Bola Tinubu approved a N100 million Federal Government Savings Bond for each player.

‎The head coach received N100 million in cash, while other members of the technical and administrative crew received N50 million each.
