Bayelsa boat mishap: more bodies recovered on river

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 3d ago 4 min read

Bayelsa Govt, NDC mourn

___________________________________________________________________

From Femi Folaranmi, Yenagoa

Joint search and rescue efforts by the Bayelsa State government and security agencies have rescued more survivors and recovered more bodies after hundreds of people were feared missing following a love boat mishap along the waterways of Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State.

The love boat, which left Ayama carrying mourners to a burial in Ukubie in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area, had a mishap at a river junction between Oweikorogha and Otuan communities.

Investigations revealed that the initial search and rescue efforts conducted by the police and security agencies rescued 24 passengers and recovered four bodies.

Nineteen of the 24 rescued victims were immediately taken to Ayama Hospital, while five in critical condition were taken to Family Care Hospital, Yenagoa, and the four passengers confirmed dead had their bodies deposited at the Federal Medical Centre (FMC).

On Saturday, six bodies were seen floating on the river, while search and rescue efforts recovered more bodies, including two children.

The Bayelsa State Government has stated that it is fully supporting the ongoing joint search and rescue operation and will continue to provide all necessary assistance to the divers, the police and other responders until the operation is concluded.

The Bayelsa State Commissioner for Information, Orientation and Strategy, Mrs Ebiuwou Koku-Obiyai, in a statement on behalf of the state government, condoled with the families of the deceased and assured them that the state government stands with them in their moment of grief.

It seized the opportunity to appeal again to boat operators and passengers across the state to strictly observe safety regulations.

‘Overloading, speeding, recklessness and travelling without life jackets must stop. No journey is worth a life. Operators must respect approved passenger capacity, ensure their boats are in good condition, and avoid night and bad weather travel.

‘The government will continue to work with relevant agencies, community leaders and transport unions to strengthen waterway safety and prevent a recurrence of such tragedies.’

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Bayelsa State, in a statement by its chairman, Hon Dauprebo Ikuromo, has expressed deep sorrow over the boat mishap.

The party, while condoling with the victims and the families of the deceased, said the disaster should serve as a wake-up call for the government to address the persistent dangers associated with water transportation in the predominantly riverine state.

It noted that waterways remain the major means of transportation for several communities in Bayelsa, stressing that the safety of residents who depend on them for their daily activities and livelihoods must be treated as a priority.

The party stated that protecting lives on the waterways requires more than occasional appeals to boat operators and passengers to observe safety regulations.

It emphasised the need for sustained government commitment through effective regulation, routine safety inspections, functional emergency response systems, adequate life-saving equipment and accessible rescue services across the state’s waterways.

The NDC therefore called on the Bayelsa State Government, under Governor Douye Diri, to urgently formulate and implement a comprehensive maritime safety policy to strengthen water transportation, prevent avoidable accidents and protect lives.

The party maintained that the government must take proactive steps to ensure that residents of riverine communities can travel safely without being exposed to unnecessary risks.

Also mourning the victims of the boat mishap, the member of the House of Representatives representing the Southern Ijaw Federal Constituency, Hon Rodney Amabaiowei, commended Bayelsa State Governor, Senator Douye Diri, for his support and the provision of necessary healthcare and other assistance to the victims of the unfortunate incident.

According to him, the timely intervention demonstrates the importance of responsive leadership and government concern for the welfare of its citizens.

Amabaiowei noted that the boat tragedy has again reinforced the urgency of the motion he previously moved at the National Assembly, titled ‘Need to Tackle the Menace of Incessant Boat/Watercraft Mishaps on Our Coastlines and Waterways’.

He stated that the recurring loss of lives through boat mishaps, particularly in riverine communities such as his own, demands urgent and decisive action from relevant government agencies and maritime regulatory authorities.

‘I remain committed to advocating stronger maritime safety regulations, effective monitoring of watercraft operations, enforcement of passenger safety standards and improved safety infrastructure across our waterways to prevent further avoidable tragedies.

‘I appeal for sustained efforts to ensure that everyone involved in this unfortunate incident is accounted for and that adequate support is extended to the affected families.

‘I also urge boat operators to strictly adhere to maritime safety regulations, avoid overloading, ensure their boats are in good condition and provide functional life jackets for passengers. No journey is worth the loss of innocent lives.’
