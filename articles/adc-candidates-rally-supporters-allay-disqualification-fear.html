ADC, candidates rally supporters, allay disqualification fear

By Punch Punch 5d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Party officials and candidates of the African Democratic Congress in the January 16 general election in the South South have dismissed the fear of being disqual

While they have accepted that the David Mark-led exco have been sacked, they noted that they remain candidates and expressed hope that the court would reinstate the national exco of the party.

In Rivers State, most of the party’s campaign remains in the social and electronic media.

The state ADC governorship candidate, Dr Gabriel Pidomson has been appearing on various radio stations in Port Harcourt selling his programmes. Also recently he appeared on a national TV where he expressed confidence that the party will be on the ballot despite the court ruling.

Dr Pidomson described the ruling as a temporary setback, noting that he is talking to his support groups in the state to win voters.

“For sure, the ruling is a temporary setback for the party but I am still campaigning in order to get the needed votes to win the election,” he added.

In Edo State, the South South PUNCH checks revealed that the campaign activities of the ADC and its candidates were visible in the past one week with the Edo South Senatorial candidate, Matthew Iduoriyekemwen leading the way.

He has met with various groups and stakeholders and sold his manifesto to them. Iduoriyekemwen’s media aide, Paul Okojie, said his principal has been busy on the campaign trail, noting that the court judgement has not slowed him down.

He said, “Yes, he is still campaigning and the court judgement has not slowed him down. We are sure that the national leadership issue will be sorted out soon.

“He has continued to meet with different groups and stakeholders. He is doing everything possible to attract votes from all the local government in the senatorial districts.”

The Spokesman of the party in the state, Chris Ojeikere, said the party is campaigning, noting that the party’s presidential candidate, Atiku Abubakar has also been busy on the campaign train.

He noted that the party is also active in Edo while its candidates were meeting with groups to secure votes in the coming election.

“No, the court judgement has not halted our campaign both at the national and state levels. There is no ban on campaigns so we have continued. The case is about leadership not ban on campaigns.

For us, the ruling is unethical but it will be sorted out soon.”

He added that all other candidates in other parts of the state were also campaigning.

In Delta, the party said its campaign activities have not been suspended despite the court ruling. The state Publicity Secretary, Fred Akpevwe, in an interview, dismissed suggestions that the legal dispute had affected the party’s political activities in the state.

Akpevwe said the court case concerned the party’s leadership and did not prevent members from continuing their political engagements.

He said, “It has nothing to do with our campaigns. Our campaigns are still on course. The dispute is centred on the question of who is the party’s recognised chairman, and that is separate from the party’s campaign activities.”

Akpevwe maintained that the party remained active in the state despite the leadership dispute.

In Akwa Ibom, the state Publicity Secretary, Solomon Johnny, said the party is focused, undeterred, strong, united and fully on the campaign trail.

‎‎He noted that the judgement has not weakened the party structure, nor has it stopped its candidates from meeting voters ahead of the elections.

‎‎‎He said, “For us in ADC in Akwa Ibom State, we are strong and still campaigning. The court judgement has not slowed us down. We are sure that the leadership issue will be sorted out soon.

‎‎He said campaign activities were visible across the three senatorial districts in the past one week, with candidates meeting youths, women, stakeholders and party members to sell their manifestos.

‎‎“Our candidates have continued to meet with different groups and we are doing everything possible to attract votes from all the local government areas in the state. Last week, we had stakeholders’ engagement in Uyo and we are building a formidable team for 2027,” he added.

In Bayelsa State, the party vowed to soon launch a statewide campaign as part of efforts to strengthen its political structures and mobilise support ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The State Chairman, Hon. Johnny Derek, said the party had not suspended its campaign activities but was currently concentrating on grassroots mobilisation and engagements at the ward level across the state.

He explained that the party would soon intensify its activities with a full-scale statewide campaign to reach more residents and communicate its political programmes.

“We are campaigning. We have not stopped. Very soon, we will come out with full force. Our activities have been in the media and very soon, we are coming out with full force to launch a statewide campaign.

“But for now, as a party, we are conducting our various campaigns at the ward level,” he added.
