FIFA President Infantino to commission Warri Stadium Saturday as Oborevwori, Keyamo campaign in Warri South

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG

From Joe Obukata Ogbodu, Warri

FIFA President Gianni Infantino will on Saturday, 21 November, commission the rehabilitated 15,000-capacity Warri City Stadium, in what is set to be one of the biggest sporting events in the state.

Delta State APC Chairman Chief Solomon Areyenka announced the commissioning during the APC campaign held at Nana College, Warri, in Warri South Local Government Area.

Chief Areyenka, who presented the party’s flag to Hon. Austin Uroye and Hon. Benson Obire, the Warri South Constituency I and Warri South Constituency II candidates respectively, said the Warri Township Stadium would be commissioned on Saturday, 21 November, by FIFA President Gianni Infantino.

Noting the bright performances of the Flamingos, who won the World Cup and were adequately rewarded by the Nigerian government, Chief Areyenka enjoined APC supporters to give their children the opportunity to learn how to play football in the stadium when it is commissioned.

“When they play well, Governor Oborevwori will reward them,” he said.

Speaking at the event, where he received decampees of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), led by the NDC candidate for Warri South Constituency II, Willy Kuku, who was joined by others, Governor Oborevwori thanked Warri South for maintaining peace, saying, “Some people thought they will fight in Warri today. We will not fight and kill each other.”

He also predicted that, before the 2027 elections, five key politicians would defect to the APC.

Minister of Aviation, Festus Keyamo, SAN, warned Warri South voters that voting against President Bola Tinubu in 2027 would hurt Governor Oborevwori the most and could lead to the abandonment of ongoing projects in Delta State.

Keyamo spoke at the APC campaign at Nana College, Warri, where Governor Oborevwori also received decampees from the National Democratic Congress (NDC), including its Warri South Constituency II candidate, Willy Kuku.

“Your power is your PVC. Don’t say I will vote for this person, I will not vote for that person. The election is from top to bottom,” Keyamo said.

“All those projects will now be abandoned. Let no one deceive you. I beg you, vote all APC candidates.”

Keyamo noted that Governor Oborevwori was performing creditably in infrastructural development because President Bola Ahmed Tinubu had made funds available for him and all other governors in the country.

He warned that if the people voted against the president, it would hurt Governor Sheriff Oborevwori the most and he might not be able to complete the projects he had started.

“All those projects will now be abandoned. Let no one deceive you. I beg you, vote all APC candidates,” Keyamo said.

Meanwhile, the Commissioner for Works (Rural Roads) and Public Information, Charles Aniagwu, had on Tuesday, during an inspection tour of the stadium, disclosed that work on the rehabilitation and upgrade of the facility was near completion. He expressed satisfaction with the quality of work while stressing the governor’s determination to ensure that the facility met the required standards.

Aniagwu, who led the inspection of the main stadium bowl, swimming pool arena, VIP lounges, media centre, dressing rooms for home and visiting teams, and other supporting facilities, said the state government would not compromise on quality in its determination to restore the stadium to its former glory.

He explained that the inspection was part of the government’s commitment to ensuring that every component of the project was delivered according to specifications, adding that a few areas requiring attention had been identified and communicated to the contractor and consultant.

The commissioner added that the stadium had been designed to provide spectators with unobstructed views of activities on the pitch from virtually every section of the stands.

He said the ongoing rehabilitation extended beyond the main bowl to include the training pitch and facilities for indoor sporting activities, noting that considerable work had gone into restoring the entire complex.

Aniagwu described the project as another demonstration of Oborevwori’s commitment to fulfilling his campaign promises through the MORE Agenda, particularly its pillars of Meaningful Development and Opportunities for All.

He said the restored stadium would strengthen Delta’s position as Nigeria’s leading sporting state while providing young people with the facilities to develop their talents in football, athletics and other sporting activities.

According to him, the facility would also serve residents of Delta South and Delta Central Senatorial Districts, who would no longer have to travel to Asaba to enjoy sporting events in a standard stadium.

He said: “Just as the governor has provided a beautiful stadium in Asaba, he has also demonstrated his determination to bring the Warri City Stadium back to life. This shows his commitment to equitable development and the delivery of infrastructure across the state.”

The commissioner noted that the investment would further consolidate the state’s reputation for excellence in sports, pointing out that the development of sporting infrastructure was essential to sustaining Delta’s impressive performances at national competitions.

He also expressed satisfaction with the stadium’s floodlighting system, explaining that only one of the four floodlights was switched on during the inspection, yet it provided sufficient illumination to demonstrate its capacity.

He added that Oborevwori had personally visited the facility to monitor progress, just as other government officials had done, to ensure that the project was delivered as planned.

On the maintenance of the stadium after completion, Aniagwu said the immediate priority was to conclude the rehabilitation work before discussions on a maintenance arrangement could begin.
