2027: Yayi realised his looming defeat the day Adebutu returned to the ballot – PDP Nat’l Sec

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 5d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Claims 60% of Ogun APC members have joined PDP through Rainbow Coalition The National Secretary of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), Samuel Anyanwu, has said

  • Claims 60% of Ogun APC members have joined PDP through Rainbow Coalition

The National Secretary of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), Samuel Anyanwu, has said the return of the party’s Ogun State governorship candidate, Oladipupo Adebutu, to the ballot signalled impending defeat for the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate, Solomon Adeola, popularly known as Yayi.

Speaking on Wednesday at the PDP’s “Ogun Rescue Mission” mega rally in Abeokuta, Anyanwu also claimed that 60 per cent of APC members in the state had joined the opposition party through the Rainbow Coalition.

Anyanwu, who represented the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory, Nyesom Wike, presented the PDP flag to Adebutu and his running mate, Lateefat Sowunmi-Kolapo, expressing confidence that the party would win the governorship and legislative elections.

“This is the true Ogun for Ogun. The day Adebutu came back to the ballot was the day Solomon Yayi realised his looming defeat.

“I know him very well because I was in the Senate with him. This is Ogun and Ogun State must take their position,” Anyanwu said.

He urged supporters to translate their enthusiasm into votes for the party’s candidates, declaring that the PDP was positioned to secure victory across the state.

“On January 16, we are going to return our three senators and all other candidates. PDP has the best and most intelligent candidates and they are going to win. With my visit to Ogun State, I have observed that everybody wants Adebutu to win. He will take care of Ogun people,” he said.

Anyanwu described Wike as the party’s national leader and leader of the Rainbow Coalition, claiming that the coalition had substantially weakened the APC’s membership in Ogun.

“Sixty per cent of the APC have joined the PDP in Ogun State through the Rainbow Coalition,” he declared before presenting the flag to Adebutu and urging him to secure victory for the party.

In his address, Adebutu pledged to reserve one-third of positions in his administration for women, support youth enterprise and pursue wealth creation through his seven-point “ITURA DE” agenda.

He said his running mate would play a substantive role in government, stressing that women would receive responsibilities that reflected their competence and leadership abilities.

“My deputy is not a spare tyre and will never be. One-third of the government sector will be occupied by females. We will continue to promote the female gender.

“I can boldly tell you that it is my plan to run a government that gives women a prominent place in governance. Women will not be sidelined; they will be fully represented and entrusted with strategic positions in my government.

“I believe women have the capacity, competence and leadership qualities to contribute meaningfully to governance and the development of Ogun State.

“Their voices will be heard, their contributions valued, and their roles recognised,” he said.

Adebutu, who distributed pamphlets outlining his manifesto, urged residents to regard the document as a covenant between him and the people.

“The basis of ITURA DE is about humanity; we believe in ourselves and in humanity.

“We truly believe that for a nation to grow, we must support our young people. We believe in releasing the power of industry and enterprise and we will create wealth. With wealth creation, we shall have less tendencies for desperate behaviours,” he said.

Promoting his “Ogun for Ogun” campaign message, Adebutu said the PDP’s candidates had longstanding family and community ties that would enable them to understand the needs of residents.

He cited his running mate, the party’s Ogun Central senatorial candidate, Iyabo Obasanjo, and other candidates as members of a team with established grassroots relationships.

“We have candidates across all three senatorial districts who understand their communities, have established relationships with the people, and know what grassroots politics entails.

“We are not strangers to our people. We are part of the communities we seek to serve. That is the strength of our team,” he said.

Adebutu also acknowledged former governors Gbenga Daniel and Ibikunle Amosun, who were absent from the rally, recognising their contributions to the state’s political history.

“Their contributions to the political history of Ogun State and their respective roles in the state’s political journey remain part of our collective history. We recognise them and extend our warm regards,” he said.

He pledged to protect public funds and urged residents to collect their Permanent Voter Cards ahead of the elections.

“I won’t steal your money and we shall make Ogun State great again. It is for us to protect the heritage of our Ogun State. It is beyond political parties. Go and take your PVCs,” he said.

Earlier, Ogun PDP Chairman, Abayomi Tella, expressed confidence in Adebutu’s chances, saying the state’s electorate would scrutinise candidates and resist attempts to influence their choices with money.

“If you are coming to Ogun State to contest, you cannot cut corners. You can’t bamboozle them with money,” Tella said.

The rally featured a procession of party leaders, candidates and supporters from the NNPC underpass to the Ake Palace pavilion, with participants singing, dancing and chanting “ITURA DE,” meaning “relief has come.”
