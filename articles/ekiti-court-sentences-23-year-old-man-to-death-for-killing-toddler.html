Ekiti court sentences 23-year-old man to death for killing toddler

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

An Ekiti State High Court sitting in Ado Ekiti has sentenced a 23-year-old man, Kayode Ademilolu, to death by hanging for murdering a 30-month-old boy, Heritage

Ademilolu was convicted on Monday by Justice Adekunle Adeleye after the court found him guilty of killing the toddler on September 14, 2025, at Odo-Emure Street, Agbado-Ekiti.

The charge stated that the defendant murdered the child, contrary to Section 234 of the Criminal Law of Ekiti State, 2021.

Testifying before the court, the deceased’s father, Abiodun Taiwo, said he was asleep when the incident occurred but woke up at about 6.30pm after hearing a crowd shouting outside his house.

“I woke up at about 6.30pm due to shouts of a crowd in front of my house. When I enquired, I was told somebody had killed my son,” he said.

He told the court that he rushed to the scene and found his son’s lifeless body, with his head smashed and blood around him.

“On getting to the scene, I saw the lifeless body of the boy. I could not recognise the dead body initially because the head had been pressed and the body was in the pool of his own blood.

“Everybody at the scene identified Kayode as the one who smashed the boy’s head with a stone,” he said.

Taiwo added that the police later took the child’s remains to the mortuary of the General Hospital, Ode-Ekiti.

The prosecutor, Dr Surajudeen Ajibola, called three witnesses and tendered the defendant’s statements to the police and photographs of the deceased as exhibits.

In his statement, Ademilolu confessed to killing the child with a stone, saying he did not know what came over him.

He said he had visited the deceased’s father at his home on the day of the incident and that the boy had asked for a biscuit.

“The late boy demanded for biscuit. We went together to buy it. I asked the boy to go in front of me, I later carried a big stone and smashed it on his head from the back. Neither the father nor the boy earlier offended me, but I did not know what came over me,” he stated.

The defendant did not call any witness in his defence.

Delivering judgment Justice Adeleye said the prosecution had established the essential elements of murder beyond reasonable doubt.

“My finding is that Taiwo Heritage is dead, and his death is instantaneous.

“The defendant also admitted that he hit the two and half years old deceased with a stone on the head. His confession is unequivocal and cogent. The admitted facts require no further proof,” the judge said.

He added that the evidence of the prosecution witnesses established the offence and found Ademilolu guilty as charged.

“I find the defendant guilty as charged, consequently, he is hereby sentenced to death by hanging,” the judge pronounced.
