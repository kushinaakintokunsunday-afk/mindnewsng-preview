La Liga: Barcelona rally from two goals down to beat Villarreal 3-2

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Spanish champions Barcelona came back from two goals behind to beat Villarreal 3-2 on Sunday and restore their three point lead at the top of La Liga. Barca edg

Barca edged clear of Jose Mourinho’s Real Madrid, who had pulled level with them on Saturday after beating Celta Vigo.

Two goals inside seven minutes from Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi gave Villarreal a commanding lead at Camp Nou and Barca winger Lamine Yamal missed a first-half penalty.

However, the Catalan giants struck three times in an 11-minute spell in the second half to claim victory.

Gerard Martin and Fermin Lopez pulled Barca level before Pau Navarro’s own goal gave them the three points.

Barca have now won four consecutive matches across all competitions since their Clasico defeat by Real Madrid in October.

“We suffered because we started the game badly, we let in two goals very early. I’ll remember how the team reacted but we have to work to ensure this doesn’t happen again,” Barca midfielder Pedri told DAZN.

“Against a team like this you can’t always turn things around from two down.”

Barcelona coach Hansi Flick hailed his team’s comeback, something they have made a habit of during his reign.

“We made two mistakes and they scored two times… but the reaction that we had was unbelievable,” Flick told reporters.

The hosts were caught snoozing in the early afternoon sunlight and Buchanan fired Villarreal ahead in the fifth minute.

Lopez gave the ball away with a careless pass and Canada international Buchanan produced a perfect sliced effort from distance to send the visitors ahead.

Two minutes later Villarreal doubled their advantage when Oluwaseyi, another Canadian, broke in down the left flank and smashed an effort over Joan Garcia into the roof of the net.

Flick’s side should have pulled one back when Oluwaseyi felled Yamal in the area and a penalty was given after a VAR review.

Yamal took it himself but dragged his effort against the post and wide.

The winger tried to make amends with a burst inside from the wing before half-time but Peter Gulacsi palmed away his cross-shot and the goalkeeper and his defence combined to thwart Eric Garcia on the follow-up.

Quickfire treble –

Barcelona came out invigorated in the second half and quickly dragged themselves back into the match.

Pedri shot wide and Gulacsi saved from Lopez’s low effort before Martin found the net in the 57th minute.

Pedri crossed the ball in from the left for the defender to head home.

Four minutes later Lopez slammed home the second after being teed up by Yamal, and the third was quick to follow.

A clever backheel pass by Yamal fed Eric Garcia, whose cross was bundled into the back of his own net by Navarro.

Substitute Anthony Gordon was denied by Gulacsi and then appealed for a penalty after a bump from Santi Comesana but went down too easily.

Barcelona’s protests on the sidelines were vehement and referee Alejandro Hernandez Hernandez’s report revealed Lopez, who had already been substituted off, was sent off for dissent, but had already walked down the tunnel before he could be shown a red card.

Flick said although he was happy with refereeing in Spain generally, he disliked Hernandez Hernandez.

“We saw which referee was there and we expect it,” said Flick.

“I’m really happy with the referees but with this referee — not.”

Navarro was sent off for a second yellow card in stoppage time as Villarreal, ninth, left frustrated.

“The (first Barca) goal psychologically killed us, they gained confidence, a couple of minutes later they equalised and from there it’s a lot more difficult,” Comesana told DAZN.

Later Sunday, Atletico Madrid, third, visit Getafe among other fixtures.
