Fashola: Obi’s 2023 Lagos victory took us by surprise

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Former Lagos State Governor, Babatunde Fashola, has recalled how the outcome of the 2023 presidential election in Lagos surprised his political camp, admitting that they were caught off guard by President Bola Tinubu’s defeat in the state.

Fashola described the outcome as being “blindsided” during an interview on Channels Television on Tuesday night.

He made the remarks while responding to a question on whether a similar electoral upset could occur in the 2027 general election.

The interviewer recalled that Tinubu, who had governed Lagos State for eight years, lost the state to the Labour Party’s Peter Obi in the 2023 presidential election, prompting speculation that a bigger surprise could happen in 2027.

Responding, Fashola said he had described the outcome at the time as being blindsided, but argued that political parties should understand that electoral victories cannot be guaranteed in every part of the country.

“Look, listen, I told you then, I think I recall the word I used was that we were blindsided in Lagos. I told you that. And, you know, our democracy is maturing,” he said.

The former governor, who served as Minister of Works and Housing under the late President Muhammadu Buhari, went on to compare electoral contests with football, arguing that teams sometimes keep visiting grounds where they have repeatedly failed to win.

He referenced former President Goodluck Jonathan’s football analogy, describing it as a useful way of understanding the unpredictability of elections.

“I heard President Jonathan in your intro speaking about how the analogy of a contest in an election and using football, and it’s a very brilliant analogy, if you ask me,” Fashola said.

He explained that some football clubs repeatedly visit opponents’ grounds despite having a history of losing there, because they still believe they can win.

According to him, political parties should similarly recognise that they cannot win every location and should concentrate on ensuring that their supporters participate in elections.

“In an election, sometimes you can’t win everywhere. And most times you can’t win everywhere. There are places where you have your ardent and fervent supporters. Your responsibility there is to ensure that you do enough to get them out to vote,” he said

In the 2023 presidential election, Obi defeated Tinubu in Lagos, polling 582,454 votes against the APC candidate’s 572,606.

Obi won nine of the state’s 20 local government areas, while Tinubu won the remaining 11.

The result was particularly significant because Tinubu had governed Lagos between 1999 and 2007 and remained a major political figure in the state.
