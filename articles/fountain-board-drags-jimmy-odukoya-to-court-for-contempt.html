Fountain board drags Jimmy Odukoya to court for contempt

By Punch Punch 13h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Incorporated Trustees of the Fountain of Life Church have initiated contempt enforcement proceedings against Olujimmy Odukoya over his alleged failure to co

The church, through its counsel, Ibitayo Reju, on Monday obtained an order of substituted service from the Lagos State High Court, authorising it to serve Odukoya with Form 48, a notice warning of the consequences of disobeying an order of court.

Justice O. O. Adewunmi-Oshin, sitting at the Lagos High Court, granted the application in suit number LD/0126PRA/2026, permitting service through newspaper publications, pasting at Odukoya’s last known address at No. 12 Industrial Estate Road, off Town Planning Way, Ilupeju, Lagos, and electronic means.

The judge also directed Odukoya to appear before the court on November 26, 2026, to show cause.

Form 48, issued on November 23 pursuant to Section 72 of the Sheriffs and Civil Processes Act and Order IX Rule 13 of the Judgment (Enforcement) Rules of Court, warned Odukoya that failure to comply with the order made on November 18 would amount to contempt of court and expose him to committal to prison.

The notice, addressed to Odukoya at his last known address in Ilupeju, stated: “Take notice that unless you obey the Order of this Honourable Court (annexed to this process) made on 18 November 2026, you will be guilty of contempt of Court and will be liable to be committed to prison.”

The contempt proceedings followed an ex-parte order made by Justice Adewunmi-Oshin on November 18, restraining Odukoya, whether acting personally or through proxies, from holding himself out as the Senior Pastor and Spiritual Head of the church.

The court also restrained him from interfering with, disrupting, meddling with or obstructing the activities, governance, and other affairs of the church.

The church had argued in its application that Odukoya had ceased to occupy the position following the appointment of a successor by its late founder and Senior Pastor, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, in accordance with the church’s constitution.

While granting the application, the judge also gave the church leave to commence the action under the pre-action protocol provisions of the Lagos State High Court (Expeditious Disposal of Civil Cases) Practice Direction No. 2 of 2019.

The court directed that the originating processes and accompanying documents be served on the respondent and that he file his response within the timelines prescribed by the Practice Direction.

The judge, however, expressly stated that the November 18 order was made ex parte and without prejudice to Odukoya’s right to apply to set it aside or respond fully to the substantive claims upon being served.

At Monday’s proceedings, Reju appeared for the applicant, while Odukoya was unrepresented. Both parties were absent.

The matter was adjourned to November 26 for Odukoya to show cause,the matter was initially adjourned to December 1, 2026, for mention before  Monday’s proceedings.
