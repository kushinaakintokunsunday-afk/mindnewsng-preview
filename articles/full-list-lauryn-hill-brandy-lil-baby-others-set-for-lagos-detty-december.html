FULL LIST: Lauryn Hill, Brandy, Lil Baby, others set for Lagos Detty December

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Detty December in Lagos, the unofficial festive-season celebration that has become a major entertainment attraction in Nigeria, is gathering momentum as interna

Over the years, the December festivities have attracted Nigerians in the diaspora, foreign visitors and international celebrities, turning Lagos into a destination for music, nightlife, tourism and entertainment.

While international celebrities were among the visitors who added glamour to last year’s festivities, several foreign artistes are billed to perform at major events in Lagos this year.

Among the international stars announced for the 2026 edition are American singers Brandy, Kehlani and Summer Walker, as well as rappers Lil Baby, Latto, Sexyy Red, A Boogie Wit da Hoodie and Young Thug.

The line-up also includes legendary singer Lauryn Hill, alongside her sons YG Marley and Zion Marley, and Jamaican rapper Wyclef Jean.

Here is a list of some of the international artistes expected to perform in Lagos during Detty December 2026.

Flytime Promotions will host a series of events at the Eko Convention Centre, Victoria Island, from December 20 to 25.

The announced international artistes and their performance dates include:

  • December 20 – Flytime’s House of R&B: Brandy.
  • December 21 – Coca-Cola Rhythm Unplugged: Lil Baby, Kehlani and Yung Miami.
  • December 22 – Flytime Fest: Latto, Sexyy Red and A Boogie Wit da Hoodie.
  • December 23 and 24 – Flytime Fest: More international artistes are expected to be announced.
  • December 25 – Flytime Circle: Further artiste announcements are expected.

Detty December Fest, scheduled to run from December 18 to 30 at Detty December Village, Ilubirin, Lagos, has also announced several international performers.

They include:

  • December 21: Lauryn Hill, YG Marley, Zion Marley and Wyclef Jean.
  • December 27: Summer Walker and Fridayy.
  • December 28: Young Thug

More performers are expected to be announced as organisers unveil additional details of the festival.

With international stars taking up headline slots at major concerts, the 2026 edition further highlights Lagos’ growing appeal as a destination for music and entertainment during the festive season.
