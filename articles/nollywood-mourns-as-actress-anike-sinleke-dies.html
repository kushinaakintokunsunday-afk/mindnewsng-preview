Nollywood mourns as actress Anike Sinleke dies

By Punch Punch 7h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nollywood has been thrown into mourning following the death of Yoruba actress Adenike Babajide, popularly known as Anike Sinleke, after reportedly battling ovar

Fellow actress Bukky Fagbuyi announced her death on Sunday in a post shared on Instagram.

Sinleke is the latest in a string of Yoruba film industry figures to have died of cancer in recent months.

Recall that actress and filmmaker Temitope Osoba died in early August at the age of 40 after a prolonged battle with breast cancer, which she had spoken about publicly. She began acting in 2005 and featured in films including Ogun Mi and Gucci Girls.

Later that month, on August 23, veteran actor Taiwo Hassan, popularly known as Ogogo, also died after reportedly battling stage-four cancer.

Barely two weeks later, actress Atinuke Remilekun Kareemalso died of breast cancer. Her death was announced by the Ogun State chapter of the Theatre Arts and Motion Pictures Practitioners Association of Nigeria.

Fagbuyi, who hosts the Instagram Live programme Rubbin Minds With Bukky, disclosed that the late actress had been scheduled to appear as a guest on the programme on the day her death was announced.

“Unfortunately, Nollywood Actress Adenike Abike Babajide A.K.A Anike Sinleke, who was supposed to join us today on our Instagram Live programme RUBBIN MINDS WITH BUKKY, has passed after a battle with Ovarian Cancer.

“May her soul rest in Peace,” she wrote.

The announcement attracted condolence messages from colleagues, including actresses Mistura Asunramu and Omoborty.

Asunramu expressed shock over the news, writing, “Omg, so sorry about this, may her soul rest in peace o.”

Omoborty also reacted, saying, “Oh No!!!”

Actresses Regina Chukwu and Seliat also expressed their sadness with crying emojis.

A Nollywood fan, Bakky Adeoye, wrote, “My condolences, babe. May her soul rest in peace with her ancestors. Aku ara fe’ra ku though I don’t know her.”

Adore by Ayeesha also recalled one of the actress’s popular roles, writing, “Wa sinleke fun mi. Anike Sinleke” before adding, “OMG. RIP beautiful Queen.”

Sinleke began her acting career under the guidance of veteran actor Remmy Shitta and worked with filmmaker Deji Adenuga on movie productions.

She also ventured into film production in the Yoruba movie industry before taking a break from acting to pursue her Higher National Diploma.
Beyond acting, she helped promote Lagos culture and empower women through the Sisi Eko beauty pageant, which she founded around 2011.

The initiative, reportedly supported by the Lagos State Ministry of Information and Strategy, provided a platform for young women to showcase their knowledge of culture, tourism, and education alongside the traditional beauty competition.

Her death has drawn tributes from colleagues and admirers, particularly within the Yoruba movie industry.
