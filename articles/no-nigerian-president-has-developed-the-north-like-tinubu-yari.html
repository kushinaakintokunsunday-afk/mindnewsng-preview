‘No Nigerian President has developed the North like Tinubu’ — Yari

The Director-General of the All Progressives Congress Presidential Campaign Council (APC-PCC), Senator Abdulaziz Yari, has claimed that no Nigerian president has done more for the development of Northern Nigeria than President Bola Tinubu.

Yari made the statement on Tuesday during Tinubu’s campaign rally at the Haliru Abdu Stadium in Birnin Kebbi, where he called on voters across the North-West to support the President’s re-election bid in the 2027 general elections.

He dismissed opposition claims that Tinubu lacked support in the North, arguing that the infrastructure projects being undertaken by the President across the region demonstrated his commitment to its development.

The former Zamfara State governor also challenged opposition candidates to present their records in office and explain what they had done for Northern Nigeria when they held positions of authority.

“I challenge opposition candidates to tell the people of the North what they did for the region when they were in positions of power,” Yari said.

He urged voters not to be swayed by what he described as misleading claims about Tinubu’s support in the region, appealing to residents of the North-West to remain with the APC.

Earlier, Tinubu thanked Kebbi residents for turning out to welcome him despite the hot weather.

The President also urged eligible voters to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs) ahead of the 2027 general elections to enable them to participate in the polls.
