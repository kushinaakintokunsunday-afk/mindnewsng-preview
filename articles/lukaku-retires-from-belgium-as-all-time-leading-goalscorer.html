Lukaku retires from Belgium as all-time leading goalscorer

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read

Romelu Lukaku has disclosed his retirement from the Belgium men’s national team, calling an end to an international career that spanned 16 years.

Lukaku, popularly called ‘Big Rom,’ revealed this on his Instagram page on Monday.

The 33-year-old striker made his international debut in 2010 and became Belgium’s all-time leading men’s goalscorer with 94 goals in 135 appearances. He represented the country in four World Cups and three European Championships.

According to BBC Sport, Lukaku is the fifth-highest men’s international goalscorer of all time, behind leader Cristiano Ronaldo with 146, Lionel Messi, Sunil Chhetri and Ali Daei.

The Fenerbahçe striker retires from international football as Belgium’s third-most-capped player, behind Jan Vertonghen (157) and Axel Witsel (140).

Lukaku, who played for top clubs in Europe, including Chelsea, Manchester United and Inter Milan, expressed appreciation to his teammates, the coaches for “pushing and trusting me to help the team” and the Belgian fans for their support.

In his retirement post, Lukaku noted that he was at “peace” with himself to call the final shot on his international career, as he vowed to remember the moments he had on the team.

“These 16 years, I gave all I had for the national team. I went through every emotion possible. I played with amazing teammates that I will always be grateful for.

“I want to thank them all. I want to thank all the coaches as well for pushing and trusting me to help the team. It was a nice ride, and I enjoyed every single moment.

“To the Belgian fans, thank you for the support you gave our team. I am proud to have represented my country in four World Cups and three European Championships.

“I will never forget those moments. I’m at peace with my decision to stop here. It was amazing and enjoyable. I’m grateful.”

Lukaku scored eight goals across four World Cups, including in the run to the quarter-finals at this summer’s tournament, and a total of six goals at three European Championships.

He missed Belgium’s Nations League win over Turkey last week with a hip injury and was an unused substitute on Sunday as they qualified for the last eight of the competition by beating Italy 4-1, BBC Sport reports.

In 2023, Lukaku scored 14 times in eight Euro 2024 qualifying appearances, breaking the record for goals scored by a player in a European Championship qualifying campaign.
