UK police arrest two over break-in at Prince William’s home

By Punch Punch 18h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Two men have been arrested on suspicion of burglary, police said Tuesday, after a reported break-in at the rural country retreat of UK royals Prince William and

Police dogs, drones and a helicopter were deployed to the remote Sandringham estate in eastern England after receiving a call just before 6:00 pm (1800GMT) on Monday of suspicious activity, Norfolk police said in a statement sent to AFP.

Anmer Hall, where William and Kate, and their three children, occasionally live — especially during the school holidays — was broken into, according to UK media.

The men “were arrested on the estate on suspicion of burglary and trespass” at the estate owned by King Charles, police said.

The property involved “was unoccupied” at the time, they added.

William and Kate mainly live at their home on the Windsor Estate, just west of London.

Kensington Palace, which handles the affairs of William, the heir-to-throne, did not immediately reply to AFP’s request for comment.

This is not the first time a royal residence has been broken into.

In 2024, several intruders were caught on the Windsor estate after they broke into a farm building and tried to steal a pick-up truck and a quad bike.

The most famous incident was in July 1982 when a man, Michael Fagan, sneaked into Buckingham Palace and made his way to Queen Elizabeth II’s bedroom.

He sat on her four-poster bed and had a 10-minute chat with her before being taken away by security. He was initially charged, but then the charges were dropped.

News of Monday’s break-in broke just as William made his first visit to Downing Street on Tuesday, where he talked about “shared priorities” with Prime Minister Andy Burnham.

The meeting was seen as another step towards the heir to the throne’s future role as monarch.
