Plateau 2027: PDP’s Scores Gov Mutfwang 15-20% In Performance

By Channels TV Channels TV 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The governorship candidate of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), Kephas Ropshik, on Wednesday said that Governor Caleb Mutfwang performed poorly in governance

The governorship candidate of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), Kephas Ropshik, on Wednesday said that Governor Caleb Mutfwang performed poorly in governance in the state.

Ropshik, who spoke during an interview on Channels Television’s Politics Today, said the governor had not scored more than 20 per cent in performance.

The PDP candidate said that leadership should be about performance and about the people.

“In fact, I can tell you on a scale of one to 100, you can give him like 15 to 20 per cent in performance,” he said. “He still remains someone that I respect, but of course that, completely, is different from politics.”

He alleged that the governor defected to the All Progressives Congress (APC) when he could not cope with the crisis in the PDP.

He, however, said that the governor did not leave with up to 15 per cent of the members of the PDP, insisting that Plateau is a PDP state.

“You know, a man’s strength and leadership qualities are being tested when there are crises, but the governor couldn’t stand the crisis. He left for APC, and so there was an opening in the PDP to contest for governorship because, of course, the truth is this. Plateau has been a PDP state.

“You can’t take that away from Plateau State, right? And so there was a vacancy, and so somebody needed to fill in the gap. And so even with the crisis, we believed in the PDP,” the candidate said.

“Mutfwang did not live with 15, 20 per cent of people, I mean, in PDP to APC. The people, in fact, not even 10 per cent, because the people that followed him to APC are his appointees, and that’s the truth.

“Ninety per cent of people on the Plateau still believe in the PDP.”

Ropshik said that Plateau is one of the frontrunners in the Rainbow Coalition.

He also said that he was working for the re-election of President Bola Tinubu.

Asked whether he was not confusing his followers by telling them to vote for the APC in the January 16 presidential election, and the PDP in the February 6, 2027 governorship election in the state, Ropshik said, “No, no, no, I am not reading because now if we want to be sincere to ourselves, the PDP presidential candidate, we all know that there’s no chance for him to win the election.

“And so why should we now waste the votes? We must find a platform to identify; what we’re after is good governance.”
