2027 Grammys: Asake, Davido, Burna Boy, Ayra Starr earn nominations

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerian music stars Asake, Burna Boy, Tems, Wizkid, Davido and Ayra Starr have earned nominations at the 69th Annual Grammy Awards, with Asake securing two nom

In a live announcement on the Recording Academy’s YouTube channel on Monday, Asake earned two nominations in the category for his songs, ‘Gratitude’ and ‘Jogodo’, his collaboration with fellow Nigerian singer Wizkid.

Tems also earned a nomination for ‘Big Daddy’, while Ayra Starr was recognised alongside Beninese singer Angélique Kidjo for their collaboration, ‘Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)’.

The other nominee in the category is ‘No Tradition, No Africa’ by King Ayisoba featuring Rocky Dawuni.

Burna Boy also earned a nomination in the Best Global Music Performance category for ‘Dìá Dìá’, his collaboration with Colombian singer Shakira.

Nigerian artistes also featured in the Best Global Music Album category, with Davido nominated for ‘Oriadé’, Ayra Starr for ‘Starrgirl’ and Tems for ‘Love Is a Kingdom’.

Tems also secured a nomination in the Best R&B Solo Performance category for her song, ‘What You Need’.

The 69th Annual Grammy Awards will hold on February 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California.
