Gov Mbah commissions Premium Trust Bank Branch in Enugu

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG

Governor Peter Mbah of Enugu State, on Monday, unveiled the first branch of Premium Trust Bank in the state, describing the development as a demonstration of growing private-sector confidence in the state’s economy and investment prospects. 

Mbah reiterated his administration’s determination to restore Enugu’s historic position as a major economic hub through deliberate investments, private-sector partnerships and policies designed to accelerate economic growth.

This was even as the bank’s Group Managing and Chief Executive Officer, Mr Emmanuel Efe Emefienim (CON, FCIB), commended Mbah for improved business environment and reaffirmed the bank’s commitment expanding its national footprint and supporting businesses, small and medium-scale enterprises (SMEs) and financial inclusion.

The Governor recalled that Enugu’s economic significance predated Nigeria’s amalgamation, noting that the discovery of coal in 1909 established the city as an important commercial and administrative centre.

He said his administration was growing the state’s economy in line with its development blueprint anchored on growing the state’s economy from $4.4 billion to $30 billion within eight years, eliminating poverty and making Enugu a preferred destination for living, working, business and tourism.

He emphasised that the administration’s strategy was to create an enabling environment for private capital to drive economic expansion, stressing that government alone could not deliver the desired level of growth.

The governor identified security as one of the major considerations for investors,recalling that the administration inherited security challenges, including the disruption of economic activities associated with the Monday sit-at-home order.

According to him, the interventions have helped restore confidence and reduce the risks associated with doing business in Enugu.

The governor further disclosed plans to establish a 660-megawatt coal-fired powerplant, expressing optimism that reliable and affordable electricity would stimulate industrialisation and create opportunities across the state’s economic value chain.

He also cited the expansion of Enugu’s air connectivity, including international routes, as part of efforts to improve access to global markets and attract investment. C

Congratulating the bank’s management, disclosed that Premium Trust Bank was already a partner of the state government and the headline sponsor of the National Sports Festival scheduled to hold in Enugu. He noted that the bank’s expansion into Enugu was consistent with the administration’s development philosophy of government-enabled, private-sector-driven growth.

The governor expressed optimism that the bank would establish additional branches in the state, potentially expanding its presence to 10 branches as economic activities and investment opportunities increase.

Earlier, Emefienim said the bank’s decision to establish a presence in Enugu was informed by confidence in the state, its people and its economic future.

He disclosed that the Enugu branch was the bank’s fourth branch opening in November, bringing its nationwide network to 38, with plans to establish four additional branches before the end of the year.

According to him, the expansion reflects the bank’s commitment to bringing financial services closer to individuals, entrepreneurs, SMEs and corporate organisations across the country.

“Our decision to establish a presence here is a strong belief in this state, its people and its future.

“More importantly, it is a demonstration of our readiness to partner with the state government, individuals, entrepreneurs, SMEs, corporates and the good people of Enugu in building a stronger and more prosperous economy,” he said.

The bank’s managing director commended the Mbah administration for its investment infrastructure, security, technology, ease of doing business and investment promotion, noting that the initiatives were helping to create an environment conducive to enterprise and investment.

He said that while the government had the responsibility of providing an enabling environment, financial institutions and private businesses also had critical roles to play the provision of capital, innovation and financial solutions.

Describing the new branch as more than an additional banking facility, Emefienim said it would serve as a platform for expanding financial inclusion and supporting individuals and businesses in achieving their financial goals.

He identified entrepreneurs seeking to expand their businesses, traders requiring efficient payment solutions, professionals planning their financial future,students pursuing education and families working towards financial security as some of the categories the bank intended to serve.

On support for SMEs, he described the sector as a major driver of economic growth and job creation, stressing that businesses required financial partners capable of understanding their needs and providing solutions to support their expansion.

He highlighted the bank’s Silver Premium Business Account, designed to help businesses manage operating costs, improve efficiency and access collections,digital payment solutions and other banking services.

Emefienim also introduced Blaze, the bank’s lifestyle and digital financial platform,which he said was designed to simplify transactions, improve banking convenience and expand access to financial services.

Reaffirming the bank’s commitment to the state, he said Premium Trust Bank was prepared to build a long-term relationship with Enugu and contribute to the growth of businesses and the wider economy.

“We are here for the long term. We are here to support enterprises. We are here to create opportunities. We are here to help businesses grow and help individuals achieve their financial goals,” he said.

He attributed the bank’s growing national presence to its emphasis on sound corporate governance, innovation, service excellence and prudent management.
