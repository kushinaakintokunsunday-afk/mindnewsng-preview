Osun federal health university appoints new VC

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Governing Council of the Federal University of Health Sciences, Ila-Orangun, Osun State, has appointed a Professor of Public Health Entomology and Parasitol

The announcement was made on Friday by the Pro-Chancellor and Chairman of Council, Prof. Wale Sulaiman, who addressed journalists on the university’s campus after a council meeting where the appointments were ratified.

Sulaiman said the university also appointed Dr Bolarinwa Feruke as Bursar, Dr Omowumi Ilori as Registrar and Prof. Elizabeth Adekanye as University Librarian.

He said Adeleke, who was the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic) before his appointment, and the other principal officers emerged after an extensive two-week selection process.

He said, “We advertised the minimum requirements for the principal officers. We had an extensive two-week selection process, interviews, interactions, and then final selections of the four principal officers, including the Vice-Chancellor of the Federal University of Health Sciences.

“For the Vice-Chancellorship, three finalists were scored over 100 marks. Prof. Kola Wahab scored 71.3 per cent, Prof. Musa Yakubu scored 72 per cent, while Prof. Monsuru Adebayo Adeleke scored 96.9 per cent to emerge as the winner.”

He commended the outgoing Vice-Chancellor, Prof. Akeem Lasisi, for his contributions to the development of the institution.

“Among the newly established federal universities of health sciences, the Ila-Orangun institution is the most advanced in terms of number of buildings, academic programmes and student enrolment. The outgoing Vice-Chancellor has done very well,” Sulaiman added.

In his remarks, Adeleke, an indigene of Igbo-Ora in Ibarapa Central Local Government Area of Oyo State, promised to govern without fear or favour and work towards building a smart campus.

He said the university would introduce innovative medical programmes, strengthen its research capacity and attract funding through grant-winning proposals, noting that the government alone could not fund education.

He said, “I am going to govern without any fear or favour. We are looking at a smart campus that is going to encompass everything. We want this university to serve as a differentiator. We are going to bring some innovative medical programmes to this university.

“We know that government alone cannot fund education. We need to think outside the box and bring innovative ways of getting the money that will be required to run this university effectively.

“The welfare of the staff will also be paramount. I am also going to make sure that nobody is denied their rightful entitlement as a staff member of this university. The same thing goes for the students. I am going to ensure that our students are attended to and their welfare is taken care of.”

The Federal Government appointed Lasisi as the pioneer Vice-Chancellor of the university in March 2022.
