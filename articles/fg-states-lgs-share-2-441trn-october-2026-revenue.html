FG, States, LGs Share ₦2.441trn October 2026 Revenue

A total sum of ₦2.441 trillion, being October 2026 Federation Account Revenue, has been shared among the Federal Government, states and the local government councils.

The revenue was shared at the November 2026 Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) meeting held in Abuja.

The ₦2.441 trillion total distributable revenue comprised distributable statutory revenue of ₦1.688 trillion and distributable Value Added Tax (VAT) revenue of ₦752.488 billion.

A communiqué issued by the Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) indicated that total gross revenue of ₦3.811 trillion was available in the month of October 2026.

Total deduction for cost of collection, according to the statement, was ₦128.797 billion, while total transfers, refunds and savings were ₦1.241 trillion.

According to the communiqué, gross statutory revenue of ₦2.999 trillion was received for the month of October 2026.

This was lower than the sum of ₦3.096 trillion received in the preceding month by ₦97.323 billion.

Gross revenue of ₦812.446 billion was available from the Value Added Tax (VAT) in October 2026.

This was lower than the ₦816.872 billion available in the month of September 2026 by ₦4.426 billion.

The communiqué stated that from the ₦2.441 trillion total distributable revenue, the Federal Government received a total sum of ₦856.065 billion and the state governments received a total sum of ₦809.909 billion.

The local government council, it revealed, received ₦568.701 billion, while the sum of ₦206.384 billion (13 per cent of mineral revenue) was shared to the benefiting State as derivation revenue.

On the ₦1.688 trillion distributable statutory revenue, the communiqué stated that the Federal Government received ₦780.816 billion and the state governments received ₦396.040 billion.

The local government councils received ₦305.331 billion, and the sum of ₦206.384 billion (13 per cent of mineral revenue) was shared to the benefiting States as derivation revenue.

From the ₦752.488 billion distributable Value Added Tax (VAT) revenue, the Federal Government received ₦75.249 billion, the state governments received ₦413.868 billion, and the local government councils received ₦263.371 billion.

In October 2026, excise duty and CET levies increased marginally, according to the communiqué, while Petroleum Profit Tax (PPT), Hydrocarbon Tax (HT), Petroleum Royalties, Import Duty, Companies Income Tax (CIT), CGT & SDT, Value Added Tax (VTA), Mineral Royalties, Gas Flared Penalty, Rental Gas Flared Fee and Misc. Oil Revenue all decreased.
