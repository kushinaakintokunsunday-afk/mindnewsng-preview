LAWMA rolls out 60-day operational efficiency plan

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Acting Managing Director of the Lagos Waste Management Authority, Olajuwon Akinyemi, has unveiled a 60-day action plan focused on rapid waste evacuation, im

 Akinyemi unveiled the plan while addressing the  management and staff at a workers’ forum held at LAWMA’s headquarters in Ijora-Olopa on Friday. He was accompanied by the Acting Executive Director, Operations, Dr Olorunwa Tijani, and the Executive Director, Finance, Mr Kunle Adebiyi.

 LAWMA disclosed this in a statement over the weekend.

 Akinyemi explained that the immediate focus of the new management was to boost the Authority’s operational capacity, “particularly in waste evacuation, field monitoring and regulatory enforcement, while introducing strategic changes to improve service delivery across Lagos State.”

 Reflecting on his earlier years of service at LAWMA and other government institutions, Akinyemi urged employees to foster teamwork, adopt constructive attitudes and renew their dedication to their duties. He noted that repositioning the Authority would depend on the collective efforts and full participation of all staff members.

 “We are bringing in resilience and a new paradigm shift. It is a new wave in handling waste in Lagos. We already have the template to transform LAWMA positively. All we need to do is recreate, modernise and do better than in the past,” he stated.

 Under the 60-day action plan, Akinyemi revealed that staff would be strategically redeployed across the state’s three operational districts, with consideration given to their knowledge of various terrains and communities. He said the move was aimed at strengthening field monitoring, improving operational response and raising service delivery standards.

 He also disclosed plans to establish regional operational centres across the state to decentralise waste management activities, enhance supervision and ensure that interventions reached communities more promptly, particularly areas requiring urgent attention.

 Akinyemi further outlined plans to intensify community engagement and public enlightenment on proper waste sorting at source and responsible waste disposal, noting that traditional rulers, Community Development Association leaders, market associations, artisans and other community stakeholders would be mobilised to support efforts to curb indiscriminate dumping.

 He emphasised the need for stronger engagement with Private Sector Participation operators and greater public compliance with approved waste collection tariffs, describing collaboration among the Authority, service providers and residents as essential to achieving a cleaner Lagos.

 On staff welfare, Akinyemi assured employees of management’s commitment to their well-being, disclosing efforts to facilitate the procurement of Compressed Natural Gas-powered staff buses to ease transport challenges.

 Earlier, the Acting Executive Director, Operations, Dr Olorunwa Tijani, expressed delight at reuniting with the LAWMA family, assuring staff that the Authority’s operational framework would receive urgent attention and revitalisation to improve performance.

 He stressed the need for a more effective operational structure and pledged his support for the new management’s drive to reposition the Authority and strengthen service delivery.

 Also speaking, the Executive Director, Finance, Mr Kunle Adebiyi, expressed confidence in the capacity of the new leadership to reposition the Authority, pledging his support and commitment to teamwork.

 Adebiyi emphasised the importance of prudent financial management, accountability and effective resource utilisation, noting that successful implementation of the 60-day action plan would require synergy among all staff, directorates, departments and units.

 He urged employees to demonstrate professionalism, commitment and a shared sense of responsibility.

 The Acting Managing Director later toured various departments within the Authority, accompanied by the Acting Executive Director, Operations, and other management officials. During the visit, he engaged with staff to obtain first-hand knowledge of their responsibilities and the operational challenges they face.
