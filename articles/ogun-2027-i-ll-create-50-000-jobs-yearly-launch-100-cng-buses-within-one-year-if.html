Ogun 2027: I’ll create 50,000 jobs yearly, launch 100 CNG buses within one year if elected governor – Adeola

Launches ITESIWAJU Manifesto

______________________________________________________________________

From Laide Raheem, Abeokuta

The All Progressives Congress (APC) governorship candidate in Ogun State, Senator Solomon Olamilekan Adeola, on Friday promised that his administration would create 50,000 jobs yearly, provide 200MW of power and launch between 50 and 100 CNG buses within the first 12 months, if elected governor in 2027.

Adeola made the promise while speaking at the formal launch of his ITESIWAJU Manifesto at the June 12 Cultural Centre, Abeokuta, the Ogun State capital. He also said his administration would construct no fewer than 2,500 kilometres of rural and urban roads across the state in four years, if given the mandate to lead Ogun.

The APC governorship candidate was addressing a gathering that included the National Leader of the APC and former governor of the state, Aremo Olusegun Osoba; Governor Dapo Abiodun; the State Chairman of the APC, Yemi Sanusi; and other party chieftains. He declared that the manifesto, titled “ITESIWAJU – Governance That Works For Everyone”, was a contract, not a campaign performance.

“This Agenda is a plan, not a performance. Read it as you would read a contract, because that is exactly what it is,” he said.

Adeola said the plan was built from his tour of all 20 local governments, where he met royal fathers, community leaders, women, youths, farmers and traders.

He described governance as a relay race, praising past leaders from Chief Olabisi Onabanjo to Chief Olusegun Osoba, Otunba Gbenga Daniel, Senator Ibikunle Amosun and Governor Dapo Abiodun.

He credited Abiodun’s ISEYA agenda with the Agro-Cargo Airport, dry ports, over 1,700km of roads, over 7,000 housing units and a ₦17 trillion economy.

“Where ISEYA built infrastructure, we will connect such to the world. Where ISEYA empowered our youth, we will turn their talent into great enterprise,” he said.

On his origins, Adeola traced his roots to Ago Isaga, Pahayi, Ilaro, and Kemta Odutolu, Abeokuta, crediting President Bola Tinubu as his political mentor.

As Senator for Ogun West, he said he had facilitated 1,015 projects across all 20 LGAs, and that his bill upgrading the Federal Polytechnic, Ilaro, to a Federal University of Technology was now law.

While introducing his running mate, Alhaja Kudirat Abiodun Adegunwa-Balogun, Adeola declared that his deputy was not a spare tyre but an important partner in governance.

He promised fiscal discipline through a Single Window Revenue Collection System, with no new taxes and no double taxation.

Within 30 days of taking office, he promised a Governor’s Delivery Unit, performance agreements for commissioners, a public dashboard tracking six indicators quarterly, and an annual State of Ogun Report.

“We will measure progress not by the ribbons we cut, but by the lives we change,” he said.

Adeola also assured the people of Ogun West, who have waited 50 years since 1976 to produce a governor, that their waiting had not been in vain.

“My roots are in Yewaland; my duty is to all of Ogun. I do not ask you to believe me. I ask you to hold me to this covenant,” Adeola concluded.

Earlier, in his welcome address, the Ogun APC State Chairman, Yemi Sanusi, said the event was meant to tell the people of the state about the next chapter of the government.

He commended Governor Abiodun for ensuring a hitch-free consensus arrangement that produced Senator Adeola, expressing confidence that the governorship candidate would continue to take Ogun to a higher pedestal.

In his remarks, Osoba expressed delight that the state would move further forward with Adeola at the helm of affairs.

Osoba urged the people of the state to vote massively for Senator Adeola as the next governor of Ogun so that he could continue the good work of the incumbent administration.
