I can go home, sleep comfortably, Allah gives power to whom he pleases – Tinubu

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 18 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

President Bola Tinubu has said he can return home and sleep comfortably because he believes that political power comes from Allah, as he urged residents of Kebbi State to support his re-election bid in 2027.

Tinubu made the remarks on Tuesday during his presidential campaign rally in Birnin Kebbi, the state capital, where he thanked residents for supporting him in the 2023 general election.

The President expressed confidence that the people of the state would turn out in large numbers to vote for him and other APC candidates in the next election.

“It’s Allah who gives power to whom He pleases. I ask for it. May Allah bless you. Thank you very much. I can go home comfortably and sleep; I know that you will come out to vote from top to bottom,” he said.

Tinubu also acknowledged the contributions of former and serving governors of the state, thanking Kebbi residents for their support during the previous election.

“I salute them, all the governors who served in this state. I come to thank you for the last election for voting for me; you voted for our senators; now I come back to say thank you,” he said.

The President assured the people of his administration’s commitment to Kebbi State and the North-West, as well as its agenda for national peace, stability and development.

He identified education, healthcare and other social infrastructure as key areas requiring continued attention, urging residents to maintain peace and unity.

“We are united with one voice; we want progress, we want development, we want education, medical health facilities that will speak for our people and will take care of all of us. I come once again to plead with you to maintain peace, friendship, and solidarity,” Tinubu added.

He also thanked residents for remaining at the rally under the sun, describing their presence as a demonstration of support for his party.
