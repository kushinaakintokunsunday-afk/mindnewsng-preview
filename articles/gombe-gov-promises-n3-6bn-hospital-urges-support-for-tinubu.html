Gombe gov promises N3.6bn hospital, urges support for Tinubu

By Punch Punch 23h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Gombe State Governor, Muhammadu Yahaya, has promised to construct a N3.6bn general hospital in Billiri Local Government Area as part of efforts to expand health

Yahaya disclosed this during a visit to the palace of the Mai Tangle, Malam Danladi Sanusi-Maiyamba, on Monday, while leading the All Progressives Congress Campaign Council on a political tour of the area.

The governor said the proposed hospital was in response to a longstanding demand by residents, assuring them that construction would commence soon.

He said, “The proposed N3.6bn general hospital is part of our commitment to improving healthcare delivery and responding to the needs of our people in Billiri.”

Yahaya explained that the facility would complement existing investments in primary healthcare centres across the wards of the local government, as well as the Federal Orthopaedic Hospital approved by President Bola Tinubu for Billiri.

According to him, the federal institution would improve access to specialised medical services and address complex healthcare needs in the area.

The governor also urged residents to support Tinubu and other APC candidates in the 2027 general elections, promising that his successor would sustain the development programmes of his administration.

He acknowledged the economic difficulties associated with the Tinubu administration’s reforms but described them as “necessary decisions that previous governments had avoided for decades”.

Yahaya expressed optimism that the reforms would eventually produce positive results, urging Nigerians to remain patient and support the process.

He attributed the development recorded in Gombe State to the relative peace and security enjoyed across the state, noting that stability remained essential to implementing government projects.

Responding, the Mai Tangle, Sanusi-Maiyamba, commended the governor for his administration’s interventions, particularly efforts to sustain peace and security in Billiri.

The traditional ruler also prayed for continued peace and stability in the local government and Gombe State before, during and after the 2027 general elections.
