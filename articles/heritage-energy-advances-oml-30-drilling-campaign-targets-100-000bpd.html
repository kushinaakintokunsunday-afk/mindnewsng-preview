Heritage Energy advances OML 30 drilling campaign, targets 100,000bpd

By Punch Punch 18 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Heritage Energy Operational Services Limited, Operator of OML 30 on behalf of its Joint Venture Partners, NNPC Exploration and Production Limited and Shoreline

The Company’s Assistant General Manager, Technology Portfolio Development, Adejare Oni, who represented the Chief Executive Officer, Ado Oseragbaje, at the recently concluded 44th Nigerian Association of Petroleum Explorationists Annual International Conference and Exhibition, highlighted the strategic importance of the campaign in advancing the company’s asset development and production growth ambitions.

He noted that the drilling campaign underscores Heritage Energy’s commitment to maximising the value of OML 30 through sustained investment, subsurface resource optimisation and disciplined operational execution.

“Unlocking the full potential of our assets requires sustained investment, a deeper understanding of subsurface resources and disciplined execution,” Oni stated.

Heritage Energy is targeting production of 100,000 barrels of oil per day by 2030, underpinned by a focused strategy encompassing sustained capital investment, disciplined reservoir management, technological advancement and operational excellence.

“Achieving this ambition requires an integrated approach to asset development, combining strategic investment, effective reservoir management, operational excellence and a deeper understanding of our subsurface potential. Our focus remains on maximising asset value, replenishing reserves and delivering sustainable, long-term value for Nigeria’s energy industry,” he added.

Oni further highlighted the importance of integrating drilling activities with effective reservoir management, technological innovation and operational discipline to sustain production growth and strengthen the asset’s long-term performance.

He also emphasised the company’s commitment to strengthening its technological capabilities through an accelerated digital transformation agenda and the implementation of its flagship Operating Management System.

These initiatives are designed to standardise processes, enhance operational efficiency, strengthen performance management and improve the quality of business and operational decision-making.

“The future competitiveness of Nigeria’s upstream industry will depend on our ability to translate resource potential into tangible value through technical innovation, strategic investment and operational discipline,” he said.
