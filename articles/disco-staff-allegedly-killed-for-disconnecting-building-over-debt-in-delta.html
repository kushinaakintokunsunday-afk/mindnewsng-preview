DisCo Staff Allegedly Killed For Disconnecting Building Over Debt In Delta

By Channels TV Channels TV 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The management of Benin Electricity Distribution Company (BEDC) has called on the Delta State Government to strengthen the protection of electricity workers following the alleged killing of one of its staff members, 38-year-old Oscar Uduka Eze, in Umunede, Ika North East Local Government Area of Delta State.

The company made the call on Wednesday during a press briefing by its Acting Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, Jonathan Lawani, at the BEDC headquarters in Benin City.

BEDC said Eze was allegedly stabbed to death on Monday, November 16, 2026, while carrying out his official duties in the community, following the disconnection of electricity supply to a property over an outstanding bill of nearly N300,000.

According to the company, the deceased was performing an authorised assignment within the regulatory framework of the electricity distribution sector when some persons allegedly traced him to another location in the community and stabbed him repeatedly.

Describing the incident as a painful loss, Lawani said the killing of the staff member was an attack on the company’s workforce and called for justice for the deceased and his family.

“Some hoodlums, some men who claim to be more powerful than God, traced him to a house within the same community and stabbed him with knives until he died on the spot,” he said.

The BEDC management explained that disconnection remained an approved regulatory mechanism for addressing non-payment of electricity bills, insisting that Eze had acted within the scope of his official responsibilities.

The company also disclosed that some suspects had been arrested in connection with the incident, expressing confidence that the relevant authorities would ensure a thorough investigation and prosecution of those involved.

“Sad as it is, we cannot recover his life. We can never bring his life back to his people. But one thing is certain: we must bring justice to his family,” Lawani stated.

BEDC further expressed concern that the incident could threaten its operations in Delta State, urging the state government to provide a safe working environment for personnel in the electricity sector.

The company warned that failure to address the security concerns could compel its management to consult with the board on the next steps regarding its operations in the state.

It stressed that the safety of its workforce remained essential to the delivery of electricity services and called on government and well-meaning Nigerians to condemn attacks on personnel carrying out legitimate official assignments.
