We’ll resolve Tinubu, Makinde’s differences before 2027 – Olugbon

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Taiwo Oluwadare, Ibadan

The Olugbon of Orile-Igbon, Oba Francis Olushola Alao, has said there is no rift between President Bola Tinubu and Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde, expressing confidence that any disagreements between the two leaders would be resolved.

The monarch spoke on Wednesday when he received the Oyo State Allied Peoples Movement (APM) governorship candidate, Hon. Bimbo Adekanmbi, his running mate, Engr. Mufutau Open Salawu, and other party chieftains at his palace in Orile-Igbon, Surulere Local Government Area of the state.

Oba Alao recalled his support for Tinubu’s presidential ambition in 2023, saying he was among the few traditional rulers in the South West who openly declared their support for the president at the time.

“Nobody can intimidate us. We have been in this game for a very long time. I was the National Secretary of the Tunji Braithwaite Foundation, but it is during critical situations that we come out,” he said.

Addressing the relationship between Tinubu and Makinde, the monarch said he was confident that the two leaders would resolve any differences, adding that he would attend the president’s scheduled visit to Ibadan on Saturday.

“There is no issue between Makinde and Tinubu. It is a family affair, a father-and-son issue. We will resolve all the issues before 2027,” he said.

Oba Alao also expressed confidence that the South West would retain the presidency until 2031, saying efforts would be made to secure the support of political stakeholders in the North.

“It is sacrosanct that the South West should retain the presidency until 2031. We appeal to our people in the North to show understanding and allow us to maintain the North-South balance,” he said.

Recalling his meeting with Tinubu in 2023, the monarch said the president had told him that his administration inherited both assets and liabilities.

“When I visited President Bola Ahmed Tinubu in 2023, he told me that he had inherited both assets and liabilities. Did he disclose the liabilities?

“He has fantastic policies, but his policies are transformative. Before they become fully effective, they require time,” Olugbon said.

Describing both Tinubu and Makinde as close associates, Oba Alao said his loyalty to the president did not diminish his relationship with the Oyo governor.

“Tinubu is a very close person to me, just like Makinde. Tinubu is a very good person, and Makinde is a very good person.

“The president knows how we played the game in 2023. But at the same time, Makinde is my man. I belong to the same political institution as the late Governor Abiola Ajimobi. Zacch is also from that political school.

“Zacch Adedeji is my son. Omo daadaa ni (He is a good child). But must we fight? We belong to the same family,” he said.

The monarch also praised Makinde’s administration for its management of state resources, particularly the payment of salaries, infrastructure development and care for retirees. He challenged Adekanmbi to surpass the governor’s achievements if elected in 2027.

“Before the allocation increased, Makinde was managing the resources of the state very well. Makinde pays salaries, promotes infrastructure development and takes care of retirees. You must do more than what Makinde has done. Please, serve Oyo State very well,” he said.

On Adekanmbi’s ambition, Oba Alao described him as a brilliant person and disclosed that the APM candidate had received endorsements from traditional rulers in other parts of the state.

“Before I arrived yesterday, Ogbomoso had already endorsed Bimbo. We must work collectively. I have known Bimbo for a while now, and he is a brilliant person.

“Onpetu, Oba Sunday Oyediran, and the Soun of Ogbomoso, Oba Ghandi Olaoye, have endorsed Bimbo. So, what am I going to do? I will pray for you.

“A week before the election, as I did in 2023, I will tell you who the next governor of Oyo State will be.

“All the governorship candidates are from Ibadan, but some Ibadan people are closer to Ogbomoso and Ibarapa. The Ibadan candidate who is close to us is the one we are endorsing.

“All the traditional rulers in Surulere asked me to tell you that they have endorsed you. We will ensure that we deliver the right candidate in 2027,” the monarch said.

Earlier, Adekanmbi said he visited the palace to pay homage to the monarch, whom he described as a father and mentor, rather than for political reasons.

“Baba is an accountant, and I am an accountant too. I have been drinking from his fountain of knowledge for a long time, and I will continue to do so.

“We must ensure that Oyo State moves forward and not backwards. It is about sustaining the good work Governor Makinde is doing. From the first day we assume office, it will be about continuity.

“We are here for Baba to pray for us. I am the APM governorship candidate and Olugbon’s son. We have heard everything you have told us, and I can assure you that we will implement them,” he stated.

Adekanmbi subsequently presented the party’s candidates for the various elective positions in Oyo North Senatorial District ahead of the 2027 general elections to the monarch.

The Director-General of the APM Presidential and Governorship Campaign Council, Dr Nureni Adeniran, said the visit was to introduce the candidates to the monarch and seek his support and prayers ahead of the elections.

The party’s deputy governorship candidate, Salawu, commended the Olugbon for his uprightness and warm reception, recalling that the monarch was among the first people he informed about his emergence as APM deputy governorship candidate.

“Our relationship with you has never been in doubt. You are one of the people with whom we have maintained a good relationship,” he said.
