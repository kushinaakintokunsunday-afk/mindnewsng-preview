Raya ruled out of Arsenal’s Champions League clash with Dortmund

By Punch Punch 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya will miss Tuesday’s UEFA Champions League clash against Borussia Dortmund after sustaining an injury during Saturday’s Premier League draw with Newcastle United.

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta disclosed this in an update published on the club’s website on Monday, saying the goalkeeper appeared to have avoided a serious injury.

“He’s not fit for tomorrow unfortunately,” Arteta told reporters at the Sobha Realty Training Centre. “But it doesn’t look too serious. We need some more tests tomorrow, but it’s quite encouraging.”

The manager said the extent of the pain Raya experienced immediately after the incident had initially raised concerns.

“With the pain that he was showing immediately after the action, we were all quite worried, but he seems to be better than we expected. When you look at the action, it doesn’t seem to be much on it, but the extent of the pain that he had and how he was struggling, it was quite worrying,” he said.

Arteta added that the initial tests had provided more encouraging indications, although further examinations would determine how long the Spain international would be unavailable.

“Yes, as I mentioned, we expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain that he had. The first test that we’ve done showed something that is much more encouraging,” he said.

He added, “There’s no need for surgery, which was the main fear, especially because that would rule him out for months, so let’s see. Tomorrow, we’ll have more tests, and we will know.”

With Raya unavailable for the Dortmund encounter, Arteta expressed confidence in the goalkeeping options available to him, naming Kepa and Illan Meslier.

“We know, and I think we’ve seen what David brings to the team, but as well that’s why we are very protective with Kepa and Illan,” he said.

“Everybody needs to be ready because these things unfortunately happen, and now we need to show how well we are protected and the good keepers we have with Kepa and Illan.

“We always assess every situation to make the right call, and it’s quite clear what we’re going to do,” Arteta concluded.
