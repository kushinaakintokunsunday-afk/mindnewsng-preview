Messi should have been sent off for throwing ball at opponent – Columbus coach

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Columbus Crew coach, Laurent Courtois, has said Lionel Messi deserved a red card for throwing the ball at an opponent during Inter Miami’s 3-0 defeat in the Maj

Columbus Crew coach, Laurent Courtois, has said Lionel Messi deserved a red card for throwing the ball at an opponent during Inter Miami’s 3-0 defeat in the Major League Soccer Cup playoffs on Saturday.

Messi lost his temper just before half-time when he hurled the ball into the face of Columbus forward Gonzalo Tapia during a throw-in, with Miami trailing 1-0.

The Argentine and Tapia were both shown yellow cards by referee Allen Chapman, but Courtois insisted after the match that Messi should have been sent off.

“Everybody knows that he deserved more than a yellow card,” Courtois said at a post-match press conference. “It’s just really sad.”

However, Chapman defended his decision to caution Messi, telling a pool reporter that the force used was negligible and did not amount to violent conduct.

“The force used was negligible and the offense did not reach the level of violent conduct,” he said.

Inter Miami were already down to 10 men when the incident occurred, having conceded an early penalty converted by Chilean forward Tapia.

The reigning MLS champions went on to suffer a 3-0 defeat at Nu Stadium, with Tapia scoring his second goal after Taha Habroune had doubled Columbus’ lead shortly after the break.

The result puts seventh-seeded Columbus, the 2023 champions, in a strong position to eliminate Messi and Inter Miami from the playoffs. They will advance with a victory in the second match of the best-of-three series in Ohio next Friday.

Miami coach Kily Gonzalez admitted that his side had failed to deliver the performance they expected.

“It was a game where nothing went right for us, and we effectively created their chances for them,” Gonzalez said.

“We didn’t produce the great performance we had hoped for. Everything went wrong, but we have to learn from it because next time, if we don’t win, we’re out,” he added.

“There’s no better challenge for us as a team than this. It’s a test of character, and I know my team has that character.”

In other MLS playoff matches on Saturday, Joaquin Valiente scored twice as Dallas defeated Portland 3-0 in their Western Conference series opener.

The 25-year-old Uruguayan midfielder scored in the 53rd and 55th minutes, while Samuel Sarver added a third goal in the fourth minute of stoppage time.

Dallas can secure qualification with a victory away to Portland next Saturday.

In the Eastern Conference, 19-year-old Peyton Miller scored once and provided an assist as New England defeated Philadelphia 2-0.

Miller’s 10th-minute effort deflected off Philadelphia defender Nathan Harriel before he sealed the victory in the 73rd minute from a Jack Harrison cross.

New England will advance to the Eastern Conference semi-finals with a win in the return fixture next Saturday.
