Emmanuel, Quadre sweep Da-Silva tennis championship titles

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigeria’s Michael Emmanuel and Oyinlomo Quadre completed a memorable campaign at the maiden Destiny Da-Silva Tennis Open Championship, winning the men’s and women’s singles titles respectively on Saturday at the National Stadium, Surulere, Lagos.

Emmanuel, who entered the tournament as Nigeria’s third-ranked player, defeated Yusuf Abubakar 6-2, 6-3 to claim the men’s singles crown, while Quadre overcame Success Ogujobi 6-1, 6-1 to win the women’s title.

The victories completed a successful outing for both players, who had earlier won their respective doubles events on Friday.

Emmanuel partnered Matthew Abamu to the men’s doubles title, with the pair defeating Ikechukwu Iloputa and Chima Michael 6-1, 3-6, 10-7.

Quadre also claimed the women’s doubles crown alongside Ntoro Bassey. They recovered from losing the first set to beat teenagers Aina Goodnews and Samuel Gloria 3-6, 6-2, 10-5.

Speaking after her singles victory, Quadre said winning the title had been her target from the start of the competition.

“That was my goal coming into this tournament and I’m very happy to achieve that.

“It wasn’t exactly easy. The points were going pretty fast, but I had to stay locked in because she’s a really good player,” she said.

Tournament organiser Destiny Da-Silva praised the quality of tennis on display, saying the championship had provided an opportunity to discover emerging talents.

“I have witnessed tennis tournaments in several countries, and Nigeria most especially, but I have never seen the kinds of talents that have been on display. We have discovered some new talents and they have been excellent on the court, and they behave well off the court too,” he said.

Da-Silva also disclosed plans to expand the championship next year, with an international edition and a separate competition for Nigerian players.

“Come next year, this tournament will become international. We will not only have the international edition; we will also have a local version for Nigerian players,” he said.

In the wheelchair women’s category, Andrea Usoh and Chitturu Nwaozuzu won the doubles title after defeating Chika Enwerem and Bisoye Bankole 6-1, 6-2.

Nwaozuzu subsequently completed a singles and doubles double, beating Usoh 6-4, 6-4 in the wheelchair women’s singles final.

In the men’s wheelchair category, top seed Alex Adewale defeated Razaki Bankole to win the singles title and also partnered Pelumi Odunuga to claim the doubles crown.

Edet Akpaso emerged victorious in the veterans’ men’s singles category, defeating Samuel Obadofin 6-4, 4-6, 10-6.

The men’s and women’s singles champions received N1.5m each, while the runners-up earned N885,000 apiece.

Doubles winners in the men’s and women’s categories received N500,000, with the runners-up taking home N295,000.

In the wheelchair events, the singles champions received N500,000 each, while the runners-up earned N295,000. The wheelchair doubles winners received N300,000, with the runners-up getting N177,000.

The veterans’ men’s singles champion also received N300,000, while the runner-up went home with N177,000.
