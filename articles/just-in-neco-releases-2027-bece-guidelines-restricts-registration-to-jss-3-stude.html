JUST IN: NECO releases 2027 BECE guidelines, restricts registration to JSS 3 students

The National Examinations Council (NECO) has released detailed guidelines for the registration of candidates for the 2027 Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

The Council announced the development in a circular shared on its official X handle on Friday.

The circular was addressed to all State Ministries of Education, the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Education Secretariat, as well as principals and commandants of Junior Secondary Schools.

According to NECO, only students in their final year of Junior Secondary School (JSS 3) are eligible to register for the 2027 examination.

The Council issued the guidelines to provide information on the registration process and ensure that schools and relevant education authorities comply with the requirements for the examination.

“The examination is strictly for school-based candidates in Nigeria and overseas. Private candidates are not allowed,” NECO said

According to the council, schools must now mandatorily nominate prospective supervisors during the registration process, saying that registration cannot be completed without this step.

Every candidate will be issued a unique National Learners’ Identification Number (NLIN).

Possession of a valid National Identification Number (NIN) is compulsory for all candidates.

Transfer or admission of students into JSS 3 from other schools is prohibited.

NECO added that schools are required to correctly indicate candidates with special needs (including low vision, complete blindness, albinism, hearing impairment, and autism) during the offline bio-data entry. Official notification to NECO is required for candidates with autism.

“Schools must complete offline registration and validation before uploading data online. Candidates must verify their NIN, passport photograph, name, date of birth, gender, state of origin, local government area, and subjects. All corrections must be done offline. A signed Offline Validation List must be submitted to the NECO State Office,” NECO averred.

NECO warned that it will not be held responsible for registration errors made by schools. Double registration of candidates will not be entertained.

It, however, warned that any request for changes to passport photograph, name, date of birth, gender, subjects, state of origin, or LGA after online upload will attract a non-validation penalty of ₦10,000 per candidate.

“Change of passport photograph is only allowed before the end of the registration period, and only one change (name or photograph) is permitted.

“Schools must first be activated by their State Coordinator after submitting the required validation documents. All payments must be made into the NECO Treasury Single Account (TSA) via the Remita platform through the school’s NECO online portal. After successful payment and verification, a registration quota will be allocated to the school,” it added.

The Council advised schools paying in instalments to make the first payment for a minimum of 20 candidates. All payments are non-refundable.

NECO urged schools and education authorities to carefully study the guidelines and strictly comply with all procedures.
