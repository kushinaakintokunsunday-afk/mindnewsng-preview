AFCON 2027 Qualifier: Super Eagles held to goalless draw by Tanzania

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

Nigeria’s Super Eagles suffered a setback in their quest to secure qualification for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) after playing a goalless draw against Tanzania on Tuesday in their Group L qualifying match in Zanzibar.

Despite dominating possession and creating several clear scoring chances, the Nigerian side failed to convert their opportunities into goals.

The Super Eagles came close to taking the lead in the 34th minute when Tolu Arokodare’s low shot beat the Tanzanian goalkeeper but struck the woodwork.

Moses Usor also threatened the hosts four minutes later with a well-directed effort, but the Tanzania goalkeeper reacted quickly to push the ball away and prevent Nigeria from scoring.

The draw means Nigeria missed the opportunity to claim maximum points from the encounter as their campaign for a place at the 2027 AFCON continues.

Fisayo Dele-Bashiru had earlier tried his luck from outside the box, but his effort went wide, while Arokodare also sent an attempt off target.

Nigeria continued to search for the breakthrough after the restart, but Tanzania’s back proved resilient.

The hosts threatened through Kibu Denis, who had two attempts from the right side of the penalty area in the opening 10 minutes of the second half, but both went wide.

Head coach Eric Chelle introduced Alex Iwobi and Akor Adams for Taiwo Awoniyi and Dele-Bashiru in the 61st minute as Nigeria sought to increase their attacking threat.

Tochukwu and Alhassan Yusuf were later brought on for Wilfred Ndidi and Usor in the 73rd minute.

Adams and Bassey also had chances in the final minutes, but their attempts were not enough.

Arokodare also failed to hit the target in the 85th minute, while an earlier through ball from Yusuf found the forward in an offside position.

Nigeria had beaten Tanzania 3-1 in Friday’s first leg at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo, with Alhassan Yusuf opening the scoring before Arokodare netted twice in the second half.

The Super Eagles began Tuesday’s fixture with six points from three matches, one behind Group L leaders Guinea-Bissau, who had drawn 1-1 with Madagascar in their previous outing.

Tanzania had already secured qualification for the tournament as co-hosts, leaving Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau and Madagascar to contest the group’s remaining qualifying place.

The 2027 AFCON will be jointly hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda from June 19 to July 17.
