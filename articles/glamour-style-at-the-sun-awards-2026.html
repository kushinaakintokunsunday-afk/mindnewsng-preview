Glamour, style at The Sun Awards 2026

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

It was glamour, style, panache at the red carpet of The Sun Awards, which took place on Friday November 13 at the Eko Convention Centre, Eko Hotel, Victoria Isl

It was glamour, style, panache at the red carpet of The Sun Awards, which took place on Friday November 13 at the Eko Convention Centre, Eko Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos. Guests –  men, women, young and old – came in their best to witness this yearly sought-after awards ceremony. Classic, timeless black in dresses and accessories, made their way into the event.  Long gowns, native attire, bright colours, all added to the beauty of the event.

Black dress

A lot of guests especially women, were seen in different styles of black. The black dress is a timeless wardrobe essential. Its neutral colour makes it a perfect canvas to pair with statement accessories, bold colours, soft pastels or metallic finishes. It flatters every body shape, suits every skin tone, and transitions seamlessly from daytime events to after-hour affairs.

Maxi dress

A maxi dress is one of the most versatile, elegant, and feminine pieces you can have in your wardrobe.  It transforms completely depending on how you style them to suit any occasion.  For a special event such as The Sun Awards, a long black dress never fails. Simply add a different touch with red, burgundy, or midnight blue tights, and complete the look with a cocktail shoe; A look that exudes sophistication, without the need for excessive sparkle or embellishment. 

Suited men

Some of the men came in suits. As it is with the suit, it adorns one’s personality and makes the person more noticeable. A suit is an outfit that makes any man’s confidence soar. The men in suit at the occasion looked well put together.

Native and traditional attires (Agbada/kaftan/Senator, Isi agu)

The male guests who wore traditional attires to the prestigious awards ceremony, also stood out.  Agbada, kaftan, senator styles, Isi agu, and so on, Nigerian native styles have always been about grandeur and dignity. Nigerian menswear continues to stand out, driven by bold new trends, rich textures, and colours that make a lasting impression.

Red cap

The Igbo red cap is a sacred symbol of authority, honour, and tradition in Igbo land, though it has increasingly crossed over into contemporary African fashion. The red cap is crafted for ceremonies, weddings, and cultural events.

Though it is worn by Igbo high chiefs, which symbolises authority; it also represents the entire institution of leadership, authority, and power in Igbo culture. Today, it has a become a fashion for men who want to make a style statement at big events.
