Yahaya unveils 30 buses as Gombe Line purchases hit 110

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The governor, who inaugurated the buses at the Government House, Gombe, commended the management of the transport company for improving service delivery, strengthening its financial position and settling more than 98 per cent of inherited liabilities.

Yahaya described the acquisition as part of his administration’s commitment to revitalising public transportation, expanding access to affordable mobility and creating employment opportunities.

He praised the Director-General of Gombe Line, Sani Sabo, and his management team for their efforts in repositioning the company, particularly in addressing its inherited financial challenges and improving operational efficiency.

The governor reaffirmed his administration’s determination to sustain investments in the transport sector to ensure residents enjoy safe, affordable and reliable transportation services.

Speaking at the ceremony, Sabo said the latest acquisition reflected the governor’s continued commitment to strengthening public transportation in the state.

He recalled that the administration procured 50 Toyota buses in 2019 and another 30 in 2022, with the latest 30 bringing the total number of buses acquired under Yahaya’s administration to 110.

Reviewing his three years in office since September 2, 2023, the Director-General said he inherited an organisation burdened by huge financial liabilities, grounded vehicles and inefficient manual operations.

He said, “When I assumed office, Gombe Line was confronted with huge inherited liabilities, grounded vehicles and operational challenges. However, through deliberate reforms and the support of the state government, we have been able to settle over 98 per cent of the inherited liabilities.”

Sabo listed the introduction of an online ticketing system, the revival and maintenance of grounded vehicles, and the expansion of operational routes to Dei-Dei in Abuja, Biu and Damaturu among the achievements recorded under his leadership.

Other interventions, according to him, include the establishment of a registered courier service, the construction of an NSCDC outpost at the Mega Park, the ongoing construction of a company workshop and improvements to existing park facilities.

He added that the management had prioritised staff welfare through improved working conditions and prompt payment of salaries, while a mosque was also constructed through voluntary contributions.

On the newly commissioned buses, Sabo assured residents that the management would ensure proper maintenance and effective utilisation of the vehicles.

He said, “We will ensure that these buses are properly maintained and efficiently managed to guarantee their sustainability and maximise the benefits of the government’s investment.”

The Director-General reiterated the company’s commitment to providing safe, affordable and reliable transportation for residents of Gombe State and passengers travelling to other parts of the country.
