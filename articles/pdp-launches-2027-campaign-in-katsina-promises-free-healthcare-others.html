PDP Launches 2027 Campaign In Katsina, Promises Free Healthcare, Others

By Channels TV Channels TV 6d ago 3 min read

The Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) has officially launched its 2027 governorship campaign in Katsina State, with the party’s National Chairman, Abdul-Rahman Mohammed, expressing confidence that the opposition will reclaim the state and build on what he described as the developmental legacy of the late President Umaru Musa Yar’adua.

Speaking on Wednesday at the official flag-off ceremony in Katsina, Mohammed declared that the PDP was fully prepared for the 2027 general elections and urged party supporters to mobilise massively to ensure victory at the polls.

He also formally presented party flags to the PDP governorship candidate, Senator Yakubu Danmarke, the party’s three senatorial candidates and 15 House of Representatives candidates, while encouraging all PDP candidates to immediately commence campaigns across their constituencies.

The national chairman praised the unity and commitment of PDP members in Katsina, insisting that the state remained a stronghold of the opposition.

According to him, the PDP offers the best alternative for the people of Katsina and would work to consolidate the achievements associated with the administration of the late President Umaru Musa Yar’adua.

“I have seen for myself that Katsina is PDP-dominated. Come out en masse and vote for the PDP. Yakubu Lado Danmarke’s success is the success of Katsina State. We will do everything possible to ensure the PDP wins Katsina and consolidates the good work started by the late President Umaru Musa Yar’adua,” he said.

In his address, the PDP governorship candidate, Senator Yakubu Lado Danmarke, declared that the people of Katsina had become disillusioned with the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), promising sweeping reforms if elected.

Lado pledged to restore the old pension scheme for civil servants, introduce free healthcare with essential drugs in public hospitals, provide free education, strengthen security, create jobs and expand economic opportunities for youths and women.

He also promised to equip hospitals with modern medical facilities to reduce the need for Nigerians to seek treatment abroad.

The PDP candidate criticised the APC-led administration in the state, alleging that key sectors had deteriorated despite significant federal allocations to Katsina.

He further expressed confidence that the PDP would emerge victorious in the 2027 elections, describing the party as the preferred choice of the electorate.

Earlier, the Katsina State PDP Chairman, Nura Amadi Kurfi, said the party remained united and well-positioned for victory, noting that the campaign flag-off featured the presentation of candidates for all elective positions, including three senatorial candidates, 15 House of Representatives candidates and 34 State House of Assembly candidates.

Kurfi thanked the party’s national leadership for its support and expressed optimism that the PDP would secure more than 1.5 million votes in the state.

Also speaking, the PDP senatorial candidate for Katsina South, Nura Khalil, described the campaign as “a battle between truth and falsehood,” urging supporters to remain committed to delivering victory for the party.

His counterparts for Katsina Central and Katsina North Senatorial Districts, Hon. Hamisu Gambo and Dauda Daura, also addressed supporters, expressing confidence that the PDP would reclaim Katsina State in the 2027 general elections.
