20 make first class as ESUT graduates 7,149 students

From Jude Chinedu, Enugu

The Enugu State University of Science and Technology (ESUT), Enugu, will graduate 7,149 students at its 22nd convocation ceremonies for the 2024/2025 academic session, with 20 of the graduands making First Class Honours.

The graduands comprise 5,635 first-degree recipients, 298 Postgraduate Diploma holders, 811 master’s degree recipients and 405 doctoral degree candidates.

Of the first-degree graduands, 1,248 obtained Second Class Upper Division, 3,956 made Second Class Lower Division, while 177 graduated with Third Class and 237 fell into the pass or unclassified category.

The Vice-Chancellor, Professor Aloysius-Michaels Okolie, disclosed this on Monday during a press briefing ahead of the convocation ceremonies at the Peter Mbah Auditorium, ESUT.

He also announced Miss Marycynthia Cheluchi Ona of the Department of Medical Laboratory Sciences, Faculty of Allied Health Sciences, as the overall best graduand, with a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 4.85.

Anthony Judith Chisom of the Department of Psychology came second with 4.74 CGPA, while Nwobodo Chukwunonso of the Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences emerged third with 4.70 CGPA.

Okolie said the outstanding graduands would receive special recognition during the ceremonies.

He described the 2026 convocation as the fifth consecutive annual convocation organised by the present management since assuming office in April 2022.

He said the university had fulfilled its commitment to restoring the tradition of annual convocation as part of efforts to reposition the institution and improve its academic standing.

The Vice-Chancellor also disclosed that six ESUT Law graduates who attended the last Nigerian Law School recorded First Class results in their respective examinations.

On the institution’s academic expansion, he said the National Universities Commission (NUC) had approved eight new programmes, including Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Intelligence and Security Studies, International Relations and Diplomacy, Religious Studies and Philosophy.

He added that the university had commenced admission into the programmes for the 2026/2027 academic session, while the Senate had approved the establishment of a Faculty of Computing.

Okolie further announced that the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria had increased the admission quota for the university’s College of Medicine from 120 to 350, making it the second-highest in the country.

He said 19 of the 20 undergraduate programmes assessed by the NUC during its October/November 2025 accreditation exercise secured full accreditation, while Surveying and Geo-Informatics received interim accreditation.

The Vice-Chancellor also disclosed that the university had been selected to host a Tertiary Education Trust Fund (TETFund)/NUC Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

The convocation lecture, scheduled for Thursday, November 19, will be delivered by the founder and former Governor of old Anambra State, Chief Jim Nwobodo.

Three eminent Nigerians, including Nwobodo, Robert Anwatu and Lady Betty Granville Akuvue, will also receive honorary doctorate degrees during the convocation.
