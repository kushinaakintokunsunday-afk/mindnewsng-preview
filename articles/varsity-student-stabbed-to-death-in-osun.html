Varsity student stabbed to death in Osun

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Some assailants have reportedly stabbed to death a student of Westland University, Iwo, Osun State, identified as Monica Mary. This has sparked panic among stud

This has sparked panic among students and residents of the town.

Sources in the Agbowo area of Iwo who where Mary lived, told PUNCH Online during telephone interviews on Wednesday that the incident occurred around 11:30pm on Tuesday.

It was gathered that Mary, a 300-level student, had previously lived on the university campus before moving into private accommodation in Agbowo, where she was killed.

A resident of the area, who spoke with our correspondent, said, “She was murdered around her hostel by 11:32pm at Agbowo, Iwo.

“Someone said she heard her noise when the incident happened but no one could come out. She had gone out to charge her mobile phone. She was a calm girl and does not get into trouble.

“It was pathetic such a thing happened to her. Her parents had been informed. The matter was reported to the police in Iwo Division but the entire student community in the town is in panic mood over the incident.”

When contacted, the spokesperson for the Osun State Police Command, Abiodun Ojelabi, confirmed the incident.

Ojelabi said police operatives had visited the scene and assured that those responsible would be apprehended.

He said, “We received the report of the murder of Monica Mary, a student of Westland University, Iwo, this morning. On arrival, we observed the area and the corpse, and a cut was discovered on her neck. It was suspected to be a knife cut.

“We got information that she went to charge her phone and was returning when the incident happened. She had been living on campus since she got admission into the university. She just moved out of campus this semester.

“We recovered a knife, a phone pouch and other items. The Commissioner of Police has directed that the case should be transferred to the Criminal Investigation Department. Investigation is ongoing and the parents are ready to support the police.”

The incident is not the first reported case of a university student being stabbed to death in Iwo.

In January 2025, a 200-level student of Bowen University, Iwo, identified as Paul Thinker, was reportedly stabbed to death by unknown assailants.

The Osun State Police Command had said detectives were deployed to the scene and an investigation commenced to identify those responsible.
