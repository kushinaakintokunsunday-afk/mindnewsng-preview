Fountain Of Life Church Board Insists Jimmy Odukoya Remains Removed As Senior Pastor, Appoints Interim Leader - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng TheCable 6d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: TheCable

The board of trustees of Fountain of Life Church has insisted that Jimmy Odukoya, son of the late church founder Taiwo Odukoya, remains removed as senior pastor, despite his public rejection of the decision, and has appointed an interim leader to run the church.

In a statement issued on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, the board said it had resolved to entrust the church's interim spiritual and administrative leadership to Pastor Rotimi Okpaise, a pastor in the church. The interim arrangement will remain in place pending the conclusion of consultations and processes for appointing a substantive leadership structure, in line with the church's governing instruments and established procedures.

The timeline of the crisis:

  • November 16: the board announced it was withdrawing its recognition of Jimmy Odukoya as senior pastor with immediate effect, asking him to step down "forthwith from the office and all functions of Senior Pastor". It cited a review of the church's leadership and succession arrangements, longstanding governance concerns, and what it described as present doctrinal and moral controversies.
  • Same day: Jimmy Odukoya publicly rejected the removal, declaring that he remains senior pastor, while supporters described the board's action as invalid.
  • November 17: the board doubled down, stating that "its resolution of 16 November 2026 remains in force" and that it "stands by its earlier statement in its entirety".

The social media battle: the board also revealed that since the removal announcement, persons linked to the pastor had taken over the church's social media channels without its authorisation, using them to publish an account of events it considers "inaccurate and misleading". The board warned that statements currently appearing on those channels do not represent its position, and that only statements issued and signed on its behalf should be regarded as official. It said it is taking steps to restore the official communication channels to authorised control.

Why the board acted: it maintained that the decision was not intended as an act of "vindictiveness, humiliation or personal condemnation", but a necessary response to circumstances that could inflict lasting damage on the church and its legacy - and that the move was needed to create an atmosphere conducive to restoring peace, rebuilding trust and resolving the church's leadership and governance challenges.

Background: Fountain of Life Church, based in Lagos, was founded by the late Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, who died in November 2023. His son Jimmy Odukoya - also a Nollywood actor - took over as senior pastor after his death. The current standoff between the founder's son and the board of trustees has dominated Nigerian social media conversation for days, with mixed reactions from members of the public.

As it stands, the church now has an interim leadership structure under Pastor Rotimi Okpaise, while consultations continue towards a substantive leadership appointment.
