Cleric urges men of God to take up political, traditional leadership positions

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG

…As Bishop Timothy Kume celebrates 57th birthday, 19th anniversary of church

A renowned cleric and Founder of Faith Hill Prophetic Assembly (FHPA), Archbishop Emmanuel Jatau, has called on Christian clerics to take up political and traditional leadership positions, saying the involvement of righteous people in governance is essential to addressing the moral challenges confronting society.

Jatau said the growing presence of men of God in positions of traditional authority and political leadership would help promote righteousness, integrity and responsible leadership in the country.

He made the call during activities marking the 57th birthday celebration of the Presiding Bishop of FHPA, Makurdi, Bishop Timothy Kume, as well as the church’s 19th anniversary and 2026 annual convention.

According to him, evil thrives in society when people with good values shy away from leadership responsibilities, thereby allowing individuals without the fear of God to occupy positions of authority.

He said, “Evil reigns in the land because righteous people stay away from taking up responsibilities. If you allow unrighteous and unholy people to occupy positions of kingship and political authority, you will get what you see.”

The Archbishop expressed delight that more people were beginning to recognise the importance of godly leadership, citing the Tor Tiv and the Och’Idoma as examples of traditional rulers who are also pastors.

He also referenced other traditional rulers across the country who are men of God, expressing the hope that more Christian leaders would accept chieftaincy titles and contribute to the development of their communities.

Jatau further urged Nigerians to pay greater attention to their health, expressing concern over increasing cases of kidney failure and other health challenges.

He attributed some of the health concerns to the consumption of adulterated food and fake drinks, urging members of the public to be more careful about what they eat and drink.

Speaking on the 57th birthday of Bishop Kume, Jatau declared God’s abundant blessings, divine favour, good health and continued progress upon the celebrant. He prayed God to reward Kume for his service in the ministry, preserve his life and grant him greater joy, honour and promotion as he marks another year.

The Archbishop also declared that the celebrant’s life and ministry would continue to experience multiplication, restoration and testimonies, praying that God’s grace would sustain him and enable him to fulfil greater assignments in His vineyard. He described the occasion as an opportunity to celebrate God’s faithfulness and prayed for lasting blessings upon Kume and his household.

Earlier, the celebrant and presiding Bishop of Faith Hill Prophetic Assembly, Dr Timothy Kume expressed gratitude to God for His faithfulness upon his life and the achievements recorded by the church in its 19 years of existence.

He described the 2026 annual convention, which held from November 11 to 15, under the theme, “Abundant Life”, as a divine opportunity for spiritual renewal and positive transformation in the lives of participants.

Kume, who appreciated Archbishop Jatau for his love, support and care for him and the Benue church, also commended other ministers of God who contributed to the success of the convention.

He said the convention was designed to bring positive changes in the spiritual lives, marriages, finances, health and general circumstances of participants.

The cleric also identified the graduation of students of the Faith Bible Institute, the training arm of the church, and the ordination of deserving ministers as major highlights of the programme.

He explained that the ordination was in recognition of those found worthy of greater responsibilities in the service of God, while urging all Christians to remain committed to spreading the gospel.

Kume called on believers not to leave the responsibility of evangelism to ordained ministers alone, stressing that every Christian had a role to play in the Great Commission.

He further appreciated his family, friends and well wishers for gathering to celebrate his birthday, praying God to uplift them and meet them all at the point of their needs.

Those ordained include Aondoakaa Msendoo, Tindi ANDREW, Ieren Sunday Jacob, Alabi Bilikisu, Harry Benedicta, Comfort Orpin, Ejigbo Doris, Bernard Moses, Asawa Udende, And Iorshe Mercy.

Others are Evang. Iorundu Mathias, Mnyamchier Isaac, Beatrice Terngu, Lawrence Lydia, Franca Enenche, Terveshima Dorcas, Abe Nathie, Onuminya Enenche, Thomas Kor, Sandra Pila, Terfa Evelyn and Hyacinth Ahua.
