The Obidient Pulse: Massive Crowd As Obi Returns To Onitsha, Visits CKC Alma Mater

By Channels TV Channels TV 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Peter Obi, on Friday attracted a large crowd in Onitsha, Anambra State, as he led his campaign

Presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Peter Obi, on Friday attracted a large crowd in Onitsha, Anambra State, as he led his campaign team on a roadshow and town hall engagements ahead of the 2027 general elections. He began with a visit to his alma mater, Christ the King College (CKC), Onitsha, where he addressed students and said the school would produce Nigeria’s president in the future.

Stay At Polling Units To Protect Votes, Obi Urges Voters

Obi urged Nigerians to remain at polling units after voting to ensure their votes are counted and transmitted, declaring that nobody would be allowed to rig the 2027 general elections. Speaking at the town hall at All Saints Cathedral, Onitsha, he told supporters: “Sacrifice one day for Nigeria. That day of voting,” and to come with food and stay until votes are counted and transmitted.

Obi, Kwankwaso Pledge 10,000MW Power Supply

Obi promised that a government led by him and his running mate, Rabiu Kwankwaso, would generate, transmit, and distribute at least 10,000MW of electricity. He said Nigeria currently only generates, transmits, and distributes about 4,000MW, and that within their tenure Nigeria would reach at least 10,000MW.

Obi Promises 10,000 Health Centres, 40,000 Classrooms

Obi promised to establish 10,000 primary healthcare centres and build 40,000 primary school blocks across the country if elected president in 2027. He made the promises on Friday during the NDC town hall meeting at All Saints Cathedral, Onitsha.

Anambra Govt Says Obi Billboard Removed Over Signage Law, Not Politics

The Anambra State Government clarified that the campaign billboard belonging to Senator Victor Umeh, which featured Obi, was removed over an alleged violation of the state’s Signage Act, 2010, contrary to claims it was pulled down because of Obi’s identity. The state said although Umeh had paid the required signage fee, Obi had not complied with the payment requirement.

Don’t Attack Defectors, Obidient Coordinator Warns Supporters

The National Coordinator of the Global Obidient Movement, Dr Moses Paul, urged supporters not to insult or attack individuals who have left the group, saying political engagement should be guided by “respect and decency rather than bitterness”. He said the directive came from Obi, urging members to refrain from insulting, attacking, or making derogatory comments about those who decided to leave the movement.
