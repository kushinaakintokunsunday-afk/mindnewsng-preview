Divorce crisis: How my wife left Government House — Kano gov

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Kano State Governor, Abba Yusuf, has narrated how his wife, Zainab, allegedly left the Government House and subsequently filed for divorce at an Abuja court. Yu

Yusuf gave the account in an interview with the BBC Hausa service aired on Sunday, where he spoke about the breakdown of his marriage to Zainab, a niece of his former political mentor and ex-governor of Kano State, Rabiu Kwankwaso.

The governor said the couple had been married for about 20 years and had children together before the relationship deteriorated.

“This woman, Zainab, is the daughter of his (Kwankwaso’s) elder brother. He previously married her off to someone, though that marriage did not last long. After that, God brought us together, and I married her. We got married about 20 years ago, and we have children.

“Many things happened, to the point where her belongings were packed and she was taken away from my house, and through it all, I remained patient and did not speak out,” Yusuf said.

He added that he had assured their children that their mother would return home.

The governor, however, said she was surprised when she allegedly encouraged to take the matter to court in Abuja rather than Kano.

“Suddenly, she dragged me to court, a court in Abuja, not even in Kano,” he said.

Yusuf described the situation as unpleasant, adding that he had evidence and detailed information about how his wife was taken away but would not disclose the circumstances publicly.

Asked why he decided to speak about the matter, the governor denied politicising the family dispute.

“I am not the one who politicised it. If this was done to you, how would you feel?” he asked.

He further explained: “To have your wife’s belongings packed without your knowledge, for her to be taken away and kept elsewhere without your knowledge, taken to someone else’s house without your knowledge, and then suddenly you hear that she is the one who has sued you in court.”

The governor said the matter was still being examined and expressed hope that a resolution could be reached.

The interview comes amid the divorce proceedings between Yusuf and Zainab at the Upper Area Court in the Gudu District of Abuja.

The case, marked CV/97/26, was adjourned until December 15 after the Kano State Commissioner for Information, Sanusi Bature, who represented the governor, requested more time to brief his principal on the court processes.

Zainab reportedly filed the suit seeking the dissolution of their marriage, which began in 2006 and produced six children.

The marital crisis became public after Yusuf, while addressing pensioners at the Government House in Kano on November 16, acknowledged the development and likened his situation to a demotion from a corporal to a constable because he now had one wife.

He also said at the time that steps had been taken towards dissolving the marriage.

The dispute has attracted attention partly because of the political relationship between Yusuf and Kwankwaso, who was instrumental in his political rise.

Yusuf served as Kwankwaso’s personal assistant and later as Commissioner for Works during his second term as governor between 2011 and 2015.

However, their relationship deteriorated after Yusuf defected from the New Nigeria Peoples Party to the All Progressives Congress in January 2026.

Kwankwaso subsequently left the NNPP and joined the New Nigeria Democratic Congress, where he emerged as the running mate to its presidential candidate, Peter Obi.
