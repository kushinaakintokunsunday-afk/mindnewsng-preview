Insecurity: Tinubu’s Five-Year Defence Plan Ready, Says APC Campaign Council

By Channels TV Channels TV 15 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The APC Presidential Campaign Council says President Bola Tinubu’s administration is preparing a five-year defence strategy, alongside plans to recruit 78,000 additional military and police personnel, as part of efforts to tackle insecurity.

The council disclosed this in a statement on Sunday by its spokesperson, Dele Alake, outlining measures the administration says are aimed at strengthening the country’s security architecture and improving the capacity of security agencies.

According to the statement, the proposed Five-Year Strategic Defence Operations Plan will provide a framework for aligning military operations, training, equipment and expenditure with Nigeria’s security challenges.

The council said the strategy would adopt a coordinated, intelligence-led and technology-driven approach to tackling terrorism, banditry, kidnapping and other forms of criminality.

It also highlighted the outcome of military operations under the Tinubu administration, claiming that more than 15,000 terrorists and bandits had been eliminated in the last three years.

The statement further claimed that over 130,000 Boko Haram fighters and their dependants had surrendered through Operation Safe Corridor, the Federal Government’s rehabilitation programme for repentant insurgents.

The council described the figures as evidence of the administration’s combination of military operations and non-kinetic measures in addressing insecurity.

On efforts to increase the number of personnel available to security agencies, the statement said Tinubu had approved the recruitment of 28,000 additional soldiers and 50,000 police officers.

It also disclosed that four new Army divisions had been created, while existing military formations had been restructured to expand operational coverage and improve responses to security threats.

The administration, according to the council, has also approved two additional Army depots in Osogbo, Osun State, and Amasiri-Edda, Ebonyi State, to increase the capacity for training new soldiers.

Each facility is designed to accommodate 6,000 recruits at a time. The council said 5,210 recruits were already undergoing training at the Osogbo depot.

It added that the new facilities would shorten the time required to train recruits and increase the number of soldiers available for deployment.

The statement also cited investments in military accommodation and operational infrastructure, including a 700-unit barracks project in Ikere-Ekiti, Ekiti State.

The council said the project was intended to improve the living and working conditions of military personnel, while providing infrastructure to support their operations.

It maintained that the proposed defence plan would guide government spending according to identified security threats and operational needs, improve combat readiness and address gaps in the capabilities of security agencies.

The statement did not give a timeline for the implementation of the five-year plan.
