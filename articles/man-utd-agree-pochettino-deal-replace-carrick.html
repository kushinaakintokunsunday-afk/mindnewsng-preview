Manchester United Agree Two-And-A-Half-Year Deal With Pochettino To Replace Michael Carrick - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Sky Sports / Telegraph 13 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Sky Sports / Telegraph

Manchester United have agreed a two-and-a-half-year deal with USA manager Mauricio Pochettino to replace the sacked Michael Carrick, with the Argentine taking c

Manchester United have reached an agreement with Mauricio Pochettino for the Argentine to become the club's next head coach on a two-and-a-half-year contract running until the summer of 2029.

Pochettino, the current manager of the United States men's national team, will replace Michael Carrick, who was sacked on Thursday with the club 18th in the Premier League and without a win in their last ten matches.

Reports say United will pay a buyout of around £7 million to secure the release of the Argentine from his US Soccer contract.

The 54-year-old is expected to take charge of the squad after the current international break, with his first match in the dugout the trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on November 22.

Before that, he remains in charge of the USA for their matches against Haiti, with the Americans in competitive action this weekend and again on Tuesday.

Carrick had been appointed in January 2026 on a deal until the end of the season after the sacking of Ruben Amorim, but a run of poor results, including just one win in ten matches, cost the former United midfielder his job.

Pochettino previously managed Tottenham Hotspur, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea, and led the United States through the 2026 World Cup cycle.

An official announcement from Old Trafford is expected in the coming days.
