Atiku has youth support despite Tinubu’s control of Lagos, says Momodu

By Adegboyega Adeleye

African Democratic Congress (ADC) chieftain Dele Momodu has defended former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar’s appeal to young voters, saying Atiku has never controlled a state while President Bola Tinubu exercises strong political influence in Lagos.

Momodu spoke in an interview on Arise Television on Tuesday, discussing Atiku’s chances in the 2027 election, age dynamics, political influence and efforts by opposition parties to agree on a consensus candidate.

“If he’s not connecting to the youths, you won’t see people shouting ‘Bamayi’ all over Nigeria. I just watched your programme now in Kogi State. The people on the streets are not geriatrics; they are young people,” he said.

The ADC chieftain argued that age should not be used to judge a candidate’s ability to connect with voters. Citing his own presidential bid, he said: “I contested 15 years ago, at the age of 51 to be president of Nigeria. Why didn’t I win? It’s not about age.”

He said Atiku was surrounded by young supporters and described him as “a completely detribalised Nigerian” and “an employer of labour.” Comparing Atiku’s political influence with Tinubu’s, Momodu said, “He has never held on to any state. Tinubu controls Lagos, life and death. Tinubu controls Lagos and Atiku does not control anywhere.”

On talks to select a consensus opposition candidate, Momodu pointed to Atiku’s past willingness to step aside for another candidate.

“In 1993, I was 33 years old when Alhaji Atiku Abubakar first came out to run as a potential candidate of SDP, and at that time, he stepped down for MKO Abiola without making any trouble. After that, he was expected to be the running mate, he didn’t get it. He never fought anybody. He then contested in his state and became governor before they now made him running mate to President Olusegun Obasanjo. He has pursued his dreams with uncommon equanimity,” he said.
