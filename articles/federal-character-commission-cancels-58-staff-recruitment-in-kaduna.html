Federal Character Commission cancels 58 Staff recruitment in Kaduna

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Federal Character Commission (FCC) has ordered the cancellation of the recruitment of 58 staff by the National Water Resources Institute (NWRI), Kaduna, ove

The Commission directed the institute to commence a fresh recruitment exercise, citing failure to ensure equitable representation of states across the federation and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT).

In a statement issued on Friday, the FCC spokesperson, Ademola Lawrence, said the directive followed a review of a report submitted by the Commission’s monitoring officials, which established that the recruitment exercise did not comply with the prescribed Federal Character balancing index.

The Commission described the breach as a violation of the principles of fairness, equity and inclusiveness governing recruitment into public institutions.

It stressed that government agencies must ensure that employment opportunities reflect Nigeria’s diversity and do not create imbalances that undermine equitable representation.

“The Federal Character Principle, as enshrined in Section 14(3) of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (as amended), is a constitutional requirement, not an administrative formality,” the statement said.

According to the commission, public institutions have a responsibility to ensure fairness and balanced representation in appointments, recruitment and the distribution of opportunities across the country.

Consequently, the FCC directed the National Water Resources Institute to cancel the entire exercise and begin the recruitment process afresh in strict compliance with the prescribed balancing index and other applicable requirements.

The commission maintained that it would not compromise its constitutional responsibility or overlook practices capable of undermining equitable representation in public institutions.

“Every qualified Nigerian deserves a fair opportunity to access public employment, irrespective of state of origin or geographical location,” it added.

The FCC reaffirmed its commitment to ensuring that fairness, equity and national inclusion are reflected in the recruitment practices of government institutions.

“Equity must be demonstrated in practice, not merely proclaimed in principle,” the Commission stated.
