2027: APC leaders rally South-West support for Tinubu

By Punch Punch 4d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Leaders of the All Progressives Congress and the Presidential Campaign Council Support Group Directorate across the South-West on Friday intensified grassroots

The leaders spoke in their various remarks at a town hall meeting held at Onirekere in Ibadan North-West Local Government Area of Oyo State.

Speaking at the event, the Director-General of the President Bola Ahmed Tinubu Support Group and former Deputy Governor of Nasarawa State, Silas Agara, said, “The directorate deliberately shifted from traditional solidarity walks to interactive town hall sessions, following an earlier outing in Awka, Anambra State to directly engage citizens with verifiable facts.

“This gathering is designed to give all our people there the conducive environment to be able to state clearly, in clear terms, what Mr President has done in every state.

“So, let me appeal to our people in this region to support groups to ensure a massive voter turnout in January 2027 to return President Tinubu to Aso Villa.”

In his remarks, the Minister of Power, Joseph Tegbe, said, “Roughly 80 million Nigerians, about 40 per cent of the population, lacked access to electricity when President Tinubu assumed office in 2023.”

Tegbe, who was represented by Rasheed Adegboyega, said, “The administration secured a $750 million publicly funded facility through the Rural Electrification Agency to catalyse an additional $1.1 billion in private sector investment for 1,120 isolated mini-grids and 250 interconnected mini-grids nationwide, alongside another $500 million earmarked for ongoing Ministry of Power projects.”

Tegbe added that eight federal universities, including the University of Ibadan, University College Hospital, University of Abuja and University of Maiduguri, were taken off the national grid under the second phase of the Energising Education Programme to relieve pressure on the decades-old grid.

He noted that comprehensive project compendiums across all six geopolitical zones had been distributed to support groups for door-to-door campaigns.

In a pointed comparison of local government funding in Oyo State across three administrations, former Commissioner for Local Government Affairs, Bimbo Kolade, revealed that monthly federal allocations to the state’s 33 councils had surged dramatically under Tinubu.

According to Kolade, “local governments in Oyo state received an average of N1.8 billion monthly under former Governor Christopher Alao-Akala and N2.4 billion monthly under former Governor Abiola Ajimobi, whereas monthly allocations under the current dispensation have risen to an average of N26 billion.”

Recalling past grassroots projects, Kolade noted that Alao-Akala executed five kilometres of road per local government, totalling 165 kilometres, while Ajimobi tarred 637 kilometres of asphalt roads and opened up earth roads totalling 1,033 kilometres across the councils.

Questioning the utilisation of the current N26bn monthly inflow in local councils such as Oriire and Egbeda, Kolade charged voters to back Tinubu’s second-term bid to entrench full local government autonomy.

On rail transportation, the Managing Director of the Nigerian Railway Corporation, Kayode Opeifa, described the Lagos-Ibadan standard-gauge rail as the most functional corridor in the country, noting that “frequency had increased since 2023 to twice daily from Thursday to Sunday and once daily from Monday to Wednesday, while container cargo operations at Moniya were boosting Oyo State’s economy.”

Opeifa revealed that plans were underway to extend passenger rail operations from Ibadan to Osogbo and Ado-Ekiti under a four-year South-West rail master plan in partnership with the South-West Development Commission, adding that a 50 per cent fare discount would soon be introduced for holiday travellers.

The Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media-Related Issues, Tunde Rahman, maintained that President Tinubu’s bold economic reforms saved the nation from total financial collapse, stabilised the naira and lifted Gross Domestic Product growth to about 4.2 per cent.

Rahman highlighted key social and infrastructure interventions, including the Nigerian Education Loan Fund, which provides a N20,000 monthly stipend to students; the clearance of N768bn in pension arrears; and major federal road contracts in Oyo State, such as the N276bn Ilorin-Ogbomoso highway, the N265bn Iseyin-Oyo-Ogbomoso road, as well as the Iwo township, Ibadan-Ijaye-Ago-Are, Iseyin-Okeho-Kishi, Ogbomoso-Igbeti and Ibadan-Abeokuta corridors.

In their separate remarks, Oyo State APC Chairman and former Deputy Governor, Moses Adeyemo, and the party’s governorship candidate, Senator Sharafadeen Alli, charged party faithful and residents to ensure their Permanent Voter Cards were ready, turn out en masse on election day and defend their votes at the polling units.

Alli also commended the developmental strides of the President, while an aide to the President, Sunday Dare, highlighted ongoing federal road interventions across 12 routes in Oyo State and emerging cargo rail opportunities between Omi Adio and Erunmu.
