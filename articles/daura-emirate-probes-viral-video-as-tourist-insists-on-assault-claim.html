Daura emirate probes viral video as tourist insists on assault claim

By Punch Punch 1d ago 7 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Daura Emirate Council in Katsina State has commenced a review of a viral video in which a Polish tourist, Mirka Czee, accused the Emir of Daura, Faruk Umar

The council, through its spokesperson, Ibrahim Daura, said it was examining the footage to establish its authenticity and determine whether it had been manipulated or artificially generated.

The development comes as Czee, who uses the Instagram handle @beduinqa, has reiterated her allegation in a series of posts, explaining why she decided to make the incident public and describing its emotional effect on her.

The Polish citizen, who was born in the Czech Republic, said she had travelled across several parts of Nigeria, including Oron, Calabar, Ibadan, Benin City, Lagos, Abeokuta, Osogbo, Ilorin, Abuja, Jos, Bauchi, Dutse, Kano, Zaria, Katsina and Daura.

She said she travelled by public transport and interacted with residents as part of her effort to experience Nigeria’s culture and diversity, adding that she had mostly encountered kind, helpful and hospitable people.

Czee said she had also met other traditional rulers in Nigeria and described those encounters as respectful.

Recounting her visit to the Daura palace, she said she was introduced to the Emir, who instructed his dignitaries to take her for a meal and show her around the palace and its surroundings.

She said she learnt about Daura’s history, including its place in Hausa history, stories about female rulers and the account of a prince from Baghdad who killed a snake at a well. She also visited different rooms and the palace stables, where she rode a horse.

According to her, she was later taken back to the Emir but was surprised when she was left alone with him in a large room.

She alleged that he asked her to sit close to him on a couch, held her hand and began playing with it, making her uncomfortable.

Czee said she lowered the Meta glasses on her head and activated their recording function while trying to maintain their conversation.

She wrote, “I felt we sat too close, I felt uncomfortable that he grabbed my hand & started to play with it, so | lowered sunglasses (meta glasses) that were on top of my head down & clicked to turn them up.”

She alleged that the Emir later hugged and kissed her on the cheek as they stood up, before touching her breasts without her consent.

The tourist said she was shocked by the alleged action and repeatedly told him not to touch her.

“I just couldn’t believe he did it, l reacted with lots of “no no no no Emir” & he repeated after me “no no no” with laughter. I said “you cannot be touching”,” she wrote.

She added that her laughter during the encounter was a reflection of her discomfort.

“I believe my “giggling” was sign of how uncomfortable I felt,” she said.

Explaining why she decided to publish the recording, Czee said she believed the Emir was too comfortable with the alleged behaviour and that other women might have experienced unwanted touching without having the opportunity or means to speak about it.

She wrote, “But when this bad experience happened to me at the Palace of Emir of Daura, I felt I needed to share it. Because it happened to me. Because I had a chance & a tool to record it, & because I believe it happens to so many other women who don’t have a chance to speak up.”

She said she initially expected the video to be seen by only a few thousand people but was surprised by how widely it circulated.

Czee also stressed that her concern was that the alleged incident happened inside the palace of a traditional ruler, rather than in a public space.

“What’s important for me, & I already wrote it in short description when uploading the video: I wasn’t grabbed by boobs on streets of Nigeria, even in crowded places, but in a palace of traditional leader who should be showing exemplary ways of behaviour. And he did not. I was a guest in his country, in his region, in his city, in his palace. And I was assaulted,” she wrote.

The tourist also addressed reports about the video’s availability on Instagram, saying she believed it had been removed after many users reported it.

She, however, said she did not request its removal but had asked the platform for a human review of the decision.

“It seems that because too many people were clicking on “report” button the Instagram algorithm has removed the video. I didn’t. I clicked for human revision of that decision, but I don’t know how much time it takes,” she wrote.

She added that several copies of the recording were already circulating online.

Czee said the incident had affected her emotionally and that she needed space to recover and move on from her experience in Nigeria.

“I’m writing this with tears coming down, & I really feel that for the healing & for getting in better mental space, I need to start thinking about other things & places to leave Nigeria & what happened there behind,” she wrote.

She said about 90 per cent of the comments and messages she received were supportive but maintained that she needed time away from the discussion.

The tourist added that she still enjoyed her visit to Nigeria and wished Nigerians well.

According to Guardian Nigeria, the Sarkin Labaran Daura, Ibrahim Murtala Daura, confirmed on Sunday that the Emirate Council was aware of the video circulating on social media. He added that the Emirate Council would issue an official statement after concluding its review of the matter.

“We are examining the video to ascertain its authenticity and establish that it has not been created or altered using technology,” he said.
Alfijir Radio also stated in its report in Hausa on Sunday that the council was examining the footage to establish its authenticity and determine whether it had been manipulated or artificially generated.

PUNCH Online also contacted the Daura Emirate Council through an official telephone number listed on its social media page.

However, a man who answered the call identified himself as a page handler and said he was unaware of the allegation.

“I am the one handling the page. I do not know anything about it,” he said before ending the call.

The response came after an unnamed source in the Emir’s palace had earlier denied the allegation, saying there was nothing of the sort during the tourist’s visit.

“There was nothing like that. She visited the palace, but how she got that information cooked up still amused us in the palace,” the source told PUNCH Online by telephone on Sunday.

The source, who spoke on condition of anonymity because he was not authorised to speak to the press, said an official statement would be released.

When contacted, the Katsina State Commissioner for Information, Culture and Tourism, Dr Bala Zango, said he had not seen the video and would watch it before responding.

Meanwhile, Amnesty International Nigeria has called on the Katsina State Government and the police to conduct a transparent and independent investigation into the allegation.

The human rights organisation described the allegation as deeply disturbing and urged the authorities to ensure accountability.

As of the time of filing this report, the Emirate Council had not released a detailed statement on the allegation, while the Emir had not personally responded to the claims.

PUNCH Online could not independently confirm the allegation.
