Police rescue 15 kidnap victims in Kogi

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Kogi State Police Command has successfully rescued 15 kidnapped victims following an intelligence-led search and rescue operation conducted in the early hou

A statement issued in the early hours of Thursday by the state police Command’s spokesperson, ASP Salisu Afusat stated that the operation was executed pursuant to the directive of the Commissioner of Police, Kogi State Command, Commissioner of Police Naziru Kankarofi.

“It  involved a joint security team comprising personnel of the Kabba Area Command, Kabba ‘A’ Division, Iyara Division, Ekerin-Adde Division, Ayetoro-Gbede Division, Violent Crime Response Unit (VCRU), 70 Police Mobile Force (PMF), the Nigerian Army’s 251 Tank Battalion, Forest Guards, Kogi State Vigilante Services, Agbekoya and Kabba Security Outfit.” ASP Afusat noted.

She said that during the operation, the joint team advanced into the forest and engaged the kidnappers in a fierce gun battle.

“The superior firepower of the security operatives forced the criminals to retreat, with some sustaining varying degrees of gunshot injuries.

The rescued victims include:

1. Okuma Terlum – Male, 47 years
2. Ojo Deborah – Female, 23 years
3. Samuel Danjuma – Male, 3 years
4. Faith Raphael – Female, 21 years
5. Emmanuella Raphael – Female, 3 years
6. Donald Tor – Male, 31 years
7. Joseph Jonathan – Male, 20 years
8. Tergu Joseph – Male, 15 years
9. Samuel Joseph – Male, 8 years
10. Engr. Joseph Adebija – Male, 58 years
11. Lois Abiodun – Female, 50 years
12. Chuks Obiakor – Male, 45 years
13. Ola Olufemi – Male, 58 years
14. Ankpe Godwin – Male, 23 years
15. Kuhwa Solomon – Male, 25 years.

She said that two other female victims, identified only as Joy and Nwakan, escaped from their abductors during the initial encounter where the victims were taken captive.

“While conveying the rescued victims out of the forest, the joint security team came under another ambush by members of the criminal gang in a desperate attempt to frustrate the rescue mission. However, the operatives successfully repelled the attack through superior firepower and safely brought all fifteen victims out of the forest.”

She said that the victims were debriefed and immediately conveyed to St. John’s Catholic Hospital, Kabba, for medical attention, having endured considerable physical and psychological distress during their captivity.

Afusat, however, said, “Although no suspects have been arrested, actionable intelligence has been generated, and an intelligence-led investigation is ongoing to track down and apprehend the fleeing members of the kidnapping syndicate, as well as identify and bring their sponsors to justice”.

The commissioner of police commended the courage, resilience and effective collaboration demonstrated by all participating security agencies and local security outfits.

He reiterated the command’s unwavering commitment to sustaining coordinated operations against kidnapping and other violent crimes across the state.

The command appreciated the support and cooperation of members of the public and urges residents to continue providing credible and timely information to assist security agencies in the ongoing efforts to rid Kogi State of criminal elements.
