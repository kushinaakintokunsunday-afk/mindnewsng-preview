Nollywood star, Chioma Chukwuka makes Nigeria proud in Texas

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Noah Ebije, Kaduna Veteran Nollywood actress, Chioma Chukwuka, has received the prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award at the 2026 Global African Diaspora

From Noah Ebije, Kaduna

Veteran Nollywood actress, Chioma Chukwuka, has received the prestigious Lifetime Achievement Award at the 2026 Global African Diaspora Awards (GADA) in recognition of her outstanding contributions to the Nigerian film industry and her enduring influence on African entertainment.

This was contained in a statement made available to newsmen in Nigeria by GADA Founder and Executive Producer, Obi-West Utchaychukwu, popularly known as the Obi of Houston.

According to the statement, the award was presented at the third edition of the GADA Awards, held on Saturday, November 7, 2026, at the Golden Tree Banquet in Houston, Texas, United States.

The glamorous ceremony attracted approximately 500 guests from different parts of the world, bringing together prominent personalities and members of the African diaspora to celebrate excellence and outstanding achievements by Africans across various fields.

Chukwuka, who is regarded as one of Nollywood’s most accomplished actresses, began her acting journey in 1998 and ventured into professional acting in 2000.

Her first movie appearance was in The Apple (2000), directed by renowned filmmaker, Lancelot Oduwa Imasuen.

Over the years, she has distinguished herself through her exceptional acting skills, versatility, dedication and consistency, earning admiration from audiences and colleagues within and outside Nigeria.

Her memorable performances and sustained contributions have also helped promote the growth and international visibility of Nigerian cinema.

The Lifetime Achievement Award, according to the organisers, recognises not only her professional accomplishments but also her enduring influence on the entertainment industry and her role in inspiring emerging generations of actors.

Speaking on the significance of the recognition, the founder and executive producer of GADA, Obi-West Utchaychukwu, popularly known as the Obi of Houston, said the award was a celebration of Chukwuka’s remarkable journey and lasting contributions to African entertainment.

He noted that the actress represents the talent, resilience and creative excellence that African entertainers continue to project on the international stage.

The organisers said the recognition was also in line with the vision of the Global African Diaspora Awards to celebrate Africans whose achievements have continued to make meaningful contributions to society across national and continental boundaries.

The third edition of the awards was held under the theme, “Bridging Continents, Celebrating Diaspora Excellence,” which underscores the importance of recognising outstanding Africans and members of the diaspora whose accomplishments have strengthened the continent’s global presence.

By honouring Chukwuka with the Lifetime Achievement Award, GADA has further acknowledged her place among the leading figures whose dedication and creative accomplishments have helped shape the Nigerian movie industry over the years.

The recognition is also seen as a celebration of the growing influence of Nollywood and the contributions of African creative talents to global entertainment.
