Senate okays N100.5bn reimbursement for Yobe’s Muhammadu Buhari cargo airport

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 6d ago 3 min read

Photo: VeryNigerian

The Senate has approved a N100.5 billion Promissory Note Programme to reimburse the Yobe State Government for the construction of the Muhammadu Buhari International Cargo Airport in Damaturu.

The approval was granted on Tuesday following the consideration of a report submitted by the Senate Committee on Local and Foreign Debts.

The report was presented by the committee’s chairman, Senator Aliyu Wamakko, who represents Sokoto North Senatorial District under the All Progressives Congress (APC).

The committee was directed to verify the total cost of the airport project, estimated at N100,509,017,234.55, following a request from President Bola Tinubu.

The presidential request was referred to the committee on October 13 for review and verification before the Senate’s approval of the reimbursement programme.

Presenting the report, Wamakko said the Yobe State Government embarked on the airport project to address security challenges, improve air connectivity in the North-East and facilitate the evacuation of agricultural produce.

He added that the facility was also designed to support humanitarian operations and stimulate economic activities in Yobe and neighbouring states.

According to him, the airport features a 3.6-kilometre runway equipped with a Category II Instrument Landing System, taxiways and other essential aviation infrastructure.

He said the facility also has an apron capable of accommodating four Boeing 747 aircraft, a terminal building and a cargo shed.

Other facilities, he noted, include an air traffic control tower, fire station, perimeter fencing, navigational aids and aeronautical ground lighting.

Wamakko disclosed that the Yobe State Government formally requested the Federal Government’s takeover of the airport in a letter dated March 12, 2024, in line with aviation policies governing the ownership and operation of international airports across the country.

He said the Federal Executive Council approved the takeover on August 21, subject to verification of the project’s cost and reimbursement to the state government.

The senator explained that the reimbursement would be facilitated through a Promissory Note Programme after the relevant government agencies completed their verification and due-process assessments.

He said the committee confirmed that the N100.5 billion project cost had undergone a technical audit by the Federal Ministry of Aviation, while the Bureau of Public Procurement conducted a due-process review and issued a Certificate of No Objection on February 25.

According to him, the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority also confirmed that the runway, safety facilities and navigational aids complied with international aviation standards.

The report further indicated that the airport had been completed and was operational, with test flights conducted by the NCAA ahead of its formal takeover by the Federal Government.

With the Senate’s approval, the Federal Government now has the necessary legislative backing to reimburse the Yobe State Government through the Promissory Note Programme.
