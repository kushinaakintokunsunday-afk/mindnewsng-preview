‘He will lose the election’ – Kano fortune teller predicts Gov Yusuf’s fate ahead of 2027

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

A self-proclaimed fortune teller identified as Malam Na Shuri Zaria has stirred reactions on social media after predicting that Kano State Governor, Abba Kabir Yusuf, would lose the 2027 governorship election.

In a video that has gone viral on social media, the fortune teller, who described himself as an expert in divination, repeatedly insisted that Governor Yusuf, popularly known as Abba Gida-Gida, would not win a second term in office.

His prediction has generated discussions across social media platforms, with users reacting to his claims about the governor’s political future.

‎”Wallahi, tallahi, billahil-ladhi la ilaha illa huwa, Abba Gida-Gida will not win the 2027 election,” he declared, repeating the oath three times.

‎He further claimed that other fortune tellers, regardless of where they lived, knew that the governor would lose the election.

‎”Every fortune teller, whether at home or in the courtyard, knows this. I swear he will lose the election,” he said.

‎Malam Na Shuri also offered to give a car to any fortune teller who disagreed with his prediction and believed Governor Yusuf would win a second term.

‎”Any fortune teller who thinks I am lying should come forward and let us agree. I swear I will give him a car if Abba wins re-election,” he added.

‎His prediction attracted reactions from supporters of the governor, including Senator Abdulrahman Kawu Sumaila, who represents Kano South Senatorial District.

‎The senator dismissed the claim, accusing the fortune teller of misleading the public and insisting that knowledge of the future belongs to Allah alone.

‎”You are lying. You do not know what will happen tomorrow. You are only deceiving people. This is the exclusive knowledge of Allah,” Sumaila said.

‎He also challenged the fortune teller to demonstrate his alleged powers by attempting to make him lose his sanity.

‎”Come and drive me mad if you have the power to do so. My name is Abdulrahman Kawu Sumaila. The Prophet Muhammad (SAW) warned against such practices more than 1,400 years ago,” he said.

‎The senator further challenged him to use his alleged spiritual powers to help tackle insecurity and banditry rather than make political predictions.

‎”If you can, use your powers to find a solution to banditry and insecurity,” he added.

‎As of the time of filing this report, Malam Na Shuri had not publicly responded to the senator’s challenge.

‎Another fortune teller links Abba’s electoral chances to marriage

‎In a related development, another self-acclaimed fortune teller, operating on Facebook under the name Kuliya Kabara, claimed on Tuesday that Governor Yusuf’s separation from Zainab Kwankwaso could affect his chances of winning the 2027 election.

‎The fortune teller advised the governor to make efforts to reconcile with his wife before the election, claiming that their marriage was connected to his political success.

‎He reportedly argued that the governor should not allow the separation to continue with the 2027 election approaching.

‎However, no evidence was provided to substantiate the claim linking the governor’s marriage to his electoral fortunes.

‎The development comes amid growing activity by self-acclaimed fortune tellers and traditional diviners on social media, where they increasingly make predictions and offer opinions on public affairs, particularly politics.

‎Their claims have continued to generate debate among social media users, with some challenging the credibility of their predictions.
