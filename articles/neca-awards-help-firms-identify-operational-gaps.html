‘NECA awards help firms identify operational gaps’

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Nigeria Employers’ Consultative Association has stated that the NECA’s Employers’ Excellence Award helps companies assess their operations with a view to kn

The Director-General of NECA, Adewale-Smatt Oyerinde, stated this on Wednesday in Lagos while speaking during a press conference ahead of the 2026 NECA’s Employers’ Excellence Award, billed for next week.

Oyerinde, who was represented at the event by the Director, Social and Labour Affairs at NECA, Mrs Adenike Ajala, stressed that the award helps companies assess even their internal processes and governance structures, and then benchmark against what is happening in the economy.

Oyerinde stated that NECA would continue to advocate for an environment that enables businesses to carry out their operations. “And that is one of the reasons why we have been able to sustain this initiative. Because businesses have been able to survive due to the advocacy that we do, the interventions that we make on their behalf, whatever business issue that they have.”

“One of the things that we also want companies to take away is for them to be able to see the gaps in their processes. And for them to be able to address those gaps so that when they apply, they are able to meet that. And that is one of the things that encourage companies to apply every year. Because it allows them to assess even their internal processes, their governance structures, and then benchmark against what is happening in the economy,” Oyerinde said.

According to him, some companies apply just to be able to check what they are doing well and what they are not doing well. “Some people will tell you, I have seen it, I am not doing well, I have to go back, I have to check my internal processes and then I will come back to apply. So it is an assessment for what you do as an organisation and you also get the reports.”

He added that out of about 200 applications received for this year’s award, only 128 had been able to scale through to the point where they were now being assessed.

Speaking on the award, Oyerinde emphasised that over the years, the NECA Employers’ Excellence Awards had evolved into a distinguished platform for celebrating organisations and employers on merit, whose leadership, enterprise, innovation and workplace practices contribute meaningfully to national development.

He reiterated that the awards reflect NECA’s enduring commitment to promoting a business environment in which enterprise success is matched by responsible employment practices, investment in people, respect for workers’ rights, innovation and corporate responsibility.
