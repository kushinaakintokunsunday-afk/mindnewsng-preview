MTN, Audiomack deepen partnership with festive music campaign

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

MTN Nigeria and global music streaming platform Audiomack are extending their partnership with a festive campaign offering subscribers backstage access to perfo

The initiative will give six Audiomack+ subscribers the opportunity to win backstage passes to two December concerts, with three passes available for each show, the streaming giant said in a release.

The selected subscribers will attend Victony’s The Bonfire Experience on 26 December and Seyi Vibez’s SWAGUU Live in Concert at Tafawa Balewa Square on 29 December.

The winners will have the opportunity to meet the artistes, watch performances from the side of the stage and receive merchandise, according to a statement by Audiomack.

The campaign, which runs from 20 November to 2 January, requires participants to maintain an active Audiomack+ subscription during the promotional period, stream the official Bonfire x SWAGUU playlist on the platform and share a video of themselves enjoying the music.

The company said subscribers could participate through any of its plans, including the N100 daily pass and N900 monthly subscription. Winners will be announced weekly on Audiomack’s Instagram and X accounts and contacted directly.

The latest initiative follows a similar activation at Felabration in October, where Audiomack+ members received VIP seating, as the streaming platform expands the benefits attached to its paid subscription service.

The partnership also builds on the companies’ airtime payment arrangement, introduced in 2024, which allows MTN subscribers to pay for Audiomack+ through their airtime balances, either within the Audiomack application or by sending an SMS to 3134.

The arrangement provides an alternative to debit card payments, allowing subscribers with an MTN line and sufficient airtime to access the platform’s premium service.

Speaking on the campaign, the Vice-President of Marketing for Africa, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa at Audiomack, Charlotte Bwana, said the company wanted subscribers to enjoy benefits beyond music streaming.

“December is when Nigerians spend the most on music: tickets, data, airtime, everything. We want Audiomack+ members to feel their subscription takes them somewhere real, and there is no better place than backstage with Victony and Seyi Vibez, two artists our listeners play on repeat,” she said.

Bwana added that the airtime payment option was intended to make the subscription accessible to more Nigerians who might not have debit cards.

Beyond concert-related benefits, Audiomack+ offers an ad-free listening experience and offline downloads, allowing subscribers to listen to music without an active internet connection.

Nigeria remains Audiomack’s largest single market, with 15.3 million monthly users, according to the company, which said it planned to introduce more benefits for subscribers during the festive season.
