Seven things to know about the next James Bond film

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

British actor Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next James Bond, taking over the iconic role previously played by Daniel Craig in the long-running film

British actor Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next James Bond, taking over the iconic role previously played by Daniel Craig in the long-running film franchise.

Amazon MGM Studios announced Pattinson’s casting on Monday, November 16, 2026, in a statement published on its official website.

The announcement was also confirmed on the official James Bond social media accounts on Instagram and X, as verified by our correspondent.

The upcoming film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay. Amy Pascal and David Heyman will serve as producers, while Tanya Lapointe will be executive producer.

Pattinson described his appointment as the honour of a lifetime, while the studio said his casting marked a new chapter for the franchise.

Following the announcement, here are seven things to know about the upcoming James Bond film, as curated in a separate release on the MGM website on Monday.

1. Robert Pattinson takes over as James Bond

Pattinson has been officially cast as the next actor to portray James Bond, the fictional British secret agent also known as 007.

The actor, whose career spans more than two decades, has appeared in independent films and major studio productions, including The Twilight Saga, Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, Tenet, The Lighthouse, Mickey 17 and The Batman.

He also starred in A24’s The Drama and Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey in 2026.

Reacting to his appointment, Pattinson said, “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

2. Denis Villeneuve to direct the film

Canadian filmmaker Denis Villeneuve will direct the upcoming James Bond production.

Villeneuve is known for directing films including Dune, Blade Runner 2049, Arrival, Sicario, Prisoners, Enemy and Incendies.

Amazon MGM Studios announced his appointment as director on June 25, 2025, alongside the announcement that Tanya Lapointe would serve as executive producer.

Speaking about taking on the project, Villeneuve said: “Some of my earliest movie-going memories are connected to 007. I grew up watching James Bond films with my father, ever since Dr. No with Sean Connery. I’m a die-hard Bond fan. To me, he’s sacred territory. I intend to honor the tradition and open the path for many new missions to come.

“This is a massive responsibility, but also incredibly exciting for me and a huge honor. Amy, David, and I are absolutely thrilled to bring him back to the screen. Thank you to Amazon MGM Studios for their trust.”

3. Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight writes the screenplay

Steven Knight, the creator of the television series Peaky Blinders, is writing the screenplay for the upcoming James Bond film.

Amazon MGM Studios announced his appointment on July 31, 2025.

The British screenwriter, producer and director is also known for creating Taboo, SAS: Rogue Heroes, See and A Thousand Blows.

His film credits include Dirty Pretty Things, Eastern Promises, Spencer, Hummingbird, Locke and Serenity.

Knight also co-created the international television game show Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?

4. Spider-Man and Harry Potter producers join the project

Amy Pascal and David Heyman will produce the upcoming film through their respective production companies, Pascal Pictures and Heyday Films.

Amazon MGM Studios announced their appointment on March 25, 2025.

Pascal is known for her work on the Spider-Man franchise, including the four films starring Tom Holland and the three animated Spider-Verse productions.

Her other production credits include Project Hail Mary, Challengers and Spider-Noir.

Heyman, on the other hand, produced all eight film adaptations of J.K. Rowling’s Harry Potter books, as well as the three Fantastic Beasts films.

His other credits include Once Upon a Time… in Hollywood, Marriage Story, Gravity and Barbie.

5. Tanya Lapointe named executive producer

Tanya Lapointe will serve as executive producer of the upcoming James Bond film.

Her appointment was announced by Amazon MGM Studios on June 25, 2025, alongside Villeneuve’s selection as director.

Lapointe is known for her work with Villeneuve, including their collaboration on the Dune films.

Amazon MGM Studios also named her as executive producer in its November 16, 2026 announcement confirming Pattinson’s casting.

6. Amazon MGM Studios has creative control of James Bond

Amazon MGM Studios’ involvement in the franchise follows its acquisition of MGM in 2022, which brought a catalogue of more than 4,000 films and 17,000 television shows under its ownership.

In March 2025, Amazon MGM Studios concluded a new joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, under which the studio gained creative control of the James Bond intellectual property rights.

The arrangement gave Amazon MGM Studios control over the creative direction of the franchise.

7. All 25 previous James Bond films are on Prime Video

Amazon says all 25 previous official James Bond films are streaming on Prime Video worldwide.

The collection features the different actors who have portrayed the British secret agent over the decades, including Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig.

Pattinson’s appointment makes him the next actor to take on the role in the official film series, following Craig’s final appearance in No Time to Die, released in 2021.
