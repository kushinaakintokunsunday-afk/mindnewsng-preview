Kano: Police arrest 8 as Kwankwaso’s, Gwarzo’s convoys come under attack

From Desmond Mgboh, Kano

The convoy of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) vice presidential candidate, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, was attacked on Sunday by opposition political thugs in Bichi LGA of Kano State.

The attack also affected the convoy of the party’s governorship candidate in the state, Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo.

The incident occurred while the party was returning from a political tour of Tofa, Rimin Gado, Kabo, Gwarzo, Shanono and Bagwai LGAs.

Confirming the attack, the spokesperson for the NDC governorship candidate, Ibrahim Garba Shuaibu, said that while approaching Bichi they had received intelligence reports that some people had blocked the road to prevent them from holding a political rally in the town.

According to Shuaibu, upon arriving in the town, they noticed signs of stone-throwing, and shortly after passing the local government secretariat, a group of youths armed with dangerous weapons emerged and attacked their convoy.

He said the attack left some supporters injured and damaged several vehicles, adding that he personally saw about seven vehicles with smashed windscreens at the scene.

Kwankwaso and Gwarzo were said to have escaped unhurt, he disclosed.

In a statement, the police in Kano said the Command had received a report at about 4.45pm on Sunday, 15 November 2026, of a clash within Bichi township, which led to the destruction of campaign banners, billboards and motor vehicles, and injuries to several persons.

The statement, signed by the police spokesman, Abdullahi Haruna Kiyawa, said that eight suspects had been arrested in connection with the incident.

It added that items recovered as exhibits included one gariyo, two cutlasses, two hard sticks, one bottle of solution, and a substance locally referred to as “Suck and Die”.

It explained that thirteen vehicles were vandalised during the clash, while five persons who sustained injuries were rushed to hospital, where they are currently receiving treatment.

The Commissioner of Police, Kano State Command, strongly condemned the resort to violence and the destruction of campaign materials by political supporters, warning that the Command would not tolerate any act capable of breaching public peace before, during and after the political season.

He urged political leaders, supporters and the general public to shun violence, remain law-abiding and report any suspicious activity to the nearest police station.

Meanwhile, the suspects have been conveyed to the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID), Bompai, for discreet investigation and will be charged to court upon completion of investigation.
