Mikano Motors launches Deepal, unveils flagship S09,  advances vision for future of mobility

By Moses Akaigwe

Mikano Motors has officially launched Deepal to the Nigerian automotive market, unveiling the brand’s flagship S09 SUV at a launch event held on recently at its showroom in Victoria Island, Lagos. 

The introduction marks a significant milestone in the company’s automotive journey, bringing a new generation of intelligent mobility technology, contemporary design and advanced vehicle engineering to Nigerian consumers.

Deepal is an intelligent electric vehicle brand within the portfolio of Changan Automobile, one of China’s established automotive manufacturers, while Mikano Motors serves as its authorised distributor in Nigeria.

Through this partnership, Mikano Motors is introducing four models to the market: the flagship S09, alongside the G318, S07 and S05.

Leading the introduction is the Deepal S09, a six-seater, full-sized SUV designed around premium comfort, space and intelligent technology. The vehicle features a dual-motor electric all-wheel-drive system, 485 horsepower and a combined driving range of over 1,100 kilometres.

Its Range Extended Electric Vehicle (REEV) technology uses electric motors as the primary source of propulsion, while an onboard petrol engine functions as a generator to support extended driving range. The S09 also features second-row zero-gravity seating, a 360-degree camera system and advanced driver-assistance technology.

Beyond introducing new vehicles, Mikano Motors is focused on building a comprehensive mobility proposition for Nigerian consumers. Its approach combines access to advanced automotive technology with a strong ownership support structure, including a six-year or 200,000-kilometre vehicle warranty and an eight-year battery warranty for the S09, alongside authorised after-sales servicing, maintenance and technical support. 

This reflects the company’s wider ambition to make contemporary mobility more accessible while giving customers confidence throughout their ownership journey.

The launch also reflected the lifestyle dimension of the Deepal brand, bringing together automotive design, Nigerian fashion and contemporary culture through showcases by local designers, creative installations and a live painting inspired by a Deepal vehicle. Guests also enjoyed an orchestra performance, dance presentations and other live entertainment.

The event, held on Friday, November 13, 2026, brought together leadership from Mikano International, Mikano Motors and Changan, including Mr Mofid Karameh, Chairman of Mikano International; Joelle Haykal, Group Executive Director of Mikano International; Mr Syam Abdulkadir, Head of Operations, Mikano Motors; and Stephanie Warl, Sales Director for African Sales (LHD), Middle East and Africa Region, Changan. Their remarks highlighted the significance of the partnership and the introduction of Deepal to Nigeria.

The evening also featured a raffle draw, announced by Joelle Haykal, which saw Mrs Bidemi Oyewo emerge as the winner of a brand-new Deepal S07, while Mrs Funmi Nwama won an exclusive trip to China with Kunle Remi, Mikano Motors’ Changan ambassador.

The introduction of Deepal represents a new chapter in Mikano Motors’ automotive journey, strengthening its relationship with Changan while expanding the mobility options available to Nigerian consumers. Through advanced vehicle technology, local distribution and a comprehensive ownership proposition, Mikano Motors is positioning Deepal as an important addition to Nigeria’s evolving automotive landscape.
