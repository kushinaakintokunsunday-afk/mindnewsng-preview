S’African golfer, Kenyan chef arrested with 50kg cannabis in Lagos

By Punch Punch 1d ago 7 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency has arrested a 37-year-old South African professional golfer, Grobler Henlo, and a 25-year-old Kenyan chef, Abubakar Nj

The suspects were arrested at the Murtala Muhammed International Airport, Ikeja, Lagos, in October, according to a statement issued on Sunday by the NDLEA spokesperson, Femi Babafemi.

The agency said Henlo arrived in Lagos from South Africa aboard a South African Airways flight on October 11 and was intercepted at the Terminal 2 arrival hall.

A search of two travelling boxes in his possession reportedly led to the recovery of 46 parcels of Loud weighing 50.40kg.

The NDLEA said investigations revealed that although the boxes were with Henlo, they bore the luggage tags of Njambi, who had collected two other boxes bearing her tags from the airport conveyor belt and left.

“The swap of luggage tags was a deliberate ploy by the syndicate to frustrate the identification of ownership so that each courier could deny the boxes bearing his or her name if intercepted.

“During a preliminary interview, the golfer, who travelled in business class while his accomplice flew economy, said he was recruited by a South African associate identified as Shanel, who linked him with other members of the syndicate he never met in person.

“He said a driver picked him up from his home in South Africa, handed him four travelling boxes and took him to the airport, where he was instructed to meet the Kenyan lady and swap luggage tags with her. He claimed he was promised 5,000 South African rand upon successful delivery,” the statement read.

It added, “Further examination revealed that tracking devices had been installed on the travelling boxes as well as on his mobile phone, a feature also found in the bags abandoned by the American basketballer a month later, which shows how closely the cartels monitor their couriers and consignments.

“The passport biodata page of Abubakar Faiza Njambi, recovered from the golfer’s phone, was profiled and placed on the NDLEA watchlist, leading to her arrest at the departure boarding gate of the Lagos airport on Thursday, October 15, 2026, as she attempted to board a South African Airways flight to return to Kenya.

“The 25-year-old private chef told NDLEA operatives she was recruited by a man, adding that a friend in Kenya persuaded her to accept the offer of US$8,000 upon successful delivery of the consignment.

“She said she left Kenya for South Africa on October 4, stayed in a hotel for about a week, and met the golfer at the airport in South Africa on October 11, where he handed her two of the four boxes before they checked them in under their respective luggage tags.

“She claimed that on arrival in Lagos, she collected the two boxes bearing the golfer’s tags before a driver took her to a hotel in the Lekki area of Lagos.

“The claim tag of the two boxes bearing her name, which she abandoned at the airport, was recovered from her hand luggage, while both suspects identified each other during an identification parade.”

In a separate operation at the Lagos airport, NDLEA operatives on November 13 intercepted a consolidated consignment from the United Kingdom aboard an Allied Air flight.

The agency said the shipment contained 10 parcels of synthetic cannabinoids weighing 10.10kg, 27 packs of marshmallow weighing 318.80kg and precursor chemicals weighing a combined 34.55kg.

A freight agent, Sobowale Oladayo, was arrested when he presented himself to clear the consignment.

The consignee, identified as Kareem Issa, 35, also known as Ola, was arrested the following day when he arrived to collect the shipment at a logistics company’s premises in a grey Lexus 460 SUV.

The agency said Issa, a plumber who lives on Lagos Island, allegedly posed as a senior security official and routinely used a siren fitted to his vehicle when visiting the logistics company to collect consignments.

In Kaduna State, operatives arrested two men, Lawali Muhammad, 35, and Aliyu Mohammed, 30, in connection with 160 pieces of explosives intercepted along the Abuja-Kaduna Expressway at Jere on November 17.

The intended recipient, Mohammed, was arrested in a follow-up operation in Gusau, Zamfara State, the agency said.

Two other suspects were arrested along the same route with 6,900 tablets of tramadol, 20,000 tablets of diazepam and 25,000 tablets of Exol-5.

NDLEA operatives also recovered 627,500 capsules of tramadol weighing 21.50kg from a Hummer bus along the Zaria-Kano Expressway. The driver reportedly abandoned the vehicle and fled.

In Ogun State, a woman, Esther Ogundipe, was arrested on November 19 during a raid on a suspected drug joint at Eleweran, Abeokuta, with two locally made firearms and five live cartridges.

Two other women were arrested in Ilaro on November 17 with 7.4g of cocaine, 638g of cannabis and 5.9g of methamphetamine.

In Osun State, a 43-year-old man, Francis Oduke, was arrested along the Ikire-Ibadan Expressway with 359 bags of skunk weighing 4,627.7kg, allegedly concealed beneath a load of granite in a truck.

In neighbouring Ondo State, operatives intercepted a truck carrying 93 bags of cannabis weighing 1,045kg and heading to Kano State. The seizure was made along the Zango-Ado Expressway, Akure, on November 16.

Four suspects, Adamu Yerima, 19; Mohammed Mohammed, 19; Nura Mohammed, 25; and Usman Mohammed, were arrested in connection with the seizure.

The agency also reported arrests and drug recoveries in Edo, Katsina, Abia and Rivers states.

In Edo, Emmanuel Eze, 48, was arrested with 11 and a half bags of cannabis weighing 156kg at Aduwawa community, Benin City, on November 20.

In Katsina, Abbas Bello, 39, was arrested in the Gafai area on November 17 with 148 blocks of cannabis weighing 72kg, allegedly concealed in the boot of his white Honda Accord.

In Abia, operatives recovered 2kg of cannabis from a hole beneath the tiled floor of a shop belonging to a 25-year-old woman, Oluebube Innocent, at Amankwa Street, Obingwa Local Government Area, on November 18.

In Rivers, a 46-year-old physically challenged man, Peter Sunday, was arrested with 300g of cannabis during a raid in Eneka on November 17.

The agency also said its commands and formations across the country continued to conduct their War Against Drug Abuse sensitisation activities in schools, worship centres, workplaces and communities.

The activities included lectures for students and staff of Local Government Primary School, Ebute Ikorodu, Lagos; Government Girls Unity College, Birnin Kebbi; and Community Secondary School, Usa, Enugu Ezike.

The Ondo State Command also paid an advocacy visit to the Deji of Akure, Oba Aladetoyinbo Ogunlade Adelusi.

Commending officers of the affected commands, the NDLEA Chairman, Brig. Gen. Mohamed Buba Marwa (retd.), said the arrests of the South African golfer and Kenyan chef, coming within the same period as that of the American basketballer, showed that international drug cartels were changing their couriers but retaining their methods.

“The two cases show a trend in which international drug cartels now recruit sportsmen and other professionals as couriers, in the hope that their profiles will shield them from scrutiny.

“The NDLEA has a clear message for such syndicates: no profession, passport or travel class will provide cover for drug trafficking through Nigeria’s ports of entry,” Marwa warned.

He urged officers at all ports of entry to remain vigilant and professional, while charging personnel across all commands to sustain the balance between drug supply reduction and demand reduction efforts, particularly the War Against Drug Abuse sensitisation campaigns in schools, workplaces, worship centres and communities.
