2027: Ibrahim Kashim, Governorship Candidate in Bauchi dumps NDC, defects to APC

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The Bauchi State governorship candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Ibrahim Kashim, has defected to the All Progressives Congress ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Kashim, a former Secretary to the Bauchi State Government, announced his defection on Monday during a ceremony at the APC State Secretariat in Bauchi.

His move comes despite the inclusion of his name on the list of governorship candidates recently released by the Independent National Electoral Commission.

Explaining his decision, the former SSG said he joined the APC because the ruling party at the federal level offered Bauchi State a stronger platform to participate in national governance and attract development opportunities.

He noted that the APC’s national reach, political influence and institutional structures would provide the state with greater opportunities to advance its interests.

“The APC is currently the governing party at the Federal level and has an overwhelming presence across Northern Nigeria. It is also the party with the political structure, national reach and institutional capacity to provide a viable platform for the political and developmental aspirations we seek for Bauchi,” he said.

Kashim added that Bauchi needed to position itself strategically within the country’s political landscape, arguing that remaining outside the ruling party could limit the state’s ability to attract federal projects, influence national policies, facilitate investments and secure adequate representation at the national level.

He maintained that his decision was based on what he considered the developmental interests of the state rather than personal or political sentiments.

“The question is not simply which party can put forward a candidate. The question is which party has the organisational strength, national reach, political influence and institutional capacity to advance Bauchi’s interests.

“I am convinced that the APC offers the stronger platform,” he stated.

The former governorship candidate said his decision to leave the NDC followed his resignation from the party on October 16, 2026.

He, however, insisted that his departure from the party had not changed his commitment to the political struggle for a better Bauchi State.

Kashim also appealed to his supporters, political associates and other stakeholders to join him in the APC, saying his political ambition was driven by the desire to improve governance and development in the state.

He listed youth employment, women’s empowerment, improved agricultural productivity, quality education and better healthcare delivery among his priorities.

“Our ultimate objective is not the triumph of one political party over another. It is the triumph of good governance over poor governance, development over stagnation, opportunity over deprivation and hope over despair,” he said.

Kashim further urged the people of Bauchi to support his decision, arguing that the state should not be left behind in accessing opportunities available through the federation.

He had earlier announced his resignation from the NDC in a statement issued on October 16, 2026, saying the decision followed careful reflection.

At the time, he maintained that leaving the party would not affect his commitment to the political struggle for a better Bauchi State.
