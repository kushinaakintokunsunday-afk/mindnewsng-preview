Kebbi APC rally: Shettima urges North to disregard opposition lies, distortions, repay Tinubu’s kindness

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 4 min read

Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Romanus Ugwu, Ola Muhammad, Birnin Kebbi

Vice President Kashim Shettima has urged the North to disregard the lies, distortions, half-truths, taunts, insinuations, threats and noise of the opposition and to repay President Bola Tinubu for his kindness to the region.

He spoke at a presidential rally organised by the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Birnin Kebbi, the Kebbi State capital, on Tuesday. He disclosed that the northern region would be the biggest beneficiary of the N200 billion worth of fertiliser that was about to be procured for distribution to farmers in the dry season.

His words: “President Tinubu sacrificed his resources and comfort to give the North what it wished for. We are grateful that the North, too, kept its promise in 2023 by giving the president 60.2 per cent of the votes that cost us a victory.”

“The North repaid their kindness. We are even more grateful that you are prepared to do the same in January next year. You have rejected the lies, the distortions, the half-truths, the taunts, the insinuations, the threats and the noise of the opposition,” he said.

Defending why the North must vote for the re-election of President Tinubu, Shettima said: “He also understands the needs of our farmers. Thousands of them participate in dry-season farming. And just on Monday, he gave me the honour of chairing a committee that will procure fertiliser worth N200 billion for distribution to our farmers in the dry season.”

“And you and I know where those farmers are. They are in Kebbi, they are in Jigawa, they are in Kano, they are in Yobe, they are largely in Kaduna, they are in Niger, they are in Sokoto, they are in Kebbi, they are in Zamfara.

“So, my brothers in the North, this is why we have to continue to support the President. On the highway the government is building from Sokoto to Badagry to ease movement across the country, 258 kilometres run through Kebbi. This is the longest stretch in any state.

“We have also undertaken refuels on the Jega, Koko, Yauri and Birnin Kebbi, Argungu Federal Roads to ease your journeys. But roads alone are not enough without electricity. We have increased the capacity of the Birnin Kebbi transmission substation while constructing a 3.5-megawatt solar project in Ambursa to serve homes and businesses.

“Your Governor, Comrade Nasir Idris, began his career in the classroom. In February, Mr President commissioned the College of Nursing and Midwifery in Ambursa to train nurses and midwives. This complements our commitment to the education of your children.

“This is why we introduced NELFUND to support their studies. In Aliero alone, the State University received 693 million naira in student loan funding. In healthcare, we have upgraded the Federal Medical Centre in Birnin Kebbi to a teaching hospital.

“While primary healthcare centres across the state receive federal support through the Basic Health Care Provision Fund. We have also invested in the skills of your young people. Mr President’s economic reforms have increased the revenue available to states to fund their initiatives and projects,” he enumerated.

Shettima strongly believes that his boss has done excellently well, stressing: “Kebbi State, like other states, struggles to fund its projects. We will not rest until every kobo and every naira from these reforms is invested in your welfare. This is why we have come again to continue what we started about three years ago.”

“On January 16th, I trust you to come out en masse and vote for President Bola Ahmed Tinubu. On February 6th, I trust you to return Governor Nasir Idris for a second term. We have so much to do to fix this great country and we must all do our part,” he said.
