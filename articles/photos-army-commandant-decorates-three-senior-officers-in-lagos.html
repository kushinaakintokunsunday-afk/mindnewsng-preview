PHOTOS: Army commandant decorates three senior officers in Lagos

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Commandant of the Nigerian Army College of Logistics and Management, Major General Adewale Adetoba, has decorated three senior officers recently promoted fr

The officers were decorated during a ceremony held at the RC Duru Officers’ Mess, Nigerian Army College of Logistics and Management, Ojo Campus, Lagos.

According to a statement posted by the Public Relations Officer, Nigerian Army College of Logistics and Management, Major Innocent Audu, on X on Tuesday, the newly promoted officers are Colonel Stephen Aluko of the Nigerian Army Electrical and Mechanical Engineers, who is the Acting Chief Instructor, Junior Leadership and Staff Officers Course; Colonel Shadrach Akom of the Nigerian Army Signals, a member of the Directing Staff and Staff Officer Grade One Training at Moloney Campus; and Colonel Polycarp Offor of the Nigerian Army Education Corps, who serves as Acting Chief Instructor, Languages.

Speaking at the ceremony, Adetoba congratulated the officers and urged them to demonstrate greater professionalism, discipline and commitment in their new positions.

He noted that promotion came through God’s grace, hard work and discipline, urging the newly decorated colonels to continue improving their knowledge and leadership abilities.

The commandant also encouraged the officers to be more empathetic in carrying out their statutory responsibilities and to maintain high professional standards.

Adetoba further acknowledged the contributions of their spouses, appreciating their patience, loyalty and support in ensuring that the officers had a stable home front.

Speaking on behalf of the newly promoted officers, Colonel Stephen Aluko expressed gratitude to God for keeping them alive to witness their elevation.

He also appreciated the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu, for considering them worthy of promotion.

Aluko equally thanked the commandant for his mentorship and guidance, describing the support as valuable to their professional development.

He said, “We appreciate the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu NAM, for finding us worthy and deserving to be elevated to the ranks of Colonels in the Nigerian Army.”

The colonel assured the college of their commitment to greater service, pledging that the officers would redouble their efforts in carrying out their responsibilities.

He also reaffirmed their determination to discharge their duties to the best of their abilities.

The event was attended by the Deputy Commandant, Director of Studies, faculty and administrative staff, as well as friends and family members of the newly promoted officers.
