WPRF 2026: Green celebrates Davido’s sacrifice, hails him as Africa’s ambassador

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 5d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Juliana Taiwo-Obalonye, Abuja

President and Chief Executive Officer of the Global Alliance for Public Relations and Communication Management Justin Green, has praised Nigerian Afrobeats superstar, David Adeleke, popularly known as Davido, for cutting short his stay in the United States to return to Nigeria and perform at the international conference.

Green who is also the global host of the World Public Relations Forum (WPRF) Abuja 2026, described the singer’s decision as an exceptional demonstration of patriotism and ambassadorial spirit, saying his global influence and commitment to projecting a positive image of Nigeria and Africa made him a worthy ambassador of the continent.

Davido thrilled delegates, dignitaries and other guests with a live performance at the forum, bringing Afrobeats energy to the international gathering Wednesday night and adding entertainment to the celebration of African identity and culture.

Green spoke on the fourth day of the forum, tagged African Day, with the theme, “Africa, the Voice of the World: Communication for Peace, Democracy and Development”.

The conference is jointly organised by the Global Alliance for Public Relations and Communication Management, the African Public Relations Association (APRA), the continental host, and the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations (NIPR), the national host.

Recounting how Davido agreed to perform at the international gathering, Green said he had publicly appealed to the singer during a television interview ahead of the conference, expressing his desire to have him perform at the event.

He said the television presenters laughed off the suggestion, believing it was impossible to secure the international music star’s participation.

However, Green said the appeal eventually reached Davido, who was in New York at the time.

According to him, during a visit to the Nigeria Customs Service, he was handed a telephone and informed that someone wanted to speak with him. To his surprise, it was Davido calling from New York.

Green said the singer, who was woken at about 5am because of the time difference, immediately asked which day he was expected to perform.

He said Davido subsequently changed his travel plans, returned to Nigeria and flew from Lagos to Abuja, arriving the previous night before heading straight to the conference.

“He was supposed to still be in New York. He agreed to fly back. He flew back, and he flew from Lagos to here. And he agreed to do it. And he landed last night, came straight here to do it,” Green said.

Commending the gesture, Green said Davido’s willingness to sacrifice his personal schedule to support the conference reflected the Nigerian spirit and his commitment to contributing to the country’s international image.

“That is the type of Nigerian spirit that we keep talking about. He wanted to contribute in a way to make this event and to show the world,” he said.

Green described Davido as a huge ambassador not only for Nigeria but for the entire African continent, noting that the singer’s international achievements and the image he projects wherever he goes have made him an influential voice for Africa globally.

“Everywhere he goes, he puts out a great image of Africa. And what he has achieved worldwide is enormous. And he’s a great, great ambassador for the African continent,” he said.

The African Day celebrations also featured a colourful parade by 54 children representing the 54 African countries, each carrying the flag of a country to showcase the continent’s diversity, identity and unity.

The forum’s Head of Protocol and member of the National Planning Committee, Aisha Gambari, said the children were demonstrating the pride Africans have in their continent, while urging participants to celebrate their shared African identity.

The parade, which featured the playing of the Global Alliance anthem, formed part of activities marking African Day and reinforced the forum’s focus on projecting Africa’s voice, heritage and identity to the world.

The WPRF Abuja 2026 has brought together public relations practitioners, policymakers, business leaders and other stakeholders from across the world to examine the role of strategic communication in advancing peace, democracy and development.
