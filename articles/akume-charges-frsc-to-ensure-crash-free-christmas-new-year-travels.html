Akume charges FRSC to ensure crash-free Christmas, New Year travels

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 3 min read

Secretary to the Government of the Federation (SGF), George Akume, has charged the Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC) to prevent avoidable road crashes and fatalities during the Christmas and New Year festivities, insisting that Nigerians must be guaranteed safe journeys, free traffic flow and prompt emergency response.

Akume gave the directive on Thursday in Abuja while declaring open the FRSC End-of-Year 2026 Corps Marshal Strategy Session with Commanding Officers, ahead of the anticipated increase in vehicular movement across the country during the festive season.

He directed the Corps to intensify intelligence-driven operations and pay particular attention to speeding, dangerous overtaking, overloading, impaired driving and driver fatigue, which remain major risks on Nigerian highways.

The SGF said the success of the Corps’ festive season operations would be measured not by activities or deployments but by tangible improvements in road safety.

“Ultimately, our success must be reflected in fewer crashes and fatalities, improved traffic flow, effective emergency response and greater public confidence,” he said.

Akume charged Zonal and Sector Commanders to take full responsibility for operations within their jurisdictions, ensuring that directives from the Corps leadership translate into visible results on the highways.

He said the strategy session, themed “Strengthening Operational Readiness and Effective Coordination for Seamless Traffic Management During the 2026 Festive Season,” must produce a practical operational plan, clear deployment arrangements, stronger inter-agency collaboration and measurable performance indicators.

The SGF warned that the festive period traditionally comes with increased movement of people and goods, heightening the risks of road crashes and traffic congestion.

He therefore urged the FRSC leadership to objectively assess its performance as the year winds down, acknowledge its achievements and address operational shortcomings.

Akume reaffirmed the Federal Government’s commitment to strengthening institutions responsible for public safety, stressing that road safety remained a national priority requiring sustained government commitment and collective responsibility.

He said the administration of President Bola Tinubu, under the Renewed Hope Agenda, recognises safe and efficient transportation as critical to economic growth, national security and citizens’ well-being.

The SGF also urged the Corps to sustain its institutional reforms, manpower development and digital transformation initiatives while ensuring prudent, transparent and accountable management of public resources.
He stressed that professionalism, discipline, innovation and measurable results must define the Corps’ operations.

“Preventable crashes and unprofessional conduct must not undermine public confidence in the Corps,” he warned.

Akume assured Nigerians travelling for the Christmas and New Year celebrations that effective arrangements would be put in place to protect lives, reduce congestion and facilitate the safe movement of people and goods.

He, however, emphasised that the Federal Government’s continued support for the FRSC must be matched by professionalism, commitment and demonstrable improvements in operational performance.

The strategy session brought together the Corps Marshal, members of the management team, Zonal Commanding Officers, Commandants of Training Institutions, Sector Commanders and other senior officers of the Corps.

Akume commended the Corps Marshal for convening the meeting ahead of the festive season, describing it as an opportunity to review performance, identify operational gaps and strengthen road safety administration nationwide.
