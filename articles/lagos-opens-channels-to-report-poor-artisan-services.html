Lagos opens channels to report poor artisan services

By Punch Punch 6h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Lagos State Government has urged residents to report artisans and other skilled service providers who delay jobs, fail to deliver agreed services or abandon

The Lagos State Consumer Protection Agency said residents experiencing disputes with tailors, plumbers, electricians, mechanics, air-conditioner and refrigerator repairers, carpenters, painters and other skilled professionals could seek redress through its complaint channels.

It also advised consumers to use its mediation services to resolve such disputes rather than resorting to confrontation or involving the police in matters that could be addressed through consumer protection mechanisms.

LASCOPA General Manager and Chief Executive Officer, Afolabi Solebo, disclosed this in a statement posted on the Lagos State Government’s Facebook page on Tuesday.

Solebo said engaging an artisan or skilled professional established a consumer-service provider relationship, entitling customers to fair treatment, satisfactory service delivery and redress when agreed terms were breached.

He said the agency had received several complaints from residents who struggled to resolve disputes with service providers, particularly where artisans failed to complete agreed jobs, delayed delivery or abandoned projects without satisfactory explanations.

He cited cases involving tailors who failed to deliver clothes as agreed and other service providers who abandoned customers after collecting payment.

“We have handled cases where consumers submitted clothes to tailors for sewing, only for the artisans to fail to deliver as agreed. In some instances, consumers were left frustrated and without any clear means of resolving the situation until they were referred to LASCOPA for intervention,” he said.

The LASCOPA chief urged residents facing such challenges to lodge formal complaints with the agency, irrespective of their language, tribe, religion, political affiliation or social status, assuring them of fair and impartial attention.

He also advised consumers to preserve evidence of transactions, including receipts, invoices, payment records, bank debit alerts, transfer confirmations, written agreements and correspondence with service providers, to facilitate the investigation and resolution of complaints.

Solebo called on artisans and other skilled service providers to uphold professionalism, transparency and accountability by delivering services according to agreed specifications, maintaining effective communication with customers and avoiding unnecessary delays or abandonment of jobs.

LASCOPA reaffirmed its commitment to facilitating amicable dispute resolution under the Lagos State Consumer Protection Law 2025 (as amended) and the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.

The agency said it operated annex offices in Ikorodu, Badagry, Bariga, Ikoyi-Obalende, Ojo, Epe, Agege and Isolo to make its services accessible to residents.

Consumers can also contact the agency through its complaint lines, 081 2499 3895 and 091 5389 4878, or its toll-free helpline, 080 0005 2726, available on weekdays.

Complaints may also be sent by email to lasgcopa@gmail.com or lascopa@lagosstate.gov.ng, or through the agency’s Facebook page, LASCOPA, and Instagram account, @lasgcopa.
