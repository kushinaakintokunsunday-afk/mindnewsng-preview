31 bag first class as Ogun college graduates 4,944

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Sikiru Adetona College of Education, Science and Technology, Omu-Ajose, in Odogbolu Local Government Area of Ogun State, is set to graduate 4,944 students a

The Provost of the College, Dr Modupe Oba-Adenuga, disclosed this on Monday during a press conference held as part of activities marking the institution’s second combined convocation ceremony.

According to the Provost, the graduating students comprise 1,932 full-time Nigeria Certificate in Education graduates, 1,816 part-time NCE graduates, 967 first-degree graduates and 229 recipients of the Professional Diploma in Education.

She explained that the NCE category accounts for 3,748 graduates, while the overall figure of 4,944 includes graduates across the College’s various academic programmes.

The first-degree graduates are affiliated with Olabisi Onabanjo University, Ago-Iwoye, while the convocation will cover NCE graduates from the 2019/2020 to 2025/2026 academic sessions, PDE graduates and degree recipients from the 2021/2022 to 2024/2025 academic sessions.

Describing the ceremony as a significant milestone in the institution’s development, Oba-Adenuga said the convocation would celebrate not only the academic accomplishments of the graduating students but also the collective contributions of parents, guardians, lecturers, administrative personnel and other stakeholders.

She explained that “For NCE full-time, we have 1,932 graduands; for NCE part-time, the figure is 1,816; and for our first degree, we have 967. For PDE, that’s Professional Diploma in Education, we have 229. So, the total for NCE is 3,748, and the grand total of all graduating students is 4,944. We have 31 first-class students.

“This 2nd combined ceremony represents an important milestone in the history of our College. It is an occasion to celebrate the academic accomplishments, perseverance and determination of our graduating students, while recognising the contributions of parents, guardians, lecturers, administrative personnel and other stakeholders who have supported their educational journeys.

“Beyond the presentation of certificates and degrees, this convocation is a celebration of the College’s enduring commitment to the production of competent, knowledgeable and professionally equipped graduates who can contribute meaningfully to society.”

The Provost emphasised that the institution remained committed to producing graduates with the requisite knowledge, professional competence and practical skills to contribute to national development.

A major highlight of the convocation activities is the College’s focus on the Federal Government’s NCE-Degree Dual-Mandate Programme, which is expected to expand the academic opportunities available to Colleges of Education across the country.

The Provost also acknowledged the support of the Ogun State Government, particularly the administration of Governor Dapo Abiodun, whom she commended for interventions aimed at strengthening the institution and supporting the implementation of the NCE-Degree Dual-Mandate Programme.

She disclosed that the state government had recently provided financial intervention to the College to support the implementation of the dual-mandate initiative and staff welfare.

She further revealed that the government had increased the College’s monthly subvention in the week preceding the press conference.

As part of activities marking the convocation, the College will confer its prestigious College Fellowship Award on three distinguished Nigerians in recognition of their contributions to education, professional development, leadership and societal advancement.

The recipients are Chief Chris Olufunmilola Okunowo, an accomplished legal practitioner, businessman and community leader; Emeritus Professor Peter Okebukola, an internationally recognised scholar and authority in education; and Dr Angela Ajala, Executive Secretary of the National Commission for Colleges of Education (NCCE).

The College particularly recognises Dr Ajala’s leadership during a period that witnessed the implementation of the NCE-Degree Dual-Mandate Programme.

The institution will also honour its immediate past Provost, Dr Adeola Kiadese Lukmon, who currently serves as Special Adviser to the Ogun State Governor on Education, with an Award of Excellence in recognition of his meritorious service and contributions to the College.

She added that the convocation lecture, themed “The Dual-Mandate NCE-Degree Programme: Prospects, Challenges and the Future of Teacher Education in Nigeria”, would be delivered by Dr Tunji Olowolafe, Chancellor of Ekiti State University and Chairman of Deux Project Limited. She said the lecture was timely, given the ongoing transformation of teacher education and the changing role of Colleges of Education in Nigeria’s tertiary education system.
