No capital needed to start business, QNET tells Nigerians

…Promises job creation for Rivers residents

From Tony John, Port Harcourt

The General Manager of QNET, Ayokunmi Solesi, says Nigerians can start a legitimate business without initial capital through the company’s direct selling model, describing it as a major boost to small and medium enterprises and the nation’s economy.

Solesi spoke on Thursday in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, during a media engagement ahead of the company’s EXPO ’26.

He said every nation that has thrived across the world has taken care of small and medium enterprises, noting that QNET falls under that category by providing business opportunities to people.

“We are providing business opportunity to people. We are saying to you that you do not need to have capital at the beginning of your business,” Solesi said.

“We are providing this business opportunity where you can buy the product at a good price and you can also sell the product, as long as you understand the benefit of the product and the consumers you are also selling it to.”

According to him, the impact goes beyond the company to individuals who will ultimately affect the economy of Nigeria as a whole.

On what stands QNET out from other wellness companies, Solesi said it is all about the product.

“It’s the product. It’s all about the product. Majority of our products already have a patent which is exclusive to us,” he said.

“You will realize that majority of our products also cannot be found anywhere else apart from our company. This is why in all the conversations we have had, we have talked about our wellness product, we have talked about our health product.”

He said the company is not only giving Nigerians good products already certified by the regulatory body, but also giving them business opportunities to sell the products in a legitimate way.

On affordability and access, Solesi said wellness products are generally not cheap, but QNET products offer value for money.

“Affordability is relative. Wellness product generally is not cheap. But I would say to you that in terms of the value itself, it is affordable,” he said.

He added that the products can be easily gotten online via http://QNET.net and delivered to customers’ doorsteps through DHL.

“And by the way, you also do not have to join the network to use our product. You can go online to buy the product directly and it is delivered to your doorstep through DHL. So, if you decide not to take the business opportunity, or you decide to use the product, you can still go on to our retail store to buy the product directly,” Solesi said.

Asked why the company, which has existed for 28 years, is just coming to Port Harcourt now, Solesi said QNET has actually been around in the city through people selling its products.

“We love Port Harcourt, so don’t think that Port Harcourt is the last. We have actually been around even in Port Harcourt. There are people that have been selling the product. But, we said that it is time for us to come to Port Harcourt to let Port Harcourt people know about the product,” he said.

“We want you all in Port Harcourt to understand and Rivers State to understand that QNET, as a company, has come to create opportunities for our youths, not just for our youths, for everyone in Port Harcourt. This is a business model that works. If you understand the product and how it works, you would understand that this is a fantastic opportunity.

“Yes, we have been existing for 28 years, but we have only been in Nigeria since 2022. So, it is a different thing entirely. So, it is just gradually we move across all the states.”

Ends.
