Nigerian Sinotrucks, Sterling Bank partner to expand vehicle ownership, advance mobility solutions

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 5 min read

Photo: The Sun Nigeria

By Moses Akaigwe

Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited has entered into a strategic partnership with Sterling Bank Limited to widen access to vehicle ownership and advance integrated mobility and financial solutions in Nigeria, in a collaboration both organisations say will create new opportunities for individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses.

Collaboration seeks to bridge vehicle acquisition financing gaps, strengthen commercial mobility and unlock new opportunities for businesses and individuals.

The agreement, formalised through the signing of a Memorandum of Agreement (MoA), underscores a shared ambition to address one of the major constraints in the mobility market: access to appropriate financing for vehicle acquisition.

Anchored on the theme, “From Partnership to Progress: Advancing Integrated Mobility and Financial Solutions”, the collaboration brings together Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited’s commercial vehicle expertise and market presence with Sterling Bank’s financial capabilities to create a more accessible vehicle ownership proposition.

For Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited, the partnership represents another significant step in its broader ambition to deepen its footprint in Nigeria’s automotive and transportation ecosystem, while making its vehicle solutions more accessible to a wider customer base.

Speaking on the significance of the agreement, the Managing Director of Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited, Alfred Okugbeni, said the collaboration was driven by the company’s determination to go beyond vehicle supply and provide solutions that respond to the broader needs of customers.

“At Nigerian Sinotrucks, we understand that access to reliable vehicles is only one part of the mobility equation. Access to appropriate financing is equally important. Our partnership with Sterling Bank is a deliberate effort to bridge that gap and create greater opportunities for individuals and businesses to acquire the vehicles they need to grow.

“We believe that when mobility and finance work together, the benefits extend beyond vehicle ownership. They translate into stronger businesses, improved logistics, greater productivity and wider economic opportunities,” he said.

For Sterling Bank, the agreement reinforces its commitment to using strategic partnerships to deliver financial solutions that address real economic needs, with transportation and mobility forming an important part of that agenda.

Speaking at the signing, Akporee Idenedo, Divisional Head, Commercial Banking at Sterling Bank, said the partnership reflects the Bank’s conviction that financial institutions and mobility providers have to work together to unlock growth.

“Transportation is the backbone of commerce. Every truck on the road is a supply chain moving, a business expanding, jobs being created and sustained. Yet for many entrepreneurs and businesses, the cost of acquiring the right vehicle remains a major barrier. Through this partnership with Nigerian Sinotrucks, we are bringing financing directly to the point of need, so customers can access quality vehicles on terms that are practical and fit their realities.

“At Sterling Bank, we believe the most meaningful financial solutions are the ones that solve real problems. This collaboration lets us support individuals and businesses who want to own their vehicles, while helping to build a more efficient and productive transportation ecosystem for the country,” he said.

The agreement also builds on Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited’s broader customer-support proposition, which extends beyond vehicle sales to include after-sales services, maintenance, genuine spare parts and technical training for drivers and mechanics.

These capabilities are particularly important in commercial transportation, where vehicle reliability, maintenance support and operational efficiency have direct implications for business performance. 

The company, which is wholly Nigerian-owned and was incorporated in 2012, is a Federal Government-recognised vehicle manufacturer and assembler, as well as an authorised distributor of Sinotruk products in Nigeria. It received Federal Government recognition as a vehicle assembler in January 2015. 

Its portfolio spans heavy-duty commercial vehicles, including HOWO tractor heads, dump trucks, tippers, cargo trucks, tankers and other specialised vehicles. The company has also expanded its alternative-energy offerings, with CNG and LNG-powered trucks, alongside its recent introduction of Sitrak electric commercial trucks into the Nigerian market. 

This evolving product offering reflects the company’s determination to respond to changing demands in the transportation industry, particularly as businesses seek more efficient, cost-effective and sustainable ways to move goods and manage logistics operations.

The partnership with Sterling Bank is expected to complement this ambition by bringing financing more directly into the vehicle acquisition process, helping to address the capital constraints that can prevent individuals and businesses from investing in the vehicles they need.

Industry observers have increasingly pointed to the need for stronger collaboration between vehicle distributors, financial institutions and other mobility stakeholders to address the high cost of vehicle acquisition and improve access to transportation assets.

Against this backdrop, the Nigerian Sinotrucks-Sterling Bank partnership represents a practical approach to connecting vehicle solutions with financial accessibility, with the potential to support businesses across logistics, manufacturing, distribution, construction and other sectors dependent on transportation.

The signing ceremony was marked by the exchange of duly executed copies of the agreement, followed by a handshake between representatives of both organisations and a brief celebratory moment, symbolising the formal commencement of the collaboration.

More than a ceremonial milestone, the occasion represented a shared commitment to building a stronger mobility ecosystem in which financial access and reliable vehicle solutions work together to unlock progress.

For Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited, the partnership reinforces its position as a mobility solutions provider with ambitions that extend beyond the supply of commercial vehicles to supporting the wider transportation and automotive value chain.

As the collaboration takes shape, both organisations will be looking to translate the agreement into tangible customer benefits, expanded access to vehicle ownership and greater opportunities for businesses to move, grow and compete.

From partnership to progress, the message is clear: when mobility meets finance, progress moves forward.

According to the Managing Director, Nigerian Sinotrucks Limited, Alfred Okugbeni, ‘Nigerian Sinotrucks is not simply putting more vehicles on Nigerian roads; it is helping create the financial and mobility partnerships that make progress possible.’’
