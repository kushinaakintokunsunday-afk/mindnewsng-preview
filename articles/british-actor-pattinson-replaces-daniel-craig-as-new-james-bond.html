British actor Pattinson replaces Daniel Craig as new James Bond

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

British actor Robert Pattinson has been officially announced as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig in the iconic film franchise. Amazon MGM Studios an

Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting on Monday, November 16, 2026, through a statement published on its official website, saying Pattinson would take on the role of the legendary British secret agent in its highly anticipated film.

The announcement was also confirmed on the official James Bond social media channels on Instagram and X, as verified by our correspondent.

The upcoming 26th James Bond film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay. Amy Pascal and David Heyman will produce through Pascal Pictures and Heyday Films, respectively, while Tanya Lapointe will serve as executive producer.

Reacting to his appointment, Pattinson described the opportunity as the honour of a lifetime.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team,” he said.

The head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, Mike Hopkins, described the casting as a defining moment for the studio and a significant milestone in the history of the franchise.

“Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond as we proudly introduce Robert Pattinson as the next 007,” Hopkins said.

“This iconic character has captivated audiences around the world for more than six decades, and there’s no doubt that Robert’s remarkable range, magnetic screen presence, and quiet intensity make him an exceptional choice to carry that legacy forward.

“With Denis Villeneuve at the helm, Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing, Tanya Lapointe executive producing, and a brilliant script from Steven Knight, we have a world-class creative team in place to usher in a new era of Bond, and we couldn’t be more excited about the journey ahead,” he added.

The head of film at Amazon MGM Studios, Courtenay Valenti, also welcomed Pattinson’s appointment, saying the studio was confident he would bring something special to the role.

“Few films carry the kind of anticipation that comes with James Bond, and we have strived to honor the passionate fans, the legacy, and the future of this iconic character. We are thrilled to officially welcome Robert Pattinson as our 007 and are confident he will bring something truly special to this role. Robert, Denis, Amy, David, and Tanya are embarking on an amazing adventure together,” Valenti said.

Villeneuve, who will direct the film, said he was delighted to welcome the actor after considering other talents for the role.

“Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin,” the director said.

Producers Pascal and Heyman also expressed confidence in the actor’s ability to carry on the franchise’s legacy.

“James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times,” they said.

Pattinson, who has built a career spanning more than two decades, is known for his roles in major film productions, including the Harry Potter franchise, The Twilight Saga, Tenet, The Lighthouse, Mickey 17 and The Batman.

He is also currently in production on The Batman Part II, the sequel to the 2022 film.

Amazon said its acquisition of MGM in 2022 included a catalogue of more than 4,000 films and 17,000 television shows.

In March 2025, Amazon MGM Studios also concluded a new joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, under which the studio gained creative control of the James Bond intellectual property rights.
