My life changed after I stopped following politicians around – Deji Adeyanju

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Human rights lawyer, Deji Adeyanju has again enjoined youths, especially those serving as political praise-singers, errand boys and social media attack dogs for politicians, to invest in education, skills and personal enterprise instead of depending on handouts from office holders.

Adeyanju gave the fresh charge in a Facebook post on Monday.

Citing a personal example, the lawyer said the decision he took 11 years ago to take his life seriously and stop hanging around politicians laid the foundation for the academic and professional turnaround he now enjoys.

He said, “PhD in Law in view! Looking back at my decision to start afresh and take my life seriously, I’m deeply grateful to God. You can achieve anything—just take the first step. People only take you seriously when you take yourself seriously. I beg everyone following useless Nigerian politicians around: after this upcoming election, abandon that life and take your future seriously. I made that choice 11 years ago, and my life has never been the same.”

The Abuja-based lawyer has often taken to his page to discourage young Nigerians from depending on politicians for survival, emphasising the value of education and skills for personal upliftment.

Adeyanju, who previously served as Head of New Media for the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP), left partisan politics to pursue full-time activism in 2017.

He later returned to school, bagging a Bachelor of Laws with First Class Honours from Baze University, Abuja in 2022, and was called to the Nigerian Bar shortly after. He went on to obtain an LL.M from the same university and is now pursuing a PhD in Law.

He has said in previous interviews that multiple arrests and trumped-up charges during his years of activism motivated him to study law, so he could defend himself and others.

Since his call to Bar, Adeyanju has co-founded the Abuja-based law firm, Deji Adeyanju & Partners, and has become one of the most visible human rights lawyers in the country, representing protesters, journalists and detained activists, including during the #EndBadGovernance protests in August 2024.
