2027: Diaspora group plans debate for N’Assembly Benue South candidates

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The incumbent Senator representing Benue South, Abba Moro, and other aspirants seeking election into the National Assembly in 2027 are set to face public scruti

The association said the exercise would allow candidates to present their programmes, explain their positions on issues affecting the district and convince constituents why they deserve their votes.

Its President, Edwin Ochai, disclosed this in a statement made available to PUNCH Online on Wednesday, saying the initiative was aimed at promoting accountability, informed electoral choices and political participation.

“We believe that the people deserve an opportunity to hear directly from those seeking their mandate. Leadership should not be about political promises alone; candidates must be able to articulate their vision, explain their priorities and demonstrate how they intend to address the challenges confronting our people,” he said.

According to the association, the senatorial candidates’ debate will hold on Saturday, November 21, 2026, while aspirants for the House of Representatives will have their session on Sunday, November 22.

He disclosed that both sessions are scheduled to begin at 9pm West Africa Time via Zoom.

Ochai said the initiative was also intended to strengthen the relationship between Idoma people in the diaspora and their homeland.

“Being in the diaspora does not diminish our interest in the affairs of our homeland. We remain deeply connected to the aspirations, challenges and future of the Idoma nation. We believe that contributing to the quality of leadership and representation is a responsibility that transcends geographical boundaries,” he added.

He said the debate would encourage voters to assess candidates based on their competence, ideas and commitment to development rather than party affiliations.

“Our objective is to promote informed choices. We want the people to understand what each candidate stands for, what they intend to achieve and how they plan to contribute to the development of our communities. The quality of representation we get in the National Assembly matters to the future of our people,” Ochai said.

Among the senatorial aspirants are Moro of the Peoples Democratic Party, Francis Ottah Agbo of the All Progressives Congress, David Olofu of the African Democratic Congress, Dan Onjeh of the Nigerian Democratic Congress and Samuel Onuh of the Labour Party.

The association, which has members across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Russia and Germany, urged Idoma people at home and abroad to participate in the debate and demand accountability from those seeking public office.
