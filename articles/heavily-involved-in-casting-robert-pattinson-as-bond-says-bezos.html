‘Heavily Involved’ In Casting Robert Pattinson As Bond, Says Bezos

By Channels TV Channels TV 6d ago 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG

Amazon boss Jeff Bezos said Tuesday he was “heavily involved” in choosing British actor Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond.

Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday that Pattinson, 40, would step into the coveted role of the world’s most famous fictional spy following months of speculation.

“There was something special about Rob Pattinson and his performances,” Bezos said at the Semafor tech conference in California.

“Initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him just because he’s already so famous,” he added.

“But in the screening tests, it was so obvious, ‘he is James Bond'”.

Amazon bought the storied Hollywood studio MGM in 2022, but it was not until last year that the company struck a deal with Bond producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael Wilson to obtain full creative control over the franchise.

It had been fiercely guarded by the Broccoli family since 1962’s “Dr. No”, when screen legend Sean Connery first starred as Bond, as they insisted on preserving the integrity of the character and brand.

Pattinson got his early break in “Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire” (2005) as Cedric Diggory, a champion for Hogwarts, but rocketed to global fame as Edward Cullen in romantic vampire saga “Twilight”.
