Pinheiro unveils six books on key areas of Nigerian law

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

After nearly four decades in legal practice, Senior Advocate of Nigeria, Dr ‘Kemi Pinheiro, will on Friday, November 20, 2026, unveil six books examining differ

The presentation, organised by Pinheiro LP, will be chaired by the Chief Justice of Nigeria, Justice Kudirat Motonmori Olatokunbo Kekere-Ekun, and is expected to attract members of the judiciary, the Bar, academia and other stakeholders in the justice sector.

The six publications are “Strategies in Criminal Trial: A Practitioner’s Guide”; “Insolvency Law in Nigeria: Practice and Procedure”; “Arbitration Law and Practice in Nigeria; Defining Moments: Chronicles of a Silk (Volume One)”; “The Compendium; and MULAN Legal: Trending Issues of Law and Faith – Essays in Honour of ‘Kemi Pinheiro, SAN”.

The books cover criminal litigation, corporate insolvency, arbitration, legal practice, professional experiences and the intersection of law and faith.

In Defining Moments: Chronicles of a Silk (Volume One), Pinheiro documents aspects of his professional journey, reflecting on experiences and defining moments from nearly four decades at the Bar.

His Strategies in Criminal Trial: A Practitioner’s Guide examines the practical demands of criminal litigation, including procedural and evidential issues confronting legal practitioners. It also addresses the treatment and admissibility of electronic evidence in criminal proceedings.

The publication on insolvency, Insolvency Law in Nigeria: Practice and Procedure, examines the legal framework for corporate rescue, restructuring and insolvency under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020.

Similarly, Arbitration Law and Practice in Nigeria explores the country’s arbitration framework under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023, with particular attention to enforcing arbitral awards and the growing role of alternative dispute resolution in commercial disputes.

The collection also features contributions from other legal practitioners and academics.

The Compendium brings together scholarly essays examining various legal issues and developments, while MULAN Legal: Trending Issues of Law and Faith – Essays in Honour of ‘Kemi Pinheiro, SAN explores the relationship between law, faith and Nigeria’s plural legal system.

Together, the publications combine practical legal guidance, academic contributions and personal reflections on the legal profession.

The presentation is expected to give members of the judiciary, legal practitioners, academics and other stakeholders an opportunity to engage with the legal issues addressed in the books.
