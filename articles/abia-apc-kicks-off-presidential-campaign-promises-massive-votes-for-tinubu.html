Abia APC kicks off Presidential Campaign, promises massive votes for Tinubu

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 3 min read

Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Abia State chapter of the All Progressives Congress (APC), has kicked off its presidential campaign rally with assurance to galvanize massive votes for President Bola Tinubu in the 2027 election.

Speaking at the rally held at Umuahia Township stadium, deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives, Rt. Hon Benjamin Kalu while assuring Tinubu of massive votes from Abia in 2027, was emphatic they were not going to negotiate with anybody for the president to get 25 percent votes in Abia as APC in the state has the capacity to give him over 75 percent votes.

Rt. Hon Kalu commended President Tinubu for choosing a tough path in his quest to revamp the nation’s economy.

Kalu said in the last three years, the President chose the tough path of removal of fuel subsidy so Nigeria could continue to exist as a nation.

He stated that the moment of hardship was over and that now is the moment of joy as the people will start to reap from their sacrifices.

He told the mammoth crowd at the stadium of some other things the president has done that should make him to be re-elected for a second term in office.

Kalu mentioned the stabilization of the currency, stoppage of strike by the Academic Staff Union of Universities (ASUU), and tax reform he said has brought stability to the fiscal policy, among others as some of the things Tinubu has done to strengthen the country.

The deputy Speaker applauded the President for the creation and funding of the the State East Development Commission (SEDC), and his efforts to fight insecurity in different parts of the country.

The State Chairman of the party, Hon Chijioke Chukwu commended President Tinubu for what he has been doing for the state.

Apart from the infrastructural development he said are going on in South East under the Tinubu administration, he equally mentioned Benjamin Kalu becoming the deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives as the handiwork of Tinubu.

“Tinubu has ensured the provision of some critical infrastructure in the state and these include the ongoing reconstruction of the Umuahia/Ikot Ekpene, Omenuko Bridge and others.

He equally mentioned the approval by the National Assembly, for the establishment of some tertiary institutions in the state which he said has the backing of the President.

He said Tinubu was doing all these despite the fact that the state gave him merger vote in 2023 and said 2027 will be the time to reciprocate the President’s goodness to the state by support and voting him for a second term.

He thanked the President for not only establishing the South East Development Commission (SEDC), but also adequately funding it for the good of the people.

The former Governor of the state and current Nigerian Ambassador to Spain, Dr. Okezie Ikpeazu said they would all work together to ensure Tinubu is re-elected in 2027.

Represented by the deputy Governorship candidate of the People’s Democratic Party (PDP), Amah Abraham, Ikpeazu recalled that the nation was in a critical condition before Tinubu and the President for his efforts to revive the country.

The Abia Governorship candidate of APC, Dr. Eric Opah said Abia will not only vote massively for Tinubu in the coming election, but also sweep other seats at the polls.

Other speakers including Mike Okoye (jnr), Managing Director of the SEDC, Cosmos Ndukwe, Henry Ikoh, Donatus Nwamara, among others, all spoke glowingly of the President and promised of massive votes for him in 2027.
