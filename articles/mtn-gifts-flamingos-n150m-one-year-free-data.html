MTN gifts Flamingos N150m, one year free data

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Telecommunications giant MTN has presented Nigeria’s U-17 women’s national team, the Flamingos, with a N150m cash package and one year of free data subscription

Telecommunications giant MTN has presented Nigeria’s U-17 women’s national team, the Flamingos, with a N150m cash package and one year of free data subscriptions following their triumph at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup in Morocco.

At a reception held in their honour on Monday, MTN Chief Executive Officer, Karl Toriola, announced the financial rewards in recognition of the team’s historic achievement at the tournament, where the Flamingos defeated Japan 2-1 in the final to become the first African nation to win the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup.

The package includes N5m for each of the 21 players, N7m for the head coach, N5m for the assistant coach, N5m for the programme liaison officer, N3m for the fitness coach and N20m for the technical team.

According to MTN, the players’ individual rewards will be disbursed through their respective parents into designated accounts provided by the telecommunications company, in line with the necessary legal requirements.

MTN’s gesture is the latest in a series of rewards and recognition extended to the Flamingos since their return to Nigeria. The Federal Government, the Senate and the Lagos State Government have also announced various incentives for the team following their World Cup success.

Speaking at the event, Toriola said the team had made history and deserved recognition for its resilience and determination throughout the tournament.

“You have made history as the first African women’s team to win a FIFA World Cup title at any level, and we are delighted to celebrate with you. You stumbled at the beginning, picked yourselves up and excelled at the end. The character of the team, the way you overcame setbacks and believed in yourselves, is impressive.

“As you move to the next chapter, remember you have shown the world what Nigerian talent can achieve, but never forget the hard work, ethics and discipline that took you there.”

Acting NFF General Secretary, Dr Emmanuel Ikpeme, commended MTN for its support of Nigerian football and expressed hope that the partnership would continue.

“It has been a wonderful partnership. In fact, I appreciate, and Nigerians appreciate, your support for all that you have done in the last five years, and we look forward to this partnership continuing.”
