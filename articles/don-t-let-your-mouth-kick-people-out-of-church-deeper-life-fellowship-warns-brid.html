‘Don’t let your mouth kick people out of church’, Deeper Life fellowship warns bridal shower critics

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Deeper Life Campus Fellowship at the University of Calgary, Canada, has cautioned church members against criticising a foreign bride and other members over

The fellowship’s reaction followed criticism from some Deeper Life members, particularly in Nigeria, after videos of the bridal shower surfaced online.

A Nigerian man living in Canada recently married a woman identified as Michelle, who is from the Philippines.

Ahead of the wedding, some Deeper Life sisters in Canada organised a bridal shower to celebrate the bride-to-be.

Videos from the event showed the women holding balloons as they prepared to welcome Michelle into the venue before celebrating with her through various activities.

The event also featured trendy social media challenges, including the “I’m not the bride, I’m the bride’s friend” trend.

However, the videos attracted criticism from some Deeper Life members, who questioned the women’s appearance, particularly their use of braids, weaves and trousers, which they said were contrary to the church’s standards in Nigeria.

Some also questioned why the bridal shower was organised, claiming that such celebrations were not permitted for brides in Nigeria.

Responding to the backlash, the Deeper Life Campus Fellowship at the University of Calgary urged members to exercise restraint and avoid making comments that could discourage people from attending the church.

The fellowship explained that Michelle was not born into the church but attended because she appreciated its teachings.

“Just to know. Michelle wasn’t born into Deeper Life.

“I believe we have people who love coming to join us because we have the Word, which is one of the great reasons she loves attending Deeper Life.

“Probably with all this long talk going around, she may decide to never be a member.

“Don’t let your mouth kick people out of the church.

“Don’t be quick to judge,” the fellowship wrote.

It urged Christians to support one another through prayers rather than condemnation, citing Hebrews 12:12 and James 1:19.

“As a Christian we should be praying and holding one another’s hand. Heb 12:12.

“Be careful of what you say. Swift to hear and slow to speak James 1:19.

“Remember to read your Bible very well and look only unto Jesus. God bless you,” it added.
