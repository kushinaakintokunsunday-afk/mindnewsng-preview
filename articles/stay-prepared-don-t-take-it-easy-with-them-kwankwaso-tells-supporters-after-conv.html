‘Stay prepared, don’t take it easy with them’ — Kwankwaso tells supporters after convoy attack

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 6d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The vice-presidential candidate of the opposition Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Rabiu Kwankwaso, has urged party supporters in Kano State to remain prepare

The vice-presidential candidate of the opposition Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Rabiu Kwankwaso, has urged party supporters in Kano State to remain prepared for possible confrontations following an attack on his campaign convoy in Bichi Local Government Area.

Kwankwaso charged his supporters to stand their ground against those attacking them, urging them to adopt what he described as an “ambush” strategy to prepare for any eventuality.

His directive was captured in a viral video recorded during a campaign rally in Bichi, shortly after youths reportedly attacked his convoy with stones.

“Continue to stay prepared. These shameless people—don’t take it easy with them. We are telling you every day to stay prepared, but you should stay down and take the pattern of ambush to ensure that you are ready for any eventuality,” Kwankwaso said.

The former Kano State governor also launched a verbal attack on the member of the House of Representatives representing Bichi Federal Constituency, Abubakar Kabir, although he did not mention the lawmaker by name.

Kwankwaso alleged that the lawmaker had benefited from proceeds of corruption and used questionable funds to advance his political ambitions.

He also made references to the lawmaker’s late father, who served as a state legislator during the early years of the Fourth Republic before becoming a commissioner under former Kano State Governor Ibrahim Shekarau.

“There is a certain issue that dates back to when we were doing politics with their parents—when their parents were our boys. At this time, this is also a special moment. Now, they are dealing with little boys—these ragtag youths,” he said.

Kwankwaso further declared that the political movement he leads was beyond the influence of those he accused of benefiting from the country’s democratic system through questionable means.

“I want to tell the boys in Abuja, and tell the boys in Kano State: this moving train is beyond them; it is even beyond their grandfathers! And I want to tell them that they are the ones reaping the benefits of this democracy; they are the ones looting money; they are the ones benefiting from dirty money,” he stated.

He also warned those he accused of wrongdoing against provoking further political tensions, saying they should be careful about making accusations against others.

Addressing the youths at the rally, Kwankwaso commended their efforts during the campaign tour and urged them to remain committed to the party’s activities.

He said he had received reports that some supporters had already engaged in confrontations with their opponents before his campaign team arrived in Bichi.

“Even before you came—before we entered this town—I heard that you had already fought hard. Keep the spirit alive and prepare for them,” he added.

The remarks followed an earlier attack on the NDC campaign convoy led by Kwankwaso alongside the party’s Kano State governorship candidate, Aminu Abdussalam.

The campaign team had earlier visited Tofa, Rimin Gado, Kabo, Gwarzo, Shanono and Bagwai local government areas as part of a campaign tour before encountering youths who reportedly pelted the convoy with stones in Bichi.

The incident has added to concerns over political violence and rising tensions among supporters of rival political groups in Kano State.
