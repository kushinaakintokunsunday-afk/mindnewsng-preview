Fashion, Nollywood, match made in heaven – Bimbo Ademoye

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Popular actress Bimbo Ademoye has described fashion as an important part of filmmaking, saying costume choices contribute significantly to storytelling and the

Speaking in an interview with Saturday Beats, the actress stressed that filmmakers should pay attention to actors’ outfits, noting that costumes should communicate something about the characters and the stories being told.

“A storyline is very important. Picture quality is also very important. But the outfits one wears in a movie are important too. Even if one is wearing a rag, it should be able to tell a story. Even if they are pressing their phones, they are listening to the lines being delivered with their ears. As they are looking at the screen, they are also looking at what you are wearing and listening to what you are saying. So, make it worthwhile. Fashion and Nollywood are a match made in heaven,” she said.

Speaking about the fashion moments in her recent movie, My 30th Wedding, Ademoye said the looks were deliberately put together and required considerable effort.

“The fashion moments in the movie were intentional. I had sleepless nights putting that together. Was it intentional? Definitely,” the actress added.

On what aspiring professionals should do when faced with discouragement, the actress advised them to reassess their approach, identify their mistakes and remain persistent.

“If you are discouraged, as easy as it is to say keep going, I would say go back to the drawing board. Know what it is you are doing wrong, then you can start over and keep going. One day, you will definitely strike the diamond,” she said.
