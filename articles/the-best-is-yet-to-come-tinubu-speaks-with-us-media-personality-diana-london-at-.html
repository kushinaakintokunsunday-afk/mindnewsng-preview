‘The best is yet to come’ – Tinubu speaks with US media personality Diana London at Presidential Villa (Photos)

President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has granted an exclusive interview to American political strategist and media personality, Diana London.

The interview is expected to be made public soon, according to an announcement by Imran Muhammad, Senior Special Assistant on New Media to the All Progressives Congress (APC) National Chairman, Prof Nentawe Yilwatda.

Muhammad disclosed the development in a post on his X account on Tuesday, confirming that the President had already sat down for the interview.

“President Tinubu has held an exclusive interview with Dianna London, a prominent American media personality. The interview is anticipated to be released soon,” Muhammad wrote.
London also shared photographs of herself at the Presidential Villa in Abuja, where she was seen alongside Tinubu and his Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila.

In a separate post, London described the security partnership between Nigeria and the United States as one that was “delivering results”.

“The U.S-Nigeria security partnership is delivering results. Peace through strength. None of this happens without the ongoing partnership between President Trump and President Tinubu, their steadfast commitment to eliminating terrorists, and Nigeria’s willingness to work closely with the U.S. government,” she wrote.

She added that the partnership could create more economic and trade opportunities between both countries.

“Because of the success of their partnership and relationship, the economic and trade opportunities are endless. The best is yet to come. Nigeria is the United States’ greatest ally in Africa,” London stated.

In another post, she said it had been an honour to work with the team representing Nigeria and supporting efforts to protect religious groups and strengthen relations between the Trump administration and the Nigerian government.

“It has been an honour to work on the team of people representing Nigeria and helping to protect religious groups and further the relationship between the Trump administration and the Nigerian government,” she wrote.
