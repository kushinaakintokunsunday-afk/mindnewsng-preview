Nollywood stars making waves on and off screen

For some Nollywood stars, the spotlight extends beyond their performances on screen. They have ventured behind the camera, taking on the demanding roles of producers and directors, shaping narratives, bringing stories to life and creating films that have captivated audiences and made their mark at the box office. VERA ALKALI profiles some of the industry’s leading talents who have mastered the art of wearing many hats, both in front of and behind the camera

Funke Akindele

Olufunke Akindele, popularly known as Jenifa, has carved out a formidable place for herself in Nollywood, establishing a successful career that spans acting, producing, directing and screenwriting.

She first rose to prominence with her portrayal of the lead character in the television series I Need to Know and later cemented her popularity with Jenifa’s Diary. Her performance in I Need to Know earned her the Africa Movie Academy Award for Best Actress in a Leading Role. Since then, she has evolved from a celebrated screen actress into one of the industry’s most commercially successful filmmakers.

Her move into filmmaking has yielded a string of cinema successes, including Omo Ghetto: The Saga, Battle on Buka Street, A Tribe Called Judah and Everybody Loves Jenifa. The latter grossed about N1.88bn, while A Tribe Called Judah raked in approximately N1.4bn at the box office. With a reported cumulative box-office gross of over N4.7bn, Akindele has established herself as one of Nigeria’s highest-grossing filmmakers.

Her 2025 production, Behind the Scenes, further extended her impressive box-office run. Yet, despite her achievements behind the camera, Akindele has remained active as an actress, continuing to take on screen roles and demonstrating her versatility in both fields.

Toyin Abraham

Olutoyin Aimakhu, popularly known as Toyin Abraham, is another Nollywood actress who has successfully expanded her career into film production and directing while maintaining a strong presence on screen.

Known for her comic timing and ability to bring relatable characters to life, Abraham has built a film brand around stories that entertain and resonate with cinema audiences. She was nominated for Best Supporting Actress for her role in the Yoruba film Ebimi Ni.

Her popular Alakada franchise remains one of her major creative ventures, with instalments including Alakada Reloaded, Fate of Alakada and Alakada: Bad and Boujee. She has also produced films such as The Ghost and the Tout, Ijakumo, The Born Again Stripper and Malaika.

In 2025, she made her directorial debut with Oversabi Aunty, in which she also starred and served as producer. The comedy crossed the N1bn mark at the box office, marking a major milestone in her filmmaking career. Abraham has, however, continued to feature in other productions, balancing her work behind the camera with her acting career.

Femi Adebayo

Femi Adebayo has built a career that extends beyond his reputation as a lawyer and actor in Yoruba-language cinema. The son of veteran actor Adebayo Salami, popularly known as Oga Bello, he has established himself as an actor, producer and filmmaker, bringing ambitious stories to the screen.

Through his production outfit, Euphoria360 Media, Adebayo has been involved in notable productions, including King of Thieves and Jagun Jagun.

Released in 2022, King of Thieves grossed over N320m at the Nigerian box office, becoming one of the notable commercial successes in Yoruba cinema.

Adebayo also starred in the film as Agesinkole, combining his performance on screen with his responsibilities behind the scenes. His subsequent production, Jagun Jagun, further demonstrated his interest in large-scale storytelling. The film earned him the Africa Movie Academy Award for Best Actor in a Supporting Role in 2024.

In 2025, his performance in Seven Doors earned him the Africa Magic Viewers’ Choice Award for Best Lead Actor. Despite his growing portfolio as a filmmaker, Adebayo remains active as an actor.

Odunlade Adekola

Odunlade Adekola is one of the familiar faces in Yoruba cinema whose career has grown beyond acting. Popular for his expressive performances, comic delivery and ability to interpret diverse characters, Adekola has taken on additional responsibilities in filmmaking, including directing and producing.

His involvement in productions such as Ajakaju: Beasts of Two Worlds and King of Thieves (Agesinkole) reflects his contribution to the industry beyond his performances on screen.

In Ajakaju: Beasts of Two Worlds, which he directed, Adekola also starred as an actor. The 2024 fantasy film grossed approximately N252.8m at the box office, making it one of the commercially successful productions of the year. His involvement in King of Thieves, another major Yoruba cinema production, further highlights his versatility.

While he continues to entertain audiences with his performances, Adekola has also demonstrated an interest in shaping the stories that make it to the screen.

Omoni Oboli

Omoni Oboli has successfully combined her career as an actress with filmmaking, directing and producing. After featuring in several Nollywood productions, she expanded her creative responsibilities, becoming one of the actresses actively involved in shaping the stories told on screen.

Her filmmaking credits include Being Mrs Elliot, The First Lady, Okafor’s Law and the Wives on Strike franchise. The franchise, which explores issues affecting women, marriage and family relationships, has become one of her notable contributions to Nigerian cinema.

The 2024 instalment, Wives on Strike: The Uprising, which she directed, recorded a reported domestic box-office gross of N127.1m. Beyond her work behind the camera, Oboli has continued to feature in films, maintaining her connection with audiences while pursuing her interests as a filmmaker.

Mercy Aigbe

Mercy Aigbe is another actress who has extended her career into film production and directing while maintaining her presence in front of the camera. Having built a strong following through her performances in Yoruba and English-language films, she has continued to explore other aspects of filmmaking.

One of her notable projects, Ada Omo Daddy, released in December 2023, featured her alongside actors such as Charles Okafor, Nkechi Blessing and Omowunmi Dada. The family drama, which she co-produced and directed, enjoyed an eight-week cinema run and grossed approximately N218.8m.

She followed it with other projects, including Thin Line, further demonstrating her commitment to developing stories for cinema audiences. Aigbe’s career reflects how established actors can draw on their experience and audience appeal to assume greater creative responsibilities without stepping away from acting.
