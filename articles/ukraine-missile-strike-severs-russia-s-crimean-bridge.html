Ukraine Missile Strike Severs Russia’s Crimean Bridge

By Channels TV Channels TV 6d ago 4 min read

A section of the bridge connecting Russia to the annexed Crimean peninsula appeared to be lying in rubble in the water on Tuesday after Ukraine hit the road-and-rail link with missiles in a brazen overnight attack.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky hailed the strike as “unprecedented” and a justified retaliation for Russia’s barrages of its infrastructure in the almost five years since Moscow invaded.

Road traffic was completely halted, with Russian-installed officials on the peninsula urging locals not to panic and saying the delivery of food and essentials would not be affected.

Moscow also launched attacks on key Ukrainian bridges, firing ballistic missiles at two major river crossings in Kyiv.

AFP reporters saw crater-like holes on Kyiv’s Northern Bridge and people crossing on foot during the morning rush hour.

“It’s cold, raining, just a total mess. It feels like I’m in some kind of movie… This is hardcore for Ukrainians,” Veronika Roshchenko, a 20-year-old jewellery workshop employee said.

The wave of attacks comes ahead of a historic meeting of the Chinese, US and Russian leaders, and could disrupt Russian President Vladimir Putin’s attempts to cast the meeting as a new Yalta: an image of unassailable world leaders dividing up spheres of influence.

Zelensky had warned of a major Russian attack in the days leading up to Thursday’s meeting in China’s Shenzhen, when Putin meets Presidents Xi Jinping and Donald Trump.

“We have repeatedly offered Russia steps toward de-escalation — and every proposal has been met with loud rejection,” Zelensky said on social media, posting footage of a Ukrainian missile approaching the bridge in the dark of night.

“We are also reminding Russia that war will always return to where it began,” he said.

Russia annexed Crimea in 2014, one of the opening moves in what became a deadly war between Kyiv and Russian-backed separatists in the east of the country.

Ukraine’s SBU security service said one of the bridge’s metal arches “and the road span beneath it have been completely destroyed”.

“The central arch and spans have collapsed, and the rail section has suffered critical damage,” Kyiv’s defence ministry added.

Images published by the SBU showed piles of concrete rubble lying on the base — the bridge broken in two parts with a clean gap, several dozen metres wide, in the middle.

The Kremlin said the bridge — a symbol of Putin’s rule and Russia’s annexation of Crimea — would be “restored and will be even better than before”.

The Russian transport ministry said that railway traffic across the bridge has been resumed “on one track” and that repair work was underway “on the second section”.

Russia’s defence ministry said it downed 1,441 Ukrainian drones in a major overnight barrage — the second-highest one-day figure this year.

Two people were killed in Crimea in the attack, the Russian-installed governor said.

Strikes On Ukraine

The strike on the bridge comes as Russia’s attacks on Ukrainian infrastructure intensified with the onset of winter.

AFP reporters in Kyiv heard waves of attacks as Russia fired missiles and drones at the city overnight.

Both the Northern and Southern bridges in the capital — key bridges across the Dnipro river that form part of the city’s ring road — were hit.

Traffic was suspended, causing gridlock on Tuesday morning.

Russia’s army confirmed it had hit both bridges.

Kyiv’s Mayor Vitali Klitschko posted a video from the Northern Bridge showing debris strewn across the road. He later said traffic was partially resumed through the Southern Bridge.

The strikes on Kyiv also disrupted heating and water in parts of the capital, he said.

New Yalta

The wave of strikes comes as the United States renews its push for an end to the war.

But diplomatic efforts have so far failed to reach a breakthrough, with fighting grinding into another winter.

Moscow wants a sweeping deal to rewrite “Europe’s security architecture” as part of any agreement to halt the fighting.

Russia has previously demanded curbs on NATO’s presence in central and eastern Europe and restrictions on the deployment of foreign troops and hardware to Ukraine.

Kyiv and Europe are resisting any attempts to overhaul the continent’s post-Cold War defence, calling for a ceasefire in the Ukraine war before any discussion of terms.

Zelensky has raised alarm that the Shenzhen meeting could be a Yalta-like “redistribution of spheres of influence” for the 21st century.

The 1945 Yalta summit — held in Crimea, then part of the Soviet Union — saw US president Franklin D. Roosevelt, Britain’s Winston Churchill and Soviet leader Joseph Stalin carve up new post-WWII territorial boundaries.
