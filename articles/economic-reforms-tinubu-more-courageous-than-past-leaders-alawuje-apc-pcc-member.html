Economic Reforms: Tinubu more courageous than past leaders – Alawuje, APC-PCC member

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 5 min read

A member of the President Bola Ahmed Tinubu Campaign Council, Hon. Comrade Abdulhakeem Adegoke Alawuje, has defended President Tinubu’s economic reforms, describing him as a courageous democrat and fearless reformer willing to confront Nigeria’s longstanding economic challenges.

Alawuje, in a press statement titled “Tinubu: A Courageous Democrat, A Fearless Reformer and a Determined Seeker of National Progress,” said the President had demonstrated the political courage required to address structural economic problems that successive administrations had struggled to resolve.

According to him, Nigeria has reached a defining moment in its history when difficult decisions and necessary sacrifices must be embraced to secure a better future.

“For Nigeria, that moment is now,” he declared.

He argued that genuine national transformation could not be achieved through political convenience, unrealistic promises or the continuous postponement of difficult decisions.

“President Bola Ahmed Tinubu has demonstrated the courage of a leader who understands that genuine national transformation cannot be achieved through political convenience, unrealistic promises or the continuous postponement of difficult decisions,” Alawuje stated.

The campaign council member acknowledged that the government’s reform policies had imposed significant economic difficulties on Nigerians but urged citizens not to allow their sacrifices to become meaningless.

He said Nigerians had demonstrated remarkable resilience despite rising economic pressures, uncertainty and other social challenges.

“Our people have endured enormous economic difficulties, uncertainty and hardship. They have carried a burden that demands national recognition and sincere appreciation,” he said.

Alawuje, however, maintained that the ultimate justification for the reforms would depend on their ability to improve the living conditions of ordinary Nigerians.

He urged the President to ensure that the foundations being laid through the reforms translated into tangible economic relief, employment opportunities, improved living standards, social justice and sustainable prosperity.

“The pains are real, the frustrations are understandable, and the sacrifices must never be dismissed or taken for granted,” he acknowledged.

“However, we must also recognise that the ultimate justification for every reform lies in its ability to deliver measurable improvements in the lives of the people.”

The Tinubu campaign council member also criticised calls by an opposition-associated presidential aspirant for the restoration of fuel subsidy, questioning the sustainability of such a proposal.

He argued that Nigerians deserved more than politically attractive promises, insisting that anyone advocating a return to subsidy must explain how the policy would be funded and how the country would avoid the economic difficulties associated with the previous arrangement.

“Is returning to fuel subsidy genuinely a sustainable economic solution, or is it another politically attractive promise designed to win public sympathy?” he queried.

Alawuje recalled that successive administrations had struggled with the fuel subsidy question because of the enormous financial burden it placed on the national economy and the political consequences associated with its removal.

He said Tinubu eventually took the decision to remove the subsidy, confronting a challenge that previous administrations had repeatedly struggled to resolve.

“My question is simple: If returning to fuel subsidy is the solution, how will it be funded, how will the country avoid the mistakes of the past, and what sustainable economic strategy accompanies that promise?” he asked.

He further cautioned politicians against making promises without explaining their economic implications.

“It is easy to promise immediate relief when one is not responsible for managing the consequences. But genuine leadership requires the courage to make difficult decisions, accept responsibility and provide sustainable solutions,” he stated.

He urged Nigerians to scrutinise the competence, consistency, courage and economic understanding of individuals seeking political power, rather than being swayed by attractive campaign promises.

While expressing support for Tinubu’s reform agenda, Alawuje stressed that the government must not take the patience and resilience of Nigerians for granted.

He called for a renewed commitment to national unity, accountability, fairness and people-centred governance, insisting that the sacrifices being made by citizens must produce meaningful results.

“Let us give the necessary reforms the opportunity to produce the desired results, while continuing to demand compassion, accountability, fairness and people-centred governance,” he appealed.

He also maintained that national progress should not be presented as the personal achievement of the President or any political organisation, but as a collective victory belonging to all Nigerians.

According to him, the success of the reform process must be dedicated to citizens who have endured the difficulties and continued to believe in the country’s future.

“This victory, when it comes, must belong to all Nigerians. It must not be celebrated as the personal achievement of President Tinubu or any political group,” he said.

Alawuje further urged Nigerians to move beyond political divisions and support efforts aimed at addressing the institutional failures and social injustices that have hindered national development for decades.

He also referenced remarks attributed to the Emir of Kano, noting that rebuilding a country whose economic foundations had suffered years of deterioration could not be expected to be an easy undertaking.

The Tinubu campaign council member expressed optimism about Nigeria’s future and called on citizens to remain resilient while demanding improved governance and better economic outcomes.

“Our pains must not be in vain. Our sacrifices must produce results. Our future must be better than our past,” he declared.

He prayed for divine guidance, wisdom and strength for President Tinubu to lead Nigeria towards economic recovery, national unity and lasting prosperity.
