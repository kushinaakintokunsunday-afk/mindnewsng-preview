Tems bags Grammy nomination for Best R&B Solo Performance with ‘What You Need’

By Vanguard Vanguard 16 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Vanguard

By Emmanuel Okogba

Nigerian singer Temilade Openiyi, popularly known as Tems, has secured a nomination for Best R&B Solo Performance at the 69th Annual Grammy Awards for her song, ‘What You Need’.

The singer will compete against Beyoncé, nominated for ‘Morning Dew (Donk)’; Kehlani, with ‘Out the Window’; Victoria Monét, nominated for ‘Let Me’; and Jill Scott, with ‘Pressha’.

‘What You Need’ was released on November 21, 2025, as a single from Tems’ extended play, Love Is a Kingdom.

The Recording Academy announced the nominees on Monday during a live YouTube programme, unveiling contenders across 100 categories ahead of the 2027 ceremony.

The 69th Grammy Awards are scheduled to take place on February 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California.

Tems remains one of Nigeria’s most celebrated Grammy-winning artistes. She won Best Melodic Rap Performance in 2023 for her contribution to Future’s ‘Wait for U’, featuring Drake, and Best African Music Performance in 2025 for her song ‘Love Me JeJe’.
