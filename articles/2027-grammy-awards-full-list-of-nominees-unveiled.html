The Recording Academy has announced the nominees for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards, paving the way for another major celebration of outstanding achievements in the global music industry.

The nominations were unveiled on Monday, November 16, 2026, recognising artistes, songwriters, producers and other music professionals whose works were released during the eligibility period.

This year’s edition will feature 100 award categories, including five newly introduced categories aimed at recognising more areas of musical creativity and production.

The 69th Grammy Awards ceremony is scheduled to take place on February 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, United States.
