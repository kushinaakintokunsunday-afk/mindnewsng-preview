Mbappe, Yamal, seven other footballers with €1bn release clauses

By Punch Punch 4d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Barcelona and Real Madrid have taken extraordinary measures to protect their most valuable players, reportedly inserting €1 billion release clauses into the con

The Spanish giants are using the enormous figures to discourage potential suitors, including wealthy Saudi Pro League clubs, from attempting to lure their prized assets away.

Under Spanish football regulations, professional players must have release clauses in their contracts.

However, by setting the figures at €1 billion, Barcelona and Real Madrid have effectively made it extremely difficult for rival clubs to sign their stars without negotiating directly with them.

Some of the biggest names in world football are reportedly covered by these eye-watering clauses.

Here are nine players whose contracts are said to include a €1 billion release clause, according to Planet Football on Thursday.

1. Kylian Mbappe (Real Madrid)

Kylian Mbappe’s long-awaited arrival at Real Madrid in 2024 was one of the biggest moments in modern football.

The French superstar joined Los Blancos as a free agent after leaving Paris Saint-Germain, signing a five-year contract running until 2029.

According to Spanish outlet COPE, his deal reportedly includes a €1 billion release clause.

Given his status as one of the world’s leading footballers, Real Madrid are unlikely to consider selling him anytime soon.

2. Lamine Yamal (Barcelona)

Barcelona wasted little time protecting their most exciting young talent after Lamine Yamal broke into the first team.

The Spanish winger signed his first professional contract in 2023, when he was just 16, with the club reportedly including a €1 billion release clause.

His remarkable development has since established him as one of the best young players in world football.

Barcelona subsequently secured his future with a new contract running until 2031, making it clear that they view him as central to their long-term project.

3. Vinicius Junior (Real Madrid)

Vinicius Junior is another Real Madrid superstar reportedly protected by a €1 billion release clause.

The Brazilian forward signed a contract extension in 2022 that included the enormous figure.

Since then, he has developed into one of the most dangerous attackers in world football, playing a key role in Real Madrid’s domestic and European successes.

Despite occasional speculation about his future and reported interest from Saudi Arabia, the Spanish giants remain determined to keep him at the Santiago Bernabeu.

4. Jude Bellingham (Real Madrid)

Real Madrid secured Jude Bellingham’s signature from Borussia Dortmund in 2023 in a deal worth approximately €100 million.

The England international made an immediate impact in Spain, quickly becoming one of the club’s most important players.

His combination of creativity, goalscoring ability and physical strength has made him a major asset in Madrid’s midfield.

Bellingham, whose contract runs until 2029, reportedly has a €1 billion release clause designed to discourage potential buyers.5. Pedri (Barcelona)

5. Pedri (Barcelona)
Pedri became the first Barcelona player to receive a reported €1 billion release clause when he signed a contract extension in October 2021.

The Spanish midfielder has established himself as one of the finest playmakers in world football, thanks to his intelligence, technical ability and exceptional passing.

After overcoming several injury setbacks, he returned to top form under Hansi Flick and played a crucial role in Barcelona’s 2024-25 campaign.

The club further demonstrated its commitment to him by extending his contract until 2030 in January 2025.

6. Gavi (Barcelona)

Gavi followed Pedri’s example by signing a long-term contract extension with Barcelona, reportedly featuring a €1 billion release clause.

The Spanish midfielder, who came through the club’s famous La Masia academy, has become an important part of Barcelona’s future plans.

Although injuries and competition for places have affected his regular involvement, his energy, determination and technical quality make him a valuable member of the squad.

His contract runs until 2030.

7. Eduardo Camavinga (Real Madrid)

Eduardo Camavinga joined Real Madrid from Rennes in 2021 as one of Europe’s most promising young midfielders.

His versatility has made him an important option for the Spanish giants, with the Frenchman capable of playing in several midfield positions and even at left-back.

Following his impressive development, Madrid rewarded him with an improved contract in 2023, reportedly featuring a €1 billion release clause.

Although he still has areas of his game to develop, the club clearly sees him as an important part of its future.

8. Jules Kounde (Barcelona)

Jules Kounde joined Barcelona from Sevilla in 2022 and has since established himself as an important figure in the team’s defence.

The French international’s versatility, defensive discipline and ability to contribute going forward have made him a valuable player for the Catalan side.

Barcelona reportedly included a €1 billion release clause in his contract, which runs until 2030.

With several Premier League clubs having been linked with him, the enormous figure puts Barcelona in a strong negotiating position.

9. Rodrygo (Real Madrid)

Rodrygo completes the list of Real Madrid players reportedly protected by a €1 billion release clause.

The Brazilian forward signed a contract extension in 2023, keeping him at the club until 2028.

Since arriving from Santos, he has delivered several important performances, particularly in the Champions League, and developed into a valuable attacking option.

Despite occasional transfer speculation and competition for places in Madrid’s attack, the club has continued to value his contribution.

His reported €1 billion release clause makes any potential move away from the Bernabeu extremely difficult.
