Jigawa ADC reaffirms Sani Gumel’s leadership, refutes rumours of false leadership change

The Jigawa State chapter of the African Democratic Congress (ADC) has urged its members, candidates and supporters to disregard rumours suggesting a change in the party’s leadership in the state.

The party reaffirmed its support for the existing leadership structure under State Chairman, Sani MuhdGumel, declaring him the authentic leader of the ADC in Jigawa State.

Our correspondent gathered that the clarification comes amid growing political activities and realignments ahead of the 2027 general elections, as political parties intensify efforts to strengthen their structures across the state.

The party’s statement is aimed at addressing uncertainty among members over its leadership and maintaining unity within its ranks ahead of the forthcoming elections.

It would be recalled that recently, a faction of the party led by MuhammadAli Gantsa claimed that there had been a change in the party’s interim leadership in the state.

The development caused confusion among party members, officials and supporters across the 27 local government areas of the state.

However, to clear the air, a special stakeholders’ meeting was held on Sunday in Dutse, the Jigawa State capital.

The meeting was led by the party’s governorship candidate, Senator Sabo Nakudu, and had in attendance three senatorial candidates, 11 House of Representatives candidates and 31 State House of Assembly candidates.

Other party leaders from across the 27 LGAs of the state also attended the meeting, where far-reaching decisions were taken.

In his opening remarks at the meeting, the State Chairman of the party, MuhdGumel, declared that whoever was not with the existing leadership and structure was not a member of the party.

“Whoever is not with this leadership and its existing structure is not with the party. They are sycophants working for the APC,” Gumel said.

He said the national leadership of the party, under its Chairman, David Mark, and Secretary, Rauf Aregbesola, had debunked the rumour of leadership change across states.

MuhdGumel urged candidates, supporters and well-wishers to remain calm and disregard the purported rumour.

He also tasked candidates with continuing all campaign activities as approved and designed by the state and national leadership of the party under the chairmanship of Mark and Gumel in Jigawa.

Speaking at the event, the party’s governorship candidate, Senator Sabo Nakudu, said no amount of intimidation would shake the party’s campaign activities.

Nakudu said the party was focused on its planned success in the forthcoming 2027 general elections, despite alleged pressure from the ruling party.

“We are not intimidated by the ruling party and the internal sycophants working for the APC. No amount of intimidation will shake our campaigns,” Nakudu said.

He vowed that the party would continue to go round the nooks and crannies of the state to reach out to voters.

According to him, the ADC was on a mission to emancipate the country from the current hardship facing Nigerians.

“The party is not fighting anyone but the enemies of the country and its citizens. We will restore hope when we take over leadership,” Nakudu added.

During the meeting, party officials and candidates for the 2027 elections reaffirmed their loyalty to the existing leadership under Sani Muhd Gumel and the governorship candidate, Sabo Nakudu.

Speaking further about the meeting to a Very Nigerian correspondent shortly afterwards, the Deputy State Chairman of the party, Nuraddeen Jidawa, said, “The meeting tasked all members, officials and supporters on unity and synergy for the success of the party.”

According to Jidawa, members were also urged to be watchful of any sabotage or manoeuvres aimed at hindering the party’s success by the ruling party.

“They were asked to blow the whistle and challenge any possible sabotage,” he stated.

He further appealed to members of the Jigawa ADC and those across the country to continue to cooperate with the leadership and make sacrifices for the party’s targeted success.
