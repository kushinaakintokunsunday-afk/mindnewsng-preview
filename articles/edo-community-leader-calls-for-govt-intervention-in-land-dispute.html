Edo community leader calls for govt intervention in land dispute

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

THE Ohen Obao of Aduwawa in Ikpoba-Okha local government area of Edo State, Chief Priest Nosakhare Odumore, has appealed to the Oba of Benin, the governor of th

 He said the disputed land is between two businessmen in the state who have contributed immensely to the development of the community.

In his statement made available to SOUTH SOUTH PUNCH on Tuesday, Odumore said he had invited the parties for dialogue and amicable resolution of the issue but that one of the parties has failed to show up for meetings.

“The reason  for issuing a press statement is not to promote division, take sides or undermine anybody’s legitimate interest, but to make the public aware of the efforts I have made to resolve a lingering land dispute and prevent a situation that could threaten the peace, security and unity of our community.

“The land in question has become a subject of dispute between two individuals. One is an Igbo businessman who has, for many years, claimed ownership of the property and used it as a parking space for his hotel.

“The other claimant is a Benin-based businessman and owner who runs a transport company and recently come forward to lay claim to the same property.

“In my determination to prevent this disagreement from escalating, I initiated efforts to bring both parties before the traditional authority so that each could present his claims, provide relevant information and be given a fair opportunity to be heard.

“I extended several invitations through the appropriate traditional channels, expecting both parties to honour the process and allow us to resolve the matter amicably.

“However, I wish to express my disappointment that while the Igbo businessman has consistently honoured the invitations and appeared before me, the other person has failed to appear despite repeated efforts to secure his attendance.

“So, I am using this opportunity to appeal to the Oba of Benin, the governor of the state, Monday Okpebholo, and security agencies to intervene in the lingering land dispute in the community and I am  that the situation can threaten public peace.

While declaring that he has not ruled on the matter  in favour of any of the parties, Odumore said he has barricaded the disputed land with the traditional palm frond and gave a fresh seven days for the parties to appear before him.
