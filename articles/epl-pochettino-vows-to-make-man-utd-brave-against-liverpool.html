EPL: Pochettino vows to make Man Utd ‘brave’ against Liverpool

By Punch Punch 5d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

New Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino has promised to make the club competitive again, insisting his side will approach Sunday’s Premier League c

Pochettino made the promise in his first interview after taking charge of the Old Trafford club on Wednesday, saying he was eager to begin work despite arriving just hours after leaving his position as manager of the United States national team.

In the video interview posted on the club’s YouTube page on Thursday, the Argentine described his appointment as a privilege and a huge responsibility, adding that he was determined to prepare the team for the difficult trip to Anfield.

“It’s an opportunity, and it’s a privilege to be head coach of Manchester United, and I think we cannot waste time. We need to be ready to compete on Sunday,” he said.

He added, “We need to be ready and prepare everything to be sure that we are going to compete in the best condition at Anfield.”

Pochettino acknowledged the challenge of taking over at United but said his immediate priority was to create an environment where players could express themselves and perform without being overwhelmed by pressure.

“We need to see the potential of this club. Of course, circumstances are circumstances, and we need to be responsible for the circumstances.

“But at the same time, we need to understand that it’s a game. The players need to express their talent, and we need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform,” he said.

The former Tottenham Hotspur and Chelsea manager said he wanted to establish a strong team culture built around unity, trust and shared responsibility.

“The culture, to create the culture, is the most important. To have the culture, create the habits and the principles. We need to look forward and find a way to create the best habits and the culture that makes us close to winning every single game,” he said.

He also called for unity among the players, coaching staff, club management and supporters, saying everyone must work together to restore the club’s winning mentality.

“We need to be united because, in the end, we are Manchester United. We need to create our own philosophy and have genuine and very natural leadership,” Pochettino said.

On his assessment of the squad, the 54-year-old said he was satisfied with the players available and would focus on helping them fulfil their potential.

“We need to love the players that we have. We need to support the players and provide all the tools for their performance,” he said.

Turning to Sunday’s meeting with Liverpool, Pochettino acknowledged the significance of the fixture but insisted United would travel to Anfield with the ambition of securing victory.

“I know very well what that game means and how important it is. I think we know very well that it’s going to be a tough game, but at the same time, we believe in our way.

“We are going to go there, to Anfield, with the idea to win, be brave and be Manchester United,” he said.

He added that his players must approach the encounter with confidence and without fear, despite facing a strong Liverpool side.

“We need to make sure that we are brave and we are protagonists, and go there with no fear and challenge a very good team like Liverpool,” he said.

Pochettino also recalled his admiration for United, dating back to the club’s dramatic 2-1 victory over Bayern Munich in the 1999 Champions League final in Barcelona.

He said he watched the match while playing for Espanyol and was impressed by the team’s performance and late comeback.

“That was an amazing game, and then what happened in the end, it’s impossible not to emphasise that team,” he said.

The coach also spoke of his admiration for former United manager Sir Alex Ferguson, whom he described as the best coach in the history of the sport.

Pochettino said he wanted to help supporters regain the feeling of watching a team capable of winning consistently, adding that the responsibility for delivering that ambition rested with him and his players.

“We are here to try to do everything to recover that feeling about winning and be in a place that you deserve to be,” he said.
