Pérez renews call for probe into Barcelona’s payments to ex-refereeing chief

By Punch Punch 3d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Real Madrid president Florentino Pérez has vowed not to stop pursuing accountability in the Negreira case, accusing LaLiga and the Spanish Football Federation o

Pérez also disclosed that Real Madrid had reported LaLiga president Javier Tebas to Spain’s Higher Sports Council over his remarks comparing the club’s communication to the propaganda machinery of Nazi Germany.

He made the remarks on Saturday during the opening address at Real Madrid’s 2026 Ordinary General Assembly of Delegate Members, according to a statement published on the club’s official website.

The Negreira case concerns payments made by Barcelona to José María Enríquez Negreira, former vice-president of the Spanish Football Federation’s Technical Committee of Referees.

In 2023, Spanish prosecutors charged Barcelona with corruption over the payments.

Two of the club’s former presidents, Josep Maria Bartomeu and Sandro Rosell are also facing the same charge.

Again on Saturday, Pérez described the matter as extraordinarily serious, alleging that Negreira received more than €8m from Barcelona over nearly two decades.

“What has happened is extraordinarily serious. The number two in Spanish refereeing, the vice president of the Technical Committee of Referees, received, over nearly two decades (as far as we know), more than 8 million euros from a club we all know.

“We are talking about payments to a key figure in the refereeing system: among other things, he was the one who informed referees about their promotions and demotions.”

The Real Madrid president also cited a statement Negreira allegedly made to tax authorities concerning the payments.

He stated, “I remind you of what Negreira himself declared to the tax authorities. He literally said the following: ‘What Football Club Barcelona wanted was to ensure that no decisions were made against them, that everything was neutral.’ Those were his words, not mine, and they are recorded in the judicial proceedings.”

Pérez said Real Madrid had submitted documentation and evidence to UEFA, adding that the European football governing body confirmed on October 1 that its ethics and disciplinary inspectors were examining the materials.

“It had to be Real Madrid, one of the aggrieved parties, that gathered all the information and filed the complaint with UEFA. We have provided extensive documentation and evidence about events of extraordinary seriousness, and on October 1, UEFA confirmed that their ethics and disciplinary inspectors are analyzing all that documentation.

“Our request is very clear: that it be rigorously investigated, that action be taken with the utmost urgency, and that justice be served. That the investigation reaches its conclusion and the consequences of all of this are addressed,” the president said.

He criticised LaLiga and the Royal Spanish Football Federation for what he described as their failure to pursue the matter, arguing that the two bodies had not taken the necessary steps to ensure the case was investigated by UEFA.

He noted, “LaLiga remains in absolute inaction, claiming it lacks authority. It is true that it joined the criminal case, but joining is not investigating: we have seen how it did not even ask questions during the judicial process interrogations, as if it were not present.

“In the face of such behavior, there are only two possible explanations: either it wants to hide that it had information and did nothing, or it is simply not fulfilling its responsibility. And neither of these is acceptable.”

The dispute has also deepened tensions between Real Madrid and the Spanish refereeing authorities.

Pérez alleged that the club had become a target of hostility since the case became public, while questioning the propriety of referees who had filed complaints against Real Madrid subsequently officiating its matches.

He said, “And on top of that, we have to endure a group of these referees filing complaints against Real Madrid. In other words, they file complaints against us and then are assigned to referee our team’s matches in La Liga. Can you imagine being judged in a court by a judge who has filed a complaint against you? Well, this is what is happening in our competition today.”

Pérez also criticised Tebas over a social media comparison involving the club and Joseph Goebbels, Nazi Germany’s propaganda minister.

“On October 16th, Mr. Tebas crossed all boundaries when he compared Real Madrid’s communication to the propaganda mechanisms exploited by Goebbels, the Minister of Propaganda for Nazi Germany.

“The propagandist of a regime that brought the world its greatest tragedy with over 6 million crimes,” the Real Madrid president declared.

He said Real Madrid had demanded Tebas’s immediate resignation and confirmed that the club had reported him to the Higher Sports Council.

“For this reason, Real Madrid demanded his immediate resignation, and I confirm to you that we have reported Mr. Tebas to the Higher Sports Council for these statements,” he included.

Pérez added that the council had independently asked the Administrative Court for Sport to examine whether Tebas’s remarks constituted a serious infraction.

He maintained that the courts and UEFA should determine the outcome of the Negreira case, insisting that his responsibility as Real Madrid president was to ensure that the allegations were thoroughly investigated.

“I want to be very precise: it is not my place to judge anyone. That will be done by the courts and UEFA, and I respect the timelines and procedures of justice.

“But it is my responsibility, as president of Real Madrid, to demand that these events be investigated thoroughly, as they directly affect the credibility of our competition.

“And I say to you very clearly that I will not stop until all responsibilities are clarified,” he concluded.
