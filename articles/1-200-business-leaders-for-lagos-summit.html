1,200 business leaders for Lagos summit

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Lagos is set to host approximately 1,200 business leaders, policymakers, diplomats, investors and academics at the 14th edition of the GAH Awards & Business Sum

The summit, themed “The New Africa Economy: Vision, Capital & Influence,” will bring together influential voices from across Africa and beyond to discuss investment, business growth, economic development and the continent’s position in the global economy.

Former United Nations Under-Secretary-General and Executive Secretary of the UN Economic Commission for Africa, Dr. Vera Songwe, will deliver a keynote address. The Consul General of the United Arab Emirates in Lagos, His Excellency Salem Aljaberi, will attend as the Special Guest.

Other confirmed keynote speakers include investment banker and Group Chief Executive Officer of Emerging Africa Group, Dr. Oluwatoyin F. Sanni; Professor Lere Baale, Director at Business School Netherlands Nigeria; Adebola Williams, Co-Founder and CEO of RED; Professor Chris Ogbechie of Lagos Business School; and Professor Ahmadou Aly Mbaye of Cheikh Anta Diop University, Dakar.

Founder and Executive Chair of the GAH Awards & Business Summit, Dr. Princess Oghene, said the gathering would provide an important platform for investors, governments and entrepreneurs to examine practical solutions to Africa’s economic challenges.

“A business owner deciding whether to open a second factory needs to know whether she can finance it, whether she can reach customers across borders and whether the rules will hold long enough for her to invest,” Oghene said.

“Those are decisions that investors, governments and entrepreneurs often discuss in separate rooms. We are bringing them together in Lagos to examine what each can do to make the next investment possible.”

According to the organisers, discussions will focus on financing African businesses, developing infrastructure, expanding regional trade and strengthening the continent’s influence in international investment decisions.

The programme will also examine opportunities arising from the African Continental Free Trade Area and the African Union’s Agenda 2063, particularly in mobilising long-term capital for industrialisation, enterprise development and job creation.

The featured speakers and panellists will include retired Commissioner of Police Fatai Owoseni, Pharm. Chidinma Odu-Obi, Professor Olu Ogunsakin, Dr. Chinyere Almona, Director-General of the Lagos Chamber of Commerce and Industry, and Hon. Joshua Audu Gana. Dr. Joseph Ogebe will serve as moderator.

The 14th edition builds on the summit’s record of bringing together prominent figures from government, diplomacy, business and traditional institutions.

The 13th edition, held in December 2025 under the theme “Africa Reimagined: Innovation, Inclusion & Sustainable Growth,” attracted more than 1,000 guests to Eko Hotels & Suites.

His Imperial Majesty, Oba Adeyeye Enitan Ogunwusi, CFR, Ajájá II, the Ooni of Ife, served as Grand Patron and Father of the Day, while Dr. Babatope Agbeyo, Group Managing Director of Cornfield Group, served as Chairman.

Other notable participants included Zamfara State Governor, Dr. Dauda Lawal; Minister of Aviation and Aerospace Development, Festus Keyamo, SAN, CON; Chairman and CEO of Air Peace, Dr. Allen Ifechukwu Onyema; and Chairman and CEO of United Nigeria Airlines, Professor Obiora Gregory Okonkwo.

Founded in 2012, the GAH Awards & Business Summit reports that its first 12 editions recognised more than 350 distinguished honourees and attracted over 20,000 cumulative guests and delegates, with more than N700 million in media visibility and partnership value.
