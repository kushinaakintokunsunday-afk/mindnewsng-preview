PHOTOS: Mayweather meets Sanwo-Olu, plans boxing academies in Nigeria

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

American boxing legend Floyd Mayweather Jr. paid a courtesy visit to Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu at Lagos House, Marina, on Sunday as part of his tr

Mayweather, the undefeated former five-division world champion, is in the country to introduce the TMT Frontrow Fight Series.

The programme aims to discover, train and promote young Nigerian boxers through academies and development schemes planned for Lagos, Abuja, Enugu, Calabar and other cities.

The first event is scheduled for 2027, with Mayweather also expected to take part in a boxing contest in Nigeria that year.

Sanwo-Olu welcomed the boxing icon and expressed the state’s readiness to partner on the project.

In a post obtained from Sanwo-Olu’s X handle on Monday, the governor was quoted as saying, “It was a pleasure to receive American professional boxing champion Floyd Mayweather Jr. at Lagos House, Marina.

“His vision of inspiring grassroots boxing talent across Africa, starting here in Nigeria, and bringing international boxing events to Lagos presents opportunities for our young people and the sports industry.

“Lagos is ready to welcome major sporting events. We have continued to invest in sports infrastructure and we are focused on creating an environment where talent can grow and succeed.

“We look forward to exploring these opportunities and strengthening Lagos’s place as a leading destination for sports and entertainment in Africa.”

Earlier, Mayweather also met the Director-General of the Lagos State Sports Commission, Lekan Fatodu, who described Lagos as an ideal location for boxing events and praised the state’s sports infrastructure under Sanwo-Olu’s administration.

Mayweather’s visit comes ahead of his scheduled exhibition bout against Australian boxer Matt “The Jedi” Floyd in Bangkok, Thailand, on December 19, 2026.

The engagement has generated excitement among Nigerian boxing fans, who see the involvement of one of the sport’s biggest global stars as a boost for grassroots talent and the potential to host more international boxing events in Lagos.

PUNCH Online reported that Mayweather was set to launch a boxing development initiative in Nigeria to discover, train, and promote emerging talent.
