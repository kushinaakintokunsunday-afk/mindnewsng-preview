2027: Armed Men Who Killed Four NDC Campaign Members Said I Was Their Target, Omo-Agege

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 3 min read

Photo: Channels TV

Former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege has alleged that the gunmen who attacked his campaign team in Delta State, killing four people, repeatedly shouted his name and declared their intention to kill him.

Omo-Agege, who is seeking election to the Delta Central Senatorial District on the platform of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) in the 2027 general elections, said he escaped the attack through the intervention of his Department of State Services (DSS) escorts.

He spoke on Monday while appearing as a guest on Channels Television’s Politics Today.

Recounting the incident, the former deputy senate president said he heard the attackers repeatedly shouting that they wanted to kill him while opening fire at the campaign venue.

“Right there at the venue, while they were shooting, we could hear them say, ‘It is Omo-Agege we want to kill. We must kill Omo-Agege.’

“But I was lucky because my DSS details were very professional. In fact, after God, the only reason I am alive today is because of the DSS operatives who were with me,” he said.

Omo-Agege described the incident as a premeditated attack, alleging that there had been a build-up to the violence.

He questioned why he would be singled out for elimination when several other politicians were also contesting elective positions.

The former lawmaker said the attackers struck less than five minutes after he began addressing supporters at the campaign venue.

According to him, he had moved towards the side of his vehicle to make eye contact with members of the audience when the gunshots rang out.

“It was by divine intervention. As I was speaking, I just moved over to the side of the vehicle to be able to make eye contact with those I was speaking with. It was immediately I did that that the volley of gunshots started,” he said.

The former deputy senate president said four members of his campaign organisation were killed, while about 15 others sustained injuries, with some in critical condition.

In a statement issued earlier on Monday by his media adviser, Sunny Areh, Omo-Agege’s campaign organisation said he survived an assassination attempt during a campaign tour in Uduere, Ughelli North Local Government Area of Delta State.

Areh alleged that armed men opened fire on the senator and his entourage while he was addressing supporters during a campaign visit.

The organisation called for an independent and thorough investigation by the Nigeria Police Force, while appealing to the Inspector-General of Police, the DSS, INEC, civil society organisations and the international community to ensure the safety of political candidates and their supporters.

It also urged supporters to remain calm and avoid retaliation, adding that its ward-to-ward campaign activities across Delta Central would continue.
