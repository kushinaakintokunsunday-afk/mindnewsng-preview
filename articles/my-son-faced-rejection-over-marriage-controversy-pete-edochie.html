My son faced rejection over marriage controversy – Pete Edochie

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Veteran Nollywood actor, Pete Edochie, has said his son, Yul Edochie, faced public and professional rejection following the controversy surrounding his second m

Pete made this known in an interview with Ugo Nwokolo TV, an excerpt of which was shared on Sunday.

The full episode is expected to be released on Wednesday.

Speaking about the criticism Yul has faced since announcing his relationship with actress Judy Austin, Pete described his son as a strong person who had remained composed despite the backlash.

“Look at my son, Yul. Yul is a very tough guy. You can never subdue him, no matter how much you try,” he said.

The veteran actor said he respected Yul for not publicly speaking ill of his first wife, May, despite the controversy surrounding their marriage.

“He has never insulted his wife for one day. Go and check everything that has been happening. He has never come out to say, this woman did this, she did this. He has never done that,” Pete said.

He also claimed that the controversy had affected how some people in the entertainment industry received his son, alleging that award organisers repeatedly left him out.

“There was a time that anything he touched, people felt it was all poison. Nobody wanted to go close to it. Social media talks about him and his wife every day,” he said.

“Each time awards are organised, they don’t include him. He hasn’t bothered. He is doing very, very, very, very well.”

Yul’s marital controversy became public in April 2022 when he announced that he had welcomed a son with actress Judy Austin and introduced her as his second wife.

At the time, he was married to May, with whom he has four children. May subsequently made it clear that she did not accept a polygamous marriage, saying it was against her beliefs, faith and values.

In December 2022, Yul publicly apologised to May over his decision to marry another woman, acknowledging that he had hurt her and taking responsibility for his actions.

However, the disagreement between the couple continued to play out publicly. In January 2024, Yul cautioned May against what he described as attempts to manipulate public opinion against him, accusing her of encouraging people to hate him. He also claimed that he had remained silent for a long time to protect her image.

May later filed for divorce in August 2023, following the breakdown of their marriage. The dispute has remained a subject of public discussion, with the actor’s personal life frequently attracting reactions on social media.

Pete’s comments offer his perspective on the public and professional consequences Yul has faced, while also drawing attention to his claim that his son has refrained from publicly attacking May.
