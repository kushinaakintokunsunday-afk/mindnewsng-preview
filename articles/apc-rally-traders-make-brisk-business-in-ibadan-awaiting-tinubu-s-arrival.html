APC rally: Traders make brisk business in Ibadan, awaiting Tinubu’s arrival

By Vanguard Vanguard 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Vanguard

Traders, on Saturday, ahead of the arrival of President Bola Tinubu for the All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign at the historic Mapo Hall in I

Items which were majorly on sale were customised APC caps and T-shirts with different inscriptions, as well as sachet/table water, soft drinks and snacks.

The News Agency of Nigeria (NAN) reports that the venue of the rally and its neighbourhood, such as Beere, Oja-Oba and Idi-Arere, were filled by thousands of APC members and supporters who came to hear from the number one citizen of Nigeria.

Sellers of brooms, the symbol of the APC, also had a good business day, as did vendors selling kolanut and bitter kola.

Some of the traders who spoke with NAN expressed satisfaction with the sales already recorded even before the start of the programme and arrival of President Bola Tinubu.

Mrs Janet Yewande, a food vendor near the venue, said she hired an additional six people to cope with the high volume of people waiting to buy from her shop.

She said that the high influx of customers had not made her increase the price or reduce the quantity of food.

On her part, Miss Rasheedat Bolade said the extra sales being recorded in sachet water would go a long way in settling some pending bills and her school fees.

Bolade said her mother had earlier bought enough bags of sachet water in preparation for the business of the day, adding that this had been her usual practice and means of survival whenever a big event was to be held at the ancient Mapo Hall.

Also, Mr Akeem Ogunwale, who sold customised caps, also said that he had made the necessary preparations by printing more caps and T-shirts in anticipation of high sales.

Ogunwale said that he had sold not less than 60 customised caps and 12 T-shirts at the campaign ground.

A soft drink seller, Mrs Mercy Kingsley, said she had already sold 10 crates of soft drinks, noting that though many people preferred cold drinks due to the high intensity of the sun, they still went ahead to buy the ordinary ones due to high demand.

A attendee at the campaign rally, Mr Kolade Ayinde, said the long hours in the sun compelled him to drink up to three sachets of water, adding that many supporters did not mind the intensity of the sun due to their love for the president.

Ayinde said the presence of the traders was normal, as it met the needs of the party faithful, especially given the intensity of the sun.

NAN reports that the mammoth crowd in and around Mapo Hall was still awaiting the arrival of the president and his team as of the time of filing this report. (NAN)
