My best is yet to come – Bruno Guimaraes

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Arsenal midfielder Bruno Guimaraes says he is yet to reach his best despite making a successful start to life at the Premier League club. The Brazilian stated t

The Brazilian stated this on Monday ahead of Arsenal’s UEFA Champions League clash against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday.

This was contained in a release posted on the club’s website.

Guimaraes, who joined Arsenal from Newcastle United in the summer, said he was still adapting to the demands of manager Mikel Arteta and believed he could improve his performances.

“Definitely I’m building up,” he told the media on Monday. “Of course because I scored two goals, I think some fans want me to score every game, but it’s not like this, I’m definitely adapting myself.

“I’ve been here three or four months, and there’s still a lot to go. But now I definitely understand my role in the team, the way Mikel wants me to play in the team, so I think I still can play much, much better.

“The welcome has been amazing, I think [our supporters] have been so good with me, I want to thank them for their support.”

The midfielder said Arsenal’s system required him to operate in different positions, adding that he was gradually becoming accustomed to the tactical demands.

Guimaraes declared, “It’s very different, here is a little bit more free. You play as a six, an eight, and a nine sometimes, so we have to move a lot, and of course I was not used to it.

“Now I understand a little bit more what [Mikel Arteta] wants from the midfield. I understand Declan because it’s a little hard, but yeah, I think now I’m getting there. I’m still not at the level that I want, but I feel I can play much more and help the team.”

Guimaraes also highlighted the competition for places in Arsenal’s midfield, saying it pushed players to maintain high standards.

“We push each other to always be playing well, because if you don’t play well, other players will come and play well; I think that’s very important for Arsenal. We want to make a dynasty here, we want to win everything possible,” he said.

He added that Arteta had made the ambition clear to him when he joined the club.

The Brazilian stated, “I think since I came to the club, the first thing Mikel said to me was create a dynasty for the club, so we want to win everything that’s possible.”

Ahead of the Dortmund encounter, the midfielder acknowledged the quality of the German side and the importance of the fixture.

“I know how much the Champions League means for the fans, and for us here. Tomorrow we have a big test with a top team, they have been unbelievable this season, so tomorrow we have to play a very good game because it’s going to be against a top side,” he said.

The Brazilian also praised his midfield teammates Declan Rice and Martin Odegaard, saying he was learning from both players.

He said, “They’re both amazing players. I think Declan is a monster, he can run 15 kilometres in a game, and Martin Odegaard, he always can find space to penetrate the opposite side.

“I’m learning a lot with the guys, they’re trying to help me where I have to position myself when we don’t have the ball, when we do have the ball, and like I said, it’s about adaptation.”

Guimaraes said he had been impressed by the team’s mentality and its determination to compete for victories.

“But I think so far it has been good. The mentality is unbelievable, we don’t play for a draw here, we play for a win every game, that’s the level,” he said.

“It’s what Mikel demands for us and his staff every day, so the mentality is very good. I think we are creating something big here, and I hope at the end of the season we can celebrate together,” he concluded.
