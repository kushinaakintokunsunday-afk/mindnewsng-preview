'Tinubu Didn't Call Me For 56 Days' - Makinde Breaks Silence On Oriire Schoolchildren Abduction - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Daily Post Nigeria 13 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Daily Post Nigeria

Governor of Oyo State and APM presidential candidate, Seyi Makinde, has revealed that President Bola Tinubu did not call him throughout the 56 days that schoolchildren and teachers abducted in Oriire, Oyo State, spent in captivity.

Makinde made the revelation on Friday during an interview on Arise Television, saying he expected the President to call him after the abduction but the call never came.

"I tried to see President Tinubu when the Oriire schoolchildren and teachers were abducted in Oyo State, but President Tinubu didn't call me during their 56-day captivity. I expected him to call me," he said.

According to the governor, he also attempted to reach the State House through the Chief of Protocol, but his calls were not picked up.

He said the silence from the Presidency during the period the victims were in the hands of their abductors was painful, especially for a governor who said he was working day and night to secure their release.

The Oriire abduction threw Oyo State into panic, with parents, teachers and residents calling on the federal and state governments to act fast to bring back the victims. After 56 days in captivity, the schoolchildren and teachers were eventually released.

Makinde's account reflects his own statements in the interview; the Presidency has not publicly responded to the claim at the time of this report. Mindnewsng presents both sides and will update as reactions emerge.
