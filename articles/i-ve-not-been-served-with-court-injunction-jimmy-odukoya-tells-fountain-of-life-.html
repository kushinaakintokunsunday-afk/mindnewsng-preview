I’ve Not Been Served With Court Injunction, Jimmy Odukoya Tells Fountain Of Life Members

The Senior Pastor of the Fountain of Life Church, Jimmy Odukoya, has said he has not been formally served with the injunction reportedly restraining him from presenting himself as the church’s Senior Pastor.

Odukoya made this known on Sunday while addressing members of the church during a service, amid an escalating leadership dispute between him and members of the church’s Board of Trustees.

The pastor said although he had heard about the court order, he had neither seen it nor received formal service of the court process.

“I heard there was an injunction against me. I heard, but I have not seen it, and I have not been served. They said that there was no substituted service. Legally, I have not been served,” he said.

Addressing the worshippers, he acknowledged their continued support amid the controversy and emphasised their importance to the church.

“You know there is no Fountain without you. You are Fountain of Life, and as long as you would have me, I will continue to speak for you. I will continue to stand for you,” he said.

Osukoya was at the centre of a leadership crisis at the church following an interim order reportedly granted by the Lagos State High Court on November 18, following an application by the Incorporated Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church.

The court, presided over by Justice O.O. Adewunmi-Oshin, granted the trustees permission to commence legal proceedings against Odukoya under the pre-action protocol of the High Court of Lagos State.

The application also sought to restrain him from presenting himself as the church’s Senior Pastor and Spiritual Head, as well as interfering with its administration, governance and activities.

The order further restrained him from disrupting the church’s affairs, either personally or through representatives, pending compliance with the court’s pre-action requirements.

The court also directed that the originating processes and accompanying documents be served on Odukoya, while preserving his right to challenge the order.

Leadership Tussle

The dispute followed the November 17 announcement by a faction of the church’s Board of Trustees withdrawing recognition of Odukoya as Senior Pastor and appointing Pastor Rotimi Okpaise to oversee its interim spiritual and administrative leadership.

The trustees cited a December 2021 video recording in which the church’s late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, reportedly identified Pastor Kunle Areogun as his preferred successor.

They also raised concerns about the circumstances surrounding Jimmy Odukoya’s appointment in September 2023, alleging that relevant information about the succession arrangement was not disclosed to the board at the time.

However, Odukoya rejected the purported removal, describing it as unconstitutional and insisting that his appointment remained valid.

In a counter-statement, his camp argued that the trustees behind the decision had themselves been asked to resign in March 2026 over alleged violations of the church’s constitution.

Despite the legal dispute, Odukoya reaffirmed his commitment to the congregation, assuring members that he would continue to stand with them and speak on their behalf.
