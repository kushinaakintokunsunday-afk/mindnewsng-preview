Senate committee hails Air Peace over ₦80,000 military fare

By Vanguard Vanguard 1d ago 3 min read

By Henry Umoru

ABUJA — The Senate Committee on Army has commended Air Peace and its Chairman, Dr Allen Onyema, for introducing a discounted one-way domestic air fare of ₦80,000 for serving and retired personnel of the Nigerian Armed Forces.

The committee also urged other airlines and corporate organisations in the country to emulate the gesture as part of efforts to improve the welfare of military personnel and veterans.

The commendation was contained in a statement issued on Sunday by the Chairman of the committee, Senator Abdulaziz Musa Yar’Adua, representing Katsina Central Senatorial District.

According to the statement, the initiative was unveiled on November 16, 2026, during the 2026 Chief of Defence Intelligence Annual Conference in Abuja.

The committee said the discounted fare was available to eligible military personnel who had served in the Nigerian Armed Forces since 1960, across all domestic routes.

It added that Air Peace would also bear the cost of tickets for regional and international flights, with beneficiaries required to pay only applicable taxes and charges.

Describing the initiative as a patriotic gesture, the committee said it was a practical demonstration of appreciation for the sacrifices made by serving and retired military personnel in defending the country’s sovereignty and territorial integrity.

It noted that members of the Armed Forces had continued to make enormous sacrifices, often at great personal cost, and deserved tangible support that would improve their welfare and that of their families.

“By reducing the burden of air travel, Air Peace has demonstrated that patriotism is not only spoken in times of ceremony, but shown in practical action that touches the lives of those who serve,” the statement said.

The committee particularly praised Onyema for what it described as his consistent commitment to humanitarian causes, national development and support for the Nigerian military.

It also endorsed the airline chairman’s call for the military leadership to establish a robust verification system to ensure that only genuine beneficiaries access the concession.

According to the committee, such a mechanism would help make the initiative sustainable, transparent and free from abuse.

It appealed to other airlines and corporate organisations to support the Armed Forces, describing the welfare of serving personnel and veterans as a collective national responsibility.

“The private sector has a critical role to play in complementing the efforts of the Federal Government in improving the welfare and morale of our troops and veterans,” it said.

The Senate committee assured Air Peace of its support and readiness to work with the airline, the Ministry of Defence and the Service Chiefs to ensure the smooth implementation of the initiative.

It reiterated its appreciation to the airline’s management for the gesture, describing it as a meaningful tribute to Nigeria’s military personnel.
