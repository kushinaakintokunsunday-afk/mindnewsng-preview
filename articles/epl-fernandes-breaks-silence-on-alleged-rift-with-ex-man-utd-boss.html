EPL: Fernandes breaks silence on alleged rift with ex-Man Utd boss

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has dismissed reports of a breakdown in his relationship with former manager Michael Carrick, insisting that his comments after their 4-1 Champions League defeat to Como were not intended as a criticism of his former boss.

Fernandes said there was no disagreement between them, explaining that Carrick understood his concerns and did not take offence at his post-match remarks.

The Portuguese midfielder made the clarification in an interview with Sky Sports on Sunday, days after his comments sparked speculation about tension between the pair.

PUNCH had earlier reported that Fernandes and Carrick appeared to disagree over Manchester United’s tactical approach following the defeat to Como, which intensified scrutiny of the former manager.

Speaking to TNT Sports after the match, Fernandes questioned the decision not to press Como aggressively, arguing that allowing the Italian side too much possession had helped them gain confidence and control the game.

“It’s a decision from the staff to not press man to man and sometimes leave space behind and try to get the ball, but sometimes when you give too much of the ball to these teams, they get confidence,” he said.

The comments came after Fernandes was left out of the starting line-up for the first Premier League game of the season, having last started a match from the bench on May 4, 2025, in a 4-3 defeat to Brentford.

He was subsequently omitted from the squad for the Premier League encounter against Bournemouth, but insisted the decision had nothing to do with his comments about the Como defeat.

Fernandes said Carrick personally informed him that he would not start against Bournemouth and explained that the decision was based on the team’s poor performances.

“The manager came to speak to me after that game, or before the Bournemouth game, to say, ‘Look, I’m not going to start you tomorrow’ and everything.

“There’s nothing to do with the words of Como; I know people will make it around that. I just want to tell you that we need to change something, I need to make a change, performances have not been great, so I just want you to know that there’s nothing to do with that,” he said.

The United captain added that he approached Carrick to clarify his remarks and ensure they were not misunderstood.

“I told him, ‘I think that you probably got our words in the wrong way, and you might be upset with me, and rightly so, if you got it in the way everyone is speaking about it’. And he said, ‘Bruno, I’m not; I just put you on the bench because I thought it was the best for the team,’” Fernandes recalled.

He said his return to the starting line-up in the following match further showed that there was no fallout between them.

United have since sacked Carrick and appointed former United States men’s national team coach Mauricio Pochettino as his successor.

Pochettino, who previously managed Southampton and Tottenham Hotspur in the Premier League, has already made his expectations clear to the United players, according to Fernandes.

The midfielder described the Argentine as a positive but demanding manager who wants the club to succeed.

“He’s someone who likes to bring a very good energy to the team, someone very positive, very strong, strict; he said straight away that he won’t allow us to not give him what he demands from the team, from each player,” Fernandes said.

“He wants the club to succeed so much, he wants to win games, and that’s pretty clear and obvious by the way he and his staff speak and want us to improve.”

Fernandes also disclosed that he nearly joined Tottenham in 2019 when Pochettino was in charge, revealing that he held talks with the club’s board about a possible move.

He said the prospect of playing alongside Harry Kane, Dele Alli and Heung-Min Son had appealed to him.

“Obviously I was a very young player who was eager to come to the Premier League. They had Kane there, they had Dele Alli, Son; the chance of playing with those players in front was very attractive,” he said.

Fernandes joined Manchester United from Sporting Lisbon in January 2020 and has since established himself as one of the club’s key players.

He was named PFA Player of the Year in August after recording a league-record 21 assists last season.

Manchester United are currently 19th in the Premier League table with eight points from 10 matches, while Liverpool occupy fifth position with 18 points, 10 behind leaders Arsenal.
