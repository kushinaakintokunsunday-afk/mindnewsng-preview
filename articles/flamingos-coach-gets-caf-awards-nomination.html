Flamingos coach gets CAF Awards nomination

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

FIFA Women’s U-17 World Cup-winning coach Akeem Busari has been nominated for the CAF Women’s Coach of the Year Award, days after leading Nigeria’s Flamingos to a historic triumph in Morocco, PUNCH Sports Extra reports.

Busari’s nomination was announced on Tuesday alongside nine other coaches as the Confederation of African Football unveiled nominees for the final three women’s categories at the CAF Awards 2026, scheduled for Lagos on Wednesday, December 2.

The other categories are Women’s Club Player of the Year and Women’s Club of the Year.

The Nigerian will face competition from coaches across the continent, including Algeria’s Farid Benstiti, Cameroon’s Valentine Nguele, Ghana’s Kim Lars Bjorkegren, Morocco’s Jorge Vilda, South Africa’s Desiree Ellis and Tanzania’s Bakari Nyundo Shime.

Others nominated are Silveria Nedio of Cabo Verde, Lovemore Fazili of Malawi and Hicham Drissi of Moroccan club AS FAR.

Busari guided the Flamingos to Nigeria’s first FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup title in Morocco, making them the first African team to win the competition.

According to CAF, voting for the 2026 awards opened on November 5 and will close on November 20, with CAF Member Associations, clubs and eligible coaches and captains participating in the process.

The awards ceremony will hold at the Professor Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Arts, formerly the National Theatre, in Lagos, with proceedings scheduled to begin at 7pm local time.

The CAF Awards 2026 will recognise outstanding achievements across 18 categories, honouring players, coaches, clubs and national teams for their performances in African football over the past year.
