No serious rift between Tinubu, Makinde – Oyo monarch

By Punch Punch 6d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Olugbon of Orile-Igbon, Oba Francis Alao, on Wednesday disclosed that there was no rift between President Bola Tinubu and Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde, s

He made this known while receiving the APM governorship candidate in Oyo State, Bimbo Adekanmbi, his running mate, Mufutau Open-Salawu, and other party chieftains at his palace in Orile-Igbon, Surulere Local Government Area of the state.

Alao stressed that he was confident that the two leaders would resolve whatever differences they had, adding that he would attend the President’s scheduled visit to Ibadan on Saturday.

“There is no issue between Makinde and Tinubu. It is a family affair. It is a father and son issue. We will resolve all the issues before 2027,” he said.

He also expressed confidence that the South-West would retain the presidency until 2031, saying efforts would be made to secure the support of political stakeholders in the North.

“It is sacrosanct that the South-West should retain the presidency until 2031. We appeal to our people in the North to show understanding and allow us to maintain the North-South balance,” he said.

Recalling his meeting with Tinubu in 2023, the monarch said the President had told him that his administration inherited both assets and liabilities.

“When I visited President Tinubu in 2023, he told me that he had inherited both assets and liabilities. He has fantastic policies, but his policies are transformative. Before they become fully effective, they require time,” Olugbon said.

Alao recalled his support for Tinubu’s presidential ambition in 2023, saying he was among the few traditional rulers in the South-West who openly declared their support for the President at the time.

“Nobody can intimidate us. We have been in this game for a very long time. I was the National Secretary of the Tunji Braithwaite Foundation, but it is during critical situations that we come out.”

Describing both Tinubu and Makinde as close associates, the traditional ruler said his loyalty to the President did not diminish his relationship with the Oyo governor.

“Tinubu is a very close person to me, just like Makinde. Tinubu is a very good person, and Makinde is a very good person. The President knows how we played the game in 2023. But at the same time, Makinde is my man. I belong to the same political institution of the late Governor Abiola Ajimobi. Zacch is also from that political school.

“Zacch Adedeji is my son. Omo daadaa ni (He is a good child). But must we fight? We belong to the same family,” he said.

The monarch also praised Makinde’s administration for its management of state resources, particularly the payment of salaries, infrastructure development and care for retirees. He challenged Adekanmbi to surpass the governor’s achievements if elected in 2027.

“Before the allocation increased, Makinde was managing the resources of the state very well. Makinde pays salaries, promotes infrastructure development and takes care of retirees. You must do more than what Makinde has done. Please, serve Oyo State very well,” he said.

On Adekanmbi’s ambition, Oba Alao described him as a brilliant person and disclosed that the APM candidate had received endorsements from traditional rulers in other parts of the state.

“Before I arrived yesterday, Ogbomoso had already endorsed Bimbo. We must work collectively. I have known Bimbo for a while now, and he is a brilliant person.

“Onpetu, Oba Sunday Oyediran, and the Soun of Ogbomoso, Oba Ghandi Olaoye, have endorsed Bimbo. So, what am I going to do? I will pray for you.

“A week before the election, as I did in 2023, I will tell you who the next governor of Oyo State will be.

“All the governorship candidates are from Ibadan, but some Ibadan people are closer to Ogbomoso and Ibarapa. The Ibadan candidate who is close to us is the one we are endorsing.

“All the traditional rulers in Surulere asked me to tell you that they have endorsed you. We will ensure that we deliver the right candidate in 2027,” the monarch said.

Earlier, Adekanmbi said he visited the palace to pay homage to the monarch, whom he described as a father and mentor, rather than for political reasons.

“Baba is an accountant, and I am an accountant too. I have been drinking from his fountain of knowledge for a long time, and I will continue to do so.

“We must ensure that Oyo State moves forward and not backwards. It is about sustaining the good work Governor Makinde is doing. From the first day we assume office, it will be about continuity. We are here for Baba to pray for us. I am the APM governorship candidate and Olugbon’s son. We have heard everything you have told us, and I can assure you that we will implement them,” he stated.

Adekanmbi subsequently presented the party’s candidates for the various elective positions in Oyo North Senatorial District to the monarch ahead of the 2027 general elections.
