Bayelsa boat accident: Tinubu directs emergency agencies to launch rescue operation

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

President Bola Tinubu has expressed sadness over a boat accident in Ukubie, near Oweikorogha community, in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State,

President Bola Tinubu has expressed sadness over a boat accident in Ukubie, near Oweikorogha community, in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, which reportedly claimed several lives on Friday.

The victims were reportedly mourners travelling to attend a funeral in the community when their boat capsized.

In a statement issued on Saturday by his Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, the President said he was saddened by the tragic incident.

Tinubu also disclosed that he had directed relevant Federal Disaster Response agencies to work together to ensure a successful rescue and recovery operation.

The President’s directive is aimed at ensuring that necessary emergency efforts are coordinated to respond to the incident and assist those affected.

“Those who lost their lives in the tragedy did not deserve to die that way. I have directed all relevant Federal Disaster Response agencies to work together to ensure a successful recovery and rescue operation,” Tinubu said.

The President also directed that the presidential campaign rally scheduled to hold in Ibadan, Oyo State, on Saturday, November 21, be observed “without much fanfare” in honour of the victims and in sympathy with their families.

“On behalf of the entire nation, I mourn those who lost their lives in the incident and sympathise with their families, the government and the people of Bayelsa State.

“I particularly mourn with Governor Douye Diri over this sad incident.

“I pray that God Almighty repose their souls and comfort their families,” he added.

The All Progressives Congress had outlined a proposed nationwide campaign rally schedule covering the 36 states of the federation and the Federal Capital Territory ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The proposed campaign kicked off in Kaduna on October 24, 2026, and will conclude with a grand rally in Lagos on January 9, 2027.

Oyo was outlined as one of the 12 states Tinubu is expected to join the rally in person and will be his fourth, after Kaduna, Port Harcourt and Jos.

Bayelsa boat tragedy

Very Nigerian initially reported that more than 30 people were feared dead, while about 300 others were reportedly missing after the boat, popularly known as a “Love Boat”, capsized near Oweikorogha while conveying mourners to Ukubie for a burial ceremony.

In a subsequent report, the Bayelsa State Police Command confirmed that four bodies, comprising two adults and two children, had been recovered, while 24 survivors were rescued. The police said preliminary investigations indicated that the boat developed a mechanical fault while navigating the waterways.

The Bayelsa State Government subsequently warned boat operators against overloading, reckless navigation and travelling without life jackets following the accident.

It also said it was supporting the joint search and rescue operation.

Earlier in November, Very Nigerian reported that a police officer and a woman died in two separate waterway accidents in Brass and Sagbama LGAs. Seven passengers were rescued after a passenger boat travelling from Yenagoa to Akassa capsized.
