Kwara begins payment of outstanding gratuities to LG retirees

By Punch Punch 9h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Kwara State Government has commenced the payment of outstanding gratuities to retired local government and Local Government Education Authority workers who

The Chairman of the Kwara State Local Government Staff Pension Board and Executive Chairman of the Local Government Service Commission, Umar Danladi Shero, disclosed this on Monday while briefing journalists in Ilorin on recent developments in the commission and efforts to settle outstanding pension obligations.

Shero said beneficiaries who retired within the 2018–2019 period had started receiving their payments following the completion of the necessary verification and screening exercises.

He described the development as another step in the state government’s efforts to settle inherited gratuity liabilities and improve the welfare of retired local government and LG workers.

“The payment exercise is part of the ongoing efforts to clear inherited gratuity liabilities from the past administration,” he said.

The pension board chairman also disclosed that the state government had spent more than N7bn on gratuity payments to local government and LG retirees who left service between 2011 and 2017.

According to him, the amount was expended between July and October, while the latest disbursement of more than N2bn extended the payment exercise to retirees from 2018 and 2019.

Shero commended Governor AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq for his support in addressing pension obligations and improving the welfare of retired workers.

“The Governor’s continued support and intervention have provided the necessary enabling environment for sustained pension administration, regular pension payments and the gradual settlement of outstanding gratuity obligations inherited from previous administrations,” he stated.

He assured retirees that the board had put arrangements in place to ensure a seamless and transparent payment process.

“Payments will be made directly into beneficiaries’ designated bank accounts,” he added.

Shero reaffirmed the board’s commitment to ensuring that all verified and eligible retirees received their entitlements as the government continued efforts to settle outstanding gratuity obligations.

He urged beneficiaries to remain patient and cooperate with the board where necessary, assuring them that the payment exercise would continue in an orderly and transparent manner.

He added that the government’s resolve was to ensure that no eligible pensioner was left behind as it worked towards clearing outstanding gratuity liabilities.

The latest disbursement comes amid the state government’s ongoing efforts to clear years of accumulated gratuity arrears owed to retired local government workers.

In October 2025, the state Commissioner for Finance, Dr Hauwa Nuhu, disclosed that the government had inherited more than N19bn in local government gratuity liabilities dating back to 2009, out of which N12bn had been paid.

She also said gratuities for local government workers had been cleared up to 2011, while N2.5bn had been earmarked for arrears covering 2012 to 2014.

The government had earlier said it increased monthly releases for gratuity payments from N20m to N100m after assuming office in 2019, as part of efforts to gradually settle the inherited backlog.
