Vinicius battles to reclaim Real Madrid spot after Brazil revival

By Punch Punch 4d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Real Madrid have cut the gap on La Liga leaders Barcelona down to three points, without the help of Brazilian winger Vinicius Junior, who faces a fight to recla

Los Blancos have the chance to pull provisionally level with the Catalan giants on Saturday when they host Celta Vigo at the Bernabeu, with Vinicius in contention to play after serving a four-match domestic ban for hair-pulling.

In Vinicius’s absence, Jose Mourinho’s side beat champions Barca in the Clasico at Camp Nou, as well as scoring wins over Sevilla, Racing Santander and Valencia.

The forward started in Madrid’s Champions League victories over AEK Athens and RB Leipzig in that period but without finding the form which made him a vital player for Los Blancos over the past few years.

Vinicius, who joined Real Madrid from Flamengo in 2018, carved out a reputation as one of the most dangerous attackers in world football, helping his side win the Champions League in 2022 and 2024.

However, this season he has struggled badly for form, with his only Madrid goal coming in August against Real Sociedad.

In the interim period, Madrid’s record signing Yan Diomande has adapted well to his new club and has four goals in his last eight appearances, staking his claim for the left wing spot.

During the international break, Vinicius found some relief with Brazil, led by former Madrid coach Carlo Ancelotti.

The forward seemed unlocked, scoring five times in two friendlies against Japan and Singapore, which he hopes can be a platform to turn his domestic form around.

Vinicius admitted that this summer, during which he was heavily linked with a departure from Madrid before ultimately penning a new deal until 2032, he let his standards slip.

Brazil suffered a surprise round-of-16 elimination at the World Cup by Erling Haaland’s Norway, which took a toll.

“I don’t think I prepared myself as well as I could have after the World Cup because the impact of that tournament is very significant for all Brazilians… being knocked out so early was something we didn’t expect,” he told reporters this week.

“I used the holidays to enjoy myself, rest, relax, and spend more time with my family. I ended up training less than in recent years.”

Vinicius said speculation on his future led to him feeling “overwhelmed” and he also highlighted the difficulty of staying at the peak of the game.

“I think only Cristiano Ronaldo and (Lionel) Messi have managed to do it for so many years, reaching the same numbers,” he added.

“I’m human, I make mistakes often, but I always try to improve.”

• Change of scene –

Vinicius’s only other goal this season came in the previous international break, for Brazil against Australia in a friendly.

Former Real Madrid player Predrag Mijatovic told Spanish radio station Cadena Ser that Vinicius seems happier playing for Brazil than his club side.

“I think last summer hurt Vinicius quite a bit because he had that doubt about whether to stay or not, whether to renew his contract or not, and he has more competition this year, right?” said Mijatovic.

“Diomande is performing well in this position, and now Mourinho is the one who has to make his own decisions.

“What’s clear is that Vini performs well when he’s happy, and I see him much happier playing with his national team than playing here.”

In previous campaigns, the Brazilian has saved his best form for the second half of the season.

“Last season, until December, I had only scored five goals. Then, I finished with more than 20 goals,” said Vinicius.

In each of the past five seasons, he has netted at least 22 times for Madrid.

On occasion Vinicius has faced criticism and whistles from the team’s own fans, but Mourinho has shown patience, while urging the most lethal version of the forward to return.

“The Vini who is at his best kills games — like he killed me in the past when I was with Benfica,” said the veteran Portuguese coach in September.

Mourinho will hope that Vinicius’s joy with Brazil can reignite his club form and bring back that killer instinct.
