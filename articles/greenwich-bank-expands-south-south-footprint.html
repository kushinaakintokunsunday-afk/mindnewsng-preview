Greenwich Bank expands South-South footprint

By Punch Punch 17h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Greenwich Bank Limited has intensified its national expansion drive with the opening of branches in Warri, Delta State, and Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, as it targets

A statement signed by Head, Corporate Communications of Greenwich Holdings, Ozena Utulu, said the new outlets would bring the bank’s physical network to seven, following the opening of five branches in Lagos, Ondo and Ekiti States in October.

The bank plans to extend its operations to Asaba, Port Harcourt, Abuja, Benin and Ibadan in the coming months.

Speaking at the commissioning of the Warri branch, Group Chairman of Greenwich Holdings, Kayode Falowo, described the Niger Delta as a strategic market, citing its economic importance, entrepreneurial potential and concentration of business activities across oil and gas, maritime services and logistics.

He said Warri’s economic prospects extended beyond its natural resources to the entrepreneurs, traders, contractors, service providers and other businesses driving commercial activities in the region.

According to Falowo, Greenwich’s more than three decades of financial services experience, combined with its commercial banking mandate, positions it to provide a wider range of financial solutions to individuals, small businesses and corporate customers.

At the Uyo branch commissioning, Chairman of Greenwich Bank, Segun Oloketuyi, said the commercial banking licence would enable the institution to extend its 32 years of financial services expertise to a broader customer base, including farmers, traders, civil servants, students and entrepreneurs.

He identified credit facilities, agricultural and SME financing, trade and treasury services, salary solutions and digital banking as some of the services through which the bank intends to support businesses and individuals.

Oloketuyi added that the bank would leverage the resources and expertise of the Greenwich Group to help local enterprises access financial services beyond conventional lending.

Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori, represented by the Commissioner for Finance, Fidelis Telije, who commissioned the Warri branch, welcomed the bank’s entry into the state, describing it as an opportunity to strengthen financial services and support economic development.

He urged residents and businesses to take advantage of the new outlet, particularly small businesses, young entrepreneurs and women-owned enterprises that require financial support to expand operations and create jobs.

Similarly, Akwa Ibom State Governor, Pastor Umo Eno, represented by the Commissioner for Finance, Emem Almond Bob, described the Uyo branch as a significant development that would improve access to banking services and credit, support business growth and potentially enhance the state’s internally generated revenue.

The Oruesen of Okere-Urhobo, Warri, His Royal Majesty (Dr.) Ogheneochuko Morris, also welcomed the bank’s establishment in the community, expressing confidence that its presence would create opportunities for businesses and residents.

Group Managing Director of Greenwich Holdings, Oyewale Ariyibi, said the bank’s entry into the region would support the emergence of new businesses and strengthen existing enterprises through specialised financial services.

He noted that the Group’s long-standing experience in the financial services industry had provided valuable insights into regional business dynamics, which would inform its approach to supporting enterprises across different sectors.

Managing Director of Greenwich Bank, Benson Ogundeji, said the Warri and Uyo branches would deepen customer engagement and bring financial solutions closer to businesses and individuals in the South-South.

He identified oil and gas, maritime services, agriculture, manufacturing, trade, logistics and small and medium-sized enterprises as priority sectors requiring responsive financial support.

Ogundeji stressed that the bank’s expansion strategy went beyond physical branch openings to building lasting customer relationships and deploying technology-driven solutions that promote enterprise growth and financial inclusion.
