‘Buhari rescued my business at its lowest point’ – BUA chairman Abdul Samad Rabiu reveals

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 2d ago 3 min read

Chairman of BUA Group, Abdul Samad Rabiu, has revealed that he committed N500 million to the acquisition and distribution of the authorised biography of late former President Muhammadu Buhari because of the personal support he received from the former Nigerian leader during difficult periods of his life.

Rabiu described Buhari as an “iroko tree” he leaned on during some of the most challenging moments of his life.

According to a statement issued on Saturday by the Muhammadu Buhari Foundation, the billionaire businessman made the remarks during the public presentation of the book titled Buhari: The Authorised Biography.

Rabiu, who was represented at the event by former Senator representing Zamfara North, Lawal Shuaibu, delivered a personal tribute in honour of the late former president, explaining the reason behind his financial commitment to the publication and distribution of the book.

The business tycoon drew on private memories, saying his relationship with Buhari spanned friendship, family ties, mentorship and moments of acute business adversity.

He said, “President Muhammadu Buhari was everything to me. He was a father, a mentor, a trusted friend and a pillar of strength to my family and me.”

He said the relationship prompted his N500m commitment to preserving the memory of the former president who passed away in July 2025 at a London medical facility.

Rabiu recalled one particularly difficult period when “disputes with government agencies threatened the survival of his business.”

At that point, he said, Buhari stood by him, resisted external pressure and insisted on acting according to what he considered just.

The reassurance, Rabiu said, moved him to tears.

“For that, I will remain eternally grateful to him. It is something I will never forget for as long as I live,” he stated.

He said “Buhari’s intervention had saved the business.”

Rabiu also described Buhari as a “cherished family friend” whose role in his life exceeded the formal boundaries of politics, government and commerce.

He recounted, “He was an iroko tree upon which I leaned when my strength was failing.”

Rabiu described Buhari as a man of integrity, humility and simplicity, who also prized loyalty, friendship and family.

“To the nation, he was a President and a statesman to me and my family, he was much more,” he stated.

According to the statement, the presentation was devoted to placing Buhari’s life before the judgement of history through the three-volume authorised biography written by Dr. Udu Yakubu.

Rabiu said the books deserved support because Buhari’s “life, service and legacy deserve to be remembered and celebrated for generations to come.”

He said his N500m acquisition commitment is based on his personal memory of Buhari as father figure, mentor, friend and protector.

It is also meant to preserve Buhari’s life and record through books intended to pass into homes, libraries, educational institutions and the wider national archive, said Rabiu.
