Anosike plans industrial clusters, housing estates for Osisioma

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Anosike unveiled the proposal at a PDP campaign rally in Osisioma, where he presented what he described as strategic development plans for each of the state’s 17 local government areas ahead of the 2027 governorship election.

He said Osisioma would be developed as a satellite town to accommodate residents and businesses spilling over from Aba, the commercial centre of the South-East, which he argued had become congested.

“Aba is already filled up. As part of the decongestion plan, government will build several housing estates in Osisioma where people can live and go to work in Aba. Also, industrial clusters will spring up in Osisioma to service Aba,” he said.

The candidate said the council’s landmass made it suitable for the proposed expansion, which he claimed would improve its internally generated revenue and make it one of the wealthiest local governments in the state within months of his administration taking office.

“Osisioma has the landmass suitable for our development plans and when we assume power, the economy of the council will be turned around and its internally-generated revenue improved. It will become one of the richest local governments in Abia in a matter of months,” Anosike said.

He said his decision to contest the governorship election was driven by what he described as the suffering of Abia residents, accusing the incumbent administration of failing to provide adequate economic support and welfare programmes.

“A government that didn’t provide empowerment, subsidy, grant, palliative and other incentives to improve the welfare of her citizens, should be changed because people need to be alive to enjoy the infrastructure built by government,” he said.

Anosike promised that his administration would introduce regular empowerment programmes, arguing that infrastructure development alone would not address the hardship facing residents.

He also blamed the high cost of living in the state partly on what he described as the government’s failure to prioritise agriculture, promising to introduce a state-backed food production programme called Operation Feed Abia.

Other proposals he outlined included establishing a traders’ microfinance bank to provide soft loans to women, youths, traders and artisans; free multiple-skills training for young people; and a N90,000 minimum wage.

He also promised to sponsor more than 30 talented footballers abroad annually and introduce health insurance and subsidised healthcare to reduce the cost of medical services.

“For a government minded to improve the welfare of the people, these programmes are possible. The money to do it is there; it is Abia money and should be spent on the people,” he said.

The candidate urged residents to support his ambition, saying his administration would focus on economic development and giving people the resources to improve their livelihoods.

At the rally, he also approved a request by Osisioma stakeholders for the council to receive more than one commissioner in his proposed administration, citing its special status.

Earlier, the Director-General of the PDP Governorship Campaign Council, Obinna Nwachukwu, had asked Anosike to consider allocating about three commissioner positions to the local government if elected.

Nwachukwu also drew attention to the condition of roads in the area, saying several had deteriorated and required urgent attention.

The Abia PDP Chairman and deputy governorship candidate, Amah Abraham, and the Chairman of the campaign council, Empire Kanu, urged party supporters to back Anosike, describing him as committed to improving the welfare of residents.

Other PDP candidates at the rally included the Abia South senatorial candidate, Chinyere Onuoha; Aba North/Aba South Federal Constituency candidate, Bob Chiedozie Ogu; Obingwa/Osisioma/Ugwunagbo Federal Constituency candidate, Solomon Akpulonu; Osisioma South State Constituency candidate, Ahamefula Young Onyike; and Obingwa West State Constituency candidate, Enyinnaya Appolos.

The Osisioma PDP Chairman, Emeka Onyenso, the local government woman leader, Blessing Ahaiwe, and youth leader, Ahamefula Ogidi, pledged the area’s support for the party in the 2027 elections.
