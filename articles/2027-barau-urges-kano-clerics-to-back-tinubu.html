2027: Barau urges Kano clerics to back Tinubu

By Punch Punch 7h ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Deputy President of the Senate, Senator Barau Jibrin, has urged more than 2,000 Islamic clerics in Kano State to mobilise support for President Bola Tinubu’

Barau made the appeal on Monday during a meeting with Islamic clerics from the 13 local government areas of Kano North Senatorial District, where he urged them to support Tinubu and other All Progressives Congress candidates.

In a statement issued on Monday by his Special Adviser on Media and Publicity, Ismail Mudashir, the senator said the President was committed to the development of Kano, the North-West and the country.

He acknowledged the contributions of religious leaders to peace and stability in the state, urging them to sustain their prayers and support for political leaders.

“This is a gathering of our fathers, our leaders, our sheikhs and our imams. You are our leaders. I warmly welcome you all to this gathering and sincerely appreciate your prayers and support,” Barau said.

Highlighting some of the Federal Government’s interventions in Kano, the Deputy President of the Senate said he had personally approached Tinubu to seek the conversion of the Federal Polytechnic, Kabo, into a university, which was subsequently approved.

He also cited the approval for the conversion of the Federal College of Education, Kano, into a university, as well as the establishment of a Police Training School, Civil Defence Training School and Immigration Training School in the state.

Barau said the establishment of the North-West Development Commission was another intervention by the Tinubu administration to address the developmental challenges facing the region.

He also said that, following Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf’s return to the APC, projects worth about N2tn had been attracted to Kano State.

On the economic challenges facing Nigerians, Barau acknowledged the hardship but argued that the situation had a global dimension.

“The economic hardship that some people are using to campaign against us is not peculiar to Nigeria. It has a global dimension,” he said.

The senator urged the clerics to pray for Tinubu, the APC, political leaders and the country, saying their supplications should extend beyond individual officeholders.

As part of the meeting, Barau announced a motorcycle empowerment programme for Islamic clerics across the 13 local government areas of Kano North.

He said 15 motorcycles would be distributed in each local government area in the first phase, bringing the initial allocation to 195, while another 195 would be distributed after three months.

He added that the programme would continue in subsequent phases until all targeted beneficiaries were covered.

“In addition, each of you will receive N50,000. We recognise your contributions to society, and we have a responsibility to support you,” he said.

Barau also commended Governor Yusuf for his development projects and interventions across the state.

In his remarks, the Chairman of the Kano North APC Campaign Council and Managing Director of the Hadejia-Jama’are River Basin Development Authority, Engineer Rabiu Sulaiman Bichi, said the meeting was organised to appreciate the clerics for their contributions to society and their role in promoting peace.

Bichi cited the ongoing railway projects linking Kano to Lagos and Kano to Maradi, as well as road infrastructure and other initiatives, as evidence of the Federal Government’s development efforts in the state.

The Chairman of the Kano North Council of Imams and Chief Imam of Bichi Local Government Area, Malam Lawal Imam, commended Barau for his interventions, including the construction of mosques.

“We will continue to pray for him, the APC and the entire country. The clerics have a wide network of influence. Each of us has family members, students, pupils and followers who look up to us. Our collective reach within the communities is significant,” he said.

Also speaking, the Kano State Commissioner for Information, Sunusi Bature Dawakin Tofa, said he had never witnessed such a gathering of religious leaders at a political meeting.

He commended Barau for bringing the clerics together and praised the governor’s efforts to promote unity among religious leaders in the state.
