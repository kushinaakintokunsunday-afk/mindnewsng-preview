ChipInHere Empowers 29 Nigerian Founders With Community-Backed Funding, Announces Edition 2.0

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 4 min read

LAGOS, NIGERIA — A community-driven startup pitching and funding platform, ChipInHere, has concluded the inaugural edition of its entrepreneurship initiative, Pitch & Fund 1.0, empowering 29 emerging Nigerian founders and awarding ₦500,000 in cash prizes to top-performing innovators.

Built on the belief that promising ideas deserve accessible support to grow, Pitch & Fund provides a platform where aspiring founders, creators, and small business owners can showcase their ventures, attract public backing, and raise funds to scale their operations.

Unlike conventional pitching competitions decided solely by a judging panel, Pitch & Fund 1.0 adopted a community-backed funding model that enabled contestants to rally financial support and public engagement directly from friends, family members, customers, and the wider public via ChipInHere and its official social media channels.

Commenting on the initiative, Founder of ChipInHere, Oyin Osundina, said the platform was created to bridge the early-stage funding and visibility gap facing everyday entrepreneurs across the country.

“Across Nigeria, brilliant entrepreneurs are building real solutions every day, yet many stall simply because they lack early backing or visibility. With ChipInHere and Pitch & Fund, we wanted to prove that when you give founders a simple tool to rally their own communities, ordinary people can become the earliest investors in extraordinary businesses,” Osundina stated.

Also speaking on the outcome of the maiden edition, Co-Founder of ChipInHere, Fadah Oyinkuro, noted that the enthusiastic response across social channels validated the power of collective support.

“Pitch & Fund 1.0 showed us what happens when technology meets African communal spirit. Watching 29 founders turn 60-second pitches and WhatsApp links into real capital, new customers, and massive social engagement across Instagram and Facebook reinforces why we built ChipInHere — to make collective support effortless for everyone,” Oyinkuro said.

WINNERS OF PITCH & FUND 1.0

The competition, which commenced on October 10, 2026, and closed at 11:59 pm West Africa Time on October 31, 2026, culminated in the emergence of three winners who shared the ₦500,000 prize pool:

• Grand Winner (₦250,000): Mamah Bonaventure, Chief Executive Officer, BuildConnect

• First Runner-Up (₦150,000): Okon Christiana Ufon, Chief Executive Officer, Elegant Bead NG

• Second Runner-Up (₦100,000): Oyinpreye Rachel Omemu, Chief Executive Officer, Creative Playground

Beyond the financial rewards, organisers noted that the initiative provided all 29 participants with national visibility, networking opportunities, customer acquisition, and practical exposure to community-driven fundraising.

HOW THE COMPETITION OPERATED

According to the organisers, Pitch & Fund 1.0 was structured around a three-step process to make pitching, visibility, and grassroots fundraising accessible to entrepreneurs nationwide:

  1. Creation of Funding Links: Each contestant generated a personalised fundraising link on the official ChipInHere platform to serve as a dedicated collection page for supporters.
  2. Short Video Pitches: Participants submitted 30-to-60-second video pitches introducing their enterprises, outlining the problems they solve, and presenting their growth plans.
  3. Social Amplification and Community Mobilisation: Organisers published the contestants’ pitch videos across ChipInHere’s official Instagram and Facebook pages, allowing their communities to watch, engage, and vote, while founders shared their unique links across WhatsApp and other social media platforms to receive direct contributions and monitor their fundraising progress in real time.

SUSTAINING GRASSROOTS BUSINESS FUNDING

While Pitch & Fund 1.0 has officially concluded, ChipInHere stated that its digital infrastructure remains open year-round for entrepreneurs, creators, and community organisers seeking collective financial support.

Through https://www.chipinhere.co, users can create personalised fundraising or collection links, invite co-hosts to manage campaigns, share links seamlessly across WhatsApp communities, and track contributions without complex administrative bottlenecks.

PITCH & FUND 2.0 REGISTRATION OPENS NOVEMBER 20

Following the strong participation recorded in the maiden edition, ChipInHere has announced that registration for Pitch & Fund 2.0 will officially open on November 20, 2026.

Prospective applicants are expected to submit their entries via the competition’s official portal at https://www.pitchandfund.co, while business owners seeking immediate funding can launch independent campaigns at https://www.chipinhere.co.
