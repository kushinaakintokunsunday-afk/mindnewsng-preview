'I no be Vincent': Zlatan Ibile dodges VeryDarkMan's 24-hour ultimatum amid raging feud - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 12 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

Zlatan Ibile has ignored VeryDarkMan's 24-hour ultimatum to publicly deny claims that his parents and three celebrities begged the activist privately. Instead,

The ongoing online war between rapper Zlatan Ibile and social media activist VeryDarkMan (VDM) has entered a new phase, after the "Zanku" crooner chose trolling over a direct response to the activist's 24-hour ultimatum.

Rather than issuing the public denial VDM demanded, Zlatan has kept his fans guessing with cryptic posts and laughing emojis, while continuing to push the song verse at the centre of the controversy.

The song line that lit the fire

Weeks ago, singer Peruzzi released his track INFERNO, featuring Zlatan and Shoday. Zlatan's verse contained a line that immediately caught attention: "I no be Vincent, I no fit masturbate."

The line is widely read as a reference to VDM, whose real name is Martins Vincent Otse - and who has spoken openly in the past about masturbation as part of his public commentary. Zlatan has never confirmed that the lyric was aimed at the activist, but few listeners needed the explanation.

VDM's ultimatum: 'Drop the names of the celebrities'

The drama escalated when VDM, in a video posted online, accused Zlatan of acting tough in public while sending intermediaries to beg him behind closed doors.

According to VDM, the rapper sent three celebrities to plead with him, and even claimed that Zlatan's parents joined a phone call with one of them to beg on their son's behalf.

"As a matter of fact, his parents were on a call with one of them to beg me that their son no fit chop, that their son is not feeling well, that this whole thing is messing him up," VDM said.

He then handed Zlatan a 24-hour ultimatum to publicly deny the claim - threatening to reveal the names of the celebrities involved and release phone recordings if the rapper stayed silent.

"Let him say it's a lie, make I drop the names of the celebrities," he added.

Zlatan's answer: cryptic posts and laughing emojis

When the deadline passed, Zlatan did not confirm or deny anything. Instead, per reports circulating on social media, he posted a cryptic "Zanku OT tested" message on X, dropped laughing emojis in comment sections, and kept promoting videos featuring his "I no be Vincent" verse.

It is not the first time the rapper has responded to the feud with riddles. In an earlier post on X, he wrote: "You can't complain about someone giving you a taste of your own medicine when you have run out of good medicine."

Why the two men are enemies

The bad blood between Zlatan and VDM traces back to the death of singer Mohbad in September 2023, a case that shook the Nigerian music industry. VDM repeatedly pressed Zlatan to speak publicly on what he knew about the circumstances surrounding the death, shared alleged audio recordings and issued public ultimatums. Zlatan largely stayed silent - and the silence became the foundation of the grudge VDM still carries today.

"That same Mohbad case make me no like Zlatan," the activist has said, while still acknowledging: "Zlatan is a great artist, I love his songs."

Davido, Burna Boy and the wider fallout

The feud pulled in bigger names. Davido was captured singing along enthusiastically to Zlatan's INFERNO verse during the welcome party for Zlatan's wedding in Marrakesh, Morocco - a moment that visibly angered VDM.

VDM responded by mocking Davido with a throwback clip, declaring he would rather "masturbate than kneel down and beg a woman". Burna Boy, watching the storm build, stepped in with a plea for calm, commenting: "Let it go, free am abeg."

VDM has also teased a diss track of his own, titled On My Knees - so if Zlatan thought silence would end this, the activist has made it clear the story is not finished.

Editor's note: The claims made by VeryDarkMan in this story are his own allegations and have not been independently verified by Mindnewsng. Zlatan Ibile has not confirmed that the INFERNO lyric was directed at VDM, and neither he nor Davido has publicly responded to the latest claims. This report will be updated as the situation develops.
