Tinubu’s visit: Kebbi commuters hail President over Malando-Ngaski road reconstruction

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 3 min read

From Ola Muhammad, Birnin Kebbi

Commuters and residents along the Malando-Garin Baka-Ngaski-Wara-Auna corridor in Ngaski Local Government Area of Kebbi state have commended President Bola Tinubu for the ongoing reconstruction of the road, saying it has cut travel time, boosted trade and improved security.

They spoke to the newsmen ahead of Tinubu’s visit to Kebbi State on Tuesday for the All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally.

Residents described the project as a dividend of democracy that has reconnected communities long cut off by bad roads.

Malam Mamman Garba, a commercial driver on the Ngaski-Auna route, said the road had slashed travel time by almost 50 per cent.

” Previously, we spent several hours navigating bad portions, especially during the rainy season.Vehicles frequently broke down, while transport fares increased.

” Now, movement is faster and safer. Our daily income has improved because we can make more trips,” he said.

Hajiya Mohammed Aliyu, a trader from Wara, said the road had made it easier and cheaper to move farm produce to markets.

” Before now, transporting farm produce to markets was difficult and expensive, leading to losses for traders and farmers. The road has reduced these challenges and created more business opportunities for our people,” she said.

A former Commissioner for Lands and Housing in Kebbi, Alhaji Mahmuda Mohammed-Wara, called the project a major federal intervention that had brought relief to the communities.

He recalled that Minister of Budget and Economic Planning, Sen. Abubakar Atiku Bagudu, had assured residents on an earlier visit that funding would not stall the project, adding that the minister had kept his word.

A former Chairman of Ngaski Local Government Area, Alhaji Abdullahi Buhari-Wara, commended Tinubu, Gov. Nasir Idris and Bagudu for the collaboration that brought the project to the area.

He said many residents had initially doubted that the road would be completed, given the history of failed and abandoned contracts along the corridor.

” However, during his visit to the state last year, the Minister of Works, Sen. Dave Umahi, assured stakeholders of strict supervision and zero tolerance for substandard work.

” We have since witnessed significant progress, which has renewed the confidence of the people,” he said.

The Federal Government awarded the contract in late 2024, after years of failed attempts and abandoned contracts largely blamed on the difficult terrain.

Our correspondent who visited the site observed considerable progress by the contractor, Epsilon Sky Global Engineering Ltd.

The firm’s Managing Director, Mr Marcel Hattab, said the work had advanced despite bad weather and occasional disruptions in material supply caused by insecurity.

He said the wearing course on the Auna-Wara section had been completed, while the binder course and final earthworks were progressing towards Ngaski town.

Hattab said the project covers the rehabilitation of about 35 kilometres of severely deteriorated road.

According to him, the road will ease the movement of goods and services between Niger, Kebbi, Sokoto and Zamfara states and other parts of the country.

He added that the project had created hundreds of jobs, supported mining activities around Wara and helped reduce travel time, vehicle damage and insecurity along the corridor.

Hattab also commended Tinubu, Idris and Bagudu for their roles in facilitating the project.
