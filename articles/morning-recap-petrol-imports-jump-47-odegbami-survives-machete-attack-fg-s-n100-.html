Morning recap: Petrol imports jump 47%, Odegbami survives machete attack, FG’s N100,000 grant, other stories

Meanwhile, families continue to agonise over 640 pupils and worshippers still in captivity months after their abduction.

In another development, the Nigeria Customs Service has suspended training for 3,852 new recruits amid Federal Character Commission probes.

Also, mega churches face renewed scrutiny over succession plans following the Fountain of Life Church crisis.

The recap in full:

Petrol imports jump 47% despite local refining

Nigeria’s petrol imports rose by 47 per cent in October to 15 million litres per day, according to the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority, reversing the previous month’s decline and highlighting continued reliance on foreign supplies even as domestic refining expands.

Domestic refineries supplied 31.9 million litres per day (up 12 per cent), accounting for about 68 per cent of the 46.9 million litres received daily.

Ex-Super Eagles captain Odegbami escapes death in machete attack

Former Super Eagles captain Segun Odegbami, 74, narrowly escaped death after armed men wielding machetes attacked him at his residence in Ibara GRA, Abeokuta, Ogun State, in the early hours of Friday.

The attackers overpowered his domestic staff and driver before assaulting him in his bedroom.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/ex-super-eagles-captain-odegbami-escapes-death-in-machete-attack/

Forgotten: Families agonise over pupils, worshippers still in captivity

No fewer than 640 Nigerians, including schoolchildren and worshippers, remain in captivity months after their abduction.

These include 78 schoolchildren abducted in Borno State (six months ago), 547 worshippers from a Niger State mosque attack (three months ago), and 15 schoolgirls taken in Rafi, Niger State, on October 31.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/forgotten-families-agonise-over-pupils-worshippers-still-in-captivity/

Customs suspend recruits’ training as FCC probes recruitment

The Nigeria Customs Service has temporarily suspended training for 3,852 newly recruited officers following concerns raised by the Federal Character Commission over alleged irregularities in the selection and invitation process.

Both agencies have agreed to further consultations to resolve the issues. The recruitment exercise had attracted over 573,000 applications.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/customs-suspend-recruits-training-as-fcc-probes-recruitment/

Mega churches face succession heat

The leadership crisis at the Fountain of Life Church, Lagos, involving Senior Pastor Jimmy Odukoya and the Board of Trustees, has sparked fresh scrutiny of succession arrangements in major Pentecostal churches, including Deeper Life, RCCG and Living Faith Church.

Comments by Pastor William Kumuyi, Pastor Enoch Adeboye and Bishop David Oyedepo on succession have resurfaced amid the controversy, which has led to a court order restraining Odukoya from acting as senior pastor.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/mega-churches-face-succession-heat/

EXPLAINER: How to apply for FG’s N100,000 family grant

The Federal Government’s Nigeria Family Strengthening and Development Programme is offering N100,000 cash support to vulnerable households.

Eligible applicants must be Nigerian citizens by birth, aged 18–65, with a valid NIN. Registration is via nfsp.ng/signup.

Beneficiaries will be selected based on poverty and vulnerability criteria and notified by SMS. The programme warns against fraudsters demanding payment for inclusion.
