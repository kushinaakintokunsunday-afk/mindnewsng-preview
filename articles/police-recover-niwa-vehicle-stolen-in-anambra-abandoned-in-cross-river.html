Police recover NIWA vehicle stolen in Anambra, abandoned in Cross River

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Cross River State Police Command has recovered a Toyota Hilux belonging to the National Inland Waterways Authority, reportedly stolen in Anambra State and t

The vehicle, with registration number NIWA 19E-68-FG, was found abandoned around Uko-Ma Petrol Station, opposite Picolo Junction, near the Spaghetti Flyover in Odukpani.

The state Police Public Relations Officer, ASP Sunday Eitokpah, disclosed this in a statement made available to journalists on Thursday in Calabar.

According to the statement, the recovery followed a report received by the Odukpani Divisional Police Officer at about 11:30am on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, from a NIWA official in Calabar, who reported that the vehicle had been stolen in Anambra State and was being tracked to Cross River.

The statement read, “The Cross River State Police Command has recovered a Toyota Hilux belonging to the National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA), with registration number NIWA 19E-68-FG, reportedly stolen in Anambra State and subsequently tracked to Odukpani LGA, Cross River State.

“The recovery underscores the command’s commitment to preventing crime, responding swiftly to credible intelligence and ensuring that Cross River State does not become a safe haven for criminal elements.”

The police spokesman said that, acting on the directive of the Commissioner of Police, CP Ajo Ordue, the DPO escalated the information, prompting the mobilisation of relevant operational units of the command.

“At about 3:00 p.m. the same day, the vehicle was tracked to the location where it was found abandoned. An individual suspected to be connected with the vehicle reportedly fled upon sighting the police,” statement read partly.

The recovered vehicle has been taken to the police station for safekeeping, while efforts are ongoing to identify and arrest the fleeing suspect, the statement added.

“Investigation has also commenced to establish the circumstances surrounding the theft and movement of the vehicle,” he said.

The Commissioner of Police commended the officers involved for their prompt response and coordination, reiterating the command’s resolve to tackle criminal activities through intelligence gathering, inter-divisional collaboration and proactive policing.

He warned criminal elements against using Cross River State as a hiding place or transit route, assuring residents that the command would continue to track down offenders and bring them to justice.

“The command urged members of the public to promptly report suspicious activities through its emergency numbers: 0704 177 7060 and 0705 335 5415,” he added.

The latest recovery comes amid other reports of vehicles stolen in neighbouring states and traced to Cross River.

On November 6, the state police command announced the recovery of a Lexus RX 330 allegedly stolen from a car wash in Enugu State and traced to Ugep in Yakurr Local Government Area. A 36-year-old suspect was arrested in connection with the incident.

Similarly, the Anambra State Police Command said in October that it had recovered a Toyota Highlander allegedly stolen in the state and traced to Calabar.
