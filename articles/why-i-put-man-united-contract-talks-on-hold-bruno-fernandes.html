Why I put Man United contract talks on hold – Bruno Fernandes

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes on Sunday confirmed that he received a renewed contract offer from the club, but he has decided to put talks on hold a

Fernandes further dismissed rumours that he demanded more money and years for the said contract, saying he had always been loyal to United.

He stated this in an interview with Sky Sports on Sunday.

The 32-year-old Portuguese has been a central figure at Old Trafford since he arrived in 2020.

Fernandes, whose current contract expires at the end of the season, last signed his contract in August 2024.

He, however, noted that the club’s initial proposal was made in the summer when discussions first began over a new deal.

He said, “No, there are no negotiations ongoing, but I’ve seen people saying that he’s asked for more money; he’s asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here.

“I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

“Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave, and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I’ve always shown how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on.

“I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract, and then we will see.”

The United captain, who expressed his continued readiness to continue playing for United, said he wouldn’t obstruct his move away from Manchester if the club decides to lay him off.

“In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it’s time to part ways’, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

“Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season; we came in at the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to one side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for the club nor for me, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to winning games,” he added.

Fernandes stated that he still aims to win the Premier League and Champions League with United.

“My aim will always be to win the Premier League and the Champions League within the club I am,” he said.

The attacking midfielder cautioned that simply qualifying for the Champions League should be the club’s target.

“I will never hide from that; I will never say, ‘ No.’ Our position at the moment needs to be to get top four.”

“They’ve been far too patient with us,” he said, commending the supporters for standing by the club despite going for some years without winning a league title.

The Red Devils will travel to Anfield on Sunday for their Premier League match against Liverpool.

United currently sit on 19th with eight points from their first 10 EPL games. Liverpool have retained the fifth position with 18 points, exactly 10 points away from table leaders Arsenal.
