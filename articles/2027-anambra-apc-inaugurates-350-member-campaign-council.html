2027: Anambra APC inaugurates 350-member campaign council

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The All Progressives Congress in Anambra State has inaugurated a 350-member campaign council to coordinate its mobilisation efforts ahead of the 2027 general el

The council, inaugurated at the party’s secretariat in Awka on Wednesday, will be led by Chief Azuka Okwusa as Director-General, while the state chairman, Senator Emmanuel Anosike, will serve as its chairman.

Other members include Obiora Okpara as secretary; Dr Valentine Ilobi, Chairman of the Media Directorate; Mark Okoye of the All Progressives Grand Alliance; Emeka Agbasi; and Dr Pascal Candle, among others.

A former Minister of Labour and Employment, Dr Chris Ngige, and the party’s candidate in the 2025 Anambra governorship election, Prince Nicholas Ukachukwu, were named patrons of the council.

Speaking at the inauguration, Anosike urged members to mobilise support for President Bola Tinubu and other APC candidates in the 2027 elections.

He said the party should base its campaign on the achievements and performance of the Tinubu administration rather than sentiments.

Anosike said, “The major purpose of the inauguration is to commence in earnest door-to-door mobilisation campaign across the 5,526 polling units that constitue Anambra State.
The Campaign Council is made up of 350 members.

“The party under my watch is fully prepared to welcome President Tinubu to Anambra State for his re-election campaign rally scheduled for November 19 at the Alex Ekwueme Square, Awka.

“We urged the council members to engage in grassroots campaigns and we charge them to remain in their polling units and not travel until the elections are conducted next year.”

In his acceptance speech, Okwusa called on members to demonstrate discipline, commitment and a willingness to make sacrifices in carrying out their responsibilities.

He described the inauguration as the beginning of a major assignment for the party in the state, warning members against treating their roles as merely ceremonial.

He said, “The 2027 elections will demand discipline, organisation, sacrifice, and absolute commitment for every one of us.

“No one should assume that the task ahead will be easy, and no members of this council should consider this our assignment or role as ceremonial.”

Okwusa also urged members to set aside personal disagreements and work for the collective interest of the party ahead of January 16, 2027.

The event was attended by party candidates, including Senator Uche Ekwunife, Kingsley Ezekwelu and Ambassador Elijah Onyeagba, as well as other party members and journalists.

The APC is seeking to strengthen its political foothold in Anambra following its defeat in the November 8, 2025 governorship election.

The party’s candidate, Nicholas Ukachukwu, polled 99,445 votes to finish second behind incumbent Governor Charles Soludo of the All Progressives Grand Alliance, who secured 422,664 votes to win a second term.

The Independent National Electoral Commission has fixed January 16, 2027, for the presidential and National Assembly elections, while the governorship and State Houses of Assembly elections are scheduled for February 6, 2027.
