Bayern’s Kane ruled out for months with ankle injury

By Punch Punch 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane will be sidelined for the next few months after suffering a tear of the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle during Friday’s

The German champions confirmed the injury in a statement published on their official website on Friday, following an examination by the club’s medical department.

“Harry Kane suffered a tear of the syndesmosis ligament in his left ankle in FC Bayern’s Bundesliga home match against 1. FC Köln on Friday evening. This was confirmed by an examination by FC Bayern’s medical department.

The 33-year-old will therefore be sidelined for the next few months,” the club said.

Kane sustained the injury during the encounter at the Allianz Arena and was substituted in the 20th minute, with Tom Bischof coming on to replace him.

The England captain had attempted to continue playing after picking up the injury but was eventually forced off.

Bayern manager Vincent Kompany expressed concern over the injuries suffered by Kane and French forward Michael Olise, who also failed to complete the match.

Speaking after the game, Kompany said the club would have to cope with the situation and find ways to secure results despite the absence of key players.

“We have other things to worry about, I must admit. Without complaining too much, we must accept what we’re in for and regain our strength so that we can get results in the next few matches despite the odds,” he said.

“It’s an extremely unusual situation at the moment. We need incredible performances from players who aren’t used to this pressure at FC Bayern. But that’s just how it is. We’ll all try to turn this situation into something positive. FC Bayern do that again and again, and that’s what we want to achieve now too.”

Bayern also confirmed that Olise suffered a bruise to the front of his foot and would receive treatment over the coming days.

The draw against Köln saw Bayern surrender points at home after Mikey Moore gave the visitors the lead in the 12th minute, before Nathaniel Brown equalised for the hosts in the 37th minute.

Kane’s injury is a major setback for the Munich side, who are scheduled to face French club Lille in the UEFA Champions League on Wednesday.

The striker has been a key figure in Bayern’s attack, and his absence leaves Kompany with the task of reorganising his forward line for the coming fixtures.

Two weeks ago, the 33-year-old England captain signed a new contract that will keep him with the German champions until 2029.

On October 26, Kane won the 2026 men’s Ballon d’Or, at the 70th edition of the ceremony at the London Palladium, becoming the first Englishman to claim football’s most prestigious individual award since Michael Owen in 2001.
