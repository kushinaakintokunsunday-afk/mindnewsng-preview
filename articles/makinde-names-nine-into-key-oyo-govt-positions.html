Makinde names nine into key Oyo govt positions

By Punch Punch 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Governor of Oyo State, Seyi Makinde, on Monday approved the appointment of nine individuals to serve in various capacities across the state government. Acco

According to a statement issued by the Special Adviser to the Governor on Media, Sulaimon Olarenwaju, in Ibadan, the state capital, the appointments take immediate effect.

The statement explained that the appointments were conveyed in a letter signed by the Chief of Staff to the Governor, Akinwole Akinwale.

They underscore the administration’s commitment to strengthening governance, promoting institutional development and enhancing service delivery for the benefit of the people of the state.

The statement read, “The newly appointed political office holders are Fajobi Adebowale – Special Adviser, Budget and Economic Planning; Josiah Idowu – Special Assistant, Community Relations I (Other Communities); Kazeem Ayobami – Senior Special Assistant, Youth and Sports; Akinola Isaac – Special Assistant, Media; Ibrahim Adebayo – Chairman, Governing Council, Oyo State College of Agriculture and Technology, Igboora; and Ogunbiyi Bukunmi – Member, Governing Council, Michael Adeniyi Koleoso Polytechnic, Saki.

“Others include Ajala Folashade – Member, Oyo State Post Primary Schools Teaching Service Commission; Moyosoreoluwa Eldreez – Member, Oyo State Independent Electoral Commission; and Tunji Okesola – Member, Governing Council, Oyo State College of Education, Lanlate.”

While congratulating the appointees, the governor charged them to regard their appointments as a call to service.

Makinde further urged them to discharge their duties with dedication, integrity, diligence and a strong commitment to the growth and development of Oyo State.

The governor further expressed confidence in their capacity, competence and wealth of experience to contribute meaningfully to the continued progress of the state and the well-being of its people.
