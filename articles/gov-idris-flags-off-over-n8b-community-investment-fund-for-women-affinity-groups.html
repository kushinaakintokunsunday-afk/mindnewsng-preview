Gov Idris flags off over N8b community investment fund for women affinity groups

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 5d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Ola Muhammad Birnin Kebbi

Kebbi State Governor,  Dr. Nasir Idris, Kauran Gwandu, on Wednesday officially flagged off the disbursement of over ₦8 billion Community Investment Fund (CIF) to Women Affinity Groups (WAGs) across the state under the Nigeria for Women Programme Scale-Up Project.

The ceremony, held at the Haliru Abdu Stadium, Birnin Kebbi, was attended by the Minister of Women Affairs, Imaan Sulaiman-Ibrahim; Minister of Budget and Economic Planning, Senator Abubakar Atiku Bagudu; representatives of the World Bank; government officials; traditional rulers; and thousands of women beneficiaries.

The fund is being disbursed to over 3,000 Women Affinity Groups across Kebbi State to strengthen their financial capacity, promote entrepreneurship, expand small-scale businesses and improve the economic well-being of women, particularly those in rural communities.

Speaking at the event, Governor Idris reaffirmed his administration’s commitment to sustaining and expanding women empowerment initiatives, assuring that the programme would be extended to cover every local government area in the state.

He emphasised that women remain critical partners in family development, community advancement and the overall economic growth of Kebbi State.

The Governor stressed that his administration would continue to create opportunities for women to achieve financial independence, noting that the initiative aligns with its broader commitment to wealth creation, poverty reduction and inclusive economic development.

He assured that every necessary measure would be taken to ensure that women across the state benefit from the programme.

Governor Idris further disclosed that his administration had made additional financial provisions to support participating local governments, with ₦250 million earmarked for each benefiting local government to strengthen the implementation and impact of the initiative.

He urged the beneficiaries to utilise the funds judiciously, invest in productive ventures and embrace the spirit of collective responsibility that underpins the Women Affinity Groups. He also called on them to sustain their commitment to peace, unity and the development policies of the government.

In her remarks, the Minister of Women Affairs, Imaan Sulaiman-Ibrahim, commended Governor Idris for his commitment to women’s economic empowerment, describing Kebbi State as a model in the implementation of the Nigeria for Women Programme.

She disclosed that the programme had mobilised over 80,000 women in Kebbi State into organised affinity groups, which had collectively saved more than ₦1.1 billion and accessed loans exceeding their initial savings, demonstrating the effectiveness of the group-based empowerment model.

The Minister explained that the Community Investment Fund was not a handout but a collective financial resource designed to strengthen women’s economic activities through organised groups. She urged beneficiaries to protect the funds, maintain proper financial records and invest in productive ventures, particularly agriculture, rice processing, livestock production, food processing and other businesses capable of generating sustainable income.

Also speaking, the Minister of Budget and Economic Planning, Senator Abubakar Atiku Bagudu, praised the Kebbi State Government for prioritising women’s empowerment and providing the necessary support for the successful implementation of the programme.

He observed that women in Kebbi had demonstrated remarkable commitment to entrepreneurship, savings and community development, urging them to maximise the opportunities provided by the initiative to improve their livelihoods and contribute more significantly to the state’s economic growth.

The World Bank representative, Mr. Michael Ilesanmi, described the flag-off as a significant milestone for Kebbi State and Nigeria, noting that the state had remained one of the leading participants in the Nigeria for Women Programme since its commencement in 2018.

He disclosed that Kebbi had recorded impressive achievements through the programme, with thousands of women organised into affinity groups and substantial savings generated through their collective efforts. He also commended Governor Idris for his commitment to expanding the programme to additional local government areas.

Earlier, the Kebbi State Commissioner for Women Affairs, Hajiya Halima Hassan Kamba, disclosed that during the pilot phase of the programme, approximately 80,000 women benefited from financial literacy training, business management and other capacity-building initiatives, while ₦3.2 billion was disbursed as individual livelihood grants.

She further revealed that under the scale-up phase, over 83,000 women had been mobilised into 3,506 affinity groups, collectively generating savings of ₦4.11 billion and providing loans amounting to ₦1.31 billion among members.

The Commissioner commended Governor Idris, the Federal Government and the World Bank for their sustained partnership and commitment to advancing women’s economic opportunities in the state.
