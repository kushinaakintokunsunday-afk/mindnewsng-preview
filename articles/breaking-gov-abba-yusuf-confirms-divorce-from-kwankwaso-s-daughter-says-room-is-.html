BREAKING: Gov. Abba Yusuf confirms divorce from Kwankwaso’s daughter, says room is now open for other women to apply

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

Kano State Governor, Abba Kabir Yusuf, has publicly confirmed the dissolution of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor

Kano State Governor, Abba Kabir Yusuf, has publicly confirmed the dissolution of his marriage to Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, daughter of his former political mentor and former governor of the state, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso.

Yusuf made the disclosure on Monday while addressing pensioners at an event held at the Government House in Kano, where he used humour and metaphors to speak about his marital status.

Speaking in Hausa, the governor likened the development to a reduction in military rank, saying he had moved from being a “corporal” to a “constable” because he now had only one wife.

“Some days ago, I was being forced to become a constable. I want to thank the good people of Kano who advised me to keep two wives, but for now I have accepted one,” he said.

The governor, however, suggested that he could marry again in the future.

“But mark you, I will still add another wife. Some of you may not understand what the governor means here,” he added.

Yusuf also appeared to confirm that the divorce process had been completed, disclosing that the relevant document had been issued.

“The governor has agreed to divorce her. The paper for the dissolution has been issued,” he stated.

He further appealed to residents to pray for him to find a suitable spouse, saying he was happy with the decision and asking Allah to guide him towards good people.

“Pray for the governor to be blessed with a good wife with proper upbringing among the applicants so that he can continue with polygamy,” he said.

The governor’s comments came barely two days after reports emerged that Zainab Musa Kwankwaso, said to be his second wife, had approached an Upper Area Court in Gudu, Abuja, seeking dissolution of the marriage.

The development has attracted public attention because of the long-standing political relationship between Yusuf and Rabiu Kwankwaso, who is widely regarded as the governor’s political benefactor.

However, neither the Kwankwaso family nor the governor’s office has issued a detailed statement explaining the circumstances surrounding the separation.

Yusuf’s remarks on Monday nevertheless appear to confirm that the marriage has ended.
