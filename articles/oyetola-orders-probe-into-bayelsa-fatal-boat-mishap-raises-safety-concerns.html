Oyetola orders probe into Bayelsa fatal boat mishap, raises safety concerns

From Idu Jude, Abuja

The Minister of Marine and Blue Economy, Adegboyega Oyetola, has ordered an immediate investigation into the remote and direct causes of the fatal boat mishap that occurred on Friday, November 20, 2026, in Ukubie, near Oweikorogha Community, Southern Ijaw Local Government Area of Bayelsa State, claiming scores of lives.

Oyetola directed the National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA) to conduct a thorough investigation into the incident and submit its report to him.

He further directed NIWA to work in collaboration with relevant government agencies and the Bayelsa State Government to ensure a comprehensive, transparent and credible investigation.

Describing the loss of lives as worrisome and unacceptable, Oyetola said the Federal Government would no longer tolerate avoidable safety lapses on the nation’s waterways.

The minister expressed concern that the passengers involved in the tragedy reportedly did not wear life jackets, recalling that he had personally visited Bayelsa State on May 2, 2025, to distribute 3,500 life jackets to water transport users as part of efforts to promote safety and protect lives.

He also raised concerns over the alleged absence of a passenger manifest, stressing that proper passenger documentation was a critical safety requirement that must be strictly enforced.

“Safety lapses on our waterways are no longer acceptable. The Federal Government will not fold its arms and watch its citizens perish on the waterways. We must get to the root of this tragedy and ensure that those responsible for enforcing safety regulations do their jobs,” the minister said.

Oyetola reiterated his call for the gradual phasing out of wooden boats by state governments and their replacement with safer, more durable and better-equipped fibreglass or fibre-reinforced plastic vessels, particularly for passenger transportation.

“I have consistently advocated for State Governments to begin gradual phasing out of wooden boats and replace them with safer, more durable and better-equipped fiberglass or fiber-reinforced plastic vessels, particularly for passenger transportation,” he said.

The minister disclosed that he had called the Governor of Bayelsa State, Senator Douye Diri, to commiserate with him, the state government and the families of the victims over the tragic incident.

Expressing sympathy to the bereaved families, the minister said the loss of lives in such circumstances was deeply painful and that the grief of families who had lost loved ones was beyond words.

“Our thoughts and prayers are with the affected families at this difficult time. We also sympathise with all those who have been affected by this tragedy and pray for strength, comfort and the fortitude to bear this irreparable loss,” he said.

He prayed for the repose of the souls of the departed and for divine comfort for their families, urging all stakeholders to work together to ensure that such a tragedy never happens again.
