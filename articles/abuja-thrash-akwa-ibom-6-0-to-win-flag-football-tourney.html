Abuja thrash Akwa Ibom 6-0 to win Flag Football tourney

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Team Abuja defeated Akwa Ibom 6-0 to emerge champions in the male category of the fourth edition of the Aketi Bowl National Championship of the Nigerian Seconda

Team Abuja defeated Akwa Ibom 6-0 to emerge champions in the male category of the fourth edition of the Aketi Bowl National Championship of the Nigerian Secondary Schools Flag Football League.

Abuja secured the six points early in the encounter and held on to their advantage despite a determined effort by team Akwa Ibom to overturn the deficit.

In the female category, however, Akwa Ibom defeated Abuja 12-0 after recovering from a slow start to claim victory.

The championship featured eight states and marked the culmination of state preliminaries held between September and October.

The winners would receive N1m, while the second- and third-placed teams would get N700,000 and N350,000, respectively, in both categories.

The winning team would also benefit from a scholarship award worth N150m at JOF University in Ondo State.

Speaking on the growth of the competition, the League Coordinator of the Nigerian Secondary Schools Flag Football League, Oluwatobi Abe, said the event was the fourth edition of the Aketi Bowl but the fifth season of the secondary schools league.

Abe, who is also the Grassroots Programmes Lead of the Nigerian American Football Association, said the league had expanded through state activations and reinforcement programmes aimed at introducing the sport to new schools and strengthening existing teams.

He said, “We’ve come a long way if you look at where we started a few years ago, 2022, 2021, when we started in Lagos, to now. The game, overall gameplay, between what you saw back then and what you see now is so different because these kids are playing like people who have been playing for years.

“Meanwhile, some of them just started playing this year or a few months back.”

Abe attributed the improvement to former players returning to their schools to train younger students and sustain the development of the sport.

He said the winners would receive N1m, while the second- and third-placed teams would get N700,000 and N350,000 respectively in both categories.

He added that the winning team would also benefit from a scholarship award worth N150m at JOF University in Ondo State.

On efforts to address the challenge of equipment, Abe said the association, in partnership with Draft Nigeria and NFL Flag, had distributed more than 2,000 balls and 5,000 flags to schools under its One Ball, One Flag, One Future initiative.

Also speaking, the Director of School Sport at the Lagos State Sports Commission and Chairperson of Education and Grassroots Sport of the Nigeria American Football Federation, Dr Oluyomi Oluwasanmi, said the competition was important for the all-round development of students.

Oluwasanmi, who recalled being involved in the programme from its inception in Lagos in 2021, said combining education with sports would help develop children’s talents and reduce social vices.

“There is no way we can separate education and sport if we want all-round development of our children, which we need to raise the total child.

“And if you give them this platform to exhibit their inherent talents, you see that social vices, juvenile delinquency, will be reduced to the barest minimum,” she said.

She disclosed that some students discovered during the pilot edition in 2022 had gone on to represent Nigeria at the World Cup qualifiers in Egypt in 2025 in both male and female categories.

Oluwasanmi called for stronger collaboration among sports authorities, school administrators and the league organisers to identify young talents and provide them with opportunities to develop.
