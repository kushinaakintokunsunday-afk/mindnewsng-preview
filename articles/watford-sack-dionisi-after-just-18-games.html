Watford sack Dionisi after just 18 games

By Punch Punch 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Watford have sacked head coach Alessio Dionisi after 18 games in charge, following Saturday’s 4-0 defeat to Sheffield United, with the club now searching for an

The Championship club confirmed the Italian’s departure in a statement on Sunday, a day after the heavy loss at Bramall Lane.

Dionisi, 46, was appointed in June following the departure of Ed Still, who had spent only three months at Vicarage Road.

The dismissal comes after a disappointing run of results that has left Watford 21st in the Championship table, with only four league victories under the Italian.

In a statement on the club’s website, sporting director Luke Dowling thanked Dionisi and his staff for their work.

“We thank Alessio and his staff for his professionalism and their efforts,” Dowling said.

“As I’m sure the supporters have seen, Alessio is a really good guy and we wish him the best for the future.

“The process has started for a replacement and we hope to be in a position to announce that shortly.”

Dionisi’s spell at Watford began positively, with his side beating Southampton in their opening league game. However, they won only two of their next 12 matches in all competitions.

Although they defeated Charlton on October 17, subsequent losses to Wolves and Norwich, followed by the heavy defeat to Sheffield United, increased pressure on the Italian.

Before joining Watford, Dionisi was in charge of Italian Serie B side Empoli, where he was sacked in March after five months following a run of five wins in 22 matches.

His appointment at Watford was his first managerial job outside Italy.

The latest departure adds to the club’s recent managerial instability. Since Tom Cleverley left at the end of the 2024-25 season, Watford have appointed Ed Still, Javi Gracia, Paulo Pezzolano and Dionisi as permanent head coaches, while Charlie Daniels also had a two-match spell as interim boss.

Dionisi was Watford’s 12th head coach, excluding interim appointments, since the end of the 2020-21 season.

The club are now searching for their 25th manager since the Pozzo family took over in 2012. Watford have had 57 permanent managers in their 145-year history.

The club’s managerial changes have continued despite previous acknowledgements of the need for greater stability. On the morning of their final-day defeat to Coventry last season, chief executive Scott Duxbury admitted that the club had made mistakes, describing the end to the campaign as “unacceptable” and “truly disappointing in the extreme”.
