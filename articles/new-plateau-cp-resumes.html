New Plateau CP resumes

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Plateau State Command has a new Commissioner of Police, Sunday Aisebiogun Okoebor, who has officially taken over the leadership of the command as its 48th C

In a statement released to members of the public on Monday and signed by the Police Public Relations Officer, Alfred Alabo, the command said the new CP, who holds a PhD, formally assumed duty on November 14, 2026.

“The Plateau State Police Command is pleased to formally announce the assumption of duty of its new Commissioner of Police, CP Sunday Aisebiogun Okoebor, fsi, PhD, who officially took over the leadership of the Command as the 48th Commissioner of Police today, 14th November, 2026,” the statement said.

According to the statement, “CP Okoebor was enlisted into the Nigeria Police Force on 10th June, 1994. He brings to Plateau State over three decades of distinguished, meritorious and exemplary service.

“A consummate law enforcement professional and accomplished academic, he possesses a B.A. (Hons) in English, a Master’s Degree in Criminology and a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Criminology, reflecting his commitment to intellectual development and the continuous advancement of professional policing,” it added.

The command described the new commissioner as “widely distinguished by his detribalised disposition, impartiality, fairness and unwavering commitment to equity.”

“Over the course of his illustrious policing career, CP Okoebor has occupied several strategic Command, operational and administrative positions, through which he has acquired extensive institutional knowledge and leadership experience,” the statement said.

His previous appointments include Area Commander, Gwagwalada; Deputy Commissioner of Police, Administration, DFA; and Commissioner of Police, Police Accounts and Budget (PAB), Force Headquarters, Abuja.

“He attained the rank of Commissioner of Police on 6th March, 2025,” the statement said.

The statement also highlighted his international experience, noting that his professional accomplishments extended to the international law enforcement arena, where he distinguished himself through contributions to multilateral security cooperation.

“He served with the United Nations Mission in Kosovo (UNMIK) as an Instructor and subsequently as an INTERPOL Regional Specialised Officer, with responsibilities spanning Cybercrime, Fake and Counterfeit Drugs, Intellectual Property Crime and Maritime Piracy between 2009 and 2018.

“Of particular significance was his historic appointment in 2014 as the first West African to lead the INTERPOL Major Event Support Team (IMEST) deployed to the Cricket World Cup in Bangladesh, an achievement that underscores his professional competence, international recognition and capacity for strategic leadership,” the statement added.

It further stated that “in recognition of his outstanding contributions to international policing and security cooperation, he has received several distinguished honours, including the United Nations Medal, INTERPOL Plaque and Certificate (2018), alongside commendations and recognitions from Ghana, Turkey, India, the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), India, and the Inspector-General of Police, Bangladesh.”

Upon assumption of duty, CP Okoebor reaffirmed his commitment to entrenching a policing philosophy characterised by professionalism, discipline, accountability, operational efficiency and a people-centred approach to law enforcement.

“He emphasised that the protection of fundamental human rights, the preservation of public order and the safeguarding of lives and property would remain cardinal priorities of his administration,” the statement said.

Addressing the prevailing security challenges, the commissioner underscored his resolve to intensify the fight against terrorism, banditry, kidnapping and other forms of violent criminality through proactive policing, intelligence-led operations and sustained inter-agency collaboration.

“He further stressed that the Command would strengthen existing security partnerships and deepen operational synergy with the Military, Department of State Services (DSS), Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC), Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC), Nigeria Immigration Service, Nigeria Customs Service, Nigerian Correctional Service, National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA), vigilante groups, hunters and other local security outfits.

“The new Commissioner of Police solicits the sustained cooperation and support of traditional rulers, religious leaders, community leaders, youth organisations, civil society groups, Market Leaders and other critical stakeholders. He recognises that the attainment of enduring peace and security is a collective responsibility requiring mutual trust, shared commitment and active citizen participation,” it added.

The command assured residents of its resolve to sustain peace and strengthen security, particularly before, during and after the electioneering period.

“Residents are consequently encouraged to remain vigilant, law-abiding and security-conscious, while promptly volunteering credible and actionable information to relevant security agencies to facilitate the prevention and timely response to security threats,” the statement said.

The command also released emergency numbers for residents to report incidents and unethical conduct by police officers.

The Control Room emergency numbers are 07059473022 and 08038907662, while complaints against officers can be made to ACP X-Squad on 08034448617, PPRO on 08060545670, CRU on 08037681026, PCB on 09016146804 and VCRU on 09051145757.
