2027 Oyo Governorship poll: Candidates to sign Peace Accord November 19

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The Central Council of Ibadan Indigenes (CCII) has fixed November 19 for governorship candidates in Oyo State to sign a peace, non-violence and democratic conduct accord ahead of the 2027 governorship election.

The President-General of the council, Chief Ajeniyi Ajewole, announced this in a statement released by the CCII Publicity Secretary, Kola Olomide, in Ibadan on Tuesday.

Ajewole said the signing ceremony would take place at Ibadan House, Oke Aremo, Ibadan, by 10 am on Thursday, November 19.

The initiative is aimed at securing the commitment of governorship candidates to peaceful conduct, non-violence and democratic principles ahead of the forthcoming election.

He said, “Among the candidates expected to participate in the exercise are Senator Sharafadeen Alli of the All Progressives Congress, Bimbo Adekanmbi of the Allied Peoples Movement, Oriyomi Hamzat of the Accord Party, Taofeek Adegoke of the African Democratic Congress, Hazeem Gbolarumi of the Peoples Democratic Party and Saheed Oladele of the African Action Congress.

“The accord is aimed at securing the commitment of governorship candidates to peaceful political engagement, issue-based campaigns and responsible democratic conduct before, during and after the election.”

Ajewole said the council considered the initiative necessary given Ibadanland’s strategic position in the political, economic and demographic landscape of Oyo State.

He noted that Ibadanland accounts for about 50 per cent of the state’s electoral strength, making it a significant constituency in the forthcoming governorship election.

“We believe that political competition should be based on ideas, policies and the capacity to deliver good governance rather than violence and intimidation.

“The initiative is part of the council’s commitment to promoting peaceful coexistence, responsible leadership and the protection of the democratic rights and welfare of residents of Ibadanland and Oyo State,” the President-General stated.

The initiative comes against the backdrop of recurring political violence in Nigeria, including recent clashes among supporters of major political parties in Oyo State.

Ajewole said the incidents underscored the importance of early engagement among political stakeholders to prevent violence, promote tolerance and ensure that political disagreements did not degenerate into confrontations capable of threatening lives and property.

He said the proposed accord would require participating candidates to reject political violence, respect their opponents, discourage hostility among their supporters, and protect lives and property.

The President-General urged all governorship candidates to honour the invitation and demonstrate their commitment to a peaceful electoral process, stressing that political differences should not threaten the unity, security and development of the state.

The statement added that the governorship candidates were also expected to return to Ibadan House on January 6, 2027, for a town hall meeting organised by the council. At the meeting, representatives of more than 250 affiliated organisations would engage the candidates on their demands, desires and expectations.

It said the town hall would provide an opportunity for the candidates to present their programmes and plans for various sectors of the state, while representatives of the affiliated clubs would raise issues affecting their organisations and the people of Ibadanland.

The CCII is the umbrella body for associations, clubs and socio-cultural organisations in Ibadanland, with more than 250 affiliated organisations.

Very Nigerian reports that the Independent National Electoral Commission has listed 16 candidates for the Oyo governorship election scheduled for February 2027.
