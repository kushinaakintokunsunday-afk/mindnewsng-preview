AFCON qualifier: Guinea-Bissau beat Madagascar 2-0, go three points clear of Eagles

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Guinea-Bissau opened a three-point gap over Nigeria at the top of Group L in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying series after defeating Madagascar 2-0 on

The victory came hours after the Super Eagles were held to a goalless draw by Tanzania in Zanzibar, leaving Nigeria with more work to do in their bid to qualify for the tournament.

Mama Baldé opened the scoring for Guinea-Bissau in the 32nd minute before Mauro Rodrigues doubled the lead in the 70th minute at the Estádio Nacional 24 de Setembro.

The result moved Guinea-Bissau to 10 points from four matches, while Nigeria remained second with seven points from the same number of games.

Madagascar and Tanzania occupy third and fourth positions, respectively, with two points each.

Nigeria had earlier dominated possession against Tanzania but failed to convert several scoring opportunities, with Tolu Arokodare coming closest when his effort struck the post in the 34th minute.

The Super Eagles also created chances after the break but could not find a breakthrough as the match ended goalless.

Nigeria had beaten Tanzania 3-1 in the first leg at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo, with Alhassan Yusuf opening the scoring before Arokodare netted twice in the second half.

Tanzania have already secured qualification for the 2027 AFCON as co-hosts alongside Kenya and Uganda, leaving Guinea-Bissau, Nigeria and Madagascar to contest the remaining qualification place in Group L.

The next round of fixtures, scheduled for March 24, 2027, will see Guinea-Bissau host Tanzania, while Madagascar face Nigeria.

Nigeria will then host Guinea-Bissau in the final round of matches on March 28, 2027, in what could prove decisive in the race for qualification.
