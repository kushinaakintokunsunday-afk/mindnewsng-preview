Sani, Abbas rally Zaria religious leaders for Tinubu’s re-election

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Kaduna State Governor, Senator Uba Sani, and the Speaker of the House of Representatives, Tajudeen Abbas, have urged religious leaders in Zaria to mobilise supp

The duo made the appeal during an engagement with Christian and Muslim leaders in Zaria, where they highlighted the achievements of the Tinubu administration and appealed for prayers and support for the All Progressives Congress.

Sani, who was represented by the Secretary to the State Government, Dr Abdulkadir Meyere, said the meeting was part of efforts to strengthen the relationship between the government and religious institutions.

He said, “We are here to strengthen our partnership with religious institutions and seek your prayers for peaceful elections and successful governance. Religious leaders have important roles to play in promoting peace, unity and development in our society.”

Meyere urged the clerics to sustain their support for the APC-led government at all levels, assuring them that the governor remained committed to delivering on his promises.

He also assured the district heads that the state government would sustain the payment of their additional allowances, while appealing to the religious leaders to continue praying for peaceful elections and the success of the administration.

Abbas, in his remarks, called on residents of Zaria and Kaduna State to support Tinubu’s re-election, saying the President deserved another term because of his contributions to national development.

The Speaker listed several interventions he had facilitated for traditional institutions, including the provision of utility vehicles for traditional rulers and the construction and renovation of district heads’ residences.

He said, “Through my interventions, district heads are now receiving monthly salaries. We have also facilitated the provision of vehicles for traditional rulers and the construction and renovation of their residences.”

Abbas disclosed that more than 7,000 students would benefit from a scholarship programme facilitated by him, while an empowerment programme was scheduled to hold the following week.

He also announced plans for the groundbreaking of an international grains market at Dambo, Zaria, attributing the interventions to the support of the Tinubu administration.

He urged the people to reciprocate the President’s support for the constituency by voting for him in the 2027 elections.

Responding, a leader of the Fityanul Islam, Darika Sufaye group, Malam Rabiu Sufi, commended Sani for maintaining cordial relations with religious organisations.

Sufi said, “We appreciate the governor for the relationship he has maintained with religious organisations. We have forgiven the government wherever it may have made mistakes, and we assure it of our continued support.”

He, however, urged the government to intensify efforts to publicise its achievements at the grassroots, saying many residents needed to be better informed about government policies and programmes.

“We will continue to mobilise our followers and communicate the achievements of the government to our people,” he added.

Similarly, a Kaduna State leader of the Izala group, Sheikh Aliyu Abdullah Telex, assured the government that religious leaders were prepared to pass its messages to their followers.

He pledged continued prayers for the country but urged political leaders to address the challenges confronting Nigerians.

The Secretary of the Christian Association of Nigeria, Zone One, Pastor Titus Yohana, also commended the government for what he described as its inclusive approach to governance.

Yohana said, “We appreciate the government for carrying Christians along in its engagements. We will continue to pray for the success of the administration and urge it to sustain this inclusive approach.”

Meanwhile, the Director-General of the First Aid Group of Jama’atu Izalatil Bid’ah Wa Iqamatis Sunnah, Engr. Imam Sitti, urged religious leaders to promote peaceful coexistence and avoid sentiments capable of dividing their followers.

Sitti advised the clerics to reflect on past mistakes, correct their shortcomings and ensure that their messages strengthened unity and peaceful coexistence.

Another religious leader, Malam Tanimu Mai Rawani, pledged support for Tinubu, citing the interventions Abbas had facilitated for traditional institutions.

He, however, appealed for the release of Sheikh Khalifa, whom he said was in custody.

In a related development, former Kaduna State Governor, Mukhtar Ramalan Yero, has urged APC members to embrace patience and unity, warning against allowing personal interests to undermine the party’s collective success.

Yero said political positions were temporary and called on party stakeholders to support whoever emerged as the party’s candidate at all levels.

“Political positions are temporary. What is important is the success of the party. We must learn to put our personal interests aside and support whoever emerges as our candidate,” he said.
