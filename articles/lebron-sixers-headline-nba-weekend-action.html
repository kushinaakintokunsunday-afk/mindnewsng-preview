LeBron, Sixers headline NBA weekend action

By Punch Punch 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

LeBron James and the Philadelphia 76ers headline the NBA weekend when they host Tyler Herro and the Milwaukee Bucks on Saturday. The game starts at 7pm WAT and

The game starts at 7pm WAT and will be shown live on SportyTV. The Sixers lead the Eastern Conference with 13 wins and three losses, making them one of the league’s early pace-setters.

The 41-year-old James is playing his 24th NBA season, extending his own record for the most seasons in league history. The 22-time All-Star lines up alongside Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey and VJ Edgecombe, giving Philadelphia a strong blend of experience and star power.

Philadelphia are monitoring the availability of Jaylen Brown, who missed the last game against Orlando with a hamstring injury. Head coach Nick Nurse said Brown would be evaluated once the team returns from its road trip. Senegal’s Tacko Fall will hope for more court time after recording three blocks in 10 minutes against the Magic on Wednesday.

Saturday’s game is a rematch of Philadelphia’s 127-116 win over Milwaukee on October 24. Embiid had a triple-double of 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists in that game, while Brown scored 27 points. James missed that contest. Milwaukee, led by 2025 All-Star Herro, who is averaging 19.4 points per game, will be looking to end a 12-game losing streak.

On Sunday, the Detroit Pistons visit the Atlanta Hawks at 9:30pm WAT, live on ESPN. Detroit are 10-4 and have won four of their last five games, led by Cade Cunningham, who averages 25.8 points and 7.8 assists. Atlanta are 9-6 and arrive on a two-game winning streak, which includes a 117-112 away win over Oklahoma City. Nickeil Alexander-Walker leads the Hawks in scoring with 21.2 points per game, while Onyeka Okongwu, in his seventh season with the club, averages 15 points and 8.2 rebounds. Both teams also play in the Emirates NBA Cup tonight.
