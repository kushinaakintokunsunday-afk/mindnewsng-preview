England’s 3-2 win over Spain our best yet – Tuchel

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

England manager Thomas Tuchel has described his side’s 3-2 victory over world champions Spain in Madrid as the best performance since he took charge of the Three Lions, praising their resilience, energy and football quality.

PUNCH Online had earlier reported Sunday that 10-man England beat the Spanish team at their turf.

Tuchel’s side produced an impressive display at the Metropolitano Stadium on Saturday, taking a 3-0 lead before Elliot Anderson’s red card and a subsequent penalty allowed Spain to fight back to 3-2.

England travelled to Madrid for their final UEFA Nations League group game knowing that only a victory would guarantee them top spot ahead of next March’s quarter-finals.

The Three Lions delivered the performance demanded by their manager, going toe-to-toe with the world champions and establishing a three-goal advantage midway through the second half.

However, the game took a dramatic turn after Anderson was sent off for fouling Lamine Yamal, with Spain converting the resulting penalty before Neco Williams scored again to reduce the deficit to 3-2 with 15 minutes remaining.

England held on despite the pressure, with goalkeeper James Trafford making important saves to help secure the victory.

Speaking after the match, in a release on the team’s website on Sunday, Tuchel said, “It was a brilliant performance and a well-deserved win.”

He added, “Overall, we were the better team and we put out a fantastic performance from start to finish.

“The red card and the penalty, it’s always a big decision and I need to understand why it’s a double punishment.

“From then it was a long, long 30 minutes, but I don’t want to focus on that. There were so, so many positive things and the performance and the result over the 60 minutes was well deserved.

“You need a complete performance when you come here and we brought everything to the table.

“We were brave on the ball, we were impatient off the ball, we were hungry, and in our own half we were patient and closed the gaps.

“A fantastic team performance, spirit-wise, energy-wise, but also in terms of football quality. That was the highest level.”

Trafford started in goal in place of Jordan Pickford, who featured in England’s first match of the international window against Croatia.

Asked whether the two goalkeepers were competing for the number one position, Tuchel said, “No, there’s no number one. It’s a group. The double save was exactly what we wanted and he had a brilliant match, because it only works if you’re top from number one to eleven.”

He added, “We showed what we’re made of and what we can do, and that we trust the process, and that we’re hungry enough to develop, and that we’re always ready to push our limits.

“We had our best result in 60 years [at the World Cup] and we were not happy with it. We wanted to get better and we bridged the gap in the things that we wanted to improve.

“It is impressive what we did in the last two camps, so full credit to the team.

“Given the circumstances, against whom and where we played, it was the best I’ve seen.”
