Morning recap: ‘Bamayi’ chant sparks APC convoy attack, FG suspends Lagos-Ibadan bridge work, other stories

Saheed Oyelakin

Top headlines this morning include violence that erupted in Gombe after youths chanted the anti-APC slogan “Bamayi” at a party campaign convoy, leaving buses damaged and supporters injured.

The Federal Government has suspended pedestrian bridge construction on the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway after a gridlock that stranded motorists for more than 20 hours.

Meanwhile, the Central Bank of Nigeria’s cash mop-up has more than doubled ahead of the 2027 elections while Lagos court has restrained Jimmy Odukoya from acting as senior pastor of the Fountain of Life Church.

The recap in full:

‘Bamayi’ chant sparks violence as suspected thugs attack APC convoy in Gombe

An anti-All Progressives Congress slogan, “Bamayi” (meaning rejection or lack of support), triggered a confrontation in Kwarge, Balanga Local Government Area of Gombe State.

The APC campaign convoy travelling from Cham to Bambam was attacked after youths emerged chanting the slogan.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/bamayi-chant-sparks-violence-as-suspected-thugs-attack-apc-convoy-in-gombe/

FG suspends Lagos-Ibadan bridge work after 20-hour

The Federal Government has suspended construction of a pedestrian bridge at the Aseese area of the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway after a diversion caused a traffic jam that lasted more than 20 hours from Wednesday afternoon into Thursday.

Motorists were stranded for hours, many abandoned their vehicles and trekked, while commercial operators hiked fares sharply.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/fg-suspends-lagos-ibadan-bridge-work-after-20-hour-gridlock/

CBN’s cash mop-up more than doubles ahead of 2027

The Central Bank of Nigeria withdrew more than N90tn from the financial system through Open Market Operations in the first 11 months of 2026, more than double the N40.9tn absorbed in 2025, as it seeks to contain excess liquidity and inflationary pressures ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Monetary Policy Committee members and analysts have warned that election-related spending could inject substantial funds into the economy and complicate disinflation efforts.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/cbns-cash-mop-up-more-than-doubles-ahead-of-2027/

ASUP strike rocks four S’West states

The Academic Staff Union of Polytechnics, Zone C, has directed all its chapters in state-owned polytechnics, colleges of technology and monotechnics in Ogun, Oyo, Osun and Ekiti states to embark on industrial action over the non-implementation of the Consolidated Academic Tools Allowance and Earned Academic Allowance.

The strike took effect on Monday, November 16, after a 15-day ultimatum expired.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/asup-strike-rocks-four-swest-states/

Court restrains Jimmy Odukoya from acting as Fountain of Life pastor

The Lagos State High Court has restrained Jimmy Odukoya from holding himself out as the senior pastor and spiritual head of the Fountain of Life Church.

Justice O. O. Adewunmi-Oshin issued the order in a suit by the church’s Incorporated Trustees, who argued that he ceased to hold the position following the late founder Pastor Taiwo Odukoya’s appointment of a successor in line with the church’s constitution.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/court-restrains-jimmy-odukoya-from-acting-as-fountain-of-life-pastor/

Mob kills Osun accident survivor, police hunt suspects

Osun State Police have condemned jungle justice after a mob attacked and killed one of the occupants of a Toyota Camry involved in a fatal accident in Ilesa that claimed the lives of two pedestrians.

The vehicle was also set ablaze.

Read the full story below: https://punchng.com/mob-kills-osun-accident-survivor-police-hunt-suspects/
