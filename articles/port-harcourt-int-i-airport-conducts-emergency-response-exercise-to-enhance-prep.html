Port Harcourt Int’I Airport Conducts Emergency Response Exercise To Enhance Preparedness

By Channels TV Channels TV 5d ago 3 min read

Photo: Channels TV

The Port Harcourt International Airport has conducted an emergency response exercise to assess its preparedness and response capacity in handling aircraft emergencies in line with regulatory requirements of the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA).

The full-scale exercise, held on Wednesday, involved emergency responders, including firefighters, security personnel, medical teams and the airport control unit.

The simulation featured an aircraft emergency that triggered an alarm, prompting responders to evacuate victims, extinguish the simulated fire, secure the affected area and transport injured persons to the airport hospital for treatment.

Speaking during the exercise, Lydia Ezike, Regional Manager, South South, Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN), said the exercise was designed to test the adequacy of the airport’s emergency plan, response capabilities and coordination among relevant agencies.

“We are conducting a full-scale emergency mock exercise in compliance with the regulatory requirements as stipulated by NCAA to test the adequacy of the airport emergency plan and procedures.

“We are testing the response capability of the primary emergency responders, as well as the communication and coordination with stakeholders,” she said.

Ezike added that the exercise would be followed by an evaluation by regulators and further simulations to equip personnel with the technical knowledge required to respond effectively to emergencies involving aircraft within the airport and its eight-kilometre radius.

She advised passengers and airport users to observe safety signs and comply with instructions from airlines and airport authorities to ensure safe flights and a positive travel experience.

Also speaking, the Airport Chief of Security, Ukaegbu Stephen, said the essence of the exercise was to prepare them to respond effectively in the event of an emergency.

He said the emergency responders demonstrated promptness in responding to the simulated incident.

He explained that firefighters arrived promptly, while aviation security personnel secured the area and controlled access to ensure an orderly response.

General Manager in the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority, Chukwu Emmanuel, who observed the exercise, described emergency preparedness as essential to airport safety.

He said accidents could occur without warning, stressing the need for continuous training and preparedness among airport personnel.

“The thing about accidents is that it doesn’t announce itself. What we do is to make sure that we get the personnel ready as to how they will respond in a situation where there is an emergency occurrence, which is either an incident or an accident,” he said.

Emmanuel added that emergency preparedness should not be limited to any particular period or season, noting that the airport must maintain the highest safety standards at all times.

He said regular training, emergency preparedness meetings and other safety programmes were necessary to ensure personnel understood their responsibilities during emergencies.
