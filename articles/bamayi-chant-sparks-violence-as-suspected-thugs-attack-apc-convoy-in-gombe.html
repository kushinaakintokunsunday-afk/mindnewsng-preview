‘Bamayi’ chant sparks violence as suspected thugs attack APC convoy in Gombe

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

An anti-All Progressives Congress slogan, “Bamayi”, reportedly triggered a violent confrontation between youths and APC supporters in Kwarge, Balanga Local Gove

 The incident occurred at about 5pm on Wednesday when the APC campaign convoy was travelling from Cham to Bambam as part of its campaign tour of Balanga South ahead of the 2027 general elections.

 Arewa PUNCH gathered that trouble started when some youths reportedly emerged from their homes and began chanting “Bamayi” as the campaign convoy approached the community.

 “Bamayi” is a local expression meaning rejection, disagreement or lack of support.

 An eyewitness, who spoke on condition of anonymity for security reasons, said the situation became tense when the youths began chanting the slogan at the passing convoy.

 “The youths came out in large numbers and started chanting ‘Bamayi, Bamayi’ as the convoy was passing. The situation quickly became tense, and before long, there was a confrontation between them and some youths accompanying the campaign team,” the eyewitness said.

 Another source familiar with the incident said the confrontation escalated into an attack on the convoy, with some of the alleged attackers wielding clubs and cutlasses.

 The source added that five Gombe Line buses conveying APC supporters were damaged during the violence, while several party supporters sustained injuries.

 However, no death was recorded in the incident.

 The circumstances surrounding the confrontation and the identities of those responsible for the attack remained unclear as of the time of filing this report.

 Wednesday’s incident occurred on the second day of the APC campaign tour of Balanga, following the official flag-off of the party’s campaign at Bajoga, headquarters of Funakaye Local Government Area, on Saturday.

 Meanwhile, Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya has led APC candidates and other party leaders on visits to the palaces of the Polo of Daadiya, Abdulkadir-Abubakar Galadima, and the Ndu of Cham, James Chachi, to seek their support ahead of the 2027 elections.

 The campaign team also visited Jessu-Nyuwar, Kindiyo, Mwona and Bambam for the commissioning of Local Council Development Areas.

 Yahaya expressed confidence in the party’s electoral prospects, saying the state would record greater development with the calibre of candidates fielded by the APC.

 “Together, we will achieve more success for our dear state,” the governor said.

 The APC Deputy Chairman, Baba Abuja, also urged residents of Nyuwar to support the party at the polls, saying, “From top to bottom, you should vote APC.”

 Similarly, the APC candidate for Balanga South State Constituency, Melton Yahaya, commended the electorate and expressed confidence in the party’s victory.
