Barcelona 2-1 Atletico Madrid: Fermin Lopez Double Comes From Behind, Ends Atletico's Seven-Game Winning Run

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 9 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

Fermin Lopez scored twice as Barcelona came from behind to beat Atletico Madrid 2-1 in a top-of-the-table LaLiga clash, with Lee Kang-in involved in Atlético's

Barcelona came from behind to beat Atlético Madrid 2-1 in a fiercely contested top-of-the-table LaLiga meeting at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano on Sunday night, with a double from Fermín López proving decisive on a night that ended Atlético’s impressive run.

How the match unfolded

  • 10th minute — Atlético lead: Giuliano Simeone finished from close range after a swift switch of play; reports credit Atlético playmaker Lee Kang-in for the pass that started the move before Alvarez delivered the low cross. Supply of passes in the build-up has been attributed differently across match reports, but the goal was officially assisted by Alvarez.
  • 45th minute — Barcelona equalise: Pedri’s driving run pulled Atlético out of shape; after the ball deflected into Fermín López’s path, he lashed a first-time shot inside the post.
  • 66th minute — Barcelona win it: Lamine Yamal threaded a ball from the right side of the box, and López fired a first-time finish past Oblak for the winner.

The aftermath

The result ended Atlético’s seven-match winning streak in the league and marks a second consecutive defeat in all competitions after their home loss to Bayern Munich in the Champions League earlier in the week. Atlético drop to third on 28 points, behind Real Madrid on 30. Barcelona top the table on 33 points with 11 wins from 12, restoring a three-point lead going into the international break.

Lee Kang-in’s night

Atlético’s attacking midfielder Lee Kang-in started alongside Julián Alvarez in a front two, playing 60 minutes before being replaced by Alexander Sørloth. While he did not register a goal or official assist, his outward-of-the-boot switch of play created the moment that led to the opener, and Spanish reports called his early influence a “catalyst” before his presence faded in the second half.

Why it matters

Barcelona’s win — and Real Madrid’s victory — sets up a tight title race heading into the international break, with all three of Spain’s big title contenders separated by just three points.
