Amnesty demands probe as tourist accuses Daura emir of sexual assault

By Punch Punch 2d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerians have reacted with mixed feelings after a female tourist accused the Emir of Daura, Faruk Umar Faruk, of touching her inappropriately during a visit to

The allegation, which has sparked debate on social media, followed the circulation of a video appearing to show the traditional ruler interacting with the tourist during her visit.

The woman, who identifies herself as @beduinqa, made the allegation in a post on Instagram on Saturday after completing a 30-day tour of Nigeria.

She said she waited until she left Nigeria before making the claim public.

According to a series of posts on her Instagram, the tourist was on a world tour with Nigeria as her 184th of 197 destinations.

She announced her arrival on October 19, 2026 and documented visits across the country, including a trip to the Oba of Benin’s palace on October 24.

She had also posted a video of her attendance at the 10th coronation anniversary celebration of Oba Ewuare II on October 25, time at the Kala Kuta shrine and Makoko in Lagos, as well as stops in Kano, Katsina and Bauchi, where she met the Emir of Bauchi at his palace.

Recounting her experience at the Daura palace, the tourist said she was taken into an elegant room where she sat with the Emir and had a conversation with him.

She alleged that the traditional ruler held her hand and began playing with it before hugging and kissing her on the cheek.

She further alleged that he touched her breasts, an action she said shocked and disturbed her.

In her Instagram post, she wrote, “That was a shock for me. I told him ‘emir no, no, no, no’ ‘emir you cannot be touching’, but he was just laughing about it.”

She said she had travelled through different parts of Nigeria without experiencing sexual assault in public spaces, making the alleged incident at the palace particularly disturbing.

“I wasn’t sexually assaulted at any moment when walking through streets of Nigeria, even in busy crowded places, but to my shock, I was assaulted in a palace by a traditional leader who should be behaving in exemplary and dignified way,” she wrote.

The tourist described the experience as disgusting, adding that she had continued to relive it in her thoughts.

She said the incident happened on Saturday, November 14, but she waited until she had left Nigeria before making the allegation public.

She also suggested that other women might have experienced similar treatment from the Emir, although she did not provide evidence to support the claim.

“I suspect I wasn’t first woman he assaulted but probably first one with a proof recorded. I have meta glasses and quickly turned them on,” she added.

The allegation has generated reactions on X, with several users expressing outrage and calling for accountability.

Amnesty International Nigeria called on the authorities to investigate the allegation, describing it as deeply disturbing.

In a post on its X account, the human rights organisation said the Katsina State Government and the police should conduct a transparent and independent investigation to ensure accountability.

It said, “The Nigerian authorities must immediately and effectively investigate strong allegations of sexual assault on a female foreign tourist by the Emir of Daura Faruk Umar Faruk.”

Amnesty added that the incident presented an opportunity for the government to demonstrate that nobody was above the law.

Another X user, identified as @mairowashere, described the alleged incident as appalling and expressed relief that the tourist had recorded the encounter.

“What a shame. Truly appalling. I’m really glad the woman managed to get it on video because, had she simply come forward to say she was assaulted, people would have demanded proof, questioned her account or dismissed her altogether,” the user wrote.

Similarly, @Bashir_GS called on the Katsina State Government to consider removing the Emir, describing him as unfit to occupy the throne.

Another user, @Nawas_masood, said the allegation had damaged the reputation of the traditional institution.

“It is really painful to watch this. The Emir of Daura has done serious damage to the dignity and standing of the Northern Emirates. What hurts most is that the damage goes beyond one emirate. It affects the image of our traditional institutions and the respect they have earned over the years,” the user wrote.

As of the time of filing this report, neither the Daura Emirate Council nor the Katsina State Government had issued an official response to the allegation.

PUNCH Online could not independently confirm the claim.

Meanwhile, a source in the emir’s palace has denied the allegation

“There was nothing like that. She visited the palace, but how she got that information cooked up still amused us in the palace.

“Meanwhile, an official statement will be released, while I advise you should not write the story or wait for the official statement,” the source, who spoke on condition anonymity because he was not authorised to speak to the press, told our correspondent by telephone on Sunday.

When contacted, the Katsina State Commissioner for Information, Culture and Tourism, Dr Bala Zango, said he had not seen the video and would watch it before responding.

“I have not seen the video. But, let me watch the video and call you back,” he said.
