Sweden Names 2 Jobs That Can No Longer Get A Work Permit - And A New Salary Rule Every Applicant Must Beat - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket) 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket)

Sweden's Migration Agency says forest berry pickers and personal assistants can no longer qualify for a work permit at all, while every other applicant must now

Sweden has confirmed that two occupations can no longer qualify for a work permit at all, no matter how good the job offer or the salary attached to it. The Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket) says forest berry pickers and personal assistants have been removed from the list of jobs that can support a work permit.

The two excluded jobs

  • Forest berry pickers - occupational subgroup 9210 under Sweden's SSYK 2012 classification. The exclusion took effect on 1 June 2026.
  • Personal assistants - subgroup 5343, also excluded since 1 June 2026.

There is a narrow exception on berries: other forms of berry picking inside the same occupational subgroup may still be considered, and a berry picker hired by an employer established in Sweden can still apply for a seasonal work permit instead.

The bigger change most applicants miss

The two exclusions came in with a far wider reform: a new salary floor of 90 per cent of the Swedish median wage. For applications received since 16 June 2026 that works out at SEK 34,470 a month, based on a median of SEK 38,300.

Twenty-seven occupation groups are exempt from the full 90 per cent rule and instead sit at 75 per cent - SEK 28,725 a month. They include:

  • IT operations technicians, IT support and network technicians, systems administrators
  • Assistant nurses, care assistants, childcare and ambulance care assistants
  • Laboratory engineers, and chemistry/chemical engineering technicians
  • Welders, maintenance mechanics, electrical distribution technicians
  • Butchers, food-industry machine operators, forestry workers and farm animal caretakers

Extensions are next: from 2 December 2026, applications to extend an existing permit must also meet the 90 per cent rule.

What the rules require in practice

  • A signed employment contract, with pay and conditions at least equal to Swedish collective agreements or normal practice in the profession
  • Health, life, industrial-injury and occupational pension insurance taken out by the employer before you start
  • The job advertised in Sweden and across the EU/EEA and Switzerland for at least 10 days before hiring
  • A first permit applied for from outside Sweden (with limited exceptions), granted for up to two years
  • An application fee of SEK 2,200, paid by the applicant

What it means for Nigerians

Neither excluded job was a realistic route for most Nigerian applicants - berry picking is largely seasonal work drawing on Asian and Eastern European labour, and personal assistant roles are low-paid care work. The part that actually matters is the salary floor: a Swedish employer must now pay roughly SEK 34,470 a month (about 75 per cent of that for the exempt trades) before your application can succeed.

That pushes Nigerian applicants towards the exempt occupations - IT support, nursing and care work, welding, maintenance and laboratory roles - or towards well-paid specialist jobs that clear the full threshold. The strongest applications also come with recognised qualifications, verified experience and, increasingly, Swedish or strong English language ability.

Warning: Swedish work permits are applied for by the employer through the Migration Agency's e-service, and the agency charges a published fee only. Any agent promising a Swedish work permit for a "processing fee" - or offering a berry-picking contract as a fast route - should be treated as a red flag.

Bottom line: Sweden still wants foreign labour, but only where the job pays properly. Two occupations are now closed completely, and everyone else has to clear a salary line set by the national median wage. Check the exact figure on migrationsverket.se on the day you apply, because it is reset every June.
