Abiodun demands arrest of Odegbami’s attackers

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Ogun State Governor, Dapo Abiodun, has condemned the attack on former Super Eagles captain and the state’s Sports Ambassador, Segun Odegbami, urging the police

In a statement released on X by his media aide, Emmanuel Ojo, Abiodun expressed deep concern over the incident, describing the attack on the former Super Eagles captain as unfortunate, unacceptable, and a threat to the safety of citizens.

He also expressed relief that Odegbami survived the incident.

“Abiodun, however, thanked God that the assailants were unable to silence Chief Odegbami, noting that it was a matter of relief that the celebrated sports icon survived the attack,” the statement said.

The governor described the former international, popularly known as Mathematical, as an outstanding Nigerian whose contributions to sports, particularly football, had earned him national and international recognition.

He noted that Odegbami occupied a special place in the state’s sports development agenda as its Sports Ambassador.

“His experience, influence and enduring commitment to the promotion of sports remain invaluable,” the statement added.

Abiodun urged security agencies, particularly the police, to conduct a thorough investigation into the incident, track down the perpetrators and ensure that they faced the full weight of the law.

“Abiodun urged the security agencies, particularly the police, to conduct a thorough investigation into the incident, track down the perpetrators, and ensure that they face the full weight of the law.

“He stressed that no individual or group should take the law into their hands, warning that acts of violence and criminal intimidation would not be tolerated in Ogun State,” the statement concluded.

The governor also wished Odegbami continued strength and good health, assuring him of the state government’s concern and support.

He reiterated his administration’s commitment to protecting lives and property and ensuring that Ogun State remained a peaceful and secure environment for residents and visitors.

Odegbami, 74, survived an attack by three armed men at his residence in the Ibara Government Reservation Area, Abeokuta, in the early hours of Friday.

The football legend, in a message to a close associate, described the incident as an assassination attempt and the closest he had ever come to death.

He said the attackers broke down his door at about 2:30am, adding that he fought them with the assistance of his driver and domestic worker before they fled.

“Thank the Creator of the Universe for me. I survived an assassination attempt. This morning at 2:30am, three unknown persons came to the house to steal and kill. I fought back with the help of God.

“My driver and house boy joined me, and we fought them before they fled. It was the closest I have ever been face to face with death. They broke down my door to get to me. I have been to the hospital,” Odegbami said.

He sustained deep machete wounds during the struggle and was taken to an undisclosed hospital for treatment before being discharged.

The Ogun State Police Command confirmed the incident and said its Commissioner of Police, Bode Ojajuni, had visited Odegbami and ordered an investigation and manhunt for the attackers.

The command’s spokesperson, DSP Oluseyi Babaseyi, said, “The command is leaving no stone unturned in addressing the incident. The Commissioner of Police immediately ordered a thorough investigation into the circumstances surrounding it, with intensive efforts ongoing to identify and apprehend those responsible. We can also confirm that Chief Segun Odegbami has been discharged from the hospital.”

The police had yet to establish the motive for the attack as of the time of filing the report.

Odegbami played a key role in Nigeria’s victory at the 1980 Africa Cup of Nations, scoring three goals to finish as joint top scorer. He also helped Shooting Stars of Ibadan win the African Cup Winners’ Cup in 1976.
