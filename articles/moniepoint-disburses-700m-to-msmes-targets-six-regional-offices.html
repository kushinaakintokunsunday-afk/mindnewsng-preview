Moniepoint disburses $700m to MSMEs, targets six regional offices

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Moniepoint Microfinance Bank has disclosed that it disbursed over $700m in loans to micro, small and medium enterprises in 2025, as it unveiled plans to establi

The bank disclosed this in a statement issued on Sunday following the inauguration of its North Central regional headquarters in Abuja, the first of six offices planned across the country.

The expansion comes amid persistent difficulties in accessing formal credit among Nigerian small businesses, despite their contribution to employment and economic activity.

In an October 2026 research report by the Development Bank of Nigeria, it was disclosed that commercial bank lending to small-scale enterprises accounted for just 0.38 per cent of total private sector credit in 2025, compared with 27.04 per cent in 1992.

According to the latest statement, Moniepoint disbursed more than $700m, equivalent to approximately N1tn, to MSMEs last year, with 75 per cent of the beneficiaries accessing formal business credit for the first time.

“In 2025, Moniepoint disbursed over $700m (about N1tn) in loans to micro, small and medium enterprises. For three out of four of those borrowers, it was the first formal business credit they had ever accessed,” the statement read.

The bank said the lending programme formed part of its efforts to address financing constraints facing small businesses and expand access to financial services.

It disclosed that the Abuja headquarters, located on Dar-es-Salaam Street, Wuse 2, would serve as the operational hub for the North Central region.

The facility was inaugurated by Vice-President, Kashim Shettima, represented by the Minister of Finance and Coordinating Minister of the Economy, Taiwo Oyedele.

Speaking at the ceremony, Shettima commended Moniepoint’s investment in Nigeria and its contribution to financial inclusion and business development.

“This investment aligns with the Federal Government’s priorities of financial and economic inclusion, agricultural development, and affordable financing for businesses, particularly small and medium enterprises,” the Vice-President said.

“These priorities are also connected. When a small business can receive payments reliably, keep proper records and obtain suitable financing, it has a better chance of expanding, employing more people and contributing to national prosperity.”

The bank disclosed that additional regional offices would be established in Kano, Kaduna, Port Harcourt, Ekiti and Ibadan.

It explained that the facilities would provide physical access to financial services, strengthen customer relationships and create employment opportunities in their host communities.

“With Abuja now open, regional offices in Kano, Kaduna, Port Harcourt, Ekiti and Ibadan are to follow. Each will serve as a base for its own region, and each will create new jobs within its host community.

“Together, the six offices will give Moniepoint a permanent physical footprint across key regions of the country, built on a network that already reaches all 774 local government areas,” the statement read.

The Managing Director of Moniepoint Microfinance Bank, Babatunde Olofin, said the expansion would complement the company’s digital banking infrastructure.

“Today, we are expanding beyond digital to give every Moniepoint customer a welcoming space to walk into for face-to-face support, and also to have contact with our business relationship managers,” Olofin said.

“By bringing our services closer to you, we are making banking more convenient, reliable and trustworthy than ever before. This is just the beginning. We plan to open more locations across additional states as we bring even better services closer to you.”

According to the bank, its services currently reach more than 30 million businesses and individuals, while its agency banking network and point-of-sale terminals cover all 774 local government areas.

It added that it had expanded its physical service network to more than 600 centres nationwide in 2026.

In a goodwill message, the Chairman of the Senate Committee on Banking, Insurance and Other Financial Institutions, Mukhail Abiru, commended the bank’s contribution to digital payments and MSME financing.

“Moniepoint has distinguished itself through its contribution to digital payments, financial inclusion and the growth of MSMEs,” Abiru said.
