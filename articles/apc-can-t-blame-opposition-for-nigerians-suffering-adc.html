APC can’t blame opposition for Nigerians’ suffering -ADC

From Ndubuisi Orji, Abuja

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) Presidential Campaign Council has said the All Progressives Congress (APC) cannot blame the opposition for public anger over hardship, challenging the ruling party to substantiate or withdraw allegations that it sponsors young Nigerians to disrupt campaign activities.

In a statement by its Director of Strategic Communication, Phrank Shaibu, the council denied sponsoring violence or recruiting youths to confront political opponents, saying a party controlling the Federal Government should produce evidence of alleged offences and submit it to investigators.

The ADC campaign accused President Bola Tinubu’s administration of worsening living conditions over the past three and a half years, arguing that Nigerians needed no prompting from the opposition to express dissatisfaction.

“For three and a half years, President Bola Tinubu has plucked the feathers from the Nigerian people with policies that have made food, fuel and basic survival painfully expensive. Now he scatters a little campaign grain and expects the wounded to follow him singing his praises.

“Nigerians are wiser than the chicken in that tale. They know who caused their pain, and they need no sponsor to tell him so,” it stated.

The council also questioned what it described as contradictory accounts from the Presidency and the APC campaign council over an alleged attack on the President’s convoy.

“The Presidency earlier denied that the President’s motorcade had been pelted. Now the APC campaign council says people were detained for pelting the convoy with stones and that President Tinubu directed their release. Which account should Nigerians believe?

“If the APC has evidence that the ADC armed or sponsored those people, why were those detained released? Why have the alleged sponsors not been identified and prosecuted? A government with evidence of such a serious offence should hand it to investigators, not fling accusations at its opponents,” it stated.

Calling for an impartial investigation into any alleged attack, the ADC campaign condemned violence against political parties and their supporters.

“We condemn attacks on campaigners, supporters, places of worship and public property, regardless of the party involved. Any alleged attack should be investigated impartially, and responsibility established through evidence,” it said.

The council also called for scrutiny of alleged inducements at campaign events, saying the ruling party should answer questions about its own campaign practices.

“Reports and images of money being distributed at campaign events deserve scrutiny. The ruling party cannot escape that scrutiny by claiming every crowd that refuses to cheer the President has been paid by the ADC.

“Nigerians are entitled to judge this government by the cost of living and by their own daily experience. Calling their anger an opposition plot will not make it disappear,” it stated.

Insisting that the APC must substantiate its allegations or withdraw them, the council said: “Tinubu cannot preside over years of hardship, offer Nigerians a handful of campaign inducements, and demand gratitude. The people know who pulled out the feathers. They will decide for themselves whether to follow him.”
