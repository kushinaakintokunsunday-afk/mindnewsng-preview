NSC Approves Postponement Of Enugu 2026 National Sports Festival To May 2027

By Channels TV Channels TV 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The National Sports Commission (NSC) has approved the postponement of the 23rd National Sports Festival, tagged the &lsquo;Coal City Games&rsquo;, to May 2027.T

The National Sports Commission (NSC) has approved the postponement of the 23rd National Sports Festival, tagged the ‘Coal City Games’, to May 2027.

The decision followed a request by the Local Organising Committee, which tendered an unreserved apology for its inability to host the event as scheduled from November 28 to December 12, 2026.

The LOC said the games were postponed due to situations beyond the control of the Enugu State Government and pleaded with the NSC to accept the new proposed date.

Director General of the NSC, Bukola Olopade, said though the postponement was regrettable, the commission would intensify its interface with the Enugu State Government to ensure that when the festival holds in May next year, it would go down as the best ever organised games in the country’s history.

Commissioner for Youth and Sports and Chairman of the Local Organising Committee, Lloyd Ekweremadu, said the postponement was a temporary setback but assured all relevant stakeholders, especially participating states and the media, that the new date will only yield better-organised games next year.

“On behalf of the LOC and the Governor of Enugu State, Peter Mbah, we want to apologise to all the states who have been preparing for this festival, and we regret the inconveniences this has caused,” said Ekweremadu.

“Although the contractors handling the projects expressed confidence that the facilities would be completed within the expected timeframe, the Enugu State Ministry of Youth and Sports and the LOC, after a careful assessment, observed that the level of work remaining raised concerns about the readiness of the stadium and Awgu Games Village to meet the required standards before the commencement of the Games.”

“The Ministry and the LOC wish to clarify that other essential arrangements and facilities, including accommodation and related logistics, are ready for the hosting of the festival. However, the completion and full readiness of the Stadium and Awgu Games Village remain critical to the successful hosting of the event.”

He also expressed commitment to upholding the global best practice the National Sports Commission has set for the Games, especially concerning sporting facilities

“The National Sports Festival is our own Olympics, and we are committed to upholding the global best practice the National Sports Commission has set for the Games, especially with regard to sporting facilities.

“The LOC, on behalf of the Enugu State Government, also wants to use this medium to appreciate the leadership of the National Sports Commission, NSC, who are the owners of the Games, for their understanding, and we are assuring that we are committed to staging world-class Games when it holds in May next year,” he added.
