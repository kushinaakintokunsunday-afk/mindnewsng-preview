S’West APC youths name advisory council chair

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The All Progressives Congress South-West Youth Leadership has appointed investment banking expert Adeola Olowe as chairperson of its Advisory Council to strengthen mentorship, strategic guidance, and youth development across the region.
The APC South-West Youth Leader, Adesuyi Daramola, said the initiative was a strategic step towards engaging accomplished individuals whose experience and credibility could provide additional guidance to young people beyond the mentorship available within the party.
According to a statement by the Media and Publicity Director, Office of the APC South-West Youth Leader, Remilekun Oyelowo, made available in Ado Ekiti on Sunday, Daramola spoke during the presentation of a certificate of appointment to Olowe.

He said, “The advisory council will help strengthen youth representation, develop leadership capacity and promote the effective implementation of programmes addressing the needs of young people across the South-West.”
According to the statement, Olowe is a finance professional with over 20 years of experience in investment banking and emerging financial markets.
It added that the appointment underscores the APC South-West Youth Leadership’s emphasis on mentorship, strategic engagement and leadership development as it seeks to strengthen youth participation and representation across the region.
Olowe, who thanked the APC South-West leadership for the appointment, described it as “an honour and a responsibility.”
The advisory council chair urged Nigerian youths “to believe in themselves, remain committed to hard work and contribute meaningfully to national development”.
While canvassing Nigerians’ continued support for the APC presidential candidate, President Bola Tinubu, in the January 16, 2027 election, Olowe described the president as a strategist whose record as Lagos State governor had shaped her confidence in his ability to plan for the country’s future.
The financial expert expressed support for the Tinubu administration’s economic reforms, saying the measures were intended to strengthen confidence in Nigeria’s economy.
She said, “I believe in Tinubu’s long-term vision, and I am confident in his approach to governance. Young Nigerians should remain committed to contributing to the country’s progress.”
