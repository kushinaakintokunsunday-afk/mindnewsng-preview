Photos: Don’t allow ADC, NDC score 25% in FCT, Wike tells Rainbow Coalition

By Omeiza Ajayi – ABUJA

Minister of the Federal Capital Territory FCT, Nyesom Wike, has told members of the Rainbow Coalition to make sure the African Democratic Congress ADC and the Nigeria Democratic Congress NDC do not get 25 per cent of the votes in the FCT in the January 16, 2027 general election.

Wike, coordinator of the FCT Presidential Campaign Council, spoke on Monday in Abuja at the inauguration of the All Progressives Congress APC Presidential Campaign Council/Rainbow Coalition FCT.

He warned the members against assuming the contest was already won, saying the election was a chance for the APC to show its political strength in the territory.

On how to sell the president’s record, he said;”You don’t need to go and see a prophet. You don’t need to go and see any doctor or anybody to tell you what to do. It is obvious; it is factual. Look at what Mr President has done for the people of the FCT. These are the tools you need to campaign”.

The minister disclosed that between 1999 and 2023, successive governments issued only about 7,000 to 8,000 Certificates of Occupancy (C of O), against more than 20,000 under President Bola Tinubu in three years.

“How can governments from 1999 to 2023 sign only about 8,000 C of Os, while the government of 2023 to 2026, in three years, has signed over 20,000? How?” he queried.

Wike also dared opposition politicians to set projects built before 2023 beside those delivered since Tinubu took office, arguing that the work going on in the city centre and the satellite towns spoke for itself.

On political inclusion, he said 50 years after Abuja was created in 1976, Tinubu is the first president to give the FCT a seat at the Federal Executive Council, so that the territory is no longer only the nation’s administrative capital but has a say in national decisions.

He listed the FCT Civil Service Commission, the appointment of a Head of Service, the promotion of FCT civil servants to permanent secretary rank, and the Mandate Secretariats for Women Affairs and Youth Development among the gains. He added that taking the FCT out of the Treasury Single Account arrangement now allows it to borrow for major infrastructure.

The minister urged council members to carry this record to wards, communities and polling units, especially in the satellite towns, where residents can see the projects for themselves.

The FCT APC Chairman, Ishaku Tanko Yamawo, said the party was set for an overwhelming win for Tinubu and its National Assembly candidates, and called the minister’s projects the party’s best asset.

“Your projects have already become our strongest campaign message. The goodwill and confidence you have earned among our people, both indigenes and residents of the FCT, are exceptional,” Yamawo told Wike.

He praised the Rainbow Coalition for bringing different political interests under one roof and urged members to work as a team so that the APC’s message reaches every ward and polling unit.

The FCT Peoples Democratic Party PDP Chairman, Ismaila Mohammed Dogara, said the structure Wike built could keep mobilising voters even if the minister is not in the territory on election day.

“Even if you are not in the FCT, our executive chairman does not allow any space, not even for one minute, before taking it from where you stopped. That shows that on the faithful day of the election, if the Honourable Minister is not in the FCT, those leaders are ready and capable of delivering Mr President, Senator Philip Aduda, Tanimu and others,” Dogara said.

He described the working relationship between APC and PDP leaders in the coalition as a significant political development.

“The big day is January 16, and the return day is January 17. By the special grace of God, we are going to return to this hall to celebrate,” he declared.
