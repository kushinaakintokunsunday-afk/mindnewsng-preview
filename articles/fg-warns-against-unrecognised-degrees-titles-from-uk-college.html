FG warns against unrecognised degrees, titles from UK college

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Federal Government has warned members of the public against obtaining unrecognised academic degrees and titles from the London College of Management and Lea

The National Universities Commission said the college was offering Executive Master’s Degrees, honorary doctorates, professorships and associate professorships for specified fees, with plans to confer the awards at a ceremony scheduled for December 11, 2026, at the NAF Conference Centre, Abuja.

The commission, in a statement on Tuesday signed by its Director of Public Affairs, Rita Nkonjo, said the college was not a recognised higher education institution in the United Kingdom and was not authorised to award recognised university degrees in Nigeria.

“The Commission wishes to state categorically that the College is not a recognised higher education institution in the United Kingdom, nor is it authorised to award recognised university degrees in Nigeria,” the statement read.

It explained that the college’s registration as a private company with the UK Companies House did not confer academic recognition or degree-awarding authority on it.

The NUC expressed concern over what it described as the commercialisation of academic qualifications and titles, noting that the college had advertised prices for professorships, associate professorships, honorary doctorates and other awards.

“The Commission is particularly concerned that the College is commercialising academic qualifications and titles, with advertised prices for Professorship, Associate Professorship, Honorary Doctorates and other awards,” it stated.

It stressed that professorship and associate professorship were academic ranks conferred by recognised universities on qualified academic staff and were not qualifications or titles to be purchased.

“The public is hereby informed that Professorship and Associate Professorship are academic ranks conferred by recognised universities on qualified academic staff and are not qualifications or titles to be purchased,” the commission added.

The NUC also said that, under its guidelines, honorary doctoral degrees could only be conferred by approved Nigerian universities that had graduated doctoral students, without payment of fees and through appropriate nomination procedures.

“Furthermore, under the NUC’s guidelines, honorary doctoral degrees may only be conferred by approved Nigerian universities that have graduated doctoral students, without payment of fees and through appropriate nomination procedures,” it stated.

The commission further faulted the college’s proposed arrangement to present the awards at the NAF Conference Centre and Suites, Abuja, through an unnamed Nigerian learning partner.

It said the use of a reputable public facility as a venue did not amount to endorsement, accreditation or recognition of the college or its awards.

“The use of a reputable public facility as a venue does not constitute endorsement, accreditation or recognition of the College or its awards,” the statement read.

The NUC warned that using unrecognised degrees and academic titles to seek employment, promotion or professional recognition in Nigerian universities and the public service was unacceptable and could attract appropriate consequences.

“The Commission warns that the use of such unrecognised degrees and academic titles to seek employment, promotion or professional recognition in Nigerian universities and the public service is unacceptable and may attract appropriate consequences,” it added.

The commission consequently advised members of the public, particularly academics, public office holders and other professionals, to disregard the awards being offered by the college.

“Consequently, the NUC strongly advises members of the public, particularly academics, public office holders and other professionals, to disregard the awards being offered by the London College of Management and Leadership Education,” it stated.

It also warned the public against applying for, paying for or participating in any of the college’s award programmes.

“Members of the public are warned against applying for, paying for or participating in any of its award programmes,” the statement added.

The NUC urged members of the public to consult its website, www.nuc.edu.ng, for guidance on recognised universities and the conferment of honorary degrees in Nigeria.

The statement ended with a warning: “Beware of degree mills and fraudulent academic titles! Do not apply, do not pay, do not be deceived.”
