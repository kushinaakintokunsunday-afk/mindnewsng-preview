2027: INEC selects 17,676 polling units for BVAS mock exercise

By Punch Punch 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Independent National Electoral Commission has released the list of polling units selected for its nationwide mock accreditation and result upload exercise s

The commission said the exercise would take place in only two polling units in each of the country’s 8,838 Registration Areas, with the selected locations published on its website for stakeholders and members of the public.

INEC announced the development in a post on its X handle on Monday, disclosing that the exercise would run from 8:30am to 2:30pm.

PUNCH had earlier reported that the commission fixed November 28 for the nationwide mock accreditation and voting exercise as part of its preparations for the 2027 general elections.

In its latest announcement, INEC said, “INEC will conduct a nationwide Mock Accreditation and Result Upload Exercise using the Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) on Saturday, 28 November 2026, from 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.

“The exercise is designed to test BVAS functionality, assess accreditation procedures and identify possible technical issues ahead of the 2027 General Elections.

“It will take place in two Polling Units in each of the 8,838 Registration Areas nationwide.”

The commission also directed the public to its website to access the selected polling units, adding, “Find the selected Polling Units here. https://x.com/inecnigeria/status/2124790206897193069?”

PUNCH Online reports that the figure is arrived at by multiplying the 8,838 Registration Areas nationwide by the two polling units selected in each area, bringing the total to 17,676 polling units.

The announcement comes amid concerns raised by political stakeholders over the preparation time for the exercise.

The African Democratic Congress Presidential Campaign Council had, on November 18, asked INEC to postpone the exercise, alleging that the short notice given to political parties would undermine their participation.

It described the timeline as inadequate and demanded that the commission postpone the exercise to allow political parties to prepare and participate meaningfully.

INEC’s mock exercise is expected to test the performance of BVAS and the commission’s electronic result upload procedures ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Meanwhile, in a separate report, INEC said that about 75 to 80 per cent of its Bimodal Voter Accreditation Systems, which amounts to 186,277 devices, has already been deployed to states ahead of the 2027 general elections.
