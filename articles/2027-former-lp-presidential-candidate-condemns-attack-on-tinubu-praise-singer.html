2027: Former LP presidential candidate condemns attack on Tinubu praise singer

From Idu Jude, Abuja

The former factional presidential candidate of the Labour Party (LP), Prince Kennedy Ahanotu, has strongly condemned the alleged physical attack on Dauda Kahutu, a popular Hausa musician and supporter of the All Progressives Congress (APC), widely known as Rarara, at the National Mosque in Abuja.

Prince Ahanotu, who is also the convener of the Renewed Hope Alliance of Labour Party Youth Leaders (RHALY), said in a statement over the weekend that his group considers the incident deeply disturbing, particularly at a time when Nigeria needs greater political tolerance, national unity and respect for the fundamental rights of citizens, irrespective of their political affiliations.

Rarara, it will be recalled, was allegedly attacked by disenchanted members who felt his support for the ruling APC and President Tinubu was deceptive.

The statement further read in part: “It is particularly unfortunate that a place of worship, which should represent peace, brotherhood and mutual respect, became the scene of such an ugly confrontation.

“We must make it clear that supporting President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, who belongs to the APC, or expressing one’s political convictions is a fundamental democratic right. Nobody should be assaulted, threatened, intimidated or subjected to public humiliation for exercising that right.

“However, our restraint must never be mistaken for weakness.

“APC supporters across Nigeria are millions of citizens with the capacity, organisation and democratic strength to defend their political interests. They equally have the right to demand protection and justice when their members are attacked. But that strength must be demonstrated through lawful, peaceful and democratic engagement, not retaliatory violence.

“We therefore caution those who may be encouraging political intimidation to understand that Nigeria belongs to all of us. No political group has a monopoly on the streets, and no individual or organisation has the right to deny another Nigerian the freedom to support a political party or candidate of their choice.

“RHALY will not encourage its members or any APC supporter to take the law into their hands. Equally, we will not remain silent in the face of intimidation and attacks against citizens whose only offence is their political preference.

“We call on the performing Inspector-General of Police, the FCT Police Command and other relevant security agencies to thoroughly investigate this incident, identify those responsible and ensure that justice is served. We also urge the government to strengthen the protection of political actors, campaign supporters and ordinary citizens as the country approaches the 2027 general elections.

“We appeal to APC supporters, particularly our youths, to remain calm, vigilant and law-abiding. Do not allow anyone to provoke you into actions that could undermine our collective democratic aspirations.

“Our commitment to President Bola Ahmed Tinubu’s Renewed Hope agenda remains firm. Our political conviction is not built on intimidation, and our determination cannot be weakened by violence.

“Nigeria must remain a country where political differences are settled through the ballot box, not through fists, threats or mob action.

“We demand justice for Rarara. We demand protection for every Nigerian. We demand an end to political intolerance.”
