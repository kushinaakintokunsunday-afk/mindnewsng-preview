Court joins Gwarzo in suit seeking to stop NDC from fielding governorship candidate in Kano

From Desmond Mgboh, Kano

The Federal High Court in Kano has joined the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) governorship candidate, Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo, as a defendant in a suit seeking to stop the party from fielding a candidate in the state’s governorship election.

Justice Simon Amobeda granted Gwarzo’s application on Monday, making him the third defendant in the suit instituted by three NDC members seeking to restrain the party from forwarding its governorship candidate’s name to the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC). The development comes amid a legal dispute over the party’s governorship ticket, following INEC’s omission of Gwarzo and his running mate, Mustapha Rabiu Kwankwaso, from its published list of candidates, which the commission attributed to a court order.

The plaintiffs, Garba Umar Isa, Yau Abubakar and another NDC member, are asking the court to prevent INEC from publishing Gwarzo’s name as the party’s governorship candidate, relying on a Supreme Court judgment in the Zenith Labour Party case. At Monday’s proceedings, counsel to the plaintiffs, Moses Ademola Falana, initially opposed the application filed by Gwarzo’s lawyer, Sagir Gezawa, seeking to join his client as a necessary party.

Falana, however, subsequently withdrew his counter-affidavit challenging the application.

Counsel to INEC, Hassan Aminu, and the NDC’s lawyer, Dahiru Ibrahim, raised no objection to Gwarzo’s inclusion in the suit. In his ruling, Justice Amobeda granted the application and formally joined Gwarzo as the third defendant, directing the parties to file the necessary court processes reflecting his inclusion.

The judge subsequently adjourned proceedings for the hearing of all pending preliminary applications.

The ruling allows Gwarzo to participate directly in the proceedings concerning the party’s governorship ticket, although the court has yet to determine the substantive dispute over his candidature.
