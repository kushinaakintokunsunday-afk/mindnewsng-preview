2027 Grammys: Davido, Asake, Tems, Burna Boy, Ayra Starr, Wizkid Earn Nominations

MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Afrobeats stars Davido, Asake, Tems, Burna Boy, Ayra Starr and Wizkid have earned nominations for the 69th Annual Grammy Awards, with Nigerian artistes featuring prominently across several categories.

The Recording Academy announced the nominations on Monday, with Nigerian artistes securing multiple nods in the Best African Music Performance and Best Global Music categories.

Tems leads the Nigerian contingent with four nominations, while Asake earned two nominations in the Best African Music Performance category.

Other Nigerian artistes recognised include Davido, Ayra Starr, Burna Boy and Wizkid, who shares a nomination with Asake.

In the Best African Music Performance category, Nigerian artistes account for four of the five nominated entries.

Asake earned nominations for his songs ‘Gratitude’ and ‘Jogodo’, his collaboration with Wizkid.

Tems was nominated for ‘Big Daddy’, while Ayra Starr received a nod alongside Beninese singer Angélique Kidjo for their collaboration, ‘Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)’.

The fifth nominee is Ghanaian musician King Ayisoba, whose song ‘No Tradition, No Africa’ features Rocky Dawuni.

Tems also received nominations in three other categories. She was recognised in the Best R&B Solo Performance and Best R&B Song categories for ‘What You Need’, as well as Best Global Music Album for her project, Love Is a Kingdom.

Ayra Starr earned another nomination in the Best Global Music Album category for Starrgirl, while Davido’s Oriadé was also nominated in the category.

Burna Boy received a nomination in the Best Global Music Performance category for ‘Dìá Dìá’, his collaboration with Colombian singer Shakira.

Other International Stars

British singer-songwriter RAYE emerged as the biggest winner among international artistes at the 69th Annual Grammy Awards, leading all nominees with 11 nominations. Her recognition includes Album of the Year for ‘This Music May Contain Hope’, as well as Record and Song of the Year for ‘Where Is My Husband!’ and Songwriter of the Year. Spanish singer Rosalía followed with nine nominations, including Album of the Year for ‘Lux (Complete Works)’ and Record and Song of the Year for ‘Berghain’, her collaboration with Björk and Yves Tumor.

Other major international contenders include American singer Olivia Rodrigo and country star Ella Langley, who each earned seven nominations, while British singer Olivia Dean secured six.

Phoebe Bridgers and Miranda Lambert also received seven nominations each, while Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas earned six apiece. Taylor Swift received five nominations, including recognition in the major categories for her album ‘The Life of a Showgirl’ and songs ‘The Fate of Ophelia’ and ‘I Knew It, I Knew You’.

Beyond the major categories, the nominations also highlighted the Grammy Awards’ growing international reach, with Tame Impala and South Korean singer JENNIE recognised for ‘Dracula’, while Thailand’s LISA and South Korea’s IVE earned nominations in the newly introduced Best Asian Pop Music Performance category.

Filipino groups BINI and SB19 also secured nominations, alongside Colombian star Karol G, who featured among the contenders in the inaugural Best Latin Song category.
