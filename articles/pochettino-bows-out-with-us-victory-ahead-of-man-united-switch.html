Pochettino bows out with US victory ahead of Man United switch

By Punch Punch 6d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Mauricio Pochettino ended his tenure as head coach of the United States men’s national team with a 2–0 victory over Haiti in the second leg of their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final on Tuesday.

Christian Pulisic and 18-year-old Julian Hall scored in the first half as the United States completed an 8–0 aggregate victory over Haiti at TQL Stadium in Cincinnati, securing qualification for the Nations League semi-finals.

The match was Pochettino’s final assignment with the American national team before he takes up his new role as Manchester United head coach on Wednesday, November 18.

According to The Washington Post, Pulisic opened the scoring in the 25th minute with a bicycle kick after receiving a cross from Weston McKennie.

Hall doubled the hosts’ advantage in the 43rd minute, controlling another pass from McKennie before directing the ball past Haiti goalkeeper Alexandre Pierre.

The victory followed the United States’ 6–0 win in the first leg played in Miami on November 14.

Pulisic scored three goals across the two matches, while Hall continued his scoring run with his fifth goal in six appearances for the national team.

The Washington Post reported that Pochettino received warm applause from 24,001 supporters at TQL Stadium after two years in charge of the Americans.

The 54-year-old Argentine finished his tenure with a 23-win, 12-loss and two-draw record, according to the newspaper.

NBC Sports reported that Pochettino spoke about his departure and the challenge awaiting him at Manchester United after Tuesday’s match.

On his new appointment, he said, “We are excited. I think the new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenaline you want to feel.”

He also described his feelings about leaving the American team, saying, “My feelings are, difficult to find my feelings.”

He added, “I think it was our dream two years ago, to play in this [amazing] way. … I think the identity of the team, the way the play, they really believe that now we are playing how we want to play. That is my best present that I can receive from the players.”

He further said, “I’m going. So happy about that, but so sad because I leave a group of amazing players, amazing people, all the staff, the people here.”

Pochettino’s departure

The United States Soccer Federation announced Pochettino’s departure on November 13, confirming that he would leave his position after completing the team’s November international camp to become Manchester United’s head coach.

In its statement, the federation said, “Mauricio Pochettino will depart his role as Head Coach of the U.S. Men’s National Team following the conclusion of the current camp to become Head Coach of Manchester United.”

It added that Pochettino and his staff would remain in charge for the two scheduled matches against Haiti on November 14 and 17.

U.S. Soccer CEO and Secretary General JT Batson said, “Mauricio has left a lasting mark on U.S. Soccer through his leadership, professionalism and commitment to our players and culture.”

He added, “What we are most proud of goes beyond the wins. He and his staff raised standards, strengthened our culture and created clearer connections from the Youth National Teams to the senior team.”

Pochettino was appointed USMNT head coach in September 2024 and led the team to the 2025 CONCACAF Gold Cup final and the round of 16 at the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Manchester United appointment

PUNCH had earlier reported Pochettino’s appointment as Manchester United’s new head coach on November 13, following the club’s decision to part ways with Michael Carrick.

In the report, PUNCH stated that the Argentine would take charge from November 18 after reaching an agreement with the United States Soccer Federation to complete his responsibilities with the national team.

His arrival in Manchester was scheduled after the Haiti fixture, allowing him to oversee both legs of the Nations League quarter-final before beginning work at Old Trafford.

NBC Sports reported that Pochettino was expected to travel to Manchester after Tuesday’s match to begin his new assignment on Wednesday.

He is expected to have a few days to work with his new squad before Manchester United’s Premier League trip to Liverpool on Sunday, November 22.
