Kebbi APC rally: Tinubu approves dry inland port for Kebbi, seeks support for APC in 2027

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 6 min read

Photo: The Sun Nigeria

_______________________________________________________________________

From Romanus Ugwu, Ola Muhammad, Birnin Kebbi

President Bola Tinubu has approved the establishment of a Dry Inland Port in Kebbi State, appealing to the state’s electorate to vote, from top to bottom, for all the candidates of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in the 2027 general elections.

Tinubu gave the approval on Tuesday during the APC presidential campaign rally held at Haliru Abdu Stadium, Birnin Kebbi, attributing the approval to requests by the Kebbi State government.

He added that his administration remained committed to the peace, stability and development of the state and the North West.

His words: “I understand your request for a dried inland port, and it is approved for Kebbi State. I have listened carefully to the Kebbi Governor, and we are working together. What I want to ask the youths of great Kebbi is: Are you ready to go? Do you have your Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs)?”

The President expressed confidence that the people would support the APC in next year’s elections, urging them to vote for the party’s candidates at all levels.

“I know you are ready to vote for all APC candidates, from top to bottom. May Almighty Allah bless you all,” he said.

While appreciating the people for the massive turnout and warm reception, President Tinubu said that their show of love, prayers and support had deeply touched him.

He acknowledged the economic challenges facing Nigerians, particularly the rising cost of living, but assured them that his administration was working to deliver tangible benefits through its ongoing reforms.

“To our farmers, traders, mothers and hardworking families, I hear you. I know the cost of living remains a burden, and I am determined to see our reforms bring real relief to your homes.

“More affordable food, better incomes for our farmers, jobs for our young people and opportunities for every child. That is my commitment to you,” he said.

Tinubu described the 2027 general election as a defining moment for the country’s future, urging Nigerians to sustain the reforms initiated by his administration.

He said the election would determine whether the country built on the foundations already laid or returned to what he described as policies that had brought Nigeria close to the brink.

“We have confronted difficult problems, dismantled harmful policies and laid the foundations for a more secure and prosperous Nigeria. We have come too far to turn back. There is more work to do, and I am determined to see it through,” he said.

The President also thanked Allah for granting him the opportunity to serve the country in his first term, saying he was seeking the people’s renewed mandate to continue his administration’s programmes.

Speaking earlier, the APC Presidential Campaign Council (PCC) Director-General, Senator Abdulaziz Yari, thanked the people for their impressive turnout, describing it as a strong demonstration of support for Tinubu’s re-election bid.

Yari said: “No previous administration had contributed as much to infrastructure development in northern Nigeria as the Tinubu administration. The establishment of the Ministry of Livestock Development and the proposed Sokoto-Badagry Superhighway are major initiatives expected to promote economic growth in the region.

“I urge Kebbi residents not to be misled by opposition parties. Tinubu is committed to addressing the country’s security challenges,” he said.

Also speaking, former state governor and the senator representing Kebbi Central Senatorial District, Muhammad Adamu Aliero, assured the President that the state would deliver votes for all the APC candidates in the 2027 elections.

Aliero commended Tinubu for visiting the state and pledged the support of Kebbi residents for the party at both national and state levels.

The Director-General of the Kebbi APC Campaign Council, Saidu Dakingari, argued that successive administrations had contributed to the state’s development, while commending Idris for his achievements.

“I urge the electorate to sustain the current administration because abandoning an administration that had initiated development projects could undermine ongoing progress. You cannot leave someone who has started bringing development and go for someone who does not know where to start,” he said.

In his remarks, Senator Abubakar Atiku Bagudu, Minister of Budget and Economic Planning, argued that Kebbi is more united in its support for Tinubu’s re-election than it was during the 2023 general elections.

Bagudu recalled that stakeholders in the state had endorsed Tinubu two years earlier, describing the rally as a reaffirmation of their commitment to his leadership.

He said the President had facilitated infrastructure projects, particularly roads, worth more than N2 trillion in Kebbi within three and a half years.

“We are facing the 2027 election more united than we did in 2023, and this is as a result of your magnanimity and hard work. You have helped us deliver a more prosperous Kebbi,” he said.

Bagudu added that the President’s inclusive leadership and support for the state had strengthened unity among political stakeholders and deepened their commitment to the APC.

Earlier, the Kebbi State Governor, Nasir Idris, assured President Tinubu of Kebbi’s total support and 100 per cent of the votes for the APC in the 2027 general elections.

Idris, who described the massive turnout at the rally as evidence of the people’s confidence in the President’s leadership, said the state had consistently supported the APC and would not disappoint the party in the forthcoming elections.

He said the people of Kebbi were satisfied with the Tinubu administration’s Renewed Hope Agenda and deserved another four years to consolidate its achievements.

The governor commended the Federal Government for supporting farmers, increasing the minimum wage, creating opportunities for youths and investing in roads, schools, hospitals and other critical sectors.

He also acknowledged the contributions of security agencies, including the military, police and other paramilitary organisations, towards protecting lives and property, while pledging continued collaboration with the Federal Government to strengthen security across the state.

Idris said ongoing road construction and other infrastructure projects across Kebbi were evidence of the commitment of APC-led governments to delivering democratic dividends.

He dismissed attempts by some individuals on social media to portray a contrary picture of the rally, saying the massive crowd at the stadium demonstrated the people’s genuine support for Tinubu and the APC.

The Speaker of the House of Representatives, Tajudeen Abbas, also canvassed support for Tinubu and Idris, describing the President as humble and hardworking.

Abbas, who disclosed that he holds a traditional title in Kebbi, said his experience at the National Assembly had exposed him to successive administrations, but maintained that he had not seen a President as responsive to the needs of ordinary Nigerians as Tinubu.

He urged the people to support Idris for another term, arguing that the governor’s leadership was necessary to sustain development in the state.
