NUPRC Opens 2026 Oil Licensing Round, Offers 40 Oil, Gas Blocks To Investors

By Channels TV Channels TV 2d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) has opened its 2026 oil and gas licensing round, offering 40 blocks across onshore, shallow water

The Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission (NUPRC) has opened its 2026 oil and gas licensing round, offering 40 blocks across onshore, shallow water and deep offshore terrains to prospective investors.

The commission announced that the bidding portal went live on Friday, November 20, with registration scheduled to close on January 15, 2027.

According to a statement signed by the NUPRC Head of Corporate Communications and Media, Eniola Akinkuotu, the blocks comprise 17 onshore, 14 shallow water and nine deep offshore assets.

The commission urged interested investors to visit the portal to access the complete list of available blocks, bidding guidelines and frequently asked questions.

It said the portal was designed to provide users with a seamless experience throughout the licensing process.

The 2026 licensing round will be conducted in five stages: registration and prequalification, access to data, submission of technical and commercial bids, technical evaluation, and the commercial conference.

The exercise was first announced on October 6, 2026, by the Commission Chief Executive, following approval by President Bola Tinubu, who also serves as Minister of Petroleum Resources.

The announcement was accompanied by statutory advertisements in local and international newspapers, in accordance with the provisions of the Petroleum Industry Act.

With the registration portal now open, the next major activity is the pre-bid conference scheduled to hold in Lagos on December 16, 2026.

The NUPRC said it would provide further information and updates on the licensing exercise through the bidding portal and its official social media platforms.
