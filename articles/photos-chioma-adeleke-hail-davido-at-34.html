PHOTOS: Chioma, Adeleke hail Davido at 34

By Punch Punch 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Wife of Afrobeats star, David Adeleke, popularly known as Davido, Chioma Adeleke and his uncle, the Osun State Governor, Ademola Adeleke, have celebrated the si

Chioma, in a birthday message shared on her Instagram page, described the singer as her forever home.

She wrote, “Thank you for your love, for our family and all that you are. I love you, my FOREVER HOME. Happy birthday DADA.”

Adeleke, in a post on his X page, also praised his nephew’s global success, his support for the family and his commitment to the development of Osun State.

The governor wrote, “Happy 34th birthday to my beloved nephew, David Adeleke #davido! I celebrate your remarkable achievements, your unwavering support for our family and your commitment to the progress of Osun State.”

He added, “Beyond your global success in music, your generosity and humanitarian spirit continue to inspire many.”

Adeleke also prayed for the singer, saying, “May God bless you with greater accomplishments, good health, divine protection and many more fruitful years.”

The governor ended his message with, “Keep shining and making us proud. Happy birthday, David.”

PUNCH Online reports that Davido announced that his foundation would provide startup funds to Nigerians with business ideas as part of his birthday celebration.
