Jaiz Bank, REA seal N100bn renewable energy deal

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Jaiz Bank Plc has signed a N100bn financing agreement with the Rural Electrification Agency to support renewable energy infrastructure development and expand el

The agreement brings the agency’s total local commercial financing commitments for renewable energy projects to N450bn, as the Federal Government seeks greater private sector participation in addressing the country’s electricity access deficit.

According to a statement issued by the bank on Wednesday, the Memorandum of Understanding was signed in Abuja on 17 November, 2026, as part of efforts to attract domestic financing for sustainable electricity infrastructure.

The statement read, “Jaiz Bank Plc, Nigeria’s pioneer non-interest bank, has reaffirmed its commitment to Nigeria’s infrastructure development and improved quality of life through a N100bn financing Memorandum of Understanding signed with the Rural Electrification Agency to support renewable energy development and expand electricity access across the country.”

Speaking at the signing ceremony, the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Jaiz Bank, Haruna Musa, said the bank’s commitment to infrastructure financing was driven by the need to support critical development projects and improve the lives of Nigerians.

He explained that investments in energy infrastructure had significant implications for economic development, business expansion, employment generation and the wellbeing of communities.

Musa noted that the bank considered infrastructure financing an important means of creating economic opportunities and supporting sustainable development.

He added that Jaiz Bank would continue to support projects capable of delivering measurable economic and social benefits, particularly in communities facing electricity access challenges.

The agreement was signed with the Managing Director of the Rural Electrification Agency, Abba Aliyu, and is expected to support the deployment of renewable energy solutions across the country.

The statement added, “The N100bn MoU, signed on November 17, 2026, further strengthens Jaiz Bank’s commitment to sustainable infrastructure financing and the deployment of renewable energy solutions as part of efforts to bridge Nigeria’s electricity access gap.

“The agreement also raises local commercial financing commitments mobilised by the Rural Electrification Agency for renewable energy projects to N450bn, representing a significant boost to domestic funding for electricity infrastructure and energy access initiatives.”
