Keeping my father’s succession video was a mistake – Jimmy Odukoya

By Punch Punch 5d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The embattled Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, Jimmy Odukoya, has explained why he retained a 2021 video in which his late father, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his preferred successor, despite allegedly being instructed to delete the recording after recovering from surgery.

Odukoya gave the explanation while addressing workers of the church on Wednesday, amid the leadership crisis that has resulted in a faction of its Board of Trustees withdrawing recognition of him as Senior Pastor, according to Church Times.

The publication reported that its correspondent was present at the meeting, where Jimmy addressed about 300 church workers.

Jimmy said the video was recorded in 2021 when his father was preparing to undergo surgery in the United States and feared he might not survive the procedure.

He said, “That was when he asked me to record him where he made the statements about succession. I was the one who recorded the video,” adding that his father’s elder brother, Pastor John Odukoya, was also aware of the recording.

Explaining what happened after the operation, Church Times quoted him as saying, “He said, however, that after the surgery turned out successful, his father asked him to delete the video as it was no longer valid. Rather than delete the video, Jimmy kept it, admitting that it was a grave mistake on his part.”

The publication further reported that he said, “He added that only a handful of people were privy to the video, including his sisters, whom he alleged released it to the public.”

Giving further insight into the leadership dispute, Jimmy said the disagreement with the board started about a year after he assumed office, when members allegedly asked him to relinquish his position as Chairman of the Board of Trustees and concentrate on his pastoral responsibilities.

According to Church Times, he said, “That was a hard pill for him to swallow. He said he sensed that what the board wanted to do was to control him, insisting that he would not allow that to happen.”

The report also quoted him as alleging that his sister, Pastor Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, had approached him and asked him to step down so she could become the board’s chairman.

Jimmy said the dispute was taken before the General Overseer of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, Pastor Enoch Adeboye, following interventions by Bishop Mike Okonkwo.

Recounting the meeting, he said, “When we got to Pastor Adeboye, I was there, and I watched every board member lie against me to my face.”

According to the report, Adeboye allowed him to explain his side of the disagreement before advising the parties to resolve their differences. He also reportedly reminded Jimmy that he had been called to preach the gospel and not to be chairman of the board, warning that if they failed to resolve the disagreement, “it will not be goodbye, it will be bye-bye.”

He further stated that the board, which included Pastors Kunle Areogun and Rotimi Okpaise, presented him with a seven-point demand, but he only agreed to four of the conditions.

On allegations of financial misconduct, Jimmy denied claims that he embezzled N250m donated to the church for a project.

According to Church Times, he told the workers that the money remained intact, explaining that the project had been delayed because of the economic situation and rising costs, which made its execution difficult.

The report added that he said he had restricted access to church funds and argued that if there had been financial wrongdoing, members of the accounts department would also be culpable.

Jimmy maintained that he remained the Senior Pastor of the church, although he expressed uncertainty about how the next Sunday service would proceed. He also said an advertised church programme would not hold because he did not want the crisis to escalate.

The development comes after a faction of the church’s Board of Trustees announced on Tuesday, November 17, that it had withdrawn recognition of Jimmy as Senior Pastor with immediate effect and appointed Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader.

The faction cited a December 2021 succession video in which Taiwo named Areogun as his preferred successor.

In the recording, the late founder said, “In case of my demise, which I don’t expect to happen soon, or a situation where I cannot write or talk, please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun.”

He also designated Jimmy as Areogun’s immediate administrative assistant and head of the youth church.

PUNCH Online had reported that the trustees claimed they only became aware of the video in late December 2025, more than two years after Taiwo’s death, and that it was not presented during Jimmy’s appointment in September 2023.

However, a statement from Jimmy’s camp rejected the board faction’s decision as constitutionally invalid, insisting that he remained the duly appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees.

Taiwo founded The Fountain of Life Church in 1992 with his first wife, Pastor Bimbo Odukoya. Following his death on August 7, 2023, Jimmy was announced as Senior Pastor in September and formally installed on September 30, 2023, while his sister, Tolu, was named Associate Senior Pastor.

The competing claims have left the church embroiled in a leadership crisis, with both factions insisting on the legitimacy of their positions.
