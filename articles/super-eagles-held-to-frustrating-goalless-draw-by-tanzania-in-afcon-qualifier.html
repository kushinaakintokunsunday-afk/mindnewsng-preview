Super Eagles Held To Frustrating Goalless Draw By Tanzania In AFCON Qualifier

By Channels TV Channels TV 17 Nov 2026 2 min read

Photo: Channels TV

Nigeria’s Super Eagles missed an opportunity to strengthen their Africa Cup of Nations qualification campaign after being held to a frustrating 0-0 draw by Tanzania on Tuesday.

Despite enjoying greater possession and producing more attempts on goal, Eric Chelle’s side lacked the cutting edge to break down the hosts, settling for a point in a contest that offered plenty of effort but little attacking reward.

Nigeria made an encouraging start, with Moses Usor having an effort blocked inside the opening 15 minutes before Fisayo Dele-Bashiru tested Tanzania’s defence with a shot from outside the penalty area.

The Super Eagles continued to push forward, with Tolu Arokodare firing wide in the 35th minute as Tanzania struggled to contain Nigeria’s attacking advances.

However, the hosts remained competitive, relying on their physical approach and occasional attempts to threaten the Nigerian defence. Kibu Denis and Iddi Selemani both had opportunities after the restart but failed to find the target.

Super Eagles resumed the second half with the same urgency, but the breakthrough remained elusive. Akor Adams missed an opportunity in the 70th minute, sending his effort from the centre of the penalty area wide.

With time running out, the Super Eagles intensified their search for a winner. Arokodare came close again with a left-footed effort from inside the box, but his attempt also missed the target.

That moment appeared to wake Tanzania up, as they also hit the post immediately following a speculative shot from the edge of the area.

Alhassan Yusuf subsequently attempted to release the striker with a through ball, only for the move to be halted by an offside decision.

The final minutes produced no reprieve for Nigeria, as Tanzania held firm to secure a share of the points.

However, that numerical advantage counted for little as the Super Eagles failed to turn their territorial dominance and attacking opportunities into goals.

The Super Eagles would be hoping Madagascar could cause an upset against Guinea-Bissau in Group L to keep them in the running
