2027: Deny opposition 25% of votes in FCT, Wike tells Tinubu campaign council

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Minister of the Federal Capital Territory, Nyesom Wike, has charged members of President Bola Tinubu’s campaign council in the FCT to prevent opposition parties

Wike gave the directive on Monday while inaugurating the 466-member All Progressives Congress Presidential Campaign Council and Rainbow Coalition in Abuja.

He specifically identified the African Democratic Congress and the Nigeria Democratic Congress as opposition parties the campaign team must prevent from securing the required percentage of votes in the FCT.

The minister said the council’s responsibility went beyond winning the election, insisting that it must deliver an overwhelming victory for Tinubu and APC candidates contesting National Assembly seats.

“We’re not talking about winning; we’re talking about winning overwhelmingly.

“We must not allow them to go with 25 per cent. They don’t deserve to get 25 per cent,” Wike told the gathering, which included council members, party supporters and members of the Peoples Democratic Party.

Dismissing claims by opposition figures about their political strength ahead of the election, Wike rejected suggestions that the ADC controlled the political space in northern Nigeria or stood a chance of taking the FCT.

He also challenged former Rivers State Governor, Rotimi Amaechi, over opposition remarks concerning an APC rally in Port Harcourt, urging him to test his party’s popularity in Rivers State.

“The FCT is for no other party other than the APC,” the minister declared, warning campaign council members that they would be held responsible for the electoral outcome in the territory.

Wike urged the campaign team to sell the achievements of the Tinubu administration rather than make promises, citing changes in the administration of the FCT since 2023.

He listed the appointment of an FCT representative to the Federal Executive Council for the first time since Abuja’s creation in 1976, the establishment of Mandate Secretariats for Women and Youth, and the creation of the FCT Civil Service Commission among the administration’s achievements.

He also cited the appointment of the first Head of Service for the FCT and the opportunity for career civil servants in the territory to attain the rank of permanent secretary.

On land administration, Wike said the removal of the FCT from the Treasury Single Account had enabled the territory to access financial markets directly to fund capital projects.

He added that more than 20,000 Certificates of Occupancy had been signed within three years, compared with approximately 8,000 issued between 1999 and 2023.

The minister further listed the elevation of traditional rulers, construction of road networks and police stations, and educational projects, including the proposed University of Science and Technology in Abaji, as part of the administration’s interventions across satellite towns and rural communities.

Wike commended the APC and PDP leadership in the FCT for setting aside their political differences to establish the Rainbow Coalition in support of Tinubu’s re-election and APC National Assembly candidates.

The candidates include Senator Philip Aduda, seeking election to represent the FCT in the Senate; John Gabaya, contesting the AMAC/Bwari Federal Constituency seat; and Danladi Chiya, the Abuja South Federal Constituency candidate.

He urged other states to adopt the alliance model, which he said was also being practised in Rivers and Akwa Ibom states.

The minister directed the campaign council to establish committees across the FCT’s area councils and take its message to markets, suburbs, traditional rulers, indigenes and non-indigene associations, while maintaining peace and complying with electoral laws.

Earlier, the FCT APC Chairman, Ishaku Yamawo, commended the party leadership for promoting political cooperation across party lines.

Yamawo described the developmental projects executed under Tinubu’s Renewed Hope agenda as the party’s major campaign message, saying, “The people can see the transformation taking place.”

He also described Wike’s appointment as coordinator of the FCT Presidential Campaign Council as a strategic decision, citing his grassroots mobilisation capacity.

The FCT PDP Chairman, Mohammed Dogara, also praised Wike for bringing rival political camps together, describing the alliance between APC and PDP leaders in the territory as unprecedented.

Using a local proverb, Dogara commended the minister for “bringing the cat and the rat to build the house and live in the house without any issues.”

He assured Wike that local council chairmen and grassroots leaders could mobilise voters across the territory, expressing confidence that the coalition would deliver victory on January 16, 2027.

“Come 17th of January, we are going to rejoice and celebrate the resounding victory,” he said.
