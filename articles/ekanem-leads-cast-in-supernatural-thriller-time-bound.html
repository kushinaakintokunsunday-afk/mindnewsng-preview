Ekanem leads cast in supernatural thriller, Time Bound

By Punch Punch 3d ago 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nollywood actor John Ekanem has taken the lead role in Time Bound, a supernatural thriller exploring themes of death, destiny, love, betrayal and second chances

The film, according to a statement made available to Sunday Scoop, blends mystery, suspense and drama in a storyline centred on a man whose life takes an unexpected turn following a mysterious curse, an untimely death and an extraordinary second chance at life.

Driven by unsettling events and hidden secrets, the protagonist finds himself caught between destiny and survival as he struggles to reclaim his life and confront forces threatening his existence.

The statement described the film as an exploration of betrayal, human relationships and difficult choices that can alter the course of people’s lives.

Ekanem stars alongside Sonia Uche, Ichie Fuego and Blessing Onwukwe in the production, which explores the thin line between life and death and questions whether people can escape destinies beyond their control.

The film combines suspense, emotional twists and supernatural elements to sustain viewers’ interest as the mystery unfolds.

It also explores the cost of love and sacrifice, raising questions about whether a second chance at life is always a blessing.
