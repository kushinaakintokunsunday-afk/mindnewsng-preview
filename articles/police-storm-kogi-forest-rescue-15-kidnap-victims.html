Police storm Kogi forest, rescue 15 kidnap victims

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The police have rescued 15 kidnapped victims during a joint security operation in Magaru Forest, Kakun-Kabba, Kogi State, following a fierce gun battle with the

The operation, conducted in the early hours of Wednesday, involved personnel of the Kogi State Police Command, the Nigerian Army, Forest Guards, vigilante groups and other local security outfits.

The kidnappers reportedly engaged the security operatives in a gun battle as they advanced into the forest. However, the superior firepower of the joint team forced the criminals to retreat, with some sustaining gunshot injuries.

The Kogi State Police Public Relations Officer, ASP Saliu Oyiza Afusat, disclosed this in a statement on Thursday.

She said two other female victims, identified only as Joy and Nwakan, escaped from their abductors during the initial attack in which the victims were kidnapped.

According to the statement, the security team came under another ambush while evacuating the rescued victims from the forest.

However, the operatives repelled the attack and successfully brought all 15 victims out of the forest.

The rescued victims were subsequently debriefed and taken to St. John’s Catholic Hospital, Kabba, for medical attention after enduring physical and psychological distress during their captivity.

The police spokesperson said no suspects had been arrested, but actionable intelligence had been generated to track down the fleeing members of the kidnapping gang.

She added that investigations were ongoing to apprehend the suspects and identify their sponsors.

The Commissioner of Police, CP Naziru Bello Kankarofi, commended the participating security agencies and local security outfits for their collaboration, reiterating the command’s commitment to tackling kidnapping and other violent crimes in the state.

The command urged residents to provide credible, timely information to help security agencies fight criminal activities.
