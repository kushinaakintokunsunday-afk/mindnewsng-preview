A foreign tourist has accused the Emir of Daura in Katsina State, Umar Faruk Umar, of sexual assault during a visit to the monarch’s palace.

The tourist made the allegation in a post shared on Instagram by @beduinqa on Saturday, where she recounted what she described as a disturbing experience during her visit to Daura.

According to her account, the incident allegedly occurred on November 14 when she visited the emir’s palace. She claimed she was taken to an “elegant” room, where she and the monarch sat together on a couch and began a conversation.

The tourist said the Emir grabbed her hand and started playing with it.

“It was strange for me that he grabbed my hand and started playing with it. Then when we stood up, he hugged me, kissed my cheek (which was also surprising for me), and then he grabbed my boobs,” the tourist wrote on Instagram.

“That was a shock for me. I told him ’emir no, no, no, no; emir you cannot be touching’, but he was just laughing about it.”

The tourist said she was shocked by the behaviour of the emir, adding that she was not sexually assaulted while walking through the streets of Nigeria, even in crowded places.

In the video published by the tourist, the emir appears to be holding the hands of a woman, while they were sitting on a couch.

In the video, the emir could be seen caressing the hand of the woman.

After some seconds, it appeared that the emir hugged the woman and touched a part of her body.

Immediately the woman responded: “Emir, no, no, no, why are you touching? Emir, you can’t be touching…”

The tourist said she recorded the video through meta glasses, an eyewear that has the capacity to take video and pictures.

A review of her Instagram page shows that she arrived Nigeria on October 19.

During her stay in Nigeria, she also visited the palace of the Oba of Benin in Benin city, Edo State, Makoko in Lagos, Bauchi, and Kano
