2027: Atiku promises cheaper fuel, stronger purchasing power

By Punch Punch 1h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar and presidential candidate of the African Democratic Congress has vowed to restore fuel subsidy and improve the purchasing

Atiku also promised to strengthen local production of goods within the country.

The ADC presidential candidate stated this on Tuesday during his campaign rally in Kogi State, according to a statement issued by his Director of Strategic Communication, Phrank Shaibu.

The statement is titled ‘Atiku storms Kogi with Affordable Life message as mammoth crowd chants Tinubu Must Go.’

Atiku stated that his ‘Affordable Life campaign’ is targeted at families and people who can no longer afford to buy food items at costly prices and the high cost of transportation.

He said, “When we say we will make life affordable again, we are speaking to the parents counting the last notes in a purse and the worker calculating whether a salary will last until month’s end.

“They came here in their numbers because they want to be heard. Their hardship is real, and their hope deserves an answer.”

The statement noted that the former VP “said his Affordable Life campaign is a promise to those families: to bring down the cost of fuel, help Nigerians produce and sell their goods, and restore the value of what people earn.”

Atiku stated further that the people of Kogi can not “endure pain with no end in sight” as they chanted “Tinubu Must Go.”

“The people of Kogi are chanting ‘Tinubu Must Go’ because they are tired of being told to endure pain with no end in sight. I hear them. I see the faces in this crowd, and I know what is at stake. No family should have to beg for the chance to live with dignity in its own country.

“A mother should not have to choose between feeding her children and paying their school fees. A trader should not open a shop each morning wondering whether anyone can afford to buy. A young person should not work hard all day and still struggle to pay for the journey home. These are the lives behind the figures this government recites,’ the ADC presidential candidate said.

He thanked the people of the state for the welcome, as he urged supporters to keep maintaining peace.

“Let no one mistake your restraint for acceptance of hardship. Let your voices be heard, and let your votes decide Nigeria’s future,” he urged.

The 2027 campaign has been underway for some weeks now as presidential candidates continue to take their promises and manifestos to selected states nationwide.

Atiku is seeking the presidency for the fourth time as a candidate in a general election, having previously contested in 2007, 2019 and 2023.

His running mate is former Rivers State Governor, Rotimi Amaechi.

Another top opposition challenging President Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress is Peter Obi, the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Cingress.
