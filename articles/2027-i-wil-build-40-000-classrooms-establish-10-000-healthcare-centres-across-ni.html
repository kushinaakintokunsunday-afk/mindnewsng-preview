2027: I wil build 40,000 Classrooms, establish 10,000 Healthcare Centres across Nigeria if elected – Obi vows

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Peter Obi, has promised to establish 10,000 primary healthcare centres and construct 40,000

The presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), Peter Obi, has promised to establish 10,000 primary healthcare centres and construct 40,000 primary school blocks across Nigeria if elected president in the 2027 general election.

Obi said the proposed projects would expand access to healthcare and education, particularly in rural communities and underserved areas across the country.

The former Anambra State governor made the promises on Friday during an NDC town hall meeting and homecoming event held at the Main Bowl of the Archbishop C.J. Patterson Building, All Saints Cathedral, Onitsha, Anambra State.

Speaking on his healthcare plans, Obi said his administration would ensure that every ward in Nigeria had access to a functional primary healthcare facility.

He noted that Nigeria has 8,809 wards but maintained that his proposed target would exceed that number, with plans to establish 10,000 primary healthcare centres.

“We will ensure that there’s a functional primary care in every ward in Nigeria. We have 8,809 wards, but we have promised Nigeria that we’ll do 10,000 primary care. That’s what we are going to do,” he said.

On education, the former governor expressed concern over the country’s out-of-school children crisis, citing an estimated 18 million children currently outside the classroom.

He pledged to construct 40,000 primary school blocks as part of efforts to improve access to basic education and reduce the number of children who are unable to attend school.

Obi said the impact of the proposed intervention would begin to become visible from the first day of his administration.

“What goes on, I have said, is that we have 18 million out-of-school children. From day one, people will see the reduction because we plan to do 40,000 primary school blocks. And we will do that,” he stated.

The NDC candidate added that his proposed administration would prioritise investment in education, healthcare and public safety, insisting that government resources must be directed towards improving the lives of ordinary Nigerians.

“What we are promising you is not a fake. We will invest in education. We will invest in health. We will invest in building that society that you’ll be proud of, that society that will care,” he said.

Obi’s appearance in Onitsha formed part of his political engagements ahead of the 2027 general election. The event followed a roadshow through parts of the commercial city and a visit to his former school, Christ the King College, Onitsha.

The former governor, who led Anambra State from 2006 to 2014, was the Labour Party’s presidential candidate in the 2023 election before joining the NDC to pursue his 2027 presidential ambition.

He is running with former Kano State Governor Rabiu Kwankwaso as his running mate.

The proposed healthcare and education projects form part of Obi’s campaign promises, although details of their funding, implementation timelines and delivery arrangements were not provided in the announcement.
