Kogi 2027: Yahaya Bello says he will defeat Natasha Akpoti ‘overwhelmingly’

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Former Kogi State Governor, Yahaya Bello, has expressed confidence that he will defeat Senator Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan in the 2027 Kogi Central senatorial elect

Former Kogi State Governor, Yahaya Bello, has expressed confidence that he will defeat Senator Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan in the 2027 Kogi Central senatorial election, predicting an overwhelming victory.

Bello made the declaration during an interview on Channels Television’s Politics Today, where he was asked whether Akpoti-Uduaghan’s popularity and political activities posed a threat to his chances at the polls.

The former governor dismissed the possibility of a close contest, claiming that his political standing across Kogi State was far ahead of that of other politicians, including those from Kogi Central.

“Yahaya Bello and any other politician in Kogi State today, not even Kogi Central, let me say Kogi State, our polls are far apart when it comes to issue of contest,” he said.

Asked whether he remained popular despite being away from active political office for almost four years, Bello said his absence had further strengthened his popularity.

“That even added to my popularity,” he responded.

He also declined to directly compare himself with Akpoti-Uduaghan, saying he did not want to give undue recognition to other potential contestants.

“I can only equate any other person with maybe my third generation or fourth generation mentee politically, as far as this contest is concerned,” he said.

When asked if he was confident of winning the election, Bello replied, “Overwhelmingly.”

He attributed his confidence to what he described as the trust and loyalty of the people, claiming that his supporters were already campaigning for him without waiting for financial incentives.

According to him, the level of support became evident during his recent homecoming to Kogi State, when residents donated farm produce, food items and money to encourage him to represent them in the Senate.

Bello recalled a political tradition involving a former leader in the area, Elijah Abdul Kukuri Ujo Chere, whose supporters reportedly contributed food and money during election periods to encourage his political participation.

He said a similar show of support occurred during his own homecoming.

“As I speak now, I will not spend one dime in the campaign and election. And by the grace of God, you see me at the Red Chambers,” he said.

The former governor described himself as a political role model to people in Kogi State, particularly in Kogi Central, insisting that his record in office had earned their continued support.

“I am their political idol. I did not mislead them. I did not betray them. I did not fall their hands,” he said.

Bello also said he intended to remain in the All Progressives Congress and support President Bola Tinubu’s policies and programmes, which he claimed were being felt by people at the grassroots.

His remarks come as he prepares to challenge Akpoti-Uduaghan, the incumbent senator representing Kogi Central, for the senatorial seat in the 2027 election.
