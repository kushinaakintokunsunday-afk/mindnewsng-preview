Army decorates 32 newly promoted soldiers

By Punch Punch 21h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The General Officer Commanding, 10 Division of the Nigerian Army, Major General Chinedu Nnebeife, has urged 32 newly promoted soldiers to demonstrate greater de

Nnebeife gave the charge during the decoration ceremony of the soldiers, who were promoted in the Nigerian Army’s 2026 promotion exercise, with their new ranks ranging from Lance Corporal to Army Warrant Officer.

The Army disclosed this in a statement posted on its X handle on Tuesday and signed by the Acting Deputy Director, Army Public Relations, 10 Division, Aliyu Danja.

The GOC urged the beneficiaries to justify the confidence reposed in them by the Army, noting that promotion came with greater responsibilities and higher expectations.

He noted that “while promotion ultimately comes from God Almighty, their elevation was equally a recognition of their hard work, commitment and contributions to the Service.”

He reminded them to uphold the Nigerian Army’s core values of courage, discipline, selfless service, respect for humanity and dedication to duty.

Nnebeife said their elevation was not only a recognition of their hard work, commitment and contributions to the service but also a call to greater responsibility.

He congratulated the soldiers and urged them to regard their new ranks as an opportunity to demonstrate greater professionalism and loyalty to the Nigerian Army.

The GOC also acknowledged the sacrifices of the beneficiaries’ families, particularly their spouses, who often shoulder additional responsibilities while the soldiers are away from home on operational assignments and other military duties.

He commended them for their patience, resilience and continued support, describing their contributions as invaluable to the effectiveness and success of military personnel.

Speaking on behalf of the beneficiaries, Army Warrant Officer Emeka Ukandu thanked the Chief of Army Staff for approving their promotions and finding them worthy of elevation.

He assured the Army leadership of their renewed commitment to duty and determination to justify the confidence reposed in them.

Ukandu also appreciated the GOC for his leadership and encouragement, while acknowledging the sacrifices and support of their families.

He prayed for personnel awaiting promotion, expressing hope that they would be successful in subsequent exercises.

The 32 promoted soldiers comprised one Army Warrant Officer, two Master Warrant Officers, three Staff Sergeants, one Sergeant, eight Corporals and 17 Lance Corporals.

The ceremony was attended by commanders, staff officers, serving and retired military personnel, family members and well-wishers.

Highlights included the reading of the beneficiaries’ citations, decoration with their new ranks, words of advice, a vote of thanks and group photographs.
