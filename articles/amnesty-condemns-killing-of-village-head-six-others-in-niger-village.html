Amnesty condemns killing of village head, six others in Niger village

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Amnesty International has condemned the killing of seven people in an attack on Lumma Ba’are village in the Borgu Local Government Area of Niger State, calling on the authorities to do more to end escalating bloody attacks on villages and communities across parts of the state.

This is contained in a Monday statement shared by the rights group on X.

Amnesty said the brutal attack carried by gunmen widely referred to as bandits, which occurred around 5:00pm on Saturday, also resulted in many homes and shops getting burnt to ashes.

“The Nigerian authorities must do more to end escalating bloody attacks on villages and communities across some parts of Niger state,” Amnesty said.

The organisation stated, “The gunmen particularly targeted a mosque in which people were performing dawn prayers and killed the village head and other worshippers.

“This attack had torn the community apart, with many people still missing. The gunmen also kidnapped unspecified number of people.”

It added, “Clearly, the spate and scale of these attacks are indication that the Nigerian authorities have left rural communities in the north at the mercy of rampaging gunmen who have been killing people and burning down entire villages.”

Amnesty said terrifying attacks on rural communities in the north of Nigeria have been going on for years, describing the ongoing failure of security forces to take sufficient steps to protect villagers from these predictable attacks as utterly shameful.

“Gunmen and other armed groups have been intensifying bloody attacks on Borgu. The majority of the people in these communities depend on farming for their livelihoods, but they are now too afraid to go to the fields.

“The government has an obligation to protect its population. The rising death toll in the north of Nigeria shows just how badly the authorities are failing in this responsibility,” it further stated.
