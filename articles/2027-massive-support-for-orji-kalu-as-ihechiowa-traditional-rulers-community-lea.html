2027: Massive support for Orji Kalu as Ihechiowa traditional rulers, community leaders pledge unalloyed backing

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Senator representing Abia North Senatorial District, Orji Uzor Kalu, has received massive expressions of support from traditional rulers, community leaders,

The Senator representing Abia North Senatorial District, Orji Uzor Kalu, has received massive expressions of support from traditional rulers, community leaders, political stakeholders and residents of Ihechiowa Clan in Arochukwu Local Government Area of Abia State during his ongoing ward-to-ward consultation tour ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The former Abia State governor received overwhelming goodwill and renewed declarations of support as he visited various communities across Ihechiowa, where traditional rulers and other stakeholders acknowledged his constituency interventions and expressed confidence in his continued representation of Abia North.

The Monday engagements provided an opportunity for community leaders to appreciate his contributions to grassroots development while presenting additional demands for roads, educational facilities, potable water and other essential infrastructure.

Kalu began the tour with courtesy visits to Chief Eze Okali of Okpo Ihechiowa and Chief Otigba Kalu Chukwu, Eze Ogo of Achara Ihechiowa. Discussions during the visits focused on community development, rural infrastructure, education and the need for sustained government attention to rural communities.

The traditional rulers acknowledged the senator’s interventions, including the construction of rural roads and classroom blocks, provision of school desks and educational support programmes, including overseas scholarship opportunities.

At the various community gatherings, Kalu received warm receptions from constituents who commended his engagement with the grassroots and expressed support for his continued representation.

His engagements covered Okpor Village Square, Eleoha Ward 4; Achara Market Square, Eleoha Ward 3; Amafia Etitii Village Square, Eleoha Ward 2; and Umuchi Market, Eleoha Ward 1.

At Amafia/Amaetiti, he met with Chief Kalu O. Kalu of Amafia Ihechiowa and Chief Eke Torty, Eze Amaetiti, who highlighted the infrastructural challenges confronting their communities, particularly poor road networks, inadequate electricity and lack of potable water.

Responding to their concerns, the senator assured the communities that he would work towards incorporating their priority needs into the forthcoming budget, with particular attention to rural roads and educational infrastructure.

He also pledged to facilitate additional school projects, stressing the importance of education and accessible roads to rural economic development.

One of the notable endorsements came from Pastor Innocent Okoro, who commended Kalu’s activities at the National Assembly and his constituency development initiatives.

Describing the senator as one of the country’s performing legislators, Okoro declared the community’s readiness to support his continued representation.

“Senator Orji Uzor Kalu is among the best in the country. If others have performed like him, Abia North would be a paradise. We are going to give him our total support,” he declared.

The senator also visited Prince Abel Uche, Eze Umuakuma, and held further engagements with community stakeholders across Ikwun Wards 1 and 2, including Chief Okorie Amannukwu of Ama Mmiri.

During the meetings, community leaders acknowledged his interventions and pledged their support for his continued representation, while presenting additional development priorities for his consideration.

Addressing the people, Kalu called for support for his re-election bid, President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and other candidates of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in the 2027 general elections.

He also introduced Chief Ikenna Nicholas Ukwa, the APC candidate for Arochukwu/Ohafia Federal Constituency, and Prince Ike Okorafor, the party’s candidate for Arochukwu State Constituency.

Beyond the political and developmental engagements, the senator demonstrated solidarity with bereaved families in Ihechiowa.

He paid a condolence visit to Hon. High Chief Offor Okorie following the death of his mother, Mrs. Mercy Boniface Okorie, aged 67.

He also visited Austine Emeka Bonny, popularly known as Delazz Inc., of Ndi Okpo, Ihechiowa, to commiserate with him over his bereavement.

Kalu expressed his heartfelt condolences to both families and prayed for God’s comfort and strength for them during their respective periods of mourning.

Among the dignitaries accompanying the senator were Chief Ikenna Nicholas Ukwa, Prince Ike Okorafor, APC Chairman of Arochukwu LGA, Mazi Sunday Torty, Chief Smart Ukoha, Hon. Paul Uche and other party chieftains and community stakeholders.

The tour concluded at Ikwun Ward 1, where Kalu expressed appreciation to the people of Ihechiowa for their hospitality, goodwill and expressions of support.

He reiterated that his ward-to-ward engagements were designed to strengthen direct communication with constituents, assess ongoing projects and identify areas requiring further government intervention.

The widespread expressions of support from traditional rulers, community leaders and political stakeholders formed a major highlight of the visit, as communities used the opportunity to reaffirm their relationship with the senator while seeking further development interventions.

Kalu assured the people that their concerns would receive attention, pledging to sustain engagement with the communities and pursue projects aimed at improving infrastructure, education and the general welfare of residents.
