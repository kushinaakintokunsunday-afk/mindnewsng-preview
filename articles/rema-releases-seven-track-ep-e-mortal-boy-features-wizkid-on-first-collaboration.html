Rema Releases Seven-Track EP ‘E-Mortal Boy’, Features Wizkid On First Collaboration

By Channels TV Channels TV 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Nigerian Afrobeats star Rema has released a seven-track extended play (EP), ‘E-Mortal Boy,’ marking his first EP since ‘Ravage,’ released in 2023.

The project, also stylised as EMB, arrived on Friday through Mavin Global Holdings and Jonzing World Entertainment, with distribution involving Interscope/Universal Music Group.

The 22-minute project features a mix of previously released singles and five new songs, with its opening track, ‘Feel Alright’, featuring fellow Nigerian music star Wizkid.

The collaboration marks the first official musical outing between the two artistes, bringing together Wizkid, one of the leading figures in the global rise of Afrobeats, and Rema, who has emerged as one of the genre’s prominent contemporary voices.

‘Feel Alright’ is a record centred on romantic attraction and satisfying a lover’s desires. Produced by Thisizlondon and AoD, the song also credits both artistes as songwriters.

The collaboration has attracted attention as Rema’s first official track with any of Nigeria’s so-called Big Three, comprising Wizkid, Davido and Burna Boy.

Beyond the high-profile opening track, ‘E-Mortal Boy’ sees Rema continue his experimentation with different musical influences.

The EP includes ‘Oh No’ and ‘TEA’, which were released as singles in September and August 2026, respectively.

Other tracks include ‘Velvet’, and ‘TANZANIA’, featuring American producer BNYX. The latter draws on Bollywood-inspired sounds.

Rema also collaborates with French-Congolese singer Theodora on ‘SUGA’, a track with grunge influences, while the closing song, ‘Bang Bang’, features Nigerian-American singer Kah-Lo and producer Thisizlondon.

Longtime collaborator Thisizlondon, who has worked with Rema on songs including ‘Calm Down’ and ‘Soundgasm’, also has production credits across the project.

The complete tracklist comprises ‘Feel Alright’ featuring Wizkid, ‘Oh No’, ‘Velvet’, ‘TANZANIA’ with BNYX, ‘TEA’, ‘SUGA’ featuring Theodora, and ‘Bang Bang’ featuring Kah-Lo and Thisizlondon.

The release follows Rema’s 2024 sophomore album, ‘HEIS,’ which earned a Grammy nomination.

With ‘E-Mortal Boy,’ the singer delivers a compact project that revisits his experimental approach to music while bringing together a range of collaborators. The Wizkid collaboration, however, has become the project’s most significant talking point, given the artistes’ influence on different generations of Nigerian music.
