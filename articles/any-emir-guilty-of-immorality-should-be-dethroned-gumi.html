Any Emir guilty of immorality should be dethroned — Gumi

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Islamic cleric, Sheikh Ahmad Gumi, has called for the removal of any traditional ruler found guilty of immoral conduct, saying such action is necessary to prote

Gumi made the call in a Facebook post on Sunday amid the sexual harassment allegation against the Emir of Daura, Alhaji Faruk Umar Faruk, by a female foreign tourist.

The cleric said any emir found culpable of immoral conduct should be dethroned and immediately replaced.

“Any Emir found guilty of immoral conduct should be removed and replaced immediately to safeguard the integrity, dignity and credibility of the traditional institution,” he wrote.

The statement comes after a female tourist, identified on Instagram as @beduinqa, accused the Daura monarch of touching her inappropriately during a visit to his palace in Katsina State on November 14.

The tourist alleged that the monarch held her hand, hugged her and kissed her on the cheek before touching her breasts without her consent.

She said she objected to the alleged conduct but that the Emir laughed at her reaction. She later shared her account on Instagram after leaving Nigeria.

Posting the video of her encounter with the king, she wrote, “Recently I finished a 30-day journey exploring all around #Nigeria. I had amazing time, saw beautiful places, met great people. Unfortunately I had also a bad experience when visiting #daura in Northern Nigeria.

“During visit to the #emirofdaura palace I was taken to an elegant room where we sat on a couch and started talking. It was strange for me that he grabbed my hand and started playing with it.

“Then when we stood up, he hugged me, kissed my cheek (which was also surprising for me), and then he grabbed my boobs. That was a shock for me. I told him ’emir no, no, no, no’ ’emir you cannot be touching’, but he was just laughing about it.

“I wasn’t sexually assaulted at any moment when walking through streets of Nigeria, even in busy crowded places, but to my shock I was assaulted in a palace by a traditional leader who should be behaving in exemplary and dignified way.

“I felt disgusted by this experience, and have been reliving it in my thoughts. It happened exactly one week ago, on Saturday 14th November, but I waited until after leaving Nigeria to post it.

“I suspect I wasn’t first woman he assaulted but probably first one with a proof recorded. I have meta glasses and quickly turned them on.”

The allegation sparked reactions on social media, with some Nigerians calling for accountability.

Amnesty International Nigeria also called on the Katsina State Government and the police to conduct a transparent and independent investigation into the allegation, stressing that the Emir should be held accountable if found culpable.

However, a source in the Emir’s palace denied the allegation, saying there was nothing improper during the tourist’s visit.

The Katsina State Commissioner for Information, Culture and Tourism, Dr Bala Zango, told PUNCH Online on Sunday that he had not seen the video and would watch it before responding.

As of the time of filing this report, the Daura Emirate Council and the Katsina State Government had not issued an official statement on the allegation.
