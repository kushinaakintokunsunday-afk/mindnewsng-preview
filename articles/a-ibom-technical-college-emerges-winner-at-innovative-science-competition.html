A/Ibom technical college emerges winner at innovative science competition

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Government Technical College, Ewet Ofot in Uyo local government area in Akwa Ibom state has emerged winner of 2026 science , technology and innovation competiti

Government Technical College, Ewet Ofot in Uyo local government area in Akwa Ibom state has emerged winner of 2026 science , technology and  innovation competition organized by Vcare for development supported by Sterling Oil Exploration and Energy Production company and supervised by the state ministry of education . 

A statement signed by the Media and Communication Manager of Sterling Oil Exploration and Energy  Production company Aniekeme Finbarr and made available to Journalists in Uyo stated that the competition was part of Corporate Social Responsibility to host communities to haunt for talents in science and technology that provide practical solutions to daily community  challenges .

Finbarr said the competition targetted 4,186 students across 141 secondary schools in the state as students worked in groups to proffer solutions to technical problems facing their communities with teachers supervising them.

Finbarr listed projects by the students to include water distribution, renewable energy, flood monitoring and alternative heating systems at the grand finale of the competition where Government Technical Secondary School , Ewet Ofot emerged winner.

“The grand finale showcased innovative student projects addressing critical societal challenges, including water distribution, renewable energy, flood monitoring and alternative heating systems

” The projects demonstrated how scientific knowledge, creativity, critical thinking and teamwork can be translated into practical solutions to real-life problems”

He said since introduction of the programme in 2024, it has provided secondary school students with opportunities to advance beyond classroom theories through applications of science, technology, engineering and mathematics knowledge to tackle challenges within their communities.

“The 2026 edition commenced with orientation sessions designed to equip teachers with the knowledge and skills to guide students through hands-on learning, critical thinking, teamwork and problem-solving activities.

“The programme targeted  4,186 students across 141 secondary and technical schools in Akwa Ibom State

” Following STEM workshops and teacher orientation sessions, students worked in teams under the guidance of their coordinating science teachers to identify challenges in their communities, examine their possible causes and develop practical, technology-driven solutions

“The journey culminated in a grand finale where 14 selected schools, out of 75 funded projects, presented their innovations before judges and stakeholders”

Finbarr explained  that the competition attracted representatives of the state government,  education stakeholders, teachers and students, who gathered to celebrate the creativity, ingenuity and problem-solving abilities of the young innovators.

He said  the Permanent Secretary in  Ministry of Education, Akparawa Okon Effiong Tom, expressed delight at the programme’s growth and expansion reach across schools in the state and commended Sterling Oil and VCDF for encouraging students to develop interest in science and particularly apply their knowledge to tackle society challenges

“I want to sincerely thank Sterling Oil and Vcare for Development Foundation because you have encouraged our students to be interested in science subjects, not just to be interested but to take a step further by being practical”

Finbarr said the  students’ ingenuity was evident in the prototypes presented at the grand finale, with each project reflecting a deliberate attempt to address practical challenges through the application of science and technology.

” Government Technical College, Ewet, Uyo, emerged overall winner with Sharepump, an automatic water pump and power-switching prototype designed to regulate water supply and improve water distribution in homes and multi-storey buildings.

” While Secondary Grammar School, Ibeno, secured second place with its Footstep Energy Harvester, a prototype that converts the mechanical energy generated by footsteps into electrical energy, demonstrating the possibilities of alternative and renewable energy sources.

“Afaha Inang Secondary School, Onna, came third with Community FloodGuard, a flood-monitoring and protection prototype designed to help communities detect potential flooding, strengthen early-warning systems and respond more effectively to flood risks”.

He said one the the teachers  Ubong Sunday Ukpong in the winning team while reflecting on his experience while coordinating the students   said the journey involved challenges that required teamwork, collaboration and determination to overcome.

“We faced challenges, but we worked together to develop ideas, identify solutions and bring them to life

” We sincerely appreciate Sterling Oil and VCDF for providing this platform and giving our students the opportunity to develop practical solutions to societal problems,” he said.
