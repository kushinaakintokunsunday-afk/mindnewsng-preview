AFCON 2027: Super Eagles held to goalless draw by Tanzania

Nigeria’s Super Eagles were held to a 0-0 draw by Tanzania in their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying match in Zanzibar on Tuesday, missing the chance to consolidate their position in Group L.

The Eagles dominated much of the first half and created several scoring opportunities but were let down by poor finishing.

Tolu Arokodare came closest to breaking the deadlock in the 34th minute when his low effort beat the Tanzanian goalkeeper but struck the post.

Moses Usor also tested the hosts’ goalkeeper four minutes later, forcing him to go down and parry his effort, while Fisayo Dele-Bashiru fired wide from outside the penalty area.

Arokodare also failed to find the target with another attempt as Nigeria went into the break without a goal.

The hosts grew more adventurous after the restart, with Kibu Denis sending two efforts wide from the right side of the penalty area in the opening 10 minutes of the second half.

Seeking a breakthrough, Super Eagles coach Eric Chelle introduced Alex Iwobi and Akor Adams for Taiwo Awoniyi and Dele-Bashiru in the 61st minute.

Tochukwu and Alhassan Yusuf later replaced Wilfred Ndidi and Usor in the 73rd minute as Nigeria continued to press for a winner.

Adams and Bassey had opportunities in the closing stages, but neither could convert, while Arokodare also missed the target in the 85th minute.

The draw followed Nigeria’s 3-1 victory over Tanzania in Friday’s first leg at the Godswill Akpabio International Stadium in Uyo. Alhassan Yusuf opened the scoring before Arokodare struck twice in the second half.

Before Tuesday’s encounter, Nigeria had six points from three matches, one behind Group L leaders Guinea-Bissau, who had drawn 1-1 with Madagascar.

Tanzania have already qualified for the 2027 tournament as co-hosts, leaving Nigeria, Guinea-Bissau and Madagascar to contest the group’s remaining qualifying place.

The 2027 AFCON will be jointly hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda from June 19 to July 17.
