City Boy Movement dismisses Mas’ud Rahamaniyya as Sokoto director

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The national leadership of the City Boy Movement has removed Mas’ud Rahamaniyya from his position as the organisation’s Director in Sokoto State.

The decision, which took effect on Tuesday, was conveyed in an official letter issued on Wednesday and signed by the movement’s Director-General, Francis Shoga.

The letter, bearing reference number CBM/DG/SD.SOK/2026/700, was addressed to Rahamaniyya in Sokoto State, formally communicating the decision to relieve him of his appointment.

However, the movement did not disclose further details surrounding the decision in the information contained in the letter.

“I write on behalf of the national leadership of the City Boy Movement to inform you that you are relieved of your appointment as State Director of the Sokoto State Chapter with effect from 17 November 2026,” the letter stated.

The national leadership, however, clarified that the decision was purely administrative and should not be interpreted as a disciplinary action or an indication of any wrongdoing by the former state director.

It also expressed appreciation to Rahamaniyya for his contributions and commitment to the activities of the movement in Sokoto State during his tenure.

“On behalf of the movement, I wish to sincerely appreciate you for your service and the time and effort you devoted to the work of the Sokoto State Chapter during your tenure,” Shoga stated.

The letter further wished him success in his future endeavours.

The development comes as political activities gather momentum ahead of the 2027 general elections, with various support groups intensifying efforts to mobilise grassroots support for President Bola Tinubu’s re-election bid.

However, the movement had yet to announce a replacement for Rahamaniyya as of the time of filing this report.
