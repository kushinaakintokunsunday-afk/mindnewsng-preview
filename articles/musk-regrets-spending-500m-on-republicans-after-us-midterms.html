Musk regrets spending $500m on Republicans after US midterms

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read

Billionaire entrepreneur Elon Musk has said he felt “foolish” for spending nearly $500m on Republican candidates during the 2026 midterm elections after Democrats made significant gains and reclaimed control of Congress.

Musk made the admission during a virtual interview at the New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday, saying he would have spent less if he could go back.

“I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much for it, or maybe it would have been much worse without that,” he said.

The Tesla chief executive added that the outcome showed that money alone could not determine the results of American elections.

“But it certainly shows that, you know, American elections are not just about money. You know, obviously you can put a lot of money into election and have an effect that is relatively minor, and I think that that was the case here. So probably I could rewind the clock. I would have spent less money,” Musk said.

According to ABC News, Musk spent heavily on key Republican races during the election cycle, hoping to help the party retain its Senate majority. However, Democrats won several contests across the country and regained control of both chambers of Congress.

During the interview, Musk also sought to distance himself from the Make America Great Again movement associated with President Donald Trump, describing his political views as moderate.

“So, you know, I think I’m ideologically quite moderate, although, you know, I don’t think I have like, you know, any kind of extreme political views,” he said.

Asked about his association with MAGA, Musk said he did not identify with what he described as blind loyalty to Republican Party policies.

“I’m not MAGA. Well, at least I’m not like, I’m not sure what MAGA means … If that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that. I think there are positives and negatives in both parties,” he said.

Musk has been a prominent supporter of Trump and Republican candidates, including during the 2024 presidential election, when he described himself as “dark gothic MAGA” at a Trump rally in New York City.

His relationship with Trump, however, later came under strain over the Republican tax and spending legislation promoted by the White House, following Musk’s involvement in the administration’s efforts to reduce government spending.

Despite the disagreement, Musk continued to support Republican candidates ahead of the 2026 midterms, including through substantial financial contributions.

He also commented on the election of Democrat Gina Hinojosa as Texas governor, expressing confidence that she would govern moderately.

“I don’t think she’s sort of a far-left governor, and based on everything I’ve heard about her, I think things will be fine in Texas with Gina as governor,” Musk said.

The 2026 midterm elections saw Democrats make significant gains, winning control of both chambers of Congress and ending the Republicans’ hold on the Senate and House of Representatives.
