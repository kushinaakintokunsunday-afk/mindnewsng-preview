APC disowns letter asking INEC to ensure Tinubu’s 2027 victory

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The All Progressives Congress has disowned a letter purportedly written by its National Chairman, Prof. Nentawe Yilwatda, to the Independent National Electoral

In a statement on Monday, the APC National Chairman’s Special Adviser on Media and Information Strategy, Abimbola Tooki, described the letter, which circulated on social media, as fake, malicious and a desperate attempt to tarnish the image of its leadership and undermine democratic institutions.

A purported letter dated November 22, 2026, and addressed to INEC Chairman, Prof. Joash Amupitan, allegedly by APC National Chairman,, sought the electoral commission’s cooperation ahead of the 2027 presidential election.

The unsigned document claimed the APC leadership expected INEC to deploy its administrative and operational mechanisms to ensure Tinubu’s declaration as the winner of the 2027 presidential election.

Reacting, APC said the document was neither authored by Yilwatda nor issued by his office, insisting that it was inconsistent with the party’s established communication procedures.

The statement read in part, “The All Progressives Congress has condemned in the strongest possible terms a purported letter circulating on social media, allegedly written by the National Chairman of the party, Professor Nentawe Goshwe Yilwatda, to the Chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission, titled ‘Preparations for the 2027 Presidential Election and Ensuring the Victory of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu.’

“The unsigned letter, dated November 22, 2026, contains an outrageous and entirely fabricated claim that the APC leadership expects INEC to deploy its institutional mechanisms, administrative frameworks and operational capacities to ensure the declaration of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu as the winner of the 2027 presidential election.

“The party categorically states that the purported letter did not emanate from Professor Yilwatda, was not authored by him and has absolutely nothing to do with the National Chairman or his office. It is a fraudulent document, a malicious fabrication and a reckless attempt to impugn the integrity of the National Chairman, the APC and the democratic process.”

APC said the absence of a signature or authentication on the purported letter exposed its fraudulent nature, alleging that it was deliberately circulated to mislead the public and tarnish the image of its National Chairman.

The party also faulted the document for violating its established communication procedures, insisting that a letter of such significance could not have been issued outside its official channels.

It added, “The APC will not allow faceless individuals, political mischief-makers and purveyors of fabricated information to drag its leadership into disrepute through the circulation of fictitious documents.

“We condemn the author of this despicable letter in the strongest possible terms and challenge those responsible to come out of hiding, take ownership of their actions and provide verifiable evidence for their claims, rather than hiding behind anonymity to spread falsehoods and create distrust in democratic institutions.

“For the avoidance of doubt, the APC remains firmly committed to a peaceful, transparent, free and fair electoral process in the forthcoming 2027 general elections. Our party believes in the sanctity of the ballot, the independence of INEC and the right of Nigerians to freely choose their leaders without intimidation, manipulation or interference.

“President Bola Ahmed Tinubu himself has consistently demonstrated his commitment to democratic principles. At campaign rallies and public engagements across the country, the President has repeatedly urged Nigerians, particularly eligible voters, to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards and exercise their civic responsibility.

“This is a clear demonstration of his confidence in the democratic process and his belief that electoral legitimacy must come from the will of the people, freely expressed through the ballot.”

APC National Chairman urged Nigerians and the media to disregard the purported letter, insisting that it remained committed to democratic values, peaceful elections and respect for electoral institutions.

The party said it would approach the 2027 elections with confidence and discipline, stressing that electoral victory must be earned through the support and mandate of Nigerians.
