Sanwo-Olu gifts Flamingos N10m, iPhones each

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Lagos State Governor, Babajide Sanwo-Olu, has rewarded Nigeria’s victorious U-17 women’s national team, the Flamingos, with ₦10 million each, iPhone 17 Pro Max

Sanwo-Olu announced the reward package on Sunday at a special dinner reception held in honour of the team at Lagos House, Marina, where he hosted the players and their technical crew to celebrate their achievement.

Under the package, each player and member of the technical crew will receive ₦10 million, bringing the total cash reward to ₦300 million. The beneficiaries also received iPhones, headphones and bespoke outfits.

The Lagos State Government’s gesture adds to the growing list of rewards and recognition announced for the team since it made history in Morocco.

The Flamingos became the first African team to win the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup after defeating Japan 2-1 in the final on November 7 at the Mohammed VI Football Complex in Rabat.

Awawu Bashiru gave Nigeria the lead in the 39th minute before tournament Most Valuable Player, Oluebube Umejiaku, doubled the advantage shortly after the restart.

Japan pulled one back through Fuki Ito in the 80th minute, but the Flamingos held on to secure the title.

The victory capped a remarkable campaign for the Nigerian side, which recovered from a 2-1 opening defeat to Poland to win six consecutive matches and claim the trophy.

They responded to the setback with a 2-0 victory over Puerto Rico before defeating defending champions Korea DPR 3-1 to strengthen their chances of progressing in the competition.

Nigeria subsequently overcame Mexico in the Round of 16 and edged Venezuela 1-0 in the quarter-finals, with Azeezat Oduntan scoring a decisive 89th-minute penalty.

The team then defeated Brazil 3-1 in the semi-finals to reach their first-ever final before completing their historic run with victory over Japan.

The triumph made Nigeria the first African country to win the women’s U-17 World Cup and the first African nation to claim both the men’s and women’s U-17 World Cup titles.

The country’s previous best finish in the women’s competition was third place at the 2022 edition in India.

Sanwo-Olu’s announcement follows the Federal Government’s reward package for the team at a presidential reception in Abuja on November 11.

President Bola Tinubu approved a ₦100 million Federal Government Savings Bond for each player, with the investment expected to generate monthly returns and remain intact after five years.

Head coach Akeem Ishola Busari was awarded ₦100 million in cash, while assistant coaches and other members of the technical and administrative crew were allocated ₦50 million each.

The First Lady, Senator Oluremi Tinubu, announced the package on behalf of the President, commending the players for their resilience in recovering from their opening defeat to win their remaining six matches.

The Senate also announced a ₦100 million reward for the team following a special reception hosted by Senate President Godswill Akpabio.

The reward followed a motion moved by Senator Abdul Ningi, Chairman of the Senate Committee on Sports, congratulating the Flamingos on their historic achievement.

Earlier, the team received a $50,000 cash gift from sports stakeholder and commission sponsor Saleh Ahmadu during a reception organised in Rabat by the Chairman of the National Sports Commission, Shehu Dikko, and its Director General, Bukola Olopade.

The Presidency also directed the National Sports Commission to facilitate the payment of outstanding bonuses and allowances owed to the players and officials from the preliminary qualifying rounds through to the World Cup final.

Beyond the team’s collective achievement, Umejiaku earned individual recognition after winning the adidas Golden Ball as the tournament’s best player.
