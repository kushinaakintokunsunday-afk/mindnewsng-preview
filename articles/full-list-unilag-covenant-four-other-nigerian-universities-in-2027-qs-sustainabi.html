FULL LIST: UNILAG, Covenant, four other Nigerian universities in 2027 QS Sustainability Rankings

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Six Nigerian universities have been ranked in the 2027 QS Sustainability Rankings, with the University of Lagos emerging as the country’s highest-ranked institu

According to data published on the TopUniversities website on Tuesday, six Nigerian universities featured in the 2027 QS Sustainability Rankings, with the University of Lagos emerging as the country’s highest-ranked institution

The university was also described as the biggest riser in Africa, having moved up more than 700 places.

Other Nigerian universities featured in the ranking were Covenant University, University of Ibadan, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and Obafemi Awolowo University.

Covenant University placed between 1,151st and 1,200th, while the University of Ibadan ranked between 1,201st and 1,250th.

Ahmadu Bello University, University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and Obafemi Awolowo University were all placed in the 1,501-and-above category.

Below is the list of the Nigerian universities and their respective global rankings:

University of Lagos (UNILAG) – Joint 697th

UNILAG was ranked 14th in Africa, with an overall score of 65.5. It recorded 61.8 in Environmental Impact, 69.1 in Social Impact, and 65.9 in Governance.

Covenant University – 1,151–1,200

The Ota-based institution, which was Nigeria’s highest-ranked university in the 2026 edition, recorded 46.6 in Environmental Impact, 58.3 in Social Impact and 51.2 in Governance.

University of Ibadan – 1,201–1,250

The university recorded 40.5 in Environmental Impact, 64.9 in Social Impact and 35.4 in Governance.

Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria – 1,501+

ABU recorded 40.7 in Environmental Impact, 52.3 in Social Impact and 23.2 in Governance.

Obafemi Awolowo University – 1,501+

OAU recorded 29.7 in Environmental Impact, 51.4 in Social Impact and 26.2 in Governance.

University of Nigeria, Nsukka – 1,501+

UNN recorded 31.8 in Environmental Impact, 58.5 in Social Impact and 30.7 in Governance.

Across Africa, 13 universities from other countries ranked ahead of UNILAG. They included the University of Cape Town, which placed 88th globally, the University of Pretoria (243rd), Alexandria University in Egypt (412th), Cairo University (525th), the American University in Cairo (627th), Mansoura University (633rd) and Ain Shams University in Egypt (665th).

Stellenbosch University of South Africa ranked joint 688th, just ahead of UNILAG, which placed joint 697th.

The QS Sustainability Rankings assess universities based on their environmental and social impact, as well as governance.
