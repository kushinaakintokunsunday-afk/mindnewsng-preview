Fire Destroys Over 100 Shops At Kano Fish Market, Traders Count Losses

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

A fire outbreak at Kano Fish Market along Zaria Road has destroyed more than 100 shops, leaving traders counting losses estimated to run into billions of naira.

A fire outbreak at Kano Fish Market along Zaria Road has destroyed more than 100 shops, leaving traders counting losses estimated to run into billions of naira.

The fire reportedly broke out at about 9:00 p.m. on Sunday, near Kano Line Motor Park, spreading across sections of the market and destroying shops, containers and goods.

Reports indicate that approximately 150 shops and 200 containers were affected by the inferno, with some traders reportedly losing goods valued at millions of naira.

One of the affected traders, Usman Sani, recounted how the fire spread through the market, destroying shops and goods before traders could salvage their belongings.

“We received the news of the fire and rushed to the market, but by the time we got here, the flames had already spread. Many shops were affected, and traders lost goods worth millions of naira. The situation is heartbreaking,” he said.

Another trader, Musa Inuwa, described the incident as a major setback for those who depend on the market for their livelihood.

“Many of us depend entirely on this business to feed our families. We have lost our goods and capital, and we do not know where to start from. We are appealing to the government to come to our assistance,” he said.

The affected traders are appealing to the Kano State Government and relevant authorities for urgent intervention to support those who lost their businesses.

The Kano State Fire Service reportedly received a distress call at about 9:58 p.m. and deployed personnel to the scene, arriving four minutes later.

Meanwhile, the cause of the fire remains unknown, as investigations continue.
