Radda Flags Off ₦1.4bn Ingawa Hospital Upgrade, Commissions Road

By Channels TV Channels TV 3d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Katsina State Governor, Malam Dikko Umaru Radda, has flagged off the comprehensive renovation and upgrade of Ingawa General Hospital at a cost of over ₦1.4 bill

Katsina State Governor, Malam Dikko Umaru Radda, has flagged off the comprehensive renovation and upgrade of Ingawa General Hospital at a cost of over ₦1.4 billion, reaffirming his administration’s commitment to strengthening healthcare infrastructure while mobilising support for the re-election of President Bola Tinubu and the All Progressives Congress (APC) in the 2027 general elections.

The hospital project, awarded to an indigenous construction firm, will provide additional structures, modern medical facilities and state-of-the-art equipment aimed at transforming the hospital into a contemporary healthcare centre capable of serving Ingawa and neighbouring communities.

Speaking during the foundation-laying ceremony on Friday, Governor Radda also commissioned the newly completed 1.65-kilometre Ingawa township road, constructed by the Ingawa Local Government Council, describing it as a practical demonstration of the dividends of democracy.

He attributed the project’s success to the local government autonomy reforms introduced by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, saying direct allocation of funds to councils has empowered them to undertake projects that directly impact the lives of the people.

“Local government autonomy under President Tinubu has strengthened councils to receive funds directly and execute projects that meet the needs of their communities,” the Governor stated.

He commended the council leadership and stakeholders for completing the road project, expressing confidence that it would improve transportation, stimulate economic activities, and enhance the quality of life of residents.

Earlier, the Commissioner for Health, Alhaji Musa Adamu Funtua, disclosed that the hospital upgrade would include the construction of doctors’ quarters, an Accident and Emergency Unit, as well as the supply and installation of modern medical equipment.

He noted that the facility, which serves Ingawa and surrounding communities, had not witnessed any major renovation since the administration of the late President Umaru Musa Yar’Adua as Governor of Katsina State, and praised Governor Radda for responding to the healthcare needs of the people.

The Governor later addressed a massive Tinubu-Dikko-Jobe Campaign Rally in Ingawa, where he described the large turnout as a strong endorsement of President Tinubu’s leadership and the APC.

“Today, we have witnessed something extraordinary in Ingawa. You have demonstrated your happiness, love and appreciation for the legacy of our father, the late Engineer Mohammed Kanti Bello,” Governor Radda told the cheering crowd.

He appreciated the people for supporting the emergence of Hon. Mustapha Muhammad Kanti Bello as the APC candidate for the Kankia-Kusada-Ingawa Federal Constituency, expressing confidence in his ability to provide effective representation.

Governor Radda also praised Engr. Dr. Sani Magaji for his contributions to Ingawa’s development and announced that his administration would soon flag off road projects linking Ingawa and Kankia to improve connectivity and accelerate development.

He conveyed the appreciation of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu and Vice President Kashim Shettima to the people of Ingawa for their continued loyalty and support.

Director-General of the Tinubu-Dikko-Jobe Campaign, Architect Ahmed Musa Dangiwa, described effective representation as one that attracts projects and opportunities to the people, urging voters to support APC candidates across all levels.

He observed that the opportunity to represent the Kankia-Kusada-Ingawa Federal Constituency had returned to Ingawa after previous representation by Kankia and Kusada, while also expressing confidence in the APC senatorial candidate for Katsina North, Malam Nasiru Hayatu.

Similarly, APC State Chairman, Dr. Bishir Gambo Saulawa, commended the people of Ingawa for the warm reception accorded to the campaign team and urged party members to sustain unity and peaceful coexistence.

The Speaker of the Katsina State House of Assembly, Rt. Hon. Nasiru Yahaya Daura, alongside other party stakeholders, also lauded Governor Radda’s development initiatives across the state.

At the palace of the District Head of Ingawa, Alhaji Tukur Suleiman Dambo II (Sarkin Fulanin Dambo), Governor Radda praised the longstanding unity among Ingawa, Kankia and Kusada, describing Ingawa as a community with deep personal significance.

“Whenever I visit this palace, I feel I have come home. My maternal grandparents hailed from this community,” he said.

The Governor commended political leaders who stepped aside to ensure consensus within the APC, describing their actions as evidence of maturity and commitment to party unity.

“The love you are showing Mustapha is recognition of the good name his late father earned. A legacy of integrity is more valuable than material possessions,” he added.

Reaffirming his role as Katsina State Coordinator of the Tinubu-Shettima Campaign, Governor Radda pledged to lead the APC campaign across Ingawa, Kankia and Kusada while seeking a second term alongside his deputy, Hon. Faruk Lawal Jobe.

Highlighting his administration’s achievements, the Governor cited the ongoing upgrade of Ingawa General Hospital and the completion of the 39-kilometre Ingawa-Shargalle Road within 11 months as evidence of his government’s commitment to delivering infrastructure and improving the welfare of citizens.

In his remarks, the District Head of Ingawa expressed appreciation for the Governor’s interventions in education, healthcare and road construction, describing the projects as visible evidence of responsive leadership.

He also commended the improved security situation in the state, noting that travelling between Kano and Katsina at night, once considered risky, had become significantly safer.

The traditional ruler assured Governor Radda of the community’s total support while appealing for the construction of a dam, rehabilitation of the Manomawa-Yaya Road and renovation of staff quarters at the Rural Boarding School in Ingawa.

The Chairman of Ingawa Local Government, Hon. Abubakar Idris 02, also thanked the Governor for his continued support and development projects, pledging sustained collaboration to advance the welfare of the people.
