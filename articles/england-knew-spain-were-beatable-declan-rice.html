England knew Spain were beatable – Declan Rice

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

England midfielder Declan Rice has revealed that the Three Lions were confident of beating world champions Spain ahead of their 3-2 victory in the UEFA Nations

England ended Spain’s 43-match unbeaten run with an impressive performance at the Metropolitano Stadium, where a Harry Kane penalty and goals from Ezri Konsa and Ben White gave the visitors a 3-0 lead inside an hour.

However, Elliot Anderson’s sending off for a foul on Lamine Yamal allowed Spain back into the contest, with Mikel Oyarzabal converting the resulting penalty before Nico Williams scored at the far post to reduce the deficit to 3-2.

England eventually held on for the victory, with Rice saying the players had believed they could secure the result before the encounter.

“We knew we were going to beat them tonight,” he said afterwards, in a release on the team’s website on Sunday.

“The messaging from the manager after the Croatia game has been so positive, and the message from the boys; everyone has been pushing together, knowing that we were going to come here and not just sit back and try to hit on the counter.

“The red card changes the course of the game because it could have been a couple more, if I’m being honest.

“If we don’t go down to 10 men, I think we see more of the ball. We start to see them jump more, we start to see more space, and we get to see our attacking players thrive doing what they did in the second half,” he added.

England were forced to defend their advantage in the closing stages after Williams reduced the deficit to one goal with 15 minutes remaining.

Spain mounted sustained pressure, but goalkeeper James Trafford produced an impressive double save to preserve England’s lead.

Rice praised the goalkeeper’s intervention, describing it as crucial to the visitors’ victory.

“For him to make two saves in the space of two of seconds was massive,” he added.

“For a second, when they got their second [goal], you might have thought it was going to go the other way [with] the amount of pressure they were putting on us.

“But that resilience, that defending, keep looking up at the clock, realising there wasn’t long left, and everyone kept emptying the tank, doing anything for this badge, for this team, for our players, for the manager,” the Arsenal star concluded.
