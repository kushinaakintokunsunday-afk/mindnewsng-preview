Natasha provides patrol van, computers for Ihima police

From Adesuwa Tsan, Abuja

The senator representing Kogi Central, Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan, has facilitated the provision of another patrol van and donated two computers to the Ihima Police Station in Okehi Local Government Area of Kogi State to strengthen security operations and improve police response to emergencies.

According to a statement issued by her media office on Sunday, the latest intervention is aimed at improving police mobility, surveillance and administrative capacity, particularly in rural communities where difficult terrain and long distances often hamper security patrols and emergency response.

The senator noted that security agencies needed practical support and adequate equipment to discharge their responsibilities effectively, stressing that protecting lives and property required sustained investment in their operational capacity.

Speaking further, she emphasised the importance of grassroots security interventions and stronger collaboration between security agencies and communities, noting that effective policing required adequate logistics and modern operational tools.

“Security is not something we should leave entirely to the men and women in uniform without providing them with the necessary tools to do their jobs. Our police officers need functional equipment, reliable transportation and the right working environment to respond effectively to the security needs of our communities.”

She expressed hope that the patrol van would enable officers to respond promptly to distress calls and strengthen police presence across their areas of responsibility, while the two computers would support administrative and operational activities, including record-keeping, documentation of complaints, crime reporting, digital case management and retrieval of information required for investigations.

“This additional patrol van will strengthen their mobility and enable them to reach more communities, while the computers will support proper documentation, record management and other essential police operations,” she stated.

The senator explained that her commitment to security was driven by the need to ensure that communities were adequately protected, while security personnel had the tools required to carry out their duties.

“My commitment is to continue supporting initiatives that improve the safety and wellbeing of our people. Every community deserves to feel protected, and our security agencies deserve the necessary support to deliver on their responsibilities,” she said.
