Fountain Of Life Trustees Name Interim Leader, Insist On Jimmy Odukoya’s Removal

The Board of Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church has named Rotimi Okpaise as its interim spiritual and administrative leader, reaffirming its decision to remove Pastor Jimmy Odukoya as Senior Pastor.

The trustees announced the appointment in a fresh statement dated Tuesday, November 17, 2026, insisting that their resolution of November 16 withdrawing recognition of Odukoya remained in force.

The development comes amid a leadership crisis at the Lagos-based church, with Odukoya rejecting his removal and insisting that he remains the church’s duly recognised Senior Pastor.

In the latest statement, the board provided further details about the circumstances surrounding Odukoya’s appointment, particularly a video recording allegedly made by the church’s late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, in December 2021.

According to the trustees, the late founder had asked Jimmy to make a video recording in which he expressly identified his preferred successor. The recording was reportedly witnessed by his elder brother, Pastor John Odukoya.

The board said the person identified in the recording was not Jimmy Odukoya and alleged that the video was not presented to it until late December 2025, more than two years after the founder’s death.

It said it had relied on Jimmy’s account that his late father had handed over the mantle of leadership to him when considering his appointment as Senior Pastor.

The trustees described the non-disclosure of the recording as a significant development, arguing that it could have influenced their deliberations.

“The Board considers this omission particularly grave, as the disclosure of such information could reasonably have influenced its deliberations and the ultimate decision reached,” the statement said.

The board added that it had completed its review of the recording, the circumstances surrounding Jimmy’s appointment and the implications for the church’s succession process.

It said the issue was not merely about the identity of the person appointed but whether the decision was reached with full and transparent disclosure of relevant information.

“The fundamental issue, therefore, is not simply the identity of the person appointed, but whether the Board’s decision was reached on the basis of full, accurate and transparent disclosure of all material information relevant to the succession process,” it stated.

Board Cites Governance, Doctrinal Concerns

The trustees said the present controversy was not an isolated incident, disclosing that they had spent several months trying to resolve concerns about leadership succession, church administration and adherence to established governance arrangements.

According to the board, it had preferred dialogue, reconciliation and peaceful resolution over public confrontation.

It also said it sought the intervention of respected Christian leaders whom the church’s founding pastors held in high esteem.

However, the trustees alleged that their efforts had not produced the desired outcome, accusing Jimmy of failing to provide the cooperation required to resolve the disagreements.

“Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has not provided the level of engagement and cooperation necessary to achieve a satisfactory resolution,” the statement said.

The board further cited what it described as unresolved governance concerns, alongside moral and doctrinal controversies, as reasons for its intervention.

It said it could not remain indifferent while the church’s spiritual heritage, institutional integrity and public reputation faced what it described as continuing erosion.

“The responsibility of the Board is therefore not merely administrative or custodial. It is also a solemn obligation to preserve the integrity of the Church, safeguard its doctrine and protect the spiritual welfare of its congregation,” it stated.

Okpaise Named Interim Leader

Following its meeting on November 16, the board resolved that Jimmy was no longer recognised as Senior Pastor of the church, with immediate effect.

It also directed him to relinquish the office and all its functions.

The statement said, “Accordingly, having previously called upon Pastor Jimmy Odukoya to step down, the Board, at its meeting held on 16 November 2026, resolved that Pastor Jimmy Odukoya is no longer recognised as Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, with immediate effect.”

The trustees subsequently announced Okpaise’s appointment to oversee the church’s spiritual and administrative affairs pending the conclusion of consultations and processes for establishing a substantive leadership structure.

“The Board has further resolved that the interim spiritual and administrative leadership of the Church be entrusted to the Pastor Rotimi Okpaise,” it added.

The board said the arrangement would remain in place until the necessary processes were completed in accordance with the church’s governing instruments and established procedures.

It described the decision as necessary to create an atmosphere for restoring peace, rebuilding trust and resolving the leadership crisis.

Board Raises Alarm Over Church’s Social Media Accounts

The trustees also accused individuals acting on behalf of Jimmy Odukoya of taking control of the church’s official social media accounts without their authorisation.

According to the statement, the platforms had been used to publish accounts of the leadership dispute which the board considered inaccurate and misleading.

It warned members of the church, the media and the general public that statements appearing on the platforms did not represent its position.

“Only statements issued and signed for and on behalf of the Board of Trustees should be regarded as the official position of the Church on these matters,” it said.

The board added that it was taking steps to restore the church’s official communication channels to what it described as proper and authorised control.

Jimmy Odukoya Rejects Removal

However, Odukoya had earlier rejected the trustees’ announcement, insisting that he remains the duly appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees.

In a counter-statement published on the church’s website, his camp described the purported removal as constitutionally invalid.

It argued that the individuals behind the announcement were operating outside the church’s constitutional framework and maintained that some trustees had been asked to resign in March 2026 over alleged violations of the church’s constitution and founding principles.

The statement maintained that Odukoya’s appointment was approved by the board in September 2023 and that no constitutionally valid decision had been taken to remove him.

The competing statements have deepened uncertainty over the church’s leadership, with both sides claiming authority to speak on its behalf.

The Fountain of Life Church was founded in 1992 by Pastors Taiwo and Bimbo Odukoya. Jimmy succeeded his father following his death in August 2023.

He was announced as Senior Pastor in September 2023, while his sister, Pastor Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, was appointed Associate Senior Pastor.

The latest dispute also follows controversy surrounding Jimmy’s remarriage. He introduced his new wife, Pastor Mo Odukoya, to the congregation on November 1.
