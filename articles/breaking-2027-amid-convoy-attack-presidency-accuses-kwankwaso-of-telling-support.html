(BREAKING) 2027: Amid convoy attack, Presidency accuses Kwankwaso of telling supporters to improvise weapons

The Presidency has accused former Kano State Governor and Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) vice-presidential candidate, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, of encouraging his supporters to improvise dangerous weapons ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The allegation was raised by President Bola Tinubu’s Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, in a post on his X account following Kwankwaso’s political rally in Bichi Local Government Area of Kano State on Sunday.

Onanuga alleged that Kwankwaso made comments capable of encouraging confrontation while addressing supporters at the rally.

The presidential aide quoted the former governor as telling his supporters: “Continue to stay prepared; do not go easy on these shameless people.”

He further alleged that Kwankwaso encouraged his followers to improvise dangerous weapons, expressing concern that such remarks could heighten political tensions and promote violence ahead of the next general elections.

Onanuga argued that prominent political figures should use their influence to encourage restraint, tolerance and peaceful political engagement rather than make statements that could be interpreted as preparing supporters for physical confrontations.

Reacting to the allegation, human rights activist Comrade Musa Bala Gaye called on security agencies to intervene, warning that the development could threaten peace in Kano State if not properly addressed.

Gaye maintained that political campaigns should provide an opportunity for candidates and parties to present their policies and ideas rather than encourage intimidation, political thuggery or the use of dangerous weapons.

The controversy comes amid reports of an attack on Kwankwaso’s convoy in Bichi on Sunday, during which several members of his entourage were reportedly injured and between six and seven vehicles damaged.

According to Ibrahim Garba Shuaibu, spokesperson for the Kano State NDC governorship candidate, Comrade Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo, Kwankwaso and his entourage had earlier visited Tofa, Rimin Gado, Kabo, Gwarzo and Shanono before proceeding to Bichi for another political engagement.

Shuaibu said the earlier visits were completed without major security challenges but claimed that tension arose when the delegation arrived in Bichi, as some individuals reportedly began throwing stones.

He explained that Kwankwaso instructed members of the delegation to proceed with their activities despite the disturbance.

“Senator Kwankwaso told us to enter the town without fear or retreat. When we arrived, there was a sign of disturbance and stone-throwing, but we held our meeting peacefully,” Shuaibu said.

However, he alleged that the situation deteriorated as the delegation was preparing to leave, when some individuals emerged from a location and attacked their vehicles, smashing windscreens.

“It was on our way out that some people emerged from a location and started attacking our vehicles, smashing windscreens,” he added.

Shuaibu said between six and seven vehicles were damaged, while some members of the entourage sustained injuries.

Following the incident, NDC presidential candidate Peter Obi condemned the reported attack on his running mate, describing it as unacceptable and calling on the Nigeria Police Force and other security agencies to investigate the matter.

In a statement issued on Monday, Obi urged the authorities to identify and arrest those responsible, including anyone found to have sponsored the attack.

He stressed that political disagreements should not lead to violence, intimidation or destruction of property, insisting that political competition must be settled through peaceful engagement and the ballot.

Obi also expressed solidarity with Kwankwaso, the Kano State leadership of the NDC and others affected by the incident, while wishing those injured a speedy recovery.

The competing allegations and reports of violence have added to concerns over political tensions in Kano as activities ahead of the 2027 general elections gather momentum.
