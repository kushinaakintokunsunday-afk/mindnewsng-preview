Benue South 2027: Idoma diaspora organises debate for Abba Moro, Ottah, Agbese, others

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Idoma people in the diaspora, under the umbrella of the Idoma in Diaspora Leadership Association, with members and representatives across the United Kingdom

The Idoma people in the diaspora, under the umbrella of the Idoma in Diaspora Leadership Association, with members and representatives across the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Russia, Germany and other countries, have announced plans to organise a virtual debate involving candidates contesting for the Senate and House of Representatives seats in Benue South ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The initiative is aimed at providing constituents, both in Nigeria and abroad, with an opportunity to hear directly from the candidates, assess their aspirations and make informed choices ahead of the elections.

According to a notice issued by the association, the engagement will be held on two separate days, with candidates contesting for the Senate scheduled to participate on Saturday, November 21, 2026, while House of Representatives candidates will have their session on Sunday, November 22, 2026.

Both sessions are scheduled to commence at 9:00 p.m. West Africa Time, WAT, via Zoom.
The association provided a common Zoom meeting ID, 249 497 5445, and passcode, 1000, for interested participants.

Explaining the importance of the initiative, the association said the virtual engagement would enable Idoma people to listen to the candidates, understand their aspirations and make informed decisions about who should represent them in the National Assembly.

The initiative is anchored on the themes of Idoma unity, informed choice and a stronger Idoma nation, with particular emphasis on effective representation in the Red and Green Chambers of the National Assembly.

The association urged eligible voters and other interested Idoma people to participate in the sessions, engage with the candidates and assess their visions for the development of Benue South.
Among those jostling for the Benue South senatorial seat are incumbent Senator Abba Moro of the Peoples Democratic Party, PDP, Francis Ottah Agbo of the All Progressives Congress, APC, David Olofu of the African Democratic Congress, ADC, Dan Onjeh of the Nigerian Democratic Congress, NDC, Samuel Onuh of the Labour Party, LP, among others.
For the House of Representatives seats, those in the race include Philip Agbese of the Labour Party, Aida of the Peoples Democratic Party, Onuh Blessing of the All Progressives Congress and Lilian Adoyi of the Peoples Democratic Party, Ojotu Ojema of the APC, and others.

The initiative is expected to provide a platform for greater political participation and accountability as political activities gather momentum ahead of the 2027 general elections.
