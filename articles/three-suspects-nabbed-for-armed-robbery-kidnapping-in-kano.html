Three suspects nabbed for armed robbery, kidnapping in Kano

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 9h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Kano State Police Command has arrested three suspects, Nafi’u Lawal alias Gamji, Auwalu Shafi’u alias Bakin Tuwo, and Yunusa Sale, alias Bammi, in connectio

The Kano State Police Command has arrested three suspects, Nafi’u Lawal alias Gamji, Auwalu Shafi’u alias Bakin Tuwo, and Yunusa Sale, alias Bammi, in connection with cases of house trespass, armed robbery, kidnapping and criminal intimidation in Rogo Local Government Area of the state.

Police Public Relations Officer (PPRO) of the command, CSP Abdullahi Kiyawa disclosed this in a Monday statement.

Kiyawa said the arrest followed a report received at about 2:00 p.m on November 3, from a resident of Aleko Village, Rogo LGA, that unknown armed men stormed and unlawfully entered his dwelling house armed with sophisticated weapons.

The suspects allegedly attacked members of the household, assaulted a female in the house, and robbed the complainant of a red Boxer motorcycle valued at N1.15 million and twenty-five bags of dry pepper valued at N850,000.

The complainant further stated that he recognised one of the suspects as Nafi’u Lawal, alias Gamji.

The police spokesman said that upon receipt of the report, the Commissioner of Police, Kano State Police Command, CP Ibrahim Adamu Bakori, directed a team of operatives attached to the Anti-Kidnapping Squad to ensure the arrest of the culprits.

Kiyawa said sustained intelligence-led follow-up coupled with coordinated operations led to the arrest of the recognised suspect and two others, identified as Nafi’u Lawal, alias Gamji, aged 21 years, of Aleko Village, Rogo LGA; Auwalu Shafi’u, alias Bakin Tuwo, aged 26 years, of the same address, who was traced and arrested at Doguwa LGA; and Yunusa Sale, alias Bammi, aged 26 years, of Jajaye Village, Rogo LGA.

According to Kiyawa, the suspects confessed to the crime during interrogation, and further confessed to their involvement in other cases of kidnapping involving two victims, Ibrahim Iro, alias Dangi, of Aleko Village, and Shadari, alias Ardo.

The suspects further disclosed that the victims were abducted and taken to an unknown destination until the sums of N900,000 and N1 million respectively were paid as ransom for their release.

The command spokesman said investigation is ongoing with a view to trace and arrest the remaining members of the syndicate and recover the stolen items.

He added that the case would be charged to court upon completion of investigation.

The Kano CP commended the operatives of the Anti-Kidnapping Squad for their swift response, professionalism and dedication to duty which led to the arrest.

The command reiterated its commitment to the protection of lives and property, and the fight against kidnapping, armed robbery and all forms of crime in the state.

The command also called on members of the public to continue to support the police with timely and credible information, and to report any suspicious persons or movements to the nearest police station or through the command’s emergency lines.
