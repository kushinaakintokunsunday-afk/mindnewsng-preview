Ex-Super Eagles captain, Odegbami escapes death in machete attack

By Punch Punch 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Former Super Eagles captain, Segun Odegbami, narrowly escaped death on Friday after armed men wielding machetes attacked him at his residence in Abeokuta, Ogun

The state police command confirmed the attack, disclosing that the 74-year-old sports administrator had been discharged from hospital after receiving medical attention.

The Commissioner of Police, Bode Ojajuni, also visited Odegbami to express the command’s sympathy and assure him that those responsible would be tracked down and brought to justice.

Odegbami, proprietor of Eagles 7 Sports Radio 103.7 FM, was reportedly attacked at about 2:30am on Friday at his residence in the Ibara Government Reservation Area, Abeokuta.

According to accounts from sources in the neighbourhood, three armed men invaded the residence, overpowering his domestic staff and driver before making their way into the football legend’s bedroom.

A resident, who spoke on condition of anonymity, said the attackers initially demanded to know Odegbami’s whereabouts, but the staff denied that he was at home.

The source said the suspects became suspicious because the generator was running and proceeded to assault the domestic workers before locating Odegbami.

“The incident is unfortunate. We heard that the armed men struck in the early hours of today [Friday] and first overpowered the domestic staff. They asked for Chief Segun Odegbami and his staff denied that he was at home,” he said.

“But the generator was on that time. So, the armed men believed he must be at home and they handed severe beatings to the staff. They immediately found their way to Odegbami’s bedroom and attacked him there.”

The attackers reportedly inflicted machete wounds on Odegbami before escaping from the property.

Saturday PUNCH gathered that Odegbami was subsequently rushed to an undisclosed hospital for treatment.

Confirming the development, the Ogun State Police Public Relations Officer, Oluseyi Babaseyi, said the command was leaving no stone unturned in investigating the incident and apprehending the suspects.

He said: “The Commissioner of Police immediately ordered a thorough investigation into the circumstances surrounding it, with intensive efforts ongoing to identify and apprehend those responsible. We can also confirm that Chief Segun Odegbami has been discharged from the hospital.”

In a separate statement announcing the commissioner’s visit, the police spokesperson said Ojajuni had conveyed his concern over the attack and wished the former Nigerian international continued recovery.

The statement partly read: “The Commissioner of Police has paid a visit to former Super Eagles captain and renowned sports administrator, Chief Segun Odegbami, to express the Command’s sympathy following the reported incident at his residence in Abeokuta.

“Ojajuni assured Chief Odegbami that the incident is receiving the Command’s utmost attention. He disclosed that he had ordered an immediate and thorough investigation to establish the circumstances surrounding the incident, while intensive investigative efforts are ongoing to identify and apprehend those responsible.

“He emphasised that the Command would leave no stone unturned in ensuring that the perpetrators are brought to justice.”

The command urged residents to remain calm and provide credible information that could assist the investigation through established police channels.

The motive for the attack had yet to be established as of the time of filing this report.
