British Singer Raye Tops 2027 Grammys With 11 Nominations

Soulful British singer-songwriter Raye on Monday topped the Grammy nominations with 11, and Spanish superstar Rosalía earned nine in a strong showing for female artists across the board.

Women have historically been underrepresented at the Grammy Awards held by the Recording Academy, whose 69th annual gala will take place on February 7 in Los Angeles.

But recent years have seen women dominate both the industry and the trophies list.

Raye’s whopping 11 nominations included the three most prestigious, recognizing best album, best song, and best record for her jazz-inflected blend of pop and R&B.

She was also recognized for her songwriting for fellow artists with a nomination for the relatively new best songwriter category.

Rosalia’s nine nominations follow a banner night at last week’s Latin Grammys, where her sweeping, avant-garde classical pop album “Lux” reigned supreme.

First-time nominee Ella Langley, the crooner behind the ubiquitous smash hit “Choosin’ Texas,” will enter the evening with seven shots at Grammys gold, as will fellow country star Miranda Lambert, who co-wrote that hit and notably scooped a nomination for producer of the year.

Pop-rock phenom Olivia Rodrigo also earned seven nods, as did the multifaceted musician Phoebe Bridgers, who released her third solo studio album “Lost Weekend” to widespread critical acclaim.

First-timers make waves

Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean — the English singer behind the smash “Man I Need” — earned six nods as did perennial Academy favorite Bruno Mars.

Taylor Swift predictably will be a threat in the top categories for her album “The Life of a Showgirl” as well as the song she penned for the latest film in the “Toy Story” franchise. She has five chances at winning.

But for all the heavyweight glory, it also proved a strong Grammys cycle for first-time nominees.

New York indie rockers Geese capped a breakout year with their first-ever nominations, garnering five.

Langley is a strong favorite for the Best New Artist category, but a crop of performers including Geese as well as brash electropop star Slayyyter and Canadian rock duo Angine de Poitrine could pull upsets.

The new blood will be joined by stalwarts of the trade who earned nominations including Madonna, Paul McCartney, Bruce Springsteen and The Rolling Stones.

The 2027 line-up features 100 competitive categories.

The Grammy Awards will air on the US network ABC and stream on Disney+ and Hulu — the first time in half-a-century that the ceremony won’t be broadcast by CBS.
