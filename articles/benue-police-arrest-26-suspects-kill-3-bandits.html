Benue police arrest 26 suspects, kill 3 bandits

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 3 min read

From Scholastica Hir, Makurdi

The Benue State Police Command has arrested 26 suspects, neutralised three suspected bandits and rescued five kidnapped persons in separate security operations across the state.

Among those arrested are nine suspects allegedly involved in the killing of a 52-year-old man, Felix Ahemba, whose body was dumped in a cassava farm at Tyo Adu Village, Gboko Local Government Area (LGA) of the state.

The police also recovered four locally made firearms, 495 rounds of live ammunition, 290 ammunition chains, a stolen Toyota Camry, a Honda motorcycle, assorted charms and a locally made knife during the operations.

The Commissioner of Police, Cletus Nwadiogbu, disclosed this while briefing journalists  yesterday at the Command headquarters in Makurdi.

He said the successes were recorded through intelligence-led operations, proactive policing and collaboration with other security agencies.

Nwadiogbu disclosed that operatives of the Katsina-Ala Division, working in collaboration with the Police Mobile Force and Operation Whirl Stroke (OPWS), rescued five persons abducted at Utange Village, Tor-Donga District, Katsina-Ala LGA, on November 8.

He, however, regretted that three of the rescued victims, who sustained severe injuries during the incident, died while being conveyed to hospital, while the remaining two survived.

The police commissioner also disclosed that 10 suspects were arrested in connection with the abduction of a commercial driver, Mohammed Musa, a woman and her six-year-old son at Ojinebe-Adoka Village.

He said operatives of the Violent Crime Response Unit arrested the suspects on November 7, adding that the vehicle in which the victims were last seen was later discovered burnt along the route.

According to him, efforts are ongoing to locate the three missing persons and apprehend other suspects still at large.

On the fight against armed robbery, Nwadiogbu said two suspects, Saviour Shagbaor and Maakaven Matthew, were arrested in Gboko following an attack on a commercial motorcyclist and his passenger.

He said the assailants allegedly used a locally made firearm during the attack before escaping with the victim’s Bajaj motorcycle.

He further disclosed that two other suspects, Michael Tyonongu and Godwin Agaibge, were arrested along the Gbajimgba-Makurdi Road on November 16, with a locally fabricated pistol.

Another suspect, Tersoo Orjime, was arrested in Makurdi over an alleged attempted robbery, with a locally made revolver pistol and a knife recovered from him.

The CP added that a suspected cult member, Dan Azumi Audu, was arrested at Yagba Community, Makurdi, following a distress call. A locally fabricated revolver pistol and live ammunition were recovered from him.

Nwadiogbu also announced the arrest of a suspected car-snatching suspect, Anthony Nagu, by operatives of Operation Zenda at Otukpa, where a red Toyota Camry allegedly stolen from Badagry, Lagos State, was recovered.

On the burning of the Traditional Council Hall at Tyem-Imongu Community in Vandeikya LGA on November 4, the police commissioner said nine persons had been arrested in connection with the incident.

He disclosed that investigation had been concluded and the suspects charged to court.

On the arrest of suspected bandits and recovery of ammunition, Nwadiogbu said operatives of Operation Zenda intercepted a vehicle travelling from Jato-Aka towards Makurdi through Gboko on November 15.

He said a passenger identified as Shawon Order Timothy was arrested during the operation, while 490 rounds of ammunition of different calibres and 290 ammunition chains were recovered from him.

The suspect, according to the CP, was allegedly linked to the killing of security personnel at Agu Centre on September 19, 2025.

He said the suspect remained in custody as investigations continued.

Nwadiogbu urged residents to remain vigilant and provide credible and timely information to security agencies, stressing that lasting security could only be achieved through the cooperation of all stakeholders.

He reaffirmed the Command’s commitment to sustaining intelligence gathering, strengthening security operations and ensuring the safety of the people.
