Anambra South 2027: NDC faithful, over 1,000 APGA stakeholders declare support for Nwachukwu

…Say 18 Months Not Enough

Party faithful and political stakeholders across the seven LGAs reaffirm support for Senator Emmanuel Nwachukwu in the January 2027 general elections, insisting he deserves a full four-year term to consolidate on his representation.

A growing number of political stakeholders and party faithfuls across Anambra South Senatorial District have declared support for the re-election of Senator Emmanuel C. Nwachukwu (Ọnọdugo), insisting that his one year and six months in office is insufficient to fully deliver on the mandate entrusted to him.

The supporters, comprising members of the National Democratic Congress (NDC), the All Progressives Grand Alliance (APGA) and other political stakeholders, expressed confidence in the senator’s representation, arguing that he deserves the opportunity to consolidate on the foundation already established and serve a full four-year term.

The endorsements followed a series of political engagements involving the senator and party stakeholders, including a meeting with more than 1,000 APGA faithfuls drawn from the State, Local Government and Ward Executive Working Committees across the seven local government areas of Anambra South.

The meeting, which took place at Senator Nwachukwu’s residence in Ukpor, Nnewi South Local Government Area, brought together party leaders, executives and grassroots representatives from across the senatorial district.

Those in attendance included members of the State Working Committee (SWC) from Anambra South, the seven Local Government Party Chairmen and members of their respective executive committees, as well as ward executives and other stakeholders representing the district’s 118 wards.

The engagement provided an opportunity for interaction, exchange of ideas, assessment of party preparedness and discussions on strengthening coordination and cooperation across the various levels of the party.

In his remarks, Senator Nwachukwu emphasised that effective representation extends beyond legislative responsibilities, noting that accessibility, consistent engagement and maintaining meaningful relationships with constituents and political structures remain important aspects of public service.

He expressed appreciation to the party leaders and faithfuls for their commitment to strengthening APGA across the district, describing their participation as an indication of the importance of unity, inclusiveness and grassroots involvement in the political process.

The senator also called on members to expand the party’s membership base by encouraging more people to join APGA, assuring them that the party remained open to accommodating new members.

“We don’t do use and dump. Our party, APGA, is big enough to accommodate all of you. I urge you to bring more people into our fold. If you go on social media, what they show you is not necessarily what is obtainable in real life,” Nwachukwu said.

He further commended the party’s leadership and acknowledged the contributions of stakeholders towards sustaining a united and coordinated political structure across Anambra South.

The senator said he was particularly encouraged by the spirit of solidarity demonstrated at the meeting, noting that the stakeholders’ declaration of support for his return to the Senate ahead of the January 16, 2027 election came with an even greater responsibility to remain accessible and committed to the people.

He assured them of his continued engagement, adding that their confidence would encourage him to sustain his commitment to purposeful representation.

NDC Faithful in Aguata Declare Support

In a related development, NDC faithful and prominent political stakeholders from Aguata Local Government Area have also reaffirmed their support for Senator Nwachukwu’s re-election following a separate strategic engagement with the senator.

The meeting provided an opportunity for the stakeholders to interact with Nwachukwu, strengthen existing relationships and discuss their collective commitment to the political process ahead of the forthcoming senatorial election.

Speaking on behalf of the NDC stakeholders in Aguata, Richard Nwankwo declared the group’s support for the senator, assuring him of their solidarity.

“You are welcome to our midst. We, the NDC in Aguata, are solidly behind you,” Nwankwo stated.

Richard described the senator’s physical appearance and direct engagement with the group as significant, observing that he was the first senator to visit them physically to address their concerns, while others had previously communicated through telephone conversations.

He expressed optimism that Nwachukwu would be given the opportunity to complete a four-year term, maintaining that the senator’s relatively short period in office should not be considered sufficient time to assess the full extent of his mandate.
The stakeholders maintained that continuity in representation would provide an opportunity for the senator to consolidate on the work already commenced and pursue further initiatives for the benefit of the district.

Across the engagements, the argument advanced by supporters was that one year and six months is not enough time for a senator to fully execute his legislative agenda, establish sustainable development initiatives and consolidate relationships with constituents.

They argued that effective representation requires consistency, experience, continuity and sufficient time to translate legislative intentions and development plans into measurable outcomes.

For the NDC faithful in Aguata and APGA stakeholders across Anambra South, the January 16, 2027 senatorial election presents an opportunity to determine whether Senator Nwachukwu should receive another mandate to complete a full four-year term.

The APGA gathering, which brought together more than 1,000 party faithful from the seven local government areas and 118 wards, also underscored the importance of grassroots participation and sustained engagement with party structures.

In his appreciation message following the gathering, Nwachukwu thanked the party leaders, executives and stakeholders for their presence and contributions, reiterating his commitment to accessibility, inclusiveness and purposeful representation.

As political activities ahead of the 2027 elections continue to develop, the separate endorsements from APGA faithfuls and NDC stakeholders in Anambra South have brought renewed attention to the senator’s re-election bid and the arguments surrounding continuity in legislative representation.
