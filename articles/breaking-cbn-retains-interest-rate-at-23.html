BREAKING: CBN retains interest rate at 23%

By Punch Punch 2h ago 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Monetary Policy Committee of the Central Bank of Nigeria has retained the benchmark interest rate at 23 per cent amid a sustained decline in inflation. The

The CBN Governor, Olayemi Cardoso, announced the decision on Tuesday at the end of the committee’s 308th meeting in Abuja.

Cardoso said, “The Committee decided as follows: retain the monetary policy rate at 23 per cent.”

The decision follows the 350-basis-point reduction in the Monetary Policy Rate from 26.5 per cent to 23 per cent in September 2026.

The apex bank had earlier reduced the benchmark interest rate by 50 basis points in February before retaining it at subsequent meetings before the September cut.

The PUNCH had earlier reported that financial market analysts warned that renewed pressure on the naira, declining foreign exchange reserves and rising global interest rates could constrain the apex bank’s ability to further ease monetary policy despite moderating inflation.

The decision to retain the benchmark interest rate also comes amid five consecutive months of declining headline inflation in the country.

According to the most recent Consumer Price Index report released by the National Bureau of Statistics, Nigeria’s headline inflation rate eased to 14.06 per cent in October 2026 from 14.63 per cent in September, representing a decline of 0.57 percentage points.
