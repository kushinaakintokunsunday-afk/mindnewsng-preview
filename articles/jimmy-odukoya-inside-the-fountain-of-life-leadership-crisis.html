Jimmy Odukoya: Inside the Fountain of Life leadership crisis

By Punch Punch 6d ago 9 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Two groups within The Fountain of Life Church are making competing claims over the leadership of the Lagos-based ministry, following an announcement that Pastor

The leadership crisis at The Fountain of Life Church, Lagos, escalated on Tuesday, November 17, after a group of trustees announced that Pastor Jimmy Odukoya was no longer recognised as the church’s senior pastor, citing the late founder’s succession instructions.

The announcement was, however, rejected by the church, which described the purported removal as constitutionally invalid and maintained that Odukoya remained its duly appointed senior pastor and chairman of the Board of Trustees.

This disagreement later intensified after the trustees released a video in which the late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, reportedly named another person as his preferred successor and appointed his son to serve as an administrative assistant to that person.

The trustees also announced an interim leader, while citing concerns about Odukoya’s recent remarriage and other unresolved leadership and governance issues.

Here is what has happened so far.

Trustees announce Jimmy Odukoya’s removal

The controversy became public on Tuesday morning when some members of the church’s Board of Trustees announced that Odukoya’s tenure as senior pastor had ended with immediate effect.

In a statement reported by PUNCH on November 17, the trustees said the decision followed a review of the church’s constitution and recorded instructions left by its founder before his death in 2023.

They said the review was intended to ensure that the leadership transition reflected Taiwo Odukoya’s wishes and complied with the church’s governing provisions.

“Consequently, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya is no longer recognised as the Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church effective immediately,” the statement said.

The trustees described the decision as an institutional action taken in the interest of the church.

However, their initial announcement did not disclose the identity of the person they said the late founder had designated as his successor.

Church rejects the announcement, says removal is invalid

Hours after the announcement, The Fountain of Life Church issued a counterstatement rejecting the purported removal.

In a report also published on Tuesday, PUNCH quoted the church as describing the announcement as constitutionally invalid.

The church said the statement reflected the position of certain individuals who had constituted themselves into a group called “Concerned Members of the Board”, rather than a valid decision of its authorised trustees.

It maintained that Odukoya remained its duly appointed senior pastor and chairman of the Board of Trustees.

“For the avoidance of doubt, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya remains the duly appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees of The Incorporated Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church,” the statement said.

The church further maintained that his appointment was unanimously approved by the board in September 2023.

It also alleged that the trustees behind the removal announcement had been asked to resign on March 14, 2026, over alleged repeated violations of the church’s constitution and founding principles.

According to the church, one of the trustees subsequently resigned, while the remaining individuals continued to take decisions outside the church’s constitutional framework.

The church urged members and the public to distinguish between the pronouncements of the individuals behind the removal announcement and what it described as its constitutionally authorised position.

Trustees release video of Taiwo Odukoya’s succession instructions

The dispute took another turn later on Tuesday when the trustees released a video recording in which the late founder reportedly named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his preferred successor.

In the video, which PUNCH said was posted by Arise TV and sighted by its correspondent, Taiwo Odukoya stated that Areogun should take over the church if he died or became incapacitated.

He also outlined a role for his son, Jimmy, in the proposed leadership arrangement.

“In case of my demise, which I don’t expect to happen soon, or a situation where I cannot write or talk, please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun,” Taiwo said.

He added, “To be his immediate assistant, that is in terms of administration, is Jimmy Odukoya, who will be the head of the youth church.”

The late founder also said, “Everything remains the same and that’s laid out in the constitution.”

Explaining the recording, he said an aspect of the church’s constitution was being reviewed but had not been concluded.

“We’re going to do that, but if for any reason I’m not able to see that through, this is what I do,” he said.

According to the trustees, the recording was made in December 2021 at Taiwo’s request by Jimmy and witnessed by his elder brother, Pastor John Odukoya.

They claimed they did not see the video until late December 2025, more than two years after the founder’s death.

The trustees further alleged that Jimmy told the board in 2023 that his father had handed the leadership mantle to him, and that the video was not presented when the succession arrangements were being considered.

They argued that the recording could have influenced the board’s decision on his appointment.

Trustees appoint Rotimi Okpaise as interim leader

Following their announcement that Odukoya was no longer recognised as senior pastor, the trustees named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as the church’s interim spiritual and administrative leader.

PUNCH reported that the appointment was announced in a fresh statement on Tuesday, with the arrangement expected to remain in place pending consultations and the establishment of a substantive leadership structure.

The trustees said the interim arrangement was intended to provide continuity while the church addressed the leadership dispute.

They also reaffirmed their November 16 resolution withdrawing recognition of Odukoya as senior pastor.

The group further alleged that individuals acting on Odukoya’s behalf had taken control of the church’s official social media accounts without its authorisation.

It warned the public that posts on those platforms should not automatically be regarded as the board’s official position, insisting that only statements signed on its behalf represented its position.

The church, however, continued to reject the trustees’ authority to remove Odukoya.

Trustees cite Odukoya’s remarriage as part of their concerns

The succession disagreement is not the only issue raised by the trustees.

In their statement, they also expressed concerns about Odukoya’s recent remarriage and the public blessing of his marriage, describing the development as contrary to the church’s teachings, the founders’ values and the standards expected of its spiritual leadership.

The development followed Odukoya’s introduction of his new wife, Pastor Mo Odukoya, formerly identified as Mo Jasyme, to the congregation during a Sunday service on November 1, 2026.

PUNCH had reported that Odukoya announced the marriage during the service at the church’s headquarters in Ilupeju, Lagos, after posting a photograph of two hands wearing wedding rings with the caption, “To New Beginnings”.

Pastor Jimmy told the congregation that he had been alone since assuming leadership of the church before finding a suitable partner.

Pastor Tunde Bakare of The Citadel Global Community Church was also invited to bless the couple during the service.

PUNCH reported that Bakare said the couple had married in the United States.
Odukoya was previously married to Oluwakemi Banjoko, with whom he has two children.

Jimmy’s remarriage generated public debate, particularly over the circumstances surrounding the end of his previous marriage and differing Christian views on divorce and remarriage among ministers.

The trustees said the development had caused distress among some members and brought the church into public controversy.

The church’s counterstatement, however, did not accept the trustees’ purported removal decision.

Social media reactions reflect divisions over the dispute

The announcement also generated divergent reactions on social media, with users debating the church’s succession arrangements, Odukoya’s appointment and the timing of the trustees’ decision.

Some questioned why the alleged succession video was not considered before he was appointed in 2023, while others criticised the trustees for seeking to remove him after he had led the congregation for about three years.

One user, Dr Uzoma Alaneme, questioned the timing of the development, writing: “Dem no review the video since 2023 abi na the new marriage cause the wahala?”

Another user, @zed589662, also questioned the timing, writing: “He’s been their pastor for that long. What now changed? Lol.”

Some users also challenged the practice of transferring leadership positions in religious organisations to relatives of founders, while others expressed support for Odukoya.

The reactions reflected differing views about the church’s leadership succession, the trustees’ authority and the controversy surrounding the pastor’s remarriage.

How Jimmy Odukoya became senior pastora

The Fountain of Life Church was founded in 1992 by Pastors Taiwo and Bimbo Odukoya.

Bimbo died on December 11, 2005, from injuries sustained in the Sosoliso Airlines Flight 1145 crash in Port Harcourt.

Taiwo (Jimmy’s father) later married South African pastor Nomthi Zulu in 2010. She died in November 2021 after battling cancer for about two years.

Late Pastor Taiwo died on August 7, 2023, aged 67, in the United States.
Following his death, Jimmy was announced as the church’s senior pastor in September 2023 and formally installed on September 30, 2023.

His sister, Pastor Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, was appointed associate senior pastor.

The trustees now argue that the appointment did not reflect the succession arrangement contained in the late founder’s video, while the church maintains that Jimmy’s appointment was unanimously approved by the board.

What happens next?

As of the reports published on November 17, the two sides had not reached a publicly announced resolution.

The trustees maintained that Odukoya’s removal took immediate effect and appointed Okpaise as interim leader.

Church, on the other hand, insisted that the announcement was constitutionally invalid and that Odukoya remained its senior pastor and board chairman.

The dispute therefore goes beyond the announcement of a new leader.

It raises questions about the authority of the church’s trustees, the interpretation of its constitution, the validity of the 2023 appointment and how the late founder’s recorded succession instructions should be implemented.

Until the competing claims are resolved through the church’s recognised governance processes or other appropriate means, the leadership of The Fountain of Life Church remains contested.
