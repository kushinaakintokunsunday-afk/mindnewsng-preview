FULL LIST: Nigerian stars, others in 2027 Grammys nominations

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 25 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerian music stars Tems, Asake, Wizkid, Ayra Starr, Davido and Burna Boy have been nominated for the 2027 Grammy Awards, alongside international artistes incl

The nominations for the 69th edition of the annual awards were announced on Monday, with British singer-songwriter Raye emerging as the leading nominee with 11 nods.

Rosalía followed with nine nominations, while Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers each received seven. Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean secured six nominations apiece.

The 2027 awards feature 100 categories, including five new ones: Best R&B Collaboration or Duo/Group Performance, Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance, Best Traditional Folk Album, Best Latin Song and Best Asian Pop Music Performance.

The ceremony is scheduled to hold on February 7, 2027, at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, United States. It will be broadcast on ABC and streamed on Hulu and Disney+.

Nigerian artistes featured prominently in the African and global music categories, with Tems also earning recognition in the R&B category.

Tems was nominated for Best R&B Solo Performance for her song, What You Need, and Best R&B Song as a songwriter on the same track. She also received nominations for Best African Music Performance for Big Daddy and Best Global Music Album for Love Is a Kingdom.

Asake received two nominations in the Best African Music Performance category for Gratitude and Jogodo, the latter alongside Wizkid. Wizkid was also nominated in the category for Jogodo.

Ayra Starr earned nominations for Best African Music Performance for Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?), alongside Angélique Kidjo, and Best Global Music Album for Starrgirl.

Davido was nominated for Best Global Music Album for Oriadé, while Burna Boy earned a nomination for Best Global Music Performance for Dai Dai, alongside Shakira.
