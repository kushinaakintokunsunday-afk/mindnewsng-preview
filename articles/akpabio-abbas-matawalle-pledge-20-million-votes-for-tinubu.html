Akpabio, Abbas, Matawalle pledge 20 million votes for Tinubu

From Okwe Obi, Abuja

Ahead of the 2027 general polls, Senate President Godswill Akpabio; Speaker of the House of Representatives, Tajudeen Abbas; Minister of State for Defence, Bello Matawalle; and National Chairman of the All Progressives Congress (APC), Yilwadta Nantewa, have pledged to galvanise 20 million votes for the re-election of President Bola Tinubu through the Tinubu Again Movement (TAM).

They made the pledge at the unveiling of TAM in Abuja.

Akpabio noted that the achievements of President Tinubu were evident for naysayers to see and feel. He charged TAM ambassadors to ignore violence and to preach the good works of President Tinubu.

He said: “Now, all the stakeholders that are represented by the Director-General, I want you to know that you are only ambassadors of that. The country is gathered here today. Immediately after you step out of here, we are going to start the groundwork.

“We are going to be fishers of men and women. You are going to go from nook and cranny of our entire nation. Fish out the votes. You are already converted.

“So today, I want to ask that, why can’t you bring the 20 million votes? Twenty million votes are possible. They are possible. Once we are united as a group, this is what one man is able to do.

“The campaign is going down now. We are getting to the level where we are going to all the wards. Every support group will now have to move to the ward and unit.”

The Senate President claimed that money from subsidy and from other areas had been ploughed into meaningful projects.

“We used to have all sorts of exchange rates. Some people buy at 98 naira a dollar, go out there and sell at 300.

“The petrol price at 300 will go to the end user and sell at 650. And they will continue. They will go back to Sydney, make money, come back.

“At the end, people were buying plants without having a single stock. He stopped all that.

“For multiple exchange rates, he brought everything into one. And today, the tests are benefiting from the reform in the economic sector, in the financial sector,” he added.

The Speaker, Abbas, insisted that both appointed and elected members of the APC must inform the public of President Tinubu’s performance.

The lawmaker said: “It is the time that each and every one of us, most in our own little way, come out to support this government and to support this party.

“What Matawalle has done today is a typical example of a man who appreciates good leadership. It is not that he is the richest among the ministers. He is not the richest among the political appointees.

“He is not the richest among even members, elected members who are serving this government. But it is the will and determination and the passion to see that this government continue to succeed.”

The Minister of State for Defence added that the campaign flag-off was to energise “our executive from the national level down to the polling unit level for the task ahead as we approach 16th January 2027.

“The victory of TAM must double their effort and ensure that we deliver more than 20 million votes because we already projected 20 million votes, but we are determined to project more than that.

“The Tinubu Agenda Movement was conceived out of conviction of its founders and the supporters that the Renewed Hope Agenda of Mr President, Bola Tinubu, has made significant impact in various aspects, ranging from road and infrastructure, rail project, AKK gas project, student loan programme, agricultural support, empowerment programme and above all, the administration’s commitment to tackling insecurity and other economic problems across the country.”
