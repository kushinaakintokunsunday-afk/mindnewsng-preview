Afreximbank unit completes equity investment in D’Banj’s C.R.E.A.M. Platform

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 1d ago 2 min read

Photo: The Sun Nigeria

By Faheem Lawal

CANEX Creations Inc., a subsidiary of Afreximbank’s Fund for Export Development in Africa (FEDA), has completed a strategic equity investment in C.R.E.A.M. Platform Limited, the Nigerian digital platform founded by Afrobeats star D’Banj.

The completion was announced at a ceremony during CANEX WKND 2026 in Lagos on 6 November. CANEX Creations, set up to invest in, develop and commercialise African intellectual property, was represented by its Chief Executive Officer, Osahon Akpata. D’Banj, whose full name is Oladapo Daniel Oyebanjo, represented C.R.E.A.M., which he founded.

The investment closes out a deal first announced at the Afreximbank Annual Meetings in 2025, when the two sides signed a term sheet setting out the framework for the transaction.

The funds are expected to support the platform’s scaling, including the rollout of CREAM 3.0, expansion of services and content, and targeted marketing to grow users and revenue. C.R.E.A.M. says it will also gain institutional backing as it works to support African creative talent.

D’Banj cast the deal as bigger than a signing. “As I have always said, content is the new crude,” he said. “This is beyond a deal signing. It is history being made: Africa’s creativity becomes Africa’s capital. African creatives now have access to the financial infrastructure to compete globally, while owning and retaining our IP in Africa.”

C.R.E.A.M. gives independent creatives tools to showcase their skills, distribute content, connect with industry opportunities and earn income. Its monetisation channels include content distribution, caller ring back tunes, streaming platforms, copyright protection, royalty collection and intellectual property management.

Akpata said the investment reflects C.R.E.A.M.’s position as “an African-owned platform that discovers talent, distributes and monetises their creative content”, and fits CANEX Creations’ mandate to back commercially viable, high-impact projects in Africa’s creative and cultural industries. He said it also supports Afreximbank’s wider creative-economy strategy of building the infrastructure, platforms and market links that African creative businesses need to scale, and the Bank Group’s aim of turning creative potential into enterprises, jobs and income.

Akpata added that C.R.E.A.M.’s mobile-first, USSD-enabled model suits African market realities and reaches emerging and grassroots talent that international distribution platforms may underserve. He expressed confidence that the investment would strengthen the platform’s ability to connect creatives with discovery, distribution, audience engagement, monetisation and future financing, and support its expansion beyond Nigeria.
