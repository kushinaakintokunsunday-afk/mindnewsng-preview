Evening recap: Tinubu receives state police bill, NLC, TUC settle allowance dispute, other top stories

By Punch Punch 5d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu has received the constitutional amendment bill seeking to establish state police and will consult the Attorney-General before signing it.

The NLC and TUC have resolved their disagreement over the Federal Government’s N70,000 transport allowance.

The US has listed 11 categories of foreign applicants exempt from green card application fees.

An Oyo monarch has said there is no serious rift between Tinubu and Governor Seyi Makinde.

The late Pastor Taiwo Odukoya’s preferred successor, Kunle Areogun, has come into focus amid the Fountain of Life Church leadership dispute.

Three Nigerian Army officers have been decorated after their promotion to the rank of Colonel.

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke has disowned the renaming of a stadium after him and ordered the removal of its signpost.

Actress Mercy Aigbe has announced that she is expecting a baby.

See the top stories below: 

1. Tinubu receives state police bill, to consult AGF before assent

President Bola Tinubu has received the constitutional amendment bill seeking to establish state police and will consult the Attorney-General of the Federation before signing it. His Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila, said all 36 state assemblies had approved the bill, meeting the constitutional requirement for the amendment.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/tinubu-receives-state-police-bill-before-assent/

2. US lists foreigners exempt from green card application fees

The United States Citizenship and Immigration Services has identified 11 categories of foreign applicants who do not have to pay the filing fee for Form I-485, the application for permanent residence. The exemptions cover groups including refugees, certain military personnel, trafficking survivors and victims of qualifying crimes. Other applicants may also seek a fee waiver through a separate process.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/us-lists-categories-of-foreigners-exempt-from-green-card-application-fees/

3. NLC, TUC end public row over N70,000 workers’ allowance

The Nigeria Labour Congress and the Trade Union Congress have resolved their disagreement over the Federal Government’s N70,000 six-month transport allowance for workers. In a joint statement, the unions described their differences as a family misunderstanding and said their shared responsibility to workers should take precedence.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/nlc-tuc-end-public-disagreement-over-n70000-workers-allowance/

4. Army commandant decorates three senior officers in Lagos

The Commandant of the Nigerian Army College of Logistics and Management, Major General Adewale Adetoba, has decorated three officers recently promoted from lieutenant colonel to colonel. The ceremony was held at the college’s Ojo Campus in Lagos, where the commandant urged the officers to demonstrate professionalism, discipline and commitment in their new ranks.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/photos-army-commandant-decorates-three-senior-officers-in-lagos/

5. No serious rift between Tinubu, Makinde – Oyo monarch

The Olugbon of Orile-Igbon, Oba Francis Alao, has said there is no serious disagreement between President Bola Tinubu and Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde. He expressed confidence that any differences between the two leaders would be resolved before the 2027 general election.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/no-serious-rift-between-tinubu-makinde-oyo-monarch/

6. Meet Kunle Areogun, Taiwo Odukoya’s preferred successor

Pastor Kunle Areogun has emerged at the centre of the leadership dispute at The Fountain of Life Church following the release of a video in which its late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, named him as his preferred successor. The recording, reportedly made in 2021, has renewed questions about the church’s leadership and the position of Odukoya’s son, Jimmy.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/meet-kunle-areogun-taiwo-odukoyas-preferred-successor/

7. Adeleke disowns stadium renaming, orders signpost removed

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke has disowned the renaming of a stadium after him and ordered the removal of the signpost announcing the change.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/adeleke-disowns-stadium-renaming-orders-signpost-removed/

8. Mercy Aigbe announces pregnancy, shares baby bump photos

Nollywood actress Mercy Aigbe has announced that she is expecting a baby, sharing photographs showing her baby bump on Instagram. Her husband, film producer Kazim Adeoti, also celebrated the news, nearly five years after their marriage.

Read full story: https://punchng.com/photos-actress-mercy-aigbe-announces-pregnancy-flaunts-baby-bump/
