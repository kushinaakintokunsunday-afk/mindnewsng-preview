People sometimes forget celebrities are human too – Chuks Joseph

By Punch Punch 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Actor Chuks Joseph has urged fans to understand that celebrities are human beings with personal struggles and private lives, noting that being in the public eye

In an interview with Saturday Beats, Joseph said one of the unrealistic expectations fans had of celebrities was the assumption that public figures were always happy, financially comfortable and willing to attend to people’s demands.

“People sometimes forget that celebrities are human beings too. They have private lives, personal struggles, bad days, and moments when one simply wants to be themselves without public attention.”

“There’s also this expectation that because someone is in the public eye, every aspect of their life should be open for public consumption. Also, there is the phenomenon that everyone on TV or in the public eye has some money stacked up somewhere and will readily send it to people because they are public figures,” he said.

Speaking about the financial realities of his acting career, Joseph admitted that although acting had been fulfilling, it was not always as lucrative as people imagined.

“Honestly, it has been both fulfilling and challenging. Acting allows me to express myself, tell stories, and connect with people in ways that are difficult to explain. Financially, I think it is an industry that requires a lot of patience, consistency, and the right opportunities. It is not always as glamorous as people imagine, but the fulfilment I get from doing what I love makes the journey worthwhile. I am still growing, and I believe the best is yet to come,” he said.
