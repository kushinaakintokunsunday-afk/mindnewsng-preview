What to know about Efemena Umukoro, Urhobo youth leader killed in Delta attack

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Urhobo youth leader, Efemena Umukoro, popularly known as General Akima, was among four people reportedly killed when gunmen attacked the campaign team of former

Omo-Agege, the Nigeria Democratic Congress candidate for Delta Central Senatorial District in the 2027 elections, reportedly escaped unhurt.

The late Umukoro’s death has drawn attention to his public profile as a youth leader and activist, as well as to a September interview in which he spoke about surviving an earlier attack.

Here are some things to know about him.

1. He was an Urhobo youth leader

Umukoro was a prominent figure in the Urhobo youth community and served as President of the Urhobo Progress Union Youth Wing Worldwide.

He emerged as president in January 2020, having previously served as the youth body’s Secretary-General and Publicity Secretary.

His emergence was celebrated by members of the Orogun community, where he hailed from, during a reception organised in his honour in February 2020.

2. He was a human rights activist

Umukoro was also known for his involvement in youth advocacy, community affairs and human rights activities.

In 2020, the National Association of Nigerian Students Joint Campus Committee, Delta Axis, honoured him with its Icon of Hope and Inspiration Award in recognition of his advocacy for the welfare of young people, particularly students.

He also received a Pragmatic Leadership Award from Citizens Focus News in December 2022 for his advocacy for good governance and the protection of young people’s rights.

In May 2024, he called for a crackdown on armed youth groups in parts of Delta State, warning that criminal activities by youths could bring traditional institutions into disrepute.

He also cautioned against the establishment of unauthorised community security groups, saying youth leaders should not recruit people into security outfits that were not backed by law.

3. He was involved in community policing

Umukoro was also associated with community security initiatives in Delta State.

He was reportedly appointed to the Delta State Community Policing Committee as a representative of the National Youth Council of Nigeria.

His public activities included speaking on insecurity, youth welfare, and disputes affecting communities in the Niger Delta.

4. He went viral for saying knives could not penetrate him

A video of Umukoro speaking during an interview with News Central TV in September 2026 resurfaced after news of his death.

In the viral interview, he recounted an earlier attack on members of the NDC during a political mobilisation exercise in Delta State.

He said some attackers had blocked their road and that he was struck from behind with a weapon during the incident.

Speaking confidently about surviving the attack, he said: “I be youth leader na, how knife go enter me? I am an Orogun man. Knives cannot get into me. It is as simple as that.”

The clip, dated September 24, circulated widely on social media following reports of his death by gunfire.

5. He was involved in the NDC’s political activities

Umukoro was associated with the Nigeria Democratic Congress and was among the supporters involved in Omo-Agege’s political activities ahead of the 2027 elections.

He was identified as one of the aides of the former deputy Senate president in the statement issued by Omo-Agege’s campaign organisation following Monday’s attack.

6. He was killed during an attack on Omo-Agege’s campaign team

Umukoro was among four people reportedly killed when gunmen opened fire on Omo-Agege and members of his entourage during a campaign visit to Uduere community in Ughelli North Local Government Area on November 23.

In a statement issued by his media adviser, Sunny Areh, the campaign organisation identified Umukoro and Moses Anaughe as two of those killed.

It also said four other people sustained critical gunshot injuries during the incident.

The organisation described the attack as an assassination attempt on Omo-Agege, alleging that the attackers opened fire at close range while he was addressing supporters.

The Delta State Police Command said it had deployed operatives to the area, while the state government imposed a curfew across Ughelli North Local Government Area following the incident.

7. He became a father in May 2026

Umukoro also shared a personal milestone earlier in the year when he became a father for the first time.

Reports said he announced the birth of his son on May 15, 2026, about six months before his death.
