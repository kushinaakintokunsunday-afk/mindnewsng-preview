10 records Arsenal’s Max Dowman has broken at 16

By Punch Punch 6d ago 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

From becoming the Premier League’s youngest goalscorer to rewriting England’s under-21 records, here are 10 milestones achieved by Arsenal youngster, Max Dowman

From becoming the Premier League’s youngest goalscorer to rewriting England’s under-21 records, here are 10 milestones achieved by Arsenal youngster, Max Dowman.

At just 16, Dowman has already established himself as one of the most remarkable teenage talents in English football, setting records at club, domestic and international levels.

The midfielder has made history in the Premier League, UEFA Champions League and several England youth teams, surpassing milestones previously held by established footballers, including Jude Bellingham, Cesc Fabregas and Phil Foden.

Dowman’s latest achievements came during England’s under-21 international fixtures in November 2026, when he became the youngest player to feature and score for the Young Lions.

According to an article published by Arsenal on November 17, 2026, by Jonathon Rogers, the following are 10 remarkable records Dowman has set in his budding career.

1. Premier League’s youngest goalscorer

Dowman became the youngest goalscorer in Premier League history when he scored for Arsenal against Everton on March 14, 2026.

Aged 16 years and 73 days, he broke a record that had stood for almost 21 years, previously held by James Vaughan, who scored for Everton against Crystal Palace in April 2005 at 16 years and 270 days.

Dowman surpassed Vaughan’s milestone by 197 days.

2. Premier League’s youngest-ever starter

The Arsenal youngster also became the youngest player to start a Premier League match.

He achieved the feat when he was named in Arsenal’s starting XI against Crystal Palace on May 24, 2026, aged 16 years and 144 days.

He surpassed the previous record held by Everton’s Jose Baxter, who started against Blackburn Rovers in August 2008 at 16 years and 198 days.

3. Youngest player to win the Premier League

Dowman became the youngest player to win a Premier League title after Arsenal clinched the 2025/2026 championship.

Having made six top-flight appearances during the campaign, he met the requirements for a winner’s medal.

At 16 years and 144 days, he surpassed Manchester City’s Phil Foden, who was 17 years and 350 days old when he won the title in the 2017/2018 season.

4. Arsenal’s youngest-ever starter

Dowman became Arsenal’s youngest-ever starting player when he was named in the starting XI for the club’s Carabao Cup fourth-round encounter against Brighton on October 29, 2025.

He was just 15 years and 302 days old.

The record had previously belonged to Jack Porter, who started against Bolton Wanderers in September 2024 at 16 years and 72 days.

Dowman became the first player to start a match for Arsenal aged 16 or younger.

5. Arsenal’s youngest goalscorer

His goal against Everton in March 2026 also made him Arsenal’s youngest-ever goalscorer.

At 16 years and 73 days, Dowman surpassed former Arsenal midfielder Cesc Fabregas, who held the record after scoring against Wolves in a League Cup encounter in December 2003, aged 16 years and 212 days.

Dowman broke the record by 139 days.

6. Champions League’s youngest-ever player

Dowman became the youngest player to feature in the UEFA Champions League when he appeared for Arsenal against Slavia Prague on November 4, 2025.

Introduced in the 72nd minute of Arsenal’s 3-0 victory, he was aged 15 years and 308 days.

He broke the previous record held by Borussia Dortmund’s Youssoufa Moukoko, who was 16 years and 18 days old when he featured against Zenit St Petersburg in December 2020.

Dowman also became the first 15-year-old to feature in the competition since it was rebranded in 1992.

7. England’s youngest under-21 player

Dowman made history for England’s under-21 team when he made his debut against Portugal on November 13, 2026.

Aged 16 years and 320 days, he became the youngest player to represent the Young Lions at that level.

He surpassed the previous record held by Jude Bellingham, who made his under-21 debut against Kosovo in September 2020 at 17 years and 67 days.

8. England’s youngest under-21 goalscorer

Three days after his debut, Dowman added another record when he scored the winning goal against France in a 3-2 victory at Bramall Lane.

He found the net in the 89th minute of the encounter on November 16, 2026, aged 16 years and 323 days.

The goal made him England’s youngest-ever under-21 goalscorer, breaking another record previously held by Bellingham, who scored against Kosovo aged 17 years and 67 days.

9. UEFA Youth League’s youngest goalscorer

Before making his senior-team breakthrough, Dowman had already made history in the UEFA Youth League.

He became the competition’s youngest-ever goalscorer when he scored on his debut for Arsenal’s under-19 side against Atalanta on September 19, 2024.

He was just 14 years and 263 days old.

He remains ahead of Arsenal teammate Luis Munoz, who scored at 14 years and 265 days, and Borussia Dortmund’s Hamzath Mohamadou, who found the net at 14 years and 274 days.

10. Premier League 2’s youngest-ever player

Dowman also holds the record as the youngest player to feature in Premier League 2, the competition for clubs’ under-21 sides.

He made his appearance for Arsenal’s under-21 team against Brighton on December 1, 2024, aged 14 years and 336 days.

He became the first 14-year-old to feature for Arsenal’s reserve side, surpassing Ethan Nwaneri, who made his debut in the competition aged 15 years and 166 days.

More records within reach

Beyond the records already secured, Dowman could make further history in the coming months.

Arsenal noted that he has until February 2027 to challenge Ansu Fati’s record as the youngest goalscorer in Champions League history.

Fati was 17 years and 40 days old when he scored for Barcelona against Inter Milan in 2019.

He could also challenge Wayne Rooney’s record as England’s youngest senior international goalscorer if he scores for the Three Lions before November 12, 2027.

Although Dowman will not be able to break Theo Walcott’s record as England’s youngest player, his achievements across club and international football have already placed him among the most record-breaking teenagers in the game.
