2027: Opposition no threat to APC – Jigawa Renewed Hope coordinator

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Coordinator of the Renewed Hope Ambassadors in Jigawa State, Musa Shu’aibu, has said that no opposition party can threaten the ruling All Progressives Congress in the state and the country at large.

Shu’aibu stated this while fielding questions from journalists during the Jigawa Northeast APC Zonal campaign held on Monday in the Hadejia Local Government Area of the state.

The statement comes amid intensifying campaign activities across political parties, political groups and various candidates vying for elective positions ahead of the forthcoming 2027 general elections in the state.

According to him, the APC has performed credibly and has enough achievements to campaign with and win the confidence of Nigerians again.

He said the APC-led administration has offered a lot to Nigerians in different sectors of the economy.

“APC has achieved a lot in the areas of infrastructure, agriculture, social investment and economic reforms that directly touch the lives of the common man,” Shu’aibu said.

He explained that in Jigawa, the impact of the APC government is visible in road construction, school renovation, healthcare delivery and empowerment programmes.

Shu’aibu added that the Renewed Hope Ambassadors of President Bola Tinubu have further strengthened the party’s connection with the grassroots.

“These are not promises; they are realities that people are seeing and benefiting from,” he said.

When asked by our correspondent about the increasing pressure and activities of opposition parties in the state, the coordinator debunked fears of any threat.

He said the APC does not need to be afraid because it can depend on its achievements to seek re-election from the electorate in 2027.

“We will depend on our achievements to ask Nigerians for their votes again. We have something to show,” Shu’aibu said.

He added that unlike the opposition, the APC is not making empty promises but presenting verifiable projects and programmes.

Shu’aibu declared that “I can even brag that the APC is unshakable and not afraid of losing elections to any opposition political party.

“APC is unshakable. We are not afraid of opposition. No opposition party can threaten us,” he explained.

He urged party members and supporters to remain united and continue to mobilise more voters for the party, as he called on the people of Jigawa to continue to support the APC for more development and progress.

He expressed confidence that the party “will win all elective positions in Jigawa and retain power at the national level in 2027.”

Our correspondent reports that the Jigawa APC zonal campaign witnessed a large turnout of party supporters, stakeholders and candidates.
