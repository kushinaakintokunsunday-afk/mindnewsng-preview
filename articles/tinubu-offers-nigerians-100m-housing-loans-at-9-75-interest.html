Tinubu offers Nigerians ₦100m housing loans at 9.75% interest

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu has announced that eligible Nigerians can access mortgages of up to ₦100m at a fixed interest rate of 9.75 per cent, with a repayment peri

The President said the facility, provided through the ₦1tn MOFI Real Estate Investment Fund (MREIF), was part of his administration’s efforts to expand access to affordable housing and help Nigerians move from renting to homeownership.

Tinubu disclosed this in a statement on his verified X handle on Tuesday, explaining that beneficiaries could secure mortgages with an initial deposit of 10 per cent of the property’s value.

Using a ₦10m house as an example, he said a prospective homeowner would need an initial deposit of ₦1m and could repay approximately ₦85,400 monthly over 20 years.

According to the President, more than 2,300 Nigerians across 27 states have accessed ₦160bn in mortgages through MREIF.

“Imagine paying house rent for 25 years. Your children have grown up, you are approaching retirement, and you are still worried about the next rent increase. This is the reality for many hardworking Nigerians,” he said.

Tinubu said expanding access to affordable, long-term credit was a priority when he introduced his economic reforms in 2023, leading to the establishment of the ₦1tn MREIF.

He also announced that the Federal Mortgage Bank of Nigeria had been strengthened to provide eligible Nigerians with housing loans of up to ₦50m at an interest rate of six per cent, repayable over a maximum period of 30 years.

The President said contributors to the Contributory Pension Scheme could also qualify to use up to 25 per cent of their retirement savings towards their initial mortgage deposit.

He added that married applicants could submit joint applications with their spouses to increase their chances of accessing higher mortgage amounts.

“For Nigerians who cannot afford a mortgage, Family Homes Fund supports the delivery of affordable housing, with more than 53 social homes delivered for vulnerable and underserved households,” he said.

Tinubu also said the Renewed Hope Social Housing Programme was targeting 77,400 affordable homes across the country.

He disclosed that the government had delivered 100 free homes to widows in Kaduna and another 50 to the wives of fallen heroes in Ibadan.

The President further said more than 15,000 homes were under construction nationwide through the Renewed Hope Cities and Estates Programme.

He assured Nigerians that access to the housing initiatives would not depend on political connections or personal relationships with government officials.

“You should not need to know a minister, a governor or anybody in the Presidential Villa to apply and benefit from these opportunities.

“These programmes are for Nigerians who meet the requirements, wherever they live and whatever their beliefs,” he said.

Tinubu said he took particular satisfaction in seeing hardworking Nigerians become homeowners and families enjoy the security of owning their properties.

“That, too, is what my economic reforms are about. From Reforms to Opportunities,” he added.

The MREIF is a Federal Government-backed housing finance initiative designed to provide long-term funding for mortgages through participating financial institutions.

Its mortgage facility offers fixed-rate financing at 9.75 per cent, with a minimum equity contribution of 10 per cent and repayment periods of up to 20 years.

The Federal Mortgage Bank’s National Housing Fund mortgage facility, on the other hand, provides eligible contributors with loans for purchasing, building, improving or renovating residential properties.

Applications are processed through accredited mortgage institutions.
