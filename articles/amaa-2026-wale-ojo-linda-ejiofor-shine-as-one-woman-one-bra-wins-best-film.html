AMAA 2026: Wale Ojo, Linda Ejiofor shine as ‘One Woman One Bra’ wins Best Film

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 7 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nollywood actors Wale Ojo and Linda Ejiofor were among Nigeria’s biggest winners at the recently concluded 2026 Africa Movie Academy Awards. The 22nd edition of

The 22nd edition of the awards was held on Sunday, November 15, 2026, at the Marriott Hotel in Ikeja, Lagos State.

It featured several films, actors, directors and other industry figures who took home awards across 26 nominated categories.

Ojo won the award for Best Actor in a Leading Role for Cameroon’s Lights Out (2025), while Ejiofor won Best Actress in a Supporting Role for The Herd (2025).

Her latest win follows her recognition for both Best Lead Actress and Best Supporting Actress at the 2026 Africa Magic Viewers’ Choice Awards. There, she made history as the first person to win both categories in the same year.

Remarkably, the Kenyan-Nigerian co-produced film, One Woman One Bra (2025), won the top award, while The Color of Exile (2025) emerged as the most-awarded film of the night with four awards: Best Director, Achievement in Cinematography, Achievement in Sound and Best Actress in a Leading Role for Naky Savane.

Directed by Vincho Nchogu, One Woman One Bra won the coveted award for Best Film, Best Debut Feature Film by a Director, and the Ousmane Sembène Award for Best Film in an African Language.

According to Afrocritik, the film had its world premiere in the Biennale College Cinema section of the 82nd Venice International Film Festival and won the Sutherland Award for Best First Feature at the 2025 BFI London Film Festival.

Founded in 2005 by the late Peace Anyiam-Osigwe, the AMAA Awards reward excellence in African filmmaking, diaspora cinema and technical mastery.
