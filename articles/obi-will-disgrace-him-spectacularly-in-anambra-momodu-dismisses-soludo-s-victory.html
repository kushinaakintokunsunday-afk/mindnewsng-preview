‘Obi Will Disgrace Him Spectacularly In Anambra,’ Momodu Dismisses Soludo’s Victory Promise For Tinubu

A chieftain of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), Dele Momodu, has dismissed Anambra State Governor Chukwuma Soludo’s prediction that President Bola Tinubu will win the 2027 presidential election, insisting that Peter Obi will defeat the President in the state.

Momodu, the Deputy Director-General of Media and Communications of the ADC Presidential Campaign Council, said Obi would defeat Soludo “spectacularly” during the election.

“With due respect to Prof. (Soludo), he has joined those people I said were speaking like robots about Tinubu.

“I guarantee you that Peter Obi will disgrace him spectacularly during the election,” he said.

The former Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) presidential aspirant made the remarks on Thursday while appearing on Channels Television’s Politics Today.

Asked whether Obi could win Anambra despite Soludo and former governor Chris Ngige supporting Tinubu, and whether he had seen the crowd at the APC rally in the state, Momodu dismissed the significance of the gathering.

“It’s good to see the crowd, but I am telling you they are beginning to nauseate Nigerians, and that is why Nigerians are shouting ‘bamayi, bamayi’,” he said.

He also claimed that criticism of Tinubu was growing, with leaders increasingly expressing their dissatisfaction with the president.

On the APC’s campaign activities across the country, Momodu argued that the ruling party’s extensive mobilisation reflected its concerns about the outcome of the election.

“The only reason they are going everywhere feverishly is because they know they will lose the election. I am 66-plus. I have never seen this kind of campaign where the President is beginning to sound like an opposition leader,” he said.

Momodu also accused Tinubu of treating APC governors like “robots,” alleging that their public statements appeared coordinated.

“I monitored each of the governors. Let me tell you emphatically that they are being treated like robots. There is no question about it,” he said.

Soludo had earlier on Thursday declared that Tinubu did not need to rig the 2027 election because his administration’s performance would secure his re-election.

“To our people, this election, to the best of our knowledge, is already won and lost. The President doesn’t need to rig the election. President Tinubu doesn’t need to rig the election.

“All over the country today, in every state of Nigeria, it is President Tinubu and Shetima’s ticket versus one other party. Whereas you have some other candidates who are strong in some parts of the country but don’t exist in others,” he said at an APC presidential campaign rally in Anambra State attended by top party chieftains.

Peter Obi, who contested the 2023 presidential election on the platform of the Labour Party, won Anambra State and finished third nationally with more than six million votes.

He is battling for the nation’s top job again in 2027 under the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), and this time he is up against main contestants like Tinubu of the APC, Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress (ADC), and Omoyele Sowore of the African Action Congress (AAC).
