I’m very healthy, says Kompany after health scare

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Bayern Munich manager Vincent Kompany has declared himself “very healthy” as he prepares to return to the dugout on Friday following his recent hospitalisation

Bayern Munich manager Vincent Kompany has declared himself “very healthy” as he prepares to return to the dugout on Friday following his recent hospitalisation over a health scare.

The 40-year-old former Manchester City captain was taken to hospital last month after falling ill while watching Bayern Munich’s second team train.

He was subsequently diagnosed with meningeal irritation, an inflammation of the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

Speaking at a news conference on Thursday, Kompany expressed gratitude to the club, medical personnel, players, coaches and supporters for their encouragement and support during his recovery.

“It was very intense. At the time, it was a new experience. Looking back, everything went perfectly. I received support right away, from the club and the doctors. I’m very grateful,” he said.

The Belgian tactician said medical examinations returned positive results, assuring supporters that he had recovered fully and was feeling well ahead of his return.

“You try to stay calm and wait until you have the results. It was a positive process, all the tests were positive.

“I have to say, I’m very healthy. We did a lot of tests, and they all came back fine. In those quiet moments, you also try to learn.

“Now I feel very good again and I’m happy to be back with the team,” he added.

During Kompany’s absence, his assistant, Aaron Danks, took charge of the team, guiding Bayern to four victories and one draw.

Kompany also acknowledged the support he received from fellow coaches across the football community, including those he had previously competed against.

“I’d also like to thank the coaching community. We’re always competing, but almost every coach has reached out to me, even if we’ve played against each other and perhaps argued as well. That’s a beautiful thing,” he said.

Bayern Munich, who have England captain Harry Kane in impressive form, currently sit at the top of the Bundesliga standings, level on points with Borussia Dortmund.

The defending German champions are scheduled to face Cologne on Friday following the conclusion of the two-week international break, with Kompany expected to return to the touchline.
