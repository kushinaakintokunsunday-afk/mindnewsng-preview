Two men convicted of sexually assaulting 14-year-old girl on UK train

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Inner London Crown Court has found two Afghan nationals guilty of sexually assaulting a 14-year-old girl on a train in Kent. Baryalai Rahimi, 25, of Southal

Baryalai Rahimi, 25, of Southall, west London, and his friend, Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, of Cricklewood, northwest London, were both convicted by the court. However, only Rahimi was charged with rape.

According to a statement by the British Transport Police on Wednesday, the convictions follow a comprehensive investigation led by its detectives from its Major, Serious and Organised Crime team.

The police account noted that the duo subjected the teenage girl to a “heinous and prolonged” sex attack on a train into London.

The jurors were informed that Rahimi and Sadat approached the girl at Sturry station near Canterbury, Kent, where they all boarded a London-bound train at 3 pm.

While on board, evidence showed that Rahimi was the first to “force himself upon her and sexually assault her.”

The police said Rahimi took the girl into one of the train’s toilets and “raped her.

Afterwards, his friend, Sadat, also sexually assaulted her.

“A short while later, Rahimi walked down the carriage and left the girl alone with Sadat –he too then subjected her to a sustained sexual assault,” the police said.

The statement noted that throughout the attack, the girl “told both men ‘no’ but was ignored. She also told them she was 14 years old.”

Upon the train’s arrival at London Bridge, the men followed the girl onto a train to Peterborough before Rahimi got off at St Pancras, and Sadat at Finsbury Park.

“The victim engaged with police at Peterborough station and bravely told officers what they did to her” the statement said.

It added that after the victim’s account, the BTP detectives launched an urgent probe to hunt down both men.

On February 10, detectives arrested Rahimi and Sadat in West London, using the victim’s account and CCTV footage.

“A file including forensic evidence, phone data and CCTV mapping was submitted to the Crown Prosecution Service (CPS), and detectives were authorised to charge the pair.

“After a two-week trial at Inner London Crown Court, a jury found the men, who are both Afghan nationals, guilty on nine counts.

“Baryalai Rahimi, 25, of Southall, west London, was found guilty of rape, sexual assault, and three counts of sexual activity with a child.

“Mirebadurahman Sadat, 29, of Cricklewood, northwest London, was found guilty of assault by penetration, sexual activity with a child, and two counts of causing a person to engage in sexual activity,” read the statement.

The duo will be sentenced at the same court on December 18, 2026.

BTP Detective Constable, Gemma Littlewood, lamented the attack, saying both men, described as “two dangerous predators,” have caused “irreparable harm to a child.

“They deliberately targeted her travelling alone, then conspired together so Rahimi could rape her and Sadat could sexually assault her.

“They knew her age, they knew she was vulnerable, and they heard her say no, but none of that stopped them from subjecting her to a heinous and prolonged attack.”

The detective further appreciated the victim for her “exceptional courage” in reporting the incident.

He noted that the police operatives are committed to “tackling sexual violence against women and girls.”

“To report something to us, text 61016, or call 999 in an emergency,” he urged the public.
