Tinubu hails Jibrin’s economic diversification blueprint, says 572-page book enriches national discourse

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG

From Juliana Taiwo-Obalonye, Abuja

President Bola Tinubu has praised Abdulmumin Jibrin, a member of the House of Representatives, for his contribution to Nigeria’s economic policy discourse. Tinubu described Jibrin’s research on economic diversification and structural transformation as a valuable addition to national knowledge.

Tinubu said Jibrin’s 572-page book, Economic Diversification and Structural Transformation in Nigeria: The Past, The Present, The Future, offered important insights into the country’s economic evolution and ongoing efforts to broaden its economic base.

The President gave the commendation on Thursday when he received the lawmaker at his residence in the State House, Abuja. Jibrin represents the Kiru/Bebeji Federal Constituency of Kano State.

Jibrin visited Tinubu to present copies of the book, which was officially launched on Tuesday at the State House Conference Centre.

According to a statement by the President’s Special Adviser on Information and Strategy, Bayo Onanuga, Tinubu commended the depth of research behind the publication. He noted that it documented Nigeria’s past attempts at economic diversification and the present administration’s efforts towards structural transformation.

The President also observed that Jibrin’s legislative experience, particularly in finance and appropriation, enriched the publication and strengthened its relevance to national economic conversations.

He said the book and the initiatives unveiled alongside it aligned with his administration’s commitment to documenting research-based knowledge for the benefit of Nigerians and the wider global community.

“These noble initiatives are in consonance with our commitment towards the documentation of researched knowledge for the benefit of citizens and humanity who seek to understand the nation’s continued effort at economic diversification,” Tinubu said.

He added that the initiatives were also consistent with his administration’s commitment to empowering citizens through access to relevant knowledge and skills for improved livelihoods and prosperity.

The book launch also featured the unveiling of Jibrin’s Global Centre for Policy Implementation and Sustainable Development and his foundation, marking his 50th birthday.

The Speaker of the House of Representatives, Tajudeen Abbas, represented Tinubu at the launch.

Jibrin is currently Chairman of the House Committee on Housing and Habitat.
