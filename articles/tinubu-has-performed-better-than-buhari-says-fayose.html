Tinubu has performed better than Buhari, says Fayose

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Former Ekiti State Governor, Ayodele Fayose, has compared President Bola Tinubu’s administration with that of his predecessor, the late Muhammadu Buhari, saying

Former Ekiti State Governor, Ayodele Fayose, has compared President Bola Tinubu’s administration with that of his predecessor, the late Muhammadu Buhari, saying the former has performed better.

He said his conviction was based on his experience serving as governor under the Buhari-led administration, describing those who disagreed with his assessment as people driven by hatred for Tinubu’s government.

Fayose stated this during an interview on TVC on Monday.

Speaking about the achievements of Tinubu’s administration and why Nigerians should support his re-election bid in 2027, the former governor cautioned that no president could meet the expectations of all Nigerians.

“There’s no perfect president. There’s no president in Nigeria who will meet the yearnings of everybody. It’s not possible. You can’t meet the yearnings of your family.

“I laugh when people say that his government is worse than Buhari’s government. I was a critic of Buhari, and I know what I’m saying. I was a governor in Buhari’s time. I know what I’m talking about. But if anybody living today says Tinubu has not done better than Buhari, that is… I consider that either hate or political lineage that taught you. You can’t praise your enemy. I can say that,” Fayose said partly.

In his inaugural address on May 29, 2023, shortly after assuming office, Tinubu announced the removal of the petrol subsidy.

The government subsequently harmonised and floated the foreign exchange market, triggering significant adjustments in petrol prices and exchange rates, with consequential effects on the cost of living and doing business.

Fayose, however, acknowledged that although the achievements of the present administration might not have reached the “perfect” level expected by Nigerians, the economy was recovering, adding that the process of recovery “is not easy”.

“Tinubu has stabilised the economy. He might not be to the extent everybody will say, he’s perfect, or he’s getting there. When things go bad, recovery is not easy.

“When the economy fails, recovery is not easy. I’m not saying Tinubu is perfect. I have not hidden my support for him, because I’ve seen him through. He has done so well, so far, so good. But there must be contest against him. Somebody must contest against him. Somebody must say ill of him to win an election,’ he added.

Fayose cited some of the present administration’s efforts to improve the living conditions of Nigerians and strengthen the economy, including the Nigerian Education Loan Fund and the removal of the petrol subsidy.

Since the launch of its student loan portal on May 24, 2024, NELFUND has disbursed a total of N375.28bn as of Monday, November 9, 2026, comprising N194.77bn in institutional fees and N180.50bn in upkeep allowances.

“Are you telling me Tinubu has done well on loans? Who remembers students in loans? Have we not gone as far as N1,670 to the dollar? Has it not gone down? Today, the dollar is under N1,400. Yes, it’s not perfect, but it’s better.

“The removal of the subsidy. Is it not better that the subsidy, whatever gains they make out, is now fairly in the space to make sure that salaries are paid, than the money to go to the hands of a few minorities?.

“If you even see those people criticising Tinubu, when you ask them, would you reverse it (the fuel subsidy)? They get confused. They don’t know what to say. ‘Today, I will reverse. Tomorrow, I will not reverse.’ You understand? So, you see, this is politics. Nigeria is not just a country you govern at will,” Fayose said, among other things.

Speaking about the opposition ahead of the 2027 elections, Fayose lamented that politicians made numerous promises during campaigns to secure electoral victory.

He said Nigerians would have to decide whom to vote for between the incumbent, Tinubu; Peter Obi of the Nigeria Democratic Congress; and former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar of the African Democratic Congress.

He said, “Nigeria today is like a young lady. Everybody comes in election season to say, ‘I would like you. I would do everything for you. Just leave your boyfriend and come to me. I will do everything for you. Don’t worry.’

“It is the decision of the young girl whether to leave a Tinubu for an Atiku, or from an Atiku to an Obi. It’s a decision they have to make. So everybody is just saying what they love to say, in their own interest.

“Atiku has had his time. I will not criticise him for contesting and contesting. If you have the means, if you have the resources, if you feel you can still make it through, fine. But if you don’t make it through, take it in good strides. We should stop hitting the system.

“No Nigerian president will be in the seat. It’s not common. Yes, it happened in the days of Jonathan, but it’s not common to be removed cheaply. Not because you want to rig, but because you put in everything humanly possible to retain your seat.”
