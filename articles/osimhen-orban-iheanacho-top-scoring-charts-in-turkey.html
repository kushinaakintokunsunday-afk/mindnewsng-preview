Osimhen, Orban, Iheanacho top scoring charts in Turkey

By Punch Punch 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Kelechi Iheanacho, Gift Orban and Victor Osimhen are leading the scoring charts in Turkey, with the trio combining for 37 goals this season, PETER AKINBO writes

Kelechi Iheanacho, Gift Orban and Victor Osimhen are leading the scoring charts in Turkey, with the trio combining for 37 goals this season, PETER AKINBO writes

Iheanacho (Bursaspor) – 17 goals

Iheanacho has 17 goals and three assists in 14 appearances for Bursaspor in the Turkish 1. Lig since joining the club as a free agent after leaving Celtic. He scored in each of his first 12 games, but the run ended when he failed to find the net in the 3-0 win over Bandırmaspor on October 31.

He was back on the scoresheet in the 1-1 draw at Antalyaspor on November 6, and he has played 1,206 minutes in the league so far.

The 30-year-old has also picked up two yellow cards. His tally has long since passed the 12 league goals he scored for Leicester City in the 2020/21 season, his previous best, and Bursaspor are top of the table.

Osimhen (Galatasaray) – 10 goals

Osimhen has 10 goals and three assists in 11 appearances in all competitions for Galatasaray. Nine of the goals have come in eight Süper Lig games, while the other was in the 4-2 win at Lille, the club’s first victory of their Champions League campaign. He scored twice in the 2-1 win over Fenerbahçe on October 26 and added another in the 4-0 win at Konyaspor on October 30.

The striker has missed four of Galatasaray’s 12 league games, two of them with a groin strain and two more after arthroscopy. He was also absent for the 4-3 win over Amedspor on November 7 and the 2-1 victory over Samsunspor on Saturday.

Even without him, the reigning champions made it 10 wins from 12 league games and stayed top as they chase a fifth straight title. The win over Samsunspor also stretched their unbeaten home run in the Süper Lig to 42 games, a new club record that surpassed the 41 set between 2017 and 2019. The all-time league record is 90, set by Trabzonspor in 1981.

Orban (Amedspor) – 10 goals

Orban has 10 league goals and five assists in 12 appearances for newly promoted Amedspor, who are on loan from Hoffenheim. His goals include a hat-trick in the 5-0 win over Başakşehir, a brace in the 2-2 draw with Kasımpaşa and a strike in the 3-2 win over Beşiktaş. He also scored in the 5-1 win over Eyüpspor and the 2-1 win at Samsunspor.

He reached 10 goals in 11 games, a total that, according to Afrik-Foot, triggered an obligation-to-buy clause in his loan agreement. Amedspor are now set to pay Hoffenheim €3m to make the deal permanent, and they have reportedly ruled out letting him leave in January. Fenerbahçe, Trabzonspor and Istanbul Başakşehir have all been linked with interest.

The 24-year-old went goalless in the 2-0 home defeat to Çaykur Rizespor on Saturday, when a first-half effort went wide in the 33rd minute. He has said before that the Premier League is his “ultimate dream”. For now, he is one goal ahead of Osimhen in the Süper Lig scoring race.

Why Nigerians thrive in Turkey

Nigerian players have a long history in the Turkish top flight, and former Super Eagles midfielder Ogenyi Onazi believes the conditions suit them. Onazi, who played for Trabzonspor between 2016 and 2020, said culture, food, weather and passionate supporters make it easy for players to settle.

“Most Nigerian transfers to Turkey have been successful because the environment and culture help Nigerians settle in and do their best,” the 33-year-old told Footy-Africa.

Other Nigerians making their mark in the country include Paul Onuachu, Wilfred Ndidi and Ibrahim Olawoyin.
