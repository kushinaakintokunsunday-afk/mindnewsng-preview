Nigerians need solutions, not presidential pity party, Atiku’s aide tells Tinubu

Phrank Shaibu, media aide to ADC presidential candidate, Alhaji Atiku Abubakar, has criticised President Bola Tinubu over remarks that the Presidential Villa in Abuja suffers the same electricity and water shortages faced by ordinary Nigerians.

Tinubu made the remarks on Sunday during a special prayer session organised by members of his family at the Yoruba Tennis Club in Onikan, Lagos.

The event followed his attendance at an All Progressives Congress (APC) presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State, the previous day as part of his 2027 re-election efforts.

Tinubu said the Presidential Villa experiences the same infrastructure failures that affect many Nigerians and vowed to address the country’s deficits in power and water supply.

Recounting a recent incident, the president said, “On the Wednesday of last week, I was shaving, and the water went off. And the villa, you won’t know punishment there too. The water went off. So we will correct all this. No light, no water, no electricity, no development. We have just started. We can do it, and we will do it for Nigeria.”

The comments have drawn criticism from opposition figures.

In a post on X on Monday, Shaibu questioned whether the comments amounted to a confession of failure more than three and a half years into Tinubu’s presidency.

He argued that Nigerians did not elect the president to join the ranks of those affected by poor governance, but to fix the problems.

“You cannot preside over a crisis and then seek sympathy from those bearing its consequences.

“Nigerians need solutions, not a presidential pity party. If even Aso Rock is suffering, what exactly is working?” Shaibu said.

He added that Tinubu should stop “mocking” Nigerians and instead own the failures of his administration, describing the president as “the President for now, not a fellow spectator.”
