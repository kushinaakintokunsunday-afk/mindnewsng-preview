FULL LIST: World Cup-winning Flamingos coach Busari nominated for CAF award

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigeria’s U-17 women’s national team coach, Akeem Busari, has been nominated for the 2026 Confederation of African Football Women’s Coach of the Year award. Bus

Busari’s nomination follows his achievement with the Flamingos, who won the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup for the first time after beating Japan 2-1 in the final in Rabat, Morocco, on November 7.

The Nigeria coach is among 10 nominees announced by CAF on Tuesday in its final list of nominees for the women’s categories ahead of the 2026 CAF Awards.

Other nominees include Farid Benstiti of Algeria, Silveria Nedio of Cabo Verde, Valentine Nguele of Cameroon, Kim Lars Bjorkegren of Ghana, Lovemore Fazili of Malawi, Hicham Drissi of AS FAR, Jorge Vilda of Morocco, Desiree Ellis of South Africa and Bakari Nyundo Shime of Tanzania.

CAF said the final three women’s categories were announced after the conclusion of the CAF Women’s Champions League in Egypt on Sunday, November 15.

“The three categories were held back to ensure that performances at Africa’s premier women’s club competition could be considered before the nominees were finalised,” the continental football governing body said.

The other two categories are Women’s Interclub Player of the Year and Women’s Club of the Year.

The ceremony will hold at the Professor Wole Soyinka Centre for Culture and the Arts, popularly known as the National Theatre, in Lagos on Wednesday, December 2, beginning at 7pm local time.

Voting for the awards is taking place from November 5 to 20, with CAF member associations, clubs and eligible coaches and captains participating in the process.
