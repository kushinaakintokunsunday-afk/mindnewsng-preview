Tinubu Rewards Flamingos With N100 Million Each After Historic FIFA U-17 World Cup Triumph - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Mindnewsng 12 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Mindnewsng

President Bola Tinubu has approved N100 million for each player of Nigeria's Flamingos after they became the first African nation to win the FIFA U-17 Women's W

President Bola Tinubu has rewarded each member of Nigeria's victorious Flamingos with N100 million, held in five-year Federal Government savings bonds, after the teenage stars made history by winning the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco.

The reward was announced by First Lady Oluremi Tinubu, speaking on behalf of the President at a State House reception in Abuja. She explained that the money would be preserved in trust for the young players rather than handed to them immediately.

"Do you know what a savings certificate is? It is money, but it is in trust for you. You are still very young. Do you want to start handling N100 million now?" she told the squad.

The full reward package

  • N100 million each for every player, in five-year savings bonds
  • N100 million cash for head coach Akeem Busari
  • N50 million each for members of the technical crew

The announcement came a day after the Senate donated N100 million to the team, with Senate President Godswill Akpabio personally distributing the cash reward at a separate reception.

History makers

The Flamingos defeated Japan 2-1 in the final in Rabat last Saturday, winning six consecutive matches to claim the title. Their victory made Nigeria the first African nation ever to win the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup - and the first African women's team to win a FIFA World Cup title at any level.

The First Lady also specially celebrated Awawu Bashiru and Oluebube Umejiaku, whose decisive goals sealed the triumph, and urged state governments and private well-wishers to add their own recognition.

"You have elevated Nigerian football, inspired millions of young girls and opened a new chapter for women's football in Africa," she said.

According to officials, the bonds will mature when the players reach adulthood, paying each roughly N1.1 million monthly in the interim while keeping the principal safe.
