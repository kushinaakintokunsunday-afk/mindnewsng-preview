Katsina suspends Emir of Daura’s engagements over assault allegation, launches investigation

By Fauziyya Lawal, Katsina

The Katsina State Government has constituted an investigative committee to probe allegations of sexual assault involving the Emir of Daura, Alhaji Umar Faruk Umar, and a foreign tourist.

The government also announced the suspension of the traditional ruler’s official engagements and travels pending the outcome of the investigation.

The decisions were contained in a statement issued on Tuesday by the Commissioner for Information and Culture, Dr Bala Salisu Zango, on behalf of the state government.

According to the statement, Governor Dikko Umaru Radda expressed serious concern over the allegations, describing the matter as one requiring careful attention and appropriate action.

The governor consequently directed the immediate constitution of an investigative committee to examine the allegations and advise the government on the appropriate course of action.

The statement said the decision was informed by the seriousness of the allegations and the need to establish the facts surrounding the reported incident.

The government assured the public that justice would be served, adding that the findings and recommendations of the committee would be fully implemented.

It said the suspension of the Emir’s official engagements and travels was among the measures taken by the government while the investigation lasted.

The government also appealed to residents and members of the public to remain calm, law-abiding and refrain from actions capable of disrupting public peace.

It urged residents to continue with their normal activities, reaffirming its commitment to handling the matter appropriately.

The outcome of the committee’s investigation is expected to guide further action by the state government.
