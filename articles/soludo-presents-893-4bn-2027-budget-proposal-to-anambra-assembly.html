Soludo presents ₦893.4bn 2027 budget proposal to Anambra Assembly

By Punch Punch 2h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Anambra State Governor, Prof. Chukwuma Soludo, has presented a proposed budget of ₦893,435,059,830 for the 2027 fiscal year to the state House of Assembly. The

The budget, themed “Solution 2.0: Unlocking New Axes for Sustainable Prosperity,” represents a 16.58 per cent increase over the 2026 fiscal estimate.

Presenting the document to the lawmakers on Tuesday, Soludo said the theme reflected his administration’s commitment to expanding its development vision for the state.

The governor expressed gratitude to the people of the state for their support in the last election and acknowledged the partnership his administration had enjoyed with the legislature.

Reviewing his administration’s performance, Soludo cited achievements in security, healthcare delivery and sustainable infrastructure, among other sectors.

He said, “The success of the state’s free education policy, which currently benefits nearly 500,000 Anambra children, and plans to recruit 5,000 new teachers to build on the 8,115 teachers initially employed, are part of the achievements recorded under the previous budget.

“On fiscal discipline, the state has not borrowed from any commercial bank so far. We assure lawmakers and citizens that any future borrowing, if necessary, will be strictly guided and economically justified.”

In his remarks, the Speaker of the Anambra State House of Assembly, Somtochukwu Udeze, commended the governor for his developmental strides across various sectors of the state’s economy.

He also appreciated the upgrade of the Assembly complex, noting that the improvements had provided a more conducive environment for legislative business.

Highlighting the productivity of the current Assembly, Udeze said 44 bills had been passed, including 26 executive bills. He added that several of the laws could serve as legislative templates for other states.

While calling for the continuation of the productive relationship between the executive and legislative arms of government, the speaker stressed the need for both arms to respect their constitutional boundaries.

He expressed hope that the 2027 budget would address the pressing needs of the people and make a meaningful impact on their lives.
