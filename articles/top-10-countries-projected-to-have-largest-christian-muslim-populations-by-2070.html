TOP 10: Countries projected to have largest Christian, Muslim populations by 2070

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Muslims are projected to overtake Christians as the world’s largest religious group by 2035, with Nigeria expected to rank among the countries with the largest

This is according to two reports published by the Pew Research Center on November 18, 2026, examining projected changes in the global Christian and Muslim populations between 2020 and 2070.

The reports said the global Muslim population would grow faster than other religious groups, while Christianity would maintain a relatively stable share of the world’s population despite significant changes in its geographical distribution.

Pew projected that Muslims would increase by 80 per cent between 2020 and 2070, compared with an 11 per cent rise in the non-Muslim population.

“Muslims are the world’s fastest-growing religious group – a trend that’s expected to continue in the coming decades,” the report on the future of the global Muslim population stated.

Countries with the largest Muslim populations

In 2020, Indonesia had the world’s largest Muslim population, with 239 million followers, followed by Pakistan with 227 million and India with 213 million.

Bangladesh and Nigeria completed the top five, with 151 million and 120 million Muslims, respectively.

By 2070, Pakistan is projected to take the top position with 461 million Muslims, followed by India with 334 million and Nigeria with 282 million.

Indonesia is expected to drop to fourth place, with 274 million, while Bangladesh is projected to rank fifth with 218 million.

Pew said Nigeria was expected to climb to third place globally by 2070, while Ethiopia and Afghanistan would enter the top 10.

It also projected that sub-Saharan Africa would account for 27 per cent of the world’s Muslim population by 2070, compared with 18 per cent in 2020.

Countries with the largest Christian populations

The United States had the world’s largest Christian population in 2020, with an estimated 217 million followers.

Brazil ranked second with 168 million, followed by Mexico with 113 million, the Philippines with 103 million and Russia with 102 million.

However, Pew projected that the Democratic Republic of the Congo would overtake the US by 2070, becoming the country with the largest Christian population, estimated at 299 million.

The US is projected to fall to second place with 174 million Christians, while Ethiopia would rank third with 150 million.

Pew projected that several sub-Saharan African countries, including Nigeria, Tanzania, Uganda and Angola, would rise in the rankings by 2070.

The report said the number of Christians in sub-Saharan Africa would more than double, from 697 million in 2020 to 1.6 billion in 2070.

“By 2070, 54% of all Christians are expected to live on the African continent south of the Sahara Desert and its nearby islands, up from 31% in 2020,” it stated.

The projections also indicated that Christians would no longer constitute a majority in the United States and nine other countries by 2070.

Pew said the projections were based on demographic trends, including age distribution, fertility, mortality, migration and religious switching.

The research covered 201 countries and territories, representing 99.98 per cent of the world’s population.
