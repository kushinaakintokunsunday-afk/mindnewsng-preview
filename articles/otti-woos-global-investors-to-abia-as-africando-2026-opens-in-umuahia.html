Otti woos global investors to Abia, as AfrICANDO 2026 opens in Umuahia

From Okey Sampson, Umuahia

Abia State Governor, Dr. Alex Otti has invited the global investment community to Abia, to explore the vast economic opportunities in the State, with the assurance to prospective investors that all investment opportunities in Abia are not only promising, but offer prospects for long-term value creation.

Governor Otti gave the invitation while declaring open the US-Africa Trade and Investment Conference and Expo, AfrICANDO 2026, at Umuahia, the state capital.

In his speech titled, “Access, Opportunities and Prosperity,” Otti said Abia has witnessed significant developments across its 17 local government areas and various sectors of the economy, creating an enabling environment for investment and business partnerships.

“There is so much going on in Abia, across the local government areas, and in all the sectors of our economic and social expressions.

“All the investment opportunities in the State are promising and have been consciously set up to create long-term value for interested investors seeking to partner with our community of resilient entrepreneurs.”

Otti identified the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA), enacted by the United States Congress in 2000, and the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), established through the African Union in 2018 and adopted by 54 member States, as important frameworks for promoting sustainable economic growth.

He told the participants that his deliberate investments in public infrastructure, security and social services have repositioned Abia, particularly Aba, as an emerging investment destination.

The Governor lauded the Foundation for Democracy in Africa (FDA) and the Abia Global Chamber of Commerce for bringing the first AfrICANDO event to be hosted on the African continent to Abia State.

Otti said the conference was of great importance to artisans, small business owners, industrialists, investors and multilateral development partners.

He informed that local government areas, government ministries and departments, manufacturers, agricultural entrepreneurs and other business interests were participating in the exhibition to showcase investment opportunities to facilitate partnerships.

He urged participants to take advantage of the conference to establish business relationships and conclude investment agreements.

The President of the Foundation for Democracy in Africa, Dr. Fred Oladeinde; the Mayor of Miami-Dade County, United States of America, Hon. Daniella Levine Cava; the representative of the Democratic Republic of Congo, Dr. Milenge Mwenelwata; and the Head of Sub-Office, Lagos, of the International Organization for Migration (IOM), Mr. Ali Ibrahim Ali, in their speeches, commended Governor Otti for his visionary leadership and commitment to economic development.

While appreciating the State for hosting the international conference, they highlighted the importance of international partnerships and intra-African trade.

Dr. Oladeinde in particular, announced plans to collaborate with the Abia State Government and other partners to establish an Africa Trade Development Centre in the State.

In the same vein, Hon. Levine Cava spoke of the readiness of Miami-Dade County to strengthen its partnership with Abia State through the Miami-Dade Gateway to Africa initiative, with emphasis in expanding trade, attracting investment and promoting innovation.

The Chairman of the Local Organising Committee (LOC) of AfrICANDO 2026 and Chief of Staff to the Governor, Caleb Ajagba, had earlier in his address described the 27th Annual US-Africa Trade and Investment Conference as a strategic platform for unlocking Africa’s economic potential, attracting investments and accelerating industrial development.

Ajagba noted that Abia hosting of the international event reflects Governor Otti’s commitment to creating an enabling business environment and positioning the State as an attractive investment destination.

Equally, the Chairman of the Abia State Diaspora Commission, Dr. Okey Agbara, and the Chairman, Abia Global Chamber of Commerce, Mr. James Umekwe, said AfrICANDO 2026 is a practical economic opportunity for connecting local manufacturers, agricultural processors and service providers with international buyers, investors and technical partners.

They said the conference would promote export opportunities, boost local production, create jobs and strengthen Nigeria’s access to regional and global markets through AfCFTA and AGOA.

Similarly, the former Chief of Army Staff, Lt. Gen. Azubuike Ihejirika (retd) and Mazi Sam Ohuabunwa lauded Governor Otti for attracting AfrICANDO 2026 to Abia.

AfrICANDO 2026, which is ongoing in Abia, attracted participants from all parts of the world.
