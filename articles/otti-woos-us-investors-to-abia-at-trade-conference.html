Otti woos US investors to Abia at trade conference

By Punch Punch 6d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Abia State Governor Alex Otti has invited the global investment community to explore the vast economic opportunities in the State, assuring prospective investor

 Otti gave the assurance on Tuesday, while declaring open the US-Africa Trade and Investment Conference and Expo, AfrICANDO 2026, at the International Conference Centre (ICC), Umuahia.

The governor, who titled his speech, “Access, Opportunities and Prosperity,” said Abia has witnessed significant developments across its 17 local government areas and various sectors of the economy, creating an enabling environment for investment and business partnerships.

He said, “There is so much going on in Abia, across the local government areas, and in all the sectors of our economic and social expressions.

“All the investment opportunities in the State are promising and have been consciously set up to create long-term value for interested investors seeking to partner with our community of resilient entrepreneurs.”

 Otti noted that local government areas, government ministries and departments, manufacturers, agricultural entrepreneurs and other business interests are participating in the exhibition to showcase investment opportunities to facilitate partnerships.

He therefore urged participants to take advantage of the conference to establish business relationships and conclude investment agreements, identifying manufacturing, real estate, hospitality, agricultural value chains and information communication technology (ICT) as priority investment areas.

“I invite all of us to take time to network and see what partnership deals can be initiated and completed right here, or shortly after.

“We are specifically targeting investments in manufacturing, real estate, hospitality, agricultural value chains, ICT and others. This is for reason of the fact that the atmosphere in Abia is very conducive and supportive of those ventures,” Otti stated.

The governor identified the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA), enacted by the United States Congress in 2000, and the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), established through the African Union in 2018 and adopted by 54 member States, as important frameworks for promoting sustainable economic growth.

He informed the participants that his deliberate investments in public infrastructure, security and social services have repositioned Abia, particularly Aba, as an emerging investment destination.

Otti thanked the Foundation for Democracy in Africa (FDA) and the Abia Global Chamber of Commerce for bringing the first AfrICANDO event to be hosted on the African continent to Abia State.

He noted that the state recognised the importance of the conference to artisans, small business owners, industrialists, investors and multilateral development partners.

In their separate speeches, the President of the Foundation for Democracy in Africa, Dr. Fred Oladeinde; the Mayor of Miami-Dade County, United States of America, Hon. Daniella Levine Cava; the representative of the Democratic Republic of Congo, Dr. Milenge Mwenelwata; and the Head of Sub-Office, Lagos, of the International Organisation for Migration (IOM), Mr. Ali Ibrahim Ali, commended Governor Otti for his visionary leadership and commitment to economic development, while appreciating the State for hosting the international conference.

They highlighted the importance of international partnerships and intra-African trade, with Dr. Oladeinde announcing plans to collaborate with the Abia State Government and other partners to establish an Africa Trade Development Centre in the State.

Similarly, Hon. Levine Cava expressed the readiness of Miami-Dade County to strengthen its partnership with Abia State through the Miami-Dade Gateway to Africa initiative, particularly in expanding trade, attracting investment and promoting innovation.

Earlier, the Chairman of the Local Organising Committee (LOC) of AfrICANDO 2026 and Chief of Staff to the Governor, Pastor Caleb Ajagba,, described the 27th Annual US-Africa Trade and Investment Conference as a strategic platform for unlocking Africa’s economic potential, attracting investments and accelerating industrial development.

He noted that Abia’s hosting of the international event reflects Governor Otti’s commitment to creating an enabling business environment and positioning the State as an attractive investment destination.

Similarly, the Chairman of the Abia State Diaspora Commission, Dr Okey Agbara, and the Chairman, Abia Global Chamber of Commerce, Mr James Umekwe, said AfrICANDO 2026 is a practical economic opportunity for connecting local manufacturers, agricultural processors and service providers with international buyers, investors and technical partners.

They said the conference would promote export opportunities, boost local production, create jobs and strengthen Nigeria’s access to regional and global markets through AfCFTA and AGOA.

In their goodwill messages, Dr Oladeinde and Mr Umekwe commended Governor Otti for providing the platform to host, describing it as a significant milestone in strengthening economic relations between Africa and the United States.

Also, some elder statesmen, including former Chief of Army Staff, Lt. Gen. Azubuike Ihejirika (rtd) and former presidential candidate, Mazi Sam Ohuabunwa, applauded the Governor for attracting AfrICANDO and underscored its potential for investment and development in Abia.
