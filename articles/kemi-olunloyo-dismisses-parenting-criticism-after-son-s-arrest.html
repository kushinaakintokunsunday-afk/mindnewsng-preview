Kemi Olunloyo dismisses parenting criticism after son’s arrest

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerian journalist Kemi Olunloyo has defended her motherhood following the arrest of her son, Olukayode Joshua Taiwo, in the United States, saying she would no

Olunloyo stated this in a post shared on her social media page on Friday, in which she also confirmed that her son had been found and thanked those who expressed concern and offered prayers.

“My son has been found,” she wrote.

“I will not succumb to negative comments mocking my motherhood without understanding the delinquency of fathers and toxic families,” she added.

The journalist said she single-handedly raised Taiwo in Canada and Nigeria without child support before sending him to Nigeria at the age of 18 to begin a new life.

She also cautioned against judging her personal life based on her journalism work, saying, “Do not associate my work in journalism investigating people or reporting entertainment news about celebrities with the journalist’s personal life.”

Olunloyo accused Taiwo’s father, Kehinde Taiwo, of taking his disability and COVID-19 benefits for years while they lived in Nigeria, alleging that he also collected tax refunds by including their son in his tax returns.

She further claimed that his father abandoned him when he was five years old, sending him to Nigeria in 2006.

Speaking about her son’s activities, Olunloyo said he worked with her on campaigns against gun violence and drug abuse in communities, schools and workplaces.

She added that he funded his Graphic Artist certificate with earnings from his music on Spotify and volunteered with the Toronto Police between 2007 and 2012.

The journalist said she would no longer provide updates on the case but planned to attend family therapy with her son.

“There will no longer be any updates on his case and plan to attend family therapy with him soon,” she said.

Taiwo, was arrested on Thursday night by the US Marshals and Newington police in Connecticut. He was wanted in Pennsylvania on charges related to an incident involving a firearm and was being held on a $1m bond as a fugitive from justice.
