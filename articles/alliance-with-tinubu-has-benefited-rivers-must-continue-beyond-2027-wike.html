Alliance With Tinubu Has Benefited Rivers, Must Continue Beyond 2027 — Wike

By Channels TV Channels TV 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The Minister of the Federal Capital Territory (FCT), Nyesom Wike, has urged the people of Rivers State to sustain their political alliance with President Bola Tinubu beyond 2027, saying the partnership has attracted critical infrastructure and development projects to the state.

Wike said residents should assess political leaders based on their records in office rather than ethnic ties or claims of brotherhood, arguing that strategic alliances were necessary to secure development for their communities.

He spoke on Monday during a town hall meeting with political leaders, traditional rulers, youths, women and other stakeholders in Khana Local Government Area of Rivers State.

The meeting, according to the minister, was aimed at explaining the political situation ahead of the 2027 general election and seeking continued support for Tinubu’s re-election.

Recalling his political relationship with the people of Khana, Wike said they had supported him during his tenure as governor and in the 2023 elections when he mobilised support for the emergence of the incumbent governor.

He described the turnout at the meeting as evidence of their continued commitment to the relationship.

Wike emphasised the importance of political alliances, saying no individual or community could achieve its objectives without building relationships with influential political actors.

He explained that the Rainbow Coalition was formed specifically to mobilise support for Tinubu’s re-election and was not an arrangement to determine who would become governor, senator or member of the House of Representatives.

“The coalition we are doing is to support Mr President. It is to vote for Mr President. I am not doing an alliance with anybody to go back as governor somewhere,” he said.

He added that the arrangement was designed to accommodate different political interests, including members of the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) and the All Progressives Congress (APC).

Amaechi’s Record

The minister also criticised former Rivers State governor and former Minister of Transportation, Rotimi Amaechi, questioning his contributions to the Ogoni people despite spending 16 years in influential public positions.

Wike alleged that the major projects associated with Amaechi’s administration in the area included a proposed prison and banana plantation.

He contrasted these with the establishment of the University of Environment by the Tinubu administration and the completion of the Bori-Bonny Road.

“Within just three and a half years, see what he has given to us. But our brother was governor for eight years and minister for eight years. The only thing he promised was prison and a banana plantation,” he alleged.

Wike also questioned the execution of projects under Amaechi, alleging that $10 million was spent on a proposed statewide mosquito fumigation programme without achieving its intended results.

He further alleged that $39 million was paid to a company identified as Trinotech for a proposed 1,000-bed Caribbean White Specialist Hospital, which he claimed never progressed to the foundation stage.

He also criticised the monorail project, alleging that ₦68 billion was spent without its completion.

The minister urged residents to demand accountability from political leaders, insisting that public office holders should be judged by their achievements and the impact of their policies.

He also referenced ADC presidential candidate Atiku Abubakar’s ambitions, questioning the records of political leaders who had occupied influential positions.

Wike urged the people of Khana to mobilise support for Tinubu ahead of the 2027 elections, particularly by ensuring that previous supporters returned to vote.

Earlier, the Paramount Ruler of Bori Nortem Community, Lenu Saro Igbara, commended Wike for establishing the Rainbow Coalition, describing it as an unexpected political strategy that had attracted national attention.
