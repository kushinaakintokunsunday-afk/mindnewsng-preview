Ogun 2027: APC Gov Candidate Unveils Nine-Point Agenda For Development

By Channels TV Channels TV 3d ago 6 min read

Photo: Channels TV

The Ogun State All Progressives Congress governorship candidate, Solomon Adeola, has unveiled his Nine-Point Economic Development and Social Contract with the people of the state, tagged “Itesiwaju”, meaning “Moving Forward”.

The blueprint, unveiled at the Cultural Centre in the state capital, has critical infrastructure development, security, education and human capital development, agricultural transformation, job creation, among others, as critical components of the plan.

While seeking the support and understanding of residents of the state on the electoral task ahead, Adeola, also known as Yayi, said the action plans aim to bring more abundant life and shared prosperity to the state.

“Today, we are gathered to launch my Economic and Social Development Blueprint for the people of Ogun State, which I tagged ITESIWAJU. We are at the threshold of historical importance in the affairs of our dear state. In the democratic history of our state, this will be the first time that we are attempting to have a peaceful succession from one friendly administration to another, and it is our fervent wish and prayer that we succeed in this desirable venture,” he said.

” It is my firm belief that before we ask for a single vote from the people of Ogun State, we owe it as a duty to you to present our agenda for development of the state. My ITESIWAJU agenda took into consideration the inputs from my recently concluded interactions with stakeholders in all our 20 local governments. We sat with royal fathers, community leaders, women, youth, farmers, and traders.”

Adeola said he listened to the yearnings of Ogun people for development and built his agenda on your voices.

“This Agenda is a plan, not a performance. Read it as you would read a contract, because that is exactly what it is.

“Since 1976, Ogun State has been blessed with leaders who left indelible footprints of progress for our people: from the solid foundation laid by Chief Olabisi Onabanjo in infrastructure, education and hospitality industry; the bold rural development initiatives of Chief Olusegun Osoba that brought modernity to some of our villages; the policies of Otunba Gbenga Daniel that created some enduring institutions; the strides of Senator Ibikunle Amosun in some mega infrastructural projects; and at present the massive industrial transformation and mega projects under Prince Dapo Abiodun. It is all a story of progressive development in the interest of our state,” he added.

He commended the current administration of Governor Dapo Abiodun for the current developmental projects, which he said have placed the state as the first industrial destination of choice.

“And today, the baton is in the safe hands of our Governor, His Excellency, Prince Abiodun, CON. Your Excellency, you came not with slogans but with a compass: the ISEYA Development Agenda. You gave us the Gateway International Agro-Cargo Airport, pressed forward with the Kajola and Ijebu-Ode dry ports and the Gateway Deep Sea Port in the making, constructed more than 1,700 kilometres of roads, the highest of any administration so far, and delivered more than 7,000 housing units. Under your dutiful watch, our economy has grown to about ₦17 trillion, our revenue has more than doubled, and Ogun now stands as Number 5 in Nigeria for internally generated revenue.

“Your Excellency, ISEYA laid the foundation for economic growth. ITESIWAJU will build the foundation for more economic growth and prosperity for our people. Where ISEYA built infrastructure, we will connect such. Where ISEYA empowered our youth, we will turn their talent into great enterprise. Where ISEYA strengthened agriculture, we will carry it from the farm to the factory and to the world. History will be kind to you, because you have been kind to Ogun State,” the senator said.

“Governance is not a sprint; it is a relay race. Each leader must take the baton, run farther, and hand it to another for further development. To abandon good projects is to waste resources of the people and betray a trust. So, we will complete what is incomplete and ongoing, maintain what works, and reform what is failing. I am not coming to disrupt, but to deliver for the people. We are not reinventing Ogun State, but repositioning it for good, from Abeokuta to Ijebu, from Sagamu to Ado Odo Ota, and every community in between.

“Together, we have travelled the length and breadth of this State and what we see is that Ogun State stands at the cusp of greatness. With Lagos and the Atlantic Ocean to our south, the Republic of Benin to our west, three states to our north and east, there is no other state in this federation that sits at such an advantageous crossroads,” he added.

“Consider where we stand. Our ₦17 trillion economy, roughly $11 billion, is the size of the entire economy of the Republic of Togo. Our per capita income, at about $1,740, is already well above the Nigerian average of about $1,220. Lagos’s economy is about 3.7 times ours, yet its per capita income is only about 1.5 times ours. The gap between Ogun and Lagos is not in our people or our potential. It is in how well we plan, organise, and collect.”

“And yet, too many of our people still live in a State that works well for some and barely at all for others. The market woman in Ilaro whose stall closes at dusk for lack of power; the nurse in Sagamu managing thirty patients alone; the graduate in Abeokuta still searching for work years after graduation; and the farmer in Ijebu East who loses a third of his harvest for want of cold storage. These are not statistics to me. They are faces, names and conversations.”

The governorship candidate took the audience, which included Governor Abiodun, former governor Olusegun Osoba, captains of industry, members of the organized private sector, market women, and associations, on the journey of his vision and mission for the state if elected.

“My vision is to transform Ogun State where our natural and human resources are sustainably harnessed to unlock our economic and agro-industrial prowess, while ensuring inclusive governance and shared prosperity for our citizens in a vibrant and secure environment, while my mission is to transform Ogun State’s economy through strategic investments, infrastructure development, agricultural diversification, transparent governance, and quality education and health services, while fostering inclusive growth for sustainable prosperity,” he added.
