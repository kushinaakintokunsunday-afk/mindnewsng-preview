Denmark seeks NIMASA partnership to boost cadet employment, blue economy growth

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 3 min read

By Steve Agbota

The Danish Government has expressed readiness to explore opportunities for Nigerian maritime cadets to gain sea-based employment as ratings aboard Danish vessels through engagements with Maersk Shipping.

It has also indicated interest in partnering with the Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA) on the agency’s Blue Economy Acceleration Programme (N-BEAP) Hackathon, signalling prospects for stronger cooperation in maritime capacity development and blue economy innovation.

The Danish Ambassador to Nigeria, Jens Ole Bach Hansen, disclosed this during a visit to NIMASA, where he led a delegation to explore opportunities for strengthening maritime cooperation between both countries.

He explained that discussions were ongoing with Maersk Shipping on the possibility of engaging Nigerian cadets as ratings on Danish vessels, providing them with valuable onboard experience while they advanced their careers in the seafaring profession.

The ambassador also acknowledged the potential of NIMASA’s N-BEAP Hackathon and expressed the Danish delegation’s interest in the initiative as a platform for promoting innovation, developing technology-driven solutions and unlocking opportunities within Nigeria’s blue economy.

“Other areas identified for possible collaboration include maritime security, intelligence sharing, digitalisation, seafarer education, cadet training, institutional cooperation and maritime financing,” Bach Hansen said.

Responding, the Director-General of NIMASA, Dr Dayo Mobereola, welcomed Denmark’s continued interest in Nigeria’s maritime industry, describing the engagement as an opportunity to consolidate the longstanding relationship between both countries.

He expressed appreciation for Denmark’s hospitality during the agency’s recent visit in September and its support for Nigeria’s successful election into the International Maritime Organization (IMO) Council.

“Our priority is to translate the longstanding diplomatic relationship between Nigeria and Denmark into practical partnerships that deliver measurable benefits to both maritime industries. We look forward to strengthening collaboration in seafarer development, maritime innovation, technical capacity building and other strategic areas that will create sustainable opportunities for Nigeria’s maritime sector,” Mobereola stated.

Mobereola emphasised that the priority of the agency was to translate the goodwill and diplomatic engagements between both countries into concrete partnerships and measurable outcomes.

He therefore called for the establishment of a clear follow-up mechanism that would connect NIMASA with relevant Danish government institutions, maritime organisations, training establishments and private-sector operators capable of providing technical support and strategic partnerships.

According to him, direct engagement with these institutions would enable both parties to identify areas of mutual interest, develop practical implementation arrangements and ensure that proposals presented during previous engagements translated into sustainable benefits for the maritime industries of both countries.

He reaffirmed NIMASA’s readiness to work closely with the Royal Danish Embassy and relevant Danish institutions to advance the identified areas of cooperation.

The NIMASA DG expressed optimism that the renewed partnership would strengthen seafarer development, promote maritime innovation, enhance institutional capacity and create new opportunities for Nigeria’s maritime sector. He assured the delegation of the agency’s openness to additional areas of collaboration that would support the shared maritime development objectives of both countries.
