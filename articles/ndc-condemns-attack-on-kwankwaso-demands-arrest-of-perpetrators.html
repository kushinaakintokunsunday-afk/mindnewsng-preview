NDC condemns attack on Kwankwaso, demands arrest of perpetrators

By Vanguard Vanguard 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Vanguard

By Henry Umoru ABUJA — The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has condemned the reported attack on its 2027 vice-presidential candidate and former Kano State Gov

By Henry Umoru

ABUJA — The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has condemned the reported attack on its 2027 vice-presidential candidate and former Kano State Governor, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, in Bichi, Kano State, calling on the police and other security agencies to investigate the incident and arrest those responsible.

The party also demanded the arrest of anyone found to have sponsored the attack, while urging security agencies to guarantee the safety of political candidates and their supporters, irrespective of party affiliation.

The NDC made its position known in a statement on Monday by its National Publicity Secretary, Osa Director, describing the incident as an attack on its candidate, supporters and democratic values.

The party alleged that the attackers were thugs loyal to the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC), although it did not provide evidence to substantiate the allegation.

According to the statement, the incident was not a mere disruption but a deliberate assault that should be thoroughly investigated.

The party expressed concern over what it described as a growing pattern of violence against opposition candidates and supporters across the country.

It alleged that such incidents were attempts to intimidate political opponents and undermine democratic participation.

“The spate of violent attacks on opposition candidates and supporters across the country is a clear sign that the APC has lost the argument, lost the people, and has now resorted to thuggery and intimidation to cling to power,” the statement said.

The NDC, however, declared that it would not be intimidated or discouraged from participating in the political process.

“The NDC will not be intimidated. We will not be silenced. We will not retreat,” Director said.

The party called on the Nigeria Police Force, the Department of State Services (DSS) and other security agencies to immediately investigate the Bichi incident, identify and arrest those responsible, including any alleged sponsors, and prevent further attacks on political actors.

It stressed that Nigeria deserved free, fair and peaceful elections rather than political violence and intimidation.

The statement added: “Nigeria deserves a free, fair, and peaceful election — not a reign of terror. A New Nigeria is possible with the NDC.”
