Nations League: Klopp speaks on Germany’s 5-0 win over Netherlands

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 17 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp has explained how his side secured a 5-0 victory over the Netherlands in their UEFA Nations League encounter on Monday. The Neth

Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp has explained how his side secured a 5-0 victory over the Netherlands in their UEFA Nations League encounter on Monday.

The Netherlands managed to concede just one goal in the first half but struggled after the break, allowing Germany to score four more times.

Florian Wirtz scored twice as Germany produced an impressive performance to secure qualification for the Nations League quarter-finals.

Speaking during his post-match interview, Klopp said his players successfully neutralised the Netherlands’ strengths and took advantage of their opportunities to score what he described as incredible goals.

“What we have just witnessed was truly extraordinary,” Klopp said.

“Against a really good opponent, you have to find a way to win. We had to adapt and make a few adjustments.

“We took away a lot of their strengths. And then we scored some incredible goals.”
