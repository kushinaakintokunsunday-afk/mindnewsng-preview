EPL: Pochettino’s Man Utd suffer heavy 4-0 defeat to Liverpool

Mauricio Pochettino endured a disastrous start to life as Manchester United manager after Liverpool thrashed the Red Devils 4-0 at Anfield.

Liverpool took control of the encounter within the opening 13 minutes, scoring twice against a 10-man United side to secure a convincing victory.

United’s hopes of getting a positive result suffered a major setback after Leny Yoro was controversially sent off just eight minutes into the match. Liverpool quickly capitalised on their numerical advantage, with Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz finding the back of the net.

The visitors struggled to recover from the early setback as Liverpool continued to dominate proceedings.

Teenager Rio Ngumoha produced a stunning individual effort to score the hosts’ third goal, before Victor Munoz added another to complete the 4-0 demolition of Manchester United.

Defeat leaves United still rooted second bottom of the Premier League and gave Pochettino an early indication of the huge job he has ahead to restore the 20-time English champions to their former glories.

Liverpool climb into the top four and within seven points of leaders Arsenal to rekindle faint hopes of challenging for the title.

Pochettino answered United’s call after Michael Carrick was sacked last week after winning just one of the Red Devils’ first 10 league games in charge.

The Argentine has been linked with a move to Old Trafford for a decade, but failed to make a strong first impression on the United fans.

Pochettino’s bold team selection backfired with the centre-back pairing of Matthijs de Ligt and Yoro at fault for the game’s decisive moment.

De Ligt, on his first Premier League start for nearly a year, let a long ball down the middle and go and allowed Alexander Isak to bear down on goal.

The Swedish striker took a heavy touch before he was clipped by Yoro, but referee Sam Barrott still deemed the French defender had denied a clear goalscoring opportunity and produced a red card.

Szoboszlai rubbed salt into United wounds by drilling home the resulting free-kick from the edge of the box.

Moments later Ngumoha skipped past Diogo Dalot and crossed for Wirtz to hook a shot in off the underside of the bar.

Having relegated Harry Maguire to the bench, Pochettino was forced to turn to the veteran centre-back just 22 minutes in to try and stem the bleeding.

United lost 7-0 at Anfield three years ago and a switch to a back five helped avoid similar damage, albeit with the help of some wayward Liverpool finishing.

Maguire made a brilliant block to deny Isak a third goal just before half-time.

Bradley Barcola and Isak were guilty of wasting huge chances early in the second half.

But Barcola’s blistering pace created Liverpool’s third just after the hour mark.

The French international destroyed Lisandro Martinez for pace before picking out Ngumoha to slot home.

Wirtz rounded off one of his best Liverpool displays since a £100 million move from Bayer Leverkusen last year with the assist for Munoz to crash home the fourth goal eight minutes from time.

Liverpool fans rejoiced in their rivals’ pain with chants of “you are going down”.

United have not been relegated since 1974 but find themselves in a battle for survival despite still enjoying one of the biggest revenues in world football.

The 34 goals they have conceded in all competitions is the worst defensive record of any team in Europe’s top five leagues this season.
