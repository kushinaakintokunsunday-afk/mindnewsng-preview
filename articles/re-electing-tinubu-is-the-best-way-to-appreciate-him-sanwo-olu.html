Re-electing Tinubu is the best way to appreciate him — Sanwo-Olu

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Lagos State Governor, Babajide Sanwo-Olu, has urged Nigerians to re-elect President Bola Tinubu in 2027, saying voting for him and other All Progressives Congre

This was disclosed in a statement released on Monday by the Governor’s Special Adviser on Media and Publicity, Gboyega Akosile.

Sanwo-Olu also called on Lagos residents to support the state’s Deputy Governor and APC governorship candidate, Kadri Obafemi Hamzat, in the 2027 governorship election.

The governor made the appeal during the APC Lagos Central Senatorial District rally held at the Mobolaji Johnson Arena, Onikan, Lagos, where he highlighted what he described as the achievements of the Tinubu administration.

He said the President had made significant progress in transforming the country through investments in road construction, rail transportation, aviation and other critical sectors.

“The only way to show appreciation to the President is by coming out to vote massively for him and all APC candidates. That is the only way we can continue to enjoy the development he has started,” Sanwo-Olu said.

The governor also expressed confidence in Hamzat’s ability to lead Lagos State, urging residents to support his candidacy and allow him to build on the achievements of the current administration.

“K.O.H. has served you well, and he will serve you even better. He will serve you with greater commitment and dedication. I urge you all to support him and give him the opportunity to continue the good work and build on the achievements already recorded.

“Let us come out in our numbers, mobilise our families, friends and neighbours, and ensure his overwhelming victory in the 2027 election,” he said.

Hamzat, in his remarks, urged APC members and supporters to intensify mobilisation for Tinubu’s re-election, saying no nation could make meaningful progress with a manifesto of grievances.

He called on party faithful to turn out in large numbers on January 16 to vote for Tinubu and other APC candidates in the National Assembly elections, while urging them to support him and all House of Assembly candidates on February 6.

The deputy governor said continued electoral support for the APC would enable Lagos to sustain its development trajectory and consolidate the gains recorded, rather than reverse the progress made.

Also speaking, the Senator representing Lagos Central Senatorial District, Wasiu Eshilokun-Sanni, urged residents to vote for APC candidates, describing them as credible leaders committed to infrastructure development and national progress.

He highlighted the achievements of APC-led administrations in improving citizens’ welfare through empowerment programmes, infrastructure development and other people-oriented initiatives.

In his welcome address, the Lagos State APC Chairman, Cornelius Ojelabi, urged registered voters to collect their Permanent Voter Cards to enable them to participate in the forthcoming elections.

He stressed that the PVC remained the only instrument through which citizens could exercise their right to vote and elect their preferred candidates.

“Let us go out and collect our PVCs because they are the only means through which we can participate and exercise our civic rights in the coming election. Let us encourage our family members, friends and neighbours to vote for PBAT/KOH in the 2027 general election. By doing so, we can ensure continuity in infrastructure development and the progress of our nation,” he said.

The Director-General of the PBAT/KOH Campaign Council, Musiliu Obanikoro, described Tinubu as a committed and steadfast APC member whose leadership and resilience exemplified the qualities of a true leader.

Obanikoro also described Tinubu and Hamzat as knowledgeable leaders interested in the development of the country, particularly in children’s education, which he identified as the bedrock of every developed society.
