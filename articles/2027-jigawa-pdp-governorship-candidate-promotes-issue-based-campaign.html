2027: Jigawa PDP governorship candidate promotes issue-based campaign

From Mohammed Abubakar, Dutse

Ahead of the inauguration of his governorship campaign council, scheduled for Wednesday, the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) governorship candidate for Jigawa State, Mustapha Sule Lamido, has expressed his commitment to promoting an issue-based campaign as the countdown to the 2027 general elections gets under way.

He has, therefore, urged party supporters in the state to recommit themselves to effectively mobilising votes for the party in all the elections, rather than resorting to name-calling and abuse of political opponents in the state.

He made the declaration at the commencement of the pre-campaign consultative stakeholders’ meeting with party faithful across the three senatorial districts in the state, with a view to galvanising support for the party ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Describing the forthcoming poll as a defining moment for the party, the state and the country at large, the scion of the Sule Lamido political family said the issue of Jigawa’s development must be paramount.

A statement from his campaign office on Monday said the first engagement brought together party stakeholders from the 75 wards in Jigawa South-West Senatorial District, where discussions centred on the party’s preparations for 2027 and its vision for the development and prosperity of the state.

The consultations were subsequently extended to Hadejia in Jigawa North-East Senatorial District, where leaders and stakeholders from 85 wards participated in discussions on political mobilisation, party unity and development priorities, and concluded with an engagement with stakeholders from Jigawa North-West Senatorial District.

Lamido said the engagements were designed to strengthen the party’s grassroots structures, consolidate support and build a common agenda for Jigawa ahead of the 2027 elections.

The PDP said its campaign would focus on issues affecting ordinary citizens, with emphasis on presenting policies and programmes capable of addressing the state’s development challenges, especially as they concern women and youths.

With consultations and mobilisation activities gathering momentum, the opposition party is positioning itself for a major electoral contest against the APC in its bid to take over the Jigawa State Government House in 2027.

Lamido said the meeting focused on strategies for taking the party’s campaign message to every part of Jigawa State, expressing confidence in the party’s determination to challenge the APC through democratic engagements.

He, however, stressed that the PDP would not resort to political hostility, personal attacks or inflammatory rhetoric in its quest to win the election.

“We reaffirm our commitment to reaching every part of Jigawa State in a peaceful, orderly and dignified manner, without rancour,” he said.

He advised supporters to promote the PDP and its candidates through constructive arguments, policy discussions and the presentation of alternative programmes, rather than insults, personal attacks or disrespect towards political opponents.

The candidate expressed appreciation to participants for their commitment and sacrifices, while acknowledging the continued support, goodwill and prayers of the people of Jigawa State.
