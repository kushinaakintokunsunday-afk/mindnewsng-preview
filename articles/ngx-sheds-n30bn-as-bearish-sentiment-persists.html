NGX sheds N30bn as bearish sentiment persists

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Equities on the Nigerian Exchange Limited edged lower on Thursday, as market capitalisation dipped by N30bn to N161.21tn from N161.24tn at the previous close. T

The All-Share Index fell 0.02 per cent to close at 248,286.56 points, down from 248,329.65 points on Wednesday, extending the market’s downward trend from a weekly peak of 252,809.78 points recorded on Monday.

Trading activity closed with 372.01 million shares traded in 28,429 deals, while market breadth was negative, with 26 losers against 19 gainers.

Sovereign Trust Insurance Plc led the gainers’ chart, appreciating 9.96 per cent to close at N2.76 per share from N2.51. Critical Minerals Financing Corporation Plc followed closely with a 9.94 per cent gain to close at N3.87, while Chellarams Plc advanced 9.86 per cent to settle at N11.70.

Cornerstone Insurance Plc gained 9.64 per cent to close at N4.55, Regency Alliance Insurance Plc climbed 8.45 per cent to N0.77, while Caverton Offshore Support Group Plc rose 7.14 per cent to N4.50. Other notable advancers included Honeywell Flour Mill Plc, up 6.94 per cent to N38.50; Livingtrust Mortgage Bank Plc, which gained 6.76 per cent to N3.00; and Fidelity Bank Plc, which rose 3.91 per cent to end at N23.90.

Conversely, Royal Exchange Plc topped the losers’ table, shedding 6.00 per cent to close at N0.94 from N1.00. McNichols Consolidated Plc dropped 5.05 per cent to N4.70, while International Breweries Plc declined 4.50 per cent to N9.55.

Veritas Kapital Assurance Plc slipped 4.42 per cent to N1.08, Oando Plc lost 4.17 per cent to close at N34.50, United Bank for Africa Plc fell 3.38 per cent to N38.65, and Transnational Corporation Plc dropped 3.13 per cent to N31.00.

Sectoral performance was largely positive despite the overall market dip. The NGX Insurance Index led the sectors, rising to 1,087.76 points from 1,070.05 points; the NGX Growth Index advanced to 26,766.13 points from 26,697.67 points; and the NGX Industrial Index edged up to 11,727.12 points from 11,713.46 points.

Modest gains were also recorded in the NGX Banking Index, which closed at 2,345.06 points, and the NGX Pension Index, which ended at 12,358.44 points.

On the downside, the NGX Consumer Goods Index eased to 4,012.07 points from 4,024.03 points, while the NGX Oil and Gas Index slipped to 5,713.28 points from 5,720.07 points.

In the Exchange-Traded Fund segment, Vetiva Consumer Goods ETF led three gainers with a 6.25 per cent rise to N42.50, Stanbic IBTC ETF 30 gained 3.89 per cent to N935.00, and The SIAML Pension ETF 40 added 3.63 per cent to close at N1,498.49.

Meristem Value ETF paced nine decliners in the segment, falling 8.70 per cent to N126.00, while Meristem Growth ETF lost 7.23 per cent to N115.04 and Greenwich Alpha ETF fell 3.84 per cent to N624.99. Overall, Exchange-Traded Products recorded 594 deals involving 182,609 units, while the bond market remained muted, with seven deals involving 5,670 units and no price movements.
