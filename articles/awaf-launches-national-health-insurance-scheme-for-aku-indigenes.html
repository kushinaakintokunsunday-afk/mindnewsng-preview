AWAF launches national health insurance scheme for Aku indigenes

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Aku Welfare Association Federated (AWAF), an umbrella organisation comprising Aku indigenes living in the diaspora, has launched a National Health Insurance

The Aku Welfare Association Federated (AWAF), an umbrella organisation comprising Aku indigenes living in the diaspora, has launched a National Health Insurance Scheme for the people of Aku in Igbo-Etiti Local Government Area of Enugu State.

The launch, which took place at the Aku Civic Centre, Onuigba, Obarazu, Aku, Igbo-Etiti LGA of Enugu State, marks a significant milestone in the association’s efforts to improve the welfare and healthcare access of its people.

The initiative also forms part of activities marking AWAF’s 40th anniversary celebration, scheduled for December 27, 2026.

The epoch-making event attracted stakeholders from various AWAF branches, the Aku General Assembly (AGA), political leaders, Health Maintenance Organisations (HMOs), healthcare providers, community leaders and beneficiaries.

The scheme represents a strategic shift from ad hoc medical assistance to a structured, sustainable, and subsidised healthcare protection system. Key highlights included the unveiling of a comprehensive healthcare benefit package covering primary healthcare, maternal health, and emergency services, as well as the issuance of temporary hospital cards to pilot beneficiaries.

Those in attendance included members of AWAF branches in Port Harcourt, Lagos, Ebonyi, Enugu, and Nsukka; members of the National Executive Council of AWAF, led by its National President, Chief Uche L. Ocho, Esq.; the Aku General Assembly, led by its Chairman, Mathew Enyi; and the President-General of Obie Progressive Union (OPU), Dr. Nnanna Tony Alaku.

Others were political stakeholders; representatives of NEM Health Maintenance Organisation, led by Mrs. Susan Okeke; representatives of the healthcare provider, Attah Memorial Hospital, including Mr. Chinonso Attah and Mr. Amuche Charles Attah Jr.; community leaders and beneficiaries.

Speaking at the event, the National President of AWAF, Chief Uche L. Ocho, Esq., welcomed participants and described the launch as the beginning of the association’s 40th anniversary celebrations and a historic transition from occasional medical assistance to a structured and sustainable healthcare protection system.

He observed that many families in Aku spend a significant proportion of their income on out-of-pocket healthcare expenses, stressing that AWAF could no longer stand by while its people continued to face such challenges. He said the scheme is anchored on the guiding principle: “No Aku man or woman will be left behind”.

He disclosed that the AWAF National Executive, with the support of the Health Insurance Committee, had signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with an accredited Health Maintenance Organisation, NEM Health Insurance, to provide subsidised healthcare coverage for members of the community. He explained that the package covers primary healthcare, secondary referrals, maternal health, and emergency services at a highly subsidised community rate.

The National President, consequently, called on all hamlets in Aku to mobilise their residents for mass enrolment, emphasising that broad participation would guarantee the scheme’s success and sustainability.

Chief Ocho also highlighted some achievements of his administration, including the provision of a borehole for the Ugwuegede community, the successful hosting of the December 2025 Aku Cultural Festival, which attracted illustrious sons and daughters of the community, and the introduction of the health insurance scheme. He further used the occasion to formally draw attention to AWAF’s 40th anniversary celebration scheduled for December 2026.

In his remarks, the Chairman, AWAF National Health Insurance Committee, Dr. Chijioke Oke, commended the national leadership of the association for its commitment to the healthcare welfare of the Aku people. He gave a detailed account of the committee’s journey, from the needs assessment conducted in early 2026 to consultations with the National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA), the Enugu State Health Insurance Agency, and private health maintenance organisations.

Dr. Oke explained the scheme’s benefit package, enrolment model, and transparent fund management structure, noting that the committee had worked diligently to ensure that the deadline set by the AWAF national leadership for the programme’s seamless processing and commencement was met.

He expressed appreciation to the HMO and healthcare service provider for their commitment and partnership in establishing a well-structured healthcare programme that would adequately serve the needs of the people.

Also speaking, the Chairman, Aku General Assembly (AGA), Mathew Enyi, commended AWAF for the laudable initiative, describing healthcare as a fundamental pillar of community development. He pledged the AGA’s full support in sensitising residents across all the villages in Aku’s three major quarters to ensure widespread participation.

Enyi urged political leaders and other stakeholders in Aku to regard the initiative as entirely apolitical and support its objectives, particularly by encouraging more residents to enroll in the second phase of the programme.

He further called on all stakeholders to work collectively towards ensuring the sustainability of the scheme and supporting other initiatives capable of liberating the people from the burden of high healthcare costs and addressing other community development challenges.

In her presentation, the representative of NEM Health Maintenance Organisation, Mrs. Susan Okeke, outlined the operational procedures for accessing the scheme. She explained that the process would involve the following:

Registration: Biometric data capture, with the immediate issuance of a temporary hospital card, followed by a permanent card.

Access to Healthcare: Beneficiaries will present their cards at the accredited healthcare facility to access covered medical services.

Coverage: The package includes medical consultations, laboratory investigations, essential drugs, antenatal care, childbirth, childcare and minor surgical procedures.

Complaints and Enquiries: A toll-free telephone number will be printed on the permanent cards to enable beneficiaries to make enquiries and report complaints.

The representative of the lead healthcare provider, Attah Memorial Hospital, Aku, Mr. Amuche Charles Attah Jr., assured beneficiaries of quality healthcare delivery, non-discriminatory treatment and round-the-clock medical services.

He further assured enrollees that the hospital would work towards addressing concerns over the high cost of medical services, emphasising that beneficiaries under the AWAF scheme would receive healthcare services in accordance with the agreed benefit package.

Speaking on behalf of the beneficiaries, Hon. Dominic Didigwu of Amaogbo Amabokwu, Mrs. Augustina Agu of Awkuzu Orda, and Mrs. Nwani Angela Ngozi of Umuneri Ugwunani expressed profound appreciation to AWAF for introducing the initiative.

They described the scheme as a timely intervention that would significantly address the healthcare challenges confronting ordinary residents of Aku, particularly vulnerable families and individuals who struggle to meet rising medical expenses.
