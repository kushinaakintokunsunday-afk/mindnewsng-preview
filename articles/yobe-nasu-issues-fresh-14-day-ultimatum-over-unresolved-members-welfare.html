Yobe NASU Issues Fresh 14-Day Ultimatum Over Unresolved Members’ Welfare

By Channels TV Channels TV 16 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

The Non-Academic Staff Union of Educational and Associated Institutions (NASU), Yobe State Council, has issued an additional 14-day ultimatum to the Yobe State

The Non-Academic Staff Union of Educational and Associated Institutions (NASU), Yobe State Council, has issued an additional 14-day ultimatum to the Yobe State Government over the unresolved welfare package of its members or face possible industrial action.

The Chairman of the Council, Ali Usman, in a press statement on Monday, revealed the additional 14-day ultimatum was in the spirit of constructive engagement with the government, having received its response.

“NASU wishes to inform its members, the general public and relevant stakeholders that it has resolved to grant the Yobe State Government an additional fourteen (14) day ultimatum to address the outstanding welfare demands affecting Non-Academic Staff in State-Owned Tertiary Institutions”

“This decision followed the receipt of the Government’s response dated 10th November, 2026, indicating that the issues raised by the Union are receiving attention”

“After carefully considering the Government’s response at its meeting held on 12th November, 2026, the State Council resolved, in the spirit of constructive engagement and industrial harmony, to extend the initial ultimatum by another fourteen (14) days, effective from 12th November, 2026, and expiring on 26th November, 2026,” the statement reads in part.

The Union emphasised that the extension granted is in good faith and should not be misconstrued as a withdrawal, abandonment, or compromise of its legitimate demands; rather, it reflected.

NASU’s commitment to peaceful engagement and its willingness to give the Government another opportunity to address the longstanding concerns.

The outstanding demands include Implementation of the 65-Year Retirement Age, Implementation of the Reviewed 2024 Salary Table (CONTEDISS), Implementation of the Consolidated Non-Teaching Tools Allowance (CONTTA) and Earned Allowance (EA).

NASU therefore calls on the Yobe State Government and the relevant authorities to utilise the additional period to expedite the necessary approvals and take concrete steps towards implementing the outstanding demands of the workers’ welfare, dignity and legitimate entitlements it deserves.
