Gulf Nations Attacked After US Strikes Iranian Island

By Channels TV Channels TV 6d ago 5 min read

Photo: Channels TV

Bahrain and Kuwait said Wednesday that they had repelled air attacks launched against them following US strikes on an Iranian island, as Gulf nations face retaliatory bombardment in the Middle East war.

The conflict — which began with US-Israeli strikes on Iran in late February — has spilled over to Gulf states that host US military facilities.

The Iranian army said Wednesday its drones “targeted Patriot air defence systems and drone and missile launch platforms belonging to the terrorist US military at Ali Al-Salem Air Base in Kuwait,” in a statement carried by state television IRIB.

Kuwait’s military said Wednesday its air defences were “responding to hostile drone threats”, urging its public to follow security and safety advice.

While Bahrain said its air defences intercepted attacks from Iran early Wednesday, a day after Tehran’s Revolutionary Guards said they attacked a base used by Washington.

The United States and Iran are in a standoff over the vital Strait of Hormuz, with the US military working to escort ships transiting the waterway in defiance of Tehran’s attempts to close the waterway.

Iran’s official IRNA news agency cited a regional official as saying the United States had hit Qeshm Island in the Hormuz Strait without causing serious damage.

US fighter jets “used precision munitions to strike an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) missile launch site on Qeshm Island”, an American official told AFP on condition of anonymity, adding that the strike was in response to Iranian “aggression targeting US personnel and assets”.

Meanwhile, the Saudi-led coalition fighting Yemen’s Houthis said on Wednesday it had intercepted a cruise missile launched by the Iran-backed group toward the Riyadh area.

Alleged Iran Plot

Explosions boomed across Bahrain’s capital on Tuesday as a mobile phone alert warned residents to seek safety.

Bahrain’s armed forces said they “confronted, intercepted and destroyed a number of treacherous Iranian aerial attacks today… before they could achieve their objectives”, while Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said they targeted a US-operated radar system.

“Combat drones belonging to the Revolutionary Guards carried out a precision operation this morning, targeting a radar system operated by US forces at the Sheikh Isa Air Base in Bahrain,” the Guards’ Sepah news website said.

The attack was in response to the “repeated violations of Iranian airspace by US military drones”, it added. The Guards also said they had intercepted and captured a US drone south of Qeshm.

Bahrain, which hosts the US Navy’s Fifth Fleet, was heavily targeted earlier in the war that started with US-Israeli strikes on Iran, but had not been hit for weeks until Iranian drone attacks on Friday and Sunday.

Despite Washington and Tehran holding back from a full resumption of hostilities, the sides have traded fire and accusations.

US President Donald Trump said on Tuesday he was taking “very seriously” reports that Iran was developing plans to use a so-called “dirty bomb” in Washington.

CNN cited seven anonymous US officials as saying Iran was plotting how to target senior administration figures with a bomb laced with chemical or radioactive material, though they stopped short of saying Iran planned to carry out such an attack.

“If there is (such a threat), they will pay a price the likes of which no other country has ever paid before,” Trump said, adding: “I hope it’s not, but we take it very seriously because they happen to be crazy.”

Iran’s Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi dismissed the report and called it “Iraq War-style propaganda”.

‘Fear And Anxiety’

“I was on my way to work when the warning sirens went on,” Abdulrahman Abdullah, a Bahraini civil servant in his forties, told AFP on Tuesday.

“Fear and anxiety rise as attacks increase… We hope things will go back to how they were.”

The violence came as the United States moved the USS Theodore Roosevelt into the region, bringing the number of American carrier strike groups in the region to three.

The deployment adds to an already large US naval presence, which includes two other supercarriers — the George H W Bush and the George Washington — and more than a dozen destroyers.

Iranian drone attacks have resumed ahead of this week’s unprecedented trilateral summit between Trump and his Chinese and Russian counterparts.

Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin will discuss “world peace” at the talks in Shenzhen, according to China. Trump has said he plans to discuss the Iran war at the meeting.
