US strategist Diana London deletes posts on Tinubu interview amid reports of receiving $4.5m lobbying fee from Nuhu Ribadu

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 6d ago 2 min read

Photo: VeryNigerian

American political strategist and media personality Diana London has deleted her social media posts concerning her engagements with the Nigerian government and the security partnership between Nigeria and the United States, amid reports linking her to a multimillion-dollar lobbying contract involving the Nigerian government.

A report by Peoples Gazette published on Wednesday alleged that London was associated with a $4.5 million payment made under a lobbying arrangement involving Aster Legal, a firm reportedly acting on behalf of National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu.

According to the publication, documents filed with the United States Department of Justice under the Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA) showed that London was affiliated with DCI Group, a Washington-based conservative lobbying firm engaged by Nigeria.

The report said the $4.5 million payment, described as a retainer, was completed on December 12, 2025.

However, the US Justice Department filings identify London as a consultant to DCI Group, with the Republic of Nigeria, through Aster Legal, listed as the foreign principal. The documents describe the payment as part of the lobbying arrangement and do not establish that the entire sum was paid personally to London.

The lobbying engagement was intended to assist the Nigerian government in communicating its efforts to protect Christian communities and maintaining US support for its campaign against jihadist groups and other destabilising elements in West Africa.

London had earlier shared photographs of herself at the Presidential Villa in Abuja, where she was pictured alongside President Bola Tinubu and his Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila.

In one of her now-deleted posts, she described the security partnership between Nigeria and the United States as one that was “delivering results”.

“The U.S.-Nigeria security partnership is delivering results. Peace through strength. None of this happens without the ongoing partnership between President Trump and President Tinubu, their steadfast commitment to eliminating terrorists, and Nigeria’s willingness to work closely with the U.S. government,” she wrote.

She also described Nigeria as the United States’ greatest ally in Africa and suggested that the relationship could create more economic and trade opportunities for both countries.

In another post, London expressed appreciation for the opportunity to work with individuals representing Nigeria, particularly on efforts to protect religious groups and strengthen relations between the Trump administration and the Nigerian government.

However, the posts were no longer visible on her X account as of Wednesday morning. She had not publicly explained why she removed them.

The circumstances surrounding the deletion remain unclear.
