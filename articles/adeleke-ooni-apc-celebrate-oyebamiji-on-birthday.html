Adeleke, Ooni, APC celebrate Oyebamiji on birthday

By Punch Punch 6d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Osun State Governor, Ademola Adeleke, the Ooni of Ife, Oba Adeyeye Ogunwusi and the All Progressives Congress in Osun, on Tuesday, congratulated the party’s can

Adeleke, in a statement signed by his spokesperson, Olawale Rasheed, obtained in Osogbo, the state capital, recognised Oyebamiji’s sincere contributions to the governance of Osun.

The governor noted in particular Oyebamiji’s tenure as Commissioner of Finance, “where his expertise guided the state through turbulent moments,” applauding him as a fine professional who never misses an opportunity to make an impact for the people through service.

He commended the Ikire-born politician for striking a balance between personal aspiration and duty to the state, saying that through political maturity and a profound demonstration of patriotism, the celebrant accepted the verdict of the Osun people and saved the state the needless distraction that comes from litigation.

Similarly, Oba Ogunwusi described Oyebamiji as a distinguished public servant, accomplished administrator and committed stakeholder in the development of Osun State, whose years of service in public office have contributed to the growth of the state and the country at large.

The monarch, in a statement by his media aide, Moses Olafare, acknowledged Oyebamiji’s “wealth of experience in governance, financial management and public administration,” noting that his contributions to public service reflect his commitment to the development of society.

He also expressed hope that “Oyebamiji would continue to deploy his experience, leadership capacity and knowledge towards promoting peace, unity and sustainable development in Osun State and Nigeria.”

The Osun APC, in a statement signed by its spokesperson, Kola Olabisi, described Oyebamiji as a peaceful politician who is “excessively and verifiably loyal to the cause of the progressive party.”

The statement further read, “Asiwaju Munirudeen Bola Oyebamiji, AMBO, has proven to be a blessing to the APC, his family, his hometown in Ikire, the state and the entire nation, as it is on record that he served meritoriously as the Chief Executive Officer/Managing Director of the National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA) without blemish.

“For someone to have successfully served two governors as a finance commissioner in a complex state like Osun State, the way Oyebamiji diligently served without any record of sharp practices, is an indication that the ‘Birthday Boy’ is a virtuous human being.

“Asiwaju Oyebamiji has proven through conduct to be a tolerant Muslim who has been investing his God-given resources for the advancement of his religion and other faiths, as it is on record that he has built churches for the Christians in his hometown despite the fact that he is a Muslim.”
