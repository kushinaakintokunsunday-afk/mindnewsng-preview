Jonathan Warns Against Ethnic Rivalry, Political Intolerance Ahead Of General Elections

Former President Goodluck Jonathan has warned that the rising tide of political intolerance, thuggery, and deliberate stoking of ethnic rivalries pose a severe threat to Nigeria’s national existence and stability ahead of the 2027 general elections.

‎Speaking at a high-level peace and security conference hosted by the Goodluck Jonathan Foundation in Abuja, Jonathan stressed that upcoming electoral cycles must not become catalysts for destructive division.

He urged political leaders and citizens alike to prioritise national unity, democratic values, and responsible leadership over narrow partisan ambitions.

The former president also urged Nigerians to treat the upcoming 2027 general elections as a critical opportunity to choose credible leadership at both state and federal levels, prioritising competence over ambition.

He warned that undermining national cohesion for political ambition poses a severe danger to the country’s existence.

“Nothing could be more dangerous to our national existence than the deliberate encouragement of ethnic rivalry. We must never allow political ambition to undermine the bonds that hold us together as one nation and one country, both at the national level and at the state level”, he said.
