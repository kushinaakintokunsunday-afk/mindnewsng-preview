‘Congress did not meet with or endorse him’ — Atiku US lobbyist speaks on Peter Obi Washington visit

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

A Washington-based lobbying firm’s managing partner, Dr Karl Von Batten, has disputed claims that the United States Congress endorsed former Anambra State Governor Peter Obi, clarifying that the Nigerian politician attended a private event and was neither formally received nor endorsed by Congress.

Von Batten said his communications director had forwarded two media reports claiming that the US Congress endorsed Obi.

He explained that he decided to address the reports after a social media commentator, @Mimi_yakigar, mentioned his firm in connection with the claims.

“Congress did not meet with or endorse Mr Obi. The event he attended was private,” Von Batten said.

He identified US Representatives Marlin Stutzman and Beth Van Duyne in the footage but noted that neither serves on the House Foreign Affairs Committee.

According to him, the video also showed no members of the committee or its Subcommittee on Africa, nor officials from the State Department’s Bureau of African Affairs.

Von Batten said he considered it necessary to clarify the nature of the event because his firm had been mentioned in the reports, while acknowledging the significance of Obi’s visit to the United States.

Obi arrived in Washington, DC, on Monday, November 16, 2026, for a series of engagements with American political figures and other influential stakeholders ahead of Nigeria’s 2027 general elections.

The former governor, who is the presidential candidate of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC), said the visit was intended to draw international attention to Nigeria’s development challenges, security situation and democratic future.

In a statement posted on his X account, Obi said he had held discussions with members of the US House of Representatives and Senate, as well as other American personalities.

Those he identified included US Representatives Marlin Stutzman and Todd Tiahrt, Congresswoman Beth Van Duyne, Suzanne Bock Grisham and retired US Army Colonel Albert V. Short.

Obi also granted an interview to American television commentator Glenn Beck, during which he discussed Nigeria’s leadership challenges, accountability, management of national resources and development prospects.

He further disclosed that he addressed an audience on Capitol Hill, where he called for international cooperation to tackle insecurity, strengthen national unity and promote economic prosperity in Nigeria.
