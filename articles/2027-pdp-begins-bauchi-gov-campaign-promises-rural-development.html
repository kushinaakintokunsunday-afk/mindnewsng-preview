2027: PDP begins Bauchi gov campaign, promises rural development

By Punch Punch 16h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Peoples Democratic Party has flagged off its governorship campaign in Bauchi State, unveiling plans to prioritise education, rural development and women’s e

Speaking at the campaign flag-off on Tuesday in Bununu, Tafawa Balewa Local Government Area, the party’s governorship candidate, Usman Suphi, dismissed claims that the PDP had lost its political relevance in the state, insisting that the party remained a strong political force.

He said, “Whoever said PDP is dead is lying. PDP has been a strong force here and is still maintaining its strength.

“We will campaign with our blueprint in education, rural development and women’s employment. If elected, we will ensure development in our dear state.”

Suphi urged residents to trust the PDP, promising to deliver impactful development and improve the living conditions of the people.

He also thanked party supporters and leaders for their presence and commitment, describing their turnout as a demonstration of confidence in the party’s leadership.

The candidate further expressed optimism about the party’s chances in the 2027 elections, saying he was confident of assuming office on May 29, 2027.

Earlier, the Bauchi State PDP Chairman, Ibrahim Kashim, described Suphi as the most qualified among the governorship contenders, expressing confidence in his capacity to lead the state.

Kashim said the candidate was an honest and modest individual with an unblemished record, adding that he possessed the qualities required to drive the state’s development.

He said, “Suphi is an honest and modest individual with no record of betrayal. He is capable and has all it takes to develop Bauchi State.”
