Delta campaign killings: Oborevwori orders arrest of perpetrators

By Punch Punch 2h ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori, on Tuesday ordered security agencies to track down and arrest those responsible for the deadly political violence in U

The governor gave the directive during a visit to the community to assess the situation and reassure residents following Monday’s attack on the campaign entourage of former Deputy Senate President, Ovie Omo-Agege, which left six people dead and several others injured.

The attack occurred during a campaign visit by Omo-Agege, the Nigeria Democratic Congress candidate for Delta Central Senatorial District in the 2027 general elections. His campaign organisation had initially reported that four people, including two of his aides, were killed when gunmen allegedly opened fire on the gathering.

However, the Nigeria Police Force confirmed on Tuesday that preliminary reports indicated that six people had died, while several others sustained injuries.

Among those identified by Omo-Agege’s campaign organisation as victims were Moses Anaughe, a former member of the Delta State House of Assembly and aide to the former senator, and Urhobo youth activist, Akima Efemena Umukoro.

The violence prompted the Delta State Government to impose a dusk-to-dawn curfew across Ughelli North Local Government Area, restricting movement between 6pm and 6am daily until further notice.

The police have also commenced an investigation into the incident, with the Inspector-General of Police, Olatunji Disu, directing a thorough and impartial probe to establish the circumstances surrounding the attack, identify those responsible and examine the conduct of officers deployed to the venue.

Oborevwori described the killings as unfortunate and unacceptable, insisting that those responsible would face the full weight of the law.

“No political disagreement or ambition is worth the loss of human lives,” the governor said, warning that his administration would not tolerate any attempt to undermine peace and security in the state.

Before proceeding to Uduere, Oborevwori had held a security meeting with heads of security agencies at the Area Command Office in Ughelli.

He was accompanied to the community by the State Commissioner of Police, CP Samuel Etaifo Erale; Commander of the 63 Brigade of the Nigerian Army, Brigadier General Ibrahim Buhari; the State Director of the Department of State Services and other security officials.

Addressing journalists during the visit, the governor condemned the invasion of communities under the guise of political activities, describing the killing of fellow Nigerians over political differences as unconscionable.

He expressed disappointment that political disagreements could degenerate into violence and the killing of innocent people, particularly when his administration had worked to maintain relative peace in the state since assuming office in 2023.

Oborevwori said his administration remained committed to peace and security, noting that the fourth pillar of his MORE Agenda was specifically designed to promote a peaceful and secure environment across Delta State.

He said, “Those who know me know that I am not a violent person. That is why, in my MORE Agenda, the fourth pillar is dedicated to enhancing peace and security.

“We have been preaching peace, and Delta State has enjoyed relative peace since 2023. The important thing is how to sustain that peace. I want to assure the people that this will not happen again.”

The governor insisted that the perpetrators must be identified, apprehended and prosecuted, declaring that the state would not allow political activities to become a source of insecurity.

“This must stop. It must stop. We cannot continue like this,” he warned.

Oborevwori also observed that sustained peace and security were critical to economic development, stressing that businesses and investments could not thrive in an atmosphere of violence, fear and uncertainty.

He appealed to political leaders, supporters and other stakeholders to exercise restraint, shun political thuggery and avoid actions capable of triggering further violence.

The governor further charged the police and other security agencies to conduct a comprehensive investigation into the incident, arrest everyone connected to the killings and ensure that those found culpable were brought to justice.

The attack has heightened concerns about the safety of political campaigns and supporters ahead of the 2027 general elections, with the police urging political actors and residents to remain calm, comply with the curfew and avoid spreading unverified information.
