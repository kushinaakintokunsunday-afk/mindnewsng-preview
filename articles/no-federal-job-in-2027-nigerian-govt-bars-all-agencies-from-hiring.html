No federal job in 2027 – Nigerian govt bars all agencies from hiring

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The Federal Government has directed all federal Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) to suspend fresh recruitment in 2027, except where special approval

The Federal Government has directed all federal Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) to suspend fresh recruitment in 2027, except where special approval is obtained.

Under the new directive, government institutions are expected to maintain their existing workforce levels and refrain from creating new positions or recruiting additional personnel without authorisation.

The government said any agency seeking to increase its staff strength must provide clear reasons for the request and demonstrate that adequate funds have been provided in its budget to cover the salaries, allowances and other financial obligations of the proposed employees.

It also warned that MDAs that fail to submit their staff records or justify their personnel budget proposals risk being excluded from the approved 2027 staff list, a move that could affect the allocation of funds for their personnel costs in the next national budget.

The directive is contained in a circular dated November 9, 2026, titled 2027 Workforce Budget Call Circular.

The circular, issued by the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation, was signed by Ishiyaku Musa Mohammed, Permanent Secretary in charge of the Service Policies and Strategies Office.

It was circulated to the Chief of Staff to the President, Deputy Chief of Staff to the Vice President, Secretary to the Government of the Federation, ministers, permanent secretaries, service chiefs and heads of federal institutions.

The document directed the institutions to prepare and submit their workforce budget proposals for 2027 for review and approval.

According to the government, the exercise is part of ongoing efforts to reform the public service, strengthen human capacity and ensure proper planning and management of personnel expenditure ahead of the next budget year.

It further instructed all federal institutions to operate within their approved staff ceilings and avoid conducting recruitment exercises without the required authorisation.

“MDAs are advised to strictly maintain their current staff position for the 2027 fiscal year,” the circular stated.

The government, however, acknowledged that some agencies might have genuine reasons to employ additional personnel, but insisted that such requests must be properly justified and supported by evidence of available funding.

It stressed that applications for an increase in workforce strength must explain the need for additional employees and show how the resulting salary and allowance obligations would be financed.

“However, where it is necessary to seek an increase, such requests should be supported with justifications attaching relevant information, including financial provision in the budget to meet additional emolument commitment; otherwise the existing workforce ceiling should not be exceeded,” the circular added.
