Plateau: Gunmen kill 11, 9 buried in mass grave

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 4 min read

From Jude Dangwam and Jude Owuamanam, Jos

At least 11 people were killed in separate attacks in Mangu and Bokkos local governments, Plateau State, with nine victims buried in a mass grave, while the burning of houses in neighbouring Qua’an Pan LG forced the council chairman to impose a curfew.

Among those killed was an Operation Rainbow commander who had recently completed a training programme in Jos. In a separate tragedy, a newly married couple died from burns sustained when fire engulfed their residence in Bukuru, Jos South Local Government Area.

The nine victims buried in a mass grave were killed during an attack on Zwagal village in Jipal District of Mangu LGA on Sunday night. Several houses were also razed at Bong community in Doemak District of Qua’an Pan LGA.

Local sources said the attackers, who reportedly arrived in their hundreds, stormed the hilltop communities, shooting residents, setting houses ablaze, looting property and stealing livestock.

Some of the animals were reportedly butchered by the attackers, who were said to have operated for several hours.

The village head of Zwagal, Jonathan Wadong, said the attack began at about 8 p.m. on Sunday, when sporadic gunshots shattered the peace of the community.

Wadong alleged that the attackers were Fulani militias, saying: “They were speaking Fulani language.”

He appealed to the government to deploy adequate security personnel to protect residents and prevent further attacks. He also commended the chairmen of Mangu and Qua’an Pan local LGAs  for promptly mobilising security personnel to the affected communities.

The burning of houses at Bong reportedly triggered panic in other parts of Qua’an Pan, with residents fleeing Doemak, the local government headquarters, amid fears of further attacks.

The Chairman of Qua’an Pan Local Government Council, Christopher Audu Manship, imposed a curfew late Monday evening to contain the situation and prevent a further breakdown of law and order.

Manship, who visited the affected communities, witnessed the mass burial at Zwagal alongside the Chairman of Mangu Local Government Council, Emmanuel Bala Mwolpun.

Describing the attacks as barbaric and inhuman, he reaffirmed the government’s commitment to tackling insecurity and urged residents, particularly youths, to remain vigilant and promptly report suspicious movements to security agencies.

During an assessment of the destruction at Bong, Manship assured residents that the government would not abandon them, pledging support for rebuilding their homes and strengthening security.

He also disclosed that efforts were being made to improve telecommunications coverage in the community to facilitate timely communication, security surveillance and monitoring.

In Bokkos LGA, gunmen killed two men in an ambush at about 3 p.m. on Monday as they returned from a condolence visit to Hurti village.

The victims were travelling to their community in Tadai, Manguna District, when they were attacked.

A community leader, Machief Paul, who confirmed the incident, said both victims were members of his community. He disclosed that one of them had recently completed a training programme for Operation Rainbow unit commanders.

“One of the victims was a newly graduated commander of Operation Rainbow who had recently completed a refresher training programme for unit commanders in Jos. He was agile and ready for the job, but unfortunately his career has been cut short,” Paul said.

Meanwhile, a newly married couple, Sikiru Habib and his wife, Ramlat Lawan, have died after suffering severe burns when fire engulfed their residence in Anguwan Miyango, Bukuru, Jos South LGA.

The fire reportedly broke out at about 2:30 a.m. on Saturday while the couple were asleep. Residents suspected a power surge, although the actual cause of the fire had yet to be established.

A neighbour, Lawal Suleiman, said residents woke up to discover the raging inferno and found that the door to the couple’s room would not open, trapping them inside as young men in the neighbourhood struggled to rescue them.

He said the rescuers eventually broke the window and brought the couple out before firefighters arrived to provide emergency assistance.

The victims were rushed to the Jos University Teaching Hospital, where Habib died on Sunday, while his wife succumbed to her injuries on Monday.
