Lagos summit to tackle growing business cybersecurity threats

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Organisers said in a statement that the risks will be among the issues examined at the 18th anniversary conference of Digital Jewels Africa, an IT governance, risk and compliance consulting and capacity-building firm, scheduled for 25 November in Lagos.

The conference, themed ‘The Trust Imperative: Securing Africa’s Digital Future’, will bring together business executives, policymakers and technology professionals from the financial services, telecommunications, insurance, energy and technology sectors to discuss ways of strengthening digital security and public confidence in technology-driven services.

As organisations increasingly depend on digital platforms to deliver services, manage information and engage customers, weaknesses in cybersecurity and technology governance could undermine business operations and expose sensitive information to security threats.

The conference will examine how businesses can manage these risks while adopting emerging technologies, particularly artificial intelligence, and strengthening their internal controls and corporate governance frameworks.

The event will be chaired by the Chair of Digital Jewels Africa, Ibukun Awosika, while the Managing Director of the Nigerian Economic Summit Group, Tayo Aduloju, will deliver the keynote address. The Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Digital Jewels Africa, Adedoyin Odunfa, will host the conference.

Participants will include the Chairman of Prudential Life Insurance, Ghana, Karen-Akiwumi Tanoh; Managing Director of Union Bank, Yetunde Oni; Managing Director of Midwestern Oil & Gas Company, Elozino Olaniyan; and Managing Director of Rex Insurance, Ebelechukwu Nwachukwu.

Other speakers include the Chief Executive Officer of eTranzact International, Niyi Toluwalope; Managing Director of Leadway Assurance, Gboyega Lesi; Director of Shared Services at the Central Bank of Nigeria, Usman Usman; and Chief Marketing Officer of MTN Nigeria, Onyinye Ikenna-Emeka.

The cybersecurity discussions will feature the Group Chief Information Security Officer of GTCO, Dr Bharat Soni; the Group Chief Information Security Officer of Access Bank, Dr Favour Femi-Oyewole; the Chief Internal Auditor of Interswitch Group, Adebayo Adebeshin; and the Chief Information Security Officer of MTN Nigeria, Jude Anietie.

The discussions will also examine the responsibilities of businesses, regulators and technology professionals in protecting sensitive information, managing technology-related risks and preparing organisations for the governance, risk and compliance demands associated with digital expansion.

Speaking ahead of the conference, Odunfa said the growing dependence of organisations on digital systems has made security and responsible technology adoption essential for Africa’s digital future.

“The trust imperative is at the heart of Africa’s digital future. As organisations rely more on digital systems, the question is no longer how fast we can innovate, but how securely and responsibly we can do so. We believe this conversation is critical, and our 18th anniversary conference provides an opportunity to bring the right voices together to address it,” she said.

Digital Jewels Africa said it had spent 18 years supporting organisations in areas including cybersecurity, information assurance, IT governance, project management, e-business and professional capacity development.

The anniversary conference is expected to provide a platform for business leaders and technology professionals to exchange experiences and discuss practical approaches to strengthening digital trust and security across Africa.
