Anthony Joshua welcomes baby girl

By Punch Punch 5d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

British boxing champion Anthony Joshua has spoken about his excitement over becoming a father to a baby girl, saying the arrival of his daughter has given him a

Joshua and his childhood sweetheart, Nicole Osbourne, welcomed their daughter on November 14, 2026.

The former two-time unified heavyweight champion and Osbourne, also share an 11-year-old son, Joseph.

Speaking in an interview with The Sun which started trending on Thursday, Joshua said having a daughter would give him a better understanding of women and how to support them.

“Now I’ve got a daughter, I will get an insight and a bit more understanding of what it means to be a woman, and how I can be supportive. My daughter will give me firsthand experience of a woman’s power.

“I’ve spoken to all the women in my family, and it’s amazing the amount of support that women give each other. We haven’t given her a name yet, but she’s my Princess,” he said.

Describing the birth as a huge blessing, Joshua said he was delighted to become a girl dad, adding that he had chosen not to find out the baby’s sex during the pregnancy.

“It’s been amazing, a big blessing and I can’t believe I’m a girl dad. I didn’t want to find the sex out during the pregnancy.

“I love boys but as soon as I found out we had a little girl I said, ‘No more Fifa, no more motorbiking, no more PlayStation!’” he said.

The boxer also expressed his willingness to take an active role in his daughter’s upbringing, including taking part in activities such as dressing up and playing with dolls.

“I’m going to do it all, so you might see me with some eyeshadow on, some nail polish on, drinking some tea in a doll’s playhouse and just trying to do the right thing by her, you know?

“I will change nappies, I’ve gotta go through all that stinky bum time again!” he added.

The development comes ahead of Joshua’s reported December 11 showdown with fellow British heavyweight boxer Tyson Fury.
