VIDEO: I attempted suicide many times, says Kunle Afolayan’s son

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Dieko Afolayan, son of Nollywood filmmaker and producer, Kunle Afolayan, has opened up about his struggles with bullying, sexual assault and multiple suicide attempts, revealing how he has turned his painful experiences into a renewed sense of purpose.

Afolayan made the disclosure during an interview on The Morayo Show on Monday, where he spoke about his journey towards healing, self-discovery and finding meaning in his experiences.

Reflecting on his journey from pain to purpose, he described it as challenging, particularly given the traumatic experiences he had endured.

“Moving from pain to purpose has not been easy, especially knowing that you are going to be doing a lot of difficult things, having been through difficult experiences,” he said.

He disclosed that beyond the bullying he experienced, he had attempted suicide several times and was also a survivor of sexual assault.

He, however, stressed that these experiences did not define his identity or determine his future.

“I have tried so many times to commit suicide, and I am also a victim of sexual assault. However, I have realised that these experiences do not define who I am, where I get to or what I do with myself,” he said.

Afolayan emphasised the importance of self-compassion, urging people dealing with difficult experiences to be kinder to themselves.

He said learning to challenge negative thoughts and adopt alternative perspectives played a significant role in changing his outlook on life.

“I feel like if there is anything anyone should practise, it is self-compassion,” he added.

Recalling the depth of his struggles, Afolayan disclosed that he once attempted to ingest broken glass after feeling that he did not belong.

“There were so many times I would cry. I remember a time I tried to ingest broken glass because I felt I did not belong here,” he said.

He, however, said his outlook began to change when he started hosting and presenting programmes in school, an experience that helped him discover his passion and purpose.

According to him, the positive response from people who approached him after his presentations made him realise that he could use his voice to make a difference in society.

“I realised that I came into this world for something. I have a mission on earth, and that is to make people feel something and use my voice for the voiceless,” he said.

Afolayan also acknowledged the support of his family, particularly his mother, whom he described as instrumental to his journey.

He expressed gratitude to her and other family members for their encouragement and support throughout his personal struggles.
