I’m scared of marriage, relationships – Mr Macaroni

By Punch Punch 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerian actor and comedian Debo Adedayo, popularly known as Mr Macaroni, has said he is scared of marriage and romantic relationships, admitting that he is unc

The comedian said his prolonged period of being single had made it difficult for him to adjust to the expectations of a relationship, particularly the need for constant communication and attention.

Mr Macaroni made this known during an appearance on the In My Opinion podcast on Sunday, where he spoke about his personal life and challenges with romantic relationships.

He disclosed that he had been having conversations with a woman he genuinely loved but was unsure whether he could commit fully to the relationship.

“I’m so scared of marriage, relationships. I’ve been single for so long. But the truth is, I don’t know how to do it anymore.

“I’m not sure. And when I say do it, I mean the constant calls. A part of me wants it. A part of me knows that I care for this person, but some days I just want to be left alone,” he said.

The actor described it as unfair to tell someone that you love them when you are not willing to commit fully to the relationship.

He said being in a relationship came with responsibilities that he was not certain he could fulfil.

“I always think that it is totally unfair to say to someone that you love them, but you’re not willing to commit all the way through.

“If you say you want to be with someone, you have responsibility,” he added.

Explaining his struggles with communication, Mr Macaroni said he sometimes found it difficult to keep up with the expectation of regular contact.

He recalled an occasion when he promised to call the woman after a conversation but became engrossed in playing FIFA after returning home.

He said although he remembered his promise, he continued playing the game instead of making the call.

The comedian also disclosed that he could go for one or two weeks without communicating with someone, adding that he sometimes preferred to be alone, play video games and have his personal space.

He, however, clarified that his reluctance to embrace relationships was not necessarily connected to his career but to how long he had been accustomed to being single.

“I think it’s not because of my career. I think it’s just because it’s been so long,” he said.

Mr Macaroni, who is known for his comedy skits and the character Daddy Waidi, began his acting career with minor roles before venturing into content creation.

During the interview, he said frustration with waiting for bigger acting opportunities partly pushed him to create his own content.

The actor announced on November 3 that he had earned a Master of Arts degree with distinction in Performance (Acting) from the Mountview Academy of Theatre Arts in London.

He announced the achievement in a video shared on his Instagram page, describing the programme as challenging.

He added that completing the degree had tested him in several ways but contributed to his personal and professional development.
