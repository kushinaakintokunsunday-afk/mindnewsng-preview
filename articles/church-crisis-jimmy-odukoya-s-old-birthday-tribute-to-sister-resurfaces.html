Church crisis: Jimmy Odukoya’s old birthday tribute to sister resurfaces

By Punch Punch 2d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Nigerians on social media have revisited a birthday tribute shared by Senior Pastor of the Fountain of Life Church, Jimmy Odukoya, to his sister, Pastor Carolin

Jimmy had celebrated Caroline on September 20, 2025, when she turned 40, describing her as the firstborn of the family and expressing affection for her.

In the birthday post shared on his X account, he wrote, “Caroline Toluwani Odukoya! First of her name! First born of the Clan! You are now officially Old SS!! Now you are 40, you won’t let us rest oh. Pictures don’t cut it, so here’s us being us. Love you, sis. 4O looks good on you!!!”

The post has resurfaced following the public disagreement involving the siblings and other members of the church’s Board of Trustees.

.

Several Nigerians reacting to the old post expressed hope that the family would resolve their differences and restore peace.

A user, Lamegh Nguher Jennifer (@IameghJ), wrote, “And now they’re in court fighting each other, God Abeg o.”

Another user, Ladi Ogunseye (@LadiSpeaks), urged the pastor to settle the disagreement, writing, “Don’t let the devil come between you both. Man up and settle the dispute/difference even if you have to compromise and beg her.”

Oluwatomilola (@IamTomieBlaq), in a reaction, prayed, “May God bring back the family.”

Another user, Naomi O (@TserunD), wrote, “Omo make devil leave this family alone abeg.”

Oyeyemi Ariyo-Osho (@oyeyemiariyo) also appealed for peace, writing, “Devil, leave this family alone!!!! Devil, leave this family alone!!!! Devil, leave this family alone!!!! @Toluwaniodukoya x @iamthatpj ni oruko Jesu, oju ma ti esu lori idile yin.”

A user identified as A Cactus is A Plant That Stands on (@…), criticised the church elders, writing, “Shame on all the church elders!”

Another user, Seyi (@ThonyBlack01), reacted, “See Wetin Una ruin fgs.”

Daniella (@UlokoDaniel…) expressed sadness over the resurfaced post, writing, “Gosh who brought this video back. I’m going to cry.”

Godfather Lagata (@HansLandaQ), whose account is labelled as a parody account, also reacted, questioning why people were surprised by the disagreement.

Sonia (@soniaezee) suggested that Caroline should have become the church’s head pastor, alleging that she was overlooked because she is a woman.

PUNCH Online had reported that Jimmy approached the Federal High Court in Lagos to challenge his purported removal as the church’s Senior Pastor and Chairman of its Board of Trustees.

He instituted the suit alongside the Incorporated Trustees of the Fountain of Life Church against eight defendants, including his sister, Pastor Tolu Odukoya, and other church leaders.

In the court processes, dated November 20, 2026, Jimmy challenged the validity of meetings held by some members of the Board of Trustees on January 22, February 28 and November 16, 2026, as well as decisions taken at the meetings.

He maintained that the meetings and resolutions, including the decision to remove him from office, violated the church’s constitution.

The pastor said he was appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees at an emergency meeting on September 14, 2023, and was subsequently installed that month.

The dispute became public on November 17, when some members of the church’s Board of Trustees announced his removal, citing disagreements over the church’s succession arrangement and other issues.

Jimmy and the church, however, rejected the removal, insisting that his appointment was valid and that the decision violated the church’s constitution.

The trustees later named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader.

The Lagos State High Court subsequently issued an interim order restraining Jimmy from presenting himself as the church’s Senior Pastor, a decision he is challenging at the Federal High Court.

The court had yet to determine the issues raised in the suit as of the time of filing this report.
