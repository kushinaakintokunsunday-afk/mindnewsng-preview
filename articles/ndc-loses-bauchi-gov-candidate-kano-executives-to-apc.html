NDC loses Bauchi gov candidate, Kano executives to APC

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Nigeria Democratic Congress governorship candidate in Bauchi State, Ibrahim Kashim, has dumped the party for the All Progressives Congress, ahead of the 202

Also, the vice-chairman of the NDC in Bichi Local Government Area of Kano State, Haladu Sa’adu, and five ward chairmen have defected to the APC.

The defections are coming also three months to the February 6, 2027 governorship election, with the list of candidates, including that of Kashim, already released by the Independent National Electoral Commission.

Kashim, a former Secretary to the State Government, announced his defection on Monday at a ceremony held at the Explaining his decision, the ex-SSG said he joined the APC because it offered Bauchi a stronger opportunity to participate in national governance and attract development.

He described the APC as the governing party at the federal level, with an overwhelming presence across Northern Nigeria, as well as the political structure and institutional capacity to advance the state’s interests.

Kashim said, “The APC is currently the governing party at the federal level and has an overwhelming presence across Northern Nigeria.

“It is also the party with the political structure, national reach and institutional capacity to provide a viable platform for the political and developmental aspirations we seek for Bauchi.

“I believe that Bauchi must be strategically positioned within this political reality.

“We cannot afford to gamble with the future of our state by placing our developmental aspirations on a platform that lacks comparable national reach and institutional influence.

“Our decision must be guided by the interests of Bauchi, not sentiment, political nostalgia or the convenience of individuals.”

The former SSG added, “The question is not simply which party can put forward a candidate. The question is which party has the organisational strength, national reach, political influence and institutional capacity to advance Bauchi’s interests. I am convinced that the APC offers the stronger platform.”

Kashim said his decision to leave the NDC followed his resignation from the party on October 16, 2026, adding that his commitment to the political struggle for a better Bauchi remained unchanged.

He appealed to his supporters, political associates and other stakeholders to join him in the APC, insisting that his political ambition was driven by the desire to improve governance and development in the state.

“Our ultimate objective is not the triumph of one political party over another. It is the triumph of good governance over poor governance, development over stagnation, opportunity over deprivation and hope over despair.

“I have chosen strategic engagement over political isolation. I have chosen a broader national platform over limited political reach.

“Above all, I have chosen what I sincerely believe offers Bauchi State a better opportunity for development and prosperity,” he said.

In Kano, the NDC vice-chairman in Bichi Local Government Area, Sa’adu, and five ward chairmen, including the party’s treasurer, Mannir Umar, ward secretaries, women and youth leaders and other members of the Kwankwasiyya movement, announced their decision to dump the party on Monday during a visit to the member representing Bichi Federal Constituency, Kabir Abubakar, at his office in Kano.

The PUNCH gathered that the five ward chairmen are from Waire, Danzabuwa, Kaukau, Muntsira and Bichi wards of the local government area.

Speaking on behalf of the defectors, the Danzabuwa Ward Chairman, Abdulkadir Lawan, said the lawmaker’s developmental projects and commitment to the people influenced their decision to join the APC.

“I want to inform you that we are all from Bichi, and we have decided to announce our defection from the NDC to the APC because we have recognised your dedication and the developmental projects you have executed in the Bichi Local Government,” Abdulkadir said.

He also commended the lawmaker for providing educational opportunities, saying he benefited from a scholarship programme facilitated by the Abubakar Kabir Foundation.

“I was previously a member of the opposition, but I benefited from your scholarship programme, which enabled me to pursue my master’s degree.

“Through your foundation, the Abubakar Kabir Foundation, I am now pursuing my PhD without any form of harassment or intimidation,” he said.

Abdulkadir also cited what he described as a lack of proper coordination and respect for members by the NDC leadership in Kano State, including issues surrounding the party’s leadership under Rabiu Kwankwaso, as reasons for their defection.

Speaking while receiving the defectors, the lawmaker welcomed them into the APC.

“I heartily welcome you all into the All Progressives Congress. Your decision to join us will further strengthen our grassroots structure in Bichi and the entire federal constituency,” he said.

He highlighted some of his interventions in Danzabuwa Ward, including the construction of a primary school, ongoing road projects and the provision of boreholes.

“We have continued to deliver projects that directly touch the lives of our people, and we will not relent. I urge you to work hard towards strengthening party unity and grassroots mobilisation ahead of the forthcoming elections,” Abubakar stated.

He also called for support for President Bola Tinubu, Governor Abba Yusuf and other APC candidates in the next elections.

“I urge all of you to continue to support President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf and all our candidates. Together, we will consolidate the gains of this administration,” he added.

The event was attended by the APC member representing Bichi Constituency in the Kano State House of Assembly, Lawan Bichi; the Director-General of the campaign council, Sani Bichi; the Vice-Chairman of Bichi Local Government, Babangida Danzabuwa; the APC State Secretary, Yusuf Sabo; and the Special Adviser on Publicity, Ahmad Aruwa, among other party leaders.
