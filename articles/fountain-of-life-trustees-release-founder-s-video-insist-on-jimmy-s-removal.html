Fountain of Life trustees release founder’s video, insist on Jimmy’s removal

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 6 min read

The Board of Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church has released a video in which its late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, named Pastor Kunle Areogun as his preferred successor, while insisting that his son, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya, is no longer recognised as the church’s Senior Pastor.

In a statement dated November 17, 2026, the trustees reaffirmed their November 16 decision to withdraw recognition of Jimmy as Senior Pastor and announced the appointment of Pastor Rotimi Okpaise to oversee the church’s spiritual and administrative affairs in an interim capacity.

The development has deepened the leadership crisis at the church, with Jimmy’s camp rejecting his purported removal as constitutionally invalid and insisting that he remains the duly appointed Senior Pastor and Chairman of the Board of Trustees.

The video, reportedly recorded in December 2021, captures the late founder outlining his preferred succession arrangement in the event of his death or incapacity. He named Areogun as the next Senior Pastor and assigned Jimmy administrative and youth ministry responsibilities.

“But the reason I’m doing this is to let you know that incase of my demise, which I don’t expect to happen soon, or a situation where I cannot write or talk, please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun and to be his immediate assistant, that is in terms of administration, is Jimmy Odukoya, who will be the head of the youth church,” Taiwo said in the recording.

He explained that the instructions were intended to address a provision in the church’s constitution that was undergoing review.

“Every other thing remains the same as laid out in the constitution. The reason I am doing this again is this, because that is an aspect of the constitution that we are trying to rework which we haven’t concluded. We will continue to do that but if for any reason I am not able to see that through this is what I intend they should folllow,” he said.

According to the trustees, they did not see the recording until late December 2025, more than two years after the founder’s death in August 2023.

They alleged that the video was not disclosed during deliberations that led to Jimmy’s appointment as Senior Pastor in September 2023, despite his assurance that his father had handed over the leadership mantle to him.

“Upon enquiry by the Board, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya informed the Board, and subsequently the Church, that our late Senior Pastor had handed the mantle of leadership over to him. The Board placed considerable reliance upon this assurance in its deliberations on the succession,” the statement said.

The trustees said they appointed Jimmy in good faith based on the information available to them and their understanding of the church’s constitutional provisions.

However, they argued that the emergence of the recording raised questions about whether the succession process was conducted with full disclosure of relevant information.

“Significantly, the individual nominated in that recording was not Pastor Jimmy Odukoya,” the Board stated.

It added that the recording was not presented during the original deliberations, depriving members of the Board of information that could have influenced their decision.

The trustees said they had reviewed the recording, the circumstances surrounding Jimmy’s appointment and the implications for the legitimacy of the succession process, taking into account the church’s constitution and other governing instruments.

They also accused Jimmy of failing to cooperate sufficiently with efforts to resolve disagreements over the church’s administration and governance.

According to the Board, months of consultations and reconciliation efforts, including interventions by respected Christian leaders described as “Fathers of the Faith”, failed to resolve the disagreements.

“Regrettably, notwithstanding these interventions, repeated representations and efforts at reconciliation, the substantive concerns raised by the Board remain unresolved.

“Pastor Jimmy Odukoya has not provided the level of engagement and cooperation necessary to achieve a satisfactory resolution,” the statement alleged.

The trustees said the dispute extended beyond the succession recording to concerns about church administration, governance arrangements and the preservation of the ministry’s founding vision. They also referred to what they described as moral and doctrinal controversies but did not provide specific details.

Reaffirming its decision, the Board said Jimmy was no longer recognised as Senior Pastor and was required to relinquish the office and its functions with immediate effect.

“Accordingly, having previously called upon Pastor Jimmy Odukoya to step down, the Board, at its meeting held on 16 November 2026, resolved that Pastor Jimmy Odukoya is no longer recognised as Senior Pastor of The Fountain of Life Church, with immediate effect. He is required to step down forthwith from the office and all functions of Senior Pastor,” it said.

The trustees subsequently announced Okpaise’s appointment to oversee the church pending consultations and other processes to establish a substantive leadership structure in line with its governing instruments.

The appointment has raised questions about the status of Areogun, whom the late founder named in the recording as his preferred successor. The trustees did not explain whether he had been approached or how his designation would feature in the process of selecting a substantive leader.

The leadership dispute has also extended to control of the church’s official communication channels. The trustees alleged that Jimmy or individuals acting on his behalf had taken control of the church’s social media platforms without their authorisation.

They accused those managing the accounts of publishing an inaccurate and misleading account of the controversy and warned members, journalists and the public against treating posts on the platforms as the Board’s official position.

The trustees said they were taking steps to restore what they described as authorised control of the church’s communication channels.

However, Jimmy’s camp has rejected the Board’s position, insisting that his appointment remains valid.

In a counterstatement issued on behalf of the church, his camp described the purported removal as constitutionally invalid and accused the trustees behind the announcement of acting outside the church’s governing framework.

It alleged that the trustees concerned had been asked to resign in March 2026 over repeated violations of the church’s constitution and founding principles.

The statement maintained that Jimmy’s appointment was unanimously approved by the Board in September 2023 and that no constitutionally valid decision had removed him from office.

The competing statements have therefore raised questions over the late founder’s succession instructions, the validity of Jimmy’s appointment and which faction has the authority to make binding decisions on behalf of the church.

The trustees, however, said their decision was not motivated by personal hostility but was necessary to protect the church’s integrity and reputation.

“The Board emphasises that this decision is not intended as an act of vindictiveness, humiliation or personal condemnation,” the statement said.

“Rather, it is a necessary and proportionate response to circumstances which, if left unaddressed, could inflict lasting damage on the Church and the legacy entrusted to its care.”

The Fountain of Life Church was founded in 1992 by Taiwo and his late wife, Pastor Bimbo Odukoya, and is headquartered in Ilupeju, Lagos. Following Taiwo’s death in August 2023, Jimmy was appointed Senior Pastor.

The trustees appealed to members to remain peaceful and prayerful as efforts to resolve the leadership and governance issues continue.
