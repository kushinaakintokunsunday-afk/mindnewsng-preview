EPL: Arteta warns Arsenal players against leaking team news through FPL

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta has warned his players against inadvertently revealing sensitive team information through Fantasy Premier League (FPL), following a

The warning comes after Tzolis reportedly selected himself as captain in his Fantasy Premier League team before Arsenal’s encounter with Liverpool on November 1, despite having been sidelined for a month through injury.

As reported by BBC Sport on Friday, Arteta, speaking ahead of Saturday’s Premier League fixture against Newcastle United, disclosed that he had addressed the issue with his players and people close to the club to prevent further leaks of confidential team information.

“With Christos, with the squad and with my kids as well,” Arteta said.

“They’re constantly asking me about this as well, because they want to know. It’s a topic.”

Tzolis was subsequently named in Arsenal’s starting line-up at Anfield, making his first appearance since Greece’s Nations League draw with the Netherlands on October 1.

Arteta described the situation as a new challenge brought about by the growing popularity of fantasy football, stressing that the club would introduce measures to protect sensitive team information.

“This is the kind of thing that is happening in our society right now. It’s something new. We haven’t dealt with it before, and we need to put new things in place,” he said.

“Obviously, confidentiality is something critical because it’s about trust and about what you are trying to do, and that should be kept as a huge secret. We’ll take measures to make sure it doesn’t happen.”

The Spaniard also confirmed that Arsenal players are restricted from selecting themselves in their FPL teams, as the club seeks to prevent information about injuries and starting line-ups from becoming public before matches.

The development follows a similar incident involving Chelsea in October, when several FPL teams reportedly linked to club staff and players removed Brazilian striker Joao Pedro from their selections, raising speculation that he had sustained an injury.

The incidents have highlighted growing concerns among Premier League clubs over how fantasy football selections could unintentionally expose confidential information about team selection and player availability.
