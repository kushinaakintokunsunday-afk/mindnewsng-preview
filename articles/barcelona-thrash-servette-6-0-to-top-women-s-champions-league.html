Barcelona thrash Servette 6-0 to top women’s Champions League

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

A Claudia Pina hat-trick fired Barcelona top of the women’s Champions League table on Thursday in a 6-0 home thrashing of Servette, while Lyon earned an impress

A Claudia Pina hat-trick fired Barcelona top of the women’s Champions League table on Thursday in a 6-0 home thrashing of Servette, while Lyon earned an impressive 2-0 win at Arsenal to keep pace.

Barcelona and Arsenal both have 13 points from five of six league phase games, while Bayern Munich cruised to a 4-0 victory over Austria Vienna and are level third on 11pts with Chelsea.

“I was just saying to my teammates that it was really important to finish first in the standings,” Pina told reporters.

“I don’t know if we can now switch off a little bit mentally but we’ve got a bit of calm.

“If we end up finishing top, even better, but it’s the Champions League, every match can be tricky,” she added.

Klara Buehl was named player of the match and she both opened the scoring and made an assist with Pernille Harder, Alara and Sophie Proost rounding out the comfortable win for the German champions.

Austria Vienna played with plenty of spirit but remain winless with only a point from five games as Bayern put on a display of firepower in the final third.

Despite the defeat, it was a memorable occasion for Austria Vienna, who showcased their progress in front of a record crowd for a women’s club football match in Austria with around 10,000 fans in attendance.

Roma took the lead twice at Benfica who battled back twice only to concede a late defeat when Roma’s Sofia Jahnukainen lost her marker late on for a superb winner making it 3-2 from a tight angle on 90+3.

Paris FC edged Danish side HB Koge 2-1, fighting back from going a goal down early thanks to midfielder Sheika Scott’s leveller on 35 minutes and the winner deep into second-half stoppage time from Celina Ould Hocine.

Veerle Buurman struck late as Chelsea edged Paris Saint-Germain 1-0 on Wednesday as English champions Manchester City and Spain’s Real Madrid earned vital wins to keep up with the early pace-setters after five rounds of matches.
