PUBLIC NOTICE

MONI PULO LIMITED: CLARIFICATION ON MISLEADING PUBLICATIONS

We consider it necessary to issue this clarification following recent media publications concerning our Executive Chairman, Dr. Seinye Lulu-Briggs. The publications contain misleading assertions and representations which warrant clarification in the interest of truth, fairness and the confidence ofour stakeholders and the general public.

These very libelous publications, which originated from a purported petition by an organisation identified as the Public Interest and Diplomatic Centre (PIDC), sought to create the impression that our Executive Chairman was the subject of an outstanding criminal process, was evading lawenforcement authorities and was liable to arrest in connection with certain court proceedings. The representations are misleading, malicious and do not reflect the true position of the matter.

For the avoidance of doubt, there is no criminal charge pending against Dr. Seinye Lulu-Briggs in respect of any matter whatsoever and definitely not in respect of the matters being referenced in these publications. Theunderlying disputes relate to shareholding and interests in the Company.They are civil and corporate matters which have been fully litigated before the courts in Nigeria with the judgements and rulings delivered in favour of Moni Pulo Limited and our Executive Chairman.

It is therefore fundamentally misleading and terrible to portray these corporate and shareholding disputes as evidence of criminal wrongdoing or to suggest that our Executive Chairman is a fugitive from justice or isseeking to evade law enforcement authorities.

Furthermore, the purported Public Interest and Diplomatic Centre, onwhose representation the publications appear to have been predicated, does not exist. It is not registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. This raises legitimate questions about these faceless unscrupulous persons and the authority under which they purport to act.

We wish to state unequivocally that our Executive Chairman remains a free citizen, continues to conduct her legitimate personal and business affairs without restraint and has no pending criminal charge arising from the referenced shareholding disputes.

Contrary to the wild, calculated, deliberate, misinformed, false, mischievous and callous publication in respect and concerning our Executive Chairman, we wish to point out the following unassailable points:

i. Our Executive Chairman is a private businesswoman who has invested heavily in the oil and gas sector in Nigeria and currently the Executive Chairman of one of the first indigenous exploration and production companies in Nigeria.
ii. For three decades, Moni Pulo Limited has maintained a significant presence in Nigeria’s oil and gas industry as an indigenous exploration and production company. And for 15 of those years, our Executive Chairmanhas remained the majority shareholder of the Company. The Company hasbuilt its reputation through sustained investment, operational experience,industry participation and contributions to the development of Nigeria’s petroleum resources.
iii. Our Executive Chairman’s huge investment in the oil and gas sector are products of her efforts in private business without any taint of any proceedof crime.
iv. She is a responsible, respectable, decent and accomplished businesswoman and philanthropist who has touched the lives of so many people both in Nigeria and beyond.
v. All through her private business endeavours, our Executive Chairmanhas maintained a very decent record and significant impact which has brought fame, pride and recognition to her person, family, her people inRivers State, Nigeria and the global business community.
vi. As an evangelist who is also committed to the propagation of the gospel of salvation and of Christ, she abhors every form of criminality, has maintained an unblemished record and shall therefore resist every effort to link her with any form of criminality as the publication would appear to have done.
vii. By recklessly assaulting her integrity and painting her as a common fugitive, the publication has assaulted the credibility and integrity of our Executive Chairman and subjected her to embarrassment, humiliation, character assassination and public ridicule.

In light of the above, we hereby refute these malicious and libelous publications and state categorically that the publications are complete falsehood as there is no order/warrant of any court

in Nigeria as at today, made or issued for the arrest of our chairman. If anything, all those cases brought against our Executive Chairman andMoni Pulo Limited in court have all been fully litigated and resolved in our favour.

Moni Pulo Limited remains an active and operating oil and gas company under the visionary leadership of Dr. Seinye Lulu-Briggs. Our business operations continue, and the Company remains focused on its corporate objectives, operational responsibilities, business relationships and long-term growth prospects.

We therefore urge the general public to kindly disregard any story, news and/or publication issued and/or disseminated by any media outlet or entity either physically or online suggesting that there

is any warrant of arrest issued against our Executive Chairman. Our Executive Chairman is a free woman and have been exercising full rights of ingress in and egress out of Nigeria without any impairment and/or harassments by any law enforcement and this is not likely to change in the foreseeable future.

We appreciate the continued confidence, support and understanding of our business partners, associates, regulators, host communities and other stakeholders. We reassure you all and the public that Moni Pulo Limited will continue to conduct its affairs with the professionalism, diligence and corporate responsibility that have characterised its operations over the past three decades.

FOR MONI PULO LIMITED:

MANAGEMENT
