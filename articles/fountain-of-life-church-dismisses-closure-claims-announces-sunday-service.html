Fountain of Life Church dismisses closure claims, announces Sunday service

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Fountain of Life Church, Ilupeju, Lagos, has urged members and the public to disregard reports suggesting that its premises have been sealed, inviting worsh

The church, in a post shared on its official X account on Friday, said its Sunday service would hold at 9am, encouraging members to attend physically or participate online.

“Don’t believe the noise; church is not sealed, see you in church on Sunday,” the church wrote in its flyer.

In a separate message accompanying the post, it invited worshippers to its 9am service, saying, “Don’t believe the noise! Join us for service every Sunday at TFOLC. There’s a place for you, you can join us in person or online! 9AM WAT. We look forward to welcoming you!”

However, the development should be distinguished from the recent court proceedings, as the certified true copy of the court order did not direct the closure or sealing of the church premises.

PUNCH Online had reported that the Lagos State High Court restrained Jimmy Odukoya from holding himself out as the Senior Pastor and Spiritual Head of the church, following an application by the Incorporated Trustees of The Fountain of Life Church.

Justice O.O. Adewunmi-Oshin made the interim orders on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, in a suit filed by the church’s trustees.

The court also restrained Odukoya, whether acting personally or through proxies, from interfering with, disrupting or obstructing the church’s activities, governance and other business.

The orders were made ex parte, meaning they were granted without first hearing Odukoya’s response to the application.

The court, however, expressly preserved his right to apply to have the orders set aside or respond to the substantive claims after being served with the court processes.

The case was adjourned until December 1, 2026, for mention.

The leadership dispute became public on November 17 when a faction of the church’s Board of Trustees announced that Odukoya was no longer recognised as senior pastor, citing a succession video recorded by the late founder, Pastor Taiwo Odukoya, in December 2021.

The trustees subsequently named Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader.

However, Jimmy and the church leadership rejected the decision, insisting that his appointment as senior pastor in September 2023 was valid.

Meanwhile, the church had separately announced the postponement of its 2026 Thanksgiving Service, earlier scheduled for Sunday, November 22, saying a new date would be communicated.

The latest invitation to its regular Sunday service indicates that the church is still asking worshippers to attend its weekly gathering despite the controversy surrounding its leadership.
