Sanwo-Olu advocates flexible mortgages to boost homeownership

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

 Sanwo-Olu stated this over the weekend during the inauguration of Treasure Estate in the Ajah area of Lagos, according to a statement.

 He stressed that the government uses both public and private financing to expand access to decent housing, while assuring residents that external road access and estate drainage infrastructure would be properly addressed.

 “Developers should explore mortgage options where buyers can demonstrate long-term repayment capacity. The government uses both public and private financing to expand access to decent housing while assuring residents that external road access and estate drainage infrastructure would be properly addressed,” Sanwo-Olu said.

 Sanwo-Olu acknowledged the long-standing land challenges that trailed the property for years before its eventual delivery.

 He disclosed that the state was preparing to inaugurate two housing estates in the Ajah area, assuring that political campaign periods would not disrupt governance or delay the handover of completed projects.

 “We know we are in a season, but the campaigning season we have must not stop the work that we do. The delivery remains a core pillar of the mandate given to his administration,” he assured.

 Sanwo-Olu explained that Treasure Estate was not designed exclusively for high-end buyers but to accommodate ordinary residents, including young professionals purchasing their first homes and growing families looking for adequate space and a secure living environment.

 “This is an estate that is not high-end. It has one-bedroom, two-bedroom and three-bedroom units. So, as a young person, you can afford a one-bedroom flat; you can do a two-bedroom as a young family, or a three-bedroom flat or three-bedroom terrace,” Sanwo-Olu explained.

 He also called on new homeowners and surrounding neighbours to foster a peaceful, welcoming community.

 Speaking on the economic realities surrounding the project, the Chief Executive Officer of AKF Properties Limited, Mr Ayodeji Fadoju, drew attention to the significant increase in construction costs between the commencement of the project around 2024–2025 and its completion.

 “A bag of cement, which cost about N5,000 when work began, rose to over N12,000 by delivery, alongside broader increases across building materials,” Fadoju said.

 He highlighted that, despite those pressures, determination and collaboration between LSDPC and AKF Properties enabled delivery to specification.

 “Gone are the days when you needed to pay 100 per cent for your houses,” the CEO said.

 He urged Nigerians and labour unions to take advantage of single-digit mortgage options when they meet documentation and repayment requirements.

 The Managing Director of LSDPC, Mr Ayodeji Joseph, described Treasure Estate as proof of how public land assets and private-sector expertise can combine to deliver housing and economic value.

 He also announced that Dogo Court in Apapa had been completed and was awaiting formal inauguration by the governor, while four additional housing estates were slated for unveiling between January and June 2027.

 Treasure Estate was developed under a joint venture agreement signed in 2023 between the Lagos State Development and Property Corporation and AKF Properties Limited. The estate occupies about 28,674 square metres of land covered by a Certificate of Occupancy issued by the state government to LSDPC.
