2026 Globe Soccer Awards: Messi, Ronaldo, Dembele, Yamal included [Full List]

By Vanguard Vanguard 6d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Vanguard

Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Kylian Mbappe, Ousmane Dembele and Lamine Yamal are among the high-profile footballers nominated for the 2026 Beyond Developmen

The nominees for the 17th edition of the awards were unveiled on Wednesday through the organisers’ official website, with Paris Saint-Germain and Spain among the prominent names following their successes in the UEFA Champions League and 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Dembele, who won the Best Men’s Player award last year, has been shortlisted again alongside 25 other players for the individual prize.

Meanwhile, Ronaldo will be seeking to retain the Best Middle East Player award after making the 2026 shortlist.

The awards ceremony is billed for December 28 in the United Arab Emirates, while public voting across 12 categories commenced on Wednesday and will end on November 30.

The organisers disclosed that fans would determine the first round of voting, with their votes combined with those of the Globe Soccer Jury during the second round.

The jury features several prominent former football figures, including Marcello Lippi, Antonio Conte, Iker Casillas and Luis Figo.

The previous edition attracted more than 30 million votes from over 200 countries and territories.

According to the organisers, supporters can cast their votes through the official website, vote.globesoccer.com, or the Globe Soccer mobile application.
