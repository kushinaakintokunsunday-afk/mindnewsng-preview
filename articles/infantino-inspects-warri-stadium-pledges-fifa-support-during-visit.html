Infantino inspects Warri Stadium, pledges FIFA support during visit

By Vanguard Vanguard 3d ago 3 min read

By Adegboyega Adeleye

FIFA President Gianni Infantino has inspected the remodeled Warri City Stadium and pledged the world football body’s continued support for Nigerian football.

Infantino visited the Delta State facility on Friday with FIFA Secretary-General Mattias Grafström, ahead of its scheduled commissioning on Saturday. He was received at Osubi Airport by Governor Sheriff Oborevwori and former Nigeria Football Federation president Amaju Pinnick.

Infantino praised the Delta State Government’s investment, saying modern sports facilities could help develop young talent and expand opportunities for boys and girls. He also celebrated Nigeria’s football heritage, highlighting the country’s contribution to the global game.

“This stadium is fantastic. It has everything you can dream of. I just would love to wear some shorts and kick the ball,” he said, adding that he hoped to return and play there once the remaining work was completed.

The FIFA president also congratulated Nigeria’s U-17 women’s team, the Flamingos, on winning the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup in Morocco, describing their triumph as a source of pride.

“You will always count on FIFA and on the FIFA president as your not just partner, but your family member,” Infantino said.

Oborevwori said the inspection was an opportunity to assess the stadium’s readiness against international standards. In a post on X, the governor described the renovation as extensive and said the state remained committed to delivering world-class sports infrastructure.

He wrote: “It was a pleasure to welcome the President of FIFA, Gianni Infantino, to Osubi this morning. We thereafter proceeded to the Warri Stadium, where we inspected the facility to assess its readiness against international standards,”

“The transformation is extensive, and our commitment remains to deliver world-class sporting infrastructure that will position Delta State to compete on the global stage and emerge as a hub for sporting excellence. In the words of the FIFA President, Gianni Infantino: “This is fantastic!”

Earlier on Friday, Infantino inaugurated the Hussey College Sports Complex in Warri, a grassroots facility donated by the Brownhill Foundation with support from the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board. During his visit, he also stressed the importance of bringing football closer to communities through local facilities.

Infantino’s visit to Warri is among several trips he has made to Nigeria since becoming FIFA president. He first came to the country in July 2016 for a two-day working visit to Abuja, where he met then-President Muhammadu Buhari, Nigerian football officials and presidents of African football associations.

He returned in February 2018 for the inaugural AITEO/CAF Football Awards in Lagos and a FIFA Executive Football Summit. In September 2021, he visited Lagos for the opening of the Aisha Buhari Cup and toured the renovated Moshood Abiola National Stadium in Abuja.
