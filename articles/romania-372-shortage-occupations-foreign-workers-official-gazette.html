Romania Releases List Of 372 Occupations With Shortage Of Workers For Foreigners To Apply - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Romania Official Gazette / Legit.ng 16 Nov 2026 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Romania Official Gazette / Legit.ng

Romania published 372 shortage occupations in its Official Gazette under the COR classification - truck drivers, welders, electricians, cooks, farm workers, hot

Romania has published an official list of 372 occupations with shortages of workers in its Official Gazette, Part I, No. 941, dated November 11, 2026, giving foreign nationals a reference for job titles they can target when seeking work in the country.

The list appears under the country’s official Classification of Occupations (COR) and covers both skilled and unskilled roles across construction, manufacturing, agriculture, transport, hospitality, healthcare, food production and administrative services.

Some of the roles on the list include: truck drivers, welders, electricians, cooks, mechanics, carpenters, bricklayers, farm workers, hotel staff, cleaners, childcare workers, nursing assistants, forklift operators, bakers, hairdressers, tailors, solar panel installers, crane operators, receptionists, accountants and many more.

How foreigners can use the list: the publication helps applicants identify occupations that match their qualifications and experience before applying. Romania runs two main employment routes for foreign nationals: one requires a Romanian employer to handle the application, while the other works through an authorised agency and is only available while the annual immigration quota has not been exhausted.

Important caveat: the inclusion of an occupation on the shortage list does not automatically guarantee a job, a work permit or a residence permit. Foreign applicants must still secure the required employment arrangements and meet Romania’s immigration conditions.

The full list is published on the government’s Work in Romania portal (workinromania.gov.ro).
