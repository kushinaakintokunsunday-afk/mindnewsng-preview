Organisers unveil judges for 7th BSMH edition

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Nigeria’s emerging digital innovators will have an opportunity to showcase their creativity, communication skills and problem-solving abilities before leading figures in business, public relations, broadcasting and entertainment at the seventh edition of Bodex Social Media Hangout (BSMH 7.0).

The organisers have unveiled a five-member judging panel comprising Paul Onwuanibe, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Landmark Africa; Adetokunbo Modupe, Founder and Chief Consultant of TPT International; Tosin Adefeko, Founder of PodFest Naija and Chief Executive Officer of AT3 Resources; Omotunde Adebowale-David, popularly known as Lolo1; and veteran actor, Keppy Ekpenyong Bassey.

The event, scheduled for December 10, 2026, at the Distance Learning Institute (DLI) Hall, University of Lagos, Akoka, Lagos, is expected to attract more than 3,000 students from secondary and tertiary institutions.

The unveiling of the judges marks a major milestone in preparations for the event, which seeks to equip young Nigerians with the knowledge, confidence and skills required to navigate an increasingly digital world.

The panel brings together professionals with extensive experience across different sectors of the economy. Onwuanibe’s entrepreneurial background and experience in business development will provide insights into innovation and commercial viability, while Modupe’s expertise in strategic communications will offer a perspective on effective messaging, reputation management and brand positioning.

Adefeko brings experience in podcasting, media entrepreneurship and the growing creator economy, while Lolo1 and Ekpenyong are expected to contribute their knowledge of broadcasting, entertainment, performance and audience engagement.
Together, the judges will assess participating students on originality, critical thinking, presentation, communication and the practical relevance of their ideas.

Beyond the opportunity to interact with established industry professionals, participating students will compete for a total prize pool of ₦4.4 million.
The competition will be divided into secondary and tertiary institution categories, with ₦2.2 million allocated to each category. In both categories, the winning institution will receive ₦1 million, while the second and third-place winners will receive ₦700,000 and ₦500,000, respectively.

An outstanding individual presenter will also receive a brand-new smartphone in recognition of exceptional articulation, confidence and presentation skills.
The organisers said the competitions were designed to encourage students to develop innovative ideas capable of addressing contemporary challenges while promoting responsible social media use and meaningful digital participation.

Speaking on the unveiling of the judging panel, Bodex Hungbo, Founder of Bodex Media and Convener of Bodex Social Media Hangout, said the selection reflected the organisation’s commitment to creating meaningful opportunities for young people to learn from experienced professionals.

“Many young people have brilliant ideas but rarely get the opportunity to present them before people who understand their potential.

“Imagine a secondary school student defending an idea before the CEO of Landmark Africa, a respected PR industry leader, a podcasting entrepreneur and established entertainment personalities. That experience can build confidence, sharpen ambition and open a young person’s mind to possibilities they may never have considered,” she said.

Hungbo added that the initiative was designed to connect young people with professionals willing to share their knowledge, challenge their thinking and help them recognise the value of their ideas.

According to her, while the financial rewards remain important, the exposure, experience and confidence gained through participation could have a more lasting impact on the students.

Since its establishment in 2019, Bodex Social Media Hangout has reportedly empowered more than 10,000 young people, engaged over 200 educational institutions and featured more than 300 speakers and thought leaders.

The initiative has also generated over 100 million cumulative digital media impressions and recognised more than 200 individuals for using social media to promote positive causes and societal development.
The seventh edition, themed “Influence with Purpose: Shaping Africa’s Digital Future,” will feature digital media masterclasses, debates, quizzes, interactive discussions, games and raffle draws, alongside other educational activities.

Participating students will also receive certificates of participation.
Through these activities, the organisers aim to strengthen digital literacy, promote responsible online communication and encourage innovation among young Nigerians while creating opportunities for meaningful engagement between emerging talents and established industry professionals.
