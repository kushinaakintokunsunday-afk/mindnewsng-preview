Fidelity Bank names Chiedoziem Amuchie new CEO

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Fidelity Bank Plc has announced the appointment of Stanley Chiedoziem Amuchie as its substantive Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, effective Januar

Fidelity Bank Plc has announced the appointment of Stanley Chiedoziem Amuchie as its substantive Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, effective January 1, 2027.

The appointment, which has received the approval of the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), will see Amuchie succeed Nneka Onyeali-Ikpe, whose tenure ends on December 31, 2026.

Amuchie is currently an Executive Director and Chief Operations and Information Officer of the bank.

The Chairman of Fidelity Bank, Amaka Onwughalu, said the appointment reflected the board’s confidence in his leadership ability, industry experience and understanding of the bank’s business.

“We are delighted to confirm Stanley Amuchie as the substantive Managing Director/Chief Executive Officer of Fidelity Bank. His appointment reflects the Board’s confidence in his leadership capabilities, strategic insight, extensive industry experience and deep understanding of the Bank’s business.

“We are confident that he will provide the leadership required to consolidate our achievements and drive the Bank’s next phase of growth,” she said.

Onwughalu also praised Onyeali-Ikpe for her contributions to the bank, describing her tenure as one marked by strategic vision, bold leadership and commitment to building a stronger institution.

“Under her stewardship, the Bank recorded significant milestones, strengthened its market position and expanded its strategic reach. We are deeply grateful for her dedication and the enduring legacy she leaves behind,” she said.

She added that the board would support the incoming CEO in driving innovation, operational efficiency and sustainable growth.

Amuchie brings more than 30 years of experience in the financial services industry, with expertise in leadership, financial management, strategy and institutional transformation.

He became Executive Director, Operations and Information Technology, at Fidelity Bank in January 2022, where he helped strengthen the bank’s operations and drive technology-led transformation.

Before joining Fidelity Bank, he worked at Zenith Bank Plc, where he held several senior positions, including Group Chief Financial Officer.

His professional achievements include playing a key role in Fidelity Bank’s acquisition of Union Bank UK, now known as FidBank UK, and contributing to the successful refinancing of the bank’s $400m Eurobond.

He also has experience in capital raising, treasury management, financial strategy and international business expansion.

Amuchie holds a First Class degree in Industrial Chemistry and a Master’s degree in Corporate Governance. He is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria and an Honorary Senior Member of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria. He is also pursuing a PhD in Management of Technology and Innovation.

His appointment comes as Fidelity Bank seeks to strengthen its market position, expand its digital services and grow its presence in local and international markets.

The bank serves more than 14 million customers through digital channels and 255 business offices in Nigeria, as well as its United Kingdom subsidiary, FidBank UK Limited.
