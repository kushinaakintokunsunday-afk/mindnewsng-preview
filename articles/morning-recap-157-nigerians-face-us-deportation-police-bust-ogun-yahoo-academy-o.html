Morning Recap: 157 Nigerians face US deportation, police bust Ogun ‘Yahoo academy’, other top stories

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

In Ogun State, police arrested 11 suspected internet fraudsters during a raid on an alleged internet fraud training centre, popularly called a “Yahoo academy”,

Meanwhile, a Lagos State High Court has ordered Pastor Jimmy Odukoya to appear on November 26 in connection with the leadership dispute at the Fountain of Life Church.

Ahead of the 2027 general elections, opposition parties have accused authorities in several states of restricting access to campaign venues, while political leaders and other dignitaries attended the traditional wedding of the daughter of the Minister of Solid Minerals Development, Dele Alake, in Lagos.

Here are five top stories this morning:

1. US adds 24 Nigerians to deportation list, total now 157

The United States Department of Homeland Security has added 24 Nigerians to its list of foreign nationals facing removal from the country, bringing the total number to 157. The individuals were listed over alleged offences including fraud, money laundering, drug-related crimes, identity theft and child cruelty, although the department did not disclose when they would be deported or their immigration status. Being named on the list does not, on its own, establish that they have been convicted of the offences.

Read Here: https://punchng.com/us-names-157-nigerians-for-deportation/

2. Police bust Ogun ‘Yahoo academy’, uncover suspected human remains

The Police Special Fraud Unit has arrested 11 suspected internet fraudsters during a raid on an alleged fraud training centre in Abeokuta, Ogun State. Police said they recovered suspected human remains, charms, blood-stained fetish items, 11 iPhones, three Samsung phones and eight laptops, while the alleged leader of the operation, Goriola Oluwaseun Olanrewaju, also known as Update, remains at large.

Read Here: https://punchng.com/police-bust-ogun-yahoo-academy-uncover-human-remains/

3. Court orders Jimmy Odukoya to appear November 26 over church dispute

The Lagos State High Court has ordered Pastor Jimmy Odukoya to appear before it on November 26 to show cause in the ongoing leadership dispute at the Fountain of Life Church. The order followed an application by the church’s trustees to serve him with court processes through substituted means, including newspaper publication and electronic channels, after an earlier ruling restrained him from presenting himself as the church’s senior pastor and spiritual head.

Read Here: https://punchng.com/court-orders-jimmy-odukoya-to-appear-november-26-over-church-dispute/

4. Campaign venue disputes deepen APC-opposition tensions ahead of 2027

The African Democratic Congress and the Nigeria Democratic Congress have accused authorities in Kaduna, Kano, Jigawa, Sokoto, Gombe and Ekiti states of restricting their access to public campaign venues and advertising spaces ahead of the 2027 elections. The APC and some state governments have denied the allegations, while former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar has also accused the Federal Government of intimidating ADC members following reported arrests in Kaduna.

Read Here: https://punchng.com/2027-campaign-venue-rows-fuel-apc-opposition-tensions/

5. Dele Alake’s daughter weds in Lagos

Wuraola Alake, daughter of the Minister of Solid Minerals Development, Dele Alake, has married her partner, Wasem Yaji, at a traditional wedding ceremony in Lagos. The Sunday event at the Eko Convention Centre attracted several dignitaries, including Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, Ogun State Governor Dapo Abiodun and the President’s Chief of Staff, Femi Gbajabiamila.

Read Here: https://punchng.com/photos-dele-alakes-daughter-weds-in-lagos/
