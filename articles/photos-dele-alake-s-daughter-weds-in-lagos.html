PHOTOS: Dele Alake’s daughter weds in Lagos

By Punch Punch 1d ago 1 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Wuraola, daughter of the Minister of Solid Minerals Development, Dele Alake, has married her partner, Wasem Yaji, at a traditional wedding ceremony in Lagos. Th

The ceremony was held on Sunday at the Eko Convention Centre, with political leaders, family members and other prominent personalities in attendance.

Photographs from the event showed the couple dressed in traditional outfits, posing together and with guests, while Alake was seen receiving and greeting attendees.

Among the dignitaries at the wedding were the Chief of Staff to the President, Femi Gbajabiamila; Lagos State Governor, Babajide Sanwo-Olu; his deputy, Obafemi Hamzat; and Ogun State Governor, Dapo Abiodun.

Other governors present included Lucky Aiyedatiwa of Ondo State, Biodun Oyebanji of Ekiti State, Babagana Zulum of Borno State, Uba Sani of Kaduna State and Hope Uzodimma of Imo State.

Former Ogun State Governor, Gbenga Daniel; former Ekiti State Governor, Niyi Adebayo; and Deputy President of the Senate, Barau Jibrin, also attended.

Others included former Minister of Defence and ex-Governor of Jigawa State, Abubakar Badaru; Executive Chairman of the Nigeria Revenue Service, Zacch Adedeji; Lagos State Commissioner for the Environment, Tokunbo Wahab; and Chairman of the Nigerians in Diaspora Commission, Abike Dabiri-Erewa.

Wasem Yaji is the son of the late Nigerian engineer, Aloysius Ahile Yaji.
