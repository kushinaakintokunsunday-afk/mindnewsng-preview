2027: Tinubu can’t save Alli, Yayi, Hamzat – Opposition

By Punch Punch 1d ago 8 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Leaders of the All Progressives Congress in Lagos, Ogun and Oyo states are regrouping and intensifying mobilisation to secure victory for the party’s governorsh

The renewed push by the ruling party comes as seven opposition parties, including the Peoples Democratic Party, African Democratic Congress, Labour Party, Nigeria Democratic Congress, Social Democratic Party, Allied Peoples Movement and Young Progressives Party, also move to strengthen their electoral prospects in the three South-West states ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The development has set the stage for a fierce political contest, with APC leaders seeking to consolidate support for their candidates and translate the President’s endorsements into electoral victories, while opposition parties insist they are determined to defeat the ruling party at the polls.

The APC is particularly keen to retain control of Lagos and Ogun states while wresting Oyo State from the opposition, with party leaders stepping up efforts to rally members and supporters behind the three governorship candidates.

At Saturday’s rally held at Mapo Hall, Ibadan, Tinubu publicly endorsed Senator Sharafadeen Alli, Senator Solomon Adeola, popularly known as Yayi, and Obafemi Hamzat as the APC governorship candidates in Oyo, Ogun and Lagos states, respectively.

The President raised the candidates’ hands in a symbolic show of support, telling the gathering, “The governor you will vote for, I will show you,” before presenting Alli to the crowd.

He also endorsed Remi Oseni, the APC senatorial candidate for Oyo South.

The endorsements have given fresh impetus to the ruling party’s campaign efforts in the three states, with the APC seeking to capitalise on the President’s political influence and mobilise support for its candidates.

However, opposition parties have dismissed the endorsements as insufficient to guarantee electoral victories, insisting that the ruling party would face stiff resistance in Lagos, Ogun and Oyo.

In separate exclusive interviews with The PUNCH on Sunday, representatives of the seven opposition parties argued that Tinubu’s backing would not automatically translate into votes, maintaining that the electorate would ultimately determine the outcome of the governorship elections.

Some of the parties described the endorsements as a potential electoral liability, arguing that dissatisfaction with the APC’s performance could undermine the chances of its candidates.

The SDP National Publicity Secretary, Rufus Aiyenigba, said Tinubu’s endorsement posed no threat to the electoral prospects of his party’s candidates.

He said, “The President’s endorsements are not in any way a threat to the chances of the candidates of the SDP. The Nigerian people will surely make their choices and rightly at the polls.”

Similarly, the NDC National Publicity Secretary, Osa Director, said the APC had lost the confidence of Nigerians, arguing that no number of endorsements could reverse the ruling party’s declining popularity.

He said, “APC has lost the trust and confidence of the people. No matter the number of endorsements, they have been massively rejected by the majority of Nigerians for their woeful failure and poor performance.

“The NDC is the preferred party by the people, and we shall win by a landslide in most of the states in the coming general elections.”

The Labour Party National Publicity Secretary, Ken Asogwa, went further, describing Tinubu’s endorsement as potentially counterproductive, given what he called the President’s poor performance in office.

Asogwa said although the President had the right to support candidates of his party, the electorate retained the power to decide their political fate.

He said, “The President has the right to endorse the candidates of his party, while the electorate has the ultimate right to decide on election day. How can the Labour Party be bothered by the endorsement made by a President whose record of performance is abysmally poor? Such an endorsement is counterproductive and an own goal against the endorsees.

“We all saw what happened in America recently, where several Republican candidates endorsed by Donald Trump lost elections in the midterms. The few who managed to win were those who were wise enough to extricate themselves from the failures of the Trump presidency.

“So, I am sure the candidates would secretly be cursing the President for bringing misfortune to their campaigns and chances of winning. Meanwhile, in Lagos, where we have Hamzat, who jointly supervised the pillaging of the state’s resources for the past eight years, what is he coming to tell Lagosians?

“The Labour Party candidate in Lagos, Rotimi Odunaike, is speaking to Lagosians, and the preponderance of opinion on the streets of Lagos is that he should be given a chance to come in and turn his dreams into reality.”

In Ogun State, the PDP also dismissed the significance of Tinubu’s endorsement of Yayi, insisting that residents had already made up their minds to vote for the party’s governorship candidate, Oladipupo Adebutu.

The Media Aide to Adebutu, Afolabi Orekoya, told our correspondent on Sunday that the President’s endorsement would not alter the political calculations in the state.

He said, “The endorsement of governorship candidates of Ogun, Oyo and Lagos by President Bola Tinubu during his campaign at Ibadan on Saturday has nothing to do with the chances of the PDP governorship candidate for Ogun State in person of Hon Oladipupo Adebutu.

“It has absolutely nothing to do with our candidate because it is not the President who will come around to vote in the state. The duty of voting in the next governor for the state is purely the prerogative of the residents of Ogun State, and the people are already talking daily that they are pitching their tent with the PDP.

“It is the people that will decide; they are the ones to determine the chances of everyone, and we are happy with the position that the people of the state are taking every day. They are confident that with the PDP back in power, all of this anguish and hardship foisted on them by the APC will become a thing of the past.

“Our campaign watchword is Itura De, meaning that relief is here. So, let the President endorse anybody; it is none of our business because we know that the people have taken their destiny into their hands to ensure that the next party in power in the state is PDP.

“By the special grace of God, the PDP is winning all 26 State House of Assembly members; we are winning all the House of Representatives and the three Senate seats.

“And for the governorship, PDP will clear all six local governments in Ogun Central; we shall clear all nine local governments in Ogun East and in Ogun West we are confident of winning three local governments. So Hon Oladipupo Adebutu will emerge victoriously as the next governor of the state come February 2027.”

The PDP State Publicity Secretary, Kayode Adebayo, also dismissed the endorsement, saying it would not give the APC any electoral advantage in the state.

He said, “Endorsement means nothing and cannot have any political advantage in the 2027 elections, particularly in Ogun State. The people of Ogun State will decide who becomes their governor in 2027, and they are already saying that it is Hon Oladipupo Adebutu and other candidates of our great party that they want.”

Also reacting, the Ogun State Chairman of the African Democratic Party, Otunba Olufemi Soluade, said although Tinubu had the right to endorse candidates of his choice, such backing did not guarantee electoral victory.

 “I can assure you that for our gubernatorial candidate, Otunba Adebisi Jimi Lawal and our other candidates, such endorsement doesn’t pose any threat to their popularity and victory at the polls.

 “The most important thing is the underground works, consultations and other strategies we employed and our continuous efforts and strategies different from the conventional one by other political parties.

“We are already harvesting defectors across the state, and in the coming days, major defectors from NNPP and other political, professional and cultural groups will speak to the popularity of our party, the ADC in the state.”

The Allied Peoples Movement also predicted keen contests for the APC candidates in the three states, arguing that growing opposition support, voter dissatisfaction and changing political alignments could work against the ruling party despite Tinubu’s endorsement.

The APM National Publicity Secretary, Abubakar Yusuf, told The PUNCH that voters in Lagos were increasingly yearning for a change, while the APC candidate in Ogun could face a difficult contest following the court clearance of the PDP candidate, Adebutu.

He said, “It will be keenly contested, particularly in the three states. First, in Lagos State, the consciousness among non-indigenes, particularly the Igbos, to vote for opposition parties, including the APM, NDC and ADC, is rising.

“Secondly, with the new movement and political orientation and reorientation going on in the states, no matter the backing and endorsement, they may lose. In Lagos, people are already tired of Hamzat’s years in government, so they will yearn for a change.
