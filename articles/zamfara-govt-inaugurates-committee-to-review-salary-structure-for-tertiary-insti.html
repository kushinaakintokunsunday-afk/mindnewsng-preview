Zamfara Govt. inaugurates committee to review salary structure for tertiary institutions

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4d ago 3 min read

From John Bassey, Gusau

Zamfara State Government has inaugurated a committee to review and address the non-implementation of revised salary structures and other staff welfare issues in the state-owned tertiary institutions.

According to the statement signed by Suleman Ahmad Tudu, Senior Special Assistant (Media), Office of the Secretary to the Zamfara State Government and made available to Journalists on Thursday, the Secretary to the State Government and Chairman of the Committee, Malam Abubakar Mohammad Nakwada, inaugurated the committee and chaired its maiden meeting in Gusau, the state capital.

The committee is expected to examine relevant government circulars towards addressing all outstanding welfare concerns of staff in the institutions.

It will also determine financial implications and funding requirements involved, and consult relevant stakeholders to facilitate amicable resolution.

Speaking at the inauguration, Malam Nakwada reaffirmed the administration’s commitment to staff welfare, industrial harmony, and the repositioning of tertiary education in the state.

He further emphasized that Governor Lawal has proven to be workers-friendly, citing the upward review of the minimum wage from paltry N7,000 to N70,000, the clearing of the backlog of gratuities, implementing new salary scale for healthcare workers, and other welfare commitments of the current administration.

“This assignment is a clear demonstration of Governor Lawal’s commitment to the welfare of workers, industrial harmony, and the development of our tertiary institutions.

“The demands presented by the Forum of Zamfara State Tertiary Institutions (FOZASTI) and the Zamfara State University on salary, allowances and promotion benefits have been a source of concern. The government considers it necessary to address these issues holistically, fairly and sustainably.

“Our assignment must be guided by objectivity, sincerity, and the best interest of the State. We must listen to all parties, examine the facts and figures dispassionately, and come up with recommendations that are implementable, affordable and just.

“I assure the staff unions and management of our institutions that this Government is not unmindful of their plight. At the same time, we must also be mindful of the financial realities of the State.

“Our goal is to find a lasting solution that will guarantee industrial peace and improve the quality of education in Zamfara State,” the SSG stated.

Malam Nakwada further appealed for patience and understanding as the government works gradually but deliberately to address staff welfare concerns, stressing that while not all issues can be resolved at once, every legitimate demand is being given the attention it deserves.

He urged aggrieved unions to give dialogue a chance and allow the committee to complete its assignment, noting that industrial action at this critical moment would only hurt but not help the system.

Members of the Committee include Honourable Commissioners for Finance, Education, Science and Technology, Local Government and Chieftaincy Affairs, and Budget and Planning.

Other members are Special Adviser on Economic and Financial Matters, the Representatives of the Forum of Tertiary Institutions, the Nigeria Labour Congress (NLC), Zamfara State Chapter, and Zamfara State University, while the Permanent Secretary, Ministry of Education, Science and Technology, serves as Secretary.
