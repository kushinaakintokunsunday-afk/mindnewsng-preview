Ex-Ajax captain abandons football for Afrobeats career

Former Ajax academy captain Elikia Mbinga has abandoned his football career to pursue music, emerging as one of the Netherlands’ rising Afrobeat artists with more than 30 million Spotify streams.

The 25-year-old once dreamed of becoming a professional footballer and playing before packed stadiums, having shared the pitch with several players who have since established themselves in the Premier League.

During his four-year spell at Ajax’s renowned De Toekomst academy, Mbinga played alongside Jurrien and Quinten Timber, Ryan Gravenberch and Brian Brobbey.

The midfielder, who captained his academy side, recalled his former teammates as exceptionally talented but said he could not have predicted how successful they would become.

“We had a feeling that we had joined this journey together. We stepped in together. Already then, you could see that Quinten and Jurrien were like machines. They were built like machines from a young age,” he told BBC Sport on Wednesday.

Mbinga also recalled Gravenberch’s technical ability and Brobbey’s physical strength, qualities that helped them progress to professional football.

However, while his former teammates continued their careers, Mbinga struggled to break into Ajax’s first team and returned to his boyhood club, PEC Zwolle, in 2021.

He made his professional debut against Ajax in September 2021 but managed only five further first-team appearances before Zwolle were relegated.

He subsequently dropped down the Dutch football pyramid, playing semi-professionally in the fourth tier while nurturing his growing interest in music.

Although football remained a major part of his life, his desire to create music and build something of his own gradually took precedence.

“I think it was also part of the personal growth. I really liked the music. I also liked looking up to the other artists at the time who were popping,” he said.

“For me, it was also a dream to do something in music, create my own songs. But at that time, I was really talented with football, but I couldn’t make music yet.”

His decision to pursue music has since paid off, with Mbinga building a growing following in the Dutch Afrobeat scene.

He now has nearly 500,000 monthly Spotify listeners, while his 2024 single, Mentality, has surpassed 16 million streams.

The former midfielder said the creativity he developed on the football pitch continues to shape his approach to music.

“As a footballer, I played in midfield and was a very creative player. That was my style on the pitch, and I try to bring that same creativity to my music,” he said.

“I want to experiment with songs, be as creative as possible and develop a sound of my own.”

Mbinga said musical success gives him the same excitement he once experienced playing football, likening the success of a song to scoring a goal or providing an assist.

He said a major hit feels like scoring the winning goal in an important final.

“And of course, if one of my songs really takes off, then it feels like I’m scoring the winning goal in a very important game – like a final!” he said.

Though his journey has taken a different path from that of his former Ajax teammates, Mbinga has found music another platform to showcase the creativity that once made him stand out on the football pitch.
