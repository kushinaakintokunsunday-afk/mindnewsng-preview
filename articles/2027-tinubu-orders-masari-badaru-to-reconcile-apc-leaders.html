2027: Tinubu orders Masari, Badaru to reconcile APC leaders

By Punch Punch 1d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu has urged leaders of the All Progressives Congress (APC) to set aside personal differences, forgive one another and embrace reconciliation

According to his spokesman, Bayo Onanuga, Tinubu gave the charge on Sunday during a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his extended family at the Yoruba Tennis Club in Lagos.

The President specifically directed his Special Adviser on Political Matters, Alhaji Ibrahim Masari, and former Minister of Defence, Alhaji Abubakar Badaru, to spearhead reconciliation efforts among aggrieved party leaders across the country.

Addressing reports of a disagreement between Katsina State Governor, Dikko Radda, and Masari, Tinubu instructed his political adviser to return home and resolve the differences.

“Alhaji Kabiru Masari, go back home and reconcile with everybody. That is the President’s order. When you finish that, you move to Jigawa. You move to other places and reconcile them, beg them because we have to reconcile and every anger must dissipate and evaporate,” he said.

He gave a similar assignment to Badaru, a former governor of Jigawa State, stressing the need for peace and cooperation among party members.

Tinubu maintained that personal grievances should not be allowed to undermine the collective interest of the APC, particularly as the party looks towards retaining power in 2027.

Reflecting on his emergence as President in the 2023 general elections, Tinubu acknowledged the difficulties Nigerians experienced during the cash scarcity and fuel shortages that characterised the period.

He recalled that despite the challenges, he secured victory, attributing his emergence to divine intervention.

“People cried. People suffered. People died. People had no five naira in their pockets because their money was in the bank and they couldn’t get it out. There was also no fuel. We invoked the spirit of Almighty Allah and said, ‘Kun faya kun’. It got us there, and we have the presidency. Allah gives power to whom he wants, and he takes it from whoever he wants,” the President said.

Revisiting his famous 2022 political declaration, “Emilokan”, meaning “It is my turn”, Tinubu told supporters that the time had come for them to reciprocate by working towards his re-election.

He described the moment as “Eyinlokan”, urging Lagos residents and other supporters to intensify grassroots mobilisation, encourage eligible voters to obtain their Permanent Voter Cards (PVCs) and participate actively in the forthcoming elections.

“The challenge of today is much easier than that of yesterday, but we cannot take it for granted. You must knock on your neighbour’s door, tell them to collect their PVC. If you have PVC, you have the power of January 16th and February 6th… We must generate enough votes to overwhelm the opponents,” he said.

The prayer session attracted several political leaders and government officials, including Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu; Kaduna State Governor Uba Sani; Kwara State Governor AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq; Ogun State Governor Dapo Abiodun; and Imo State Governor Hope Uzodimma.

Others present were National Security Adviser Nuhu Ribadu, Chief of Staff to the President Femi Gbajabiamila, Lagos State Deputy Governor Obafemi Hamzat, Minister of Education Tunji Alausa and Speaker of the Lagos State House of Assembly, Mudashiru Obasa.

In his remarks, Sanwo-Olu described Tinubu as an epitome of justice, fairness, equity and inclusive governance.

Badaru, who spoke on behalf of northern leaders, recalled Tinubu’s support for former President Muhammadu Buhari during the 2015 and 2019 presidential elections, urging voters in the North to reciprocate by supporting the current administration.

Also speaking, elder statesman Alhaji Lateef Femi Okunnu, 94, who chaired the occasion, pledged to campaign and vote for Tinubu in 2027.

Okunnu commended the administration’s infrastructure initiatives, particularly the Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway and Sokoto-Badagry Superhighway, as well as its efforts to strengthen the naira and boost the country’s foreign reserves.

Representatives of the Tinubu family and Muslim and Christian clerics offered prayers for the President’s health, success and the progress of Nigeria.

The Grand Chief Imam of Lagos, Sheikh Sulaimon Oluwatoyin Abou-Nolla, coordinated the prayer session and sought divine guidance for the President and his administration.

Tinubu, in his closing remarks, reaffirmed his commitment to purposeful leadership, national unity, justice and the building of a prosperous Nigeria, while urging party leaders to work together towards achieving their political objectives ahead of the 2027 elections.
