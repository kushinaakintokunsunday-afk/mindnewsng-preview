PHOTOS: Natasha donates patrol van, computers to Kogi police station

By Punch Punch 2d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Senator representing Kogi Central, Natasha Akpoti-Uduaghan, has facilitated the provision of an additional patrol van and two computers to the Ihima Police

This was disclosed in a statement issued by Akpoti-Uduaghan’s media aide, Mike Idoko, on Sunday.

She said the intervention is aimed at addressing some of the logistical and operational challenges confronting police officers, particularly in rural communities where difficult terrain and long distances often hamper patrols and emergency responses.

According to her, the additional patrol van is expected to improve the mobility of officers, expand security coverage and enable them to respond more promptly to distress calls across Ihima and neighbouring communities.

The two brand-new computers are also expected to strengthen the station’s administrative and operational capacity, particularly in record-keeping, crime reporting, documentation of complaints, case management and information retrieval for investigations.

Speaking on the intervention, Akpoti-Uduaghan said security agencies needed practical support and adequate equipment to discharge their responsibilities effectively.

She said, “Security is not something we should leave entirely to the men and women in uniform without providing them with the necessary tools to do their jobs. Our police officers need functional equipment, reliable transportation and the right working environment to respond effectively to the security needs of our communities.

“This additional patrol van will strengthen their mobility and enable them to reach more communities, while the computers will support proper documentation, record management and other essential police operations.

“My commitment is to continue supporting initiatives that improve the safety and wellbeing of our people. Every community deserves to feel protected, and our security agencies deserve the necessary support to deliver on their responsibilities.”

The lawmaker stressed that effective grassroots policing required adequate logistics, modern operational equipment and stronger collaboration between security agencies and the communities they serve.

She added that supporting security institutions remained an important part of her efforts to improve the safety and wellbeing of residents across Kogi Central.

The latest donation comes amid growing concerns over insecurity in parts of the country, with police formations, particularly those serving rural communities, facing the challenge of inadequate operational resources.

Residents of Ihima and neighbouring communities are expected to benefit from improved police visibility, faster emergency response and more efficient information management at the station.
