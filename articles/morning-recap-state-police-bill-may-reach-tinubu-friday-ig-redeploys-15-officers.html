All these and many more must-read stories lead today’s headlines

1. State police: Tinubu may receive bill Friday

The National Assembly may transmit the proposed state police bill to President Bola Tinubu for assent on or before Friday, following the expiration of the November 15 deadline for state Houses of Assembly to communicate their decisions. Parliamentary sources said the required two-thirds approval had been secured, paving the way for the next stage of the constitutional amendment process.

Read the full story here: https://punchng.com/state-police-tinubu-may-receive-bill-friday/

2. JABU alumni condemn INEC chair harassment in UK

The President of the Joseph Ayo Babalola University Alumni Association, Akinkunmi Akintayo, has condemned the alleged harassment of INEC Chairman, Professor Joash Amupitan (SAN), during his visit to London. Protesters reportedly surrounded his vehicle outside Chatham House and hurled insults at him. Akintayo described the incident as unfortunate and called for civility and responsible public engagement.

Read the full story here: https://punchng.com/jabu-alumni-condemn-inec-chair-harassment-in-uk/

3. Police shake-up: IG redeploys 15 AIGs, CPs

Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Disu has ordered the redeployment of 13 Assistant Inspectors-General of Police and two Commissioners of Police to various commands and departments nationwide. The postings, contained in a police wireless message dated November 15, take immediate effect.

Read the full story here: https://punchng.com/police-shake-up-ig-redeploys-15-aigs-cps/

4. GTBank customer petitions EFCC over N2.74m fraud, unauthorised loan

A GTBank customer, Stella Adepegba, and her husband, Adelani, have petitioned the EFCC over alleged fraudulent withdrawals totalling N2.744m from their family accounts. The petition also raised concerns over an alleged unauthorised N138,000 loan, a N24,000 repayment deduction and another N19,000 withdrawal. The family is seeking an investigation into the alleged fraud and possible insider involvement.

Read the full story here: https://punchng.com/gtbank-customer-petitions-efcc-over-n2-74m-fraud-unauthorised-loan/

5. 10 killed, 150 houses razed in Anambra clash

The National Emergency Management Agency has reported that at least 10 people were killed or declared missing, while about 150 houses were destroyed during a communal clash between Umuoba Anam and Aguleri communities in Anambra State. The agency also said about 2,000 people were displaced. NEMA is assessing the damage to determine the assistance required by affected residents.

Read the full story here: https://punchng.com/10-killed-150-houses-razed-in-anambra-clash/
