Protesters Block INEC Chairman Amupitan's Vehicle In London, Demand Resignation - Check Below For Full Details

By Mindnewsng Punch 13 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

INEC Chairman Prof. Joash Amupitan was heckled by Nigerian protesters outside Chatham House in London over allegations of irregularities in the preliminary vote

The Chairman of the Independent National Electoral Commission, Prof. Joash Amupitan, was on Thursday heckled by Nigerian protesters in London who surrounded the vehicle conveying him outside Chatham House, where he was scheduled to speak.

The protesters hurled insults and demanded his resignation over allegations of irregularities in the preliminary voters' register ahead of the 2027 general elections. Video clips of the incident circulated widely on Nigerian social media, reigniting debate over the credibility of the commission's preparations.

The allegations, and INEC's response

The protest was organised by a coalition of Nigerian groups in the diaspora who claim the preliminary register contains anomalies, including duplicate and suspicious registrations. The commission has not publicly confirmed the specific figures cited by the protesters, and no independent verification has established the claims as presented.

Amupitan, who was appointed INEC chairman in 2025, has repeatedly pledged to deliver credible polls. In recent days he has also faced separate online claims about his personal social media activity, which he has denied, and he told reporters the commission would abide by court rulings on candidate disputes.

Why it matters

Nigeria's 2027 elections are already shaping up as fiercely contested, and public confidence in the voter register is central to the legitimacy of any outcome. The commission has announced digital verification initiatives and says it is cleaning the register ahead of the polls.

Observers note that protest pressure abroad has become a recurring feature of Nigerian electoral politics, with diaspora groups using international platforms to amplify domestic grievances.

Editor's note: The allegations mentioned in this report remain subject to official investigation. Mindnewsng reports them as claims made by those involved and does not present them as established fact.
