We are making event planning easier for Nigerians, says Akoh

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 4h ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Event-planning platform, Celedom, has recorded more than 1,500 sign-ups since the introduction of its second version in February, its Project Lead, Chris Akoh, has said.

Akoh said the platform was developed to address difficulties faced by people searching for vendors and service providers for weddings, birthdays and other social events.

He noted that many event vendors use social media to advertise their services, making it difficult for prospective clients to verify their credibility, compare options and establish contact.

Speaking on the platform’s operations, Akoh said it provides separate applications for event organisers and service providers.

According to him, the vendor application allows service providers to create profiles and receive enquiries from prospective clients, while the celebrants’ application enables users to search for vendors, make enquiries and communicate with service providers.

“Celedom is a problem solver. It literally solves the service-provider-finding problem we have here,” he said.

Akoh added that the platform conducts verification checks on vendors, with businesses that meet its requirements receiving a verification badge.

The platform also has a feature that allows users to organise their event requirements and identify service providers based on their needs.

Its Marketing Lead, Alaezi Akpuru, said the platform caters to different categories of events, including small gatherings and large celebrations.

Akpuru said, “Whether it’s small get-togethers or big events like weddings, Celedom covers it all.”

The platform operates through separate applications for celebrants and vendors, with further information available on its website.
