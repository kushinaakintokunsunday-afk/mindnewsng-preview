Adamawa APC denies crisis in the party over Ribadu’s absence at campaign inauguration

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Adamawa state chapter of the Ruling All Progressive Congress (APC) has denied the allegations that the party has enveloped into crisis over the candidature

Speaking to Arewa Punch exclusively on Monday the state publicity secretary of the party Mr. Victor Dogo, said that the allegations were baseless and  a deliberate disinformation to create confusion in the party.

“You don’t expect  Nuhu Ribadu National Security Adviser NSA to the president to come to Yola when the president was going on campaign the following day”, he said.

“ The security of the president is more important to Nigerians than NSA attending campaign flag-off in his state, when the governor and other chieftains are there doing the good jobs for APC to win the general elections without losing one position, “he stated.

Ribadu was conspicuously missing onThursday, during the party’s campaign flag-off in Yola.

However, the master of ceremony claimed that Ribadu was represented, but his representative was not giving chance to present Ribadu’s message to the APC supporters in Adamawa state.

Punch investigations revealed that Ribadu was not happy with the composition of the campaign team, which according to sources close to the the NSA, Governor Ahmadu Fintiri’s close political associates dominated the campaign team.

Speaking at the flag-off of the APC campaign Fintiri, commended President Bola Tinubu’s economic reforms, pointing out that the second coming of the president will reshape the country for better economic reforms.

Fintiri claimed that the president’s reforms had created opportunities for state governments to access more resources and execute developmental projects and tackle security challenges, particularly in the North-east region of the country.

The governor who confirmed that the reforms has  brought economic hardship to Nigerians, noted that the reforms were necessary policy decisions whose benefits would become evident over time.

According to the governor Adamawa State had benefited from the opportunities created by President Tinubu’s reforms, pointing out that the president deserved re-election for more people-oriented projects in the country.

“President Bola Tinubu has taken the bullet. He has introduced a policy that is making this country to work. He has introduced reforms. Yes, we agree they are hardship, but then they will not last forever because they are very good policies that are working.”

“If you have agreed that we have really worked in Adamawa, Bola Ahmed Tinubu should have a share in the work that the state government has done in Adamawa State because he has taken the bullet for all of us”.

The governor disclosed that Tinubu-led Federal Government, security agencies and state governments were working together to restore peace and create an enabling environment for development to thrive.

The governor assured Adamawa state people that his administration would continue to prioritise their welfare, reduce insecurity and deploy available resources to cushion the effects of economic hardship.

In his welcome address, the APC state chairman Alhaji Hamza Madagali, threatened to sack any party executive that failed to deliver his ward to his party.

“If you fails to deliver your place to APC, just consider yourself out of the party”, he warned.

The former governor Jibrrila Bindow, urged the people of Adamawa state to vote massively for Tinubu for economic prosperity according to him.

“ Late president Muhammadu Buhari and other leaders did 8 years so, Tinubu too should not be denied the same opportunity to serve 8 years two terms, “he said.
