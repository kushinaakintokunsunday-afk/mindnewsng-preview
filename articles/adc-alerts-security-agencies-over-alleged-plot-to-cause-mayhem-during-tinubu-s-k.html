ADC Alerts Security Agencies Over Alleged Plot To Cause Mayhem During Tinubu’s Kebbi Visit

By Channels TV Channels TV 17 Nov 2026 1 min read

Photo: Channels TV

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has alleged a plot to deploy suspected thugs to cause mayhem in the name of its members ahead of President Bola Tinubu’s visit to Kebbi State.

The party said it had uncovered a plan to distribute ADC-branded shirts to some unscrupulous elements, who would allegedly be used to create disturbances and portray the party in a negative light.

The allegation was contained in a press briefing addressed by the party’s Deputy Publicity Secretary, Atiku Abubakar.

According to Atiku, the alleged plot was aimed at creating the impression that ADC members were responsible for any disruption during the President’s visit.

He said the party was alerting members of the public and security agencies to the development, insisting that anyone caught engaging in such activities under the guise of being an ADC member should not be regarded as representing the party.

“We therefore call on the general public and security agencies to take note that our party has uncovered this alleged plot. Anyone caught engaging in such acts is not our member and should be dealt with in accordance with the law,” Atiku said.

He maintained that the ADC was a party of credible and peace-loving individuals and urged its members to remain peaceful before, during and after the President’s visit.
