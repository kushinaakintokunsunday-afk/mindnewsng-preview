Meet Kunle Areogun, Taiwo Odukoya’s preferred successor

By Punch Punch 6d ago 5 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Pastor Kunle Areogun, a senior pastor at The Fountain of Life Church and a maritime executive, has emerged at the centre of the church’s leadership crisis follo

The video, reportedly recorded in December 2021 and released by the church’s trustees on Tuesday, has renewed attention on the succession controversy surrounding the church, particularly the disputed removal of Odukoya’s son, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya, as senior pastor.

In the recording, the late founder stated his preference for Areogun to take over the spiritual leadership of the church.

“Please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun,” Odukoya said.

He, however, assigned Jimmy the positions of administrative assistant and head of the youth church in the proposed leadership arrangement.

The disclosure has added another dimension to the disagreement between factions within the church over who has the authority to determine its leadership and whether the succession arrangement outlined by its founder should supersede the decisions taken after his death.

The recording was reportedly made by Jimmy at his father’s request and witnessed by his elder brother, Pastor John Odukoya.

Explaining why he made the video, the late founder said the succession provision was among the aspects of the church’s constitution being reviewed at the time.

Things to know about Areogun

1. He is a longstanding pastor at The Fountain of Life Church

According to Church Gist, Areogun has been associated with The Fountain of Life Church for several years and was listed among its pastors in the memorial booklet published for the late Pastor Nomthi Odukoya in November 2021.

The church’s sermon archive also documents his preaching engagements dating back to at least July 2017.

Among his recorded sermons are God of the Unusual (2017), See the Invisible, Experience the Incredible (2018), Jesus, Our Image (2022), The Word of God and the Supernatural (2023) and The Unlimited God (2023).

His relationship with the late founder was also acknowledged by Odukoya’s daughter, Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, during a Truth Table interview transcribed by Church Times Nigeria in October 2025.

“Uncle Areogun was quite close to my dad. His wisdom was top-notch,” she said.

2. He is a chemical engineer

Beyond his pastoral responsibilities, Areogun has a professional background in engineering.

Corporate publications identify him as a chemical engineer, while Excel Exploration and Production’s official website lists him as one of its directors.

3. He is an oil and gas executive

Areogun has built a career in Nigeria’s maritime and oil and gas industries.

He has served as managing director of Bourbon Interoil Nigeria Limited, an oil and gas and maritime services company.

A July 2009 report by maritime publication Riviera identified him as the company’s deputy managing director.

By December 2012, a Worldfolio report identified him as managing director and quoted him discussing the company’s operations and expansion into aluminium vessel construction.

Bourbon Interoil also identified him as its managing director in a corporate statement marking the International Day for Women in Maritime in May 2024.

4. He worked with Taiwo Odukoya outside the church

Areogun’s association with the late founder was not limited to religious activities.

An Afren regulatory announcement dated July 18, 2007, listed both Areogun and Taiwo Odukoya among the board and management team of Excel Exploration and Production.

The connection suggests that their relationship extended into the business sector before the current succession controversy.

5. He celebrated his 60th birthday in August 2024

A congratulatory advertisement published by Petromarine in THISDAY on August 15, 2024, celebrated his 60th birthday.

The advertisement identified him as Engineer Adekunle A. Areogun, managing director of Bourbon Interoil Nigeria Limited.

It described him as a leader in the marine, oil and gas industry and praised his professional achievements.

6. Odukoya named him as his preferred successor

Areogun’s name became a subject of national attention after the emergence of a video reportedly recorded in December 2021.

In the footage, Taiwo Odukoya explained that he was making the recording to clarify his wishes should he die or become unable to communicate.

“Please, the next pastor of the church should be Kunle Areogun,” the late founder said.

He also assigned his son, Pastor Jimmy Odukoya, the roles of administrative assistant to the incoming pastor and head of the youth church.

Odukoya explained that the arrangement was connected to an aspect of the church’s constitution that was undergoing review but had not been concluded.

7. His emergence has intensified the Fountain of Life leadership crisis

Following Taiwo Odukoya’s death in August 2023, Jimmy was appointed senior pastor of the church in September of the same year, while his sister, Tolu Odukoya-Ijogun, became associate senior pastor.

However, the emergence of the succession video prompted a fresh dispute over the circumstances surrounding Jimmy’s appointment.

The Board of Trustees subsequently withdrew recognition of Jimmy as senior pastor, citing the late founder’s recorded instructions and other concerns, including controversy surrounding his recent remarriage.

Jimmy rejected the decision, describing it as unconstitutional and insisting that he remained the duly appointed senior pastor and chairman of the Board of Trustees.

The trustees, meanwhile, appointed Pastor Rotimi Okpaise as interim spiritual and administrative leader pending further consultations.

8. He has not formally taken over as senior pastor

Although the recording identifies Areogun as Taiwo Odukoya’s preferred successor, he has not been formally installed or announced as the substantive senior pastor of The Fountain of Life Church.

The trustees have instead appointed Okpaise as interim leader while the dispute over the church’s leadership and constitutional arrangements continues.

His possible emergence as the church’s next substantive leader will therefore depend on how the competing positions over the founder’s wishes, the church’s constitution and the authority of its governing board are resolved.
