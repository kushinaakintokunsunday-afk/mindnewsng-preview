EXPLAINER: How to apply for FG’s N100,000 family grant

By Punch Punch 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The Federal Government’s Nigeria Family Strengthening and Development Programme has outlined the eligibility requirements and application process for its N100,0

The programme, coordinated by the Hope for Indigenous Family Support Initiative’s Coordinating Office under the Federal Ministry of Youth Development, is designed to cushion the effects of economic hardship, rising food prices and economic reforms.

According to a statement by the programme’s management board sighted by PUNCH Online on Friday, the intervention was announced by the ministry on September 10, 2026, with selected beneficiaries expected to receive N100,000 in financial support.

The programme, known as the NFSP, is a cash transfer initiative targeting poor and vulnerable families across the country.

Who is eligible for the N100,000 grant?

The management board listed the eligibility requirements for prospective beneficiaries, saying applicants “must be a Nigerian citizen by birth”, have a valid National Identification Number and be between 18 and 65 years old at the time of registration.

It added that the NIN would be validated through the National Identity Management Commission.

The programme targets poor and vulnerable households identified through the Social Safety Net Systems and verified national identity records.

However, meeting the stated requirements does not automatically guarantee selection, as beneficiaries will be assessed based on poverty and vulnerability criteria.

How to apply for the grant

The management board identified the programme’s registration portal as nfsp.ng/signup, where interested individuals can submit their details for consideration.

It said the selection process would involve identifying eligible households through the Social Safety Net Systems, verifying their national identity records and assessing their circumstances against the programme’s poverty and vulnerability criteria.

“The selection process involves identifying eligible households through the Social Safety Net Systems, using verified national identity records, assessing households based on poverty and vulnerability criteria, and selecting beneficiaries in phases across the six geopolitical zones,” the board said.

It added that the programme would cover a limited number of households in each phase, meaning beneficiaries would not receive payments at the same time.

“If you have not received a notification, it does not necessarily mean you have been permanently excluded from the programme,” the board said.

How will beneficiaries receive the N100,000?

The board said payments would be made into the bank accounts submitted by selected beneficiaries.

“Selected beneficiaries will receive an SMS notification from their designated payment provider,” it said.

According to the statement, the phone number used for the notification would be obtained from the records used during the beneficiary selection process.

Recipients are expected to follow the instructions in the message and provide the required consent before the money is credited to their submitted bank accounts.

Warning against fraud

The management board warned Nigerians against paying individuals or agents who claim they can secure access to the grant or facilitate their inclusion on the beneficiary list.

It said, “Do not pay anyone to add your name to the beneficiary list.”

The board also cautioned applicants against paying anyone to release the N100,000 grant, stressing that only selected beneficiaries would receive official SMS notifications from approved payment providers.

It further advised Nigerians to avoid unofficial registration websites and rely on recognised communication channels for information about the intervention.

For enquiries and clarification, the statement provided the email address help@nfsp.ng and advised Nigerians to contact the Federal Ministry of Youth Development through its recognised communication channels.
