Offa Bank Robbery: Saraki, Ahmed Can’t Be Arraigned Without Proper Service — Kwara Court

By Channels TV Channels TV 5d ago 7 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Justice Osuolale Ajayi of the Kwara State High Court in Ilorin said that the first and second defendants in the ongoing Offa robbery case, former Senate President, Dr. Abubakar Bukola Saraki, and former Governor of Kwara State, Alhaji Abdulfatah Ahmed, cannot be arraigned until they are properly served with the charges.

The presiding judge, Justice Ajayi, delivered the court’s ruling on the pending issues, having reserved judgment at the previous sitting after hearing arguments from both the prosecution and defence.

At the resumed hearing, the judge read the court’s decisions and directed that the charges be served on the defendants by November 20 while fixing December 3, 2026, for the arraignment to proceed.

The prosecution had argued for the immediate commencement of the trial with the arraignment of the four defendants.

The court ruling follows the defence team’s earlier objection that the defendants had not been personally served with the charges, arguing that service on their lawyers alone did not satisfy the requirements of due process and a fair hearing.

At the previous sitting, counsel to Saraki, Adebayo Adelodun (SAN) drew the court’s attention to the improper service and questioned the urgency of the proceedings, asking, “My Lord, why the hurry?”

He had also reminded the court of the pending appeal before the Court of Appeal concerning issues of jurisdiction and the competence of the trial court to entertain the matter.

The defence had urged the court to take cognisance of the pending appellate proceedings and the legal objections already raised before taking further steps in the case.

The judge, however, said he would not suspend hearing because there was no indication as to when the Court of Appeal would give its ruling on the application of the defence for an order on all parties to maintain status quo ante bellum.

The latest directive now gives the parties a timeline for completion of service of the charges, with December 3 fixed for the next stage of proceedings.

Also, the Court of Appeal sitting in Ilorin had reserved its ruling on the application of the applicants (Saraki and Ahmed) on whether an order to maintain status quo ante bellum should be given to the parties and the trial court.

The development is connected to the April 5, 2018, robbery in Offa, when armed men launched coordinated attacks on several commercial banks and the police divisional headquarters, killing more than 30 people, including police officers and civilians.

The attackers targeted branches of Guaranty Trust Bank, First Bank, Ecobank, Zenith Bank and Union Bank, as well as Ibolo Microfinance Bank, during the operation.

They reportedly stormed the banks with sophisticated weapons and explosives, engaging police personnel in gun battles and overpowering security operatives at the divisional headquarters.

The attack resulted in the deaths of over 30 people, including 12 police officers and a pregnant woman, according to widely reported accounts of the incident.

The robbers also reportedly carted away millions of naira and 21 AK-47 rifles from the police armoury.

The scale of the attack generated nationwide outrage and prompted the then Inspector-General of Police, Ibrahim Idris, to deploy the Police Intelligence Response Team, led by then Deputy Commissioner of Police, Abba Kyari, to investigate the incident.

The police subsequently arrested several suspects, including Ayoade Akinnibosun, Ibukunle Ogunleye, Adeola Abraham, Salahudeen Azeez and Niyi Ogundiran, who were later prosecuted for their involvement in the robbery.

The state government recently reduced the original 20 charges against Saraki, Ahmed and two former aides, Yusuf Abdulwahab and Alabi Olalekan, to four counts.

Reacting to the development, Saraki accused the Kwara State Government of abusing the judicial process and using the robbery case to persecute him and former Governor Ahmed.

Saraki, through his media office, described the development as an indication that the allegations linking him to the deadly robbery lacked credible evidence.

He accused Governor AbdulRahman AbdulRazaq’s administration of deploying the criminal proceedings to damage his reputation ahead of the 2027 general elections.

In a statement issued on Monday by the Head of the Abubakar Bukola Saraki Media Office, Yusuph Olaniyonu, in Abuja, the former Senate President said the government had abandoned all the allegations relating to armed robbery, the killing of police officers and illegal possession of firearms.

The statement read, “The amendment to the charge sheet only reveals that there was no case against these gentlemen from the beginning, and what the state government is indulging in is abuse of power, a travesty of the justice system, and mockery of the prosecutorial powers of the state.

“The government has retained allegations that the four defendants conspired to belong to a gang of thieves and actually belonged to such a gang, alongside two counts relating to the alleged concealment or disappearance of a vehicle used for an unlawful purpose.”

The development came amid renewed political controversy over the robbery after a former chieftain of the All Progressives Congress in Kwara State, Saheed Popoola, admitted that he played a leading role in the campaign linking Saraki to the incident during the political struggle that culminated in the defeat of the former governor’s political structure in 2019.

Popoola, who is now the Peoples Democratic Party senatorial candidate for Kwara South, made the disclosure on Sunday at the palace of the Olofa of Offa, Oba Mufutau Gbadamosi, during Saraki’s political tour of the district.

He said the robbery allegations were deliberately revived and politicised by some APC members to undermine Saraki’s political influence in the state during the “O to ge” movement.

He said, “I was a leading member of the APC then that started the politicisation and revival of the issue of Offa robbery just to get to Saraki. But after we finished and won, it was I who was being persecuted.

“This includes the use of songs and slogans to associate Saraki with the incident just because of politics. However, God has intervened, and the reality is that Saraki is back in Kwara.”

Popoola, who expressed regret over his participation in the political struggle against Saraki, challenged those who continued to politicise the tragedy to demonstrate genuine concern for the victims and their families.

He alleged that many of the individuals who repeatedly raised the robbery allegations had neither visited the affected families nor offered them meaningful assistance since the incident.

Dismissing suggestions that Saraki could have been motivated by financial considerations to sponsor the robbery, Popoola said the former governor had resources far beyond the amount that could have been stolen from the banks.

“All the money in the entire banks in Offa, Ilorin, Patigi and the entire Kwara State put together is not enough for Saraki to spend in three days,” he declared.

However, the Kwara State chapter of the All Progressives Congress defended the evidence presented during the trial of the convicted robbers, insisting that it linked Saraki to members of the gang.

The state APC Publicity Secretary, Abdulwaheed Babatunde, stated this on Monday while addressing a press conference in Ilorin, following Popoola’s admission.

Babatunde maintained that Popoola’s confession could not erase evidence presented during the trial of the five convicted robbers, alleging that gang leader Ayoade Akinnibosun and other members implicated Saraki in their statements and testimonies.

He said, “Whether Saraki likes it or not, he is an accessory before and after the fact. The vehicles and arms were given at his instance, without which they couldn’t have attempted a robbery.”

The APC spokesman also alleged that a vehicle used in the operation was initially concealed at the Government House in Ilorin before being moved to the Ministry of Environment, adding that the suspects had links with Saraki and his family.
