Apple to face trial over alleged iPhone repair restrictions in France

By Punch Punch 17 Nov 2026 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Apple is to stand trial in Paris in June 2027 on suspicion of having organised the planned obsolescence of various iPhone models by preventing repairs outside o

The case brought by French prosecutors against the US tech giant on charges of “obstructing repair and refurbishment” is a first, campaign group Stop Planned Obsolescence said, welcoming the move after filing a complaint on December 7, 2022.

“We strongly contest these unfounded allegations,” Apple said when contacted by AFP.

Planned obsolescence refers to techniques used by manufacturers to deliberately shorten the lifespan of their products, generally electronic devices, thereby encouraging consumers to discard and replace them more frequently.

According to the source close to the case, Apple Distribution International and parent company Apple Inc are due to appear in court for a four-day trial starting on June 17.

They are accused of preventing repairs to a range of iPhone models for more than two and a half years, from November 2021 to July 2024.

Apple is accused of configuring its smartphone software to “make it impossible to repair or refurbish” devices outside its authorised network, the source added.

The alleged aim was to boost sales of Apple’s flagship product and increase the revenue of the multinational, which now generates more than $400 billion a year, about half of that from iPhone sales.
