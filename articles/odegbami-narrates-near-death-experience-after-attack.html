Odegbami narrates near-death experience after attack

By Punch Punch 3d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Former Super Eagles captain and renowned sports administrator, Segun Odegbami, has recounted his close brush with death after surviving an early-morning attack

In a message to a close associate, the 74-year-old football legend, popularly known as Mathematical, described the ordeal as an assassination attempt and the closest he had ever come to death, explaining how he fought off the assailants alongside his driver and domestic worker after they broke down his door at about 2:30am.

“Thank the Creator of the Universe for me. I survived an assassination attempt. This morning at 2:30am, three unknown persons came to the house to steal and kill. I fought back with the help of God. My driver and house boy joined me, and we fought them before they fled. It was the closest I have ever been face to face with death. They broke down my door to get to me. I have been to the hospital,” Odegbami said.

The proprietor of Eagles 7 Sports Radio 103.7 FM sustained deep machete wounds during the struggle inside his home in the Ibara Government Reservation Area, before he was rushed to an undisclosed hospital for treatment and subsequently discharged.

Neighbourhood accounts indicated that the intruders first overpowered Odegbami’s domestic staff and driver upon invading the compound, beating the workers severely after noticing a running generator despite claims that the former international was not at home.

“They immediately found their way to Odegbami’s bedroom and attacked him there. Not willing to succumb easily, Odegbami resisted his attackers. They eventually fled the scene, leaving him with deep machete cuts and bleeding heavily,” a resident said on condition of anonymity.

Confirming the incident and Odegbami’s discharge on Friday, Ogun State Police Command spokesperson DSP Oluseyi Babaseyi stated that Commissioner of Police Bode Ojajuni visited the football icon to sympathise with him and ordered an immediate manhunt to apprehend the culprits.

“The command is leaving no stone unturned in addressing the incident. The Commissioner of Police immediately ordered a thorough investigation into the circumstances surrounding it, with intensive efforts ongoing to identify and apprehend those responsible. We can also confirm that Chief Segun Odegbami has been discharged from the hospital,” Babaseyi said.

While the motive behind the attack had yet to be established as of the time of filing this report, the police appealed to residents to remain calm and provide credible information through established channels to aid the ongoing investigation.

Odegbami played a pivotal role in Nigeria’s squad that won the country’s first Africa Cup of Nations title on home soil in 1980, scoring three goals to finish as joint top scorer, and also helped Shooting Stars of Ibadan lift the African Cup Winners’ Cup in 1976.
