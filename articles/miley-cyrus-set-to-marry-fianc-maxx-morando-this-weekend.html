American pop star Miley Cyrus is reportedly set to marry her fiancé, musician Maxx Morando, in Southern California this weekend.

According to a report by TMZ on Thursday, November 19, sources close to the couple confirmed plans for the wedding, although details surrounding the ceremony remain undisclosed.

Neither Cyrus nor Morando has publicly confirmed the reported wedding plans.

The marriage would be the second for the 33-year-old singer, who was previously married to Australian actor Liam Hemsworth.

Cyrus and Morando got engaged in 2025 after dating for about four years. The singer later revealed that Morando proposed during a trip to Japan, describing the experience as a surprise.

The couple recently attracted attention when Cyrus invited Morando onstage during her Hollywood Bowl performance in October to perform their song, Let’s Get Married.

Their relationship reportedly began in 2021 after they met on a blind date. They made their first public appearance together at the Gucci Love Parade fashion show in November of that year.

Cyrus married Hemsworth in December 2018 after dating for several years. However, the marriage ended in separation in August 2019, with their divorce finalised in January 2020.
