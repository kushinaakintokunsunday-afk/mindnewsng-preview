IGP urged to play role in Lagos property demolition

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Nigeria Policy Group has condemned the reported demolition and alleged unlawful seizure of a private property located at No. 12 Tumbuli Road, Ikoyi, Lagos S

The Nigeria Policy Group has condemned the reported demolition and alleged unlawful seizure of a private property located at No. 12 Tumbuli Road, Ikoyi, Lagos State and called on the Inspector-General of Police to investigate the circumstances surrounding the incident, particularly the alleged involvement of some police officers.

The group expressed concern over the allegations that officials and individuals linked to Lagos State property development agency, alongside members of the Nigeria Police, were involved in the action.

This was contained in a statement by Prince Ike Onuoha yesterday.
According to the group, the allegations raised serious questions about respect for private property rights, the rule of law and the proper conduct of public institutions.

It expressed concern about the reported presence and involvement of police personnel, calling on the Inspector-General of Police to urgently establish the identities of the officers involved, the authority under which they acted, the purpose of their visit and whether their conduct complied with the established police procedures and the law.

“The Nigeria Police Force exists to protect lives, property and the rights of citizens. It must never be perceived as an instrument for intimidation, unlawful dispossession or the enforcement of private interests. Any officer found to have abused the authority of the office must be held accountable in accordance with the law,” the group stated.

It also called on the Lagos State Government and the relevant property development agency to publicly clarify the legal basis for the reported demolition and any subsequent activities on the property.

“If due process was followed, the relevant authorities should provide clear explanations and supporting documentation. If it was not, appropriate steps must be taken to address the alleged violations and ensure accountability,” it stated.

The group, among other things called for an immediate and impartial investigation by the Inspector-General of Police into the alleged involvement of police officers; the identification of all officers and other individuals involved in the incident, including the authority under which they acted; a clear explanation from the Lagos State Government and the relevant property development agency regarding the legal basis for the demolition and subsequent activities at the property; appropriate action against any individual or officer found to have acted unlawfully or abused public office and measures to prevent further interference with the property while the matter is being properly investigated.

The group also urged President Bola Tinubu to ensure that the matter receives the attention it deserves, insisting that the protection of citizens’ property rights and the enforcement of the rule of law must not depend on an individual’s political influence, financial standing or social connections.

“Nigeria must remain a country where public institutions serve the people, where the law applies equally to everyone and where no citizen is left without protection against alleged abuses of power. The law must protect every citizen and nobody should be above it,” the group submitted.
