National security: GOC 5 Division, Gara, charges newly promoted colonels on professionalism, value addition

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 2d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From Scholastica Hir, Makurdi The General Officer Commanding (GOC), 5 Division, Nigerian Army, Major General Moses Gara, has charged the newly promoted colonels

From Scholastica Hir, Makurdi

The General Officer Commanding (GOC), 5 Division, Nigerian Army, Major General Moses Gara, has charged the newly promoted colonels to ensure greater professionalism, commitment and value addition in the discharge of their responsibilities.

Gara, who is also the Force Commander, Operation Whirl Stroke (OPWS), gave the charge during the pipping and decoration of five officers who were promoted from the rank of Lieutenant Colonel to Colonel.

He described the promotion as recognition of the officers’ hard work, dedication and exemplary service, saying it would serve as a morale booster for the division’s operational activities.

The GOC said the promotion came with greater responsibilities and higher expectations, urging them not to disappoint the Nigerian Army and the nation.

Gara said: “As you have already observed, we have just finished the decoration of our newly promoted officers, as approved by the Army Council under the guidance of the Chief of Army Staff, Lieutenant General Waidi Shaibu.

“This promotion is a testament of the hard work of the officers who were considered for the promotion and for the division, it’s going to be a very big morale booster for us. If you have seen the faces of the officers that were decorated, you can see that they were rejoicing, they were highly motivated.

“But I must also add that it comes with a lot of responsibility, which we have reminded them not to fail the army authorities on the confidence that has been imposed on them to discharge the responsibilities of their new ranks.

“But by and large, it also motivates those who are aspiring to be promoted. We also have Colonels who are aspiring to be practitioners in the DIV and by the special grace of God, I am confident that they will pass the benchmark that has been set and it will be a very big plus for the division in the discharge of our operational activities.

“I have told them that once they add value to the army in all that they are called to do that the army will always add value to them and so that must be their watchword to ensure that in all that they do they bring value on board,” he said.

Also speaking, the 5 Division Mess President, Brigadier General Murtala Abdulsalam, urged the newly promoted officers to see their promotion as a reflection of their hard work and the confidence the Nigerian Army had in them.

Abdullahi urged them to regard the promotion not only as an achievement but as a call to greater service and sacrifice to the nation, and charged them to be worthy ambassadors of the Nigerian Army, particularly in discipline, courage and selfless service.

He appreciated the wives of the officers for their unwavering support in sustaining the family while their husbands carried out military assignments.

The Logistics Component Commander, Operation Whirl Stroke, Brigadier-General Charles Akpuogwu, congratulated the newly promoted officers, urging them to deploy their knowledge, expertise and emotional intelligence and to demonstrate greater commitment.

Akpuogwu said their promotion went beyond personal achievement but was an invitation for them to move beyond tactical considerations and prepare for higher operational and command assignments.

He urged them to be more professional and firm in their leadership, guiding their subordinates and supporting them.

Speaking on behalf of the newly promoted officers, Colonel John Ajiya appreciated God, the Chief of Army Staff, the GOC 5 Division, colleagues and families for their contributions to their training and service, assuring that they were committed to carrying out their duties with a full sense of responsibility and professionalism, and to adding value to the Nigerian Army and the security of the nation.
