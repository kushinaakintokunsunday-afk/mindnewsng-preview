Experts urge investors to fund pipeline infrastructure

From Adanna Nnamani and Francis Onyedikachukwu, Abuja

The Pipelines Professionals Association of Nigeria (PLAN) has urged local and international investors to increase funding for the development and sustainability of Nigeria’s pipeline infrastructure, warning that the country’s energy security and industrial growth depend largely on reliable pipeline networks.

The President of PLAN, Engr. Ngozi Adeleke, made the call at the Nigeria International Pipeline Technology and Security Conference (NIPITECS 2026), which opened in Abuja on Tuesday.

Adeleke said significant investment, innovative financing solutions, advanced technology, strong regulatory support and effective security frameworks were required to develop and sustain critical pipeline infrastructure.

She said pipelines remained the backbone of the energy value chain, connecting producing fields to processing facilities, power plants, industrial hubs, export terminals and consumers.

According to her, without reliable pipeline networks, Nigeria’s ambitions to achieve energy security, economic diversification and industrial development would be difficult to realise.

She noted that the conference, with the theme, “Financing the Future: Unlocking Capital for Resilient Pipeline Infrastructure in Nigeria,” was particularly relevant as the country seeks to increase hydrocarbon production, deepen domestic gas utilisation, expand energy access and strengthen industrial capacity.

The PLAN president said the two-day conference would provide an opportunity for industry stakeholders to examine ways of attracting investments into new and existing pipeline infrastructure, while exploring how technology could improve asset integrity and security.

She also called for stronger collaboration among operators, financiers, regulators, service providers, technology companies, academia and host communities to address challenges confronting the sector.

Adeleke stressed that NIPITECS should go beyond discussions to facilitate partnerships, stimulate innovation, encourage knowledge sharing and generate practical recommendations for strengthening Nigeria’s pipeline ecosystem.

“As an association, PLAN remains committed to promoting professionalism, operational excellence, safety, integrity and capacity development across the pipeline industry.

“We will continue to serve as a platform for engagement, learning and collaboration, while advocating policies and practices that support the growth and sustainability of the sector,” she said.

She further urged participants to use the conference to build meaningful relationships and develop partnerships capable of driving progress in the industry beyond the event.

According to her, Nigeria’s energy future would not be determined by its natural resources alone but by the collective ability of stakeholders to finance, build, protect and sustain the infrastructure needed to convert those resources into economic value.

She added that collaboration, shared innovation and collective responsibility were essential to unlocking investment and securing the country’s energy future.

The conference brings together industry leaders, government representatives, regulators, investors, technology companies and other stakeholders to discuss financing, technology and security challenges in Nigeria’s pipeline sector.
