Ogun 2027: PDP presents governorship flag to Adebutu

By Punch Punch 5d ago 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The National Secretary of the Peoples Democratic Party, Senator Samuel Anyanwu, has formally presented the party’s flag to Hon. Oladipupo Adebutu as its governo

Anyanwu presented the party’s flag to Adebutu on Wednesday at the Ake Palace during the PDP governorship rally, describing the former House of Representatives member as the right candidate to lead Ogun State and insisting that the forthcoming election would provide an opportunity for the people to determine their political future.

The event had in attendance the party’s deputy governorship candidate, Alhaja Yemi Sowunmi-Kolapo; the campaign council chairman, John Obafemi; the party chairman, Dr Abayomi Tella; the Ogun Central senatorial candidate, Prof Iyabo Obasanjo; her counterparts for Ogun East and Ogun West, Femi Omotayo Omoowo and Babatunde Gbadamosi, among others.

Speaking before party leaders, supporters and other stakeholders, the PDP National Secretary said Adebutu’s return to the governorship race had strengthened the party’s chances, adding that his political experience and familiarity with the candidate placed him in a position to assess his capacity for leadership.

He described Adebutu as the incoming governor of the state and acknowledged his running mate, Yemi Sowunmi-Kolapo, while commending the Ogun PDP leadership, led by Dr Abayomi Tella, and members of the State Working Committee for their commitment to the party.

The presentation of the flag, which symbolises the party’s formal endorsement and mobilisation behind its candidate, was accompanied by a strong declaration of electoral confidence from the national leadership.

Anyanwu disclosed that “I said that the day that Adebutu return back to the ballot paper was the day Yayi went back home. I know him very well, because I was in the Senate with him. There’s no way you can compare Adebutu to Yayi. This is Ogun State, and the state must take its rightful position; it must be Ogun for Ogun.

“He was actually denied the opportunity in 2023, but this time around, nobody is going to rig it again. He’s going to win and win big because this one is only for Ogun State. A foreigner cannot come here and be a governor. It’s only an Ogun man who will be governor of Ogun State

“Adebutu is not coming to take money from government. He’s not coming to run a corrupt government. The government in Ogun now is very corrupt.

This is the man who will come and deliver. He has come to take care of the youths of Ogun State. Ogun people are very intelligent people. Ogun people, youths, they want to go to school. They need somebody who will help them, and Honourable Lado Adebutu has come. Today, that is why the slogan of the campaign is “Itura day”, which means that the much-needed relief is here in Ogun State.

“All the leaders, former governors, former deputy governors, are supporting Ladi Adebutu. As I was coming here, I got a call from former governors who told me that if I come here, I should tell the people that they believe in Rainbow Coalition, and for that Rainbow Coalition, they want Adebutu to be the governor of this state.

“Adebutu is not going to be just a governor. He must be a governor with 26 House of Assembly members. So as you’re voting on the 6th of February, as you’re voting for the governor, you’re voting for the 26 House of Assembly PDP candidates.

“And therefore, by the power conferred on me, on behalf of our national leader, PDP Nigeria, our own very right, His Excellency… the leader of PDP, and the national leader of Rainbow Coalition, on behalf of our national chairman, Honourable Mohammed, on behalf of the National Working Committee of PDP, we are handing over the flag of PDP today. What we are giving you is power, and so shall it be after the February 2027 governorship elections”.

Speaking on the party’s senatorial and legislative ambitions, Anyanwu predicted victory for PDP candidates across the three senatorial districts, naming Engineer Femi Omotayo Omoowo for Ogun East, Iyabo Obasanjo for Ogun Central and Hon. Babatunde Gbadamosi for Ogun West.

Anyanwu also expressed confidence in the party’s nine House of Representatives candidates, describing them as among the most competent and intelligent contestants in the state.

He challenged party members to intensify grassroots mobilisation across Ogun’s electoral constituencies, pointing to the state’s polling units and wards as critical to the PDP’s electoral calculations.

“We have 5,024 polling units in Ogun State. We have 236 wards in Ogun State. All these units and wards, PDP must win,” he said.

In his response, Adebutu expressed appreciation to the PDP national leadership, the Ogun State chapter and party supporters for their loyalty and commitment, assuring them that the party had continued to strengthen its structures across the state.

He specifically acknowledged the contributions of the national leadership and the state party structure under Hon. Abayomi Tella, while commending party members working across the state’s polling units.

Adebutu said the party had moved beyond merely building political structures at the state level, explaining that its mobilisation efforts had extended to grassroots engagement across thousands of polling units.

On the economic direction of his proposed administration, Adebutu identified wealth creation, entrepreneurship and enterprise as essential ingredients for sustainable development.

He argued that Ogun State, with its strong entrepreneurial culture, needed leadership capable of translating its economic potential into improved living conditions for residents.

“We believe in the power of industry, the power of enterprise in Ogun State. We are very enterprising people. We are great entrepreneurs, and the only way forward is to create wealth.

“With wealth creation, we shall have a better state. With wealth creation, we shall have less tendency for desperate behaviour. Security will improve, and there will be a better life for all,” he stated.

He described the party’s political mission as fundamentally centred on humanity, inclusion and the development of Ogun State.

Adebutu also used the occasion to reaffirm his commitment to gender inclusion, insisting that his running mate, Alhaja Yemi Sowunmi-Kolapo, was not a spare tyre but a substantive partner in the party’s governorship project.

He added that women would continue to occupy prominent positions in the party’s political and governance agenda, noting that the inclusion of women in leadership was essential to building a more representative political system.

He called on residents to defend their interests and take a more active role in determining the political future of the state, insisting that citizens must demand accountability from those seeking public office.

Adebutu promised to run transparent governance and responsible management of public resources, describing accountability as the first dividend of democracy.

“You people must get annoyed righteously, fight for your right, and take your right, and protect Ogun State. Your first dividend of democracy is that if I become your governor, I shall conduct the affairs of this state transparently.

“I shall be able to stand before you and say that I have not committed fraud or mismanaged the resources that are meant for the development of our dear state,” he declared.
