JUST-IN: FIFA President Gianni Infantino arrives in Warri for Stadium commissioning

The President of the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA), Gianni Infantino, has arrived in Warri, Delta State, ahead of the commissioning of two football facilities in the area.

Infantino arrived at Osubi Airport on Friday morning, where he was received by Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori.

His visit is expected to include the commissioning of the Warri City Stadium and the football pitch at Hussey College.

The visit is being seen as a boost to grassroots football development in the state, according to former Nigeria Football Federation (NFF) president and Deputy Chairperson of the FIFA Men’s Teams Competition Committee, Amaju Pinnick.

Speaking on Arise Television, Pinnick said Infantino’s decision to visit Warri demonstrated his interest in grassroots football and the development of the game beyond major cities.

“Who will ever believe the president of FIFA will go to Warri, a subnational? Gianni loves grassroots football development,” he said.

Pinnick recalled that during an earlier visit to Nigeria, Infantino observed people playing football while they were being driven from the airport.

He said he subsequently introduced the FIFA president to the history of Hussey College, which he noted had produced 17 players for the Super Eagles.

“The first time I picked him from the airport, as we were driving, he counted the number of people playing football, and I told him about Hussey College. This idea was also crystallised. He loves football, and he has seen everything,” Pinnick said.

He added that the institution’s contribution to Nigerian football was among the reasons the visit to Warri was significant.

“Hussey College produced 17 Super Eagles. Can you hear that? So, Gianni believes in these legends. I’m happy to say that Gianni will be going to the Warri City Stadium,” he added.
