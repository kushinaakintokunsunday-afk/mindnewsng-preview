Osun renames stadium after Adeleke

By Punch Punch 6d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Osun State Government has renamed what was previously known as Osogbo City Stadium as Ademola Adeleke Imole Stadium, Osogbo. The move came about one week to the

The move came about one week to the second term inauguration of the governor, billed to hold at the renamed stadium on November 27.

The facility, currently under rehabilitation, had been the home ground for the state male and female football teams, until its condition deteriorated, forcing the teams to consider other alternatives for their league games.

Our correspondent, who visited the stadium on Wednesday to monitor preparation for the second term inauguration, reports that a large signpost announcing the new name of the facility had already been placed at a conspicuous point at the entrance to the stadium.

According to the programme line-up, the weeklong activities for the inauguration will commence with a Jummat at Osogbo Central Mosque, Oja Oba, Osogbo.

Inter-faith prayer session, the final of Ademola Adeleke Victory Soccer Tournament, inauguration lecture by Senator Shehu Sani on Federalism Dilemma: Navigating Institutional Friction, Local Government Allocations, and Executive Overreach, and thanksgiving service at All Souls’ Anglican Church Cathedral, Osogbo, are some other programmes scheduled to hold during the inauguration week.

A staff member of Osun Sports Council, whose office is located within the premises of the stadium, while speaking on condition of anonymity on the development since he was not officially permitted by government to speak on its behalf, said Adeleke is expected to pay an inspection visit to the facility in the coming days.

He added, “We all just observed that a big signpost announcing the new name was brought early this morning, Wednesday.

“It is true that the stadium has been named after the governor. We all witnessed it this morning, when those in charge brought the signpost and erected it at the entrance. However, there is no official announcement yet on the development.”

Efforts to reach the state Commissioner for Information and Public Enlightenment, Kolapo Alimi, and the spokesperson to the governor, Olawale Rasheed, for comment on the development failed.

The two officials could not be reached through their mobile lines as of 4:10pm on Wednesday when this report was filed.

They have also not responded to the messages that accompanied the pictures of the new name of the stadium sent to them.
