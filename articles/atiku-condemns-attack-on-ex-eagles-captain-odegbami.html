Atiku condemns attack on ex-Eagles captain Odegbami

By Punch Punch 2d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

African Democratic Congress presidential candidate, Atiku Abubakar, has condemned the attack on former Super Eagles captain, Segun Odegbami, at his residence in

Atiku described the incident as disturbing, saying no Nigerian, irrespective of age, should feel unsafe in the comfort of their home.

He made the remarks in a post on X on Saturday, where he also wished the former footballer a speedy and complete recovery and called on security authorities to apprehend those responsible for the attack.

“I was saddened to learn of the attack on Chief Segun Odegbami at his home in Abeokuta. Mathematical, as a generation of fans called him, gave Nigeria some of its proudest football memories.

“I wish him a swift and complete recovery, and I urge the authorities to find those responsible. No Nigerian, young or old, should feel unsafe in their own bedroom,” he wrote.

Odegbami, popularly known as Mathematical, reportedly escaped death after he was attacked with a machete at his residence in Abeokuta.

The former Green Eagles captain was said to have sustained injuries during the incident, which raised concerns about his safety.

Odegbami is one of Nigeria’s most celebrated footballers and played a leading role in the country’s victory at the 1980 Africa Cup of Nations, Nigeria’s first continental title.

He was also a key member of the national team that represented the country at the 1980 Olympic Games in Moscow and later became a prominent figure in sports administration and football development.

The attack has drawn attention to security concerns surrounding residents, with Atiku urging the authorities to ensure that those behind the incident are brought to justice.
