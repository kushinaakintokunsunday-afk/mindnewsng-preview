Anti-Tinubu protest: We’re not involved in your predicament – ADC spokesman fires back at APC

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 6d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

National Publicity Secretary of African Democratic Congress (ADC), Bolaji Abdullahi has dissociated the party from alleged sponsorship of protests against President Bola Tinubu.

Abdullahi spoke in an X post on Wednesday in response to a Tuesday statement signed by spokesman of APC Presidential Campaign Council, Dele Alake.

Alake had alleged that opposition parties, specifically ADC and Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) were sponsoring young Nigerians to protest against the Tinubu administration to portray it as unpopular before Nigerians.

Alake cited last Friday’s incident involving the president’s convoy in Abuja as example of the machinations of the opposition against the ruling APC.

Abdullahi said, “We deny any involvement in the APC’s current predicament.

“We also cannot take responsibility for citizens who are expressing their genuine grievances against a failed regime that has pauperised them more than any other in recent history.”

The ADC spokesman accused the ruling party of clutching at straws.

“They have no evidence to support their claims. Every Nigerian knows that the only murderous party in Nigeria today is the APC. We warned them a long time ago that they would have their day of reckoning with the people. Now, judgment day has arrived, and they are looking for someone to blame.

“By the way, didn’t they deny that the President’s convoy was stoned at the mosque? Liars,” he said.

In Tuesday’s statement, APC Presidential Campaign Council accused the ADC and NDC of endangering innocent young Nigerians.

The APC said hours before the flag-off of its presidential campaign in Kaduna on October 24, 2026, it alerted Nigerians to a plot by opposition elements to create tension, sow disharmony, and introduce violence into the campaigns.

“We specifically informed Nigerians that the opposition, the African Democratic Congress and the Nigeria Democratic Congress, had perfected elaborate schemes to use deepfakes, artificial-intelligence-generated images, fake news, sponsored protests, and other forms of propaganda to manipulate public opinion and spread lies about our nation, President Bola Tinubu, the All Progressives Congress, and the processes leading to next year’s general elections,” its spokesman, Dele Alake, said.

He said the plots have started unfolding, with the major objective of deceiving Nigerians and the international community by driving a narrative of discontent against President Tinubu.

“To create the false narrative of citizens’ discontent against the President, the ADC and NDC now sponsor hoodlums, arm them with cudgels and banners, and commandeer them to chant ‘Bamaiyi’ at APC campaign rallies. The latest instance of this abhorrent and anti-democratic stunt was the desecration of an Abuja Mosque last Friday, where these thugs were mobilised to pelt the presidential motorcade with stones,” Alake said.

The APC commended security personnel attached to the presidential convoy for exercising restraint, and President Tinubu for directing the police command in Abuja to release those detained.

The party also raised concerns about violence targeting its members in the Southeast, where they are allegedly being threatened and labelled as saboteurs for not supporting Mr Peter Obi, the NDC presidential candidate.

It also complained about the destruction of its campaign billboards under cover of night by opposition-sponsored agents.

Alake said, “Our party and campaign council stand for peaceful, credible, free, and fair elections. We advocate that the democratic space should not be constricted for any party or candidate anywhere in the country.

“Again, we call on the ADC and NDC leaders to stop the dangerous conscription of ignorant young Nigerians for unwholesome political activities that put their lives at risk, while those leaders’ own children luxuriate in splendour abroad.”
