APC plotting to rig polls in Niger’s banditry-hit LGAs –SDP

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 5d ago 4 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

From John Adams, Minna

The Social Democratic Party (SDP) in Niger State has accused Governor Mohammed Umaru Bago and the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) of plotting to exploit the worsening insecurity in seven local government areas to manipulate the 2027 general elections.

The party alleged that the state government’s failure to contain banditry and other violent crimes in the affected communities was deliberate, claiming that the APC intended to take advantage of the possible disruption of voting to manufacture election results in its favour.

It identified Agwara, Mashegu, Shiroro, Rafi, Munya, Mariga and parts of Paikoro as areas allegedly being targeted for the planned electoral manipulation.

The SDP also accused the governor of diverting local government funds to finance his re-election campaign, alleging that billions of naira had been squandered on political mobilisation and patronage while development projects remained abandoned and insecurity continued to claim lives.

Spokesperson and Director of Contacts and Mobilisation for the SDP governorship candidate, Mohammed Ndarani Mohammed, Jonathan Vatsa, raised the allegations in a statement issued in Minna.

Vatsa, a former Commissioner for Information, Culture and Tourism in the state, claimed that the administration had deliberately failed to provide adequate support to security agencies despite the persistent killings and abductions in several communities.

“The government’s attitude towards addressing the security challenges in the state is deliberate. They are planning to write results from those areas because elections may not take place there.

“That is why the government has refused to address the situation despite the killings and abductions going on in the state.

“We have it on good authority that local government areas like Agwara, Mashegu, Shiroro, Rafi, Munya, Mariga and parts of Paikoro, where there are serious security challenges, are the places they want to use to rig elections,” he alleged.

Vatsa maintained that the opposition parties were aware of the alleged plot and would resist any attempt to manipulate the electoral process.

“Despite the enormous resources at its disposal, the government has refused to empower the security agencies in the state just because they want to use the situation to rig elections.

“Unfortunately for them, the opposition parties are very much aware of these plans, and we are prepared for them. We will resist any attempt to rig the forthcoming elections in the state,” he declared.

The SDP spokesperson expressed concern that the administration appeared more interested in securing political advantage than protecting communities devastated by banditry, accusing the government of attempting to turn the suffering of residents into an electoral opportunity.

“It is sad that the government wants to use the people’s nightmare to its electoral advantage.

“I, therefore, urge the people to be vigilant. If the government cannot help you to address the security challenges, you should not allow them to use your situation to their advantage,” he said.

Turning to the management of public resources, Vatsa alleged that the state government spent N10 billion on the flag-off of the governor’s re-election campaign last week, while ongoing projects across the state were starved of funds.

He further claimed that more than N10 billion had been expended on welfare packages and financial inducements to political groups visiting Government House, Minna, to declare support for the governor.

According to him, the administration’s spending priorities have left critical development projects abandoned, even as local government councils struggle to discharge their responsibilities.

Vatsa specifically accused Bago of withholding funds meant for local government councils and allegedly channelling the resources into political activities, leaving council chairmen without sufficient money to meet their operational obligations.

He argued that the alleged financial strangulation of the councils had contributed to the worsening security situation, as some local governments could no longer afford to fuel vehicles used by security personnel for routine patrols.

“These local governments cannot even afford to fuel security vehicles in the various councils for routine patrols,” he claimed.

“Any leader who has no respect for public funds, the implication is that the people suffer. That is exactly what is happening in the state under the APC administration.

“This is one administration that spends public funds with recklessness, yet the people expect development. It doesn’t work,” he added.

The former commissioner also criticised what he described as the governor’s extravagant political spending, citing the reported presentation of seven expensive vehicles and N700 million to Hausa musician Dauda Rarara as another example of alleged misuse of public resources.

He challenged residents to reject what he called the ruling party’s attempt to use public funds to secure electoral victory, urging them to adopt the approach of voters in the Osun State governorship election.

“The government has gathered the fuel subsidy money to buy the elections.

“You should collect their money and vote the government out. Any attempt to allow this current administration to return to office will spell doom for the state,” Vatsa warned.
