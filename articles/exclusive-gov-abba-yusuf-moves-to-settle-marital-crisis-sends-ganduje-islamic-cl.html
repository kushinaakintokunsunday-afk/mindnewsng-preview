EXCLUSIVE: Gov Abba Yusuf moves to settle marital crisis, sends Ganduje, Islamic clerics on peace mission to Kwankwaso

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 16 Nov 2026 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

Kano State Governor, Abba Kabir Yusuf, has reportedly assembled a delegation of prominent political leaders, Islamic clerics, elders and influential stakeholder

Kano State Governor, Abba Kabir Yusuf, has reportedly assembled a delegation of prominent political leaders, Islamic clerics, elders and influential stakeholders to intervene in the marital disagreement between him and his wife, Hajiya Zainab Musa Kwankwaso.

The delegation is expected to visit the Abuja residence of former Kano State Governor and former presidential candidate, Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, in an effort to resolve the crisis involving his daughter and Governor Yusuf.

A source at the Kano Government House, who spoke exclusively on condition of anonymity, disclosed that the delegation would be led by former Kano State Governor and former National Chairman of the All Progressives Congress, Dr Abdullahi Umar Ganduje.

Other prominent figures expected to participate in the peace mission include the spiritual leader of the Qadiriyya Islamic movement in Nigeria and West Africa, Khalifa Sheikh Qaribullah Sheikh Muhammad Nasiru Kabara; prominent Islamic scholar and member of the Kano State Shura Council, Sheikh Abdulwahhab Abdallah; and Malam Tijjani Yammota, alongside other respected elders and political leaders.

According to the source, the delegation plans to travel from Kano to Abuja to appeal to Kwankwaso to reconsider his position and allow his daughter to return to the Government House.

The delegation is also expected to encourage both families to resolve the disagreement through dialogue, mutual understanding and respect for the marriage, rather than allowing political differences to worsen the situation.

The source further claimed that Governor Yusuf remained committed to his marriage and wanted to reconcile with his wife, particularly for the sake of their six children.

The couple reportedly married on July 29, 2006, and have been together for more than 20 years.

“Governor Yusuf still loves his wife, who is the mother of his children, and he also continues to regard Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso as his father. This is a family matter that deserves understanding, reconciliation and the intervention of respected elders.

“Politics should not be allowed to come between a husband and wife who love each other, especially after more than 20 years of marriage and six children. The governor wants peace, and we believe that dialogue and the intervention of these respected personalities can help restore understanding between the two families,” the source said.

However, when contacted for confirmation, the Kano State Commissioner for Information, Sunusi Bature Dawakin Tofa, said he was unaware of any delegation constituted to intervene in the matter.

He described the issue as a personal family matter that did not require public explanation, warning government officials against commenting on it without approval.

“According to our rules of engagement, personal issues of the Governor can only be discussed with prior approval. I therefore urge all government officials to exercise restraint on this issue.

“No comment is the only official response to this issue,” the commissioner stated.
