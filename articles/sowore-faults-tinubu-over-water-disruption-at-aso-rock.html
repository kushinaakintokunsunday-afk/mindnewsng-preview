Sowore faults Tinubu over water disruption at Aso Rock

By Punch Punch 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The 2027 presidential candidate of the African Action Congress, Omoyele Sowore, has criticised President Bola Tinubu over the disruption of water supply at the

Sowore accused the President of failing to address a basic infrastructure need within the seat of government, saying the situation raised questions about his administration’s ability to manage the country’s affairs.

In a post on his official X handle on Sunday night, the human rights activist said those around Tinubu should have advised him to resign if he could not guarantee a steady water supply at the Presidential Villa.

He wrote, “You can’t even keep the water running at Aso Rock Villa, and the goons clapping for you must really hate Nigeria.

“They should have told you to your face that if you can’t manage the water supply at Aso Rock, you should resign. Shameless people.”

The criticism followed Tinubu’s disclosure that water supply was interrupted at the Presidential Villa while he was shaving last Wednesday.

The President made the revelation on Sunday during a special prayer session organised by members of his family at the Yoruba Tennis Club, Onikan, Lagos.

Recounting the experience, Tinubu said, “On the Wednesday of last week, I was shaving, and the water went off. And the Villa, you won’t know punishment there too.

“The water went off. So we will correct all this. No light, no water, no electricity, no development.

“We have just started. We can do it, and we will do it for Nigeria.”

The President, however, expressed confidence that his administration would address the challenges, insisting that Nigeria had the capacity to overcome its infrastructure deficits.

Tinubu’s remarks highlighted the infrastructure challenges facing the country, including unreliable electricity and water supply, which he acknowledged also affected the Presidential Villa.
