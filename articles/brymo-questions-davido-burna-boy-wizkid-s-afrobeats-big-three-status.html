Brymo questions Davido, Burna Boy, Wizkid’s Afrobeats Big Three status

Singer Brymo has challenged the position of Davido, Burna Boy and Wizkid as Afrobeats’ leading trio, arguing that their popularity does not make them owners of the genre.

He expressed his views during an interview in which he discussed Rema’s place in the Nigerian music scene and the influence of established artistes on the industry.

According to Brymo, the recognition given to the three musicians should not be interpreted as exclusive control over Afrobeats or its direction.

He said he had concerns about what he considered their attitude towards the mainstream, adding that the genre should not be reduced to the influence of a few successful performers.

“My real problem with those so-called Big Three before is that they’ve forgotten that it’s called the mainstream,” he said.

The singer also questioned how the trio earned the Big Three label, asking whether their ranking was based on their songwriting abilities, vocal delivery, stage performances or willingness to express independent opinions.

“If you want to nail yourself down somewhere and say, ‘I’m the king, I’m the…’ Then go and create something. First of all, Afrobeats is not yours. Yours is a derivative of the real thing. So what’s Big Three? Big Three as what? As songwriting? As vocal performances? As live performances? As speaking up for yourself?” he queried.

Brymo further criticised what he described as a lack of personal independence among some prominent musicians.

Without mentioning the individual by name, he referred to a pregnancy-related controversy in which an artiste’s father reportedly came forward to defend him.

He also questioned the maturity of another unnamed musician, making reference to his mother’s reported involvement in his overseas travels.

“You claim to be the number one artist in Nigeria, your mom carries you around the whole world, you’re saying you’re the biggest artist in Nigeria. You’re not a man! Put on a man!” he declared.

On Wizkid, Brymo specifically discussed the singer’s decision to reside outside Nigeria.

He suggested that the country’s prevailing circumstances could be a factor behind the decision, arguing that Wizkid might have spent more time at home if conditions were different.

“I know Wizkid for sure, and I know why he’s here out here. If things were really working out in Nigeria, he probably would be back home full-time, just like me,” he added.
