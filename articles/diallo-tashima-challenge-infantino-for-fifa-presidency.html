Diallo, Tashima challenge Infantino for FIFA presidency

FIFA President Gianni Infantino is set to face his first electoral challenge after the global football governing body announced two candidates seeking to contest his bid for a fourth term in office.

The candidates are Philippe Diallo, president of the French Football Federation, and Kohzo Tashima, former head of the Japan Football Association.

FIFA announced their candidacies in a statement on Thursday, ahead of its 77th Congress scheduled for March 18, 2027, when the election will take place.

Infantino, who has been FIFA president since 2016, was returned unopposed in the 2019 and 2023 elections.

Diallo, 63, announced his intention to contest the presidency on Monday and has the backing of UEFA member associations, while Tashima is expected to draw support from the East Asian voting bloc. He has also served on FIFA’s executive council.

Infantino, however, continues to enjoy strong support among member associations in Africa and the Middle East.

His leadership has faced criticism over an abandoned proposal to sell certain World Cup stakes to private investors, a move that reportedly cost him the support of UEFA and several influential football confederations.

FIFA said the electoral committee would examine the candidates’ submissions before deciding whether to approve their participation in the election.

The 2027 contest will therefore be the first time Infantino faces opposition in his bid to retain the FIFA presidency since securing re-election unopposed in 2019 and 2023.
