A/ Ibom 2027: Eno begins LG campaign, seeks 80,000 votes from Mkpat Enin

MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

The Governorship candidate of All Progressive Congress (APC) in Akwa Ibom state pastor Umo Eno has commenced grassroot campaigns in local government areas with a promise to reconstruct decayed infrasture if the people can support his re election with 80,000 votes in 2027 .

Speaking during APC rally in Mkpat Enin local government area of the state. , Eno said he had already constructed 100km of roads, build compassionate homes and provided Modern Primary School for the people in his first term and ready to do more if elected for second term.

Eno warned the people of Mkpat Enin not yield to the antics of other political parties in the state who have nothing to offer them except tongue lashing his administration even when they have nothing in the state.

He lauded president Bola Tinubu for providing all the money for projects in the area and solicited votes to reciprocate such gestures

” Vote for president Bola Tinubu whó have provided all the money for the projects we have executed here in my first term

” I have come to solicit votes from you because of what I have done in this local government during my first term in office

” We have constructed 100km of roads , build compassionate homes for the needy and constructed Model Primary School in this local government area . You have to vote for me so that I can continue to provide more infrastructure here.”

Eno disclosed that the decayed Technical Secondary School in the area would be reconstrucred while Ukam market would be reconstructed and upgraded to modern market ststus in the area .

” This can only be possible if you give your 80,000 votes in 2027 election

” My campaign Director General Ambassador Assam Assam has said that we need only 60,000 votes from you but I am saying that I need 80,000 votes

” If you give me this 80,000 votes , I would execute more projects in this local government area

” Look at what my administration has done in Akwa Ibom state University (AKSU) located in your community. All professional courses have been accredited courtesy of this administration

” Get your PVC from INEC and prepare to vote for APC in all the elections come 2027″

Earlier, the Director General of the campaign Ambassador Assam Assam had promised N30m for the people if they could vote for APC in 2027 election.

” If you vote APC next year , I promise you N30m .Just vote , we would do our own part of the bargain”

Highlight of the campaign was the presentation of APC flag to all candidates for House of Assembly, Federal House of Representatives , Senate in the 2027 general elections.
