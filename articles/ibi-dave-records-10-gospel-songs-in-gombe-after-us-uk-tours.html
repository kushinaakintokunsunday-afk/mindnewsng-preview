Ibi Dave records 10 gospel songs in Gombe after US, UK tours

Gospel reggae artiste and songwriter, Ibrahim-David Gajere, popularly known as Ibi Dave, has recorded 10 new songs at a live concert in Gombe State, following his music tours of the United States and the United Kingdom.

The concert, held in October at All Saints Federal Low-Cost, Gombe, attracted more than 6,000 attendees, with several gospel artistes and entertainment personalities joining him on stage.

The 10 songs recorded at the event include Push It Up, Zion, Better Days, Pray, Karatu, Jah Grace, Thirsty, Miracles, Brighter Than the Sun and Testify.

Speaking with Sunday Scoop,  on the significance of the concert, Ibi Dave said the live recording offered him an opportunity to reconnect with his home audience while introducing them to his new gospel compositions.

“This concert is very special to me because it is an opportunity to share the new songs God has given me with the people. Recording these songs live in Gombe makes the experience even more meaningful,” he said.

The event featured guest performances by Minister Kaestrings, Rev. Silas Yako, Dave Azi, Mumaa Lucee and James Alkali, alongside other emerging gospel artistes.

The singer said the participation of fellow musicians underscored the importance of collaboration and unity in promoting gospel music.

“I am grateful to the artistes who joined me on this journey. Their presence and contributions made the concert memorable. Beyond recording the songs, we wanted to create an atmosphere of worship, fellowship and encouragement,” he added.

Ibi Dave, who hails from Filiya in Shongom Local Government Area of Gombe State, began his musical journey as a child when he joined his church choir.

His international exposure includes a musical tour of the US in April 2017 and subsequent tours of the UK in 2024, 2025 and 2026.

Reflecting on his journey, he said, “Music started for me in childhood when I joined the church choir. It has grown from that humble beginning into a ministry that has taken me to different places. I am grateful for every opportunity to use my music to inspire people and spread the gospel.”

The artiste is the lead performer and owner of No Pranks Music Band International, based in Gombe State. He has also received several awards in recognition of his contributions to gospel music.

His honours include the Exousia Award for Best Reggae and Best Collaboration with Solomon Lange in 2016, the Arewa Gospel Mega Awards in 2019, the National Gospel Merit Award in 2020, the Arewa Gospel Royal Ambassador Award in 2021 and the Africa Gospel Reggae Artist of the Year Award in 2024.

Beyond music, Ibi Dave is a certified accountant. He is married to Prisca Ibrahim, and they have three children.
