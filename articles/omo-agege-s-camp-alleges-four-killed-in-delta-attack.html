Omo-Agege’s camp alleges four killed in Delta attack

By Punch Punch 23h ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The campaign organisation of former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege has alleged that four people were killed and four others critically injured when gunm

The organisation, in a statement issued on Monday by Omo-Agege’s Media Adviser, Sunny Areh, said the former lawmaker, who is the Nigeria Democratic Congress senatorial candidate for Delta Central, escaped unhurt during the incident at Uduere in Ughelli North Local Government Area.

It described the incident as an assassination attempt, although the circumstances and casualty figures had yet to be independently verified.

The organisation said, “We are pained to report that four people, including two of our aides — Moses Anaughe and Akima Efemena Umukoro — were shot dead.”

It also named four others who were reportedly shot and critically injured as Chief Evans Iwuvrie, Chuks Erhire, Tobore Agba, who was allegedly shot in the neck, and Ayodo Wanogho.

“They are currently receiving medical attention. We pray for their speedy recovery and we stand with their families at this dark hour,” the statement added.

According to the organisation, armed men opened fire at close range on Omo-Agege and his entourage during what it described as a peaceful campaign tour.

The organisation described the incident as a targeted attempt to assassinate the former deputy senate president.

The campaign organisation also accused the Delta State Police Command of failing to prevent the attack, despite the alleged presence of its officers at the scene.

It called on the Inspector-General of Police to personally take over the investigation, saying it had lost confidence in the state command.

“We in fact, call on the Inspector-General of Police to personally take over the investigation as we no longer have confidence in the Delta State Police Command whose officers were present with Six trucks but did nothing to prevent the attack and killings,” it stated.

The organisation also called on the Director-General of the Department of State Services and the Delta State Commissioner of Police to intervene.

It urged Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori, to publicly condemn the attack, ensure the arrest and investigation of those accused of participating in or directing it, and guarantee the safety of opposition candidates in the state.

“Delta State is not a war zone. Campaigning is not a crime. Seeking the votes of the people should not be a death sentence,” the statement added.

The organisation urged its supporters not to retaliate or take the law into their hands, saying its ward-to-ward campaign tour of Delta Central would continue.

The Delta State Police Command had yet to comment on the reported attack as of the time of filing this report.

The casualty figures and allegations made by the campaign organisation had not been independently verified.
