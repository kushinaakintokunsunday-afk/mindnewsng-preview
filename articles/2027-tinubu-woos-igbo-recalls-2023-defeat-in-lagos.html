2027: Tinubu woos Igbo, recalls 2023 defeat in Lagos

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu has appealed to Igbo stakeholders in Lagos State to support his re-election bid in the 2027 general election. Tinubu stated this on Monday

Tinubu stated this on Monday during the Lagos Central PBAT-KOH Campaign Rally, held at the Mobolaji Johnson Arena, Onikan Stadium, Lagos.

The President arrived in Lagos on Saturday after attending the All Progressives Congress presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State.

The Lagos rally was a move to promote Tinubu’s candidacy for a second term in office, and also canvass votes for the APC governorship candidate and current Lagos deputy governor, Obafemi Hamzat.

While addressing the Igbo stakeholders, Tinubu recalled losing the 2023 election in the state, which is considered a stronghold of the APC.

The PUNCH reported in February 2023 that the then presidential candidate of the Labour Party, Peter Obi, defeated Tinubu to win the presidential election in the state.

In the 20 Local Government Areas in the state, Obi won in some LGAs that many had considered major strongholds for the APC in previous elections.

Polling 582,454 votes in total, Obi cleared votes in Ajeromi-Ifelodun, Amuwo-Odofin, Eti-Osa, Ikeja, Kosofe, Oshodi-Isolo, Somolu, Ojo and Alimosho

While Tinubu garnered 572,606 votes from Agege, Apapa, Badagry, Epe, Ibeju-Lekki, Ifako-Ijaiye, Ikorodu, Mushin, Lagos Island, Lagos Mainland, and Surulere.

The report added that despite winning two more LGs in the state than the LP candidate, Tinubu still lost the election to Obi on the number of votes won by 9,848 votes.

In his remarks, according to a video shared by an APC member, Imran Muhammed, via his X handle on Monday, Tinubu jokingly told the Igbo stakeholders that he was disappointed over the 2023 voting result in Lagos.

“Some of you didn’t vote for me in 2023; I vex o. We don settle. Get your PVC,” he urged the crowd.

Tinubu also asked if they would vote for him, to which they replied with a resounding “Yes.”

Tinubu said, “Come January 16, get your PVC; you come vote. Agreement na agreement o, na Bola Tinubu be APC o. As you do for me, I do for you.

“You must follow who know road. I have to work hard to make sure the economy improves, to ensure that your children are in school, to eliminate crises in the universities, to build good roads that will maintain our cars.

“The future is in our hands. For every one of us, our children must be in school. Because we are poor doesn’t mean they shouldn’t attend the universities.

“We are doing that. The university is stable today because we are doing well.”

The President, who served as a two-term Lagos governor, noted that he was ready to go for a second term in Aso Villa.

“The critical line in our National Anthem is what I am standing here for. I begged for it; I am ready to serve you.

“I have served the first term. The Constitution said I must ask for a second term. I want a second term.

“As you are here today, you make me happy; you make me excited. I will go home happy and eat akpu,” Tinubu added.
