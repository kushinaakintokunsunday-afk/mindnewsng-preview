Water stopped while I was shaving in Aso Rock – Tinubu

By Punch Punch 1d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu on Sunday revealed that the Presidential Villa in Abuja suffers from the same electricity and water supply failures that plague ordinary N

He revealed that the water went off while he was shaving earlier in the week and vowed to fix the country’s infrastructure deficit.

Tinubu made the disclosure on Sunday at a special prayer session organised in his honour by members of his family in Lagos.

The meeting followed his attendance at the All Progressives Congress presidential campaign rally in Ibadan, Oyo State, on Saturday.

He explained, “On the Wednesday of last week, I was shaving, and the water went off. And the villa, you won’t know punishment there too.

“The water went off. So we will correct all this. No light, no water, no electricity, no development.

“We have just started. We can do it, and we will do it for Nigeria.”

The President also rallied supporters ahead of the 2027 general elections, charging them to maximise voter turnout in Lagos and urging warring factions within his political camp to reconcile.

“To me, Lagos is the big deal. How can you have seven million registered voters and produce 100,000, 200,000? That’s failure. Even if you win, I say a win is a win. No. You have to generate the maximum,” he said.

He directed supporters to canvass their neighbours for Permanent Voter Cards and to channel grievances into electoral participation rather than internal conflict.

According to him, “It’s not anger. Anger will not do it. You are angry with Sylva, or you’re angry with Mulika, call Mulika and let her go vote. We finish the anger that day; you go back. We must generate enough votes to overwhelm them.”

Tinubu also ordered one of his associates in attendance, described as his “confidant and bulldozer”, to return home and broker reconciliation within the party’s ranks before moving on to other states.

He ordered, “Go back home and reconcile with everybody. That is the order of the president. Go back home and reconcile with everybody. And when you finish that, you move to Jigawa, you move to other places and reconcile them. Beg them.”

Drawing on his own experience, the President said he had set aside deep personal grievances during the 2023 campaign in order to pursue a broader goal.

“I had my own anger in 2021–22. When he called me, I said, okay, you are co-chairman of the campaign. I had my own anger, full of hot feelings, but I let go. Today, that is free,” he said, adding that the spirit of forgiveness was inseparable from seeking it.

Tinubu noted that “If you cannot give it, why are you asking Allah to give you forgiveness? What you want from him, you must be able to give to humanity.”

The President credited divine support and family loyalty for his journey from governorship to the presidency, tracing a line of prayer and perseverance through each contest.

“First time, prayer. Second time, prayer. We did that. We won. After that, presidency, prayer, vision, mission, everything,” he said.

To the younger generation at the meeting, President Tinubu said the 2027 election was theirs to win or lose.

“The challenge is yours now. It’s in your hands to knock on doors, get all the PVC out, and do what is right.

“You are the leaders that we take over. We paved the way for you. But it’s left in your hands to do the great things that Nigeria expects,” he said.

Several governors attended the prayer session, including the Lagos, Kwara, Imo, Gombe and Kaduna state governors.
