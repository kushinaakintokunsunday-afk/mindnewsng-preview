Tinubu pardons 78 convoy attack suspects, police arrest 8 over Kwankwaso assault

From Desmond Mgboh, Kano and Molly Kilete, Abuja

President Bola Tinubu has ordered the release of 78 suspects arrested over Friday’s attack on his motorcade in Abuja, granting them presidential clemency, while police in Kano have arrested eight suspects over a separate attack on the campaign convoy of Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) vice-presidential candidate, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, and the party’s governorship candidate, Aminu Abdussalam Gwarzo.

The Abuja attack occurred on Friday, November 13, after Tinubu attended Jumu’ah prayers at a mosque in Garki District, while the Kano violence erupted on Sunday, November 15, during the NDC campaign team’s visit to Bichi Local Government Area.

The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Police Command announced the presidential directive yesterday, saying the 78 suspects were undergoing release procedures. The Kano Police Command, on its part, confirmed that 13 vehicles were vandalised and five persons injured in the attack on the opposition campaign entourage.

FCT Police Public Relations Officer, Josephine Adeh, who announced the presidential intervention in a statement, said the suspects were arrested after some individuals attacked Tinubu’s departing motorcade with stones and other dangerous objects, damaging several vehicles and injuring some security operatives.

She explained that a joint security team, led by the police, responded to the incident, while subsequent intelligence-led raids in the area resulted in further arrests, bringing the total number of suspects apprehended to 78.

“Following the extension of presidential clemency to the individuals involved in the attack, the Presidency directed that the suspects arrested in connection with the incident be warned and released.

“In compliance with the directive, the Command wishes to inform the public that the suspects are currently undergoing the necessary release processes,” Adeh stated.

The police did not disclose whether any of the suspects had been formally charged before the presidential intervention or whether their release would be subject to conditions beyond the warning issued by the Presidency.

FCT Commissioner of Police, Ahmed Mohammed Sanusi, condemned the attack and directed Area Commanders, Divisional Police Officers and tactical units to intensify security operations across the territory as preparations for the January 16, 2027 presidential election gather momentum.

He also urged political parties and their supporters to shun violence, intimidation and inflammatory statements capable of threatening public peace.

In the separate Kano incident, police arrested eight suspects following Sunday’s attack on the campaign convoy of Kwankwaso and Gwarzo in Bichi Local Government Area.

The incident occurred as the NDC campaign team was returning from a political tour of Tofa, Rimin Gado, Kabo, Gwarzo, Shanono and Bagwai local government areas.

Spokesperson for the NDC governorship candidate, Ibrahim Garba Shuaibu, alleged that suspected opposition political thugs had blocked sections of the road leading into Bichi to prevent the party from holding a campaign rally in the town.

Shuaibu said the campaign team had received intelligence reports about the planned disruption while approaching Bichi but subsequently encountered hostile youths who began throwing stones at the convoy.

According to him, shortly after the entourage passed the local government secretariat, another group of youths, allegedly armed with dangerous weapons, emerged and attacked vehicles conveying party leaders and supporters.

He said several supporters sustained injuries, while a number of vehicles were damaged during the disturbance.

Shuaibu disclosed that he personally counted about seven vehicles with smashed windscreens at the scene, adding that Kwankwaso and Gwarzo escaped unhurt.

Confirming the incident, the Kano State Police Command said it received a report at about 4:45 p.m. on Sunday, November 15, concerning a violent clash within Bichi township, which resulted in injuries and the destruction of campaign materials and vehicles.

In a statement issued by its spokesperson, Abdullahi Kiyawa, the command disclosed that 13 vehicles were vandalised, while five persons sustained injuries and were taken to hospital for treatment.

The police also recovered one gariyo, two cutlasses, two hard sticks, a bottle of solution and a substance locally referred to as “Suck and Die”.

Kiyawa said the eight suspects had been transferred to the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID), Bompai, for further investigation and would be charged in court upon completion of the investigation if sufficient evidence was established against them.

The Kano Commissioner of Police condemned the violence and destruction of campaign materials, warning that attacks on political gatherings and other acts capable of breaching public peace would not be tolerated.

Although the NDC alleged that opposition political thugs carried out the Bichi attack, the police have yet to establish the political affiliations of those arrested or identify the individuals who allegedly organised the violence.
