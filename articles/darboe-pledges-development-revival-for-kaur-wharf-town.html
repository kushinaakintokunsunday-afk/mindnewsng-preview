Darboe Pledges Development Revival for Kaur Wharf Town

Gambiaj.com – (KAUR, THE GAMBIA) – United Democratic Party (UDP) leader and Gambia First Coalition flagbearer ANM Ousainou Darboe has pledged to revive Kaur Wharf Town, popularly known as Dande Mayoo, if elected president in the December 5 presidential election.

Darboe made the pledge while addressing residents during his campaign tour of Kaur, where he urged voters to support the UDP-led coalition, arguing that the town and the wider Saloum area have remained largely neglected in terms of development.

Reflecting on his first visit to the area in 1996, Darboe questioned how little the town had changed over the past three decades, lamenting what he described as a lack of progress in Kaur and the wider Saloum region.

“When I came here in 1996, and today, there are no changes in Kaur. In Saloum, it continues to be the same. There is no development,” he said.

Darboe challenged residents to assess what successive administrations, from former President Yahya Jammeh’s government to the current administration of President Adama Barrow, had delivered to the town.

“Since the time of Jammeh up to President Barrow’s government, what meaningful development have they brought to Kaur Wharf Town?” he asked.

The UDP leader said his coalition would make the revival of Kaur Wharf Town a priority if elected, presenting the town’s restoration as part of his development promise to the area.

“We can only revive Kaur Wharf Town, ‘Dande Mayoo’, if you vote for the UDP,” he said.

The pledge forms part of Darboe’s campaign message as the Gambia First Coalition seeks to consolidate support across the country ahead of the December 5 presidential election.
