FG’s ₦100,000 Grant: Who can apply, How beneficiaries will be selected [Full Details]

By VeryNigerian VeryNigerian 4d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: VeryNigerian

The Federal Government has outlined the eligibility conditions and registration process for its advertised ₦100,000 family grant under the Nigeria Family Strengthening and Development Programme (NFSP), an initiative said to target poor and vulnerable households across the country.

According to a statement attributed to the programme’s management board, the intervention is designed to provide financial support to families struggling with economic hardship, rising food prices and the effects of ongoing economic reforms.

The programme is coordinated by the Hope for Indigenous Family Support Initiative’s Coordinating Office under the Federal Ministry of Youth Development.

However, the management board has clarified that meeting the basic registration requirements does not automatically guarantee payment, as beneficiaries are expected to undergo further verification and selection based on poverty and vulnerability criteria.

Who is eligible for the ₦100,000 grant?

The programme’s management board listed the following basic requirements for prospective applicants:

  1. Applicants must be Nigerian citizens by birth.
  2. They must possess a valid National Identification Number (NIN).
  3. They must be between 18 and 65 years old at the time of registration.

Applicants’ NIN details are expected to be verified through the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC).

The programme is specifically intended for poor and vulnerable households identified through social safety net systems and verified national identity records.

Therefore, meeting the age, citizenship and NIN requirements does not automatically qualify an applicant to receive the money.

How to apply for the ₦100,000 grant

The programme’s advertised registration portal is:

https://nfsp.ng/signup

Interested Nigerians are directed to visit the portal and provide the personal information requested on the registration page.

Applicants are expected to submit their details for verification and consideration under the programme’s selection process.

How beneficiaries will be selected

According to the management board, the selection process will involve identifying eligible households through social safety net systems, verifying their identity records and assessing their circumstances against established poverty and vulnerability criteria.

Beneficiaries are expected to be selected in phases across Nigeria’s six geopolitical zones.

The programme will cover a limited number of households in each phase, meaning that not everyone who registers will receive payment immediately.

The board also stated that applicants who have not received a notification should not automatically assume they have been permanently excluded.

How successful applicants will receive the ₦100,000

The management board said selected beneficiaries would receive the money directly into the bank accounts submitted during registration.

Successful applicants are expected to receive SMS notifications from designated payment providers.

Recipients will then be required to follow the instructions in the messages and provide the necessary consent before the funds are credited to their accounts.

Warning against fraud

The programme’s management board has warned Nigerians against individuals or agents demanding money in exchange for registration, inclusion on the beneficiary list or guaranteed payment.

It advised applicants not to pay anyone who claims to have the ability to secure the grant on their behalf.

The board also urged Nigerians to avoid unofficial registration websites and rely on recognised communication channels for further information.

Prospective applicants should exercise caution and independently confirm the registration portal through recognised government channels before submitting sensitive personal information, including their NIN and banking details.
