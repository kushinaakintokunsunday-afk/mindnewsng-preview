Abia-North 2027: Kalu bags multiple endorsements in Arochukwu, secures Philip Nto’s backing, 18-seater bus

By The Sun Nigeria The Sun Nigeria 3d ago 6 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: The Sun Nigeria

Kenneth Udeh from Umuahia Former APC Senatorial aspirant says Kalu has no competitor, pledges political structures for his re-election Senator receives 18-seate

Kenneth Udeh from Umuahia

  • Former APC Senatorial aspirant says Kalu has no competitor, pledges political structures for his re-election
  • Senator receives 18-seater bus, bags fourth Senate Press Corps Award

With the 2027 general elections fast approaching, incumbent Abia North Senator, Orji Uzor Kalu, has received multiple endorsements from political leaders, traditional rulers and community stakeholders during his ongoing ward-to-ward tour of Arochukwu Local Government Area of Abia State.

Among those backing his re-election is former APC senatorial aspirant, Prof. Philip Nto, who has declared support for Kalu’s return to the Senate.

Nto, a former Abia State Commissioner for Finance and Economic Planning, declared that Kalu had no serious competitor in the forthcoming senatorial election, pledging to deploy his political structures, supporters and resources towards securing the senator’s re-election.

He also dismissed suggestions of disunity within the Abia APC, declaring that the party remained united ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Nto made the declaration on Saturday when he hosted Kalu and members of his entourage at his residence in Ututu, Arochukwu LGA, following the senator’s ward-to-ward engagements across the community.

The former commissioner, who accompanied Kalu throughout the tour, said his decision to support the senator was informed by his performance, political pedigree, grassroots connections and commitment to the collective interests of Abia North.

He expressed confidence in Kalu’s political experience and popularity, insisting that the former governor remained the candidate to beat in the forthcoming election.

Kalu, who represents Abia North Senatorial District and currently chairs the Senate Committee on the South East Development Commission (SEDC), commenced his Arochukwu tour with a courtesy visit to the palace of the Eze Aro, where he met members of the traditional ruler’s cabinet to formally announce his ward-to-ward engagements.

Accompanied by prominent philanthropist, Engr. Dr. Emeka Maduagwu, and the APC candidate for Arochukwu State Constituency, Prince Ike Okoroafor, among other party leaders, the senator said the exercise was designed to strengthen his relationship with constituents and identify areas requiring further development.

He highlighted his interventions across Arochukwu, including road construction and rehabilitation, the construction of over 20 schools, installation of streetlights, distribution of educational materials and overseas scholarship opportunities for indigenes.

Traditional ruler recalls Kalu’s relationship with Ututu

At the palace of His Royal Majesty, Eze Solomon Nwaizu Okereke, JP, Ezeoma I of Ezema Ututu, the traditional ruler recalled Kalu’s longstanding relationship with the community, particularly his appointment of several Ututu indigenes to positions of responsibility during his administration as governor of Abia State between 1999 and 2007.

The monarch described Kalu as a familiar figure whose relationship with the people extended beyond his years in the Senate.

He commended the senator for maintaining his connection with the community and expressed support for his continued representation, while appealing for additional developmental projects to improve the living conditions of residents.

Similarly, Princess Evelyn Nwankwo applauded Kalu’s representation, describing him as a longstanding friend of the Ututu people.

She expressed appreciation for his interventions over the years and pledged the community’s continued goodwill and support.

APC candidates endorse Kalu’s developmental record

The APC candidate for Arochukwu State Constituency, Prince Ike Okoroafor, commended Kalu for attracting developmental projects to the area, particularly in road infrastructure, educational facilities and other constituency interventions.

Describing the senator as one of their own, Okoroafor said his longstanding relationship with the Aro and Ututu people had further strengthened his connection with the communities.

Also speaking, the APC candidate for Arochukwu/Ohafia Federal Constituency, Chief Ikenna Nicholas Ukwa, pledged to collaborate with Kalu, if elected, to attract additional federal projects and development opportunities to the constituency.

Ukwa maintained that cooperation among elected representatives at the National Assembly would accelerate development and address infrastructure challenges confronting rural communities.

A community leader, Ohachi Victor, also declared support for Kalu’s re-election, insisting that Ututu stood firmly behind the senator.

Kalu receives 18-seater bus

Beyond the political endorsements, Kalu received an 18-seater bus donated by supporters at Obinkita, Arochukwu Ward 2, during the tour.

The presentation was coordinated by Engr. Emeka Maduagwu, who joined representatives of his foundation and other support groups in handing over the vehicle to the senator.

Expressing appreciation for the gesture, Kalu said: “I am deeply touched by this collective gesture of goodwill, solidarity and confidence in my representation. Your kindness and continued support mean a great deal to me, and I do not take them for granted.

“May God bless you all abundantly.”

At the various communities visited, including Amannagwu, Ugbo, Asaga, Obinkita and the five Ututu wards, Kalu interacted with residents, community leaders, women and youths, listening to their concerns and discussing opportunities for further development.

At Asaga, the senator reflected on his developmental interventions across the area, particularly road projects, school construction, streetlight installations, overseas scholarships and the distribution of educational materials.

He acknowledged that more work remained to be done, stressing the need to sustain the relationship between elected representatives and their constituents.

Addressing the people during the engagements, Kalu reiterated his commitment to purposeful representation and sustainable community development.

He said: “I have come to listen to our people, understand their priorities and deepen our partnership for progress and development.”

The former governor also emphasised that his ward-to-ward engagements were not merely political visits but opportunities to assess the needs of communities and identify areas requiring further legislative and developmental attention.

He assured constituents that their concerns would continue to receive attention, maintaining that effective representation required regular consultation and sustained engagement.

Kalu bags fourth Senate Press Corps Award

The Arochukwu tour comes shortly after Kalu received the 2026 Best Impactful Representation Award from the Senate Press Corps (SPC), in recognition of his contributions to constituency development, legislative representation and human empowerment.

The latest recognition brings to four the number of Senate Press Corps awards received by the former Abia governor since his election into the Senate in 2019.

The award further highlights his legislative and constituency development engagements over the years.

Kalu’s Arochukwu tour forms part of his ongoing ward-to-ward consultations across Abia North Senatorial District ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The engagements have provided an opportunity for the senator to reconnect with constituents, receive feedback on his representation and secure renewed commitments from political stakeholders and community leaders.

The endorsement by Nto, a former senatorial aspirant who has pledged to mobilise his political structures for Kalu’s re-election, emerged as one of the major political highlights of the Arochukwu tour.
