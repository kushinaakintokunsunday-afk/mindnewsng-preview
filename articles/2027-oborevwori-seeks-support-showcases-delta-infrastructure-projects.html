2027: Oborevwori seeks support, showcases Delta infrastructure projects

By Punch Punch 5d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

Delta State Governor, Sheriff Oborevwori, has highlighted his administration’s infrastructure projects, particularly the construction of the PTI and DSC flyover

Oborevwori spoke on Wednesday during APC campaign rallies in Warri South and Uvwie Local Government Areas of the state, according to a statement issued on Thursday by his Chief Press Secretary, Festus Ahon.

The governor also witnessed the defection of the Nigeria Democratic Congress House of Assembly candidate for Warri South Constituency II, Owin Ikuku, and the party’s vice chairman into the APC.

Speaking at the Uvwie rally, Oborevwori said the construction of the PTI and DSC flyovers had significantly reduced travel time and improved movement within the area.

“Anytime I pass through this route, it gives me joy. Three and a half years ago, travelling through here towards Bayelsa could take as long as three hours. Today, the story is different,” he said.

At the Warri rally, the governor attributed the state’s transformation to deliberate investments and his administration’s commitment to development, stressing that governance should be measured by projects that directly improve the lives of the people.

He also called for peaceful conduct during the forthcoming elections, insisting that political differences should not lead to violence.

“I am not a violent person. I don’t believe in violence. We must allow the people to exercise their democratic rights peacefully,” he said.

Oborevwori, who was accompanied by his wife, Deaconess Tobore Oborevwori, and other political leaders, urged eligible voters to turn out and exercise their civic responsibility, while appreciating residents for their support.

The Deputy Governor, Monday Onyeme, described Oborevwori as a performing leader, saying the large turnout at the rallies reflected the growing acceptance of the APC in the areas.

The state APC Chairman, Solomon Arenyeka, presented party flags to the party’s House of Assembly candidates for Warri South Constituencies I and II and formally received the defectors from the NDC.

At the Uvwie rally, Arenyeka also presented flags to the APC candidates for the House of Representatives, Ransom Onoyake, and the Uvwie State Constituency, Edafe Emakpor.

Similarly, the Director-General of the Delta APC Campaign Council, James Manager, commended the governor for what he described as the visible impact of his administration.

Manager listed road construction, bridge projects, market development and youth empowerment initiatives among the achievements of the Oborevwori administration.

The Minister of Aviation and Aerospace Development, Festus Keyamo, urged residents to support President Bola Tinubu and Oborevwori, cautioning against misinformation about their administrations.

Keyamo described the developments in Uvwie and other parts of Delta State as unprecedented, praising the governor for rehabilitating roads that had remained in deplorable condition for several years.

He assured Oborevwori that residents of Uvwie, Warri and other parts of the state would support his administration.
