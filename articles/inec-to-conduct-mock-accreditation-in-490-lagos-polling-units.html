‌‍‍‍‍⁠⁠‍‍⁠⁠​​‌⁠‌INEC to conduct mock accreditation in 490 Lagos polling units

By Vanguard Vanguard 1d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Vanguard

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) says it will conduct a mock accreditation and result upload exercise in 490 polling units across Lagos Stat

Prof. Ayobami Salami, the Resident Electoral Commissioner (REC) in Lagos State, disclosed this in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria (NAN) on Tuesday in Lagos.

Salami said the exercise would take place between 8:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. in selected polling units across the state.

He said it was designed to assess the performance of the Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) and identify possible gaps or challenges that would be addressed before the 2027 general elections.

According to him, the exercise will cover the 245 Registration Areas (RAs) across the state’s 20 Local Government Areas (LGAs), with two polling units selected in each Registration Area.

He urged residents, particularly eligible voters in the selected polling units, to participate in the exercise to enable the commission to assess the effectiveness of its equipment and electoral processes.

The REC said the list of selected polling units would be displayed at the respective local government area offices to enable residents to identify the designated locations.

Salami said he had directed all electoral officers in the state to convene stakeholder meetings in their respective LGAs ahead of the exercise.

He said the meetings would provide an opportunity to engage political parties and other stakeholders on the importance of participating in the mock accreditation exercise.

The REC said the engagements would also help mobilise eligible voters in the selected polling units to participate in the exercise.

He outlined the activities preceding the exercise, saying BVAS configuration would take place on Nov. 25, followed by the training of nominated personnel on Nov. 26.

According to him, personnel and devices would be deployed to the selected polling units on Friday, ahead of the exercise.

Salami reiterated that the exercise formed part of INEC’s preparations to ensure a smooth and efficient electoral process during the 2027 general elections. (NAN)
