3,000 runners set for Capital City Race

By Punch Punch 3d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

The third edition of the Capital City Race will hold on Saturday, November 21, in Ikeja, Lagos, with 3,000 runners expected to take part in the 10-kilometre eve

The organisers have set aside at least N2m in cash prizes, but insist that promoting fitness, healthy living and recreational running matters more than the financial rewards.

The race convener, Dapo Ajayi, said at a press conference on Thursday at the Teslim Balogun Stadium, Lagos, that the event had grown steadily since it began in 2024. The first edition attracted about 1,000 runners, while 1,467 registered for the second edition in 2025. All slots for this year’s race have been filled.

The race starts at Ikeja City Mall and runs through Ikeja. Ajayi said the organisers were working with partners to improve the participants’ experience and make the event more accessible.

“We are happy with the steady progress and impact we are making. Very soon, Capital City Race may expand across other states,” he hinted.

The Director-General of the Lagos State Sports Commission, Lekan Fatodu, described the race as an important part of efforts to build a stronger culture of physical activity in the state. He said the rising number of road races and fitness events showed growing interest in healthier lifestyles among Lagos residents.

“What the competition does is to motivate them and push them to get on the road. We have realised that every single day, a lot of Lagosians want to step out to engage in outdoor activities related to healthy living, fitness and wellness,” Fatodu said.

He added that the government would keep supporting such initiatives because of their health and economic benefits, noting that businesses producing sports merchandise and selling healthy drinks also gain from the growing sports industry.

The co-convener, Folashade Falokun, said the growing number of running events in Lagos showed that more organisers were embracing fitness and longevity.

“We are encouraging fitness because we are encouraging longevity. People keep saying, ‘I am sick, I am feeling tired.’ When you keep fit, it is good for everybody. So, it is a win-win for all of us, for the runners and the organisers,” she said.

Falokun appealed to government institutions, businesses and corporate organisations to support initiatives that encourage mass participation in physical activity.

Three Crowns Milk and Pocari Sweat are among the sponsors of the 2026 race, and both brands said they were delighted to support a platform that promotes healthy living.

The race was conceived by sports and marketing consultant Ajayi to give Lagos a professionally organised 10-kilometre road race.
