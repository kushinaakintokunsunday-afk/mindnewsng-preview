Man dies after 10km race in Lagos

By Punch Punch 4d ago 2 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Punch

A member of an Ikeja-based running community in Lagos, Roy Okeke, has died days after he reportedly became dizzy during a 10km race on Sunday. The Team Active r

The Team Active running community announced his death in a statement shared on its Instagram page on Thursday , expressing sadness over the loss of one of its members.

The group said what was meant to be a regular morning of fitness and community bonding turned tragic when Okeke became dizzy at about the 6km mark of the race.

“During the 10km dash organised by our sponsors on Sunday morning, while running alongside some of our members, he reportedly became dizzy at about the 6km mark,” the statement said.

According to the community, Okeke was immediately attended to and rushed to a hospital by ambulance.

“At the hospital, he experienced some seizures, but the doctors were able to stabilise him and bring him back to normal,” it added.

However, the group said he died on Tuesday despite the medical intervention.

“Sadly, despite these efforts, he eventually passed away on Tuesday,” the statement said.

Describing the deceased as an important member of the community, Team Active said his death had left members heartbroken.

“Team Active has always been more than just a running community. We are a family built on friendship, shared experiences, love and a passion for staying active. And today, we have lost one of our own,” it said.

The group extended its condolences to Okeke’s family, loved ones and others affected by his death.

“Our thoughts and prayers are with his family, loved ones and everyone who has been affected by this tragedy,” it said.

It also acknowledged the emotional impact of the incident on members who were present during the race.

“We also want to send our love and strength to the members who were present that morning and witnessed the unfortunate events,” the statement added.

As part of its mourning, the community announced the suspension of its scheduled runs for the following day and Saturday.

“In honour of our departed member and out of respect for this difficult moment, there will be no scheduled run tomorrow or on Saturday morning,” it said, adding that the decision was to give members time to “pause, reflect, mourn and stand together as a community.”
