Payaza CEO Seyi Ebenezer Joins Livestock Feeds Board After Sunbeth Treenuts And Sesame’s ₦19.5bn Acquisition

By Channels TV Channels TV 6d ago 3 min read
MINDNEWSNG
Photo: Channels TV

Payaza Africa founder and Chief Executive Officer Seyi Ebenezer has been appointed to the Board of Directors of Livestock Feeds Plc, one of Nigeria’s longest-established publicly listed agribusinesses.

Effective November 17, 2026, his appointment follows Sunbeth Treenuts and Sesame Limited’s successful ₦19.5 billion acquisition of UAC of Nigeria Plc’s 73.29 per cent controlling stake in Livestock Feeds.

Payaza Africa, in a statement on Wednesday, disclosed that the landmark transaction, which received full regulatory approvals from the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), and Nigerian Exchange Limited (NGX), ends UAC’s 13-year ownership of the company and ushers in a bold new era for the agribusiness.

Ebenezer is one of five newly appointed directors joining the company’s reconstituted eight-member board, bringing a wealth of experience in corporate finance, financial technology, business expansion, and strategic management.

His appointment marks a significant expansion of his corporate leadership portfolio from financial technology into Nigeria’s agricultural sector.

The payment solutions firm stated that Ebenezer’s appointment introduces a financial services and technology perspective to the governance of Livestock Feeds, a company with more than six decades of experience in Nigeria’s agricultural value chain.

According to the statement, the new board member is a chartered accountant with over 17 years of experience in financial services, having begun his professional career at KPMG before moving into corporate banking, where he held senior leadership positions at Access Bank and Keystone Bank.

“During his banking career, he structured and executed more than $1 billion in debt and capital transactions across industries, including energy, real estate, logistics, technology, and financial services, according to his professional profile published by Lagos Business School.

“During his banking career, he structured and executed more than $1 billion in debt and capital transactions across industries including energy, real estate, logistics, technology, and financial services, according to his professional profile published by Lagos Business School.

“He subsequently founded Payaza Africa in 2022, building a payments infrastructure company that supports businesses operating across African and international markets. The company has expanded its presence to 31 markets globally, providing payment processing and cross-border transaction solutions,” said Payaza Africa.

Ebenezer described his appointment as one that presents a profound opportunity to bring his experience into an industry fundamental to Nigeria’s economic prosperity.

“For much of my career, I have been driven by a singular focus: building financial systems that empower businesses to grow and thrive. Joining the board of Livestock Feeds presents a profound opportunity to bring that experience into an industry that is fundamental to Nigeria’s economic prosperity,” he was quoted as saying.

“Agriculture is not just about food production. It is about access to finance, efficient distribution, resilient supply chains, and the ability of businesses to operate sustainably at scale. What excites me most about this appointment is the opportunity to contribute my experience in financial services, technology, and business expansion to a company with more than six decades of history.

“I believe there is immense potential to strengthen operational efficiency, support long-term growth, and create sustainable value for shareholders, while actively contributing to Nigeria’s broader food security goals,” he added.

Alongside Ebenezer, the newly appointed board members are Sam Okuwlehi, Professor Franklin Nnaemeka Ngwu, Mary Mulili and Farrukh Siddiqui, who join continuing directors Joseph I.D. Dada, Adegboyega Adedeji and Abayomi Adeyemi, bringing together professionals with experience across financial services, corporate governance, aviation, investment management and business leadership.
