Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Mindnewsng ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit mindnewsng.com.

Information We Collect

  • Automatically collected data: when you browse the site, our servers and analytics tools collect standard log data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent and referring website. This is used in aggregate to understand traffic and improve the site.
  • Information you give us: if you contact us by email or subscribe to a newsletter, we receive the name and email address you provide. We use it only to reply to you or send you the content you requested.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: small files stored on your device that help the site function, remember preferences and measure performance.

Advertising & Third-Party Vendors

We may display advertising on the site, including ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and time on site in anonymised form. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

How We Use Data

We use collected data to operate and improve the website, measure audience, respond to enquiries, deliver newsletters you subscribe to, and display relevant advertising. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention & Security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we apply reasonable technical measures to protect it. No internet transmission is ever 100% secure, but we treat your data with care.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you by emailing editor@mindnewsng.com. Nigerian residents may also refer to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External Links

Our articles and store link to third-party websites (for example, partner news outlets and our ebook checkout). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email editor@mindnewsng.com.
