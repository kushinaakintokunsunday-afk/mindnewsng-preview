About Mindnewsng

Mindnewsng (mindnewsng.com) is a Nigerian digital newspaper delivering breaking news, politics, business, sports, entertainment, jobs and education, technology, health and world news to Nigerian readers at home and in the diaspora.

We publish fast, verified summaries of the day's most important stories, drawing on trusted national and international news wires, and we always credit the originating outlet. Where we report allegations or unverified claims, we say so clearly and link to the full source.

Our Mission

To keep Nigerians informed with news that is accurate, balanced and independent — delivered quickly, in clear language, and free to read.

What Makes Us Different

  • Speed with standards. Stories are published as events unfold, but every report is checked and attributed before it goes live.
  • Reader-first format. Our social posts link back to full stories here, so the complete picture is always on our own site.
  • Independence. Editorial decisions are not influenced by advertisers or political interests. Sponsored content, where it ever appears, is clearly labelled.

How We Work

Our newsroom combines automated monitoring of trusted feeds with human editors who select, verify and frame each story. Sensitive topics — deaths, crime, court cases and allegations — are handled with extra care and carry clear disclaimers.
